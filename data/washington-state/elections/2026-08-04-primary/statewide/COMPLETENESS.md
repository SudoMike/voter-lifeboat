# Statewide Completeness Note

Election: 2026 Washington primary, August 4, 2026.

The statewide package is treated as complete for the primary election when it
includes every statewide contest that VoteWA marks as appearing on the primary
ballot, plus every statewide measure, if any.

As of 2026-07-17, the statewide primary contests in this package are:

- Justice Position No. 1, Supreme Court
- Justice Position No. 3, Supreme Court
- Justice Position No. 5, Supreme Court
- Justice Position No. 7, Supreme Court

Supreme Court Position No. 4 is intentionally excluded from the August primary
package because VoteWA lists that race's candidates with an election status of
`Advanced to General`, not `In Primary`.

No statewide measures are included for this August primary. The Washington
Secretary of State's current-election materials describe the August measures as
local measures, and the statewide candidate/guide sources did not identify an
August statewide measure.

## Why `interim/contests.json` has no Supreme Court contests

This package's `interim/contests.json` is generated by
`pipeline/normalize_research_inputs.py`. It holds only the 94 congressional
and legislative contests deduplicated from the 38 non-King county
`app-contests.json` files. It has never contained the Supreme Court contests,
and its `research-plan.json` (83 contested races) does not plan them.

The four Supreme Court contests above come from King County Elections' primary
list instead: they are in `counties/king/interim/contests.json` and in King's
`research-plan.json`. Their dossiers and scoring files were moved into this
package's `dossiers/` and `scoring/` when the primary became an
election-scoped package. `validate_scoring.py` falls back from the King plan to
`statewide/scoring/`, `merge_scores.py` reads every package's `scoring/`, and
`assemble_app_data.py` looks for dossiers in every package. The scored data is complete. The gap is only in which
interim file lists the contests, and it is left as is so the primary's frozen
plans and outputs stay byte-identical. The November general's statewide package
lists its Supreme Court contests in its own `interim/contests.json`.
