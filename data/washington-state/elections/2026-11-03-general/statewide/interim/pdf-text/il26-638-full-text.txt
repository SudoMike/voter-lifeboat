AN ACT Relating to defending equity in interscholastic sports; 1 amending RCW 28A.600.200 and 28A.640.020 ; adding a new section to 2 chapter 28A.600 RCW ; and creating new sections. 3 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON: 4 NEW SECTION. Sec. 1. The people find: 5 (1) Existing Washington state law and school district policies 6 require students to undergo a routine physical examination prior to 7 participation in interscholastic sports, which includes documentation 8 of the student's sex assigned at birth; 9 (2) This established requirement provides a reliable and 10 medically verified basis to determine a student's biological sex; 11 (3) Protecting the integrity and fairness of women's 12 interscholastic sports necessitates that students compete in athletic 13 activities consistent with the gender assigned at birth; and 14 (4) Leveraging the existing physical examination process to 15 verify biological sex ensures equity, safety, and competitive balance 16 in sports intended exclusively for female students, while respecting 17 the rights and opportunities of all participants. 18 Sec. 2.
