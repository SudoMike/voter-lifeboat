Statement
I am excited to have the opportunity to continue serving you as your Lincoln County Sheriff. I was raised in Lincoln County and have worked for Lincoln County Sheriff’s Office since 2004. During this time, I have served in all facets of the operation to include 911 dispatcher, corrections deputy, school resource officer, patrol deputy, and patrol sergeant, and sheriff along with numerous specialty positions. I am a conservative, community service orientated, and believe in the golden rule. I also believe local experience is what Lincoln County needs for this office. Someone who not only has a record of serving you, but also understands our culture, community values, and our high quality of life that is second to none.
The Washington State Constitution defines the sheriff as the chief executive officer and conservator of the peace of the county. I believe the sheriff’s job is not only to serve and protect their people and property, but to also ensure our citizens individual liberties are not infringed upon. As a candidate for sheriff you have my pledge that I will take the responsibility to assure our county is safe and secure, and that your rights will be protected!
