Statement

I am honored to have the opportunity to serve a second term for you, Lincoln County citizens, as your County Clerk. My family has been in Lincoln County since my great-great grandparents settled here and it is my home! I graduated from Davenport High School in 2002, and I graduated summa cum laude from Eastern Washington University in 2006. I started working as a deputy clerk in the Lincoln County Clerk’s Office fresh out of college and I have been there for 20 years so far. The County Clerk is the record keeper and financial officer for the Superior Court, and the office requires accuracy and knowledge of what the law requires. I have benefitted from 20 years of experience, including one term as your elected Clerk. I am ready to serve a second term to maintain the trusted reputation of the Lincoln County Clerk’s Office. Thank you for your vote and support!
