Statement

Over the past four years serving in the Auditor’s Office, I have had the privilege of working alongside an extraordinary staff dedicated to integrity, professionalism, and public service. Every day, I have witnessed the commitment our team brings to protecting public records, ensuring transparent elections, and serving the people of our community with accuracy and care. Their hard work has inspired me and strengthened my belief that leadership begins with service.
My first term has been a tremendous learning experience. I have gained a deeper understanding of the responsibilities entrusted to this office and the importance of listening, adapting, and continually improving how we serve the public. Together, we have worked to modernize processes, improve accessibility, and maintain the trust our citizens place in us.
I am proud of what we have accomplished, but I also know there is more we can achieve. Looking ahead to the next four years, I remain committed to building on that progress with transparency, accountability, and teamwork at the center of every decision. I am excited to continue serving our community, supporting the outstanding professionals in our office, and ensuring the Auditor’s Office remains responsive, reliable, and focused on the people we serve.
