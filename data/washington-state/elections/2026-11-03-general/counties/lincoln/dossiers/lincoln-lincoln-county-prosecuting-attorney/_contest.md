## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes crimes for the county and is the legal adviser to the commissioners and county officials, representing the county in civil cases [S4][S9].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested: Prosecuting Attorney Ty Albertson of Odessa, appointed in December 2024, is the only candidate in his first election to the office [S1][S3][S7].
