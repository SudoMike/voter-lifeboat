## Background

- Tracy Arlene Staab submitted no statement, experience or education to the 2026 online guide [S1].
- She won the open seat in 2020 against attorney Marshall Casey, to replace retiring Judge Kevin Korsmo [S2]; in Lincoln County she received 3,264 votes to Casey's 2,447 [S3].
- Before that she was a Spokane Municipal Court judge from 2009, and earlier a Court of Appeals law clerk and an appellate attorney (attorney of record in 70 Ninth Circuit cases); she was admitted to the bar in 1993 [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- In 2020 she stressed her criminal-law background, since many of the court's cases are criminal, and called the written briefs the most important part of an appeal [S2].
