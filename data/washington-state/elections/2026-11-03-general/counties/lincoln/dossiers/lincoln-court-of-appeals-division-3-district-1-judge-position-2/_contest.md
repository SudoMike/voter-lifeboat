## What the office does

Court of Appeals judges decide appeals from superior-court cases; District 1 of Division III is elected by voters in Ferry, Lincoln, Okanogan, Pend Oreille, Spokane and Stevens counties and has two judges [S5]. The seat is not in the county's printed pamphlet, which covers county offices only; it is on the sample ballot [S4].

## Race dynamics
