## Background

- Traci Gants, of Davenport, prefers the Republican Party [S1][S6].
- She has worked in the Lincoln County Clerk's Office since college, about 20 years, first as a deputy clerk; she graduated from Davenport High School in 2002 and summa cum laude from Eastern Washington University in 2006 [S1][S2]. In 2022 she ran when the previous clerk retired, after 16 years as deputy clerk [S3].
- She was unopposed in November 2022 (4,904 votes, 74 write-ins) [S4] and in the August 2026 primary (2,936 of 2,985 votes) [S5].
- She registered with the PDC under the mini-reporting option [S7].

## Positions (from own statements)

- Her statement asks for a second term "to maintain the trusted reputation of the Lincoln County Clerk's Office," citing 20 years of experience [S1][S2].
