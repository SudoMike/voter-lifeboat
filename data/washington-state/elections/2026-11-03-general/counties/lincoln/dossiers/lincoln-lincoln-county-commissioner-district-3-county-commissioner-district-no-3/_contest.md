## What the office does

The three commissioners are the county's legislative body and chief administrators: they adopt the budget, provide for county buildings and roads, and set county policy [S3][S12]. Each is nominated by the voters of their district in the primary and elected by the whole county in the general [S3][S11]; the 2022 District No. 3 primary counted District 3 votes only [S5], and the 2026 primary race reported 16 of the county's 46 units [S9].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested: Commissioner Rob Coffman of Wilbur, first elected in 2010, is the only candidate for a fifth term [S1][S3][S4][S13].
