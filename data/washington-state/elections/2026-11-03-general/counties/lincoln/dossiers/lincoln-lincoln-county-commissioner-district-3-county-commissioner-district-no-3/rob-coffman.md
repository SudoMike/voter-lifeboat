## Background

- Rob Coffman, of Wilbur, prefers the Republican Party and submitted no experience, education or statement to the 2026 pamphlet or online guide [S1][S2][S13].
- First elected in 2010; current term January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2026; 2026 board member [S3]. In 2022 he sought a fourth term [S4] and beat Jason DeBord county-wide, 3,613 to 1,899 [S8].
- In November 2024 he became the 118th president of the Washington State Association of Counties, after two years as its 2nd and 1st vice president [S6].
- He won the District 3 primary in August 2026 unopposed (1,175 of 1,208 votes) [S9]. He registered with the PDC under the mini-reporting option [S10].

## Positions (from own statements)

- In 2022 he said the board had balanced 11 budgets "with declining revenues and more unfunded mandates from the state," and that public safety and road funds need more resources [S4].
- In June 2025 the board adopted a resolution declaring the county not a "sanctuary" jurisdiction after the county appeared on a Department of Homeland Security list; Coffman wrote that the classification "could not be further from the truth" [S7].

## Record

- Commissioner since 2010; sworn in as president of the Washington State Association of Counties in November 2024 [S3][S6].
