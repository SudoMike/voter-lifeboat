## What the office does

The sheriff is the county's chief law enforcement officer: patrol, investigations, the jail, civil process and court orders [S11]. Lincoln's office includes 911 dispatch, corrections and marine patrol positions [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested: Sheriff Gabe Gants of Davenport, appointed interim sheriff in 2022 and elected that November, is the only candidate [S1][S3][S4][S12].
