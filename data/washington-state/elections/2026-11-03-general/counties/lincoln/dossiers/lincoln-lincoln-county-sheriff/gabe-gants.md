## Background

- Gabe Gants, of Davenport, prefers the Republican Party [S1][S12].
- He has worked for the Lincoln County Sheriff's Office since 2004 as 911 dispatcher, corrections deputy, school resource officer, patrol deputy, field training officer, marine patrol captain and patrol sergeant [S1][S2].
- The commissioners named him interim sheriff from July 1, 2022, after Sheriff Wade Magers retired, choosing him from three finalists named by the Lincoln County Republican Central Committee [S3]. In February 2021, as a sergeant, he had received the allegation that later led to Magers' guilty plea to third-degree rendering criminal assistance [S5].
- He was elected in November 2022 over Brian Telford, 3,881 to 1,922 [S8], and was unopposed in the August 2026 primary (2,898 of 2,988 votes) [S9].
- Former Davenport school board and Davenport City Council member; B.A. in secondary education, Eastern Washington University, 2004 [S1].
- He registered with the PDC under the mini-reporting option [S10].

## Positions (from own statements)

- Describes himself as "a conservative, community service orientated" and says the sheriff's job includes ensuring "our citizens individual liberties are not infringed upon" [S1][S2].
- Said in 2022 that staffing would be his first priority [S4]; in a 2025 column he wrote that Washington ranks 51st in officers per capita and that hiring is "ultra-competitive," while his office's staffing has been fairly stable [S6].
- In a December 2025 column he described the office's 2025 focus as technology: replacing failed radio equipment and new investigative tools [S7].
