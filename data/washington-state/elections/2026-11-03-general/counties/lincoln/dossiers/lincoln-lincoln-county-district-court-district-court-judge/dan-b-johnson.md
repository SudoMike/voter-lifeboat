## Background

- Dan B. Johnson, of Davenport, has been the Lincoln County District Court judge since July 1, 2015, and is also the elected judge pro tem for Lincoln County Superior Court [S1][S2][S3].
- Licensed as an attorney since 1980; Stevens County deputy prosecutor 1981-1987, later in private practice based in Spokane [S1][S3].
- B.S. in business administration, University of Minnesota, 1977; J.D. cum laude, Gonzaga, 1980 [S1].
- Elected by fellow judges in 2016 to the state Board for Judicial Administration and served six years [S1].
- The 2022 profile said he had been re-elected twice without opposition since his appointment [S3]; in November 2022 he was again unopposed (4,350 votes, 65 write-ins) [S6]. In 2020 he ran for Lincoln County Superior Court and lost to Jeff Barkdull, 4,380 to 1,957 [S7].
- At 67 in 2022 he said he had eight years before mandatory judicial retirement [S3]. He registered with the PDC under the mini-reporting option [S8].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- His 2026 statement says only that it has been his honor to serve and that he looks forward to continuing [S1].

## Record

- From 2023 the court has held weekly Tuesday sessions for people to resolve outstanding misdemeanor warrants, under a National Center for State Courts technical-assistance grant that six courts nationwide received [S4].
- In April 2026 he and the court administrator asked the commissioners to approve an additional clerk ahead of the statewide case-management transition [S5].
