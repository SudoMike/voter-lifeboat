## Background

- Chandra L. Schumacher, of Harrington, prefers the Republican Party [S1][S7].
- The commissioners chose her in July 2021 to complete Auditor Shelly Johnston's term after Johnston's resignation, starting September 1, 2021 [S3][S4]; she was unopposed in November 2022 (4,973 votes, 49 write-ins) [S5] and in the August 2026 primary (2,924 of 2,966 votes) [S6].
- Her statement lists her as a CPA since 2001, 10 years as chief budget officer of the Eastern Washington University Foundation, and chief financial officer of Odessa Union Grain Growers; B.A. in professional accounting, EWU, 2000 [S1][S2]. The 2021-2022 coverage describes her as from a third-generation farming family [S3][S4].
- Community service listed: Innovia Foundation finance committee, Harrington Golf and Country Club [S1].
- She registered with the PDC under the mini-reporting option [S8].

## Positions (from own statements)

- Her statement says the office has worked to modernize processes, improve accessibility, protect public records and ensure transparent elections, and pledges "transparency, accountability, and teamwork" over the next term [S1][S2].
