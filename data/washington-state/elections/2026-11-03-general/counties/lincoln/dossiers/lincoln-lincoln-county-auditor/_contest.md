## What the office does

The auditor records deeds and other instruments, keeps the county's accounts with the treasurer [S9], and, in a non-charter county, maintains voter registration and runs elections [S10].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested: Auditor Chandra L. Schumacher of Harrington, appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022, is the only candidate [S1][S3][S7].
