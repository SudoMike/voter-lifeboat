## ExplanatoryStatement
Passage of Proposition No. 1 would allow Riverside School District to replace an existing educational programs and operations levy that expires at the end of 2027. The replacement levy will be used to pay expenses of programs, services and staff not funded by the State, including elective and advanced courses, vocational education, nurses, counselors, technology support, safety, security, performing arts, athletics, and extracurricular activities. Further information is available at: www.riversidesd.org.
The proposed three-year replacement levy would authorize the collection of taxes to provide up to $3,996,811 in 2028, $4,116,715 in 2029 and $4,240,217 in 2030. The levy rate required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $1.58 per $1,000 of assessed value. The exact levy rates and amounts to be collected may be adjusted based upon the actual assessed value of the taxable property within the District and the limitations imposed by State law at the time of the levy. Exemptions from taxes may be available, call your county assessor for more information.

## ArgumentFor
Vote Yes to Keep Riverside Students Supported
Proposition 1 replaces the current EPO levy when it expires; it does not create a second levy. Voting yes means continuing a local investment in the programs, staff, and opportunities that help Riverside students succeed. We know what these programs mean to our own children, and we want every student in our district to have access to them. Please vote yes on Riverside School District Proposition 1.
As parents of children who attended or attend all Riverside schools, we see firsthand how important these programs are at every stage of a student’s education. The Educational Programs and Operations levy helps provide opportunities and support that state funding does not fully cover.
This replacement levy supports elective and advanced courses, vocational and career-focused education, school nurses, counselors, technology support, safety and security, performing arts, athletics, and extracurricular activities. These are not “extras” to the students who depend on them. They help children discover their strengths, prepare for life after graduation, stay connected to school, and receive academic, emotional, and health support when they need it.
Families throughout our district benefit from these services every day. Whether a student needs a counselor, wants to take an advanced class, participate in music or sports, learn job-ready skills, or simply attend a school that is safe and properly supported, this levy makes those opportunities possible.
