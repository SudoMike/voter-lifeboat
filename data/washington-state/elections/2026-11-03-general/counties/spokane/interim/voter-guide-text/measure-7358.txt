## ExplanatoryStatement
Passage of Proposition No. 2 would allow West Valley School District to replace a capital levy that expires at the end of 2027. The replacement levy will be used to pay costs of making District‑wide safety, security and infrastructure improvements, including: (1) modernizing, upgrading, acquiring, installing and implementing entrance security (including cameras, access controls, door locks, communications systems) and other safety and security systems; (2) constructing a library addition at Pasadena Park Elementary School; (3) replacing heating, ventilation, and air climate control systems and equipment (HVAC) at Spokane Valley High School; and (4) making other health, safety, security, infrastructure and capital improvements to school facilities (including sprinkler system installations and facility abatement projects). Further information is available at www.wvsd.org.
The proposed two-year replacement levy would authorize the collection of taxes to provide $4,191,809 in 2028 and $4,317,563 in 2029. The levy rate required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $1.00 per $1,000 of assessed value. Exemptions from taxes may be available, contact the Spokane County Assessor 509-477-3698.

## ArgumentFor
Vote “Yes” on West Valley School District Proposition 2
West Valley friends and neighbors, our schools are the heart of the community. Every day, students from our district walk into buildings that should be safe, healthy, and ready to support their learning. Many of our facilities are aging, and the needs are real. That’s why the 2-year capital project’s levy is so important.
This levy focuses on safety and security. It will modernize our school entrances with updated cameras, access controls, door locks, and communication systems; these tools protect students and staff and allow our schools to respond quickly in case of an emergency. It also addresses a major need at SVHS to replace the outdated HVAC system. Reliable air quality and temperature aren’t luxuries; they’re essential for students’ comfort, health, and academic success.
This levy will fund critical health, safety, and infrastructure improvements. Including replacing antiquated fire sprinkler systems and completing necessary abatement projects; work that keeps our buildings safe, compliant, and functional for years to come.
Supporting this levy means supporting our students. It gives them learning environments that meet today’s building and learning standards. It’s a responsible, time-limited measure that ensures West Valley schools remain strong places for our children to learn and grow. We encourage our community to join together and support this levy. When we invest in safe, healthy schools, we invest in the future of every child in the West Valley School District.
