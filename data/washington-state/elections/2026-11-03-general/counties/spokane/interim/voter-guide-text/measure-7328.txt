## ExplanatoryStatement
Proposition 1 would replace an existing educational programs and operation levy expiring in 2027. This replacement levy will fund expenses not funded by the State, including: athletics, extracurricular activities, electives, class size reduction, counselors and mental health supports, classified and certificated staff above the prototypical model, salaries for enrichment activities, substitutes, safety and security, instructional technology, transportation costs, nutrition services costs not funded by state or federal sources, special education costs above state and federal funding allocations, nursing staff above funded levels, early learning programs not funded through other sources and other enrichment activities for students. Visit: www.cheneysd.org.
The proposed replacement levy would authorize the collection of up to $18,450,000 in 2028, $19,000,000 in 2029 and $19,550,000 in 2030. The levy rate required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $2.10 per $1,000 of assessed value. Exemptions from taxes may be available, contact your county assessor.

## ArgumentFor
Cheney School District is asking voters to support the Replacement Education, Programs and Operations Levy on November 3rd, 2026 and I urge you to vote "yes" in support of this levy.
Your Yes vote supports student learning. The levy allows CSD to maintain the exceptional educational and extra-curricular programs currently offered in our school district. Supplemental levies are necessary to provide well-rounded programs for students.
The Replacement EP&O levy goes directly to providing students with the high-quality instruction and academic support they need to succeed. Failure to pass this measure would result in drastic cuts to educational programs, student support, and services. These critical programs, smaller class size, and instructional support staff for targeted learning have been shown to significantly improve student learning and lead to better outcomes after graduating.
Levy funds will allow the CSD to continue to provide crucial programs and support not funded by the state. This levy will replace the current levy, which means they are renewing an expiring levy so the educational services can continue to thrive. This is not a new tax. It’s time to renew our commitment and ensure a quality education for all our students. Let's vote Yes for Proposition 1 on November 3rd.
