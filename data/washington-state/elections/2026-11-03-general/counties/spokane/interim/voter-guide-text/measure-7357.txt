## ExplanatoryStatement
Passage of Proposition No. 1 would allow West Valley School District to replace an educational programs and operations levy that expires at the end of 2027. The replacement levy will be used to pay expenses of educational programs and operations not funded by the State, including smaller class size, advanced courses, nurses, counselors, technology, safety, music, athletics, extracurricular activities, facility maintenance/repair and operations. Further information is available at www.wvsd.org.
The proposed three-year replacement levy would authorize the collection of taxes to provide up to $10,479,522 in 2028, $10,793,908 in 2029 and $10,955,816 in 2030. The levy rate required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $2.50 per $1,000 of assessed value. The exact levy rates and amounts to be collected may be adjusted based upon the actual assessed value of the taxable property within the District and the limitations imposed by State law at the time of the levy. Exemptions from taxes may be available, contact the Spokane County Assessor 509-477-3698.

## ArgumentFor
Vote “Yes” on West Valley School District Proposition 1 EP&O Levy
The 3-year Educational Programs and Operations (EP&O) replacement levy is essential to maintaining the high quality learning experience our community expects for every West Valley student. This is not a new tax. It replaces the expiring levy that has long supported the programs and services our students rely on every day.
State funding does not fully cover the cost of educating our students. This replacement levy fills critical gaps by supporting smaller class sizes, providing advanced courses, allowing for on-site nurses and counselors, providing and updating technology resources, and enhancing school safety measures. It also sustains the programs that enrich students’ lives and keep them engaged in school; including music, athletics, and extracurricular activities.
As West Valley supporters, we know that supplementary staff, programs, and additional security measures that keep our students safe, rely upon the continuation of this levy and not state funding. This levy enables the staffing, programs, and building enhancements that other funding measures do not.
This levy supports the full educational experience our community values. It ensures that students in the West Valley School District continue to benefit from strong academics, safe schools, and well-rounded opportunities. By renewing this levy, we protect essential programs, maintain stability, and invest directly in our students’ futures, all without increasing the tax rate. Vote “yes” for Proposition 1 and West Valley students.
