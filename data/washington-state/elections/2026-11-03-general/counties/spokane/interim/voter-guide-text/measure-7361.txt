## BallotTitle
Proposition No. 1
Spokane County Fire Protection District No. 9
Property Tax Levy for Fire Protection and Emergency Services
The Board of Fire Commissioners of Spokane County Fire Protection District No. 9 adopted Resolution No. 26-8 concerning a proposition to maintain and adequately fund District operations.
This proposition authorizes the District to restore its regular property tax levy to $1.50 per $1,000.00 of assessed valuation to be levied in 2026 and allows annual levy revenue increases of up to 6% per year for the next five years (but not to exceed the $1.50 rate limit). The dollar amount of the final levy shall serve as the base for subsequent levy limitations as provided by RCW 84.55.
Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
Spokane County Fire District 9 (District) provides fire protection and emergency medical services across 125 square miles. This proposition would authorize a six-year levy lid lift for the District’s regular fire levy. If approved, the levy rate for collection in 2027 would be $1.50 per $1,000 of assessed value. During the following five years, the District could increase levy revenue by up to 6% annually, but the regular levy rate could not exceed $1.50 per $1,000, under state law.
The District’s call volume has nearly doubled since 2013, and the District has not requested a voter-approved increase to its general fire levy in over 45 years. If the measure passes, for property assessed at $500,000, the increase from the current $0.65 rate to $1.50 would be $423 per year, or $35.25 per month. The District commits to reducing its maintenance and operations levy by $0.20 per $1,000, reducing the net increase on a $500,000 assessed property to $323 per year, or $26.92 per month. The 2032 levy amount would be used to calculate future limits.
