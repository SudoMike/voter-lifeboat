## BallotTitle
Proposition 1
City of Liberty Lake, Washington
Impose a Sales and Use Tax of One-Tenth of One Percent for the Purpose of Funding Criminal Justice Services
The Liberty Lake City Council is calling for an election to consider the imposition of a sales and use tax of one-tenth of one percent for the purpose of funding public safety and criminal justice services pursuant to RCW 82.14.450.
Should the City impose this sale and use tax?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The City Council is calling for an election to impose a sales and use tax of one-tenth of one percent to continue to provide the current level of service for public safety and criminal justice services in the City.
The City has incurred substantial increases in costs associated with providing public safety and criminal justices services to its citizens. The imposition of the sales and use tax sought by this Proposition 1 will allow the City to continue providing public safety and criminal justice services consistent with the current level of service as the city continues growing faster than most cities in Washington. If approved, this tax will generate 10 cents on a $100 purchase and is projected to yield an estimated $750,000 in annual revenue.

## ArgumentFor
Liberty Lake is growing, and public safety must grow with it. More residents, businesses, traffic, and activity create greater demands on the police and public safety services we rely on. Maintaining the high level of service our community expects requires adequate staffing, equipment, technology, and resources to respond quickly and effectively when they are needed most.
Public safety operating costs have increased significantly over the past four years. While growth in existing sales tax revenue has helped meet these needs, relying on the City's general fund alone is not a sustainable long-term solution. Without a dedicated funding source, rising public safety costs will compete with other essential City services for limited resources. Planning today avoids more difficult budget choices tomorrow.
This proposition provides a dedicated, reliable source of funding for public safety and criminal justice services. The proposed one-tenth of one percent sales tax equals just 10 cents on a $100 taxable purchase. Everyone who makes taxable purchases in Liberty Lake contributes—including visitors and shoppers—not just our homeowners. Dedicated funding helps ensure officers have the personnel, equipment, and technology needed to maintain the level of service our growing community deserves.
Public safety consistently ranks among Liberty Lake's highest priorities. This measure reflects responsible financial stewardship by providing a sustainable funding source that strengthens emergency response capabilities while protecting other essential City services. Vote YES to preserve high level of public safety that defines Liberty Lake and keep our community safe, strong, and prepared for the future.

## ArgumentAgainst
After repeated recruitment attempts, no volunteers came forward to write a statement against the ballot measure.
