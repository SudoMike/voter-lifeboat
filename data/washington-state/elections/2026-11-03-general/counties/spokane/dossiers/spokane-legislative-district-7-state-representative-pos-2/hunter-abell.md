## Background

- Hunter Abell (Prefers Republican Party) has been a state representative since 2025 [S1].
- He is an attorney and rancher (part owner of Long Rifle Ranch in Inchelium), a U.S. Navy commander with more than 20 years of service, a former appointed Ferry County District Court judge, and 2023-2024 president of the Washington State Bar Association [S1].
- Education: Inchelium High School, the College of William & Mary, Gonzaga University School of Law [S1].

## Positions

- Describes himself as a Christian, father, conservative and "constitutionally-conservative attorney" [S1].
- Running to protect constitutional rights, support the rural way of life, and make Washington more prosperous for raising a family and running a business [S1].
