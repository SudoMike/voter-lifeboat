## Background

Michael Addams is an attorney and owner of Addams & Associates (since 2022) and the municipal prosecutor for the City of Cheney and Eastern Washington University [S3][S5]. He has been a Spokane County Superior Court commissioner pro tempore since September 2021 and a Spokane County District Court judge pro tempore since March 2025 [S5]. From 2019 to 2022 he directed the Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys' Support Enforcement Program, which coordinated child-support prosecution statewide. Before that he was a government liaison for DSHS's Division of Child Support (2018-2019) and managing partner of Addams & Leavitt, PLLC (2014-2018), handling family, juvenile, criminal, immigration and administrative matters [S5]. He teaches business law and ethics at Eastern Washington University [S3][S5]. He served 24 years in the U.S. Air Force and Washington Air National Guard, enlisted and then as an officer, retiring in 2026 [S1][S2][S5]. He holds a J.D. and an MBA from Gonzaga (2014) and a B.A. in criminal justice from Weber State University (2011) [S1][S3][S5]. He is a former president and current secretary of CASA Partners and has chaired the WSBA Pro Bono Public Service Committee and the WSBA Juvenile Law Section [S3].

## Positions

- **Accountability and public safety.** Priorities: "Hold individuals accountable in a fair and consistent way, reduce repeat offenses, and reinforce the expectation that the court's orders and processes are to be taken seriously," and "decisions that help reduce repeat offenses" [S2].
- **Therapeutic courts.** Says he supports "solutions that address root causes like addiction and mental health while maintaining accountability and protecting the community" [S2].
- **Efficiency.** Wants to "reduce delays and improve how cases move through the system" [S2].
- **Judicial approach.** Says every person deserves "a judge who listens, applies the law equally, and leads with integrity" [S1], and he commits to "applying the law with fairness, consistency, and respect" [S2].
- No statement on bail was found.

## Record

- As District Court judge pro tem he presides over arraignments, pretrial conferences, show-cause and review hearings, and small-claims trials, and he reviews proposed plea agreements and sentencing recommendations (self-reported) [S5].
- As Superior Court commissioner pro tem he covers family, juvenile and ex parte matters, including domestic-violence and guardianship matters (self-reported) [S5].
- His site says his legal background includes "extensive work in domestic violence cases" [S3].
- No published rulings were found.

## Ratings

- Spokane County Bar Association Judicial Candidates Evaluation Panel (2026): "Qualified" [S6].
- SCBA bar poll (2026, about 17% of members responding, 1-4 scale): legal ability 2.86, judicial temperament 3.10, integrity 3.14, relevant legal experience 2.82, suitability 2.91 [S6].

## Endorsements

His site lists Rep. Suzanne Schmidt (LD4); Mike Padden, listed as a retired LD4 state representative and retired Spokane County District Court presiding judge; Supreme Court candidates Dave Larsen and Laura Christensen Colbert; Superior Court Judge Dean Chuang and three Superior Court commissioners; District Court judges Aimee Maurer, Patrick Johnson and Jeff Smith; and Airway Heights Court Commissioner Deanna Crull [S4].
