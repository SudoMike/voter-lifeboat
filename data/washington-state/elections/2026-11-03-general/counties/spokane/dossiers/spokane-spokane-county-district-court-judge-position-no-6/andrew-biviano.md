## Background

- Spokane County District Court judge since January 2023; District Court pro tem judge in 2022 [S1].
- Attorney in private practice from 2012 to 2023 and Assistant United States Attorney from 2006 to 2012 [S1].
- Mental health case manager at Frontier Behavioral Health (2001-2003) and in Seattle (1997-2001) [S1].
- Education: J.D. summa cum laude, Gonzaga University School of Law (2006); B.A. in clinical psychology, Yale University (1997) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
