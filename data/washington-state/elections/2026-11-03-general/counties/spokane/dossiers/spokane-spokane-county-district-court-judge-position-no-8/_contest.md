## What the office does

Spokane County District Court has criminal jurisdiction over misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors committed in the county (sentences of up to one year in jail and a $5,000 fine unless a statute says otherwise), hears traffic infractions, conducts preliminary hearings, and sets bail and arraigns defendants [S2]. Its judges are elected countywide, so the whole county votes on every position [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Judge Richard M. Leland is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. The contest is uncontested. Leland was re-elected unopposed in 2022 with 97.95% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S3].
