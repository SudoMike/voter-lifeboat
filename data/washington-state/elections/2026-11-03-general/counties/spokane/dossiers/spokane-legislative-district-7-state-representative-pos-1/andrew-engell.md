## Background

- Andrew Engell (Prefers Republican Party) is the 7th District state representative, finishing his first term [S1].
- He was deputy district director for Congresswoman Cathy McMorris Rodgers, a self-employed farmer, and a volunteer firefighter with the Addy Fire Department for 14 years [S1].
- He was home-schooled in the Colville area and completed the Washington AgForestry leadership program [S1].
- He lists leadership roles in 17 community organizations, including the Colville Food Bank, CASA, Colville Rotary and Stevens County Republicans [S1].

## Positions

- Says four of his bills passed in his first term and that he opposed policies that make life harder for rural communities [S1].
- Pledges "practical policies, local voices, and the values" of the district [S1].
