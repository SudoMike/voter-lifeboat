## Background

Carmela Conroy is a former U.S. Foreign Service Officer (24 years, with postings including Afghanistan, Japan, Norway and Pakistan) and a former Spokane County Deputy Prosecuting Attorney [S1][S2]. She holds a J.D. and B.A. from the University of Washington and an M.A. in national security from the U.S. Naval War College [S1]. She lists service as Spokane County Democrats Chair and a Precinct Committee Officer [S1]. She has never held public office [S1]. She lost to Baumgartner in the 2024 general election and finished second in the August 4, 2026 primary with 20.8% in unofficial election-night results [S4].

## Positions

- **Trade and affordability:** pledges to "end the tariffs punishing our farmers and consumers" [S1][S3]; in debate she said ending "the trade chaos" and the Iran war would lower prices [S6]. Her site lists investment in housing, child care, education and infrastructure [S2].
- **Healthcare and social programs:** pledges to fund rural hospitals, Social Security, Medicare and Medicaid [S1]. Her platform would restore Medicare and Medicaid funding "at previous levels," undo the 2025 H.R. 1 eligibility rules, extend Medicaid to all children to age 19, and let federal health programs negotiate drug prices [S3]. The Spokesman-Review reports she supports "a universal, single-payer healthcare system" [S4].
- **Labor:** supports an inflation-indexed federal minimum wage with paid time off and paid sick leave [S3]; the Spokesman-Review reports a $16 federal minimum wage [S4].
- **Immigration:** platform would defund construction and operation of immigration detention centers "as fast as humanely possible" and release detainees except those facing criminal charges [S3]. In debate she said border security did not require DHS assets "pulled away from the border to terrorize members of our communities" [S6].
- **Defense and foreign policy:** calls the Iran conflict a war and a cost to taxpayers [S5]; would "defund aggressive and otherwise illegal military operations" [S3]. She now supports U.S. recognition of a Palestinian state and says aid to Israel should be halted, a change from her 2024 campaign [S5].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "I'll keep politicians out of your doctor's office" [S1]. On gender-affirming care for minors she said decisions "ought to be up to patients and their doctors," with parents having a say [S6].
- **Parents and schools (recorded, not scored for a federal race):** would "restore funding for public education, including support for children with special educational needs," and ensure public funds are not used in violation of church-state separation [S3].
- **Technology:** said a Spokane data center could worsen drought conditions and that data-center decisions belong at the city, county and state levels; she criticized Baumgartner's bill as preempting local decisions [S6].
- **Accountability:** would prohibit members of Congress from trading individual stocks, and calls for action on people named in the Epstein files [S3][S1].
