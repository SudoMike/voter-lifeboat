## What the office does

The U.S. Representative for Washington's 5th District votes on federal law, taxes, spending and oversight for 12 Eastern Washington counties: Adams, Asotin, Columbia, Ferry, Franklin, Garfield, Lincoln, Pend Oreille, Spokane, Stevens, Walla Walla and Whitman [S1]. The term is two years [S2].

## Race dynamics

A 2024 rematch: Republican incumbent Michael Baumgartner, who won the open seat with just over 60% in 2024, faces Democrat Carmela Conroy, a former diplomat and deputy prosecutor [S3]. In the August 4 primary Baumgartner led with 48% and Conroy took 20.8%, ahead of independent Nate Powell (14.4%) [S3]. Their debates centred on the Iran war, Israel aid and tariffs, then data centers, tax cuts, gender-affirming care and immigration enforcement [S4][S5].

## Differentiating issue axes

- taxes: Baumgartner's H.R. 1 votes and "reduce taxes" record versus Conroy's focus on tariffs as a cost; she has no stated federal tax plan in the sources found.
- spending and healthcare: Baumgartner's call to cut social services and his H.R. 1 votes versus Conroy's pledge to restore Medicaid/Medicare funding, expand Medicaid to children and (per the Spokesman-Review) single-payer.
- immigration: enforcement record versus defunding detention centers.
- defense: Iran war "as long as it takes" and Israel aid versus ending the war and halting Israel aid.
- social: federal ban on gender-affirming care for minors versus patient-doctor-parent decisions.
- tech: both voice data-center concerns; Baumgartner favors federal rules with incentives, Conroy local control and resource limits.
- experience: first-term incumbent with long elected record versus first-time office seeker.
- reform: both support banning congressional stock trading.
