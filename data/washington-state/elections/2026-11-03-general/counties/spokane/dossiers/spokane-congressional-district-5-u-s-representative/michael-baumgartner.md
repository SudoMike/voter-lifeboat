## Background

Michael Baumgartner is the incumbent U.S. Representative for Washington's 5th Congressional District, in office since January 3, 2025 [S1][S2]. He was Spokane County Treasurer (2019-2025) and a Washington State Senator (2011-2018) [S1]. He worked for the State Department in Iraq during the 2007 "surge" and on a civilian counternarcotics team in Afghanistan in 2009 [S1]. He holds a BA in economics from Washington State University and an MPA from Harvard [S1][S2]. His campaign site says he serves on the House Foreign Affairs, Judiciary, and Education and Workforce committees [S2]. In the August 4, 2026 primary he led with 48% in unofficial election-night results, ahead of Democrat Carmela Conroy (20.8%) [S12]. He beat Conroy in the 2024 general election with just over 60% [S12].

## Positions

- **Taxes:** says that in his first term he kept his promise to "reduce taxes" and voted for "permanent tax relief for working families" [S1]. In debate he cited "no taxes on tips, no taxes on overtime" as affordability measures [S14]. He told the Spokesman-Review that "an income tax would be terrible for the state of Washington" and that Conroy "is wrong to support the state income tax and other tax increases" [S15].
- **Spending:** his official site says he supports policies to "curb reckless government spending," which he blames for prices and the national debt [S4]. In April 2026 he backed more military spending despite the debt and said the U.S. should cut costs on social services rather than defense; he said the Constitution "doesn't say that it's the job of Congress to provide massive social welfare programs" [S16].
- **Immigration:** "strongly support[s] securing our border and enforcing existing immigration laws" [S8]. In debate he called the administration's immigration crackdown "largely successful," while saying "I always haven't liked the way that ICE has carried out some of their operations" [S14].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** supports Congress banning gender-affirming care for minors: "I voted that way to make sure that no child goes through coerced sexual transition" [S14]. His official site says he supports "each state's right to determine its own abortion laws" [S6].
- **Parents and schools (recorded, not scored for a federal race):** "I support local control of education and empowering parents to have choice and options in their children's schools" [S9].
- **Healthcare:** supports a "patient-centered, affordable, and transparent" system and voted to create a $50 billion Rural Hospital Transformation Fund [S5][S1].
- **Defense and foreign policy:** said the Iran conflict should continue "as long as it takes" to keep Iran from a nuclear weapon, and supports U.S. aid to Israel as in the American interest [S13]. Backs the President's call for higher defense spending [S16].
- **Energy and environment:** his official site lists his statement applauding the withdrawal from the 2023 Columbia Basin agreement [S7]; he says he has worked to protect the Snake River dams [S1]. He said the U.S. should not follow "the European model" of investing in "environmental fads" at the expense of the military [S16].
- **Technology:** said concern about data centers is warranted and the industry "needs to step forward," while stressing a "race with China"; he introduced a bill requiring data centers to cover the cost of the power and water systems they need, with federal incentives to build [S14].
- **Congressional ethics:** sponsored legislation to bar members of Congress from trading individual stocks [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-08. He has said a state income tax "would be terrible for the state of Washington" [S15].
- **I-1 (parental rights in public schools):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08; his official site supports "empowering parents" in education [S9].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- Voted Yea on H.R. 1 (2025 tax and spending package) on passage, 2025-05-22, and Aye on concurring in the Senate amendment, 2025-07-03 [S10]. President Trump called him a pivotal vote for it [S15].
- Voted Yea on H.R. 22, the SAVE Act (proof of citizenship to register to vote), 2025-04-10, and on H.R. 29, the Laken Riley Act, 2025-01-07 [S11][S1].
- Says he helped pass the Farm Bill through the House with his amendment on Eastern Washington water resources [S1].
- Campaign site lists federal funding he secured, e.g. $4 million for the Sullivan & Trent interchange in Spokane Valley [S3].

## Endorsements

- Pamphlet: Mark Schoesler, Mary Dye, Alex McGregor, Mike Padden, Phil Altmeyer, Duane Vaagen, Barry Baker, and the sheriffs of Ferry, Stevens and Walla Walla counties [S1].
