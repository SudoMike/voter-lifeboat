## Background

- Mike Volz (Prefers Republican Party) was appointed and later elected Spokane County Treasurer in 2025, after serving as chief deputy treasurer [S1].
- He has been the 6th District State Representative since 2017 [S1].
- He is a U.S. Army veteran and licensed CPA with an EWU business degree in finance and accounting and a Gonzaga MBA [S1].

## Positions

- Says he is committed to protecting taxpayer dollars with integrity, accountability and transparency, and to government living within its means [S1].
- In a November 2025 op-ed with Assessor Tom Konis he promoted property-tax relief programs for seniors and disabled residents, including the state senior and disabled-persons exemption [S2].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S3].
- **I-1 (parental rights in schools):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- As a state representative he voted Nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on household income over $1 million, on March 9, 2026 (passed 51-46) [S3].
