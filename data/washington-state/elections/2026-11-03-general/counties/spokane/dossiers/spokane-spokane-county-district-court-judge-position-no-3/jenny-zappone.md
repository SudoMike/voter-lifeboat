## Background

- Elected Spokane County District Court judge, Position 3, in 2022 [S1].
- Previously a senior deputy prosecuting attorney for Spokane County and supervisor of the prosecutor's Victim Advocate Unit [S1].
- District Court representative on a 2024 crime-victim services work group and a 2023 remote-proceedings court-rules work group [S1].
- Education: J.D. with honors, Gonzaga University School of Law; B.A. in history, Whitman College [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says she started a language-access committee across the Municipal, District and Superior courts [S1].
- Says that while she presided over the domestic violence docket the court won a grant so defendants can get evidence-based domestic-violence treatment, aimed at victim safety and lower recidivism [S1].
