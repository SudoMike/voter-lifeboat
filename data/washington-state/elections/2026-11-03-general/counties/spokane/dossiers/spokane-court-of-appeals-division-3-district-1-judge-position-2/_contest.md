## What the office does

The Court of Appeals hears appeals from the superior courts; its judges review the trial record and briefs and decide whether legal errors were made. Division III sits in Spokane, and its District 1 (Ferry, Lincoln, Okanogan, Pend Oreille, Spokane and Stevens counties) elects two judges [S2]. Judges are elected to six-year terms [S3].

## Race dynamics

Judge Tracy Arlene Staab is the only candidate on the ballot; she submitted no statement to the voters' guide [S1]. She won this position in 2020 with 62.84% of the district vote over attorney Marshall Casey [S4]. The contest is uncontested.
