## Background

- Tracy Arlene Staab submitted no statement, experience or education to the 2026 voters' guide [S1].
- She won this Court of Appeals position in November 2020, taking 62.84% of the district vote to Marshall Casey's 36.44%, in the race to replace retiring Judge Kevin Korsmo [S2][S3].
- Before that she was a Spokane Municipal Court judge, appointed by Mayor Mary Verner in 2009 and elected in 2009, 2013 and 2017 [S2].
- Earlier career: four years as a law clerk at the Court of Appeals; appellate attorney for the city public defender's office from 2003; appellate attorney for the Federal Defenders of Eastern Washington from 2005 until her 2009 appointment, including as attorney of record in 70 Ninth Circuit cases [S2].
- Education: bachelor's degree in paralegal studies, Western Washington University (1990); law degree, University of Puget Sound (1993) [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- In 2020 she cited her criminal-law background as important on a court where many cases are criminal, and described the written briefs as the most important part of an appeal [S2].
