## Background

Karina Wallace, of Cheney, is a Democratic first-time candidate [S1][S2]. She is a Washington State Certified Peer Support Specialist and betrayal-trauma recovery coach, has done direct-care and caregiving work, and describes earlier advocacy work in domestic violence shelters and the DSHS Job Center [S1][S2]. She holds an associate degree from Lower Columbia College and was an alternate delegate to the 2026 state Democratic convention [S1]. She describes herself as a mother of six and grandmother living with lupus and Sjögren's syndrome [S1][S2]. She took 38.1% in the August 4 primary [S3].

## Positions

- **Workers and families:** priorities are "paycheck protection, rural healthcare access, and closing the revolving door between lobbyists and our legislature" [S1]; supports predictable schedules, fair pay and enforcement of worker protections [S2].
- **Disability access:** proposes workplace protections for chronic-illness "flare days" and an ombudsman for disabled and chronically ill residents [S2].
- **Lobbying and money in politics:** "No corporate PAC money. No lobbyist donations" [S1]; supports "sunlight on lobbyist influence, stronger disclosure rules, and closing gift loopholes" [S2].
- **Immigration:** supports "orderly legal immigration pathways, full due process protections, and humane treatment"; says "fear-based enforcement doesn't make communities safer" [S2].
- **Taxes:** says she will "respect taxpayer dollars" [S2]; no specific tax position found.
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy; parents and schools:** no statements found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645, I-1, I-638:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Endorsements

Her site lists the Washington State Labor Council (AFL-CIO), Spokane Regional Labor Council, the Environment and Climate Caucus of the Washington State Democratic Party, National Women's Political Caucus of Washington, Spokane County Democrats and 9th Legislative District Democrats [S2].
