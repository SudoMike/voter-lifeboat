## What the office does

A State Representative writes and votes on Washington law and the state budgets; the term is two years. The 9th District's ballot spans Adams, Asotin, Columbia, Garfield, Lincoln, Spokane and Whitman counties [S1]. No other county package in this wave lists it, so this package researched it in full.

## Race dynamics

Republican Rep. Joe Schmick, in office since 2007, faces Democrat Karina Wallace, a peer support specialist and first-time candidate; the primary result was 61.6% to 38.1% [S1][S2].

## Differentiating issue axes

- taxes and spending: Schmick's Nay record on the 2025-2026 tax increases and budget versus no stated tax position from Wallace.
- reform: Wallace refuses corporate PAC and lobbyist money and wants lobbying disclosure.
- parental-rights: Schmick's parental-involvement plank and Nay on ESHB 1296.
- immigration: Wallace's due-process plank (state-level).
- experience: 19-year incumbent versus first-time candidate.
