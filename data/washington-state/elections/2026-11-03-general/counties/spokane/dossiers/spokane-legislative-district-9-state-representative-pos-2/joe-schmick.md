## Background

Joe Schmick is the incumbent Republican State Representative for the 9th Legislative District, Position 2, appointed in November 2007 and serving since [S1]. He is a former second-generation farmer and small-business owner with a BA in accounting from Eastern Washington University, and has served on the Washington State Farm Bureau board and the Washington Barley Commission [S1]. In the August 4 primary he took 61.6% to Karina Wallace's 38.1% in election-night results [S3].

## Positions

- **Taxes and spending:** "government spending has outpaced taxpayer's income" and the state should "live within its means and give back to taxpayers in times of excess" [S1]. His site says "We don't have a revenue problem, we have a spending problem" and calls for curbing state spending; it says his caucus sought property-tax, B&O and sales-tax relief [S2].
- **Regulation and agriculture:** cites "the impacts of burdensome regulations on employers" and the importance of agriculture and water access [S1][S2].
- **Public safety:** his site says Democratic police reforms "have made our communities, families, and students less safe" and that police need "the tools, support, and clarity of law" [S2].
- **Healthcare:** advocates "accessible and affordable healthcare" [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** his site's three education principles: more control for local school boards, keeping "the burgeoning state education bureaucracy" in check, and that "parents have a right to be involved in their children's education and know what is being taught in the classroom" [S2]. Voted against ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S8].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S5].
- **I-1 (parental rights):** no statement on the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments to the I-2081 parental-rights list that I-1 would repeal [S8]; his site supports parents knowing "what is being taught in the classroom" [S2].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- Voted Nay on ESSB 6346 (tax on household income over $1 million), House final passage 2026-03-09 (51-46) [S5].
- Voted Nay on SB 5813 (2025 capital gains and estate tax increases), 2025-04-23, and on HB 2081 (2025 B&O tax increases), 2025-04-22 [S6].
- Voted Nay on the 2025-27 operating budget (SB 5167), House passage 2025-03-31 and conference report 2025-04-27 [S7].
- Voted Nay on ESHB 1296 (2025-03-12 and on concurrence 2025-04-24) [S8].

## Endorsements
