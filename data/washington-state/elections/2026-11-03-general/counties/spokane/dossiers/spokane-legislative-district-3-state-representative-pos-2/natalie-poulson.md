## Background
Natalie Poulson, a Republican, is running for the open Position 2 seat held since 2003 by Democrat Timm Ormsby, who announced in April 2026 that he would not seek re-election [S12]. She taught special education for 18 years in Spokane Public Schools until November 2021, when she was escorted from Finch Elementary for refusing to comply with the state mask mandate; she was academic director and teacher at Faith Christian Academy (2022-2025) and since 2025 has taught at the private Christian school Equip 210 Spokane [S6][S7]. She holds an associate degree from Spokane Community College, a BA in elementary and special education from Gonzaga and an MA and professional certification from Whitworth [S1][S7]. She lists service as a district leader and precinct committee officer, Head Start advocacy, and community service with Serve Spokane, Habitat for Humanity, Maddie's Place and World Relief [S1]. Her website also describes her as owner of the home bakery business All Things Sourdough [S2]. She ran against Ormsby in 2022 and lost with about 40% of the vote [S8]. In the August 4, 2026 primary she finished first with 13,790 votes (32.93%), ahead of Democrats Luc Jasmin III (13,581, 32.43%), Pam Kohlmeier (30.23%) and Donovan DeLeon (4.42%) [S7][S9]. A Spokesman-Review column noted that her one-third share is in line with the district's base Republican vote and that she outpolled the combined Democratic vote in only four of 112 precincts [S10].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** website says "Lower them, remove them, and stand against any form of state income tax" [S2]. Told RANGE "no new broad-based taxes until we prove fiscal discipline," while leaving room to "responsibly explore targeted new revenue streams" [S4]. Wants to repeal or lower the state business and occupation (B&O) tax [S6]. Says new taxes and fees should have a clear nexus to a specific cost [S3].
- **Spending and budget:** website plank "Responsible & Accountable Government" calls for living within our means and cutting "wasteful bureaucracy" [S2]. Supports an independent audit of state spending and of Apple Health and housing programs, and says programs that cannot show results should not be renewed [S3][S4].
- **Housing:** calls for "supply-side solutions, not more spending or mandates": cutting permitting delays and fees, reviewing land-use rules that restrict density, and limiting new taxes [S4]. Supports market-rate housing, office-to-residential conversion, and limits on state mandates that add construction cost [S3]. Cited natural-gas restrictions in new construction as a mandate that raises costs [S5]. Supports evaluating a revenue-neutral Spokane property-tax pilot with taxpayer guardrails [S4].
- **Public safety, homelessness and addiction:** "Enforcement first": laws restoring consequences for property crime, violence and public drug use, ending "catch-and-release," and custody or mandatory programs for repeat offenders [S3]. Supports involuntary treatment where someone cannot care for themselves or is a danger to others and a treatment-first rather than housing-first approach [S3][S5][S8]. Calls the post-Blake period "one of the most costly and counterproductive policy shifts" and supports enforcing laws against open drug use [S4]. Website says billions are "wasted on unaccountable NGOs" in homeless services [S2].
- **Climate and energy:** opposes natural-gas restrictions in new builds on cost grounds [S5]. Would roll back special tax exemptions for data centers, saying they should pay their full share for energy and water [S4].
- **Health care:** for Apple Health, supports audits, prioritizing children, pregnant people, seniors and disabled residents, and work requirements for able-bodied adults paired with treatment access [S4].
- **Immigration and federal relations:** opposes using state funds to sue or obstruct lawful federal actions on Medicaid, immigration or public health; supports cooperating on serious criminals and enforcing laws "without creating sanctuary incentives" [S4].
- **Technology and AI:** favors a "targeted, cautious approach," opposing broad AI regulation as harmful to innovation and backing only narrow measures with cost-benefit review and sunsets [S4].
- **Government transparency:** opposes using legislative privilege to exempt legislators' records from public disclosure [S4].
- **Local control:** says housing decisions should be made locally, with no one-size-fits-all approach [S8].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** describes herself as "a pro-life candidate" who respects Washington voters' support for current abortion law and would focus on adoption resources and support for women with unplanned pregnancies [S4]. Supports reviewing current gender-affirming care law and, for minors, requiring mental-health evaluation and parental consent and restricting "irreversible procedures until adulthood," while respecting adult choice [S4].
- **Parents and schools:** website plank "Strong Families First and Parental Rights": "Protect parental rights, support quality education, and ensure families — not government — make the most important decisions for their children" [S2]. Endorser Rep. Suzanne Schmidt says Poulson "will fight for parental rights" [S2].

- Enforcement-first safety platform with involuntary treatment, backed by law-enforcement groups [S2][S3].
