## Background

- Spokane County District Court judge for about 12 years, including terms as assistant presiding and presiding judge [S1].
- Former dean (now dean emeritus) and faculty member of the Washington State Judicial College, where she helped train judges statewide [S1].
- Has served on the Washington State Bar Association Civil Rules Drafting Task Force and the Washington pattern jury instructions committee [S1].
- Education: J.D., Gonzaga University School of Law (2006); B.A., Gonzaga University (2003); A.A., Spokane Falls Community College (2000) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
