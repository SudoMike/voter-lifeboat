## Background

- Jeff Holy (Prefers Republican Party) is a two-term state senator and was previously a three-term state representative [S1].
- He is a U.S. Army veteran and retired Spokane police officer and detective (22 years), a former Police Guild board member, and now an attorney [S1].
- He is the ranking Republican on the Senate Law and Justice Committee [S3].
- Education: J.D., Gonzaga Law School; B.S. in psychology, Washington State University; Washington State Police Academy [S1].

## Positions

- Says he has fought "costly policies that have hurt every family budget" and that more business regulation will hurt economic recovery [S1].
- Stresses public safety and being "tough on crime" based on his police career [S1].
- Names West Plains wildfire recovery and economic and workforce development as commitments [S1].
- Worked with Democratic Sen. Yasmin Trudeau on SB 6002, the Driver Privacy Act, which would limit automated license-plate reader use to purposes such as stolen vehicles, missing people and felony warrants and restrict sharing of the data [S3].
