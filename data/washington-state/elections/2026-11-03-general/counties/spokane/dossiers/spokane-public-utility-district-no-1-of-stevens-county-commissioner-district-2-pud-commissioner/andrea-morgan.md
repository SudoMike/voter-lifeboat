## Background

Andrea Morgan is a registered nurse of nearly 40 years whose career has focused on "holistic nursing, preventative care, and patient advocacy" [S1]. She earned an associate degree in nursing from Everett Community College (1988) and a bachelor's in nursing from Seattle Pacific University (1990) [S1]. She has owned property in Stevens County for 33 years [S1]. She lists no prior elected experience [S1]. Her PDC registration lists a Chewelah, Stevens County address [S2].

## Positions

- **Preventive maintenance and rates.** Says "preventative maintenance of our water and wastewater systems is far more cost-effective than reacting to emergencies" and that she will "prioritize long-term stability to keep rates predictable" [S1].
- **Infrastructure, resources and property rights.** Says the PUD must "balance modern infrastructure needs with the preservation of our natural resources and private property rights" [S1].
- **Transparency.** Says the ratepayer is her "patient" and that she will advocate "for transparency and honest communication" so the PUD stays accountable [S1].
- **Rural character.** Describes herself as "dedicated to preserving our local quality of life and rural character" [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (taxes), I-1 (parental rights), I-638 (girls' sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

No public office held. She is challenging the incumbent on a three-member board that governs a water and wastewater utility serving about 5,000 customers [S1][S3].
