## Background

Howie Kubik is the incumbent Stevens PUD commissioner for District 2. He says he has served as a PUD commissioner for 17 years, overseeing the PUD's water and sewer systems [S1]. State election results show him winning the District 2 seat unopposed in 2014 and 2020 [S2][S3]. He retired after 37 years with Avista, where he was a lineman, foreman and "local representative" [S1]. He has a high school diploma and an auto mechanic certificate from Spokane Community College, and is a journeyman lineman [S1]. He coaches youth baseball and trap shooting [S1]. His PDC registration lists an Addy, Stevens County address [S4].

## Positions

- **Utility service.** Says he has "worked to ensure residents receive reliable, safe, and cost-effective utility services" [S1].
- **Employees and customers.** Calls his relationships with PUD employees and customers "the most meaningful part of my work" [S1].
- No statements on rates, specific capital projects or Spokane County systems were found.

## Record
