## Background

Callie Gee, 24, is a Democrat running for office for the first time [S6][S7]. She graduated from Washington State University in 2024 with a business administration degree in accounting and finance [S1][S8]. She has worked in tax preparation at public accounting firms including Moss Adams and Baker Tilly, where she is working toward CPA certification [S6][S8]. She has also done financial work for her family's coffee roasting business [S1][S8]. She co-chairs the Spokane County Young Democrats, is a precinct committee officer and was a delegate to the state Democratic convention [S1][S5]. She says she has held a supervisory role at her accounting firm for two years [S7].

In the August 4 primary she led the three-candidate field with 45.6% in the election-night count. Michael Cathcart (28.5%) edged fellow Republican Dale Whitaker (25.7%) for the second general-election spot [S5].

## Positions

### Elections
- Her statement promises "independent oversight, protecting vote-by-mail, and ensuring every voter can participate with confidence" [S1]. Her website pledges to "protect every eligible citizen's right to vote-by-mail" [S2].
- Wants to expand election-observer training, including sessions outside the workweek [S6][S8].
- Said a priority would be countering election misinformation and raising turnout [S7]. She cited worry about "pressure that's coming from our federal government on our local elections" [S6], and on primary night said the office must "protect our election security, especially with pressures coming in from the Trump administration" [S5].
- Said ballot boxes need to withstand wildfires and arson [S6].

### County finances and transparency
- Would build a public portal tracking how the county spends tax dollars. She said it could help with the budget shortfall by showing "where the bottlenecks are" and where cuts could be made [S6].
- Her statement promises "transparency, dedication to fiscal responsibility and efficiency" [S1]. Her website says "public money should serve the public, every resident, not just the well-connected" [S2].
- Wants to explain the county budget and annual financial report more clearly to residents frustrated by property taxes [S8].

## Scoring notes

- Experience: a 24-year-old first-time candidate who argues the job "isn't about who's been in politics the longest." Cathcart is a two-term city councilmember.
- Spending: proposes a spending-transparency portal to find cuts and pledges fiscal responsibility, a milder version of Cathcart's "budget hawk" framing.
- Elections: both pledge to protect vote-by-mail. Gee stresses federal pressure and observer access. Cathcart stresses expanded audits and livestreaming.
