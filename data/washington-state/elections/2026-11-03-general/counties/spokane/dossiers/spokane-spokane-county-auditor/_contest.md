## What the office does
The county auditor runs elections and voter registration, records property deeds and other documents, handles vehicle and marriage licensing, and keeps the county's financial records and payroll, including the annual financial report (S2, S3). The auditor carries out election law as written and does not set it (S2).

## Race dynamics
Democrat Vicky Dalton, auditor since 1998 and the only Democrat elected countywide, is retiring (S2, S3). Democrat Callie Gee, a 24-year-old accountant and first-time candidate, led the August primary with 45.6% in the election-night count. Republican Spokane City Councilmember Michael Cathcart (28.5%) edged Republican Dale Whitaker (25.7%) for second (S3). Cathcart has endorsements from both parties, including Dalton's, and the county GOP chair criticized him for accepting them; the Republican Party has since endorsed him (S2; michael-cathcart.md S4, S10). Both candidates pledge to protect vote-by-mail and publish county spending online. Cathcart adds expanded audits and livestreamed canvassing; Gee stresses federal pressure on local elections and wider observer training (callie-gee.md S6, S7; michael-cathcart.md S2, S8, S9). Cathcart reports $52.5k raised to Gee's $37.3k (michael-cathcart.md S5; callie-gee.md S4).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a two-term city councilmember and former finance chair against a first-time candidate who stresses youth and accounting training.
2. **Spending:** Cathcart calls himself a "budget hawk" and financial "watchdog." Gee pledges fiscal responsibility and a spending portal to find cuts.
3. **Reform/open government:** Cathcart cites a redistricting charter amendment and proposes livestreamed canvassing and expanded audits. Gee proposes wider observer access.
4. **Taxes:** only indirect evidence, from an endorser's description of Cathcart's council record. Not a strong differentiator for this administrative office.
