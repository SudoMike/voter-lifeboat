## Background

Dave Lucas, a Republican, is the Cheney Municipal Court administrator and a retired U.S. Marine Corps lieutenant colonel [S1][S5]. His campaign says he served in Iraq, Afghanistan and Africa [S2]. He holds an economics degree from UC Davis and a master's in military studies from Marine Corps University, and completed the Washington State Court Administrators Academy [S1]. He has chaired a Spokane neighborhood council, served as a precinct committee officer, and chaired Spokane Veterans Stand Down; he is also active in Scouting [S1][S2]. He is running to succeed Republican Clerk Tim Fitzgerald, who is retiring after four terms [S5].

## Positions

- His statement opens "Experience Matters." It promises clear and respectful communication, better speed and accuracy, and "smart modernization improving service and protecting sensitive information" [S1].
- His plan says he will "continue to implement modern tools and systems." It says financial operations "from collections to fines and restitution" should be "accurate, transparent, and cost-effective" [S3].
- At the October 4 debate he said moving from municipal court administrator to county clerk is a natural step. He cited e-filing reforms he made in Cheney and a court food bank and Narcan center he opened [S5].
- He criticized unfunded state mandates on courts: "The state expects all of you to pay for decisions that they make" [S5].
- Said partisanship should play no role in the job: "This position is people above politics" [S5].
