## Background

Elliot Robison, a Democrat, is a business intelligence analyst at CHAS Health. His work covers capacity analytics, workflow optimization and database reporting, and he has also worked in software engineering and hospitality [S1][S4]. He holds a BS in mathematics from the University of British Columbia [S1]. He chairs the Northeast Chapter of the Washington Native Plant Society and is director of strategy for the Spokane County Young Democrats [S1]. He volunteers in habitat restoration for Spokane City Parks and as an audio engineer for Spokane Public Radio [S1]. This is his first run for office; he has not held an elected government post [S1].

## Positions

- His statement says "We need a new generation of leadership who can expertly wield technology" and that "more of the same won't solve our challenges" [S1].
- Proposes expanded court guides, notifications and translated forms, and "public operational reporting" on the clerk's office [S1][S2].
- Pledges "a constructive, union-friendly workplace" and fair pay for staff [S1][S2].
- At the October 4 debate he said: "I can take an archaic, clunky document database and bridge that to the public." He also said "What we don't have is people who have these new skills to innovate. I'm hoping to offer something a little newer, a little bit fresher, a little bit high-energy" [S4].
- Said partisanship should play no role in the job [S4].
