## What the office does
The county clerk is clerk of the Superior Court. The clerk keeps court records, receives filings, fees, fines and restitution, and runs the court's administrative operations so judges, prosecutors and defense attorneys can work their cases (S1, S2). The job is largely administrative (S2).

## Race dynamics
Republican Clerk Tim Fitzgerald is retiring after four terms (S2). Republican Dave Lucas, the Cheney Municipal Court administrator and a retired Marine lieutenant colonel, runs on court-administration experience, citing e-filing reforms in Cheney (dave-lucas.md S1, S5). Democrat Elliot Robison, a CHAS Health business intelligence analyst, runs as a "new generation" technologist. He proposes translated forms, notifications and public operational reporting, and has labor endorsements (elliot-robison.md S1, S2). Both said partisanship has no place in the office (S2). Robison reports $28.8k raised to Lucas's $20.8k (elliot-robison.md S3; dave-lucas.md S4).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** direct court-administration experience against outside data and technology skills. This is the only axis with clear evidence for both candidates.
2. Neither candidate takes a position on taxes, spending levels or other policy axes. Lucas's complaint about unfunded state mandates and Robison's union-friendly workplace pledge are recorded but map to no scored county axis.
