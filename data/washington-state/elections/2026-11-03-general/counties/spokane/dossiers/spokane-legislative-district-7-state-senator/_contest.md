## What the office does

The State Senator writes and votes on Washington law and the state budgets, and confirms gubernatorial appointments; the term is four years. The 7th District's ballot spans Chelan, Douglas, Ferry, Okanogan, Pend Oreille, Spokane and Stevens counties [S1]. This package researched the race in full because no other county package in this wave lists it.

## Race dynamics

Republican incumbent Shelly Short, Senate Republican Floor Leader and in the Legislature since 2009, faces independent Ronald McCoy, a former Chewelah mayor [S1]. Short took 57.9% and McCoy 27.7% in the primary [S2].

## Differentiating issue axes

- experience: an 18-year legislator and caucus leader versus a former small-city mayor.
- taxes and spending: Short's Nay votes on the 2025-2026 tax increases and budget; McCoy also talks about small-business tax burdens but without a record.
- reform: McCoy's independent, anti-lobbyist framing.
- parental-rights: only Short has evidence (Nay on ESHB 1296).
