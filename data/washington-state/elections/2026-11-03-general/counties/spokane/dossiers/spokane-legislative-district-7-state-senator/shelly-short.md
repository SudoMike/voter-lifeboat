## Background

Shelly Short is the incumbent Republican State Senator for the 7th Legislative District and the Senate Republican Floor Leader [S1]. She was elected to the state House in 2008, appointed to the Senate in 2017 after Sen. Brian Dansel resigned, won the 2017 special election, and was re-elected in 2018 and 2022 [S1][S3]. Before office she ran district offices for U.S. Reps. George Nethercutt and Cathy McMorris Rodgers, was a legislative assistant to Rep. Joel Kretz, and was a paralegal for 10 years [S1][S3]. The Spokesman-Review reports she has largely focused on agriculture and environmental issues [S3]. In the August 4 primary she took 57.9% to Ronald McCoy's 27.7% in election-night results [S4].

## Positions

- **Taxes and spending:** pledges to "stand against government's appetite for endless taxes and unchecked spending" [S1].
- **State regulation and local control:** says she leads in "fighting against unnecessary and burdensome agency rules and regulations that undermine individual rights and our economic prosperity" and will "battle state government when it stands in the way of our prosperity" [S1][S2].
- **Natural resources and rural economy:** champions farming and ranching, natural-resource and wildlife management, local healthcare and access to water [S1]; supports agriculture, mining and timber [S3].
- **Wildfire and insurance:** calls wildfire risk and homeowner insurance her "No. 1 priority" [S3].
- **Parents and schools:** lists "student centered education" as a priority [S1]; voted against ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S8].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement of her own found. Her campaign site lists a 2018 Human Life PAC endorsement [S2].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S5].
- **I-1 (parental rights):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments to the I-2081 parental-rights list that I-1 would repeal [S8].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- Voted Nay on ESSB 6346 (tax on household income over $1 million) on Senate passage 2026-02-16 (27-22) and on concurrence 2026-03-11 (27-21) [S5].
- Voted Nay on SB 5813 (2025 capital gains and estate tax rate increases), 2025-04-19 and 2025-04-24, and on HB 2081 (2025 business and occupation tax increases), 2025-04-26 [S6].
- Voted Nay on the 2025-27 operating budget (SB 5167) on Senate passage 2025-03-29 and the conference report 2025-04-27 [S7].
- Voted Nay on ESHB 1296 in the Senate, 2025-04-11 (30-19) [S8].
- Chaired the Senate Local Government Committee per the Spokesman-Review profile; her campaign site describes her as its ranking minority member [S3][S2].

## Endorsements

Her campaign site lists awards and endorsements from farm, hunting, property-rights and business groups (Washington Farm Bureau, Cattle PAC, Washington Hospitality Association, Northwest Credit Union Association), Human Life PAC (2018), and the Ferry and Okanogan County Republican parties [S2].
