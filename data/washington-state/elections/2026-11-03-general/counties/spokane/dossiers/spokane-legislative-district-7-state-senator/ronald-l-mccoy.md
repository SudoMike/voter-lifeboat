## Background

Ronald L. McCoy, of Chewelah, is running as an independent ("Prefers Independent Party") [S1]. He was Chewelah's mayor from 1998 to 2002 and served on the Chewelah City Council [S1][S3]. He is a retired U.S. Navy Boatswain's Mate First Class (20-plus years) and retired from Safeway [S1][S2]. Gov. Gary Locke appointed him to the State Building Code Council in 2001 [S1]. He has an associate degree in legal administration from Spokane Community College [S1]. In 2024 he ran as a Republican for the 7th District House seat against Rep. Hunter Abell and received 13.7% in the primary; he says he now runs as an independent because he is "more than just a Republican or Democrat" [S3]. He finished second in the August 4, 2026 primary with 27.7% [S4].

## Positions

- **Taxes:** lists "taxes that burden our small Business owners and everyday citizens" among his concerns [S1] and "protect taxpayers" among his goals [S2]; told the Spokesman-Review he would address tax burdens on small businesses [S3].
- **Healthcare:** rural healthcare access, especially for older and disabled residents, is his main focus [S2][S3].
- **Housing and jobs:** would "support affordable housing" and invest in job creation, rural infrastructure and youth programs [S1][S2][S3].
- **Government and politics:** says the district "was not built by Lobbyist and special interest groups" [S1]; said of his opponent that "sometimes it's about big money instead of small people" [S3]; "Good government is built from bottom up not top down" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy; parents and schools:** no statements found.
