## Background

- Mary Dye (Prefers Republican Party) was appointed to the Legislature in 2015 and has served five and a half terms [S1].
- She is ranking member of the House Environment and Energy Committee and serves on the Appropriations and Capital Budget committees [S1].
- She has managed a third-generation wheat farm since 1987 and was a Peace Corps volunteer in Thailand (1984-86) [S1].
- Education: B.S. in plant science and crop management, University of Idaho (1983) [S1].
- Recognition listed: Washington Farm Bureau "Legislator of the Year" 2024 [S1].

## Positions

- Her statement focuses on energy, water and environmental issues in the 9th District: water and hydropower, farming and fisheries, and collaborative stewardship of the watershed [S1].
