## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would let the Town of Spangle levy an excess property tax in 2027 "in an approximate rate of $1.40 per $1,000 of assessed value to raise $49,000 to be used to provide adequate fire protection services in 2027" [S1]. The council enacted Ordinance No. 476 for the measure [S1].

The explanatory statement says the town's volunteer fire department provides fire and medical response, and that "No other funds, grants, or loans are available to defray the cost of a Fire Protection Services Contract with Fire District #3 for the year 2027" [S1]. Approval "will allow the Town to maintain the level of fire protection provided to its residents" [S1].

The council's July 13, 2026 meeting agenda lists a public hearing on "Fire & Police Levies" [S7].

## Cost

- About $1.40 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2027, raising $49,000 [S1]. On a $300,000 home that is about $420 for the year (computed for this dossier from the stated rate).
- The levy is for one year (2027) only [S1].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Spangle puts a one-year fire levy on the ballot every year. This year's request matches the last two years [S1][S2][S3][S4]:

| Levy for | Rate per $1,000 | Amount | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $1.50 | $23,000 | |
| 2025 | $1.40 | $49,000 | Approved, 74.36% [S6] |
| 2026 | $1.40 | $49,000 | Approved, 73.08% [S5] |
| 2027 (this measure) | $1.40 | $49,000 | |

- The amount more than doubled from 2024 ($23,000) to 2025 ($49,000) [S3][S4]. The pamphlet records do not explain the change.
- The 2027 request repeats the 2025 and 2026 rate and amount [S1][S2][S3].

**Related measure.** Spokane County Fire District 3, which holds the town's fire contract [S1], is separately asking its voters for a new EMS levy. Its plan names Station 36 in Spangle as a preferred location for new 24-hour staffing [S8]. This research did not determine whether town residents are inside Fire District 3's taxing boundary.

## Arguments for

No statement for was filed: "After repeated recruitment attempts, no volunteers came forward to write a statement for the ballot measure" [S1]. The town's case is that no other money is available for the Fire District 3 contract [S1].

## Arguments against

No statement against was filed [S1].
