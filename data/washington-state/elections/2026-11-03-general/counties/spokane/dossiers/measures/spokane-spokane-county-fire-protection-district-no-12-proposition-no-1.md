## What it does

Proposition 1 asks whether Spokane County Fire Protection District No. 12 may "levy a regular property tax in the sum of $.50 per $1000 assessed valuation each year for six consecutive years beginning in 2027 to be collected in 2027 through 2032, inclusive, for the provision of Emergency Medical Services," under Resolution No. 26-4 and RCW 84.52.069 [S1]. The district provides emergency medical services to "the residents of Latah and Waverly and the surrounding unincorporated areas" [S1]. The explanatory statement says the measure "will authorize the district to retain its existing regular real property tax levy rate of $.50 per $1000 assessed valuation for the next six years" [S1].

Fire District 2's chief told the Spokesman-Review that his district works closely with Fire District 12: "We respond borderless with Fire District 12" [S5].

## Cost

- $0.50 per $1,000 of assessed value a year for six years, collected 2027 through 2032 [S1]. On a $300,000 home that is $150 a year (computed for this dossier from the stated rate).
- The pamphlet calls this the district's "existing" rate [S1]. This research did not check the district's actual 2026 collected rate against county levy records (the Spokane County website blocks automated access).

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

This renews the district's six-year EMS levy.

- In November 2020, voters approved the expiring levy at $0.50 per $1,000 "each year for six consecutive years beginning in 2021 to be collected in 2021 through 2026," with 65.57% yes (200 to 105) [S2][S3].
- RCW 84.52.069 caps EMS levies at 50 cents per $1,000 and requires only a simple majority for the "subsequent approval" of a six-year levy [S4]. The pamphlet does not state the threshold [S1].

## Arguments for

No statement for was filed: "After repeated recruitment attempts, no volunteers came forward to write a statement for the ballot measure" [S1]. The commissioners' stated reason is that the levy "is necessary to adequately provide emergency medical services to the many residents in our district" [S1].

## Arguments against

No statement against was filed [S1].
