## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would replace West Valley School District's educational programs and operations (EP&O) levy, which expires at the end of 2027, with a three-year levy collected in 2028-2030 [S1]. The board adopted Resolution No. 26-04 [S1]. The levy pays for expenses the state does not fund, "including smaller class size, advanced courses, nurses, counselors, technology, safety, music, athletics, extracurricular activities, facility maintenance/repair and operations" [S1].

The district lists the following as levy-funded [S2]:
- Teachers, certificated staff, principals and technology staff
- Counselors, nurses, and maintenance, custodial and grounds staff
- 100% of athletics and extracurricular activities
- Career and technical education and advanced coursework

The levy is about 15% of the operating budget [S4][S5]. Superintendent Kyle Rydell said voters have approved it for more than 50 years [S5]. The board voted unanimously to send it to the ballot [S4].

## Cost

Ballot title figures [S1]:

| Collection year | Estimated rate per $1,000 | Levy amount |
|---|---|---|
| 2028 | $2.50 | $10,479,522 |
| 2029 | $2.50 | $10,793,908 |
| 2030 | $2.50 | $10,955,816 |

- The total is about $32.2 million over three years [S4][S5].
- For a $375,000 median home, the district projects $1,308 a year for this levy and Proposition 2 combined [S2][S5]. It gives no figure for this levy alone.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

**The expiring levy.** In November 2023 the board sent a $30 million levy to the February 2024 ballot at an estimated $2.44 per $1,000 [S6]. West Valley's levies passed in February 2024 [S7]. The district says the November levies "replace levies that were last approved in 2024" [S3]. The levy collects $2.50 per $1,000 in 2026 [S4][S5].

**How the new levy compares.**
- The rate is the same as in 2026, $2.50, which is the state maximum [S5].
- The amount rises from $30 million to $32.2 million over three years, about 7%. That comparison is computed for this dossier [S4][S6].
- With Proposition 2, the district projects a total rate of $3.50 per $1,000 in 2028, compared with $3.49 in 2026 [S3][S4].
- The district: "These are not new taxes, but replacements of expiring levies" [S2].

## Arguments for

From the statement in favor, by the EP&O Replacement "For" Levy Committee - WVSD (Eric Rosentrater, Jodee Cahalan) [S1]:

- "This is not a new tax. It replaces the expiring levy" [S1].
- It supports "smaller class sizes, providing advanced courses, allowing for on-site nurses and counselors, providing and updating technology resources, and enhancing school safety measures," plus music, athletics and extracurriculars [S1].
- It does all this "without increasing the tax rate" [S1].

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted: "After repeated recruitment attempts, no volunteers came forward" [S1]. No organized opposition was found.
