## What it does

Proposition No. 2 would replace West Valley School District's capital levy, which expires at the end of 2027, with a two-year capital levy collected in 2028 and 2029 [S1]. The board adopted Resolution No. 26-05 [S1]. The money would pay for four kinds of work [S1]:

1. Entrance security, including cameras, access controls, door locks and communications systems, and other safety systems.
2. A library addition at Pasadena Park Elementary School.
3. Replacing HVAC at Spokane Valley High School.
4. Other health, safety, security and infrastructure work, including sprinkler installations and abatement projects.

The district says the Pasadena Park project would also bring the nurse's room up to standard, and that capital levies cannot be used for staff salaries [S2]. Superintendent Kyle Rydell said Pasadena Park is the district's fullest elementary school. The library addition would free up rooms now used as library space for classrooms [S5].

- For a $375,000 median home, the district projects $1,308 a year for this levy and Proposition 1 combined [S2][S5].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

**The expiring levy.** Voters approved the current capital levy in November 2024 [S4]. The district describes it as a two-year levy that paid for the Seth Woodard Elementary parking lot, a West Valley High roof and heating at Millwood Early Learning Center [S2]. No source found gives that levy's rate or amount on its own.

**How the new levy compares.** The district projects the combined rate of both November levies at $3.50 per $1,000 in 2028, compared with $3.49 in 2026 [S3][S4]. The EP&O levy is $2.50 in both years [S4], so the capital portion appears to stay at about $1.00. That inference is drawn for this dossier and is not stated by the district.

**Two years and a possible bond.** The board chose a two-year term instead of the usual three. Rydell said the district may consider a bond, possibly in 2028 [S4]. West Valley's $92 million bond failed in February 2024 [S4][S5].

## Arguments for

From the statement in favor, by the Capital Projects Levy "For" Committee - WVSD #363 (Eric Rosentrater, Jodee Cahalan) [S1]:

- "This levy focuses on safety and security": entrance cameras, access controls, door locks and communication systems [S1].
- It addresses "a major need at SVHS to replace the outdated HVAC system," along with fire sprinkler replacement and abatement [S1].
- "It's a responsible, time-limited measure" [S1].
