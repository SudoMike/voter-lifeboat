## What it does

Proposition No. 2 would let the Town of Spangle levy an excess property tax in 2027 "in an approximate rate of $1.19 per $1,000 of assessed value to raise $28,000 to be used to provide adequate police protection services in 2027" [S1]. The council enacted Ordinance No. 477 for the measure [S1].

The explanatory statement says the town's police service "provides crime prevention, assistance to victims of crime, maintains public order and overall safety of the community," and that "No other grants, or loans are available to defray the cost of the Police Protection Services Contract with the Spokane County Sheriff's department for the year 2027" [S1]. Approval "will allow the Town to maintain the level of police protection provided to its residents" [S1].

The council's July 13, 2026 meeting agenda lists a public hearing on "Fire & Police Levies" [S7].

## Cost

- About $1.19 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2027, raising $28,000 [S1]. On a $300,000 home that is about $357 for the year (computed for this dossier from the stated rate).
- The levy is for one year (2027) only [S1].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Spangle puts a one-year police levy on the ballot every year to pay its sheriff's contract [S1][S2][S3][S4]:

| Levy for | Rate per $1,000 | Amount | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $1.19 | $17,000 | |
| 2025 | $0.80 | $28,000 | Approved, 70.32% [S6] |
| 2026 | $1.19 | $28,000 | Approved, 66.23% [S5] |
| 2027 (this measure) | $1.19 | $28,000 | |

- The 2027 request repeats the 2026 rate and amount [S1][S2].
- The amount rose from $17,000 (2024) to $28,000 (2025) [S3][S4]. The 2025 entry lists a lower rate ($0.80) for the same $28,000 that the 2026 entry raises at $1.19 [S2][S3]; the pamphlet records do not explain the difference, and all of these rates are labeled approximate.

## Arguments for

No statement for was filed: "After repeated recruitment attempts, no volunteers came forward to write a statement for the ballot measure" [S1]. The town's case is that no other money is available for the sheriff's contract [S1].
