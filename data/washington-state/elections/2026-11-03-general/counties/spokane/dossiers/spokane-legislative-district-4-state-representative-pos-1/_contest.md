## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The November 3, 2026 general election decides the seat between the two candidates who advanced from the August primary.

## Race dynamics
The 4th District covers Spokane Valley, Liberty Lake and east Spokane County to the Idaho line, north to around Mount Spokane [S2]. The seat is open: Rep. Suzanne Schmidt (R) is running for Spokane County commissioner instead of re-election (hillary-q-pham.md S3, S6; trent-maier.md S7). Both finalists are Republicans running for office for the first time, so a Republican will hold the seat either way [S2]. In the primary Hillary Pham took 32.82% and Trent Maier 30.80%, ahead of Debra Long and George Wagner (hillary-q-pham.md S5).

Pham, 29, is an Air Force veteran who since 2024 has been legislative assistant to Rep. Rob Chase, the district's other representative, who endorses her (hillary-q-pham.md S2, S3, S4). Maier, a Liberty Lake cidery co-founder and electrical engineer, is a former Greater Spokane Valley Chamber chair endorsed by Schmidt, two county commissioners, the county prosecutor and sheriff, and business associations [S1] (trent-maier.md S3). Both run on affordability: Maier on lower taxes, less regulation and public safety; Pham on affordability, housing and public safety (trent-maier.md S2; hillary-q-pham.md S1, S4). Pham supports Chase's bill to create two autonomous regions in the state; Maier gave a mixed answer (hillary-q-pham.md S3). Maier reports more money: $47.2k raised to Pham's $29.4k in PDC data (trent-maier.md S8; hillary-q-pham.md S7). Neither has stated a position on I-645, I-1 or I-638.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** both are first-time candidates; Pham's background is legislative and county government staff work, Maier's is business and chamber advocacy.
2. **Taxes:** Maier makes lower taxes and opposing tax increases a repeated, explicit plank; Pham's platform centers affordability more broadly.
3. **Public safety:** Maier lists specific enforcement priorities (officer recruitment, accountability, fentanyl) and law-enforcement endorsements; Pham pledges to defend public safety and stand with law enforcement.
4. **State structure / local control:** Pham supports the autonomous-regions bill; Maier is mixed.
5. **Climate:** both criticize the Climate Commitment Act; Pham would change parts of it, Maier favors market approaches over mandates.
