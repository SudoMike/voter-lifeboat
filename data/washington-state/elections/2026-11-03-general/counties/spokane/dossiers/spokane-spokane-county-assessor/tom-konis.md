## Background

- Tom Konis (Prefers Republican Party) has been Spokane County Assessor since 2019 [S1].
- He joined the Assessor's office as an appraiser in 1993 [S1].
- He is legislative co-chair of the Washington Association of County Assessors [S1].
- Community service: 16 years as a high school and college volleyball official [S1].

## Positions

- Says he has led technology improvements that made the office more efficient and cost-effective, with more coming [S1].
- Says he played a key role, through the assessors' association, in passing SSB 6162, which he describes as property tax reform to help seniors, disabled residents and disabled veterans stay in their homes [S1].
- In a November 2025 op-ed with Treasurer Mike Volz he promoted the state Senior Citizen and Disabled Persons property-tax exemption, run by the assessor's office, for owner-occupants over 61, unable to work because of disability, or with a VA disability rating of 80% or more, and household income of no more than $50,000 [S2].

## Record

- June 2026: his office mailed 2027-tax assessments for more than 214,000 parcels; the average single-family residence was assessed at $441,703, a 0.83% increase, while downtown commercial values kept falling [S3].
- Konis told the Spokesman-Review the market had calmed since the pandemic-era jump of about $100,000 in average home value from 2021 to 2022 [S3].
