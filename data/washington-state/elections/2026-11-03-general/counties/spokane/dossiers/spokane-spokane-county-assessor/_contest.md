## What the office does

The assessor lists and values property for taxation. State law requires all taxable real property to be listed and assessed every year at its value as of January 1 [S2]. In June 2026 the Spokane County Assessor's Office mailed assessments for 2027 taxes to the owners of more than 214,000 parcels [S3]. The assessor values property; levy rates are set by the taxing districts and voters.

## Race dynamics
