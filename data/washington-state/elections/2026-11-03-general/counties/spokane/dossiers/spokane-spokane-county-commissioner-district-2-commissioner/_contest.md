## What the office does

The Board of County Commissioners is Spokane County's legislative authority. Under state law it provides for courthouses, jails and other county buildings, lays out and maintains county roads outside cities and towns, fixes the amount of county taxes, and audits the accounts of county officers [S2]. This seat is elected by District 2 [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Amber Waldref (Prefers Democratic Party), a commissioner since 2023, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
