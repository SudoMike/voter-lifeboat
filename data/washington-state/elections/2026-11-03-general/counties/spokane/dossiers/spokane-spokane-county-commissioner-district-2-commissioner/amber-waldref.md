## Background

- Amber Waldref (Prefers Democratic Party) has been a Spokane County Commissioner since 2023 [S1].
- She served on the Spokane City Council from 2010 to 2017, including as President Pro-Tem, and has chaired the boards of the Spokane Regional Health District, Spokane Transit Authority and Spokane Regional Transportation Council [S1].
- Other work: director of The Zone Project at the Northeast Community Center, development director at The Lands Council, and owner of Mission to Impact Strategies [S1].
- Education: B.A. in sociology and English from Georgetown University; M.A. from Antioch University [S1].
- Community service listed: WSU Spokane Advisory Board, Spokane Housing Authority board, Human Rights Spokane board and Hanford Advisory Board, among others [S1].

## Positions

- Says she will continue to lead efforts to address the opioid crisis, increase housing through smart growth, reduce homelessness and protect drinking water [S1].
- Says rising costs of housing, childcare and transportation can be reduced through partnerships between government, businesses and community organizations [S1].
- Names housing affordability, fixing the justice system and investment in substance treatment as goals [S1].
