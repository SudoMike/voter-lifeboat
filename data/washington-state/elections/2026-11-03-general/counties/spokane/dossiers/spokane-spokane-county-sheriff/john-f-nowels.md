## Background

- John F. Nowels (Prefers Republican Party) has been Spokane County Sheriff since 2023 [S1].
- He spent more than 28 years with the Sheriff's Office as patrol deputy, detective, sergeant, lieutenant, captain, commander of the Spokane Regional Drug Task Force and undersheriff, and was a law enforcement fellow with the U.S. Department of Justice's community-policing office [S1].
- Education: B.A. in criminal justice from EWU, M.A. in administrative leadership from the University of Oklahoma, FBI National Academy graduate [S1].
- He is president of the Washington Association of Sheriffs and Police Chiefs for the 2025-2026 term [S1].

## Positions

- Cites eliminating a vacancy rate of 40+ in 18 months, launching a Real Time Crime Center and strengthening training [S1].
- Pledges community-oriented policing that reduces crime, addresses homelessness and builds relationships across the county, with training, accountability and transparency [S1].
- Opposes the 2026 state law that lets the Criminal Justice Training Commission's decertification remove a sheriff, calling it "legislative overreach" and saying "voters should be the only people that have the authority to remove us from office" [S2].
