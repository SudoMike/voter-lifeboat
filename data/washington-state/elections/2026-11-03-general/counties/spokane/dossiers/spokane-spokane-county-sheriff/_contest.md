## What the office does

The sheriff is the county's chief executive law-enforcement officer and conservator of the peace: the sheriff and deputies enforce state law, make arrests, keep the peace and carry out court orders and warrants [S2]. A 2026 state law requires sheriffs to hold peace-officer certification and to have five years of full-time law-enforcement experience, and lets loss of certification end a sheriff's tenure [S3].
