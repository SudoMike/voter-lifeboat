## What the office does

The sheriff leads county law enforcement countywide, runs the county jail with its corrections staff, and employs the county's dispatchers [S1][S4]. In Adams County the sheriff is also coordinating the jail remodel [S1]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Rematch.** Sheriff Dale J. Wagner of Othello, at the Adams County Sheriff's Office since 1997, faces Matthew Carlson of Ritzville, who spent 12 of his 29 law-enforcement years with the Ritzville Police Department [S1][S4][S5]. Both prefer the Republican Party [S1]. Wagner beat Carlson in 2022, 2,180 votes to 1,710 [S3].
- **Primary.** In the certified August primary Wagner led 1,329 to 1,060 (55% to 44%) [S2].
- **Immigration enforcement.** In a newspaper Q&A both were asked about the state's Keep Washington Working Act. Wagner supports lawful cooperation with federal immigration authorities on serious crimes; Carlson says immigration enforcement is a federal job [S4][S5]. Immigration is not a scored axis for county offices; it is recorded for voters.

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: a sheriff in office since 2015 or 2016, with a 28-year career in the office, against a challenger who has never held elected office.
- `safety`: Wagner states priorities on staffing, training, equipment and jail funding; Carlson's statements describe community presence and trust without specific policy.
