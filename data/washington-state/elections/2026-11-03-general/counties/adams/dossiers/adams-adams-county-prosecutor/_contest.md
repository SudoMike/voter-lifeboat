## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney is chief legal advisor to the county commissioners and to all other county officials and department heads, and represents the State of Washington in criminal cases referred by county and state law enforcement [S1]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, unopposed.** Prosecuting Attorney Randy J. Flyckt (prefers Republican), in office since 2003, is the only candidate [S1]. He was unopposed in 2022 (3,008 votes) [S2].
