## Background

- Adams County Prosecuting Attorney since 2003 [S1]; elected unopposed in 2022 [S2].
- Deputy prosecuting attorney 1999-2002; private practice with Strohmaier & Flyckt, PLLC, 2002-2003 [S1].
- B.A. in history and political science, Gonzaga University (1996); J.D., Gonzaga University School of Law (1999) [S1].
- Member of the Adams County Law & Justice Council, People Against Illegal Drugs, the Ritzville Historic Preservation Commission, Ritzville Lions Club and other local groups [S1].
- Prefers the Republican Party [S1]. Files under the PDC mini-reporting option [S4].
