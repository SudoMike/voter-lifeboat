## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize Adams County Park and Recreation District No. 2 to levy a special property tax of $85,000 "for maintenance and operation of the Washtucna Pool for the year 2027" [S1][S2].

- VoteWA lists the district as "Washtucna Park and Recreation 2" [S1]. Its voters are in the Washtucna town and Washtucna rural precincts [S4].
- No explanatory statement and no statements for or against were posted in the voters' guide [S1]. Adams County prints no local voters' pamphlet.
- A one-year special (excess) levy needs 60% yes under Washington law; the ballot title does not restate the threshold [S1].

## Cost

- $85,000, an estimated $0.83 per $1,000 of assessed value (2026 valuation), collected in 2027 [S1].
- Computed from the estimated rate: about $249 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- **Second try this year.** The same $85,000 levy at the same estimated $0.83 rate drew 70 yes to 52 no (57.4%) in the August 4, 2026 primary [S3], short of 60%; this is the district's second attempt for 2027.
- **History.** November 2022: 89 to 84 (51.4%) [S4]. November 2023: 78 to 51 (60.5%) [S5]. November 2024: 108 to 81 (57.1%) [S6]. August 2025: 73 to 34 (68.2%) [S7].
- **What DOR shows.** A $50,000 Washtucna special levy at $0.52748 per $1,000 for taxes due in 2024 (after the 2023 approval), and none for 2025 (after the 2024 vote fell short of 60%) [S8][S9]. The 2026 levy roll is not yet in a DOR release, so whether the August 2025 approval produced a 2026 levy is not confirmed here.
- **The pool.** In July 2025 the pool board president said the pool had been empty for about five years because it leaked, that a pool company estimated about $98,000 to get it running, and that the August 2025 levy asked for $80,000 of the repairs, which would also make the town eligible for grants [S10]. The 2026 measure is described as maintenance and operation, not repair [S1].
