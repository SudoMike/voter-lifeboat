## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize Adams County Fire Protection District No. 4 to levy a special property tax of $11,000 "for construction, maintenance and operation of pumper equipment in the year 2027" [S1][S2].

- No explanatory statement and no statements for or against were posted in the voters' guide [S1]. Adams County prints no local voters' pamphlet.
- The district is small: DOR's levy detail names it "Fire Dist #4 Harder-McCall" and puts its assessed value at about $22.5 million [S7]; the sample ballot shows the measure on the Ritzville Rural SE-1 ballot style [S2], and the past levies were each voted on in the single precinct Ritzville Rural SE [S3][S6].
- A one-year special (excess) levy needs 60% yes under Washington law; the ballot title does not restate the threshold [S1].

## Cost

- $11,000, an estimated $0.42 per $1,000 of assessed value (2026 valuation), collected in 2027 [S1].
- Computed from the estimated rate: about $126 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- The district asks voters for this levy every year. Approvals: November 2022, 7 to 0; November 2023, 4 to 0; November 2024, 9 to 0; November 2025, 6 to 1 [S3][S4][S5][S6].
- DOR's levy detail shows the special levy at $11,000 for taxes due in 2024 ($0.56492 per $1,000) and 2025 ($0.48995), on top of the district's regular levy ($18,855 at $0.8398 for 2025) [S7][S8]. This request is the same dollar amount, at a lower estimated rate as assessed value grows.
