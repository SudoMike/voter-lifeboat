## Background

- More than eleven years as an appraiser, appraiser analyst and Deputy County Assessor [S1][S2]. Neither his statement nor the visible part of the Q&A names the county or counties where he held these jobs; he is an accredited real property appraiser in Adams and Lincoln Counties [S1].
- Serves on the education committee of the Central Washington chapter of the International Association of Assessing Officers [S1].
- Fourth-generation Adams County farmer, rancher and businessman; he lives on and operates a family farm and ranch outside Ritzville [S1][S2]. Earlier work in healthcare, retail and food service, government and agriculture [S2].
- M.B.A., Eastern Washington University; B.A., Washington State University; accounting and construction certificates, Spokane Community Colleges; state appraiser accreditation [S1].
- Age 57 [S2]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1]. Seeking his first elected office [S1].
- Led the 2026 primary, 1,295 votes to 1,039 [S5]. On election night he said the result "shows people who might want some change in our local government" [S3].
- Files under the PDC mini-reporting option [S6].

## Positions

### Running the office

- Promises "fair, transparent, and accurate valuations" and says that "recent years have illustrated the difficulties of operating without a foundation of seasoned professional knowledge"; he would "return our county assessment system to a commonsense footing" [S1].
- Says he has worked on "equalizing values, improving assessment systems, reducing errors, and making property tax information easier for residents to understand" [S1].
- Would improve response times, ensure "equitable assessments across all neighborhoods," strengthen outreach, and explain to owners how values are set and how to appeal [S1].
- Says the county property tax system "must be accurate, equitable, and easy to navigate" and that he will "restore trust" [S1].

## Endorsements

- A reader's letter backs him on experience and says the county "cannot afford four more years of the current leadership" [S4].
