## Background

- Adams County Elections Administrator. She has worked 19 years in the Auditor's office: 5.5 years in recording, 2.5 years in accounts payable, the past 11 years as Elections Administrator, and is now learning payroll; she has also worked in Department of Licensing services [S1][S2].
- Certified Elections Administrator; Department of Licensing certification; attended Big Bend Community College; Lind High School graduate [S1][S2].
- Age 58; has lived in Lind for 46 years [S2]. Twelve years volunteering with the swine program at the Wheat Land Communities' Fair [S1]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1]. First-time candidate [S1].
- Led the certified primary with 1,060 votes to Fannin's 646 and Stephanie Dormaier's 576 [S4][S3].
- Files under the PDC mini-reporting option [S5].
