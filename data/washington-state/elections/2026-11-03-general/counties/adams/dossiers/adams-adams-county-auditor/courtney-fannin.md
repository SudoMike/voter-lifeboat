## Background

- Payroll deputy in the Adams County Auditor's Office, where she has worked about five years, previously in accounts payable and recording [S4][S3]. Before that, eight years of management experience, including six years helping establish and run a medical practice as office manager (payroll, finances, budgeting, operations) [S1][S5].
- Elected to the Ritzville School Board (her statement lists it as elected experience) [S1]. Bronc Boosters board member, Lind Elementary School PTO member, and organizer of fundraisers for Hope's House [S1].
- Ritzville High School graduate [S1]. Age 44, of Ritzville [S4]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Second in the certified primary with 646 votes to Laird's 1,060 [S6].
- Files under the PDC mini-reporting option; the PDC summary shows $3,756.92 in expenditures [S7].

## Positions

### Running the office and county finances

- Says she implemented the county's online electronic recording service and "played a key role in launching our new county finance software system from the ground up," which she says saved the county money [S1].
- Will "continue embracing technology to streamline operations, reduce costs, and insure accurate and timely reporting of county finances" [S1].
- Pledges to "be a careful steward of taxpayer dollars" and to provide "timely, accurate, and meaningful financial reporting" to commissioners [S3].
- Uses data to "track performance, measure outcomes, and ensure we're meeting the standards taxpayers expect" [S2].

### Elections

- Calls election integrity "one of the most important responsibilities" of the office and lists chain-of-custody controls, accurate voter-registration maintenance, observable processes and trained staff [S1][S2].
- Would expand voter outreach to increase participation [S1][S2].
- Says "running elections isn't about opinions" but about following established procedures [S2].

## Scoring notes

- A deputy in the office with a shorter tenure than her opponent, an outside management career and an elected school-board seat [S1][S4].
- Explicit cost-reduction and stewardship framing for the office's budget role [S1][S3].
