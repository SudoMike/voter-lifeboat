## What the office does

The auditor runs county elections, assembles the county budget, handles accounts payable and payroll, records documents and oversees vehicle licensing for the Department of Licensing [S1]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Open seat.** Auditor Heidi Hunt is not seeking a fourth term; her tenure ends Dec. 31 [S3].
- **Two deputies from the same office.** Jacque Laird of Lind, the county's Elections Administrator, has 19 years in the Auditor's office; Courtney Fannin of Ritzville, a payroll deputy and Ritzville School Board member, has about five years there after eight years of management experience, mostly in a medical practice [S1][S4]. Both prefer the Republican Party [S1].
- **Primary.** Laird led the certified three-way primary with 1,060 votes; Fannin had 646 and Stephanie Dormaier 576 [S2].

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: both are insiders in the office; Laird's 19 years and elections-administration role against Fannin's shorter tenure, outside management career and an elected school-board seat.
- `spending`: Fannin pledges to use technology to "reduce costs" and stresses stewardship of taxpayer dollars; Laird's statement does not address spending.
