## What the office does

District Court judges hear misdemeanor criminal cases (arraignments, trials, pleas and sentencing), civil cases, small claims, infractions, protection orders and search warrants [S1]. Adams County District Court is one county-wide court; each judge serves a four-year, nonpartisan term [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, unopposed.** Judge Andrea K. Russell, on the bench in Ritzville since 2021, is the only candidate [S1]. She was unopposed in 2022 (2,927 votes) [S2].
