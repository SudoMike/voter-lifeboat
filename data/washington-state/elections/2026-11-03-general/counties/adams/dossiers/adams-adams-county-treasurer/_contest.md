## What the office does

The county treasurer collects property taxes and safeguards and accounts for county tax dollars [S1]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, unopposed.** Treasurer Kayla Meise (prefers Republican), who says she first announced her candidacy more than eight years ago, is the only candidate [S1]. She was unopposed in 2022 (3,316 votes, as "Kayla M. Meise") [S2].
- **Primary.** She drew 2,013 votes in the August primary as the only candidate [S3].
