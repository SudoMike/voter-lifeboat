## Background

- **Appointment.** Gov. Bob Ferguson appointed Hill to Division III on Nov. 19, 2025, effective Jan. 1, 2026, to fill the seat of retiring Judge George Fearing; it was Ferguson's first appellate appointment [S2]. As an appointee he must stand at this general election for the remaining two years of the term [S1][S3].
- **Trial bench.** More than 12 years as a Grant County Superior Court and District Court judge [S1]; Grant County Superior Court judge for more than four years at the time of the appointment [S2]. He co-founded Grant County's first therapeutic drug court [S2].
- **Earlier career.** Deputy prosecutor and lead appellate attorney in the Grant County Prosecutor's Office; law clerk to Washington Supreme Court Justice Jim Johnson; consultant and staff assistant to the U.S.-China Economic & Security Review Commission; Senate Finance Committee intern [S1][S2].
- **Education.** J.D., University of Washington School of Law (2008); B.S., Brigham Young University (2004) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Describes his role as reviewing trial-court decisions "to ensure the law is followed," adding, "I have no agenda beyond that" [S1].
- Says parties, law enforcement, victims and the community "want a judge who is impartial, takes their case seriously, and correctly applies the law" [S1].
