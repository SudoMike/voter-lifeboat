## Background

- Served 16 years on the Othello City Council; her statement also says she "served as a legislative appointee to the Othello City Council" [S1].
- More than 30 years in banking, finance and investments; at the forum she described herself as a former bank business development officer who held a securities license [S1][S2][S3].
- Has lived in Othello and Adams County for more than 50 years; involved with the Adams County Family Network, the Chamber of Commerce and economic development initiatives [S1].
- Prefers the Democratic Party ("Prefers Democrat Party" on the ballot) and calls herself "a conservative Democrat" [S1].
- Trailed in the 2026 District 3 primary, 227 votes to 334 [S4]. Files under the PDC mini-reporting option [S5].

## Positions

### County budget and taxes

- Stands for "common-sense leadership, fiscal responsibility, public safety" and says she knows how to "manage budgets, protect taxpayer dollars" [S1].
- At the October forum: "We've got to build our budget because it's really tight right now," and "we have to live within our means. We can't just keep taxing and taxing our citizenry. In these financial times it's tough for families" [S2].
- Says the commissioners "have the power of the purse" and that state law (an RCW) sets the limits on county revenues [S2].
- On revenue, says commissioners can encourage responsible economic development, support existing businesses, pursue state and federal grants, and make sure the county collects the revenue it is legally entitled to [S3].

### Growth and economic development

- Says recruitment takes persistence ("You keep calling; you keep calling") and making employers welcome, citing Othello's dual-language school as an attraction [S2].

### City-county relations

- Says improving relations "starts with communication" and that the split between Othello and Ritzville "shouldn't happen" [S2].

## Record

- 16 years on the Othello City Council [S1]. No county office held.

## Scoring notes

- New to county office but with a long city-council record [S1].
- Explicitly opposes further taxation in the current budget climate and stresses living within means [S2]; her revenue answer favors growth and grants over new taxes [S3].
- No stated positions on housing or climate.
