## Background

- Adams County Commissioner (District 3) since 2023. He won the 2022 general by 58 votes, 1,882 to 1,824, over incumbent Commissioner Terry Thompson, overtaking him in late-counted ballots [S4][S5].
- Owner and agent of Garza Insurance Agency, LLC (13 years); earlier 20-plus years as a supervisor at Simplot Grower Solutions, more than eight years as a New York Life financial services specialist and two years in car sales [S1][S3].
- Former Othello School District board member and president; Columbia Basin Health Association board member and former chairman; elected in 2023 to the Columbia Basin Development League board [S1][S3][S6].
- Age 57, of Othello [S3]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Led the 2026 District 3 primary, 334 votes to 227 [S7]. Files under the PDC mini-reporting option [S8].

## Positions

### County budget and taxes

- Says "taxpayers deserve transparency, fiscal responsibility," and cites "promoting responsible budgeting from elected officials" [S1].
- At the October forum said expenditures have been outpacing revenues, and that last year every office, the commissioners' included, was told to cut a percentage of its operating costs: "We can't have rules for thee and not for us" [S2].
- Said "revenues need to exceed our expenditures – it's math," with department heads prioritizing within their allotted funds [S2].
- Asked how to support development without additional tax burdens on current residents, he called for collaboration with the cities to make the county attractive to employers [S2].

### Growth and economic development

- Says growth should come from employers that create jobs ("This industry came in, this manufacturer is going to make their parts here") rather than new housing that moves residents between homes: "I don't know that I want to call that growth" [S2].
- Will keep "fighting for infrastructure improvements, public safety, economic growth and development" [S1].

### Infrastructure and public safety

- Cites helping secure funding for the county jail remodel and supporting irrigation bridges over the East-Lo canal to improve water delivery to farmers [S1].

### City-county relations

- Says county-city relations are better than perceived and that "communication is key, and getting everybody around the table" [S2].

## Record

- Commissioner since 2023 [S4][S5]; lists the jail-remodel funding, project accountability and the canal bridges as results [S1].
