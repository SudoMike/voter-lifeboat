Statement

As your elected Prosecuting Attorney, I am tasked with the administration of an office which serves vital public functions within local government. The duties of the attorneys and staff of the office include: serving as chief legal advisor to the Board of County Commissioners, legal advisor to all other county officials & department heads; representing the State of Washington in criminal matters referred by county and state law enforcement agencies. As a public official, I strive to ensure that local government operates in accordance with state and federal law; and that our criminal justice system remains fair, effective, and impartial. I also strive to carry on the duties of the office in a manner consistent with common sense community values, and maintain an open-door policy to meet with citizens who may have questions or concerns. I have been honored to serve the citizens of Adams County in this office, and respectfully ask for your vote in the upcoming election.
