## What it does

Proposition 1 asks Auburn School District voters to authorize up to $491,000,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 20 years, repaid by annual excess property tax levies [S1]. The money would (1) build and equip a new Middle School #5, (2) build a larger middle school to replace Cascade Middle School and (3) build a larger elementary school to replace Alpac Elementary School [S1]. The explanatory statement says the projects will increase capacity, reduce class sizes and portable use, improve safety and security, modernize technology and improve playfields for student and community use [S1]. The district expects about $36,248,500 in state construction assistance if the bonds are approved [S1].

The district estimates the projects at $184 million for Middle School #5, $189 million for Cascade and $118 million for Alpac [S3]. It currently uses 43 portables at its four middle schools, housing more than 1,100 students, and expects 45 portables in 2026-27 [S3][S5].

Passage requires a 60% yes vote and turnout of at least 40% of the voters who cast ballots in the last general election. The district puts that minimum at 7,038 voters: 6,292 in King County and 746 in Pierce County [S4].

**The Pierce County side.** The district lies in both King and Pierce counties. The VoteWA record lists the counties as "King, Pierce" and is filed under county code 17 (King) [S1]. Its explanatory statement tells voters to call the King County Assessor about exemptions [S1]. Pierce-side property is assessed by the Pierce County Assessor, and the district's Q&A refers exemption questions to both "the King County Assessors office and the Pierce County Assessors office" [S3]. Its Q&A tax-estimate link goes to a King County tool [S3]. Of the 2024 bond's 36,594 votes, 3,630 came from Pierce County: 2,321 yes and 1,309 no [S4]. That is about 64% yes on the Pierce side, computed for this dossier [S4]. This research could not read Pierce County's own local voters' pamphlet, because piercecountywa.gov refused automated requests (HTTP 403).

## Cost

- The ballot title and explanatory statement give no tax rate. The explanatory statement points to the district website for "the anticipated tax impact" [S1].
- The district gives an "Average cost of $1.10 per $1,000 of assessed valuation" [S2]. Its Q&A works an example: a $600,000 home, 600 × $1.10 = $660 a year, or $55 a month [S3].
- The statement in favor uses the same $1.10 rate: "about $50.41 a month for a $550,000 home" [S1].
- The statement against says the $1.10 figure "completely fails to cover repaying the $491 million principal plus the $350+ million in interest debt" and that "true initial rates" reach "$1.93 per $1,000" [S1]. Its rebuttal works $600,000 × $1.93 into a school tax rising from $4,800 to $5,958 a year [S1].
- The district's tables project the combined district bond rate (existing plus new) at $1.50 per $1,000 in 2026, the existing bonds only, rising to $2.53 in 2027-2031 [S4]. In the same tables, $1.93 is the actual 2024 rate for all district bonds [S4]. The King dossier's year-by-year reading of Table 3 (new-bond rates from $0.93 in 2027 to $1.61 in 2041-2048) comes from the same document [S4].
- Senior and disabled homeowners who meet income limits may qualify for exemptions [S1][S3].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- This is a new bond authorization, not a renewal. The same three schools were in bonds that failed in November 2024 and November 2025 [S4].
  - **November 2024:** $532,100,000 at a projected 81 cents per $1,000 [S7]. The package was larger and also covered capital improvements at other buildings [S4]. Result: 55.05% yes, short of 60% [S4].
  - **November 2025:** $490,000,000 at an estimated $1.04 per $1,000 [S6]. It got 54.05% yes [S4]. A separate six-year capital levy passed [S4][S6]. The district's 2025 table shows King and Pierce "Approved" counts (8,082 and 4,999) that do not add up to the 9,296 total shown [S4].
- The board approved Resolution 1426 on April 27, 2026 after workshops on February 11 and April 13, 2026. The April workshop agenda included "Status of Pierce County 2026 Tax Collection" [S4]. The district says the main change from 2025 is the schedule: Cascade's rebuild was moved up to open alongside Middle School #5 [S4].
- If the bond fails, the district says middle schools will keep exceeding capacity and it will keep maintaining Alpac and Cascade, "diverting resources from other uses" [S3].

## Arguments for

From the statement in favor, by Cari Manry and Terri Herren [S1]:

- "Vote Yes to build a brand-new middle school and replace the aging, outdated Cascade Middle School and Alpac Elementary." More than 1,100 middle schoolers "are crowded into 45 temporary portables" [S1].
- The district "has a proven track record of delivering projects on time and under budget while keeping taxes low," at "an estimated cost of just $1.10 per $1,000 of assessed value" [S1].
- "A majority voted Yes in the last election, but we need a 60% supermajority to secure critical state matching funds" [S1].
- Rebuttal: "Misleading comparisons ignore differences in school size, site conditions, and long-term construction standards. Auburn is building schools designed to last a century" [S1].

## Arguments against

From the statement against, by Eric Petersen [S1]:

- The package means "a staggering $163 million per individual facility," while "the neighboring Tacoma School District is successfully building eight separate schools for an average total cost of $66 million each" [S1].
- Auburn "demands a whopping $118 million" for Alpac: "where exactly is the other $68 million going for this school alone?" [S1]
- The advertised $1.10 rate ignores "$350+ million in interest debt," pushing "true initial rates up to $1.93 per $1,000" [S1].
- Rebuttal: "Tacoma's approved $650 million bond replaces or renovates 11 aging schools; Auburn wants $491 million for just 3. Don't be bullied, make them resubmit this with real cost" [S1].

In the VoteWA record the two rebuttals are stored in each other's fields: the pro rebuttal text is under `RebuttalAgainst` and the con rebuttal under `RebuttalFor` [S1]. This dossier attributes each rebuttal by its content. The Tacoma cost figures in the con statement were not checked.
