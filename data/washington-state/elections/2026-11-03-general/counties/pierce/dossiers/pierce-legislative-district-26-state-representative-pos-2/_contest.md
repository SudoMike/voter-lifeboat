## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets. LD26 spans parts of Pierce and Kitsap counties (katy-cornell.md S10); this contest is also on the Kitsap package's ballot and Pierce researched it once.

## District and race dynamics
Open seat: Republican Representative Michelle Caldier-Valdez is not on the ballot and endorses Republican Katy Cornell as her successor (katy-cornell.md S1, S4). Cornell, a nonprofit leader, pastor and Gig Harbor planning commissioner, faces Democrat Renee Hernandez Greenfield, an educator and union faculty representative making her first run (katy-cornell.md S1; renee-hernandez-greenfield.md S1). In a four-way primary Cornell had about 44.7% and Greenfield about 39.6%, with Democrat Tedd Wetherbee about 13.3% and Randy Phillips about 2.4% (katy-cornell.md S10). Cornell has raised about $300k to Greenfield's about $273k (PDC; katy-cornell.md S9, renee-hernandez-greenfield.md S3).

Cornell runs on lowering costs: opposing the income tax, reversing ESSB 5814, deregulating housing, delaying CETA deadlines, and pairing treatment with enforcement (katy-cornell.md S3, S5, S6, S7). Greenfield runs on climate policy, abortion rights, workers and childcare funding, with labor, WEA, Washington Conservation Action and Planned Parenthood endorsements (renee-hernandez-greenfield.md S1, S2).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Climate and energy:** Cornell would extend CETA deadlines to 2040; Greenfield calls herself "a steadfast champion of strong climate policies."
2. **Taxes:** Cornell opposes the income tax and the 2025 services sales tax; Greenfield pledges only to oppose tax increases on working and middle-class families.
3. **Housing:** Cornell would cut regulations, impact fees and mandates; Greenfield names supply and wage-matched prices without a mechanism.
4. **Reproductive policy:** Greenfield is "100 percent pro-choice"; Cornell states no position and says she is not running on "polarizing social battles."
5. **Public safety:** Cornell supports court-mandated treatment, prosecuting repeat property crime and license plate readers; Greenfield states no position.
