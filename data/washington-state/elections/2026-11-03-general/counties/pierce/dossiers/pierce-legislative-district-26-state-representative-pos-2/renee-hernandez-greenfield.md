## Background
Renee Hernandez Greenfield (prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate for State Representative and an elected faculty representative of the Tacoma Community College AFT-WA local [S1]. She is a special educator at Holly Ridge Center and an adjunct instructor at Tacoma Community College, a former CPS investigator for the Suquamish Tribe, and has managed ECEAP, Early Head Start and childcare programs; she is also a former small business owner [S1][S2]. She holds an MS in Psychology from the University of Phoenix, a BA in History from Occidental College, and early childhood program administration certificates from Olympic College [S1][S2]. She is president of the South Kitsap School District Native Education Parent Council and sits on the Salish Behavioral Health Administrative Services Organization advisory board [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary she finished second with 20,023 votes (about 39.6%) to Republican Katy Cornell's 22,635 (about 44.7%) in official results [S4].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** says she "will fight against tax increases on working and middle class families," increase housing supply, and "cut wasteful spending at the state level" [S2]; says schools are "forced to do more with less" [S1].
- **Childcare and early learning:** says childcare facilities "don't receive enough funding to accept children on subsidies" and that this gap needs to be addressed [S2].
- **Housing:** will "fight for home prices that match local wages" and increase housing supply [S1][S2].
- **Climate:** calls herself "a steadfast champion of strong climate policies" supporting measures that "prioritize clean energy, reduce carbon emissions, and hold corporate polluters accountable" [S2]; endorsed by Washington Conservation Action [S1][S2].
- **Workers:** supports workers "in their fight for fair wages, safe working conditions, and dignity on the job" and is an AFT member [S2].
- **Healthcare:** will "fight for a better healthcare system so seniors and families aren't bankrupted by medical expenses" [S2].
- **Transportation:** wants roads and ferries "we've waited decades for" [S1].
- **Tribal consultation:** says tribal consultation should be "a consistent and routine practice" [S2].
