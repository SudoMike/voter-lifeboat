## Background
Krista Perez (prefers Democratic Party) has not held elected office [S1]. She is Strategic Partnerships Manager at the Washington State Department of Commerce and founder and owner of Perez Consulting, a diversity, equity, accessibility and inclusion consultancy [S1][S3]. She has also worked as a Learning Network Equity Lead for Washington Communities for Children and as a business instructor and coach [S1]. She holds a B.A. in Law, Economics & Public Policy from the University of Washington (2016) [S1][S3]. She founded the Tacoma Women of Color Collective, co-founded The Community Market, and published a book, *Unearthing Our Roots*, in 2021 [S3]. She is the daughter of immigrants and a mother of two living on Tacoma's Eastside [S1][S3]. She has raised $67,649.85 and spent $35,322.32 (PDC, data updated 2026-10-05) [S7]. She is challenging incumbent Rep. Melanie Morgan and says she is running "to bring new leadership to the 29th LD" [S1].

## Positions

### Taxes and spending
- Supports "fair taxation" [S1]. "I back the recently passed millionaires tax that asks the wealthiest in our state to finally pay their fair share" [S4].
- Calls the state's tax structure among the most regressive and says she supports "flipping that system" [S4].
- Would expand the Working Families Tax Credit and Working Connections Child Care eligibility, and raise childcare-worker pay [S4].
- Says fixing the tax structure is "about unlocking the revenue we need to make all of these investments possible" [S4].

### Healthcare
- Says she will "advocate for state investments that bring down the cost of healthcare and prescription drugs for everyday Washingtonians" and "strengthen consumer protections against price gouging" [S4].

### Housing
- Would expand state funding for affordable housing construction and preservation, strengthen tenant protections, and add shelter and transitional-housing capacity [S4].
- Would reform the Growth Management Act "to remove barriers to mixed-use and middle-density development" [S4].
- Supports state down-payment assistance and foreclosure prevention [S4].

### Public safety
- Names youth gun violence a top priority and backs gun buybacks, lockbox distribution and community-led programs [S4].
- Would fund wraparound mental-health, substance-use and job-training services that address "the root causes of homelessness" [S4].

### Labor
- Supports collective bargaining, stronger L&I enforcement, pension security and tying the minimum wage to the cost of living [S4].

### Climate and environment
- Supports "immediate action that reduces carbon emissions and water waste" and investment in sustainable infrastructure with green union jobs [S4].

### Immigration
- Pledges "to ensure Washington continues to be a sanctuary where people feel secure and comfortable, regardless of their immigration status" [S4]. The pamphlet lists "protecting immigrant communities" [S1].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Lists "reproductive freedom" among her priorities [S1]. Pledges to preserve and expand access to contraception, protect clinics and providers, and "push back against any effort to limit reproductive autonomy" [S4].

### Parents and schools
- No position on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found.
