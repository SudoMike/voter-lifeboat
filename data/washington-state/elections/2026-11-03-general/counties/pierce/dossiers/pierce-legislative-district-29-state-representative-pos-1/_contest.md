## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House. Representatives write and vote on state law and budgets. The 29th covers South and East Tacoma, Parkland, and parts of Spanaway and East Lakewood (melanie-morgan.md S3).

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election. Both finalists prefer the Democratic Party (pamphlet page 43; krista-perez.md S1, melanie-morgan.md S1).

Melanie Morgan is a four-term incumbent, former Franklin Pierce school board director and Army veteran. She is running without a campaign website or listed endorsements, and describes herself as "unbought and unbossed" (melanie-morgan.md S1, S2). Krista Perez, a Department of Commerce partnerships manager and DEI consultant who has not held office, has the 29th LD Democrats, Washington State Labor Council, Fuse Washington and seven Tacoma councilmembers behind her (krista-perez.md S1, S5, S6). Fundraising is similar: Morgan about $97k, Perez about $68k (PDC; melanie-morgan.md S15, krista-perez.md S7).

The clearest record difference is the millionaires' tax. Morgan voted nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346 on 2026-03-09, while her seatmate Rep. Sharlett Mena voted yea (melanie-morgan.md S4). Perez says she backs the tax (krista-perez.md S4). Morgan did vote for the 2021 capital gains tax and the 2025 revenue bills (melanie-morgan.md S7-S10).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Morgan voted against ESSB 6346 but for the other progressive revenue bills; Perez backs the millionaires' tax and calls for "flipping" the regressive system.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** four terms and a school-board record, against a first-time candidate running for "new leadership."
3. **Reproductive policy and immigration:** Perez makes explicit pledges on reproductive freedom and keeping Washington a sanctuary; Morgan's evidence is her ESSB 5599 vote, and no immigration statement from her was found.
4. **Housing:** largely shared. Morgan voted for rent limits (EHB 1217) and middle housing (E2SHB 1110); Perez pledges tenant protections and GMA reform for middle density.
5. **Money and power:** Morgan's "unbought and unbossed" framing, against a challenger with broad party and labor endorsements.
