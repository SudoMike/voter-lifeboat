## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House. Representatives write and vote on state law and budgets. The 29th covers South and East Tacoma, Parkland, and parts of Spanaway and East Lakewood (Position 1 contest, melanie-morgan.md S3).

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election for an open seat. Position 2 is held by Rep. Sharlett Mena, who is running for State Senate (Senate contest, sharlett-mena.md S22). Six candidates ran in the August primary (primary _contest.md). Both finalists prefer the Democratic Party (pamphlet page 44; joe-bushnell.md S1, patrick-stickney.md S1).

Joe Bushnell is Tacoma's Deputy Mayor and a Marine Corps veteran. He runs on universal healthcare, anti-price-gouging and anti-corruption bills, and more community policing paired with treatment funding. His endorsements combine police and firefighter unions, business groups (AWB, Realtors, Master Builders) and Democratic officials (joe-bushnell.md S1, S4, S5). Patrick Stickney is a state Office of Equity policy advisor and lawyer. He runs on housing, worker and civil-rights protections, and a prevention-first public-safety platform. He has the 29th LD and Pierce County Democrats, the state Labor Council and Rep. Mena (patrick-stickney.md S1, S4, S5). The two have raised similar amounts: Bushnell about $67k, Stickney about $59k (PDC; joe-bushnell.md S7, patrick-stickney.md S6).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Public safety:** Bushnell wants more community policing and has police-union endorsements; Stickney emphasizes root causes, co-response and youth restorative justice.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** a sitting deputy mayor with a council record since 2021, against a first-time candidate who is a state-agency policy professional.
3. **Healthcare:** Bushnell's headline plank is "Universal Healthcare for all"; Stickney would "work toward" universal healthcare and protect Apple Health.
4. **Coalitions:** Bushnell's business and police endorsements against Stickney's party and labor slate. This describes their supporters and is not scored as a position.
5. **Immigration:** Bushnell co-sponsored Tacoma's ordinance limiting civil immigration enforcement on city property; Stickney supports protections in general terms.
