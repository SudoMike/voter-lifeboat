## Background
Joe Bushnell (prefers Democratic Party) is Deputy Mayor of Tacoma and the District 5 Tacoma City Councilmember (South Tacoma, the South End and East Tacoma) [S1][S3]. He was elected in 2021 as the first Cambodian-American councilmember in Washington [S1][S3]. He is a U.S. Marine Corps combat veteran (staff sergeant), a former chair of the South Tacoma Neighborhood Council and a Tacoma Public Utilities Board member, and has worked as a substitute teacher in Tacoma Public Schools [S1][S3]. He attended Stadium High School and Tacoma Community College and holds a B.A. from The Evergreen State College [S1][S3]. The PDC lists his committee under the name Chanjolee Bushnell (Joe Bushnell), with $66,721.09 raised and $44,748.68 spent (data updated 2026-10-06) [S7]. Position 2 is open; his opponent is fellow Democrat Patrick Stickney [S1].

## Positions

### Taxes and affordability
- "I'll work to ensure that the rich pay their fair share, while cutting taxes for regular people by expanding the Working Families Tax Credit and making more households eligible for the Senior Property Tax Exemption" [S4].
- Says "we're paying rising costs and rising taxes, while large corporations see record breaking profits" [S2][S4].
- Would introduce anti-price-gouging legislation and work to increase grocery competition [S1][S4].
- As Deputy Mayor, says he is leading work to raise Tacoma's minimum wage [S1][S4].

### Healthcare
- "I'll champion Universal Healthcare for all" [S1]; "I fully support universal healthcare for all" [S2][S4].

### Public safety
- "We need more community policing to ensure that everyone feels safe"; says he led work on the council to reduce emergency response times and crime in hot spots such as Hosmer [S4].
- Calls for state action on mental-health and addiction treatment, saying the state must support vulnerable people "before they end up living on the street" [S4].

### Immigration
- Says "immigrants across the country are living in fear. We need to fight back" [S1].
- Co-sponsored Tacoma Ordinance 29105, passed unanimously in May 2026, which bars unauthorized use of city property for civil immigration enforcement [S6]. He said the council wants every action to be "defensible and grounded in fact" [S6].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- "I will always vote to protect the right to choose and access to reproductive healthcare" [S4].

### Reform
- Would introduce "anti-corruption" legislation [S1].

### Parents and schools
- No position on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found.

## Endorsements
- Pamphlet: former 29th District legislators Rosa Franklin, Connie Ladenburg and Steve Kirby; Rep. Jake Fey; County Councilmember Rosie Ayala; Tacoma Mayor Anders Ibsen; labor unions [S1].
- Campaign list adds Secretary of State Steve Hobbs, Auditor Pat McCarthy and U.S. Rep. Adam Smith. It also adds Tacoma, West Pierce and Pierce County firefighter locals, Teamsters 117, Tacoma Police (IUPA Local 6) and the Washington Council of Police & Sheriffs. Business groups on the list include the Association of Washington Business, Tacoma-Pierce County Association of Realtors, Master Builders Association of Pierce County, Washington Multi Family Housing Association and Washington Hospitality Association. It also includes the Puyallup Tribe, VoteVets and Moms Demand Action [S5].
