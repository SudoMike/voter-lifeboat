## Background
Patrick Stickney (prefers Democratic Party) lists his elected experience as Democratic Precinct Committee Officer [S1]. He is Senior Policy & Legislative Affairs Advisor at the Washington State Office of Equity and was a Financial Legal Examiner at the Department of Financial Institutions, enforcing investor-protection laws [S1][S3]. He holds a B.A. from Western Washington University and a J.D. from Penn State Law [S1][S3]. He has worked as a union grocery worker (UFCW) and chairs the Washington State Lawyer Chapter of the American Constitution Society [S1][S3]. He has served on the Washington Student Achievement Council's Higher Education Affordability Workgroup [S1]. He lives in Tacoma with his wife, a teacher [S3]. He has raised $58,689.65 and spent $27,257.28 (PDC, data updated 2026-10-05) [S6]. Position 2 is open; his opponent is fellow Democrat Joe Bushnell [S1].

## Positions

### Affordability and housing
- Lists three main priorities: "finding solutions to rising costs, building and incentivizing affordable housing, and bolstering worker and civil rights" [S2].
- Says the district faces a housing shortage and that costs are too high, especially for people on fixed incomes [S4].

### Public safety
- "True public safety requires addressing the root causes of crime, such as mental health, substance use, and generational trauma, through early intervention and treatment" [S4].
- Would "invest in co-response models, prioritize restorative justice for our youth," and wants jails and prisons to "do more to change behavior" [S4].
- Supports funding so cities and counties can meet new public-defense caseload standards [S4].

### Healthcare and data privacy
- Would protect Apple Health/Medicaid from federal cuts and "work toward real universal healthcare" [S4].
- Would protect the data privacy of Apple Health users [S4].

### Climate and environment
- "We must combat any rollbacks to protecting our climate, support a just transition to a clean energy future" [S4].

### Immigration and civil rights
- Supports resources and protections for immigrants, refugees and communities of color, and the freedom to protest [S4]. The pamphlet lists "federal overreach" among his priorities [S1].

### Labor and consumer protection
- Supports more opportunities to unionize, fair wages and worker well-being [S4].
- Would strengthen consumer financial protections, based on his investor-protection work [S4].

### Parents and schools
- No position on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found.

## Endorsements
- Pamphlet: Washington State Labor Council, WFSE, AFSCME Local 120, members of the Pierce County and Tacoma councils, local Democratic parties [S1].
- Campaign list: 29th LD Democrats, Pierce County Democrats, WEA-PAC, SEIU 775/925/1199NW, UFCW 3000, Washington State Building and Construction Trades Council, Washington Conservation Action, Washington Housing Alliance, Washington Stonewall Democrats, CAIR, Stand for Children, Indivisible Tacoma and others. Elected officials include Rep. Sharlett Mena, Sen. Yasmin Trudeau and Tacoma Councilmembers Diaz, Rumbaugh, Walker and Sadalge. Former primary candidate Erin Chapman-Smith also endorses him [S5].
