## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House, which writes state law and passes the state budgets. The 25th District is in central Pierce County around Puyallup and South Hill.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general, two candidates who have both served on the Puyallup School Board: first-term Republican incumbent Michael Keaton (board 2015-2023; F-16 pilot for 20 years) and Democrat David Berg (board since 2021; gifted-education and PTA advocate) (michael-keaton.md S1, S4; david-berg.md S1, S4). In the primary Keaton had 50.19%, Berg 36.28% and Democrat Nick Oloo 13.47% (michael-keaton.md S17); The Urbanist called Keaton's position precarious (michael-keaton.md S18).

Keaton runs against "reckless government spending and high taxes," promotes the Republican "Save Washington" budget, and in the ESHB 1296 debate offered amendments to strengthen implementation of the I-2081 parents' bill of rights (michael-keaton.md S1, S2, S13, S14). Berg runs on public education, workers' rights and economic opportunity, and has written about AI and data-center costs, with labor, WEA, Planned Parenthood, environmental and housing-advocacy endorsements (david-berg.md S1, S3, S5). Keaton has raised about $72k against Berg's about $32k (michael-keaton.md S16; david-berg.md S10).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes and spending:** Keaton's nay votes on ESSB 6346, ESHB 2081 and ESSB 5814 and anti-tax platform; Berg states no tax position.
2. **Parents and schools:** Keaton's I-2081 amendments against Berg's defense of teacher-led curriculum adoption on the school board.
3. **AI and big tech:** Berg's AI skepticism and support for clean-power requirements on data centers; Keaton has no stated position.
4. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Berg's DEI writing and Planned Parenthood and Stonewall endorsements; no Keaton statement or vote.
5. **Record vs. renewal:** a one-term legislator against a sitting school director; both have local elected experience.
