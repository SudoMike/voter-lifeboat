## Background
Michael Keaton (prefers Republican Party) is the 25th District's Position 1 state representative; the Legislature lists his voting records for 2025 and 2026, consistent with a first term beginning in 2025 [S1][S5]. He served on the Puyallup School Board from 2015 to 2023 [S1][S4]. He flew F-16s in the Air Force for 20 years as an instructor pilot and squadron commander and now works in the defense industry as an Air Force operational subject-matter expert [S1][S4]. He holds a BA from the University of Washington and a master's in aerospace sciences from Embry-Riddle [S1]. He is Assistant Ranking Minority Member on Education and sits on Appropriations, Postsecondary Education & Workforce, and Technology, Economic Development & Veterans [S5]. In the August 4, 2026 primary he received 50.19% to David Berg's 36.28% and Nick Oloo's 13.47% [S17]; The Urbanist described him as "holding on for dear life" [S18].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** "will continue fighting against the reckless government spending and high taxes that drive up the cost of living" [S1][S3]; says he remains "committed to standing up for taxpayers" [S2]. His site promotes the Senate Republican "Save Washington" budget plan to "live within its means, and avoid new taxes and fee increases" [S2].
- **Capital projects:** says he worked across the aisle to pass legislation "that will save millions of taxpayer dollars on state capital projects" (bill not named) [S1].
- **Regulation:** says "unnecessary bureaucracy... adds costs to building a home, opening a business, or just filling a tank of gas" and the state should get rid of "bad legislation" [S3].
- **Public safety:** "Holding people accountable to be good citizens only makes common sense"; cites shoplifting, overdose deaths and crime rates [S3]; supports "improving public safety" [S1].
- **Traffic and education:** supports "reducing traffic congestion, and strengthening education" [S1].

### Parents and schools
- Says that on the school board he "fought to keep politics out of the classroom" and backed a strategic plan putting academics first, "closely followed by parental and community engagement" [S4].
- Record: offered amendments to SHB 1296 to require school staff to support implementation of the Initiative 2081 parents' bill of rights, to remove OSPI's authority to withhold funds from noncompliant districts, and to add intent language on parental rights (none adopted) [S13][S14][S15]; voted nay on ESHB 1296 [S11], which amended the I-2081 list [S12].
