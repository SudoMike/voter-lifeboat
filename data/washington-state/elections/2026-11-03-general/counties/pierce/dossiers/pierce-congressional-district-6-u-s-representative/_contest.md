## What the office does
A U.S. Representative serves a two-year term in the House, writes and votes on federal law and the federal budget, conducts oversight of the executive branch, and handles constituent casework with federal agencies. Randall describes the 6th District as the Olympic Peninsula and South Puget Sound across six counties, including part of Pierce County (emily-randall.md S1, S11). Pierce researches this contest once; the Kitsap package also lists it.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general, two candidates. First-term Democratic incumbent Emily Randall, a state senator from 2019 to 2024, placed first in the August primary with 58.8%. Republican Teresa Fox, a Bremerton safety and compliance manager and Kitsap County precinct committee officer, placed second with 25.5% (emily-randall.md S12; teresa-fox.md S1, S4).

Randall runs on lowering costs, protecting Medicaid and the ACA, abortion rights, a transition away from fossil fuels, and oversight of the Trump administration. She refuses corporate PAC money. Her endorsers include Sen. Patty Murray, Planned Parenthood, the NEA, the State Labor Council and the LCV (emily-randall.md S1, S3, S4, S5, S6, S7). Fox runs on public safety and police staffing, lower taxes and spending, deregulation, naval and resource-industry jobs, term limits, and sex-based eligibility in girls' sports. County Republican parties endorse her (teresa-fox.md S1, S2). Campaign-finance totals were not retrieved: the FEC API's shared demo key was rate-limited during this research.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes and spending:** Randall wants big business to pay its fair share and voted against H.R. 1. Fox calls the state income tax and the Climate Commitment Act burdens and pledges to stop "runaway government spending."
2. **Public safety and immigration:** Fox proposes a federal "Public Safety Floor" to fund police hiring. Randall voted against the HALT Fentanyl and Laken Riley Acts and condemns ICE raids.
3. **Social policy:** Randall states a 100% pro-choice record and voted against the federal girls' sports bill. Fox backs sex-based eligibility under Title IX.
4. **Defense:** Fox wants "Naval dominance" and more shipyard funding. Randall secured military-family provisions but voted against the final FY2026 NDAA and for cutting military aid to Israel.
5. **Climate:** Randall calls for a transition away from fossil fuels. Fox favors "science-based stewardship" and opposes rapid electrification mandates at the shipyard.
6. **Record vs. renewal:** Randall has one House term plus six years in the state Senate. Fox has no prior elected office beyond PCO and has signed a term-limits pledge.
