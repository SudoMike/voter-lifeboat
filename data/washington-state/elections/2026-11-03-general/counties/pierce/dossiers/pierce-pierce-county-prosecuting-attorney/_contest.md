## What the office does
The Pierce County Prosecuting Attorney is a countywide elected office with a four-year term. It decides which criminal cases to charge and prosecutes them in Superior and District Court, and it serves as the county's civil legal counsel. Both candidates registered with PDC with no party (coreen-schnepf.md S7; bryan-chushcoff.md S7), and Chushcoff calls it a "nonpartisan position" (bryan-chushcoff.md S4). The contest was not on the August primary ballot because only two candidates filed.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, open seat: Prosecuting Attorney Mary Robnett is not seeking re-election and has endorsed her Chief Criminal Deputy, Coreen Schnepf (coreen-schnepf.md S5). Schnepf is a 20-year trial prosecutor in Kitsap and Pierce counties (coreen-schnepf.md S2). Her opponent is Bryan Chushcoff, who his campaign says served 28 years as a Superior Court judge, including as presiding judge (bryan-chushcoff.md S2). Schnepf's endorsements cross party lines: Legislative District Democrats, Mainstream Republicans, WEA, firefighters, the trades council and the Tacoma Business Council (coreen-schnepf.md S4, S6). Chushcoff is endorsed by the Pierce County Republican Party and three law enforcement unions (bryan-chushcoff.md S4, S5). Schnepf leads in fundraising, about $95k to Chushcoff's about $26k plus a $10k loan (PDC; coreen-schnepf.md S7, bryan-chushcoff.md S7). No candidate statements could be read, since the Pierce County local pamphlet was blocked by Cloudflare and VoteWA had not posted statements.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Public safety:** both run on enforcement. Chushcoff attacks the office's rising decline-to-charge rate and falling felony filings. Schnepf emphasizes her violent-crime convictions and more focus on car theft and organized retail theft. Neither states a position on diversion.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** an internal successor, presented by the incumbent as "the next generation of leadership," against a judge with 28 years on the bench who is running as the corrective to current office management.
3. **Spending and accountability:** Chushcoff frames the race around measurable office performance but also wants more funding to retain staff. Schnepf promises to show what the office delivers "for every dollar."
