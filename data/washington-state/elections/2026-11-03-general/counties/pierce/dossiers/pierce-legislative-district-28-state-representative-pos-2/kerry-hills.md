## Background
Kerry Hills (prefers Nonpartisan Party) is a probation officer (2014 to present) and was a clinical case manager from 1994 to 2014 [S1]. Hills lists a BA in community psychology from Central Washington University [S1]. Hills's elected experience is the St. Frances Cabrini School parent board (2005-2006) and president of a homeowners association (2024 to present) [S1]. Community service listed is about $28,000 raised since 2005 for LAF, Mary Bridge and the YMCA, and coaching youth swimming, cross country and volleyball [S1]. The statement says Hills has provided "mental health and domestic violence treatment services to the community" since 1994 [S1].

**Identity check.** The VoteWA candidate list gives a Lakewood address (11403 Cloverdale Ct SW), the email VoteKerry28@gmail.com and the phone (253) 254-5421 [S2]. The pamphlet lists the same email and phone [S1]. The PDC registration for "Kerry M. Hills (Kerry Hills)," State Representative, 28th District, Position 2, gives the same Lakewood address and phone [S3]. No campaign website is listed in any of these records [S1][S2][S3]. Neither the 2024 primary nor the 2024 general statewide results list a candidate named Kerry Hills [S6][S7].

**How Hills reached the general ballot.** Hills's name was not printed on the August 4, 2026 primary ballot: the official results for this position list only Dan Bronoske (17,782 votes) and 1,826 write-in votes, about 9.3 percent of the 19,608 cast [S4]. State law puts the top two primary vote-getters on the general ballot if each received at least 1 percent of the votes cast for the office [S5]. The PDC records the candidacy as declared on July 27, 2026, during the primary voting period, with registration filed August 25 [S3]; VoteWA lists a filing date of August 18, 2026, two weeks after the primary [S2]. These dates are consistent with Hills having run as a declared write-in in the primary and qualifying for the general under the 1 percent rule, but the sources reviewed do not break the 1,826 write-in votes down by name, and Pierce County's own write-in report could not be read (piercecountywa.gov returned a Cloudflare challenge). The PDC record's status fields ("on_primary_election_ballot: true", "Lost in primary", "on_general_election_ballot: false") conflict with VoteWA, which lists Hills as an active general-election candidate, and with the Secretary of State's general-election pamphlet, which prints Hills's statement [S1][S2][S3].
