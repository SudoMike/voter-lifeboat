## Background

Sven Nelson has been a Pierce County deputy prosecuting attorney since 1996 [S1][S3]. His campaign says his courtroom experience "spans misdemeanor, juvenile, and felony matters," including property crime, identity theft, elder abuse and special assault cases [S1]. He has served as Team Chief of the office's Special Assault, Elder Abuse and Property Crimes units [S1][S3]. His statement says he led Pierce County's Elder Abuse Multidisciplinary Team for a decade, coordinating law enforcement, prosecutors and social services [S2]. His campaign and a retired judge's testimonial on it say he worked for Pierce County Superior Court Judge Brian Tollefson early in his career [S1].

Since 2019 he has served as a judge pro tempore in municipal and district courts. He says he has presided over jury trials, motions, arraignments, sentencing hearings and community court [S1][S3]. His statement names Thurston County District Court and Olympia Municipal Court as his pro tem courts [S2]. He holds a B.A. from Pacific Lutheran University and a J.D. from Willamette University College of Law [S2][S3]. Civic roles [S1][S3]:
- Elected trustee, Tacoma-Pierce County Bar Association.
- Board member, Washington Council of Police and Sheriffs.
- Former board president and treasurer, Rebuilding Hope Sexual Assault Center.
- Campaign treasurer for University Place Councilmember Ed Wood (2018, 2020, 2024).

Only two candidates filed for Position 8, so the race did not appear on the August primary ballot. No primary dossier exists.

## Positions

Judicial approach:
- Says "the decision has to come from the facts, the law, and the record. It cannot come from politics, public pressure, personal preference, or assumptions about the people in front of the court" [S3].
- Says judicial ethics rules mean he "cannot promise outcomes, suggest how I would rule in future cases" [S3].
- Supports plain-language explanations, interpreter and accommodation access, and remote options "when they are allowed and appropriate" [S3].
- On equity, supports interpreter access, implicit-bias awareness, "responsible use of court data" and reducing financial barriers. He adds that "equity in the courts does not mean favoring one side" [S3].

Criminal-justice formation, bail and diversion:
- His formation is prosecution, about 30 years, with a victim-focused specialty in sexual assault, child abuse and elder abuse [S1][S2][S3].
- Says "accountability and rehabilitation are not opposites." In the right District Court cases that may mean "community court models, treatment-focused options, compliance reviews, restitution where appropriate" [S3].
- Says restorative justice "should never minimize accountability or pressure victims" [S3].
- His campaign describes "a justice system that is both accountable and compassionate" [S1].
- No sourced statements or rulings on bail or pretrial release were found.

Court funding and fees:
- Says fines and fees "imposed without attention to ability to pay" can destabilize people, and that courts should be funded "through regular public budgeting" [S3].
