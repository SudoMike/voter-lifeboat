## What the office does
The 29th Legislative District elects one State Senator to a four-year term in the 49-member Senate. Senators write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, and confirm gubernatorial appointments. The House Democrats' district description puts the 29th in South and East Tacoma, Parkland, and parts of Spanaway and East Lakewood (melanie-morgan.md S3, in the Position 1 contest).

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election for an open seat: Fuse Washington reports that Sen. Steve Conway is retiring (sharlett-mena.md S25). Both finalists prefer the Democratic Party, so party preference does not separate them (pamphlet page 42; sharlett-mena.md S1, david-anderson.md S1).

Sharlett Mena is a two-term State Representative and chair of the House State Government & Tribal Relations Committee. She runs on her record: the millionaires' tax, rent stabilization, a low-income utility assistance program and the Washington Voting Rights Act. She has broad Democratic, labor and progressive-group endorsements and about $164k raised (sharlett-mena.md S1, S10, S22, S24). David Anderson is a first-time candidate and Sumner gym owner with no reported endorsements or contributions. He runs on budget restraint, says the millionaires' tax violates the state constitution, and would bar data centers (david-anderson.md S1, S5, S7). The Senate race, unlike the two House races, pits an established Democrat against a self-described outsider who campaigns against the party's main 2026 tax bill.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Mena voted for ESSB 6346 and the 2025 capital gains, services and B&O revenue bills; Anderson opposes the millionaires' tax but would raise property tax on $1M+ homes.
2. **Spending:** Anderson's main theme is "out-of-control spending"; Mena backs budgets funding food aid, schools and childcare.
3. **Record vs. renewal:** a committee chair with four sessions of record, against a first-time candidate.
4. **Technology:** Anderson would allow no new data centers; Mena voted for data-center guardrails and against data-center tax breaks.
5. **Public safety:** Anderson pairs social-worker response with loosening limits on police for theft; Mena voted against Initiative 2113 (police pursuits).
6. **Social policy:** both back LGBTQ+ and reproductive rights; Mena also has the ESSB 5599 and ESHB 1296 votes.
