## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House, which writes state law and passes the state budgets. The 2nd District covers southern Pierce County and part of Thurston County; the Thurston County package lists this contest too, and it is researched once here.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general, two candidates: first-term Republican incumbent Matt Marshall, a former Eatonville school director, Army veteran and physician assistant who won the seat in 2024 (matt-marshall.md S1, S2, S14), and Democrat Angela Taylor, a Yelm small-business owner with no elected experience (angela-taylor.md S1). In the primary Marshall had 56.90%, Taylor 33.56% and Democrat Martin L Miller 9.41% (matt-marshall.md S15).

Marshall pledges to oppose new taxes and "fight the unconstitutional income tax," cut spending and regulations, defend parents' authority, keep an elected sheriff, and oppose a mega airport in the area (matt-marshall.md S1). His record includes nay votes on ESSB 6346, ESHB 2081, ESSB 5814 and ESHB 1296 and amendments to widen parents' access to school-held health and counseling records (matt-marshall.md S3, S7, S8, S9, S11, S12). Taylor runs on school funding, rural healthcare access, correcting a "regressive tax system" toward property-tax relief, and a pledge to limit health-industry contributions, with WSLC and Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates endorsements (angela-taylor.md S1, S2, S3, S4). Marshall has raised about $86k against Taylor's about $14k (matt-marshall.md S13; angela-taylor.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Marshall opposes the income tax and voted against the 2025-26 revenue bills; Taylor wants to correct a regressive system, though she names no specific new tax.
2. **Parents and schools:** Marshall's parental-authority pledge and record-access amendments; Taylor states no position.
3. **Money and power in politics:** Taylor's health-industry contribution pledge.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** first-term incumbent against a first-time candidate.
5. **Spending:** Marshall's "out of control government spending" against Taylor's fully funded schools and a rural hospital.
