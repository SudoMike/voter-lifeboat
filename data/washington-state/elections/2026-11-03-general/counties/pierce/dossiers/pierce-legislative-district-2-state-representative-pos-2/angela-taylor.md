## Background
Angela Taylor (prefers Democratic Party) is a small-business owner from Yelm who lists no elected experience [S1]. She is a Northwest University alumna (English, with a focus on education) [S1]. She lists volunteer work with the Salvation Army, Habitat for Humanity, Scouts, booster clubs, school levy campaigns, food banks, Yelm Rising, Rotary and Yelm Lions, and on her site also Citizens for Yelm Schools and the Thurston County and Washington State Democrats [S1][S2]. She describes herself as a working mother and caregiver for her parents, a survivor of domestic violence, and a former single mother [S1][S2]. In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 33.56% to incumbent Matt Marshall's 56.90% [S6].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** would "champion bills that address rising costs and correct our regressive tax system" and work with the Finance Committee "to redirect new revenue streams in ways that ease property tax burdens, especially for seniors and low-income families" [S2]. Also seeks "relief from burdensome Business & Occupation (B&O) taxes" for small businesses [S2].
- **Schools and budgets:** running to bring "fully-funded schools, accessible rural healthcare, and responsible budgets" to Olympia [S1]; says she will continue work on "my education bill that fully funds Pre-K - 12 education equitably state-wide" and expand skills centers and technical certification pathways [S2].
- **Healthcare:** criticizes "a healthcare system designed to profit from our struggles" [S1]; would work to lower medication costs, advocate for a centralized rural hospital, and "fight for healthcare for all" [S2].
- **Money in politics:** pledges not to take contributions over $200 from executives, lobbyists and PACs affiliated with private insurers, pharmaceutical companies and private hospitals [S2]; criticizes "career politicians beholden to corporate and insurance special interests" [S1].
- **Public safety:** would support legislation that "expands resources for law enforcement" and encourages collaboration between sheriffs, state representatives and communities [S2].
- **Growth and land:** supports "responsible, transparent housing development, including traffic impact assessments before new projects move forward," and conservation of the aquifer, farms, forests and salmon [S2].
- **Bipartisanship:** proposes meeting monthly with district Democrats and Republicans [S2].
