## What the office does
A U.S. Representative serves a two-year term in the House, writes and votes on federal law and the federal budget, conducts oversight of the executive branch, and handles constituent casework with federal agencies. The 10th District spans parts of Pierce and Thurston counties, which Strickland calls the South Sound (marilyn-strickland.md S1). Pierce researches this contest once; the Thurston package also lists it.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general, two candidates. Democratic incumbent Marilyn Strickland was elected in 2020, was Tacoma mayor from 2010 to 2017, and serves on the House Armed Services Committee. She placed first in the August primary with 47.3%. Republican Chris D. Chung, a Lakewood pharmacist, placed second with 31.4%. Two other Democrats, Alex Scheel and Adam Arafat, together took about 20% (marilyn-strickland.md S1, S11; chris-d-chung.md S1, S2).

Strickland runs on her record: $50 million in local projects, opposition to tariffs and to cuts to health care and food aid, and civil and voting rights. Planned Parenthood, the State Labor Council, LCV and Sierra Club endorse her (marilyn-strickland.md S1). Chung's pamphlet statement names cost of living, housing, health care, education and veterans as concerns without specific proposals. He lists no endorsements and has no campaign website. He ran in 2024 for Insurance Commissioner under a Democratic party preference and in 2025 for Federal Way mayor (chris-d-chung.md S1, S3, S4). Campaign-finance totals were not retrieved: the FEC API's shared demo key was rate-limited during this research.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** Strickland has six years in Congress, two terms as mayor and House whip roles. Chung has never held elected office.
2. **Defense and foreign policy:** Strickland voted for the final FY2026 NDAA and against cutting Israel military aid, while questioning the Iran operations. Chung states no position.
3. **Social policy:** Strickland is endorsed by Planned Parenthood and voted against the federal girls' sports and born-alive bills. Chung states no position.
4. **Evidence gap:** apart from experience, Chung's positions are too general to place on most axes, so most comparisons rest on Strickland's record alone.
