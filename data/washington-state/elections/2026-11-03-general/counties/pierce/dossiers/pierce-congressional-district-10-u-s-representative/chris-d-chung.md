## Background

Chris D. Chung is a pharmacist who reports owning a pharmacy in Tacoma (Lakewood) for 33 years [S1]. He graduated from St. John's University College of Pharmacy and Health Sciences in Queens, New York, and attended the South Korea Air Force Academy and Chung-Ang University in Seoul [S1]. His VoteWA filing lists a mailing address on South Tacoma Way in Lakewood [S6]. He has not held elected office; his pamphlet lists him as a primary candidate for Insurance Commissioner in 2024 and for Federal Way mayor in 2025 [S1].

- **2024 Insurance Commissioner:** he ran as "Prefers Democratic Party" and received 54,469 votes (2.91%) in the August 2024 primary [S3]. He now states "Prefers Republican Party" [S1][S6].
- **2025 Federal Way mayor:** early returns on primary night showed him with 6.66% of the vote [S4]. He did not answer the Federal Way Mirror's candidate questionnaire [S5].
- **2026 primary:** he placed second in the certified August 4 primary with 48,953 votes (31.4%), behind incumbent Marilyn Strickland's 73,689 (47.3%) [S2].

## Positions

- Says his father served in law enforcement and that, as the child of immigrant parents, he values opportunity being "open and fair to everyone" [S1].
- Names rising cost of living, housing affordability, health care and education as residents' concerns, and says some who served the country "feel they are not receiving the full support they deserve" [S1].
- His stated focus is "to help ease the burdens families are facing, to improve access to education and healthcare, to ensure that those who have served this community and country are treated with respect and dignity" [S1].
- Says "I don't claim to have all the answers" and promises "to listen carefully" [S1].
- No campaign website was found; the pamphlet and VoteWA list only a phone number and a personal email address [S1][S6].
