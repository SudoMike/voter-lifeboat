## Background
Gary Parker (prefers Republican Party) lists no elected experience [S1]. He describes more than 30 years as an engineer and executive at Intel Corporation overseeing global operations, and his campaign site also cites work with IBM [S1][S4]. He owns BBQ2U in Gig Harbor, which he says the Gig Harbor Chamber of Commerce named 2025 Business of the Year [S1]. He has a Bachelor of Science in Electronic Physics from Park University [S1]. He is president of the Rotary Club of Gig Harbor and a trustee of the Gig Harbor Chamber of Commerce board [S1]. The primary-stage review of Washington State Standard coverage described him as a Gig Harbor restaurant owner and philanthropist challenging Senator Krishnadasan [S7]. In the August 4, 2026 primary he received 22,328 votes to Krishnadasan's 28,575 (about 43.8% to 56.1%) in official results [S6].

## Positions
- **Taxes and cost of living:** says he wants seniors "not taxed out of their homes" and families able to afford gas and food [S1]; his site lists "Tax relief" and "Property tax" among his priorities, says he "will fight rising prices and taxes so seniors aren't forced out of their homes," and asks "just how much of what you earned today belongs to the government?" [S2][S3].
- **Spending and accountability:** criticizes "out-of-control spending" in Olympia and commits to "Ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly," "Demanding transparency in state government," and measurable results [S3].
- **Housing and regulation:** will push to "Increase housing availability" and "Remove regulatory barriers that raise costs for families and local employers" [S3].
- **Energy:** will push to "Expand energy supply to stabilize energy prices" [S3].
- **Local employers:** will work to "Reduce unnecessary regulatory burdens" and make Washington more competitive for investment [S3].
- **Public safety:** calls for "fully funding law enforcement" [S2]; says recent state policies have "made it harder for law enforcement to do their jobs and easier for criminals to exploit the system," and commits to supporting law enforcement and protecting "your right to defend yourself, your family, and your property from criminals" [S3].
- **Money and influence:** says serving means "saying no to outside special interests" [S1]; his site says campaign money goes to field work with "No consultants taking 30 points off the top" [S2].
- **Healthcare:** says he wants to protect seniors' "access to affordable care" [S1].
