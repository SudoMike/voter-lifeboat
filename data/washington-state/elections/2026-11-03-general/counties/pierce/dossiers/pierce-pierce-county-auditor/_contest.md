## What the office does
The Pierce County Auditor is a nonpartisan, countywide elected office with a four-year term. It runs elections for nearly 600,000 registered voters and handles document recording, vehicle and other licensing, marriage licenses and passport acceptance (linda-farmer.md S1; damon-townsend.md S1). Under the county charter it does not do general county accounting or financial auditing, which sit with the Executive, the Assessor-Treasurer and the State Auditor (damon-townsend.md S1). The office was not on the August primary ballot because only two candidates filed.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates: incumbent Linda Farmer, elected in 2022, a former Lakewood councilmember and communications executive who earned the CERA elections credential in 2025 (linda-farmer.md S1, S2), and Damon Townsend, Pierce County Elections Supervisor from 2014 to 2019 and an operations manager in telecommunications, who also ran for the office in 2022 (damon-townsend.md S1, S4). Farmer is endorsed by 25 other county auditors, Democratic organizations, the county executive and labor councils (linda-farmer.md S4). Townsend is a Cascade Party board member endorsed by that party, Mainstream Republicans of Washington, the Tacoma-Pierce County REALTORS, IAFF Local 31 and AFSCME Council 2/Local 120 (damon-townsend.md S1, S3). Both campaigns are small: Farmer reports about $17.8k raised plus carryover, Townsend about $19.9k (PDC; linda-farmer.md S5, damon-townsend.md S6). Townsend's campaign also runs an opposition site about Farmer, linked from his home page (damon-townsend.md S1), which was excluded under the source policy. No candidate statements could be read, since the Pierce County local pamphlet was blocked by Cloudflare and VoteWA had not posted statements.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a one-term incumbent with a communications and city council background, against a former elections supervisor who has not held elected office and pitches "competence over politics."
2. **Spending and accountability:** Townsend proposes public performance dashboards and process-driven cost cuts. Farmer runs on customer service and access and states no spending position.
3. **Money and power in politics:** Townsend is a third-party board member and is also pursuing a new-county effort for the peninsulas. Farmer runs with a conventional party, labor and officeholder coalition.
4. Taxes, housing and climate are largely outside this office's duties. Townsend's only statements on them come from a 2022 questionnaire.
