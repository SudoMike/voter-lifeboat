## Background
Linda Farmer is the incumbent Pierce County Auditor, elected in 2022; the office is nonpartisan and oversees elections for nearly 600,000 voters plus recording, licensing and passport services [S1]. She previously served on the Lakewood City Council and the Pierce County Charter Review Commission [S1][S2]. She began her career as a newspaper reporter and editor, and worked in communications and government affairs for the cities of Tacoma and Federal Way, Pacific Lutheran University, the Pierce County Library System and Comcast, and as Chief Communications Officer of the Washington State Department of Enterprise Services [S2]. She earned the Certified Elections/Registration Administrator (CERA) credential in 2025 and holds an Accreditation in Public Relations (2001) [S2]. She co-chairs the Executive Steering Committee for VoteWA, serves on the state Voting Systems Review Board, and co-chairs the Washington State Association of County Auditors' legislative committee [S2]. Her statement lists her occupation as "Pierce County Auditor; Former Lakewood City Councilmember," describes her as a "Nationally Accredited Elections Administrator," and cites "two decades of public service and executive management experience," including senior leadership roles in city and state government [S6]. She has an M.S. in communications management from Syracuse University and a B.A. in editorial journalism and political science from the University of Washington, and has lived in Lakewood for more than two decades [S2].

## Positions
- **Elections:** says that in her first term she increased access to ballot drop boxes and vote centers, expanded voter education and outreach, strengthened election security and cybersecurity, and improved ballot processing and transparency [S3].
- **Statement (record claims):** says that in her first term she "increased access to ballot drop boxes, added two new vote centers and expanded voter outreach," strengthened election security and improved transparency; says she oversees elections for nearly 600,000 voters [S6].
- **Statement (says she will):** says that with "confidence in public institutions ... being tested" the office must "communicate openly and make sure every eligible voter can participate with confidence," and that government "should be easy to access and straightforward to navigate," with responsive service for car tabs, passports, recorded documents, and services for people starting a business or getting married [S6].
- **Services:** says she has focused on customer service and digital access for tab and license renewals, document recording, passports and marriage licenses, so people can "access the services they are already paying for with their tax dollars efficiently" [S3].
- **Leadership:** runs on "experienced, steady leadership" and more than two decades of public service and executive management [S1][S3].
- On the Lakewood council she represented the city on the South Sound Housing Affordability Partners executive board and on the state Sex Offender Policy Board [S2]; no stated housing or public-safety positions for the auditor's office were found.
