## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House, which writes state law and passes the operating, capital and transportation budgets. The 2nd District spans southern Pierce County and part of Thurston County (Yelm, Roy, Eatonville, parts of Lacey); the Thurston County package lists this contest too, and it is researched once here (william-dehnel.md S2).

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general, two candidates: Republican incumbent Andrew Barkis, a House member since 2016, Transportation ranking member and Lacey property-management owner (andrew-barkis.md S1, S3, S14), and William Dehnel (prefers Labor Democrat Party), a Pierce Transit dispatch supervisor and ATU Local 758 officer making his first run (william-dehnel.md S1, S3). The same two were the only primary candidates; Barkis led 58.08% to 41.74% (andrew-barkis.md S18).

Barkis campaigns on transportation maintenance without gas-tax increases, housing supply, and opposition to income and gas taxes, with business, real-estate, law-enforcement and some labor endorsements (andrew-barkis.md S2, S3, S4, S19). Dehnel campaigns on a more progressive tax system, explicitly backing the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million, plus school funding, rural healthcare access and LGBTQ+ protections, with WSLC and Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates endorsements (william-dehnel.md S4, S6, S7, S8, S9). Barkis has raised about $162k against Dehnel's about $6k (andrew-barkis.md S17; william-dehnel.md S10).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Barkis voted against ESSB 6346, ESHB 2081 and ESSB 5814 and opposes income taxes; Dehnel "fully support[s]" the millionaires' tax.
2. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Dehnel opposes I-638 and backs gender-affirming care protections; Barkis voted against ESSB 5599 and is endorsed by Human Life PAC, with no campaign statement.
3. **Parents and schools:** Dehnel opposes I-1 by name; Barkis voted against ESHB 1296.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** ten-year incumbent against a first-time candidate.
5. **Housing:** Barkis's supply-and-deregulation focus; Dehnel addresses housing only through property-tax relief for seniors and veterans.
