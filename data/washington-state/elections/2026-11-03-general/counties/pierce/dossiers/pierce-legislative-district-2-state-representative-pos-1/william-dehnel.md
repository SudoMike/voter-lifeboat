## Background
William Dehnel (prefers Labor Democrat Party) is a Pierce Transit dispatch supervisor who worked six years as an operator; he says he has worked at Pierce Transit since 2016 [S1][S3]. He was Recording Secretary of Amalgamated Transit Union Local 758 (2022-2025), is a shop steward and Vice President of the ATU Legislative Council of Washington, and has been a delegate to the Pierce County and Washington State labor councils since 2022 [S1][S3]. He co-founded the Transit Togetherness Pride Caucus of ATU Local 758 and identifies as LGBTQ+ [S1][S3]. He studied non-destructive testing at Spartan School of Aeronautics and software engineering at University of Phoenix [S1]. He has held no public office [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary he received 41.74% to incumbent Andrew Barkis's 58.08% [S11].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** says Washington has "the second most regressive tax system in the country" and he will fight for "a tax system that asks more of those who have more" [S2][S4]. He says of the 2026 9.9% tax on income above $1 million, "I fully support it," and will work to make "this progress" hold [S4]. He would expand the Working Families Tax Credit, reduce or eliminate the B&O tax burden on small businesses, and expand property-tax exemptions for seniors and veterans [S4].
- **Schools and spending:** would close the state's special-education funding gap, update the school funding formula, raise teacher pay, restore and expand early-learning funding cut in 2026, and expand career and technical education [S5]. Wants state investment in roads, bridges, broadband and flood resilience in rural areas [S2][S3].
- **Healthcare:** wants more state investment in rural health clinics and community health centers, higher pay for EMS, mental-health parity, no cuts to Apple Health (Medicaid), and more state negotiating power on drug prices [S6]; frames healthcare as "a right" [S6].
- **Labor:** says he has lobbied to protect collective bargaining, expand child care access, and against "corporate predatory pricing" [S1].
- **School safety:** wants counselors and psychologists in every school building and fewer restraint and isolation practices [S5].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Opposes I-638's ban on transgender girls in girls' school sports [S2][S7]; would protect state law guaranteeing access to gender-affirming care and oppose weakening anti-discrimination protections in employment, housing and health care [S6][S7]. Says "No legislation that targets LGBTQ+ people will get my vote. Ever." [S2]
- Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S9].

### Parents and schools
- Opposes I-1, which he says "would strip anti-harassment protections for Trans students, eliminate student privacy safeguards, and restore provisions that could force the outing of LGBTQ+ kids to their parents" [S7]. Would support "school policies that protect LGBTQ+ students" [S7].
