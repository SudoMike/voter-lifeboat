## Background
Carole Sue Braaten (prefers Republican Party) filed on May 8, 2026 with a Fife mailing address [S6]. Her pamphlet lists her elected experience as precinct committee officer, union negotiator and representative, and pigeon club secretary [S1]. Her listed work experience includes agricultural aide, parks and grounds maintenance, utility forest technician, field inspector, code enforcement and compliance, enumerator, and caregiver [S1]. She lists agricultural science coursework at Washington State University, an associate degree from Tacoma Community College, and critical-areas certification (floodplains, wetlands, geological hazard areas) [S1]. Her community service includes the Fife Parks Board, the Fife Tree Commission, and 4-H youth activities [S1]. She ran for the 27th District State Senate seat in 2024 and received about 25.9 percent in the primary and about 28.1 percent in the general against Yasmin Trudeau [S2][S3]. In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 7,580 of 33,783 votes (about 22.4%) to Laurie Jinkins's 26,099 [S4]. The PDC lists her 2026 candidacy with no contribution or expenditure totals reported [S5].
