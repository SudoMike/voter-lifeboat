## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House. Representatives write and vote on state law and the state's operating, capital and transportation budgets. The 27th District is in Pierce County and includes Tacoma and Fife; both candidates list addresses there (laurie-jinkins.md S1; carole-sue-braaten.md S6). The Speaker of the House, the seat's incumbent, presides over the chamber and chairs the Rules Committee, which decides which bills reach the floor (laurie-jinkins.md S5).

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates, the same two who were on the primary ballot. Democrat Laurie Jinkins, Speaker since 2020 and a House member since her 2010 election, received about 77 percent in the primary; Republican Carole Sue Braaten, a Fife precinct committee officer who lost the 2024 State Senate race in this district with about 28 percent, received about 22 percent (laurie-jinkins.md S1, S4, S23; carole-sue-braaten.md S2, S3, S4).

Jinkins runs on the Legislature's record under her speakership: K-12 and housing investment, health care, "fixing our regressive tax system," reproductive rights and gun-violence reduction (laurie-jinkins.md S1). Her floor record includes yes votes on ESSB 6346 (the 2026 tax on income over $1 million), the 2025 revenue package, the 2025 rent-increase limit, ESHB 1296 and ESSB 5599 (laurie-jinkins.md S6, S12, S14, S16, S18, S8, S10). Braaten's pamphlet statement lists opposition to an income tax, a yearly balanced budget, "tough on crime," parents' rights, sex-based limits on restrooms, sports and prisons, and "No LNG" (carole-sue-braaten.md S1). Jinkins reports about $217,000 raised for 2026; Braaten's PDC record shows no reported totals (laurie-jinkins.md S22; carole-sue-braaten.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Jinkins voted for ESSB 6346 and the 2025 revenue bills and calls the tax system regressive; Braaten says "No Income tax."
2. **Record vs. renewal:** the sitting Speaker against a candidate whose only office is precinct committee officer.
3. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Jinkins pledges to keep reproductive rights legal and voted for ESSB 5599; Braaten opposes "biological boy/men" in girls' and women's restrooms, sports and prisons.
4. **Parental rights:** Jinkins voted for ESHB 1296's changes to the Initiative 2081 rights list; Braaten says "Support Parents Rights."
5. **Public safety:** Braaten's "tough on crime" and law-enforcement support against Jinkins's policing-accountability and gun-violence framing.
