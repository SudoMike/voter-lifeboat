## What the office does
Each legislative district elects two State Representatives to two-year terms in the 98-member House, which writes state law and passes the state budgets. The 25th District is in central Pierce County around Puyallup, South Hill and Orting.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general, two candidates: Republican incumbent Cyndy Jacobsen, in the House since 2021 and a former Puyallup councilmember and accountant (cyndy-jacobsen.md S1, S3), and Democrat Jenn Marie Strickling, a Pierce County Charter Review Commissioner, small-business owner and former laborers' union member (jenn-marie-strickling.md S1, S3). It was the closest of the four LD2/LD25 House primaries: Jacobsen 48.59%, Strickling 46.72%, Republican Ren Fanony 4.62% (cyndy-jacobsen.md S15). Fundraising is close: about $86k for Jacobsen and about $83k for Strickling (cyndy-jacobsen.md S14; jenn-marie-strickling.md S9).

Jacobsen says the state overspends its revenues and opposes new taxes, and runs on police funding and offender accountability (cyndy-jacobsen.md S1, S2). Strickling wants the wealthiest to "pay their share," would cut wasteful spending, and emphasizes utility costs, housing protections, labor rights, and limits on data harvesting, Flock cameras and data-center grid costs (jenn-marie-strickling.md S1, S4).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Jacobsen's nay votes on ESSB 6346, ESHB 2081 and ESSB 5814 and amendments to narrow or sunset the income tax, against Strickling's call for the wealthiest to pay more.
2. **AI and big tech:** Strickling's privacy, surveillance-camera and data-center platform; no Jacobsen position.
3. **Public safety:** Jacobsen's enforcement and police-retention focus against Strickling's staffing-plus-prevention approach.
4. **Housing:** Strickling's tenure-protection charter amendment and workforce-housing incentives; Jacobsen is a rental housing provider and Housing assistant ranking member but states no housing platform.
5. **Record vs. renewal:** third-term representative against a first-time legislative candidate.
