## Background
Cyndy Jacobsen (prefers Republican Party) has been a 25th District state representative since 2021 and served on the Puyallup City Council from 2018 to 2021 [S1][S2][S3]. She is co-chair of the Washington State Institute for Public Policy board and a member of the Select Committee on Pension Policy [S1][S3]. She is Assistant Ranking Minority Member on the House Housing and Finance committees and a member of Civil Rights & Judiciary [S3]. She has taught math at Pierce College, worked as a para-educator in Puyallup schools and as a private-sector accountant, and lists herself as a rental housing provider [S1][S2]. She holds a master's in professional accountancy from Central Washington University and a BA in economics and accounting from Western Washington University [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 48.59% to Jenn Marie Strickling's 46.72% and Ren Fanony's 4.62% [S15].

## Positions
- **Taxes and budgets:** says "every year, Washington outspends our revenues," leading to raids on pension, rainy-day and trust funds and an "insatiable desire for taxation that most of us cannot afford"; will "push for responsible budgets that preserve our social safety net without taking more of your money" [S1]. Running in part on "standing firm against new taxes and bringing balance to the current one party rule" [S2]. Says on the city council she "fought to bring a new vision of keeping taxes low, holding government accountable" [S2].
- **Public safety:** advocates "supporting our first responders, and holding offenders accountable" and "protecting children from fentanyl exposure in homes" [S1]; would fund police departments and pass measures "to encourage police officer recruitment and incentivize retention" [S2]. Says on the council she worked toward "dramatically increasing our investment in first responders" [S2].
- **Homelessness, addiction and mental health:** wants to "sensibly address the crisis of homelessness, addiction and mental health" and says these issues are best addressed at the state and regional level [S2].
- **Jobs and traffic:** wants "to get commuters out of traffic and into new local jobs by removing barriers to economic development" [S2].
