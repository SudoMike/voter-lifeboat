## What the office does

The assessor lists and values all taxable real and personal property in the county every year at its January 1 value [S3], the basis for property tax bills. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Assessor Tammy Groom Ketterman (Prefers Republican Party) is the only candidate [S1][S2]. She was elected unopposed in 2022 with 1,801 votes [S5] and received 1,035 votes in the August primary [S4].
