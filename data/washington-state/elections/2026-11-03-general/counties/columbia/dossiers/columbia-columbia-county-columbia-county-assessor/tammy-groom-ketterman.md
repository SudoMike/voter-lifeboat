## Background

- **Office.** Columbia County Assessor since 2023; chief deputy appraiser in the Assessor's Office from 2016 to 2022 [S1].
- **Earlier career.** Residential and farm appraiser for the Walla Walla County Assessor (1992-2000), ISU Credit Union branch manager (2000-2013) and Banner Bank construction loan processor (2013-2016) [S1].
- **State role.** Member of the Washington State Association of County Assessors committee on open space and exemptions since 2022 [S1].
- **Education.** Department of Revenue certificate of accreditation; IAAO and USPAP appraisal coursework; Walla Walla Community College bookkeeping certificate [S1].
- **Party.** Prefers the Republican Party [S1].

## Positions

- Pledges "a fair and equitable valuation of each individual property in our county" and notes that the office follows rules enforced by the Department of Revenue [S1].
- In 2025 she attributed the county's 4.46% drop in total assessed value mainly to the closure of the Columbia Pulp plant and said, "We need more value in the county," meaning new residents, infrastructure, industry and homes that spread the tax burden [S6].

## Record

- Elected unopposed in 2022 (1,801 votes) [S5].
