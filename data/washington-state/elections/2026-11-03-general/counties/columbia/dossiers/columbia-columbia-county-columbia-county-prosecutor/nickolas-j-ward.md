## Background

- **Office.** Appointed interim Columbia County Prosecutor; serving since November 2025 [S1][S5].
- **Earlier career.** Private practice in felony public defense and family law in Asotin, Columbia, Garfield and Whitman counties (2021-2025); Asotin County deputy prosecuting attorney (2019-2020); private practice and public defense (2016-2019) [S1]. Before his appointment he practiced in Clarkston [S5].
- **Military.** U.S. Marine Corps sergeant, 2006-2011; Iraqi Campaign Medal [S1].
- **Education.** J.D., Gonzaga University (2016); B.A. in history, University of South Carolina-Beaufort (2013) [S1].
- **Party.** States no party preference: "I am not running with a party affiliation because justice should be blind" [S1].

## Positions

- Says that since his appointment he re-opened the prosecutor's office to the public and will meet any resident who brings a concern [S1]; he told the commissioners in 2025 that opening the office was his first goal [S5].
- Told the board his longer-term goal was to "get the county through the issues that are making the paper," referring to conflict between the prosecutor's and sheriff's offices under his predecessor [S5].

## Record

- Appointed by the county commissioners on Sept. 22, 2025, to finish Dale Slack's term; started Nov. 1, 2025 [S5].
