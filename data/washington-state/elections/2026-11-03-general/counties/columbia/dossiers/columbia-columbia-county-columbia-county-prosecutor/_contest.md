## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes crimes on behalf of the state and is legal adviser to the county commissioners, county officers and school districts [S3]. The ballot carries both the short term (the rest of the current term) and the next full four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Appointed incumbent, uncontested.** Prosecutor Dale Slack, elected in 2018 and re-elected in 2022, resigned effective Sept. 1, 2025, citing health concerns [S6]. The commissioners appointed Nickolas J. Ward, the only formal applicant, as interim prosecutor from Nov. 1, 2025 [S5]. Ward (States No Party Preference) is the only candidate [S1][S2] and received 908 votes in the August primary [S4].
