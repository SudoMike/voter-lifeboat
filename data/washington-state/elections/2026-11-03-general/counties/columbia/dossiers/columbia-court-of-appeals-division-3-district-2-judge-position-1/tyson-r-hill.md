## Background

- **Appointment.** Appointed to Division III on Nov. 19, 2025, effective Jan. 1, 2026, replacing retiring Judge George Fearing; Ferguson's first appellate appointment [S4].
- **Trial bench.** Grant County Superior Court judge for more than four years; before that more than seven years on the Grant County District Court. Co-founded Grant County's first therapeutic drug court; regular pro tem judge on Division III [S4]. Describes 12+ years as a superior and district court judge [S1].
- **Earlier career.** Four years as lead appellate attorney in the Grant County Prosecutor's Office; law clerk to Justice James M. Johnson; consultant to the U.S.-China Economic & Security Review Commission [S1][S4].
- **Education.** J.D., University of Washington (2008); B.S., Brigham Young University (2004) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Describes his role as reviewing trial-court decisions "to ensure the law is followed," adding "I have no agenda beyond that" [S1]. On his appointment he said he looked forward "to transitioning from a referee on the field to a replay official in the booth" [S4].
