## Background

- **Office.** District 3 commissioner since January 2023 ("Four years as Columbia County Commissioner District 3") [S1]. He won the 2022 general 1,094 to 1,005 over incumbent Charles Amerein [S13]. He lost a 2021 Port of Columbia race by 12 votes [S11].
- **Work.** Managed the Coeur d'Alene Tribal Farm for more than 30 years while running his own farming operation in Columbia County; owner/operator of BARJAK Farms; now a retired farmer [S1][S3][S11].
- **Roots and education.** Grew up near Prosser; moved to the Touchet Valley in 1975; Prosser High School and a WSU B.S. in agricultural economics [S1][S11].
- **Community.** Director of the Walla Walla Gun Club, Spokane Gun Club and Washington State Trapshooting Association; state trapshooting hall of fame; volunteer welding instructor; equipment fabrication for the Dayton Pavilion and Waitsburg Town Hall; Dayton Chamber of Commerce volunteer [S1][S3].
- **Party.** Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- **Primary.** Second in the District 3-only primary with 198 votes to Warren's 215 [S5]. On election night he said the primary "doesn't really tell anybody much" because the general has a different, county-wide electorate [S4].

## Positions

### Taxes and spending
- "A problem for all citizens is over taxation. Property tax, sales tax, income tax, gas tax, licenses, etc. take a large percentage of personal income. Raising the income limit for property tax exemptions is a step in the right direction. At the county level, the commissioner's job is to spend money as efficiently as possible" [S3].
- "My priority is always to maintain a positive cash flow." He cites being "extremely careful in the handling of other people's money, in this case tax dollars" and pledges to "spend carefully" [S1].

### Growth and regulation
- Wants to "reduce red tape and support new business in our county" and "reduce regulations and focus on local issues" [S1]. His 2022 campaign was on economic development, reducing regulations, reducing partisan politics and coalition building [S12].

### Immigration (state sanctuary law)
- "State sanctuary laws are incredibly short-sighted ... Sanctuary laws need to be abolished" [S3].
- In August 2025, when the board discussed Franklin County's invitation to declare non-sanctuary status, he agreed county business is "dollars and cents" and proposed "just 'no comment' on the whole subject"; the board declined to join [S8].

### Governing approach
- Says he finds common ground with people who do not share his politics and that "it is very important to investigate all sides of any issue" [S3].

## Record

- August 2025: with the other two commissioners, declined to join Franklin County's non-sanctuary resolution [S8].
- April 2025: voted with the board to declare the former planning and building office surplus and auction it; proposed a minimum bid, otherwise renting it to the Conservation District [S10].
- April 2025: asked at a meeting on solar-farm standards whether developers were "raring to go" to build in the county, while the board prepared wind-energy development standards [S9].
