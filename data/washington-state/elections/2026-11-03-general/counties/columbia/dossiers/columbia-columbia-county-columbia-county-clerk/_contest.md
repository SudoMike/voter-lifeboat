## What the office does

The county clerk is clerk of the superior court: it keeps the court's filings, dockets and case records [S3]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Clerk Kriston Chapman (Prefers Republican Party) is the only candidate [S1][S2]. She was elected unopposed in 2022 with 1,803 votes [S5] and received 1,020 votes in the August primary [S4].
