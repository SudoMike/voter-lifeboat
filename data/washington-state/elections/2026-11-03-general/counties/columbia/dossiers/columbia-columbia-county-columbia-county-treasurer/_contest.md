## What the office does

The treasurer receives all money due the county, collects property taxes and pays out county funds on warrants [S3].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Treasurer Carla Rowe (States No Party Preference) is the only candidate [S1][S2]. She was elected unopposed in 2022 as Carla J Rowe with 1,802 votes [S5] and received 1,013 votes in the August primary [S4].
