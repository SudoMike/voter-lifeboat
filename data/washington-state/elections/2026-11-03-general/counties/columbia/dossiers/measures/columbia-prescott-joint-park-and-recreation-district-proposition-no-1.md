## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize the Prescott Joint Park and Recreation District to levy $175,000 of excess property taxes for its maintenance and operation expenses during 2027, as provided in the board's Resolution No. 7-14-2026 B [S1][S2]. It is a one-year operations and maintenance levy under RCW 84.52.052, in addition to regular property taxes; the explanatory statement calls it "the primary source of funding used by the Prescott Joint Park and Recreation District to operate and maintain its facilities" [S1][S8].

- **The district.** It covers parts of Walla Walla and Columbia counties [S2][S3]; its main facility is the Prescott community pool [S1][S3]. In Columbia County it is a thinly settled strip on the county's western edge: the SOS precinct results put its levy only on parts of the ALTO and STARBUCK COUNTRY precincts, with 7 votes cast in 2022 and 10 in 2024 [S6][S7]. WA DOR's park-district layer (PKR2025) holds the Columbia County part as polygon `PRES`. Walla Walla County voters decide the same measure on their own ballots.
- **History.** The district asks for this levy every year, and it had passed in all years but one for more than two decades as of 2025; the 2024 levy was $145,000 (about $0.31 per $1,000) [S3]. The November 2025 request for $175,000 at $0.35 failed, missing 60% by less than half a percent [S3][S4]; Columbia County's part voted 3 yes, 1 no [S5]. The 2026 statement for describes a summer without the pool: "families had to drive to other towns for swim lessons" [S1].

## Cost

- $175,000 collected in 2027 across the whole district, approximately $0.35 per $1,000 of assessed value [S1][S2]. At that rate the Union-Bulletin estimated the 2025 version at about $70 a year on a $200,000 home [S3].
- One year only; a no vote means no additional levy [S1].
- An excess levy of this kind needs 60% approval [S3].

## Arguments for

Written by committee members Hailey Hudson and Debby Phillips: the pool is where "kids learn to swim, families make memories, teenagers find summer jobs, and neighbors gather"; "Voting YES ... is about making sure our kids don't have to experience another summer without these opportunities"; "Once a community pool is gone, it's incredibly difficult to bring it back" [S1].
