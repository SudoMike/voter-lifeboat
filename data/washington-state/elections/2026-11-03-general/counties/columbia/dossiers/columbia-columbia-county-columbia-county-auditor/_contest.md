## What the office does

The auditor records deeds and other documents and keeps the county's accounts [S3]; in Columbia County the office also handles budgeting, finance, licensing and elections [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Auditor Will Hutchens (States No Party Preference) is the only candidate [S1][S2]. He received 1,042 votes in the August primary [S4]. The 2022 general elected Anne D. Higgins, unopposed [S5]; Hutchens lists his service as auditor from 2023 [S1].
