## Background

- **Office.** Columbia County Auditor, 2023 to 2026 [S1].
- **Other work.** Office manager and equipment operator for the Hutchens family farm; freelance editor; personal assistant and editor at PCS Press [S1].
- **Education.** Master of International Management, Portland State University; B.S. in crop science, Washington State University [S1].
- **Community.** Volunteer firefighter for Columbia County Fire District 3; treasurer of the Dayton Volunteer Firefighters Association; youth basketball coach [S1].
- **Party.** States no party preference [S1].

## Positions

- Describes the job as leading and supporting the staff who handle "budgeting, finance, licensing, recording, and elections" in line with the law [S1].
- In 2024 he said the office tries to be transparent and has "a lot of trust between the auditor's office and the voters"; he described the county's hand recount of the 2024 Commissioner of Public Lands primary (1,349 ballots, no changes) [S6].

## Record

- Oversaw the county's part of the 2024 statewide hand recount [S6].
