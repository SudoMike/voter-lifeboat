- **Office.** Columbia County Sheriff since winning the 2018 election with 64% over Deputy Jeff Jenkins; before that, chief deputy for 11 years [S6]. Re-elected in 2022 over Jenkins, 1,306 to 793 [S5].
- **2026 statement.** No statement submitted [S1][S2].
- **Training and education (2022).** Associate of arts, Walla Walla Community College; B.A., Eastern Oregon University; Basic Law Enforcement Academy and Northwest Command College [S6].
- **Party.** Prefers the Republican Party [S1].

## Positions

These positions come from his 2022 campaign; he made no 2026 statement.

- In 2022 he named crime and drugs as his top priority, to be addressed through community-oriented policing, inter-agency collaboration, proactive enforcement and more staffing [S6].
