## What the office does

The District Court hears misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors (up to a $5,000 fine and one year in jail), along with other limited-jurisdiction cases [S3]. Columbia County has one county-wide district court judge; the 2022 race was on all 13 voting precincts [S4].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Judge Kimberly (Kim) Boggs, appointed in 2022 and elected unopposed that November with 1,636 votes [S1][S4], is the only candidate [S1][S2]. The seat was not on the August primary ballot.
