## Background

- **Bench.** Appointed Columbia County District Court Judge in 2022; elected that year and serving since 2023; Columbia County Superior Court commissioner (2022) and judge pro tem (2023-present); District Court judge pro tem from 2002 to 2021 [S1]. Elected unopposed in 2022 (1,636 votes) [S4].
- **Practice.** General law practice since 1994, including contract negotiation, civil litigation, family law and prosecution [S1].
- **Bar.** Member of the Asotin-Columbia-Garfield Bar Association; past president of the Walla Walla Bar Association [S1].
- **Education.** B.A., Eckerd College (1991); J.D., Seattle University School of Law (1994) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says the court is non-partisan and she is independent, and pledges to "apply the law to the facts of each case, regardless of who appears before me" and "to be a judge for everyone" [S1].
