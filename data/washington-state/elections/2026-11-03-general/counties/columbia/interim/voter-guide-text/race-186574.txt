Statement

It has been my
pleasure to serve the citizens of Columbia County when I was appointed
as the Prosecutor in November and I look forward to
continue serving as your Prosecutor.

Since I was appointed, I re-opened
my office to the public. If you feel the need to address a concern to your elected Prosecutor, feel free to stop by my office, and I will absolutely meet with you to address that. 

I am not running with a party
affiliation because justice should be blind. I have been a
public servant almost my life. I first swore an oath to uphold the
United States Constitution when I joined the Marine Corps and shipped to boot
camp at MCRD Parris Island, SC in 2006. I then swore oaths to
uphold the U.S. and Washington Constitutions when I was a Deputy Prosecutor in Asotin County. I spent several years in private practice as a
felony public defender with a small private practice, before being appointed in Columbia County. 

I have a
wife and three beautiful children and pets.

I look forward to continuing to
serve the citizens of Columbia County, upholding and enforcing the
Constitutions for everyone, equally, and being your Prosecutor.
