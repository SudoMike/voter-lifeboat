Statement

I am invested, personally and professionally, in Dayton and Columbia County. In my 30+ years of practicing law in Columbia County I’ve had the opportunity to help individuals and my community. I’ve practiced law from many angles: contract negotiation, civil litigation, families in conflict and prosecution. As a lawyer, I conduct myself in a professional manner: advocate for my client, be prepared, be courteous, act with integrity. My varied experience prepares me to be a strong judge for Columbia County. District Court judge is non-partisan. I am independent. I will apply the law to the facts of each case, regardless of who appears before me. I will work to understand the personal and sometimes complicated issues before me, approach each case without bias and to treat each person with fairness and courtesy.  I pledge to be a judge for everyone.
