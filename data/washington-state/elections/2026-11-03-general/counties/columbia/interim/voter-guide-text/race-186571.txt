As your current Auditor, I would like to say thank you for the opportunity to serve Columbia County, a place that has always been home to me, no matter where I was living. 

The auditor’s office oversees many different functions for Columbia County: budgeting, finance, licensing, recording, and elections. It takes a team of people to ensure these run smoothly and in accordance with all applicable laws and regulations. We currently have a great team in place, and my job as auditor is to lead it and to support staff members as they perform their individual roles. 

Thank you for placing your trust in me. I appreciate your support, and your vote.
