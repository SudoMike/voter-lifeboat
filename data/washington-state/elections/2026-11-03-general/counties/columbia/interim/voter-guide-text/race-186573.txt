Statement

I am a
lifelong resident of Columbia County. My experiences as a small business owner,
farmer, and supporter of many non-profit initiatives have shaped my
understanding of the needs in Columbia County. Maintaining essential services
and functions of county government is a high priority.

 As operating costs continue to rise,
supporting job growth in a rural county of small businesses and agribusinesses
is critical, as it reduces economic vulnerability and creates stable
opportunities for residents and local government. I will fulfill my roles and
responsibilities while advocating in the county and beyond for opportunities
that create value and security for our residents. I appreciate the sense of
connection our small rural community can provide for families. As your
commissioner, I would support and maintain investments in critical
infrastructure and focus on identifying risks and modernizing systems to
improve efficiency and better serve the future.

My
roles in labor, orchard management, and serving as a trustee at Walla Walla
Community College have helped shape my leadership in policy development, labor
relations, and budget management, and have reinforced the importance of
transparent communication to build trust, improve collaboration for
decision-making, and reduce conflict.
