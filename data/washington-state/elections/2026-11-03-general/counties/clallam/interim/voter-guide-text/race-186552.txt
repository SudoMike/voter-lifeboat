Statement
Bruce Hanify was born and raised on the Olympic Peninsula. He attended Dry Creek Elementary and Stevens' Junior High before graduating from Forks High School in 1975. He then graduated from Peninsula College, Western Washington University, and Gonzaga School of Law. Hanify was a deputy prosecuting attorney for 15 years, which included two years with the Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys in Olympia, WA. He was a criminal defense attorney for 17 years. Hanify brings a wide range of courtroom experience and a deep commitment to Clallam County District Court II. He will continue to focus on the particular challenges that our criminal justice system faces at this time.
