Statement
For over a decade, I have promoted safety, justice, and
service for all the citizens of Clallam County. During this time laws have
changed, and so too has the complexity of public safety challenges. More than
ever, keeping our neighborhoods safe, reducing crime, and protecting victims requires proven experience, leadership, and innovation. With over 20 years of
prosecution experience I am uniquely equipped to address these challenges. I am
also deeply committed to improving the quality of life for all the citizens of
Clallam County.
In my fourth term, public safety will remain my top priority.
I will maintain focus on prosecuting violent offenders, especially those who
target vulnerable members of the community. I have also become increasingly concerned
about the impact of repeat offenders and will use all legal means to address the
disproportionate impact they are having on the criminal justice system. I will advocate
to ensure victims’ constitutional and statutory rights are respected throughout
the criminal justice system. I will continue to prioritize positive and
collaborative working relationships with law enforcement, and other community stakeholders,
recognizing that we are stronger and far more effective when we work together.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
