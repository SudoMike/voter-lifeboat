## What the office does

District Court 2 hears misdemeanor criminal cases and small-claims civil matters arising on Clallam County's West End. Port Angeles-based District Court 1 does the same for the rest of the county [S2]. The judge must live in the district, which runs from Lake Crescent to the Pacific [S2]. The county's GIS layer places Forks in District Court 2 [S5]. In 2020 the post was a three-days-a-week job paying about $98,000 a year [S2]. District Court 2 is a separate electoral district and a small one: its 2022 judge race drew 1,940 votes [S4]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Bruce Hanify is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. The county commissioners appointed him in January 2021 to finish the term of retiring Judge Erik Rohrer [S3]. He was elected unopposed in 2022 with 98.81% of the vote [S4] and filed for re-election in May 2026 [S6]. The contest is uncontested.
