## Background

- Born in Port Angeles and raised on the Olympic Peninsula. Graduated from Forks High School in 1975, where he was senior class president [S1][S2].
- A.A., Peninsula College (1979); B.A., Western Washington University (1981); J.D., Gonzaga School of Law (1984). Admitted to the Washington bar in 1985 [S1].
- Deputy prosecuting attorney for 15 years, mostly in Yakima County, including two years with the Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys in Olympia [S1][S2].
- Criminal defense attorney for 17 years, in Cowlitz County from 2007 [S1][S2].

## Judicial service

- Appointed District Court 2 judge by the Clallam County commissioners in January 2021 to finish the term of retiring Judge Erik Rohrer. He was chosen from four applicants [S2][S3].
- Elected unopposed in November 2022 with 98.81% of the vote [S4].
- No published rulings were found; district court decisions are not reported opinions.

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- When appointed, he told commissioners he would try to address the underlying causes of criminal behavior, citing rising drug use, mental health needs and housing difficulty [S2].
- On filing for re-election: "We've got a lot of mental health and addiction kind of issues at the district court level. The criminal justice system has changed quite a bit in the last 10 to 20 years with mental health issues" [S5].
- His statement says he "will continue to focus on the particular challenges that our criminal justice system faces at this time" [S1].
