## What the office does

The Court of Appeals hears appeals from the superior courts. Division II sits in Tacoma, and its District 2 elects three judges from Clallam, Grays Harbor, Jefferson, Kitsap, Mason and Thurston counties [S2]. Judges serve six-year terms [S3]. In his statement the incumbent says the court's 22 judges decide about 1,500 cases a year, and that for most litigants it is the only chance at review [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Erik D. Price, on the court since 2021 and now Division II's acting chief judge, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. On the 2022 ballot, also unopposed, he took 98.6% of the Clallam County vote [S4]. The contest is uncontested. Kitsap, Thurston and the district's other counties carry the same race.
