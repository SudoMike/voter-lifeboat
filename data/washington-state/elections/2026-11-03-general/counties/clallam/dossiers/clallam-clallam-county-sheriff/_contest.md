## What the office does

The sheriff is the chief executive officer and conservator of the peace of the county. The sheriff and deputies enforce state law, arrest lawbreakers, execute court process and warrants, and keep the peace [S2]. The office is nonpartisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Sheriff Brian King, completing his first term, is the only candidate [S1]. In September a Kitsap County jury returned a gross verdict of about $30.2 million against Clallam County for a former deputy injured in a 2020 rifle-qualification exercise. King said the county had been advised by counsel not to comment while it explores its options [S3].
