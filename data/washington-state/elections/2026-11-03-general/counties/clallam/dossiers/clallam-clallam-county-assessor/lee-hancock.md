## Background

Lee Hancock of Port Angeles has worked in the Clallam County Assessor's Office for 16 years as a residential and commercial appraiser and is currently its lead appraiser [S1][S3]. Before joining the office he spent 13 years in mortgage banking and real estate finance and four years as a self-employed residential contractor [S1]. He holds a Bachelor of Business Administration from Stephen F. Austin State University, with an emphasis in finance and economics, and is an accredited property appraiser through Washington State [S1]. His listed elected experience is past president of the Port Angeles Salmon Club. His community service includes volunteer senior care and earlier volunteer work at Queen of Angels School [S1]. His family has lived in Clallam County since 2007 [S1].

He was the first candidate to file for the open seat, on the first day of filing week [S3]. Antonio Price filed later that week [S4].
