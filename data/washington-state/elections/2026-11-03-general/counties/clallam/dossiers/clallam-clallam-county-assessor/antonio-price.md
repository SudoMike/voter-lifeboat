## Background

Antonio Price of Sequim is running for office for the first time [S1][S3]. His statement describes him as "a blue-collar" young man "that has worked the farmland." His work experience is with commercial vehicles, as a diesel mechanic, a farmer and a tow truck driver [S1]. His listed education is a high school diploma [S1]. For community service, he helps every year with the log show and helps farmers with hay season and cattle work [S1].

## Positions

### Reasons for running
- Says he wants "the best for my wife and for my kids and for my friends and community to have a safe and thriving community. That is why I'm running for assessor" [S1].
- At filing he said: "Assessor was one thing I felt that I could actually understand and figure out what needs to be done." He said his priority would be "to help out the community the best that he can" [S3].
- Says his work has let him "talk to a lot of people that have different kinds of backgrounds, so I believe I understand what the people need" [S1].
