## What the office does

The county treasurer receives all money due the county, including property taxes collected for every taxing district. The treasurer disburses it on warrants issued by the auditor and invests county funds [S2]. The office is nonpartisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Treasurer Jennifer L. White, in office since 2023, is the only candidate [S1].
