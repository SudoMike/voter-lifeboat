## Background

Jennifer L. White of Port Angeles has been Clallam County Treasurer since 2023 and filed for re-election in May [S1][S3]. Before that she was chief deputy treasurer. Earlier roles include Superior Court clerk deputy, juvenile corrections officer, Systems of Care grant manager, Healing Court manager, juvenile and dependency court officer, human resource specialist and reserve police officer [S1]. She chairs education for the Washington State Association of County Treasurers (WSACT) and sits on its legislative committee. She is also a member of the Washington Association of County Officials (WACO) personal property tax reform committee and property fraud task force [S1]. She holds a BA in business administration, an AAS-T in criminal justice and an AA, plus a law enforcement certification, and is a graduate of Neah Bay High School [S1].
