## What the office does

Clallam is one of the few counties where the head of community development is elected. Under the charter the director administers and enforces, and advises the commissioners on, all laws except health that deal with the environment, natural resources, and land and shoreline development. That includes zoning, land divisions, environmental policy, building and fire codes, forest management, mining, agriculture, watershed planning and floodplains [S2]. The director prepares comprehensive plans and development regulations for the commissioners to adopt [S2]. The office is nonpartisan and has a four-year term [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent Bruce Emery, elected in 2022, is the only candidate [S1].

None: there is no opponent. The office's work touches housing and land use, and this year it includes the county's comprehensive plan update [S1].
