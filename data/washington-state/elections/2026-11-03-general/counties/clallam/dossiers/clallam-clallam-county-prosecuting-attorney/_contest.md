## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes criminal and civil actions for the state and county, defends suits against the county, and is legal adviser to county officials and school districts [S2]. In Clallam the office is nonpartisan, after a 2020 charter amendment that Nichols advocated [S3]. Its coroner duties are being separated into an appointed position [S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Mark Nichols, prosecuting attorney since his election in 2014, is seeking a fourth four-year term and is the only candidate [S1][S3].
