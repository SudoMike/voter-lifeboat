## What the office does

The county auditor is the recorder of deeds and other filed instruments, keeps the county's accounts with the treasurer, prepares the county's annual financial statements and warrant registers, and serves as clerk of the board of commissioners [S2]. The auditor is also ex officio supervisor of all primaries and elections in the county [S3]. The Clallam office also handles vehicle and vessel licensing and passport applications [S5]. The office is nonpartisan under the county charter [S4].

## Race dynamics

Auditor Shoona Riggs, first elected in 2014 and a 36-year employee of the Clallam and Jefferson auditor's offices, faces Virginia Shogren of Sequim, an attorney who runs on election integrity and fiscal restraint [S1][S5]. With only two candidates, the race skipped the primary. At an August Port Angeles Business Association forum the two "disagreed on nearly every question." Shogren called the county's voter registration list "a mess" and raised chain-of-custody concerns about ballot pickup; Riggs disputed both points [S5]. Both said they would support consolidating elections to once a year [S5]. In 2022 the state Supreme Court sanctioned Shogren and the client she represented over a petition alleging that noncitizens had been registered to vote [S8]. The League of Women Voters cancelled its auditor forum under its rule against "empty chair" forums [S6]. The Clallam County Democrats scheduled a forum with both candidates for October 10 [S7].

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Experience:** an incumbent auditor since 2015 with 25 years in elections administration, against a challenger from private legal practice with no county administrative experience [S1][S5].
2. **Spending:** Shogren pledges to watch "every county expense closely" and cut waste, and cited DOGE in her opening remarks. Riggs stresses "responsible stewardship of public resources" [S1][S5].
3. **Election administration** (not a rubric axis): the main dividing line. It covers hand-countable paper ballots and voter-roll accuracy (Shogren) against current procedures and certification (Riggs). Both candidates expressed reservations about ranked-choice voting [S5].
