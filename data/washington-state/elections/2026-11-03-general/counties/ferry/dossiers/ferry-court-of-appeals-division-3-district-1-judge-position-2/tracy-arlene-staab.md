## Background

- Won the seat in 2020 against Marshall Casey to succeed retiring Judge Kevin Korsmo [S3]; Ferry County gave her 1,940 votes to Casey's 1,582 [S2].
- Spokane Municipal Court judge from 2009; earlier in the city public defender's office (2003) and an appellate attorney for the Federal Defenders of Eastern Washington from 2005; admitted to the bar in 1993 [S3].
- Cited high ratings from independent bar associations in her 2020 race [S3].
- Submitted no statement to the 2026 voters' guide [S1].

## Positions

- No statement in the 2026 guide [S1]. In 2020 she said her criminal-law background was needed on the court to replace Korsmo's [S3].
