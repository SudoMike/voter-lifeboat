## What the office does

A Court of Appeals judge decides appeals from superior-court cases. District 1 of Division III is elected by the voters of Ferry, Lincoln, Okanogan, Pend Oreille, Spokane and Stevens counties [S1]. Nonpartisan, six-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Tracy Arlene Staab, who won the open seat in 2020, is the only candidate; she submitted no statement to the voters' guide [S1]. In 2020 Ferry County voted 1,940 for Staab and 1,582 for Marshall Casey [S2].
