## What the office does

Ferry, Pend Oreille and Stevens counties share one Superior Court with three judge positions, each on Ferry's ballot [S1][S3]. The appointee describes juvenile and civil dockets alongside criminal cases [S1][S2]. This is a two-year unexpired term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Thomas Franklin Webster, appointed by the governor and sworn in on January 23, 2026 to replace retiring Judge Jessica Taylor Reeves, is the only candidate for the rest of the term [S1][S2]. Reeves had been re-elected unopposed in 2024 (2,622 Ferry votes) [S3].
