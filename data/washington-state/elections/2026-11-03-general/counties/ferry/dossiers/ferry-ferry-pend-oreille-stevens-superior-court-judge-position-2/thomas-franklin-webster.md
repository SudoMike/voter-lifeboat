## Background

- Superior Court judge for Stevens, Ferry and Pend Oreille counties since January 2026 [S1]; sworn in on Jan. 23, 2026 after the governor appointed him to succeed Judge Jessica Taylor Reeves, who retired on Dec. 31 [S2].
- Webster Law Office PLLC in Colville, 2009-2025 (mainly personal injury and family law); associate at McGrane & Schuerman, 2006-2009; partner at Tingle, Knight, Webster, and Juckette PLLC, 2000-2006 [S1].
- Cornell College (BSS, environmental science and history, 1996); Drake University Law School (J.D., 2000) [S1]. Colville Rotary, 2007-2025 [S1].
- Lives in Evans [S1].
