## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes criminal cases in Superior and District Court and is legal adviser to the commissioners and other county officials; in Ferry County he is the office's only attorney [S1]. Partisan office, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Prosecuting Attorney Michael Golden, in the office since November 2024, is the only candidate [S1]. He drew 1,760 votes in the August primary [S2]. The 2024 general elected Jesse D. Lamp unopposed (2,934 votes) [S3]; Golden's statement does not say how he came to hold the office in November 2024 [S1].
