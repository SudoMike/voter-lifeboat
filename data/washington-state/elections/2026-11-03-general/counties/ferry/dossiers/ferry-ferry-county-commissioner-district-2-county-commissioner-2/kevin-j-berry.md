## Background

- Grew up in the late 1970s off the grid near Chesaw, Wauconda, Bonaparte and Curlew, went to school in Curlew and worked summers for the Department of Natural Resources [S1].
- Joined the Army in 1985 and served nine years, overseas and in Texas [S1].
- Helped build and manage RV dealerships in Lake Havasu City, Arizona, then drove trucks from 2000 for 25 years and "over 2 million safe miles with one company" [S1].
- Returned to Ferry County in 2006; lists wildfire relief, food deliveries during COVID and annual bike donations for youth programs [S1].
- Reports no elected experience [S1]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Led the August 2026 District 2 primary with 539 votes to Dean's 294 and Jeremiah Adams's 185 [S2]. PDC mini reporting [S4]. His general statement repeats his primary statement [S1][S3].

## Positions

### Economy, roads and budget

- Calls District 2 "the business hub" of the county [S1].
- Says he is "committed to supporting local jobs, strengthening small businesses, maintaining roads and critical infrastructure, and ensuring responsible, transparent budgeting for taxpayers" [S1].
- Says "it's easy to point out problems — the real challenge is finding practical solutions," and presents himself as "a hands-on problem solver" [S1].
- Wants to protect "our rural way of life while building a stronger future for Ferry County families and businesses" [S1].
