## What the office does

Ferry County is governed by a three-member board of county commissioners, which sets the county budget and policy. Each commissioner must live in his or her district and is nominated by that district's voters in the primary, but the whole county elects every commissioner in the general (RCW 36.32.050(1)) [S4]. In the 2022 general the District 2 race was on all 19 Ferry County precincts [S3], while the 2026 primary for it was counted in 7 of 19 reporting units [S2]. Partisan office, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent against a newcomer.** Commissioner Bob Dean, who won the seat in 2022 by 1,632 votes to 1,571 [S3], faces Kevin J. Berry, an Army veteran and long-haul trucker who returned to Ferry County in 2006 [S1]. Both prefer the Republican Party [S1].
- **Primary.** Berry led the District 2 primary with 539 votes to Dean's 294; a third candidate, Jeremiah (Aymish) Adams, drew 185 [S2].
- **Platforms.** Dean runs on fiscal responsibility and government efficiency; Berry on local jobs, small businesses, roads and "responsible, transparent budgeting" [S1]. Neither names a specific tax, levy or spending proposal [S1].

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: a one-term incumbent running to "continue the progress made" against a first-time candidate.
- `spending`: both lean toward restraint in general terms; Dean's statement is more explicit ("the importance of every taxpayer dollar", "government efficiency").
