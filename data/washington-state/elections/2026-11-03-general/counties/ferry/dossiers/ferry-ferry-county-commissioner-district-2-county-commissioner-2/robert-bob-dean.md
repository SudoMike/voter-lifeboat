## Background

- Ferry County Commissioner for District 2 for "the last 3.5 years" [S1]; won the seat in the 2022 general as "Robert P. Dean Jr", 1,632 votes to Nathan Davis's 1,571 [S3]. His PDC filer name is Robert P. Dean [S5].
- Served in the U.S. Army and then made a career in the U.S. Air Force, followed by 20 years as a commercial flight engineer [S1].
- "Pittsburg Institute of Aeronautics, AA in Flight Engineering"; University of Maryland [S1].
- Sits on the Tri-County Health board, the Tri-County Economic Development board, the EMS board, the "WA Counties Risk group" board and the tourism and recreation commission [S1]. Minutes of Ferry County EMS District No. 1 list him as the county's member of its board [S2].
- Prefers the Republican Party [S1]. Second in the August 2026 District 2 primary, 294 votes to Berry's 539 [S4]. PDC mini reporting [S5]. His general statement repeats his primary statement [S1][S6].

## Positions

### Budget and taxes

- Says "I understand the value of hard work and the importance of every taxpayer dollar" and that his focus "remains on the fiscal responsibility to our county and ensuring our county treasury is managed with integrity and transparency" [S1].
- Says he will "continue to advocate for government efficiency that respects your time and your tax dollars" [S1].

### County direction

- Wants "to protect and promote the rural lifestyle that makes Ferry County special" and to "continue the progress made, manage our resources, and ensure a stable, prosperous future" [S1].

## Record

- As commissioner he says he has "focused on contract negotiations, fiscal responsibility and working to maintain an effective county government" [S1].
- On the EMS District No. 1 board in January 2025 he seconded the motion classifying the agency director position as exempt at a 2025 salary of $69,305.60; the board that day approved a $52,000 transfer from the district's general fund to its EMS building construction fund [S2].
