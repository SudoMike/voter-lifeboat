## What the office does

Ferry County District Court is one county-wide court with one judge, elected on all 19 precincts in 2022 and 2024 [S2][S3]. The incumbent describes its Therapeutic (drug) Court, post-conviction conditions and pre-trial monitoring [S1]. Nonpartisan, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Sarah Cuellar, elected in 2024, is the only candidate [S1]. She won the seat in November 2024 over Nick Force, 1,957 votes to 1,529 [S2].
