## Background

- Ferry County District Court Judge since 2024 [S1]; elected in November 2024 over Nick Force, 1,957 to 1,529 [S2].
- Managing attorney of the Law Office of Sarah N. Cuellar, PLLC, from 2015 [S1].
- J.D., Gonzaga School of Law, 2013; B.S. in political science, Boise State University, 2009 [S1].
- Treasurer of the Ferry County Bar Association; a voting member of the state bar's Small Town and Rural Committee in 2022-2023; served on the Ferry County Canvassing Board in 2022 [S1].

## Positions

- Says she has worked to revive the county's Drug Court, now expanded into a Therapeutic Court, and will keep building one that "complies with state law but also fits our community's values and needs" [S1].
- Says she secured state grant funding to improve compliance with post-conviction conditions such as ignition interlock devices, addiction treatment and mental-health services, and hopes to win state funding to expand pre-trial services that monitor defendants awaiting trial [S1].
