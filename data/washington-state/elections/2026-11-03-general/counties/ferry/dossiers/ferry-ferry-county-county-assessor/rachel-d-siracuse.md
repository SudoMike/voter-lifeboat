## Background

- Has worked in the Assessor's office since 2000, as an employee and then as Assessor, and says she has been elected Assessor six times [S1].
- Elected to the Republic City Council in 2017 and 2021, and several times as a precinct committee officer for the Ferry County Republican Central Committee [S1].
- Republic High School; one year at Spokane Community College; an accredited appraiser [S1]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Unopposed in the August 2026 primary (1,861 votes) [S2]; PDC mini reporting [S3].

## Positions

- Says clear information on property taxes and the assessment process is "essential so people can make informed decisions and trust the fairness of the system" [S1].
