## Background

- Reports 30 years of management experience, including owning and running a real-estate company and a domestic-law mediation company [S1].
- Reports a bachelor's degree in law and justice and more than 10 years in the legal field "specifically in the Pierce County court system" [S1].
- Serves on the Ferry County Civil Service Committee, the Ferry County Solid Waste Advisory Group and the state Wolf Advisory Group [S1]. The Department of Fish and Wildlife's roster lists Sheri L Mott of Malo on the Wolf Advisory Group as a livestock-producer member, affiliated with Pacific Cascade Properties [S2].
- Has not held elected office [S1]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Second in the August 2026 primary, 851 votes to Breezee's 1,200 [S3]. Her general statement repeats her primary statement [S1][S4].

## Positions

### Running the office

- Says the clerk's office "should be managed with integrity, consistency, and respect for the people it serves" and should "run smoothly, treat people respectfully, and provide reliable support the court system depends on" [S1].
- Promises "steady leadership, accountability, and common-sense administration" [S1].
