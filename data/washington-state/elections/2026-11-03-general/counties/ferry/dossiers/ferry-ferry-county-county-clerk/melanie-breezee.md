## Background

- Ferry County Clerk: appointed in August 2021 and elected, taking office January 1, 2022 [S1]. The state court directory lists her as the county clerk [S2].
- Deputy clerk from November 2020 and chief deputy from August 2021 [S1]. Earlier held managerial roles, including apartment management, and worked as a bank teller and bartender [S1].
- Pe Ell High School [S1]. Volunteers with the Republic Eagles and the Ferry County Fair [S1]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Elected unopposed in 2022 with 2,489 votes [S3]; led the August 2026 primary, 1,200 to 851 [S4]. Her general statement repeats her primary statement [S1][S5].
