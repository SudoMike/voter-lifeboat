## What the office does

The county clerk is the clerk of the Superior Court: the office keeps the court's case files and records and supports its operations [S1]. In Ferry County it is a partisan office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent against a challenger.** Clerk Melanie Breezee, appointed in August 2021 and elected in 2022 (unopposed, 2,489 votes), faces Sheri L. Mott, who reports owning and running a real-estate company and a domestic-law mediation company who reports more than 10 years in the Pierce County court system [S1][S3]. Both prefer the Republican Party [S1].
- **Primary.** Breezee led the August primary 1,200 to 851 [S2].
- Both statements are about running the office accurately, efficiently and respectfully; neither takes a position on a policy question [S1].

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: the incumbent's five years as clerk against an outside manager with legal-field experience. No other axis has evidence.
