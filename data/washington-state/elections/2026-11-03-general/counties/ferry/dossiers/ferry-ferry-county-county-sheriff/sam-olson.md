## Background

- Grew up in Curlew and graduated from Curlew High School in 1993 [S1].
- U.S. Marine Corps infantry rifleman, 1993-1997 [S1].
- Ferry County Sheriff's Office, 1998-2005: one year on patrol for the City of Republic, three years on Ferry County patrol, and three years as a narcotics detective with the Ferry County and North Central Washington Narcotics Task Force [S1].
- U.S. Border Patrol for 20 years: Yuma, Arizona (2005-2008) and Curlew (2008-2025), as a K9 handler trained in human and narcotics detection [S1].
- Coaches youth baseball and cross country in Curlew and substitute-teaches at Curlew School [S1]. Has not held elected office [S1]. Prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Led the August 2026 primary, 1,281 votes to Maycumber's 995 [S2].
- PDC mini reporting; $989.40 raised and spent per the summary [S4]. His general statement repeats his primary statement [S1][S3].

## Positions

### Public safety and the sheriff's office

- Says people "feeling secure within their homes and on their property are high on my list of priorities" [S1].
- Says he will pursue that "with an aggressive approach towards theft and drug abuse" and "direct that same energy to all matters concerning the office" [S1].
- Says he will lead "through transparency, integrity and compassion" with "a fresh set of eyes" [S1].
