Kevin J. Berry grew up in the late 70's, off grid in the mountains near Chesaw, Wauconda, Bonaparte, and Curlew, attending school in Curlew and working summers for the DNR. He joined the Army in 1985, serving nine years overseas and in Texas. After helping build and manage RV dealerships in Lake Havasu City, Arizona, he began a trucking career in 2000, earning 25 years and over 2 million safe miles with one company. Since returning to Ferry County in 2006, Kevin has supported wildfire relief, food deliveries during COVID, and local youth programs through annual bike donations.

Statement

Kevin Berry believes District 2 may not be the largest district in Ferry County, but it is one of the most important being the business hub. With a lifetime rooted in hard work, service, and community, Kevin understands the challenges facing rural families, small businesses, and local industries. He is committed to supporting local jobs, strengthening small businesses, maintaining roads and critical infrastructure, and ensuring responsible, transparent budgeting for taxpayers.
Kevin knows it’s easy to point out problems — the real challenge is finding practical solutions. Throughout his life and career, he has proven himself to be a hands-on problem solver who is not afraid to roll up his sleeves and work directly on the issues facing the community. Kevin is dedicated to protecting our rural way of life while building a stronger future for Ferry County families and businesses.
