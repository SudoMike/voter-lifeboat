## Michael Golden | Countywide PROSECUTING ATTORNEY | party: (Prefers Republican Party) | ballot counties: Ferry | website:  | email: LMike64@hotmail.com | term: 4 Year Term
L. Michael Golden
Ferry County Prosecuting Attorney
 
Biography
Michael Golden has practiced law in Ferry County since January 2011. He has been a Deputy Prosecuting Attorney here, a defense attorney here and is currently the elected Prosecuting Attorney for Ferry County. Mr. Golden grew up in Eastern Washington, attended Washington State University and Seattle University School of Law. He has practiced civil and criminal law in State and federal courts in Eastern and Western Washington and has tried numerous cases before juries in Eastern and Western Washington. He has been the Ferry County Prosecuting Attorney since November 2024, and he resides in Republic.
 
Statement
 
It is an honor to serve the people of Ferry County as Prosecuting Attorney. In the 19 months I have been in this Office, my focus has been on being a steady and reliable resource for the employees and officials of the County, being a stalwart defender of victims of crime, and being a strong advocate for justice.
 
I am the legal advisor to the County Commissioners and the other elected officials of the County, and it has been my goal to ensure that they each receive any assistance they may require. We have a very good group of elected officials in this County, and I am proud to serve with each of them.
I am the only attorney in this Office, so I prosecute all criminal cases in Superior and District Court, but I am only one member of a team. My legal assistants, victim/witness advocate and Public Records Officer all work together to make sure that defendants are held accountable, victims are assured of their rights, and that we protect the citizens of this County.
It is an honor to be your County Prosecuting Attorney, and I ask for your vote.
