Statement
I have been diligently working for all customer owners of Douglas County PUD for 12 years. Douglas County residents benefit from low cost, reliable power because we own Wells Dam.
We are seeing unprecedented growth in energy demands. US energy consumption is projected to increase by more than 50% by 2050, and data center growth alone will increase by 300% in the next decade. What does this mean for Douglas County? Under my watch, I’ll continue to protect customer owners.
In 2024, the commission adopted a policy requiring all new large loads (over 1.5 megawatts) to purchase their power on the open market. They pay a “power delivery fee” for use of transmission lines and infrastructure, plus pre-pay for equipment. Microsoft is the first customer on this plan, and we are moving all large crypto and data loads to this plan by 2028. Growth needs to pay for growth.
This model is now being adopted throughout the industry by other utilities, and Douglas PUD is viewed as a leader in protecting its customer owners from steep rate increases. If you support this fiscally conservative approach to meeting growth, while protecting low-cost reliable electric service, I’d appreciate your vote.
