## BallotTitle
SPECIAL 1 YEAR EXCESS MAINTENANCE AND OPERATIONS LEVY
The Douglas County Hospital District #2 (ambulance services) Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 91 on 28 July 2026, containing a proposition to finance maintenance and operations. This proposition would authorize the District to levy excess taxes upon all taxable property with the District in the sum of $80,000, requiring collection of approximately $0.36 per thousand of assessed valuation in 2027.
Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
Douglas County Hospital District #2 (Ambulance Services) provides medical services to Waterville residents including a clinic, which is contracted with Wenatchee Valley Medical Center, and its own ambulance services.
The Hospital District is placing a one-year excess maintenance and operations levy on the November 2026 general election ballot to provide sufficient funds for these services.
If approved, this proposition will authorize the District to raise $80,000, which is approximately $0.36 per $1,000 of assessed value on all taxable property within the District in 2027.
