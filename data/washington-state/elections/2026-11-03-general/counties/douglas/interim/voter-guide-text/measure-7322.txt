## BallotTitle
DOUGLAS COUNTY CEMETERY DISTRICT #2
SPECIAL 1 YEAR EXCESS MAINTENANCE AND OPERATIONS LEVY
The Douglas County Cemetery District #2 Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2026-001 on 4 August 2026, concerning a proposition for an excess maintenance and operations levy. This proposition would authorize the District to levy excess tax upon all taxable property with the District in the sum of $50,000, requiring collection of approximately $0.22 per thousand of assessed valuation in 2027.
Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
Douglas County Cemetery District #2 is placing a one-year excess maintenance and operations levy on the November 2026 General Election Ballot. The Cemetery District provides general maintenance and operations, and cemetery services, for 7 Cemeteries and 1 Mausoleum within the District. If approved, this proposition will authorize the District to collect $50,000.00 through property taxes for continued operations, which is approximately $0.22 per $1,000.00 of assessed value on all taxable property within the District in 2027.
