## BallotTitle
FORMATION OF RIMROCK MEADOWS FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT #9
The Douglas County Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution CE 26-19B on July 28, 2026, concerning the formation of the Rimrock Fire Protection District, and adopted resolution CE 26-25 on 18 August 2026 designating the official name to Rimrock Fire Protection District #9. This proposition would approve the formation of a fire protection district pursuant to RCW 52.02 to provide fire protection to the residents and property owners within the boundaries described in the submitted petition. The district would be financed by a property tax levy and managed by three elected fire district commissioners.
Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
This proposition asked whether a fire protection district should be formed to provide fire protection and emergency medical services within the proposed Rimrock Meadows Fire District boundaries.
The proposed fire district has been approved for placement on the ballot following review by the Boundary Review Board for Douglas County and the adoption of a resolution by the Douglas County Board of Commissioners,
If approved by a majority of those voting on the proposition, the Rimrock Meadows Fire District will be established as a municipal corporation under Title 52 RCW. The district will be governed by an elected three-member board of Fire Commissioners. The district will have the authority to provide fire protection and exercise the powers granted to fire protection districts under Washington Law.

## ArgumentFor
My wife and I moved into the Rimrock Meadows region of Douglas county in 2017. We enjoy the rugged landscape, wildlife, and natural beauty. As all of us know, these same qualities turn into a tinderbox during the hot dry summer months. Oddly through the years, this section of the county has been overlooked for the formation of a fire district. We've received three evacuation notices due to impending wildfires. In 2020, we evacuated for three days while waiting for the hopeful news that the fire had been contained. The fire, driven by high winds, had been advancing at a rate of twelve miles per hour. We thankfully received the news that adjoining fire districts along with state and federal support, had contained the fire "eight" miles from our home. It's apparent the threat remains constant, I urge you, please vote yes for the passing of this resolution.
