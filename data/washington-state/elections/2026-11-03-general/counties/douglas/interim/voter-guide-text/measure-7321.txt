## BallotTitle
BONDS TO REBUILD AND MODERNIZE DETERIORATING SCHOOLS AND IMPROVE SAFETY
The Board of Directors of Eastmont School district No. 206 adopted Resolution No. 2026-02, concerning bonds to improve deteriorating school facilities, educational opportunities and safety. This proposition would authorize the District to: rebuild and modernize the oldest and deteriorating portions of Cascade, Kenroy and Lee Elementary Schools, including adding permanent classrooms to replace portables; make districtwide priority building and safety upgrades, including improving parking, student pickup/drop off areas, athletic facilities, deteriorating roofing, outdoor lighting, HVAC; issue $125,000,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 20 years; and levy annual excess property taxes to repay the bonds, all as provided in Resolution 2026-02. ## ExplanatoryStatement
Proposition 1 authorizes school construction bonds to rebuild and modernize deteriorating schools, including improving student and staff safety and security, improving and expanding educational spaces, and improving outdated infrastructure.
The bonds will allow Eastmont School District to: (1) rebuild and modernize the oldest and deteriorating portions of Cascade Elementary School, Kenroy Elementary School and Lee Elementary School (including constructing permanent classrooms to replace existing portable classrooms; upgrading existing classrooms, playfields, parking, student pickup/drop off areas, and other school facilities; and making other safety, security, facility and site improvements); and (2) make districtwide priority building and safety upgrades (including improving parking and student pickup/drop off areas; upgrading student and community use tennis courts, track and stadium bleachers; replacing deteriorating roofing; upgrading outdoor lighting; upgrading HVAC; and improving interior finishings; and making other districtwide priority building and safety upgrades. Further information is available at www.eastmont206.org.
The $125,000,000 of general obligation bonds will be repaid from annual excess property taxes sufficient to pay bond debt service over a maximum term of 20 years. Exemptions from taxes may be available to certain homeowners, call the Douglas County Assessor at 509-745-8521.

## FiscalStatement


## ArgumentFor
Eastmont students need schools that are safe, functional, and ready for learning. Vote yes on the November 3 bond to rebuild Kenroy, Lee, and Cascade elementary schools, which are aging, overcrowded, and increasingly expensive to maintain.
The bond would replace 15 portables with permanent classrooms, create secure entrances and interior hallways, eliminate 162 exterior classroom doors, and improve bus and parent pick-up areas away from busy streets.
It would also fund maintenance and repair projects in schools across the district, replacing aging and inefficient building systems before failures become more costly and disruptive. This community-developed plan focuses on Eastmont’s highest-priority facility needs and ensures our students have safe, healthy schools.
Approval would qualify Eastmont for approximately $25 million in state construction assistance, reducing the amount funded locally. Strong schools build strong communities. Please join families, educators, and neighbors in voting yes by November 3!
