Elected Experience
I have a varied background with experience in farming, real estate, banking and county appraisal work. For the past 29 years I served all citizens of Douglas County overseeing various functions of the office including elections, licensing, recording, accounts payable, budgeting and payroll. I am a state and nationally certified election official. Our election office has successfully conducted more than 80 elections since 1997. I am past president of Washington State Association of County Auditors, Finance Chair for Washington Counties Risk Pool, Licensing Committee Legislative Lead, DOL Education Committee, County Representative on the AWC L&I Retro Pool Advisory Board, etc.

Statement
It has been my pleasure and distinct honor to serve all the citizens of Douglas County since 1997. I appreciate all the support from voters who elected me County Auditor 8 times. I not only thank the voters of Douglas County but especially my staff members past and present who worked tirelessly to provide excellent and efficient customer service to all the citizens of Douglas County. The world is very different now than when I was appointed County Auditor in 1997. I look forward to the challenges that lie ahead for county government and know that I and my team are up to the task. I pledge to serve with all my skill and ability in this vitally important office. As your current County Auditor, I would be remiss if I didn't urge all eligible voters in Douglas County to register and vote in every election. Remember, your voice is your vote.
