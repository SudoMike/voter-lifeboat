I have dedicated my career to public safety and community service in Douglas County. With nearly twenty years in the Douglas County Sheriff’s Office and thirty years in the fire and EMS service, I have built my professional life around serving the citizens and visitors of our county. I currently serve as Sheriff, following my appointment in 2026, and continue my long-standing role as Fire Chief. These positions have given me firsthand experience in emergency response, interagency coordination, and the day-to-day responsibilities of protecting our communities.
My commitment to public service extends beyond public safety. I served three terms on the Mansfield School Board, where I worked to support students, staff, and families while helping guide district operations. I have also volunteered with the Sage Advocacy Center and remain active in local youth and community programs.
As a fifth-generation Douglas County resident, I understand the values, challenges, and strengths that define our communities. My family has deep roots here, and my wife and children continue to be a source of support and motivation in this work. My focus remains on providing steady, continued leadership grounded in experience, accountability, and service to the people of Douglas County.
