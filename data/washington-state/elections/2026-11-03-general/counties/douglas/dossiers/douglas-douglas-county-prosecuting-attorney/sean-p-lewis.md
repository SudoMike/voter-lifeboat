## Background

- Sean P. Lewis (Prefers Republican Party) was appointed Douglas County Prosecuting Attorney by the Board of County Commissioners to finish Gordon Edgar's term after Edgar retired in June 2025 [S1][S2].
- He lists 30 years of legal experience [S1].
- He was unopposed in the August 2026 primary (7,154 votes) [S4].
- Lewis resigned effective September 30, 2026, citing health reasons; KPQ reports that his name will still appear on the November ballot [S3]. The commissioners appointed Edgar interim prosecutor from October 1 [S2].

## Positions

- His statement says he sought the appointment to return the office to "fiscal conservatism", cut what he called unnecessary spending on criminal case management, and reduce a backlog of unprosecuted cases, and that both have been achieved [S1].
- Plans to convert the office to a nearly paperless one [S1].

## Record

- Prosecuting attorney by appointment from 2025 until his September 30, 2026 resignation [S1][S2][S3].
