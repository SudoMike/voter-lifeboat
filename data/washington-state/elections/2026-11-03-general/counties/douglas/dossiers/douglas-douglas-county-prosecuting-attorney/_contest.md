## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney is legal adviser to the county commissioners and county officials, brings and defends the county's civil cases, reviews law-enforcement referrals and decides whether to file criminal charges, and prosecutes them in superior, juvenile and district court [S2].

## Race dynamics

Sean P. Lewis (Prefers Republican Party), appointed by the commissioners after Gordon Edgar retired in June 2025, filed unopposed for the short and full terms [S1][S5]. Lewis resigned effective September 30, 2026, citing health reasons; his name stays on the general ballot. The commissioners appointed Edgar as interim prosecutor from October 1 and said they will name another interim appointee, from Republican Central Committee nominations, in early 2027 [S3][S4].
