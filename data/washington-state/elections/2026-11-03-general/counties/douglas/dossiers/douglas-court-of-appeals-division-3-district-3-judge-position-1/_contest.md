## What the office does

The Court of Appeals hears appeals from superior court decisions. Division III, District 3 is Chelan, Douglas, Kittitas, Klickitat and Yakima counties and elects two judges [S2]; every voter in those counties votes on the seat, so Douglas ships it county-scoped for information.

## Race dynamics

Judge Megan K. Murphy, appointed in January 2025 and elected unopposed in November 2025 to finish the term, is the only candidate for the six-year term [S1][S3]. Chelan and Yakima ship their own copies of this contest.
