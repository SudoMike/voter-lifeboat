## Background

- Judge of the Washington Court of Appeals, Division III, since January 2025, and the only candidate for this seat [S1].
- Gov. Jay Inslee announced her appointment on January 13, 2025, to replace Judge Rebecca Pennell, who had been confirmed as a federal district judge for Eastern Washington; she was one of four applicants [S2].
- She was unopposed in the November 4, 2025 election for the seat, winning 98.5% of Douglas County votes and 98.1% across the district, the rest write-ins [S3].
- Began her career in 2001 as a Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney, then moved to civil litigation in private practice [S1]; the Yakima Herald-Republic reported about five years as a prosecutor, a Yakima practice specializing in medical-malpractice defense, and a partnership at Fox Ballard in Seattle, and that she had appeared before Division III in numerous cases [S2].
- Education: Eisenhower High School (1993); Claremont McKenna College (1997); University of Colorado School of Law (2001) [S1].
