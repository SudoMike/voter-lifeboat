## Background

- David Walker says he recently retired after 38 years in the fire service, including as a fire chief and commissioner of a rural district [S1].
- He lists elected service as a commissioner of Skagit Fire District 5 and of United General Hospital District 304; these were not independently verified as of 2026-10-08 [S1].
- B.S. in business, A.A. in fire science, and National Fire Academy executive leadership and fire service finance courses; volunteer firefighter [S1].
- His filing gives a Leavenworth mailing address [S2].

## Positions

- Says the new district will need an "active and engaged board" for its first years to set up its legal, administrative and operational foundations [S1].
- Wants reliable emergency service "while keeping costs reasonable" and calls Rimrock a high-fire-risk area [S1].
