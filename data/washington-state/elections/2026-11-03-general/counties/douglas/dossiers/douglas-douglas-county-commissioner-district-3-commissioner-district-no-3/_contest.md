## What this office does

Douglas County's three commissioners are the county's legislative and executive
body: they adopt the budget and property-tax levies, set land-use and
development rules for the unincorporated county, and oversee county roads,
facilities and departments. A commissioner is nominated by district in the
primary (only District 3 voters chose Straub and Lesky in August [S2]) and
elected by the whole county in the general [S3]; in the 2024 general both
commissioner races were on all 49 Douglas precincts [S4].

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Marc Straub, first elected in 2018 over Norman (Buck) Tupling
  with 64.8% [S5], seeks a third term on a record of economic development,
  infrastructure and fiscal restraint [S1].
- Michael S. Lesky, who states an Independent party preference, filed no
  voters' guide statement [S1]. In the district-only primary Straub took
  2,583 votes to Lesky's 710 [S2]; both advance to the county-wide general
  [S3].

## Axes that differentiate

- `experience`: an eight-year incumbent against a challenger with no
  documented record.
- `spending` and `safety` rest on Straub's own platform; Lesky has no
  stated positions in any source found, so his axes are omitted.
