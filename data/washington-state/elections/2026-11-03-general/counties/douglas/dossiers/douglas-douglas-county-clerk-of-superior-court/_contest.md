## What this office does

The Superior Court clerk "oversees the records and billings of the county court system" [S2]: filings and case records, jury administration, and collection of court fines, fees and restitution are the duties both candidates discuss [S1][S4]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Jenn Biggar, appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022, faces Andrea Jester, a current court facilitator and former deputy clerk in the same court system; both live in Waterville and both are Republicans [S1][S2].
- Biggar led the August primary 5,285 to 3,812 [S3].
- Biggar runs on modernization (electronic filing, online calendars, a new jury system) and statewide clerks' association leadership; Jester runs on customer service, staff training and retention, and has the endorsement of the union that represents the office's staff [S1][S4].

## Axes that differentiate

- `experience`: incumbent with a record versus a challenger from the office's own staff ranks.
- Little or no evidence on `taxes`, `spending`, `safety`, `housing`, `climate` or `reform`; the race is about office administration.
