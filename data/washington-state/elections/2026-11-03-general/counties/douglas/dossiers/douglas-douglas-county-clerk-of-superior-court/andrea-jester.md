## Background

- Andrea Jester is the Douglas County court facilitator and has worked in the county court system as deputy clerk, jury program administrator and financial collections officer [S1][S2]. Her campaign lists about three years as a deputy clerk, three years running the jury program, two years managing financial operations and nearly two years as court facilitator and collections officer [S2].
- She is administrative director and an EMT with Waterville EMS (Waterville ambulance) [S1][S2]. Earlier she was a bank teller and worked at Auvil Fruit on food-safety compliance and audits [S1].
- She has an AA from Wenatchee Valley College and studied entrepreneurship at Eastern Washington University; she has been a union steward and a Republican precinct committee officer [S1].
- She was raised in Waterville and has lived in Douglas County for more than 26 years [S2].
- She placed second in the August 4, 2026 primary with 3,812 votes to Jenn Biggar's 5,285 [S4].

## Positions

- Priorities: improving customer service and simplifying processes for people "navigating stressful legal situations," strengthening staff training and retention, and increasing operational efficiency and consistency [S1][S2].
- Says "high turnover creates inconsistency and frustration for both staff and the public" in the office and would add training programs, clearer expectations and consistent supervision [S2].
- Pledges to improve access to records and give clear, timely communication with the public [S2].

## Endorsements

- AFSCME Council 2, Local 846-DC, the union that represents the Clerk's Office and other Douglas County departments [S1][S3].
- Waterville Mayor Loyd Smith, a current Douglas County deputy clerk, three former deputy clerks, 911 dispatchers and a Douglas County sheriff's deputy, among others [S3].

## Campaign finance

- PDC reports $4,688.53 raised and $3,970.49 spent (updated 2026-09-17) [S5].
