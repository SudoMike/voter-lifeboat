## Background

- Jenn Biggar is the incumbent Douglas County Clerk. Commissioners appointed her in July 2021 to replace Clerk Tristen Worthen, choosing among three nominees of the Douglas County Republican Party; she had worked five years as a legal assistant in the Douglas County Prosecutor's Office [S5].
- She won the 2022 general election unopposed (11,807 votes) [S6].
- She reports more than 13 years in the local court system [S1][S2]. She is vice president of the Washington State Association of County Clerks and is to serve as its president in 2026-2027 [S1][S2][S4].
- Identity note: NCWLIFE reported in 2021 that she is the daughter-in-law of Douglas County District Court Judge Eric Biggar, who is a separate candidate on this ballot [S5].
- She lives in Waterville and volunteers with Waterville youth sports, swim team and 4-H [S1][S2].
- She led the August 4, 2026 primary with 5,285 votes to Andrea Jester's 3,812 [S7].

## Positions

- Runs on continuing an "efficient, accurate and secure" office and keeping Douglas County "on the cutting edge" of court technology and legislation [S1].
- Plans to finish a new jury system with electronic responses and text reminders, and a document-preservation partnership with the Washington State Archives [S3].

## Record

- Says she recovered about $90,000 from the State of Washington for Douglas County while refunding legal financial obligations after the State v. Blake decision [S2][S3].
- Implemented the TrueFile electronic filing system in 2023, which her campaign says was self-funded by 2025 [S3].
- Began publishing court calendars and Zoom hearing links on the Clerk's website in 2022 [S3].
- Updated billing practices to "maximize accountability and collections" [S2].

## Endorsements

- Retired Douglas County Superior Court Judge John Hotchkiss, retired Clerk Tristen Worthen, Douglas County Coroner Tanner Bateman, Treasurer Felisha Rosales, Rep. Brian Burnett (12th District), Douglas County Director of Public Defense Jesse Collins, and county clerks from across the state [S4].

## Campaign finance

- PDC reports $14,610.79 raised and $13,673.75 spent (updated 2026-09-22) [S8].
