## Background

- Felisha L. Rosales (Prefers Republican Party) of East Wenatchee was appointed Douglas County Treasurer in December 2021 and elected in 2022 [S1].
- She lists 20 years in the financial industry in the public and private sectors and an associate degree from Wenatchee Valley College (2007) [S1].
- She was unopposed in the 2022 general (11,584 votes, 98.2%) [S2] and the August 2026 primary (7,409 votes) [S3].

## Positions

- Says she increased the county's investment interest revenue and put in place banking practices to safeguard taxpayer money; pledges "transparency and sound financial management" [S1].

## Record

- County Treasurer since her December 2021 appointment [S1].
