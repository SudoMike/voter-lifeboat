## What the office does

The treasurer receives all money due the county and pays it out on warrants attested by the auditor [S2]; the Douglas County office collects property taxes, due April 30 and October 31 in 2026 [S3], and manages county investments [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Felisha L. Rosales (Prefers Republican Party), appointed in December 2021 and elected in 2022, is the only candidate [S1][S4].
