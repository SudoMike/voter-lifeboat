## What the office does

The coroner determines the cause and manner of any unexpected, unattended or suspicious death in the county: investigating the scene with law enforcement, notifying next of kin, ordering toxicology and autopsies, and completing death certificates (RCW 68.50.010) [S2].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Tanner Bateman (Prefers Republican Party), coroner since 2023, is the only candidate [S1]. He was unopposed in 2022 [S3].
