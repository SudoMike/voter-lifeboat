## Background

- Tanner Bateman (Prefers Republican Party) has been Douglas County Coroner since 2023 after serving as deputy coroner [S1].
- U.S. Army active-duty veteran; biology degree from Central Washington University; certified medicolegal death investigator; worked with the Kittitas County Coroner and an Ellensburg funeral home while in college [S1].
- Board president of the Chelan Douglas Community Action Council [S1].
- He was unopposed in the 2022 general (11,657 votes, 98.3%) [S2] and the August 2026 primary (7,492 votes) [S3].

## Positions

- Says he brought autopsy-case transport in house to save money over a third-party contractor, and set up tissue and organ donation with SightLife and LifeNet Health [S1].
- Pledges "timely, compassionate, and cost-effective" death investigations if re-elected [S1].

## Record

- Coroner since 2023, running what he calls the "now independent" coroner's office [S1].
