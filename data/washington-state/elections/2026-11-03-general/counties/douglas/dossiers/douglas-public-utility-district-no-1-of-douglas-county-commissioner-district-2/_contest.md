## What this office does

Douglas County PUD's three elected commissioners set policy, budgets and
electric rates for the county-wide public utility, which owns Wells Dam on
the Columbia River, a hydrogen production facility and the Douglas County
Community Network [S1][S2]. Commissioners serve six-year terms and are
nominated from a district but elected by the whole PUD: the 2024
Commissioner No. 1 race was on all 49 Douglas precincts [S3].

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Molly Doneen Simpson, board president and on the commission
  since 2015 [S2], runs on low rates from Wells Dam and the PUD's 2024
  policy making large new loads buy their power on the open market [S1].
- East Wenatchee Water District commissioner Nick Warner argues the PUD is
  holding too much cash, opposes recent rate increases and wants local
  "Public Power Benefit" discounts [S1]. Only two filed, so there was no
  primary; both appear in the general [S4].

## Axes that differentiate

No rubric axis applies to PublicUtility contests, so neither candidate is
scored. The difference voters will see is rates and reserves: Simpson's
"growth pays for growth" large-load policy and fiscal conservatism against
Warner's call to spend down working capital on lower local bills, school and
church rates and rural broadband.
