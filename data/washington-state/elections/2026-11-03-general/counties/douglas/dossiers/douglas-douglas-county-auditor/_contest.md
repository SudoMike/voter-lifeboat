## What the office does

The auditor records deeds and other documents and is the county's chief supervisor of elections [S2]; in Douglas County the office also handles licensing, accounts payable, budgeting and payroll [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Thad Duvall (Prefers Nonpartisan Party), auditor since his 1997 appointment, is the only candidate [S1]. He was unopposed in 2022 [S3].
