## Background

- Thad Duvall (Prefers Nonpartisan Party) was appointed Douglas County Auditor in 1997 and says voters have elected him eight times [S1].
- He lists earlier work in farming, real estate, banking and county appraisal, state and national certification as an election official, and past presidency of the Washington State Association of County Auditors [S1].
- He was unopposed in the 2022 general (12,700 votes, 98.9%) [S2] and the August 2026 primary (7,850 votes) [S3].

## Positions

- His statement urges every eligible voter to register and vote and pledges to serve "with all my skill and ability" [S1]. No policy positions found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- Auditor since 1997; he says the office has conducted more than 80 elections in that time [S1]. The office records documents and supervises elections [S4].
