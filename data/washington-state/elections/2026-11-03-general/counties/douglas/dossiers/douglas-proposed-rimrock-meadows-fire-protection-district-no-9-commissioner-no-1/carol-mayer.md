## Background

- Carol Mayer filed on August 31, 2026 for Commissioner No. 1 of the
  proposed district, with an Ephrata post-office box [S2].
- She says she lives on her family's fourth-generation dryland wheat farm
  bordering Rimrock Meadows, lived at Rimrock Meadows for 23 years and still
  owns a lot there, served about eight years on the Rimrock Meadows board
  and worked five years in its office [S1].
- She describes more than 25 years at Grant County PUD as administrative
  assistant, executive assistant, clerk of the board of commissioners and
  senior manager of supply chain and procurement, plus incident-management
  training and safety-committee service [S1] (her own account; Grant PUD
  records were not checked).
- Her PDC registration shows no contributions or expenditures reported as of
  2026-09-21 [S3].

## Positions

- Says forming a fire district is "essential for ensuring consistent
  emergency response and fire protection" for farmers, residents and the
  Rimrock Meadows community [S1].
- Would apply "administrative proficiency and fiscal accountability" to
  build the district, citing procurement, regulatory-compliance and
  auditing experience [S1].
