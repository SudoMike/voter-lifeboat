## Background

- Ron Cabral filed on August 31, 2026 for Commissioner No. 1 of the proposed
  district, with an Ephrata mailing address [S2]. He says he has owned
  property and lived in the Rimrock Meadows community for 15 years and that
  this is his first run for public office [S1].
- A fourth-generation Washingtonian, he describes work in design,
  production, marketing, transportation, logistics, fabrication and
  communications-industry construction [S1].
- He says he led a campaign to restore the Washington State ferry Puyallup
  and has given input on the state ferry system's future [S1] (his own
  account; not independently confirmed).
- His PDC registration shows no contributions or expenditures reported as of
  2026-09-21 [S3]. His guide entry lists a Facebook page rather than a
  website [S1].

## Positions

- Wants to give Rimrock Meadows "representation and professional Fire
  protection" [S1].
- Would use "all available resources" (county, state, federal, and grants
  from local and national organizations) to build fire protection [S1].
