## What this office does

The three fire commissioners would be the first board of a new fire
protection district under Title 52 RCW, governing fire protection and
emergency services for the proposed Rimrock Meadows district and levying its
property tax [S2]. Rimrock Meadows is a private HOA recreational and
residential community in the Moses Coulee [S6]. The commissioner races and
the formation proposition are on the same ballot for voters inside the
proposed boundary [S3][S5]; the commissioners take office only if the
district is formed [S2]. A special three-day filing period, August 24-26,
was held for these seats [S4].

## Race dynamics

- Position 1 is the only contested seat of the three (Positions 2 and 3
  have one candidate each) [S3].
- Ronald "Ron" Cabral, a Rimrock Meadows property owner for 15 years,
  running for office for the first time, emphasizes using county, state,
  federal and grant resources to build fire protection [S1].
- Carol Mayer, who farms next to Rimrock Meadows, served on the Rimrock
  Meadows board and had more than 25 years at Grant County PUD including as
  clerk of the board, emphasizes administrative and fiscal experience [S1].

## Axes that differentiate

No rubric axis applies to this contest category, so neither candidate is
scored. What differs is background: Cabral's community-advocacy and
private-sector record against Mayer's public-utility administration and HOA
board experience.
