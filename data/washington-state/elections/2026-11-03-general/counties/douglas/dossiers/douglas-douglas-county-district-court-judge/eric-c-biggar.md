## Background

- Eric C. Biggar has been Douglas County District Court Judge since 2018 [S1].
- He ran a private practice in public defense and general civil work from 1988 to 1991, then served in the Douglas County Prosecutor's Office as deputy and chief criminal deputy prosecuting attorney until 2014 [S1].
- Bachelor's degree in business management from Eastern Washington University; J.D. from Gonzaga Law School [S1].
- He coached youth sports and high school basketball in Waterville [S1].
- He was unopposed in the 2022 general (11,156 votes, 98.6%) [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says his 30-plus years in the courtroom give him the experience "needed to maintain a healthy justice system in Douglas County" [S1].
