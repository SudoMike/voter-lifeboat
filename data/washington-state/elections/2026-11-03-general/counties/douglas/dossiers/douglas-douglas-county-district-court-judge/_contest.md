## What the office does

The district court hears misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor cases, traffic infractions, civil cases up to $100,000, small claims up to $10,000, civil protection orders, name changes and felony preliminary hearings; its probation department monitors sentencing conditions [S2]. Douglas County has one district court judge, elected county-wide [S3].

## Race dynamics

Judge Eric C. Biggar, on the bench since 2018, is the only candidate [S1]. He was unopposed in 2022 [S3].
