## What it does

Proposition No. 1 is a one-year excess maintenance and operations levy of $50,000 for Douglas County Cemetery District #2, at approximately $0.22 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 (Resolution No. 2026-001, adopted August 4, 2026) [S1][S2][S3].

- The district "provides general maintenance and operations, and cemetery services, for 7 Cemeteries and 1 Mausoleum within the District" [S1]. Its office is at 218 E Elm St, Waterville [S3], and last year's statement says it operates "the mausoleum and shop/office for the Waterville Cemetery" [S4].
- The resolution says the district "will require an excess levy for additional funds for continuing maintenance and operations of all Cemeteries located within the District in 2027" [S3].
- RCW 84.52.052 lets a cemetery district levy above its regular limits when voters authorize it [S10].

## Cost

- $50,000 in 2027, about $0.22 per $1,000 of assessed value [S1][S3].
- On a $300,000 property that is about $66 for the year (computed for this dossier from [S1]).

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

A recurring one-year levy that has not passed every year:

| Collection year | Amount | Approximate rate per $1,000 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 (this measure) | $50,000 | $0.22 | on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot [S1] |
| 2026 | $60,000 | $0.25 | 64.33% yes (348-193), Nov. 2025 [S4][S6] |
| 2025 | $50,000 | $0.25 | 54.88% yes (478-393), Nov. 2024 [S5][S7]; the district later wrote "We are currently without this levy in 2025" [S4] |

- November 2022: two district propositions drew 56.06% and 56.46% yes [S8]. November 2020: 60.86% yes [S9]. This research did not retrieve those years' amounts.
- The 2027 request is $10,000 smaller than 2026's, at a lower estimated rate [S1][S4].

## Arguments for

The statement in favor (Jenna Dixon, Sandra Stoddard, Terri Thomsen) says the levy would let the district "keep providing affordable Cemetery services" and irrigation, keep up care of the rural cemeteries and "resume normal irrigation with upgraded systems." It closes: "Cemeteries are vital to our community" [S1]. In 2025 the district said going without the 2025 levy "resulted in cuts to irrigation and improvements" [S4].

## Arguments against

No statement against was filed. The guide says no one came forward to serve on the committee against or write a statement [S1]. No news coverage was found.

## Endorsements and campaign

- Committee for: Jenna Dixon, Sandra Stoddard, Terri Thomsen [S1]. No committee against [S1].

## Lean notes

- `taxes`: a yes vote renews the district's recurring one-year maintenance levy, smaller than 2026's ($50,000 vs $60,000) [S1][S4]. A renewal, not a new tax: +1.
- `spending`: routine cemetery upkeep and irrigation [S1]; no mapping.
