## What it does

Proposition No. 1 is a one-year excess levy of $80,000 for maintenance and operations of Douglas County Hospital District #2, at approximately $0.36 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1][S2]. The board adopted it as Resolution No. 91 on July 28, 2026 [S1].

- The district "provides medical services to Waterville residents including a clinic, which is contracted with Wenatchee Valley Medical Center, and its own ambulance services" [S1]. Its resolution letterhead reads "DCHD2 Waterville Ambulance" [S3].
- The resolution says the district "will require additional funds for maintenance/operation during 2027" and places the levy under RCW 84.52.050 through 84.52.056, to be levied in 2026 for collection in 2027 [S3].
- RCW 84.52.052 lets a public hospital district levy above its regular limits when voters authorize it [S10].

## Cost

- $80,000 in 2027, about $0.36 per $1,000 of assessed value [S1].
- On a $300,000 property that is about $108 for the year (computed for this dossier from [S1]).

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

This renews a one-year levy the district puts on the ballot every year. It is not a new tax.

| Collection year | Amount | Approximate rate per $1,000 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 (this measure) | $80,000 | $0.36 | on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot [S1] |
| 2026 | $80,000 | $0.36 | 70.13% yes (432-184), Nov. 2025 [S4][S6] |
| 2025 | $80,000 | $0.36 | 62.03% yes (647-396), Nov. 2024 [S5][S7] |

District voters also approved its one-year levy in November 2022 (67.85%, 614-291) and November 2020 (66.88%, 749-371) [S8][S9]; this research did not retrieve those years' amounts.
