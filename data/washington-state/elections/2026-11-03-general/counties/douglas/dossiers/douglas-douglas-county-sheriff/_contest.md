## What this office does

The sheriff is the county's elected chief law enforcement officer and runs the Douglas County Sheriff's Office: patrol, investigations, emergency response coordination and school resource officers are the duties both candidates' statements and platforms address [S1]. The winner takes office January 1, 2027 for a four-year term [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

- Open seat in practice: Sheriff Kevin Morris retired March 1, 2026, and commissioners appointed Undersheriff Tyler Caille to serve until December 31, 2026 [S2]. Commissioners tied the appointment to continuity and criticized Senate Bill 5974, which requires sheriff candidates to pass state background checks before they appear on the ballot [S2][S5].
- Both finalists are Republicans [S1]. In the August primary Caille led with 4,936 votes, Chelan County Sheriff's Sgt. Adam Musgrove had 2,780, and former Quincy Police Chief Kieth Siebert, who was eliminated, had 2,028 [S3][S4].
- Caille runs on continuity, with the endorsement of the three living former Douglas County sheriffs; Musgrove runs on change from outside the office and has the Douglas County Republican Central Committee's endorsement [S1].

## Axes that differentiate

- `experience`: the clearest difference. Caille is the sitting appointed sheriff and longtime undersheriff; Musgrove is a first-time candidate from another agency running against "the status quo."
- `safety`: both are enforcement-oriented with mental health partnerships; Musgrove's platform is more explicitly zero-tolerance (gangs, "Broken Windows").
- `spending`, `taxes`, `housing`, `climate`, `reform`: little or no evidence from either candidate.
