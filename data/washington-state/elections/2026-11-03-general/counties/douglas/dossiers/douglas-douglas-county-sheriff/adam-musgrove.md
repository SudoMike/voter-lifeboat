## Background

- Adam Musgrove is a sergeant and night-shift supervisor with the Chelan County Sheriff's Office, where he has worked since 2012 [S5][S6]. Sheriff Brian Burnett named him chief of patrol in 2020 [S7]; he served in that post under Burnett's administration [S5][S6].
- He began in law enforcement with the Sedro-Woolley Police Department in 2006 [S5][S6].
- His statement lists 25 years in the U.S. Navy, retiring as a senior chief, and work as a field training officer instructor for the Criminal Justice Training Commission [S1]. KPQ described "a ten-year stint in the U.S. Navy" [S5]; the sources do not reconcile the two figures (his statement may count reserve service; not confirmed).
- He has a BA in criminal justice from Columbia College [S1]. He founded the nonprofit Santa's Stars, is president of Washington Fraternal Order of Police Lodge 17, and sits on the Douglas County Veterans Advisory Board [S1].
- His campaign says he co-founded the Chelan County Behavioral Health Unit [S3].
- This is his first run for elected office [S1]. He placed second in the August 4, 2026 primary with 2,780 votes to Tyler Caille's 4,936 [S9].

## Positions

### Public safety and enforcement
- Says he is "not interested in maintaining the status quo" and wants "meaningful change"; priorities are public safety and protection of constitutional rights, "firm and fair crime reduction," and faster response times [S1].
- Supports "Broken Windows" policing and "zero-tolerance" for gang-type behavior and crime, to be "handled swiftly and to the fullest extent of the law" [S2].
- On drugs and opioids: community education, holding dealers accountable and working with federal and state partners "to fight the war on drugs" [S2].
- Supports school resource officers [S2].

### Mental health
- Calls mental health "one of the biggest issues" facing law enforcement and says "we cannot just pass the issue onto the hospital or the jail" [S2]; would expand collaborative mental health and veteran outreach programs [S3].

### Constitutional rights
- Says he "will not enforce laws that infringe upon your rights and government overreach" [S2].

### Agency management and spending
- Would decentralize leadership and build a culture where deputies "are not afraid to make decisions" to improve recruiting and retention [S1][S2].
- Describes "a fiscally responsible mindset" and "realistic goals" with transparency [S1].
- Wants Douglas County to become a training hub [S2].

## Endorsements

- Douglas County Republican Central Committee (19-1 vote), Chelan County Republican Central Committee, and Teamsters Local Union 760 [S4].
- Individual endorsements from Navy veterans, first responders and retired Sedro-Woolley police officials; former Chelan County Undersheriff John Wisemore [S4].

## Campaign finance

- PDC reports $12,850 raised and $8,540.62 spent (updated 2026-09-21) [S8].
