## Background

- Tyler Caille is the appointed Douglas County Sheriff. The Board of Commissioners appointed him effective March 1, 2026 to serve the rest of retiring Sheriff Kevin Morris's term, which ends December 31, 2026 [S5]. He was sworn in by Commissioner Dan Sutton [S9].
- Before the appointment he was undersheriff for seven years, acting as the department's chief in Morris's absence [S6]. He reports nearly 20 years with the Douglas County Sheriff's Office, including patrol and administrative leadership, and earlier service as a reserve deputy [S1].
- He is also fire chief of Douglas County Fire District No. 5, based in his hometown of Mansfield, and has served as chief since 2004, with 30 years in fire and EMS service [S1][S6][S7]. His statement says he continues in that role while sheriff [S1].
- He served three terms on the Mansfield School Board and was a volunteer board member of the Sage Advocacy Center [S1]. He holds a bachelor of applied science in criminal justice administration from Waldorf University [S1]. He is a fifth-generation Douglas County resident from Mansfield [S1][S2].
- In the August 4, 2026 primary he finished first with 4,936 votes, ahead of Adam Musgrove (2,780) and former Quincy Police Chief Kieth Siebert (2,028) [S12].

## Positions

### Public safety and enforcement
- Lists priorities of "lawful enforcement guided by policy and constitutional rights," focused attention on illegal drug use and gang activity, and prevention and visible presence suited to rural and urban areas [S3].
- Would plan staffing and deployment to strengthen countywide coverage, train for major incidents and disasters, and coordinate with fire, EMS and dispatch [S3].
- Says he will keep school resource officers in Douglas County schools and plan threat identification and prevention with schools [S3].

### Mental health and deputy wellness
- Pledges wellness resources for deputies and families, continued partnerships with crisis and behavioral-health professionals, and advocacy for community access to behavioral health services [S3].

### Transparency and accountability
- Promises regular community feedback opportunities and town hall meetings, clear public communication about priorities, and "a system of accountability within the Sheriff's Office" [S3].
- Describes his approach as "preserve what works, strengthen what matters, and prepare for the future" and running on continuity of the office's leadership [S1][S2].

## Record

- As appointed sheriff, launched a Sheriff's Office mobile app on October 1, 2026 with news, alerts, sex offender listings, concealed pistol license applications and a complaint form; he said "the community deserves clear, timely information they can rely on" [S10].
- Commissioners said they appointed him "to maintain consistency and create the least amount of disruption to the organization of the office" [S5][S8].

## Endorsements

- Former Douglas County sheriffs Kevin Morris, Harvey Gjesdal and Dan LaRoche [S4][S6][S7].
- His site lists 22 Washington county sheriffs (including Chelan County's Mike Morrison and Spokane County's John Nowels) and the mayors of Bridgeport, Mansfield, Rock Island and Waterville [S4].

## Campaign finance

- PDC reports $37,135 raised and $28,829.53 spent (updated 2026-10-04) [S11].
