## What the office does

Fire commissioners govern a fire protection district: they set its budget and property tax levy and oversee fire protection and emergency services. This district is being formed at this election; if voters approve the formation proposition on the same ballot, the winners become its first three-member board [S2][S3].

## Race dynamics

Kurt M. Blanchard is the only candidate for Position 2 [S1][S3]. The seat exists only if the formation proposition passes.
