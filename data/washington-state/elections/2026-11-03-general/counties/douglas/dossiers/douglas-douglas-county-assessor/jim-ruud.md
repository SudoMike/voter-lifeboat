## Background

- Jim Ruud (Prefers Republican Party) of Waterville describes himself as a fourth-generation Douglas County resident with 20 years as County Assessor [S1].
- Real estate managing broker; graduate of Washington State University; member of the Waterville Lions Club and a volunteer at the Badger Mountain ski hill [S1].
- He was unopposed in the 2022 general election (11,939 votes, 97.9%) [S2] and in the August 2026 primary (7,533 votes) [S3].

## Positions

- His statement commits to "fair, accurate, and transparent property assessments" and an efficiently run office [S1]. No policy positions found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
