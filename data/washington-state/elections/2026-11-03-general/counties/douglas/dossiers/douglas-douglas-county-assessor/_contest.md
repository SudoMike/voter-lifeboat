## What the office does

The assessor values all taxable real and personal property in the county for property tax purposes, at 100% of market value, revaluing Douglas County properties every year; valuations can be appealed to the County Board of Equalization, and the office administers senior, disability and open-space exemptions [S2].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Jim Ruud (Prefers Republican Party), who says he has 20 years as County Assessor, is the only candidate [S1]. He was also unopposed in 2022 [S3].
