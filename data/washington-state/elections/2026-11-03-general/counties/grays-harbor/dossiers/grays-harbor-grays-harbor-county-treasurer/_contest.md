## What the office does
The treasurer is custodian of all county funds and acts as the "bank" for other local governments, including school, fire and water districts. The office collects property taxes (including foreclosure and distraint on delinquent accounts), special assessments, excise and gambling taxes. It accounts for revenue for the state, county and junior taxing districts, manages county cash flow and investments, administers debt financing and oversees banking services [S2][S3]. It is a partisan county office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics
Shawn Hill (prefers Democratic Party) was chief deputy treasurer for about nine years. In July 2025 the Board of County Commissioners appointed him treasurer by a 2-1 vote after Treasurer Ken Albert retired. He was one of three nominees the Grays Harbor Democrats submitted under the state's same-party vacancy rule [S3]. This is his first election for the office [S1][S3]. His challenger, Wes Cormier (prefers Republican Party), served two terms as a Grays Harbor County commissioner and earlier spent seven years as a senior real estate appraiser [S1][S5]. Hill led the certified primary with 9,442 votes (53.37%) to Cormier's 8,231 (46.53%) [S4]. In the Daily World's written Q&A, Hill emphasized trust, staff continuity, payment options and security. Cormier emphasized upholding state law, customer service, accessibility and transparency, and a "streamlined" office [S5].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. record:** Hill has about a decade inside the treasurer's office, the last year-plus as appointed treasurer. Cormier offers eight years of elected budget and policy oversight as a commissioner plus appraisal work, but no time in the treasurer's office.
2. **Spending:** Cormier's commissioner record includes votes against filling unbudgeted positions and the view that "every budget is negotiable." Hill speaks of careful stewardship, healthy reserves and responsible debt management.
3. **Taxes:** Cormier says he never voted to raise property taxes and led repeal of the county admission tax. Hill has no tax-policy statements. The treasurer collects taxes but does not set them.
4. **Reform:** Cormier kept a two-term pledge as commissioner and in 2020 said he accepted no PAC, special-interest or lobbyist money. Hill is endorsed by his party and a public-employee union local.
5. **Housing, safety, climate:** no evidence relevant to this race.
