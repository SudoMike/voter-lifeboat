## What the office does
The assessor values all taxable property in the county as the state Legislature requires and calculates the levies set by taxing districts and approved by voters. The assessor does not set or raise tax amounts [S2]. In Grays Harbor the office also runs the Taxsifter parcel-records site and the MapGeo mapping platform [S1][S3]. It is a partisan county office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics
Dan Lindgren has been assessor since 2015 and worked in the office as an appraiser from 2007 [S1][S3]. He won earlier terms as a Democrat and announced in April 2026 that he was changing his party preference to Republican. He said the office remains a nonpartisan, professional one [S3]. His challenger, Josh Nuxoll (prefers Independent Party), is a senior appraiser in Lindgren's office and has not held elected office [S1]. Lindgren led the certified primary with 9,629 votes (55.23%) to Nuxoll's 7,775 (44.60%) [S4]. Nuxoll's campaign site says the office's core duties "have been neglected causing unfair tax burdens" in recent years. It does not give specifics [S5].

Research note: The Daily World ran written candidate Q&As for other contested county races on Oct. 7, 2026. A site search found none for the assessor race as of 2026-10-08.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** an 11-year incumbent with 19 years in the office, against a staff appraiser of four years who argues the office needs new leadership.
2. **Taxes:** Lindgren says he does not support unnecessary tax increases and has worked against property-tax-increase bills through the state assessors' association. Nuxoll's tax language is about fair valuation and helping owners save, not about tax levels.
3. **Spending, housing, safety, climate, reform:** no evidence in this race.
