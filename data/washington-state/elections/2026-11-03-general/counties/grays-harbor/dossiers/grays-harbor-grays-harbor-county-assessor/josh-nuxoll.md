## Background

Josh Nuxoll prefers the Independent Party and filed for assessor in May 2026 [S1][S4]. He was born and raised in Elma [S3]. He is a senior appraiser in the Grays Harbor County Assessor's Office, where he has worked as an appraiser for the past four years. Before that he was a Grays Harbor County custodian [S1][S3]. He is a shop steward for Local 275 [S1]. Earlier he was a wilderness trail technician and a wildland firefighter/first responder with the California Conservation Corps [S1][S3]. He is an IAAO-certified appraiser and attended Shasta County Community College [S1]. He has not held elected office [S1].

He placed second in the certified August 2026 primary with 7,775 votes (44.60%) to Dan Lindgren's 9,629 (55.23%) [S5]. He spoke at a Sept. 16 candidate forum hosted by Grays Harbor Young Professionals in Aberdeen [S6].

Identity: PDC registers the candidate as Joshua Aaron Nuxoll, running for Grays Harbor County Assessor as an Independent [S7].

## Positions

### Property assessment and office administration
- "I believe that the most important role for the Assessor is to ensure all properties are assessed fairly and equitably" [S1][S2].
- His campaign site says that "in recent years, these essential duties have been neglected causing unfair tax burdens across Grays Harbor County," and that he can "correct these issues and lead towards restoring public trust." The site does not say which duties or properties he means [S2].
- Promises to be "the first one in and the last one out of the office each day" and to give "complete transparency and customer service" [S1].
- Says he has built tools and guides in the office that streamlined workflow and improved efficiency and accuracy [S1].
- Says he meets property owners face to face and guides "citizens to save every possible tax dollar" [S3].
