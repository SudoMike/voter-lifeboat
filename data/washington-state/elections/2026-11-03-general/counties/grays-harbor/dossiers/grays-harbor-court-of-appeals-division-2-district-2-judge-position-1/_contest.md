## What the office does

The Court of Appeals has appellate jurisdiction over nearly all cases, with exceptions set by statute [S2]. In practice it hears nearly all appeals from the superior courts; its 22 judges decide about 1,500 cases a year, according to the candidate's statement [S1]. District 2 of Division 2 elects two judges, who represent six counties of the west Puget Sound, the coast and the Olympic Peninsula, Grays Harbor among them [S1]. The term is six years [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Judge Erik D. Price, on the Court of Appeals since 2021, is the only candidate [S1]. In 2022 he was also unopposed; Grays Harbor voters gave him 17,622 votes (97.45%) [S3]. The contest is uncontested.
