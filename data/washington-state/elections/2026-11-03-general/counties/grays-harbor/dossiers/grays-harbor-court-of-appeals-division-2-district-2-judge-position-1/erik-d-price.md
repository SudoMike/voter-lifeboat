## Background

- Judge on the Court of Appeals, Division II, since 2021 and Acting Chief Judge since 2026 [S1].
- Thurston County Superior Court judge from 2012 to 2021, including presiding judge from 2020 to 2021 and three years as chief judge of the Adult Drug Court [S1].
- Chaired the Thurston County Pre-trial Justice Improvement Team and co-chaired the Felony Leadership Improvement Project [S1].
- Nineteen years as a civil litigation attorney in private practice; former chair of the WSBA Judicial Recommendation Committee; past president of the Thurston County Bar Association [S1].
- J.D. with honors from the University of Washington School of Law (1993) and B.A. cum laude from the University of Washington (1988, Phi Beta Kappa) [S1].
- Board member of the Thurston County Dispute Resolution Center from 2012 to 2018 [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statement)

- Says each Court of Appeals judge decides about 200 cases a year and writes 60 to 70 opinions, and commits "to continuing to decide cases fairly and consistently with the law" [S1].
- Describes himself as a fifth-generation Washingtonian and lifelong resident of the district [S1].
