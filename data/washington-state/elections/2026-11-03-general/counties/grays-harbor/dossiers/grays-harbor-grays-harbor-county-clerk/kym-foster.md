## Background

- Kym Foster (Prefers Practical Democrat Party) has been the elected Grays Harbor County Clerk since 2019 and is running unopposed [S1][S6].
- She won the office in the 2018 general election, defeating then-chief deputy clerk Janice Louthan by 771 votes, and was re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S2]. In 2022 she ran as "Prefers Democratic Party" and received 18,740 votes (93.51%) [S3].
- She graduated from Montesano High School, worked a variety of jobs on the East Coast, and was a nutrition educator with the WSU Extension office in Elma for nine years. Before running she worked part time for attorney Vini Samuel, now a Superior Court judge [S2].
- She volunteers in youth sports (football, Little League, wrestling), with Miss Grays Harbor and community events such as the Elma Wine and Seafood Festival, and is a member of P.E.O. [S1].

## Positions

### Office operations

- Says her office has cut processing times, enters many documents within 24 hours, returns calls, and brought in electronic filing done in-house "to save taxpayer dollars" [S2].
- Says she made changing the office culture her first priority on taking office [S2]. Her statement describes the office as "more responsive. More efficient. More accountable." [S1].
- Says she believes in "practical, common-sense solutions, fiscal responsibility, and doing the job right" [S1].

## Record

- County Clerk since 2019 [S1].
- **Jury administration dispute (2025).** In February 2025 her office told District Court it would stop summoning juries for District Court trials, saying her office had one person handling jury work alongside other duties and had processed 48,000 prospective jurors in a year to District Court's 4,500 [S4]. In March 2025 she agreed to keep providing the service, with District Court and Superior Court reimbursing the costs. She said she changed her mind after seeing District Court's proposal to spend about $130,000 to $140,000 to do the work itself [S4][S5].
