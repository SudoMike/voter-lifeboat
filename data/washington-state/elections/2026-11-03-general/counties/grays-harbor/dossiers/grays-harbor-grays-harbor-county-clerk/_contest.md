## What the office does

The county clerk is the clerk of the superior court. State law requires the clerk to keep the court's records: appearances and filings in each case, the docket, witness and juror records, and the court's daily proceedings, verdicts, orders and judgments [S2]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Kym Foster (Prefers Practical Democrat Party), clerk since 2019, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1][S3][S4]. The contest is uncontested.
