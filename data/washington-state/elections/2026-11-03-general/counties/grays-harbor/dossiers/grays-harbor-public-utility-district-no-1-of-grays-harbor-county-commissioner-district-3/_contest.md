## What the office does

Grays Harbor PUD is a not-for-profit public utility governed by a three-member, nonpartisan elected board. Commissioners appoint the general manager, set rates, establish policy, approve budgets and direct the utility's operations. They serve six-year terms [S1][S3]. Commissioner District 3 covers Hoquiam, Ocean Shores, North Beach and North Grays Harbor [S4].

## How the seat is elected

Only voters in Commissioner District 3 may vote in the primary to nominate candidates, and a commissioner must live in the district. In the general election, voters of the entire PUD elect the District 3 commissioner [S2]. In practice the seat is on ballots across Grays Harbor County: in the 2022 general, the District 3 race drew 18,623 votes in the county's results, about as many as the countywide District Court #2 race (18,521) [S6]. This year only one candidate filed, so there was no primary [S5].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Commissioner Jon Martin, elected unopposed in 2022 with 18,150 votes (97.46%) [S6], is the only candidate [S1][S5]. He submitted no voters'-guide statement [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
