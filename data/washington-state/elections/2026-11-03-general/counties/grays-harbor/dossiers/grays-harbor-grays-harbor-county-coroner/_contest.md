## What the office does

State law gives the county coroner jurisdiction over the bodies of people who die suddenly without recent medical attendance, by unnatural, violent or unknown causes, under suspicious circumstances, in jail, or unclaimed [S2]. In Grays Harbor the office investigates deaths and determines their cause; autopsies are contracted out, typically to Thurston County [S3]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent George J. Kelley (States No Party Preference), coroner since January 2023, is running unopposed [S1][S3][S4]. He submitted no voters'-guide statement [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
