## Background

- George J. Kelley (States No Party Preference) is the incumbent Grays Harbor County Coroner and is running unopposed. He submitted no voters'-guide statement [S1][S6].
- He was elected in November 2022 over Bob Kegel, 16,444 votes (60.63%) to 10,547 (38.89%), and took office Jan. 1, 2023 [S2][S5].
- He grew up in Grays Harbor, enlisted as a Marine Corps military policeman, then spent about 36 years in law enforcement in Washington; his father was a Grays Harbor deputy sheriff [S2].

## Positions

### Office operations

- Said on taking office that he wanted the coroner's office to be more visible and engaged with the public and to "pull back the veil" on its work [S2].
- Says his preference is for the office to respond on scene to sudden and unexpected deaths, which frees police and fire crews to return to service [S2][S3].
- Says every death should be treated with dignity, "whether it's the unhoused person on the street to someone who owns a mansion" [S2].

## Record

- In 2023 the office moved out of space in a Harbor Regional Health building to a county building. Kelley said the old space would not have met accreditation standards. The office also added three employees [S3].
- The office has worked to find final resting places for about 90 sets of unclaimed cremated remains, including veterans [S3].
- He reports monthly to the county commissioners. The office recorded 822 deaths in 2025 and carried out 259 on-scene investigations and 64 post-mortem examinations that year. It also worked with Othram to identify remains from 1995 and 2006 cases [S4].
