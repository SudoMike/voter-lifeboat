## What the office does

District court has jurisdiction, alongside superior court, over misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors committed in the county, with sentences capped at a $5,000 fine and one year in jail unless a statute provides otherwise. It also hears traffic infractions, holds preliminary hearings and approves bail [S2]. The seat is countywide and carries a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Judge Andrea Vingo, on the District Court bench for four years, is the only candidate, so the race skipped the August primary [S1][S3]. The contest is uncontested.
