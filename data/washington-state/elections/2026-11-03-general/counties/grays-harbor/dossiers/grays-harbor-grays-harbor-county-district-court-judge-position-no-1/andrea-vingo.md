## Background

- Andrea Vingo is the incumbent judge in District Court Position 1, running unopposed. She reports four years as a District Court judge, 30 years as an attorney and 19 years of judicial service, including as a District Court pro tem judge and Hoquiam Municipal Court judge [S1].
- She holds a J.D. from Seattle University School of Law and a B.A. from the University of California, Davis [S1]. The Daily World reported that she graduated from law school in 1996 [S3].
- When the Hoquiam mayor appointed her municipal court judge in December 2017, she told the city council she had worked as a prosecuting attorney locally, handling violent felonies and sex offenses. She also said she had served for a time as Island County prosecutor, had worked in the Attorney General's Office civil rights unit and was then a staff attorney at the state Department of Health [S3].
- She won the seat in November 2022 over Geoff Arnold, 13,128 votes (54.12%) to 10,912 (44.98%) [S4].
- Her community service includes Judges in the Classroom [S1].
- The supplemental county pamphlet carries the same statement as the VoteWA guide [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statement)

- Says she will keep "providing fair, efficient, and accessible justice," treat every person in court with dignity and respect, and work with court staff, law enforcement and community partners on "improving public safety, supporting accountability, and connecting people with the services they need" [S1].
- Pledges to "modernize court processes while being a careful steward of public resources" [S1].

## Record

- In March 2025 she announced the court's new online services, including online protection-order petitions and email requests for mitigation or contested hearings [S7].
- In the February-March 2025 dispute over jury administration, she told the county commissioners the clerk's office had "shut down" District Court's attempts to resolve it, and pressed County Clerk Kym Foster to confirm on the record that her office would keep summoning District Court jurors. Foster agreed, with District Court reimbursing the costs [S5][S6].
- No published rulings were reviewed.
