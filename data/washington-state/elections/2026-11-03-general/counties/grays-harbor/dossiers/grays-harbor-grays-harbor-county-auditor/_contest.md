## What the office does
The county auditor records deeds and other instruments, keeps accounts with the county treasurer, prepares the county's annual financial statements to State Auditor standards, issues the warrant register and serves as clerk of the board of county commissioners [S2]. The auditor is also, by law, the supervisor of all primaries and elections in the county [S3]. The Department of Licensing may appoint the county auditor as its agent for vehicle and vessel licensing, overseeing local subagents under a standard contract [S4][S7]. It is a partisan county office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics
The seat is open: incumbent Joesph R. MacLean (R) did not seek reelection [S5]. MacLean was elected in 2018 and re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S10]. His office's licensing contract drew a second breach-of-contract notice from the Department of Licensing in January 2026 [S6]. In March the department issued a Level III breach notice and terminated the contract, and the commissioners appointed the county administrator as licensing agent [S7]. Commissioner Georgia Miller then called for MacLean's resignation, citing a delay of more than 100 days in submitting materials to the State Auditor that resulted in findings [S8].

Both candidates are first-time candidates from banking backgrounds: Charlee Paull (Prefers Republican Party) and Brielle S Joyce (Prefers Democratic Party) [S1][S5]. Both promise timely audits and the return of licensing to the auditor's office [S1][S5]. Paull led the two-candidate primary 9,835 (55.44%) to 7,885 (44.45%) [S9].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience:** neither has held elected office. Paull stresses 19 years in banking and mortgage lending, professional training and preparation for the office [S5]. Joyce frames her run as an everyday citizen stepping up [S1].
2. **Spending:** both emphasize clean, timely audits and fiscal reporting. Joyce also wants added staffing, cross-training and back-up across the office [S5].
3. **Reform:** Joyce's statement says "it's time to get money out of politics" [S1]. Paull says she will run the office for residents, "not for any party or political agenda" [S5].
4. **Taxes, housing, safety, climate:** no evidence in this race; the office does not set policy in these areas.
