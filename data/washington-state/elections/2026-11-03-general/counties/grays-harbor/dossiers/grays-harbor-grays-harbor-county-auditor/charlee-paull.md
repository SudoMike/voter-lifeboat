## Background

Charlee Paull prefers the Republican Party [S1]. She was born and raised in Oakville, calls herself a sixth-generation Harborite, and lives on a farm near where she grew up [S1][S2][S6]. She has worked 19 years in banking and financial services: she started as a part-time teller, then became a personal banker and bank manager, and has worked exclusively in mortgages since 2018 [S1][S7]. She is a 2002 graduate of the Washington Homeschool Organization and lists professional education through the Washington Finance Officers Association and the Municipal Research and Services Center [S1]. She sits on the NeighborWorks of Grays Harbor board. Past roles include 4-H leader, Greater Grays Harbor Inc. ambassador, Olympia Master Builders, and the Grays Harbor/Pacific County Farm Bureau board [S1].

She has not held elected office; this is the first public office she has sought [S1][S6]. She announced her candidacy in November 2025 at Oakville High School [S7]. She won the August primary with 9,835 votes (55.44%) to Brielle Joyce's 7,885 (44.45%) [S8]. She took part in a Republican candidate forum at the county fairgrounds in July 2026 [S9].

## Positions

### Auditor's office operations
- Says her first priority is to bring Department of Licensing services back under the auditor's office [S6].
- Promises "more efficient and transparent fiscal reporting" and "timely, accurate audits" [S6]. Her statement says delayed audits, unclear records and inconsistent communication "can erode trust" [S1][S3].
- Says her mortgage and recording background gives her a detailed understanding of the recording department [S6].
- Says that over the past year she has taken professional training, attended county meetings and toured peer offices to prepare [S6].

### Elections
- Says she will run the office "for the people of Grays Harbor, not for any party or political agenda," and that election administration "must follow state and federal laws exactly as written" [S6].
- Lists secure processes, clear communication and voter confidence as goals [S6]. Her campaign slogan is "Community First – Integrity Always" [S2][S6].
