## ExplanatoryStatement
EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

Proposition 1 authorizes school construction bonds to make capital improvements to the McCleary School. Proposition 1 will improve student, staff and community safety and security, improve and expand educational opportunities and programs, and provide safe, secure, and adequate facilities for learning.

Proposition 1 will allow McCleary School District to make safety and security upgrades at the McCleary School, such as constructing a single-point entry security vestibule and upgrading door locks, keycard access and the fire alarm system. Proposition 1 will improve McCleary School facilities and grounds, such as building exterior, HVAC, parking area, drainage system, grounds and access improvements. Proposition 1 will also upgrade outdoor spaces and modernize playgrounds to create safe and accessible student play areas with reduced hazards. Further information is available at mccleary.wednet.edu.

The $12,800,000 of general obligation bonds will be repaid from annual excess property taxes sufficient to pay bond debt service over a maximum term of 21 years. Exemptions from taxes may be available, contact the Grays Harbor County Assessor 360-249-4121 or Mason County Assessor 360-427-9670.
