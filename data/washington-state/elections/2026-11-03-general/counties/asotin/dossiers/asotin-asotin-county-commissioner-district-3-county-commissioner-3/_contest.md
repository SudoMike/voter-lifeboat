## What the office does

The three county commissioners are Asotin County's legislative authority: they adopt the budget and oversee county roads, buildings and the jail [S3]. A commissioner must live in the district, but all county voters elect each commissioner in the general election [S4]; the 2020 and 2022 Commissioner 3 races were counted in all 26 precincts [S5][S6], while only District 3 voted in the August primary (7 units) [S7]. Four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Commissioner Chuck Whitman (States No Party Preference) is the only candidate [S1]. He was elected unopposed in 2020 (9,099 votes) and 2022 (6,409) [S5][S6].
