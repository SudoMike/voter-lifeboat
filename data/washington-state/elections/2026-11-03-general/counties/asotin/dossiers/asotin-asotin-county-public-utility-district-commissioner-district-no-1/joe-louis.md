## Background

- **Career on the water system.** Hired by Washington Water Power as a meter reader in 1975; moved to the Clarkston Water Department in 1976. Stayed on as a crewman, and later crew leader, after General Water Works bought the system in 1983. Continued after the PUD bought the system in 1987; promoted to field foreman in 1999, directing the crew, job setup, well maintenance and customer relations [S1][S2].
- **Certifications.** Water Distribution Manager III and Cross Connection Specialist [S1].
- **Residence.** Asotin County resident for 49 years; Clarkston address [S1].
- **Previous run.** Joseph Lee Louis (Joe Louis) also filed for this seat in 2020 [S4] and lost to Judy Ridge, 3,682 to 5,472 [S5].
- **Campaign finance.** Files under the PDC mini-reporting option [S3].

## Positions

- Wants to work "in conjunction with Asotin County PUD in continuing good service to our customers" [S1].
- Says the PUD needs to "continue updating water services, mains, maintain wells and water tanks" [S1].
- Believes his 40 years with the water department would help him as a commissioner [S1].
