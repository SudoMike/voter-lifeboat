## Background

- **Office.** Prosecuting attorney for three and a half years; his statement refers to "the loss of Mr. Nichols in May of 2023" [S1]. Benjamin C. Nichols was elected prosecutor in 2022 [S4]; Liedkie was elected unopposed in 2024 [S5].
- **Career.** More than 25 years as a prosecuting attorney [S1].
- **Education.** Clarkston High School (1992); B.A. in biology, Lewis-Clark State College (1997); J.D., University of Idaho (2000) [S1].
- **Campaign finance.** PDC mini-reporting option [S7].

## Positions

- Says the office has been short of attorneys since 2023 and that the county commissioners cut funding for a third deputy prosecutor position, "leaving us short handed for the foreseeable future" [S1].
- Says he "will continue to make community safety the number one priority" [S1].
