## What the office does

The treasurer receives and disburses county money and collects property taxes [S3]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Treasurer Holly Steiner (States No Party Preference) is the only candidate [S1]. She was re-elected unopposed in 2022 (6,691 votes) [S4] and received 4,794 votes in the August primary [S5].
