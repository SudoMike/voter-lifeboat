## What the office does

The district court hears misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors and city ordinance violations [S3], plus civil cases, for Asotin County and the Cities of Clarkston and Asotin [S1]. Nonpartisan, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Judge Tina L. Kernan is the only candidate [S1]. She was re-elected unopposed in 2022 (6,403 votes) [S4].
