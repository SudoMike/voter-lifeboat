38 Local Voters’ Pamphlet Letter from the Auditor's Office Asotin County Dear Asotin County Voters, Your General Election ballot will be mailed by October 16, 2026, and should arrive a few days later. For your ballot to be counted, you must sign your yellow envelope with a verifiable signature, or if you are unable to sign please mark an X witnessed by two people who must also sign the envelope. Power of Attorney is not valid for voting purposes. Your ballot must be received in any one of our drop boxes before 8:00 p.m. on Election Day or postmarked no later than Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Please return ballots as soon as possible. Ballot drop boxes are in the alley behind City Hall in downtown Clarkston, in the parking lot behind Lincoln Middle School in the Clarkston Heights, at the Asotin County Courthouse and a new box is located at the County Shop in Anatone. Stamps are no longer required to mail your ballot. Unofficial election results will be available at https://results.votewa. gov/results/public/asotin-county-wa/elections/20261103 on election night near 8:30 p.m. Your vote Matters! Throughout history, there are many examples where an election was decided by just a few votes and in some cases, one vote. Please join us in exercising one of the most precious rights we have as an American, the right to vote. Sincerely, Asotin County Auditor’s Office

48 Local Voters’ Pamphlet Asotin County Rural EMS District No. 2 | Proposition No. 1 ASOTIN COUNTY RURAL EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICE DISTRICT NO. 2 PROPOSITION NO. 1 EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES REGULAR PROPERTY TAX LEVY Shall Asotin County Rural Emergency Medical Service District No. 2 be authorized to levy a regular property tax in the sum of 28 cents (or less) per $1,000 assessed valuation each year for six consecutive years beginning in 2027 to be collected for the provision of Emergency Medical Services, all as provided in Asotin County Rural Emergency Medical Service District No. 2, Ordinance No. 152 and the Laws of the State of Washington RCW 84.52.069? [ ] YES [ ] NO Resolution No. 26-27 A RESOLUTION FOR APPROVAL OF A CONTINUING PROPERTY TAX LEVY TO SUPPORT EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES IN ASOTIN COUNTY RURAL EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICE DISTRICT 2 (REMS #2) AS CREATED IN ORDINANCE NO. 20-21. WHEREAS, Asotin County voters within Asotin County Rural EMS #2 are funding emergency medical services within Rural EMS #2 by a property tax currently in place; and WHEREAS, the property tax currently in place expires on December 31, 2026; and WHEREAS, the Asotin County Board of Commissioners has determined that an ongoing property tax levy is needed to continue providing necessary emergency medical services within Rural EMS #2; and WHEREAS, the population of Rural EMS #2 and the demand for emergency medical services within Rural EMS #2 are continuing to grow; and WHEREAS , the Board of Commissioners has entered into an agreement with the Lewiston Fire Department, in cooperation with the Asotin County Fire District #1, to provide emergency medical services that include emergency medical rescue, treatment and transportation of the ill and injured within Asotin County Rural EMS #2; NOW THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED, the Board of County Commissioners of Asotin County, Washington does hereby authorize an election to be held in the County of Asotin for the registered voters residing within the boundary of Rural EMS #2 service area on November 3, 2026, to vote on a proposed levy to continue funding of emergency medical services for a 6-year period beginning in 2027. The proceeds of said levy will be placed in the special revenue fund of the County of Asotin to be used to pay for contracted emergency medical services directly provided to the resident of the Rural EMS #2 District. This resolution will take effect upon signature. Dated this 27 day of July in the year of 2026. THE BALLOT SHALL READ AS FOLLOWS: EMS BALLOT TITLE Six-Year Levy Limit Asotin County Rural Emergency Medical Service District No. 2 PROPOSITION NO 1 Emergency Medical Services Regular Property Tax Levy Shall Asotin County Rural Emergency Medical Service District No. 2 be authorized to levy a regular property tax in the sum of 28 cents (or less) per $1,000 assessed valuation each year for six consecutive years beginning in 2027 to be collected for the provision of Emergency Medical Services, all as provided in Asotin County Rural Emergency Medical Service District No. 2, Ordinance No. 152 and the Laws of the State of Washington RCW 84.52.069. Yes _____ No______ The Board of Commissioners of Asotin County, Washington adopted Resolution No. 26-27 concerning a proposition to continue to finance emergency medical services within rural EMS #2. If approved, this proposition would continue to fund the necessary emergency medical services within Rural EMS #2 through a property tax levy. This would increase the current property tax levy of up to 15 cents (or less) to 28 cents (or less) per $1,000 assessed valuation each year for six consecutive years beginning in 2027.
