Legal/Judicial Experience

I have had the honor of serving as the District Court Judge for Asotin County since 2016.  It is an honor and privilege to serve our county in this capacity.  The position allows me to hear a variety of criminal and civil cases in Asotin County and the Cities of Clarkston, Washington and Asotin, Washington.  I bring to the bench my 23 years of experience as an attorney licensed in both the states of Idaho and Washington, and my last ten (10) years as Judge.   In 2014, I was appointed as a Superior Court Commissioner, and in 2015 I was appointed as Asotin County District Court Judge. I began to serve on January 1, 2016 and was subsequently elected as your District Court Judge in 2016, 2018, and 2022.  I have been an attorney and judge for 33 years.

Statement

 As District Court Judge, I strive to ensure that all people who appear before the court are treated fairly, equitably and with respect.  I remain committed to educating myself in the many changing laws and our community needs in order to serve efficiently and justly.   During my terms, I have worked hard to ensure the fair and impartial administration of justice.  My experience as an attorney, court commissioner and judge has provided me with the knowledge and skills necessary to serve the court as judge, and I have done so successfully for the last several years. Although I am unopposed in this election, I want to thank the voters for your confidence in choosing me as District Court Judge.  Thank you for your continued support.
