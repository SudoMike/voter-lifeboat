Leadership Experience

Blake Richards currently serves as Undersheriff for the Asotin County Sheriff’s Office. For nearly two decades, his life’s work has been in public safety: serving in law enforcement, fire service, emergency medicine, higher education, and operational leadership.

Other Professional Experience

Blake’s experience includes patrol operations, criminal investigations, emergency response, interagency coordination, personnel leadership, training development, and the management of complex public safety operations and budgets. He has served as a firefighter, paramedic captain, Life Flight paramedic, SWAT medic, fire investigator, boating officer, law enforcement instructor, and paramedic program director. As Lewiston’s Life Flight Base Manager, Blake managed personnel, critical emergency response resources, and multimillion-dollar operations serving families and communities across the region.

Education

Blake holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Paramedicine from Central Washington University and is a graduate of the Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission Basic Law Enforcement Academy. He has taught at multiple higher education institutions, including Central Washington University, and has taught college-level finance and law enforcement courses.

Community-minded

Blake lives in Asotin County with his wife, Jeni, a nurse and owner of Vybrance Med Spa, and their seven-year-old daughter, Mila, who attends Asotin Elementary School. For Blake, this race is personal. This is where his family lives, where his daughter is growing up, and where he is committed to protecting the safety, future, and strength of the community they call home.

Statement

Washington has spent years making excuses for criminals while victims, families, and law-abiding citizens are left to deal with the consequences. I am running for Sheriff because Asotin County needs leadership that will stand up, enforce the law, and put this community first.
Drug dealers who prey on vulnerable people should be held accountable. Violent offenders should answer for their crimes. Victims should feel supported, not forgotten. As Sheriff, I will work closely with the Prosecutor’s Office to build strong cases, support victims, and make sure the people harming our community face real consequences.
Asotin County is growing, and we cannot afford weak leadership or soft priorities. We need a Sheriff’s Office ready for the next decade, focused on safe streets, protected homes, strong deputies, and constitutional policing.
I believe the Constitution still matters. I have already stood up for that principle by challenging Olympia and defending the right of Asotin County voters to choose their next Sheriff. As Sheriff, I will stand for public safety, personal freedom, and constitutional rights.

Elected Experience

As Chief, I managed budgets responsibly and maintained fiscal accountability.

Education

Education: I earned a BS in Justice Studies with Summa Cum Laude honors and an AAS in Law Enforcement. I graduated from Command College and the FBI-LEEDA Trilogy Courses and completed multiple supervisory and instructor training programs. 

Community Service

I am active in Clarkston Rotary, Back Country Horsemen, Clarkston EPIC, and Twin River Special Olympics. I coached Little League and Clearwater Football, served on the Little League Board, and volunteered at Twin County United Way. Accomplishments: I created the idea for the LPD Police Officer Memorial, helped organize National Night Out for eight years, revived Shop with a Cop in the valley, and proposed naming streets after fallen officers.

Statement

With more than 31 years of full-time law enforcement experience in the Lewis-Clark Valley, I have dedicated my career to protecting our communities and leading with integrity. As Sheriff, I will continue the community-oriented approach I practiced as Police Chief by building trust, listening to citizens, and working collaboratively to solve local problems and strengthen public safety throughout Asotin County.

My priorities as Sheriff are clear. I will aggressively confront the fentanyl epidemic through strong enforcement, prevention efforts, and community education. I will lead the newly opened Jail with strength, integrity, accountability, and collaboration while partnering with local organizations to control costs and expand programs that help incarcerated individuals break the cycle of reoffending and successfully return as productive citizens. I will address jail release concerns, strengthen deputy training, and ensure deputies are fully prepared to serve professionally and effectively. 

I deeply respect the Sheriff’s responsibility to protect constitutional rights and will work tirelessly to uphold every citizen’s rights.

I respectfully ask for your support and would be honored to serve as your next sheriff.
