## Background
Juan "Jerry" Garcia (Prefers Democratic Party) was born and raised in the area, graduated from Moses Lake High School in 2014 and lists some college [S1]. He lists work in agricultural fields, wildlife conservation, fast food, retail and a factory, more than six years as a corrections officer, and past work as a landlord [S1]. He was a candidate for Othello School Board director in 2025 and has not held elected office [S1]. He is the founder and chapter leader of Indivisible Othello WA and of the Othello Dog Club, and volunteers with Multicultural Association Services, Whole Washington and the Othello Food Bank [S1]. The Columbia Basin Herald describes him as a first-time challenger [S2]. His PDC registration lists an Othello committee address; he qualified from the August 4 primary for the November ballot [S3]. He lists no campaign website [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** says he intends to "fix our regressive tax system" [S1]. Proposes amending RCW 84.36.070 so that stocks and bonds above a wealth level of around $100 million are no longer exempt from property tax, with the stated aim of protecting 401(k)s, pensions and bank accounts [S2].
- **Money in politics:** pledges to accept no corporate PAC money [S1]. Says he would push to remove corporate PAC influence and for ranked-choice voting, "even if I risk losing the next election," and would not invoke legislative privilege [S2].
- **Health care:** lists health for Washingtonians as a priority [S1] and names "championing Universal Healthcare" as one of two issues he would spend political capital on, along with expanding non-emergency medical transportation for elders, disabled residents and veterans [S2].
- **Water:** lists water rights as a priority [S1] and says he wants to finish the Odessa Groundwater Replacement Program because local aquifers are drying up [S2].
- **Labor and farmworkers:** lists union rights and farmworker protections as priorities [S1].
- **Housing:** says families deserve homes they can afford, maintain and improve [S1]. Supports housing regulatory and permitting reforms aimed at "operational bottlenecks" rather than removing environmental protections or deregulating safety standards [S2].
- **Environment:** names PFAS ("forever chemicals") in water as an issue he would work on [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
