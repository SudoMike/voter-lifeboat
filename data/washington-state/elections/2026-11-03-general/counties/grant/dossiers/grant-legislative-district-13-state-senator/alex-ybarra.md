## Background
- Alex Ybarra (Prefers Republican Party) is the current State Representative for the 13th District, Position 2 [S1]. He was first appointed and then elected in 2019 [S2]. He is running for the Senate seat, leaving his House seat open [S7].
- He is ranking member of the House Postsecondary Education and Workforce Committee, assistant ranking member on Labor and Workplace Standards, and a member of Environment and Energy [S2].
- He served on the Quincy School Board from 2011, including as president, and was vice president of the Washington State School Directors' Association [S1][S2].
- He worked 14 years as an engineer at Rocket Research Company and 16 years at Grant County PUD as a hydrologist, energy market analyst and compliance auditor [S1][S2]. He holds a mathematics degree from Central Washington University and an MBA [S1][S2]. He is a lifelong Quincy resident [S2].

## Positions
- His statement says he has advocated "for accountability, tax relief, and common-sense policies," and that "government should live within its means" [S1].
- Lists defending water rights, supporting law enforcement, strengthening schools and supporting agriculture and small business as his legislative work [S1].
- **Taxes:** called ESSB 6346 "an unconstitutional income tax opposed repeatedly by Washington voters" and said it "will start with high earners, but it won't stop there" [S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** voted against ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S5].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted against ESSB 6346 and called it unconstitutional [S3][S4].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted against ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments to the I-2081 parental-rights law [S5][S6].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- Voted nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million (2026-03-09, passed 51-46) [S4].
- Voted nay on ESHB 1296 on House passage (2025-03-12, 56-37) and on final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, 59-39) [S5]. ESHB 1296 (chapter 369, Laws of 2025) added, modified and removed rights in the I-2081 parents' list [S6].
