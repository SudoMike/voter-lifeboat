## What the office does

A Washington State Senator is the single Senate member elected from each legislative district to the 49-member State Senate for a four-year term. Senators write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, and the Senate also confirms gubernatorial appointments.

## Race dynamics

Rep. Alex Ybarra (Prefers Republican Party), who holds the district's House Position 2 seat, is the only candidate on the ballot for the Senate seat [S1][S2]. The contest is uncontested.
