## Background

- Grant County District Court judge since 2021; Grant County Superior Court pro tem judge since 2024 [S1].
- Grant County Department of Public Defense, District Court supervisor and staff attorney, 2014-2021 [S1].
- Private practice and public-defense contract attorney in Grant, Okanogan and Spokane counties, 2000-2014 [S1].
- J.D., Gonzaga University School of Law; B.A. in criminal justice and sociology, Washington State University [S1].
- Board member of the Boys & Girls Club of the Columbia Basin and of the Big Bend Community College and CB Tech criminal justice department; administrator and board member of Moses Lake Grid Kids [S1].
- Lives in Moses Lake [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says he developed a therapeutic court "focused on treatment, resources, stability and hope for those suffering from substance use / mental health disorders within the criminal justice system," and worked on court technology [S1].
- Says he will "follow the law fairly and impartially, ensuring all litigants are treated equally and free from bias," keeping citizens' safety in mind [S1].

## Record

- Elected unopposed in 2022 with 18,090 votes (99.36%; the rest write-ins) [S3].
