## Background
Joshua Thompson (Prefers Republican Party) is a current Ellensburg City Council member and a three-term Precinct Committee Officer for the Kittitas County Republican Party; he was a delegate to the 2024 Republican State Convention [S1]. He reports 20 years of technical, digital marketing and cybersecurity work as an engineer and consultant and holds a business/economics degree from Eastern Oregon University (2006) [S1][S2]. His site lists him as co-owner of Thompson Rental Properties [S2]. He has coached youth basketball and baseball [S1]. On the council he says he defended residents' right to speak at meetings about the city's use of DEI practices and challenged a consultant's report he calls fraudulent [S2]. He has not served in the Legislature [S1]. In the August 4 primary he placed second with 8,886 votes (34.15%) to Deanna Martinez's 9,687 (37.23%), per preliminary results [S5]. He did not respond by deadline to the Columbia Basin Herald's first Position 2 questionnaire but answered its priorities question a day late, citing a family medical emergency [S3].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "We need to cut taxes dramatically," and calls the push for a state income tax "one more example" of a spending problem [S2]. Says he would oppose new income taxes and unnecessary tax increases [S3]; his statement says he is focused on reducing taxes [S1].
- **Spending and budget:** "We have a spending problem — on things Washingtonians don't even want" [S2]. Describes himself as a "fiscal hawk" [S2].
- **Housing:** says the state builds 20,000 of the 50,000 units a year it needs because of "extreme regulation and absurd green mandates" that raise building costs by 30%, and will fight to lower building costs [S2]. Would reduce regulations that raise housing costs [S3]; at a July forum supported more duplexes, fourplexes and cottage housing [S4].
- **Public safety:** says Washington ranks last in officers per capita and that he would work with legislators and the federal administration to fix it [S2]. Supports law enforcement and addressing the fentanyl and mental-health crises [S1][S3].
- **Local control:** "I believe decisions should stay closer to local communities" [S1]; says he would protect local control [S3].
- **Climate and energy:** calls for finishing the Columbia Basin Project, protecting dams and new energy policies so Central Washington can "flourish," and criticizes "absurd green mandates" in housing [S2].
- **Technology and economy:** sees technology and artificial-intelligence industries as the district's biggest opportunity of the next decade, with a "fair balance" between natural resources and opportunity [S4]. Says agriculture "has to come first" in discussions of energy and data centers [S5].
- **Agriculture and water:** says state agencies and legislators have waged "a campaign of unconstitutional abuse against farmers and ranchers" [S2]; would secure Odessa Groundwater Replacement Program funding and invest in farm-to-market roads and freight corridors [S3].
- **Health care:** supports rural health-care workforce programs, telehealth and local training [S3].
- **Constitutional rights and faith:** pledges to protect First and Second Amendment rights and the right to worship [S1][S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** his public-schools priority objects that "boys continue to compete in girls' sports and locker rooms" [S2].
- **Parents and schools:** says "parents' rights are being dissolved," "children are under attack in schools," and that "parents' rights are limited" while test scores fall [S2]. Lists parents' rights and safe schools under his families priority [S2].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement naming the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. Opposes new income taxes [S2][S3].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** no statement naming the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. His platform says parents' rights are being dissolved [S2].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** no statement naming the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. His platform objects to boys competing in girls' sports [S2].
