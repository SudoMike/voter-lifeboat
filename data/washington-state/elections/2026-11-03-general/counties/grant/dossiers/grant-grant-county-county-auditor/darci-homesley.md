## Background

Darci Homesley, 57, of Moses Lake, is a Republican running for elective county office for the first time [S9]. Her campaign says she has been a business owner in Grant County for more than 30 years and has worked for and closely with Grant County for the past five years [S4][S5]. She cites 35 years of accounting and budgeting experience [S1]. GCJ.news described her as an accounting technician at filing [S8]. It later reported she is listed as financial services manager for the Grant County Multi-Agency Communications Center and earlier handled accounting in the county fair office [S9]. Her statement says she has been elected to boards but does not name them [S1].

She did not respond to GCJ.news's primary candidate questionnaire [S9]. In the certified August primary she finished second with 4,283 votes to Shannon Falstad's 7,188 [S10].

## Positions

### Spending and financial oversight
- "Every taxpayer dollar should be handled carefully and responsibly. Families in Grant County have to live within a budget, and our county government should do the same" [S3].
- Pledges to "implement rigorous internal controls to prevent waste and unnecessary spending," "audit county expenditures to ensure absolute compliance with state and local laws," and "improve operational efficiency without sacrificing essential public services" [S3].
- Calls the auditor "the watchdog of taxpayer dollars" [S2], and her statement closes on "fiscal responsibility" [S1].

### Taxes
- Lists "focus on long-term financial stability to avoid future tax burdens" among her fiscal priorities [S3]. No position on specific taxes found as of 2026-10-08.

### Transparency and public records
- Wants financial reports made easier for the public to read, timely and complete responses to public records requests, and consistent reporting across county departments [S3].
- "Transparency is not about politics — it is about accountability" [S3].

### Elections and independence
- Pledges a "strictly non-partisan approach to financial and election oversight" and says the office "should serve the public — not politics" [S3].
- No detailed elections-administration proposals found as of 2026-10-08.

### Parents and schools; gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- No public statements found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

She has not held elective county office [S9]. Her county work has been in financial roles outside the auditor's office [S9].

## Endorsements

GCJ.news reported the Grant County Republican Party Central Committee endorsed her (and Falstad) before the primary [S9]. Her campaign's endorsements page said "Endorsements coming soon" as of 2026-10-08 [S6].

## Campaign finance

PDC summary as of the September 17, 2026 update: $5,700 raised and $5,458.23 spent [S7].
