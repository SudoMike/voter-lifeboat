## Background

Shannon Falstad, 57, an Ephrata native and 1987 Ephrata High School graduate, is the interim Grant County auditor [S5][S6]. County commissioners appointed her effective July 1, 2026, after Auditor Michele Jaderlund retired [S4][S6]. She joined the auditor's office in 1996. There she was a recording deputy, then recording/licensing lead deputy and a certified licensing deputy, supported the elections department and entered accounts payable and payroll [S1][S2][S5]. From 2016 until her appointment she was the accounting deputy in the Grant County Clerk's Office, managing tills and deposits, reconciling bank accounts, supervising cashiers and processing payroll [S4][S6]. (Her voters'-guide statement still lists the clerk's office post as current [S1].) Earlier she worked as a U.S. Bank vault teller and a Chicago Title recording clerk and title assistant [S1][S7]. She is vice president and former treasurer of the Grant County Public Employees Association bargaining unit [S1][S7]. Before the appointment she had not held elective office [S7].

In the certified August primary she led with 7,188 votes to Darci Homesley's 4,283 and Andrew Koeppen's 3,905 [S8].

## Positions

### Office operations
- Says her 30 years in county government, including two decades in the auditor's office, prepare her "to lead as the Auditor" and to make each department run efficiently "from day one" [S1].
- Pledges to "build on what works, thoughtfully review current policies and make improvements," with "clear communication and accountability" [S1].
- On appointment, named her immediate priorities as a smooth transition, continuity in election administration, supporting the county's new accounting system, and retaining staff [S4]. "There's been some turnover, and I want to focus on supporting our team and keeping that knowledge in-house," she said [S4].
- For a full term, wants to finish putting in the updated accounting system, cross-train all staff, and improve policies and procedures for county departments and the public [S7].
- Says she took part in rolling out a new technology system that improved accuracy, efficiency and access to public records [S7].
