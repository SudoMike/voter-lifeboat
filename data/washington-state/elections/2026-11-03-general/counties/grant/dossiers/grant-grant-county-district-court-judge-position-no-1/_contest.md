## What the office does

Grant County District Court has criminal jurisdiction over misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors committed in the county (sentences of up to one year in jail and a $5,000 fine unless a statute says otherwise), hears traffic infractions, conducts preliminary hearings, and sets bail and arraigns defendants [S2]. Its judges are elected countywide to four-year terms, so the whole county votes on every position [S3].

## Race dynamics

Tom Middleton, Grant County Superior Court commissioner since 2017, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. The seat is open: Judge Brian Barlow of Moses Lake chose not to seek re-election, and Middleton will succeed him [S4]. Barlow was re-elected unopposed in 2022 with 99.28% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S5]. The contest is uncontested.
