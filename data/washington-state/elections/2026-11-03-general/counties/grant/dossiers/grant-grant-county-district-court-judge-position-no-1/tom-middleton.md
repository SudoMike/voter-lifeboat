## Background

- Grant County Superior Court commissioner since 2017 [S1].
- Associate and partner at Hansen and Middleton Law Firm in Ephrata, 1996-2004 [S1].
- Ephrata City Council member, 2004-2008; owner and general manager of Lindsay Water Pool & Spa, 2004-2016, after taking over the family business when his father became ill [S1].
- Raised in Grant County; Ephrata High School (1988), B.A. in economics and political science from Pacific Lutheran University (1992), J.D. from the University of Washington School of Law (1996) [S1].
- Lives in Ephrata; will succeed retiring Judge Brian Barlow [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says he "will treat all who come to court with dignity and respect and will fairly apply the law to the cases before him" [S1].
