## Background

Shannon Durfee, 40, of Quincy, prefers the Republican Party. She graduated from Quincy High School in 2004 and attended Wenatchee Valley College [S1][S4]. She is an elected director on the Quincy School District board and president of the Rotary Club of Quincy [S1][S4]. She owns Durfee Virtual Administrative Services, which provides administrative, marketing and communications support to businesses, nonprofits and public agencies [S2][S4]. She is public relations director for Quincy Valley Medical Center and executive director of the Quincy Valley Hospital Foundation [S2][S4]. She was a Washington State Patrol communications officer for 10 years [S2]. She also sits on the Quincy Youth Baseball League board and does church administration and volunteer work [S2].

She placed second in the certified District 3 primary with 1,233 votes, behind Kelly Raap's 1,377 [S5].

## Positions

### Growth and the local economy
- "Grant County is growing, and with that growth comes important choices." She wants leadership that "protects our rural way of life while also recognizing opportunities that strengthen our economy, support local jobs" [S1].
- Wants growth to benefit "the people who already live here — not just outside interests — by planning responsibly and protecting our quality of life" [S4].

### County finances and government
- "I believe in fiscal responsibility, infrastructure, transparency, and ensuring local voices remain at the center of county decisions" [S1].
- Says Grant County "deserves practical, accountable leadership that focuses on doing the basics well" [S4].
- Her campaign lists "fiscal stewardship, public safety, responsible growth, and keeping local government accountable" as its themes [S2].
- Cites her public-safety and business background but declined to rank one county issue above others [S6].
