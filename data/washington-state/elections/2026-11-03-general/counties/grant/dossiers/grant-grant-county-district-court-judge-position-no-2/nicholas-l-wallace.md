## Background

- Grant County District Court judge since 2021; serves as presiding judge [S1].
- Superior Court judge pro tem since 2022 and Superior Court commissioner pro tem for more than 10 years [S1].
- Trial lawyer and managing partner at Schultheis Tabler Wallace PLLC for 25 years [S1].
- B.A. in business administration (cum laude), University of Washington; J.D. (cum laude), Seattle University School of Law [S1].
- Volunteer assistant varsity boys' soccer coach at Ephrata and Moses Lake high schools, Grant County Special Olympics coach, pro bono legal services for low-income people; recipient of the Washington State Bar Association Local Hero Award [S1].
- Lives in Moses Lake [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says that as presiding judge he and his colleagues have expanded remote hearings, made technology improvements, are implementing new case-management software, and "helped establish a therapeutic court focused on accountability and breaking the cycle of repeat offenses" [S1].
- Says he applies the law "fairly and consistently, regardless of background or belief" and is "committed to protecting constitutional rights while supporting public safety and personal accountability" [S1].

## Record

- Elected unopposed in 2022 with 18,318 votes (99.25%; the rest write-ins) [S3].
