## What the office does

Public Utility District No. 2 of Grant County (Grant PUD) provides electric power and fiber-optic broadband in Grant County, with about 56,750 active meters. It owns and operates the Priest Rapids Project on the Columbia River (Priest Rapids and Wanapum dams), which can produce more than 2,000 megawatts [S4]. A five-member board of commissioners, elected on a nonpartisan basis, sets policy, reviews operations and approves budget expenditures [S3][S4]. Three commissioners represent districts 1-3 and serve six-year terms; two at-large commissioners (A and B) serve four-year terms. Terms are staggered so two seats are elected every two years [S2][S3]. Only voters in a commissioner district vote in its primary, but voters of the entire PUD vote in the general election [S2]. Grant PUD is countywide, so every Grant County voter sees this race [S3][S5].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Larry Schaapman of Quincy, board president, is the only candidate on the ballot for a six-year term [S1][S5][S6]. The contest is uncontested. He was first appointed to fill a vacancy in 2012 [S3] and was re-elected unopposed in 2020 with 98.71% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S7]. He did not submit a voters'-guide statement [S1].
