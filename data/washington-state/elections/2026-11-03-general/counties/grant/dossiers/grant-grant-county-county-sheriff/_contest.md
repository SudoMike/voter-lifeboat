## What the office does
The sheriff leads crime prevention and investigation with other agencies, arrests and jails offenders, serves warrants and court orders, keeps the peace and responds to emergencies. In Grant County the sheriff is also the county's director of emergency management (S3). The 2026 budget is $18.5 million for operations plus nearly $10 million for the jail, funding nearly 150 employees in patrol, major crimes, narcotics, traffic, corrections and administration (S3). It is a partisan county office with a four-year term (S1, S2).

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Joey Kriete, elected in 2022, is seeking a second term against Moses Lake police Sgt. Darrik Gregg, a former chief deputy in the sheriff's office. Both prefer the Republican Party (S1, S3). Gregg led the certified primary 5,839 to 5,734; Sheriff's Sgt. Josh Sainsbury (4,518) was eliminated (S4). The campaign has centered on leadership inside the office. Topics include the Deputy Sheriffs' Association's no-confidence vote in Kriete (31 no-confidence, 6 confidence, 10 abstentions), Kriete's January demotion of Sainsbury after Sainsbury entered the race, and Gregg's pending federal retaliation lawsuit against the county, which he says he would continue if elected (S5). Kriete runs on his record (restaffing, a crime reduction team, accreditation). Gregg runs on "servant leadership," accountability and visibility (S6). At a Grant County Republican Party debate, both said they would work with federal agencies, including ICE, on criminal matters within state law (S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a first-term incumbent with 33+ years in the office, against a challenger who left the sheriff's office in 2021 and asks for "a fresh approach to leadership."
2. **Safety:** both use enforcement framing ("tough on crime"; proactive policing and a property-crime unit). Kriete also cites expanded mental-health response and behavioral-health and opioid partnerships. The difference is small.
3. **Spending:** Kriete stresses living within the budget and financial sustainability as the new jail opens. Gregg proposes a full review of the office, including its spending. Neither statement is strong.
4. **Taxes, housing, climate, reform:** no evidence in this race.
