## What it does

Proposition No. 1 is a one-year excess property tax levy of $12,000 for collection in 2027, at an approximate rate of $0.1756086959 per $1,000 of assessed value, for Grant County Cemetery District No. 2 [S1][S2]. The district runs the cemetery in the town of Wilson Creek [S1].

- The explanatory statement says the money "pays for our maintenance worker, pays for electricity, gas for our mowers, oil kits, and repair costs for our mowers" [S1].
- The district "has to run a Special Levy every year for maintenance and operations on the November Ballot" [S1].
- The board adopted Resolution No. 26-1 at a budget hearing and regular meeting on July 13, 2026, "all of said commissioners being present and voting." The commissioners are Claudia Pfeifer (chair), Howard Pfeifer and Jim Eastridge [S2].
- The resolution cites "a lack of sufficient funds for the Maintenance and Operations of the Cemetery" and RCW 84.52.052, 84.52.054, 68.52.290 and 68.52.310 [S2]. (Its recital refers to funds "for the 2026 year"; the ballot title and the levy table say 2027 [S1][S2].)
- The district's filing says passage needs 60% [S2]. RCW 84.52.052 lets a cemetery district levy above its regular limits when voters approve it in the manner set by Article VII, section 2(a) of the state constitution [S11].

## Cost

- $12,000 in total for 2027 [S1].
- At the stated rate of about 17.56 cents per $1,000, the levy on a $300,000 property would be about $52.68 for the year (computed for this dossier from [S1]).
- **A rate note.** The approximate rate printed for 2027 ($0.1756086959) is identical to the rate printed for 2026 [S1][S3], even though the amount rises from $11,500 to $12,000. At the 2026 rate's implied valuation, $12,000 would be about $0.183 per $1,000 (computed for this dossier). The printed rate is an estimate, and the final rate depends on the district's 2027 assessed value [S1][S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

This renews a levy that district voters have approved every year. It is not a new tax.

| Collection year | Amount | Approximate rate per $1,000 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 (this measure) | $12,000 | $0.1756 | on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot [S1] |
| 2026 | $11,500 | $0.1756 | 78.75% yes (63-17), Nov. 2025 [S3][S6] |
| 2025 | $11,500 | $0.1882 | 60.23% yes (103-68), Nov. 2024 [S4][S7] |
| 2024 | $11,500 | $0.2176 | 82.69% yes (86-18), Nov. 2023 [S5][S8] |

Voters also approved the district's levy in November 2022 with 65.79% (100-52) [S9]; this research did not retrieve that year's amount.

- The 2027 amount is $500 (about 4.3%) more than each of the last three years (computed for this dossier from [S1], [S3], [S4], [S5]).
- RCW 68.52.310 caps a cemetery district's regular annual levy at 11.25 cents per $1,000 [S10]. This measure is an excess levy "in excess of the regular maximum tax levy specified [by] law for cemetery districts" [S2].
