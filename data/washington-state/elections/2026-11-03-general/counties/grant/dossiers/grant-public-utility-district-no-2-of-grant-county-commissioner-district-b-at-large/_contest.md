## What the office does

Public Utility District No. 2 of Grant County (Grant PUD) provides electric power and fiber-optic broadband in Grant County, with about 56,750 active meters. It owns and operates the Priest Rapids Project on the Columbia River (Priest Rapids and Wanapum dams), which can produce more than 2,000 megawatts [S4]. A five-member board of commissioners, elected on a nonpartisan basis, sets policy, reviews operations and approves budget expenditures [S3][S4]. Three commissioners represent districts 1-3 and serve six-year terms; two at-large commissioners (A and B) serve four-year terms. Terms are staggered so two seats are elected every two years [S2][S3]. Voters of the entire PUD vote in the general election [S2]; Grant PUD is countywide, so every Grant County voter sees this race [S3][S5].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Nelson Cox of Warden, board secretary, is the only candidate on the ballot for a four-year at-large term [S1][S5][S6]. The contest is uncontested. He was first elected in 2018 [S3] and re-elected unopposed in 2022 with 99.39% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S7]. He did not submit a voters'-guide statement [S1].
