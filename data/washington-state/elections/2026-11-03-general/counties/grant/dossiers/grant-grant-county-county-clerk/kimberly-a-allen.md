## Background

- Kimberly A. Allen (Prefers Republican Party) of Soap Lake was first elected Grant County Clerk in 2006 and is running unopposed for re-election [S1][S2].
- Before that she was office manager and legal secretary at the Schultheis Tabler Wallace law firm for 11 years [S1].
- She serves on the statewide clerks' association education committee, trains at the Newly Elected Officials Conference and mentors new county clerks [S1].
- She graduated from Ephrata High School in 1986 and attended Big Bend Community College [S1].
- She was previously a Republican precinct committee officer and was Republican Women treasurer from 2007 to 2025; she serves on the worship team at Community Church of Ephrata [S1].
- In the primary she received 10,213 votes (98.3%) in the August 7 count [S3].
