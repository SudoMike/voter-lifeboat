## Background

- Craig Morrison (Prefers Republican Party) of Moses Lake has been Grant County Coroner since November 2010 and is running unopposed [S1][S2].
- He says he has worked as a death care professional for 43 years, starting at age 20 [S1].
- He was educated at Oakley High School, Ricks College, Boise State University and Mt. Hood Mortuary College [S1].
- He holds an executive board position with the Washington Association of Coroners and Medical Examiners and serves on the education committee of the Washington Association of County Officials [S1].
- In the primary he received 10,507 votes (98.9%) in the August 7 count [S3].
