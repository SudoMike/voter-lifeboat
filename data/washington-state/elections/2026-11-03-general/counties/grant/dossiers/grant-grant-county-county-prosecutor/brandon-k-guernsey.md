## Background

- Brandon K. Guernsey (Prefers Republican Party) of Moses Lake is the appointed Grant County Prosecuting Attorney and is running unopposed for the short and full four-year term [S1][S3].
- County commissioners unanimously picked him on July 22, 2025, from three candidates nominated after prosecutor Kevin McCrae resigned; he was sworn in on August 4, 2025 [S2].
- He holds a bachelor's degree in Spanish from Weber State University and a 2017 law degree from the University of Louisville Brandeis School of Law [S2].
- He joined the Grant County Prosecutor's Office in December 2017 and worked in district, juvenile and superior courts, including prosecuting major drug cases from the Interagency Narcotics Enforcement Team. He left in early 2025 to work as an assistant district attorney in Augusta, Georgia, and returned on his appointment [S2].
- In the primary he received 9,964 votes (98.5%) in the August 7 count [S4].

## Positions

### Public safety and office management
- Says he has worked "to strengthen office culture, improve efficiency, support victims, and build a team committed to public service and public safety" [S1].
- Says the job means "supporting law enforcement, treating people fairly, improving communication between agencies, and ensuring cases are handled with professionalism and integrity" [S1].
- Wants to keep "modernizing the office" and improve collaboration "throughout the justice system" [S1].
