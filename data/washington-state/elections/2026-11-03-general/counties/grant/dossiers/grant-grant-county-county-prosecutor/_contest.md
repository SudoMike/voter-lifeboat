## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes criminal and civil actions for the state and county, defends suits against the county, and is legal adviser to the county commissioners, other county and precinct officers, and school directors [S2][S4].

## Race dynamics

Brandon K. Guernsey (Prefers Republican Party) was appointed prosecutor by the county commissioners in July 2025 after Kevin McCrae resigned, and is the only candidate on the ballot [S1][S3][S4]. The ballot lists a short and full four-year term [S3]. The contest is uncontested.
