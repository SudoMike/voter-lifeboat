## Background

- Carrie Heston (States No Party Preference), 46, of Ephrata, is the interim Grant County Treasurer. The county commissioners appointed her effective July 1, 2026, after Treasurer Darryl Pheasant retired [S3].
- She has worked in the treasurer's office for 28 years and was its accounting and investment officer before the appointment [S1][S3]. Her statement lists the Investment & Accounting Officer post as "2000-present" and says she worked alongside Pheasant for two years preparing for his retirement [S1].
- She graduated from Ephrata High School in 1998 and attended Big Bend Community College, focusing on accounting [S1].
- She and her family own Four Seasons Farm Service in Ephrata [S1].
- She describes herself as a first-time candidate [S1]. In the primary she received 10,128 votes (99.2%) in the August 7 count [S4].

## Positions

- Pledges "conservative financial practices," "accurate budgeting, responsible debt management, timely reporting," and stronger "fraud prevention and cybersecurity safeguards" [S1].
- Says she would use the network of Washington county treasurers to interpret state law and adjust county procedures "to maximize efficiency" [S1].

## Record

- Cites work on property tax software conversions, an ERP system following the state's BARS accounting standards, and investment software [S1]. The commissioners cited her implementation of multiple software systems and her knowledge of cash management, investments and bond tracking [S3].
