## What the office does

The treasurer receives all money due the county and pays it out on warrants issued by the auditor [S2]. The office handles tax collection, financial transactions and the county's investment portfolio [S3].

## Race dynamics

Treasurer Darryl Pheasant, the longest-serving county treasurer in Washington, retired June 30, 2026, after 39-1/2 years [S3]. Carrie Heston (States No Party Preference), a deputy in the office, was the only candidate to file. The county commissioners appointed her interim treasurer effective July 1 [S3][S4]. The new four-year term begins January 1, 2027 [S3]. The contest is uncontested.
