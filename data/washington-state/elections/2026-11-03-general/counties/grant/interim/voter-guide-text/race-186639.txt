Statement
I've had the pleasure of serving as Judge for Grant County since 2021.  I'm dedicated to serving my community with a passion for strengthening all relationships associated with our local court system.
In my time as judge I have advanced our Court by developing a therapeutic court, focused on treatment, resources, stability and hope for those suffering from substance use / mental health disorders within the criminal justice system.  Additionally, I have worked collaboratively with others to improve technology within our court.
My love for my community drives me within this position, keeping our citizens in mind when it comes to safety and the legal standards that benefit all of Grant County.  I will continue to follow the law fairly and impartially, ensuring all litigants are treated equally and free from bias.
I am the right choice for our community and appreciate you in advance for your support and vote.
