## ShortDescription
BONDS FOR EXPANSION OF MCKAY HEALTHCARE & REHABILITATION

## BallotTitle
The Commission of the Public Hospital District No. 4, Grant County, Washington (McKay Healthcare & Rehabilitation), adopted Resolution No. 2026-4 concerning bonds to fund an expansion of the district’s healthcare facilities. This proposition would authorize the District to expand McKay Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center to include a 16-bed assisted living unit, a 16-bed memory care unit, and other capital improvements deemed necessary or advisable by the Commission; issue no more than $9,940,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 30 years; and levy annual excess property taxes to repay the bonds, all as provided in Resolution 2026-04. ## ExplanatoryStatement
Grant County Public Hospital District No. 4 (the “District”) operates McKay Healthcare & Rehabilitation Center.
In response to a critical gap in healthcare services due to a rapidly aging population and rising incidence of dementia, the District has determined that it is necessary to construct a 16-bed assisted living unit, a 16-bed memory care unit, and other capital improvements that are planned to include, an intergenerational community wellness center with licensed daycare, meeting, and classroom spaces and dining for use by the residents and members of the community (the “Project”).
The majority of the costs of the Project will be funded from grants and low interest loans. The District is proposing a ballot proposition to pay for the remaining costs of the Project. This proposition authorizes the District to issue no more than $9,940,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 30 years to be repaid from an annual excess property tax levy at an estimated rate of $0.61 per $1,000 of assessed value, or an estimated $15.27 per month for a $300,000 home.
Exemptions from taxes may be available to certain homeowners. For more information, please call the Assessor’s Office in Grant County 509-754-2011, extension 2672.

## ArgumentFor
70% of our population over the age of 65 will require skilled services at some point, whether for rehabilitation, assisted care, dementia support, or long-term care. Passing this bond ensures these vital services remain close to home, keeping patients near their family, friends, and loved ones.
This bond will also add essential daycare options for children and elderly respite, where none are otherwise available. Additionally, this project will create jobs, and provide an annual boost of $4.5 million to our local economy.
McKay Healthcare has been a responsible steward serving our community since 1938 and has never had a bond increase, despite rising operational costs and increasing patient needs each year. The facility has already proven highly resourceful by pursuing the majority of funding independently.
This is a small ask for a major community win; the remaining contribution per resident is minimal compared to the necessary outcome.
