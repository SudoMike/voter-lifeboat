## ExplanatoryStatement
Grant County Cemetery District No. 2 operates the cemetery in the town of Wilson Creek, Washington in Grant County.
The Grant County Cemetery District No. 2 has to run a Special Levy every year for maintenance and operations on the November Ballot. The Special Levy, Resolution No. 26-1, is voted on by our District voters. The levy amount is $12,000. This money pays for our maintenance worker, pays for electricity, gas for our mowers, oil kits, and repair costs for our mowers, when needed. The Board of Commissioners, Grant County Cemetery District No. 2, adopted Resolution No. 26-1, concerning a proposition to finance maintenance and operation expenses. The Levy rate is 17.5 cents per $1,000. of the assessed value to our District voters.
