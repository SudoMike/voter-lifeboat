Statement
It is an honor to hold the sacred office of Grant County Coroner.  I have served in this capacity since November 2010.  Since then, the coroner’s office has received its international accreditation through The International Association of Coroner’s and Medical Examiners and certifications through The American Board of Medicolegal Death Investigators.
I currently hold an executive board position with the Washington Association of Coroners and Medical Examiners and serve on the education committee of Washington Association of County Officials.
Since the age of 20, I have dedicated myself to serving those families who have lost loved ones.  I received my formal education from Oakley High School, Rick’s College, Boise State University and Mt. Hood Mortuary College.  As a death care professional, I have served my community for the past 43 years.
I am asking for your continued support by re-electing me as your Grant County Coroner.
Sincerely,
Craig Morrison
