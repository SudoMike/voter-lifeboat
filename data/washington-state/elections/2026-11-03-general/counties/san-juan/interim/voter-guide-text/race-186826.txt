Statement
Ron Krebs is a 19-year veteran of the San Juan County Sheriff's Office and served as your elected Sheriff from 2015 to 2022. During his tenure, he modernized emergency communications, expanded the marine unit, and led the agency to state accreditation while maintaining fiscal responsibility. Ron is committed to restoring strong, steady leadership, improving recruitment and retention, and strengthening morale within the Sheriff's Office. A fourth-generation resident, he cares deeply about the safety and future of our community. Ron will prioritize accountability, transparency, and partnerships to ensure professional, effective public safety services for San Juan County residents.
