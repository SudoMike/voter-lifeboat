### ArgumentFor
To sustain current services, including our free recycling programs and Take It Or Leave It (TIOLI), Lopez Solid Waste Disposal District (LSWDD) requires dependable, recurring support from several sources, including garbage disposal fees, sale of recyclables, volunteer hours, and our annual levy. Historically, the levy has generated one-third or less of LSWDD’s annual expenses. 
Washington State authorizes disposal districts to seek levy funding only one year at a time, which means LSWDD must pass a levy each year to secure funding for the following year. 
We strongly encourage a YES vote on Proposition No. 1 - Excess Property Tax Levy 2027 for the Lopez Solid Waste Disposal District (LSWDD).
Fellow Lopezians, this is our opportunity to THINK GLOBALLY, ACT LOCALLY. Support your local Dump. Set an example for the world by engaging with your entire Lopez community in reducing waste while minimizing the costs of waste disposal. Your vote can change the world, or at the very least our little island. 
Your YES vote on this essential levy demonstrates support to the LSWDD board, staff, and volunteers. The LSWDD team works hard to reduce our collective waste and lower the costs of our solid waste operations, thereby keeping this annual levy at a minimal level year after year.

### BallotTitle
The Governing Body of the Lopez Solid Waste Disposal District adopted Resolution No. 20-2026 concerning an excess levy for the District.  This Proposition would authorize the District to collect $210,000 by a levy of excess taxes upon all taxable property within the District in an amount estimated to be $.091 cents per $1,000 assessed valuation for one tax year – 2027 – for the purpose of funding operations and capital improvements of the District.

Shall the proposition be approved?

### ExplanatoryStatement
Each year, the Governing Board of the Lopez Solid Waste Disposal District has proposed a balanced budget that includes revenue from tipping fees based upon weight or volume, and tax revenue to be paid for with a one-year property tax levy. For the year 2027, the amount of the tax revenue that is budgeted is $210,000. 

The Governing Board has adopted Resolution No.2026-02 calling for an election to approve the one-year property tax measure to generate $210,000. If adopted, the property tax rate for the Solid Waste District would be an amount of approximately $0.091 per $1,000 of assessed value for taxes imposed for the year 2027. The Lopez Solid Waste Disposal District must use the revenue raised for district operations and capital expenses. A “yes” vote is a vote to approve the proposal; a “no” vote is a vote against the proposal. A sixty percent majority is needed to pass this measure.
