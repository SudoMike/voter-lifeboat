## Background

- San Juan County Prosecuting Attorney for one term; a deputy prosecutor in the county for more than a decade before that [S1][S2].
- Felony and misdemeanor prosecution plus civil work on land use, public records compliance and general liability [S1].
- Western Washington University; University of Minnesota Law School [S1]. Prefers the Democratic Party [S1][S2].
- Unopposed in the Aug. 4, 2026 primary: 4,812 votes to 241 write-ins [S3].

## Positions

- Pledges "accurate and unbiased legal services" and to uphold the law "in a fair and just manner that treats victims with respect and honors the rights of all parties" [S1].
