## Background

- Shaw Island School District board director 2010-2021 (11 years) and superintendent 2014-2021; elementary teacher 1996-2003; now a substitute teacher at Shaw Island School [S1][S3].
- San Juan County Ferry Advisory Committee member since 2018, now its chair [S1][S3].
- Co-founder and president of the Shaw Island Community Land Trust; member of the Shaw Island School District Teacher Housing Finance Committee, which she says has raised over $1 million [S1][S3].
- Secretary of Shaw Islanders Inc.; started a monthly community brown-bag lunch on Shaw; 2026 Leadership San Juan Islands cohort [S1][S3][S8].
- B.A., Seattle Pacific University. Has lived on Shaw Island, in District 3, for more than 20 years [S1][S3][S8].
- Led the Aug. 4, 2026 primary with 2,944 votes (46%) to Bryant Ingman's 2,501 [S9].

## Positions

### County budget and spending
- Top priorities: "address the county's budget shortfall and rebuild reserves" [S1].
- "This is not the time to expand county services ... I will work to live within our means" [S5].
- Would protect mandated services (courts and justice, law enforcement, elections, health services for the most vulnerable), then prioritize senior services, environmental conservation, county parks, agriculture and code revisions that help affordable housing [S5].
- "A fiscally responsible government lives within its means"; will look for efficiency "without compromising essential public services" and rebuild the county's emergency reserves [S4].
- On the status quo: "we can't keep going with the status quo because we can't afford it" [S8].

### Taxes and revenue
- Reads the April 2026 levy lid lift defeat (60.67% no) as "residents are not looking to pay more in property taxes," and notes the county can raise regular property tax revenue only 1% a year without a vote [S5].
- "For 2028, I will look for alternative ways the county can increase revenue"; names no specific tax [S5].
- Supports "policies that encourage year-round jobs and private investment" [S4].

### Housing
- Will "advocate policies that expand access to affordable housing for the full income diversity of our county," including housing that helps farmers stay [S4].
- Record: co-founded the Shaw Island Community Land Trust and raises money for Shaw School teacher housing [S3].
- Lists code revisions to increase affordable housing development among her priorities [S5].

### Environment and climate
- "Protecting our environment is of the utmost importance as we continue to see impacts to our islands from climate change"; will advocate conserving natural resources and protecting sensitive areas [S4].
- Took part in the inaugural county Climate Convergence (April 2, 2026) with the Madrona Institute [S6].

### Ferries
- Will press for policies that maintain and strengthen ferry service with local, regional and state partners [S4].

## Endorsements

- San Juan County Democrats (Sept. 12, 2026) [S6].
- Former councilmembers Rick Hughes (District 2) and Rhea Miller (District 3), and more than 100 individual islanders [S3][S7].

## Scoring notes

- The more experienced candidate by her own account: 11 years elected, "the longest track record and ... most experienced, seasoned candidate" [S8].
- Emphasizes restraint: no expansion of services, live within means, rebuild reserves [S4][S5].
- Revenue: no named tax; promises to look for alternative revenue for 2028 [S5].
