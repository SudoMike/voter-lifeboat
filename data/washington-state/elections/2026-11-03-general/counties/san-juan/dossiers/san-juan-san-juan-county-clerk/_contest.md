## What the office does

The county clerk is clerk of the superior court: keeps its records and files, and receives and accounts for court fees and trust funds [S3]; nonpartisan, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Lisa Henderson, Clerk since 2019, seeks another term. [S1][S2]
