## What the office does

The treasurer collects property taxes, receives and disburses county and district money and invests idle funds [S3]; nonpartisan, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Rhonda Pederson, Treasurer since 2015, seeks another term. [S1][S2]
