## Background

- San Juan County Assessor since 2015; earlier chief appraiser (2007-2014) and appraiser (2004-2006) in the Assessor's Office [S1][S2].
- Trustee of the Washington Association of County Officials (2025-2026); chaired the state assessors' association government and public relations committee (2018-2022) [S1].
- Washington State accredited appraiser (2004); J.D., West Virginia University (1989); B.A., Dartmouth College; earlier a civil litigation associate attorney, certified farrier and naturalist [S1].

## Positions

- Says the office's most important responsibility is "an assessment process that is professional, fair, and clearly understood" [S1].
- Told the Journal in May 2026 that assessed values follow market sales and that "the thing that controls the amount of taxes is the taxing district's budget," and urged eligible seniors to apply for the senior exemption [S3].
