## Background

- San Juan County Sheriff from 2015 to 2022; 19 years with the Sheriff's Office [S1][S3].
- Executive board member of Safe San Juans; chair of the San Juan County Veterans' Advisory Board [S1].
- Says his family has lived in the county for four generations [S1][S3].

## Positions

- Cites, from his first tenure, modernized emergency communications, an expanded marine unit and state accreditation "while maintaining fiscal responsibility" [S1][S3].
- Says the office now faces "challenges in recruitment, retention and organizational morale"; priorities are visible leadership, retaining staff, accreditation standards, fiscal responsibility and transparency, and community partnerships [S3].
