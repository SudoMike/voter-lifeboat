## What the office does

The sheriff is the county's chief law enforcement officer, keeping the peace and running the county jail and civil process [S3]; nonpartisan in San Juan County, four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. former Sheriff Ron Krebs (2015-2022) is the only candidate. [S1][S2]
