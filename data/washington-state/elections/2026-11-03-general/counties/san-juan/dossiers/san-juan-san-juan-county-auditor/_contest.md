## What the office does

The county auditor is the recorder of deeds and other documents and the supervisor of all primaries and elections [S3]; both candidates also describe the office's licensing (vehicles, vessels, dogs) and county financial work [S1][S6]. In San Juan County the Auditor's Office also prepares the county budget: it drafted the 2027 preliminary budget the County Council received on Aug. 25, 2026 [S4]. Nonpartisan, four-year term [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

Two candidates who have both run the office's day-to-day work. Natasha Warmenhoven, Auditor since January 2023 and Chief Deputy Auditor from 2020 to 2023, seeks a second term on her record ("Experience. Integrity. Stability.") [S1][S6]. Heather Lee was her Chief Deputy Auditor for three years and resigned in March 2026, saying the office "has been moving in a direction that falls short in transparency, responsiveness, and consistency" [S1][S5]. With two candidates the race was not on the August primary ballot. The San Juan County Democrats endorsed Lee on Sept. 12, 2026 [S7].

The race turns on management of the office, not on policy: neither candidate states a position on taxes, spending levels or other rubric topics beyond office administration [S1][S5][S6].

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: a first-term incumbent running on her record against a former deputy running on a change of direction [S1][S5][S6].
- No other axis has evidence that separates them: both promise transparent finances and secure elections [S1].
