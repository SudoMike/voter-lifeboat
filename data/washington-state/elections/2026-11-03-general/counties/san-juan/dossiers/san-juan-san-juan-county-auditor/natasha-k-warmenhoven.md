## Background

- San Juan County Auditor since January 2023; Chief Deputy Auditor from January 2020 to 2023 [S1][S3].
- Before the county: accounting for healthcare foundations (2012-2017) and the University of Washington (2017-2020); Washington Secretary of State Certified Elections Administrator [S1][S3].
- B.S. in Accountancy and a finance certificate, Northern Arizona University [S1].
- Community service: Volunteer Income Tax Assistance tax preparer (2009-2019); foster volunteer with the Animal Protection Society of Friday Harbor [S1][S3].

## Record

- Says she helped deliver balanced budgets for 2024-2025 and 2026, standardized the budget book, introduced a Budget-in-Brief and expanded quarterly financial reports [S3][S4].
- Leading the county's move to a new financial management system, delayed by staffing and now set to go live in March 2027 [S3][S4].
- Built an online dog licensing system; cites hand recounts with 100% accuracy [S3][S4].
- Her office prepared the 2027 preliminary budget the County Council received on Aug. 25, 2026, balanced after about $3.7 million in staffing and service changes [S5].
- As elections supervisor, sought volunteers to write arguments against two November measures so the voters' guide would carry both sides [S6].

## Positions

### Office administration
- Running on "Experience. Integrity. Stability."; describes herself as "a calm, steady hand to the daily challenges of local government" [S1][S3].
- Plans for a second term: finish replacing the county's enterprise resource planning software (finance, then human resources/payroll and asset management), protect election integrity, maintain fiscal management standards [S3][S4].

No statement found on taxes, county spending levels, housing, climate or other rubric topics beyond the office's own administration.
