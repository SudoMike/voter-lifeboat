## Background

- Chief Deputy Auditor for San Juan County for three years, through March 2026, working in elections, licensing, budgeting, financial oversight and public records; earlier the office's Payroll Deputy and Elections Specialist [S1][S6].
- Chaired the county's Capital Committee, which reviews and prioritizes infrastructure projects for the County Council [S1][S3].
- Has not held elected office [S1]. Business studies at Salt Lake Community College, with additional training in government finance and elections administration [S1].
- Currently a financial consultant for the Port of Orcas and the Orcas Island Health Care District [S1]. In June 2026 the Friday Harbor Town Council hired her as a consultant, for up to $25,000, to assess the town finance department's operations [S7].
- Worked in the county seasonally for years and made it her permanent home in 2021; homeowner and parent [S3][S6].

## Positions

### Office administration
- Resigned as Chief Deputy Auditor in March 2026 because "the office was moving in a direction I could not support, lacking transparency, responsiveness, and consistency"; running "to rebuild trust and morale in the Auditor's office" [S1][S6].
- Commitments: efficient, responsive service for residents and county departments; "transparent and accountable financial practices"; honest, secure elections [S1][S3].
- Says she will work to reopen the licensing desk five days a week [S3].

No statement found on taxes, county spending levels, housing, climate or other rubric topics beyond the office's own administration.

## Endorsements

- San Juan County Democrats, unanimous, at their Sept. 12, 2026 meeting [S4][S5].
- Former San Juan County Auditor Milene Henley, who hired her [S4][S5]; former Prosecuting Attorney Randall Gaylord [S4][S5]; former state senator and county council member Kevin Ranker [S4]; former Friday Harbor finance director Bethany Berry and former chief deputy auditor José Domenech [S4][S5]; former Auditor's Office staff [S4].
