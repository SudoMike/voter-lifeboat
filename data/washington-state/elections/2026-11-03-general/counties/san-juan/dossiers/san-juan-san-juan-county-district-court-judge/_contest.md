## What the office does

District courts hear misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor criminal cases [S3]; San Juan County has one District Court judge, elected county-wide, nonpartisan, four-year term [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Judge Carolyn Jewett-Platts, on the bench since 2019, seeks another term. [S1][S2]
