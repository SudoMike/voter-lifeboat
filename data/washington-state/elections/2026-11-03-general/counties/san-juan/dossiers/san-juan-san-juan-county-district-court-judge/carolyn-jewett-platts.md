## Background

- San Juan County District Court Judge since 2019 [S1][S2].
- Deputy prosecuting attorney in San Juan County District Court (2015-2018) and in the King County Prosecuting Attorney's District Court Unit (2014) [S1].
- District and Municipal Court Judges' Association board of governors (2022-2025) and co-chair of its legislative committee; volunteer legal advocate for the Immigrant Families Advocacy Project [S1].
- J.D., University of Washington (2015); B.A. in sociology and Spanish, Washington State University (2012) [S1].
