## Background

Byron Burres has been Walla Walla County Assessor since 2023 [S1]. His statement promises a property tax system that is "fair, transparent, and consistent," a well-trained staff and "necessary technological improvements" [S1]. He listed no other experience, education or community service [S1]. He prefers the Republican Party and is the incumbent [S1][S3].

In the August 4 primary, unopposed, Byron Burres received 11,141 votes (97.33%) [S5].
