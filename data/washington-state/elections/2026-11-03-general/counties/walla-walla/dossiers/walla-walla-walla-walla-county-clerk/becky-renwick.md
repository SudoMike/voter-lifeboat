## Background

Becky Renwick is Chief Deputy Clerk and has worked for Walla Walla County since 1997: 24 years in the Department of Court Services (detention manager, juvenile community supervision officer, family court liaison, detention officer) and three years as trial clerk at the District Court before joining the Clerk's office in 2024 [S1]. She is a first-time candidate, a Columbia-Burbank High School graduate who attended Washington State University (1997), and serves on the Touchet Education Foundation [S1]. Clerk Kathy Martin, in office 38 years, did not seek reelection; Renwick was the only candidate to file [S3]. She prefers the Republican Party [S1].

In the August 4 primary, unopposed, Becky Renwick received 11,141 votes (97.89%) [S5].
