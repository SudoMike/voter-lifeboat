## Background

Janelle Carman-Wagner submitted no statement for the county pamphlet or VoteWA guide (S1). Her campaign site says she was an Assistant Attorney General from 2001 to 2005 and has owned Carman Law Office in Walla Walla since 2005. Her practice covers civil, family, administrative and criminal cases at trial and on appeal, including appeals she says she won in the Washington Supreme Court (S2, S3). Since 2015 the Walla Walla County Superior Court has appointed her judge pro tempore more than 20 times, and in 2026 she was appointed judge pro tempore for the District Court (S2, S3). She earned a J.D. from Gonzaga University School of Law (2001), where she graduated first in her class with the Dean's Medal, and a B.A. in English from Whitman College (1994). Before law school she taught high school English at McLoughlin High School in Milton-Freewater (S2, S3). She is vice chair of SOS Health Services, a free community clinic, and has volunteered with 4-H and Pony Club (S2).

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- "The courtroom runs the same way for everyone who walks in. First-timer or regular. Lawyer or none. You get heard, you get respect, you get a fair shot under the law" (S2). She notes that most people in District Court have no lawyer (S3, S4).
- At the Waitsburg forum: "The biggest change we can make is to really hold people accountable. The district court has the capacity to do that but not if we're sending folks to treatment facilities that are having them watch videos and do superficial work." She said the judge can mandate treatment at reliable facilities so that defendants do not end up in superior court (S5).
- At the AAUW forum she supported more treatment options for non-felony offenders and declined to give a personal opinion on a local issue, saying the judge's job is to apply the law (S6).

## Endorsements

None listed on her campaign site as of October 8 (S3).
