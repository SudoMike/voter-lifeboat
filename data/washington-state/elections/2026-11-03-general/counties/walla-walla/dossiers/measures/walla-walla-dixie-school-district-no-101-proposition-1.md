## What it does

Proposition 1 would authorize Dixie School District No. 101 to levy excess property taxes "in place of an expiring levy" to keep funding health, safety and energy-efficiency improvements at Dixie School, as provided in the board's Resolution No. 1 [S1][S2][S3]. The current capital levy expires at the end of 2026. The replacement is a six-year levy for 2027 through 2032 for repairing, modernizing and remodeling school facilities [S1].

- **What the district says it would fund.** The argument for says the school gym floor has settled; the levy would shore up the foundation at the building's northeast corner and level the gym floor and the back/accessible entrance, restoring full use of the gym. It lists past levy-funded work: playground equipment and an underground sprinkler system, dry wells to keep water out of the basement, a revamped bus lane, new exterior stairs, attic insulation and mini-split heating units [S1].
- **Second attempt.** The same levy ($75,000 a year at $0.50 per $1,000 for 2027-2032) failed in the February 2026 special election, 107 yes to 108 no; a hand recount the district requested confirmed the result [S4][S5]. The argument for says it "failed by one vote in the last election cycle" [S1].
- Senior and disabled homeowners may qualify for exemptions through the County Assessor [S1].

## Cost

- $75,000 a year, 2027 through 2032 (six years, $450,000 in all), at an estimated $0.50 per $1,000 of assessed value [S1][S3].
- The argument for says "This levy does not create a new tax," since it replaces an expiring capital levy [S1]. The prior levy's rate was not found in the sources reviewed.

## Arguments for

Written by committee members Lisa Davis and Dean Davis: a replacement levy for "essential health, safety, and energy-efficient improvements"; the gym repairs; past levy work as evidence that "the school board is fiscally responsible with your tax dollars"; "Vote YES to support our students, our school, and our community" [S1].

No argument against was printed in the county pamphlet [S1]. VoteWA's guide record names Peter Berg as the committee against but also carries no argument [S2].
