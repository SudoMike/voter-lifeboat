## Background

Jared N. Hawkins has been Walla Walla County District Court's part-time judge since winning the open seat in 2022 over Nicholas Holce (about 62% to 37% on election night) [S2][S3]. At that time the Union-Bulletin reported that he owned Hawkins Law in Walla Walla and served as a lieutenant colonel in the Air Force Reserve, providing legal services for the reserves [S3]. In the 2022 race he talked about bringing a drug-treatment court to district court [S3]. He filed for the full-time seat that Judge Kristian Hedine is vacating and is unopposed [S2][S4]. He submitted no statement for the 2026 pamphlet or online guide [S1].
