## What the office does

The full-time judge is the presiding judge of Walla Walla County District Court, which hears misdemeanors, traffic infractions, small claims, protection orders and civil disputes not involving real estate for the county and the cities of Walla Walla and College Place [S2].

## Race dynamics

Judge Kristian Hedine, on the full-time bench since 2014, is retiring; part-time Judge Jared N. Hawkins is the only candidate [S1][S2].
