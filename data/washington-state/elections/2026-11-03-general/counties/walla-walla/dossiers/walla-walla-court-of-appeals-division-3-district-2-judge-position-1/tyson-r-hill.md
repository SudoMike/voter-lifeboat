## Background

- **Appointment.** Gov. Bob Ferguson appointed Hill to Division III on Nov. 19, 2025, effective Jan. 1, 2026, filling the seat of retiring Judge George Fearing; it was Ferguson's first appellate appointment [S2]. An appointee serves until a successor is elected, so the seat is on this ballot for the two years left in the term [S1][S3].
- **Trial bench.** Grant County Superior Court judge from 2021 (appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee), after more than seven years on the Grant County District Court bench. He co-founded and presided over Grant County's first therapeutic drug court and has served as a pro tem judge for Division III [S1][S2].
- **Earlier career.** Four years as lead appellate attorney in the Grant County Prosecutor's Office, prosecuting felonies including murder, arson and assault; law clerk to Supreme Court Justice James M. Johnson; consultant and staff assistant to the U.S.-China Economic & Security Review Commission; Senate Finance Committee intern [S1][S2].
- **Education.** J.D., University of Washington School of Law (2008); B.S., Brigham Young University (2004) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- His role is to review trial-court decisions "to ensure the law is followed. I have no agenda beyond that" [S1].
- Parties, law enforcement, victims and the community "want a judge who is impartial, takes their case seriously, and correctly applies the law" [S1].
