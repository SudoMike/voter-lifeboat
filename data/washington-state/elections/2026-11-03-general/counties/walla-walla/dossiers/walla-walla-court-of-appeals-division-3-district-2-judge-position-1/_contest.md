## What the office does

The Court of Appeals reviews trial-court decisions to check that the law was followed [S1]. Division III's District 2 (Adams, Asotin, Benton, Columbia, Franklin, Garfield, Grant, Walla Walla and Whitman counties) elects this judge; Walla Walla County ships its own county-scoped information-only copy, as Benton and Grant do.

## Race dynamics

- **Appointed incumbent.** Gov. Bob Ferguson appointed Grant County Superior Court Judge Tyson R. Hill effective Jan. 1, 2026, to replace retiring Judge George Fearing [S2].
- **Why it is on the ballot.** An appointee serves until a successor is elected at the next general election; this race is for the two-year unexpired term [S1][S3].
- **Uncontested.** Hill is the only candidate [S1].
