## Background

The county commissioners appointed Sheryl Park auditor on February 2, 2026, after Auditor Karen Martin retired at the end of 2025 (S1, S3). She is an IRS Enrolled Agent with more than 20 years as a tax professional and a background in bookkeeping, banking and financial services. She owns Sheryl's Bookkeeping & Tax Service and formerly ran a daycare business (S1, S2). She holds a B.S. in Office Administration from Walla Walla University and an Executive MBA in Strategic Leadership, and has completed CPA exam-qualifying coursework (S1, S2). She has volunteered with the AARP Tax-Aide program since 2015 and is treasurer of the Walla Walla County Republican Party (S1, S4). She has lived in the county for 30 years (S1). She prefers the Republican Party (S1).

In the certified August 4 primary she placed first with 6,700 votes (43.12%) (S9).

## Positions

### Office management and reporting
- Says that since taking office she has "focused on modernizing systems, improving customer service, and strengthening staff morale" (S1).
- On budgeting: "My focus is on maintaining strong internal controls, improving efficiency and ensuring transparency," with "clear procedures, accurate and timely financial information, and consistent communication with departments and the County Commissioners" (S4).
- At the Waitsburg forum she said her staff was implementing a new program for more accurate and timely financial reporting, with data posted on the county website (S6).
- Platform: "Every Dollar Accounted For"; she says she treats tax dollars "like her own" (S2).

### Elections
- Elections was the one area where she lacked work experience; she says she completed more than 50 hours of Secretary of State election training after her appointment (S4).
- Asked about federal requests for voter information, she said she would follow the Secretary of State's directives (S5).
- She will not participate in ballot processing while she is a candidate; other certified staff will oversee it (S8).
- Platform promises "secure, accurate, and transparent elections with accountability at every step" (S2).
