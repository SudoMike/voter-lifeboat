## What the office does

The auditor oversees the county's accounting, elections, licensing and recording departments, and prepares and presents the countywide budget to the Board of Commissioners (S3, S4). The winner serves the rest of 2026 and a four-year term through 2030; the 2027 salary is about $122,315 (S3).

## Race dynamics

Karen Martin, auditor for 27 years, retired at the end of 2025 with a year left on her term. The commissioners appointed Sheryl Park in February 2026 to serve until the election (S3). The county is in its second year of a hiring freeze and has cut department budgets (S4). In the certified August primary Park (prefers Republican) received 6,700 votes (43.12%) and Tim Reeves (prefers Democrat) 5,727 (36.86%). Gregory Tompkins (15.93%) had withdrawn after becoming interim fairgrounds manager but stayed on the ballot; Marjorie Denton Sanborn received 4.03% (S2, S3). Each finalist's largest donor was their county party, $5,000 each (S6). Because the auditor runs elections, Park has said she will not take part in ballot processing while she is a candidate (S5).

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Experience:** Park is the appointed incumbent, a 20-year tax professional and IRS Enrolled Agent who stresses the training and modernization she has done since February. Reeves, a retired economist and finance consultant, has never held office and says "something is broken in the Auditor's office" (S1, S3).
2. **Spending and accountability:** both stress accurate, timely reporting. Reeves proposes actual-to-budget reporting, a public dashboard of key performance indicators and quarterly "State of the County" updates. Park points to internal controls, efficiency and a new reporting program (S3, S4).
3. **Elections administration:** asked about federal requests for voter data, Park said she would follow the Secretary of State's direction. Reeves said a request not authorized by the Secretary of State "must be refused and reported" (record in the candidate dossiers).
4. Positions on the 2026 statewide initiatives: none found for either candidate.
