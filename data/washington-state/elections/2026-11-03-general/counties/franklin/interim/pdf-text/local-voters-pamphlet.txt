46 Local Voters’ Pamphlet Franklin County | Voting Tips Keep Your Registration Current We are constantly working to keep our voter rolls up to date with the help of the Department of Licensing, the USPS, local obituaries and the Department of Health, but you can also help by following these simple steps: • Update your address Call our office to update your address or go online to VoteWA.gov. You can also email us or visit us in person! • Set up a mailing address Contact our office to set up a seasonal, temporary, or a different mailing address you need your ballot mailed to. • Return to sender! If you receive a ballot for a person who does not live at your address, write “Return to Sender” on the unopened envelope and put it back in the mail. Do not drop it in a ballot drop box. If you receive a ballot for a deceased voter, write “Deceased” on the unopened envelope and put it back in the mail. We will research and follow up. Voting Tips Use a Blue or Black Ink Pen to Fill in Your Choices. You are not required to vote in every race. To Correct a Vote Simply cross out the incorrect choice and fill in the oval next to your new choice. Sign Your Envelope Your signature must match the signature on your voter registration record in order for your ballot to move on to the next step. Check the Status of Your Ballot on VoteWA.gov to verify if it has been received by the Franklin County Elections Division. Election staff will contact you if: • Your signature is missing • Your signature does not match the signature on your voter registration record View Election Results Online After 8:00 p.m. on election night, tallied results are posted at sos.wa.gov/elections. Results are unofficial and may change until certification. Return Your Ballot Early! If Using a Ballot Drop Box: • Deposit your ballot before 8:00 p.m. on Election Day • There are 8 ballot drop boxes in Franklin County, available 18 days before an election • Only our permanent election staff collect ballots from our ballot boxes • All ballot drop boxes close at 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. If Returning By Mail: • No postage necessary • Mail your ballot as soon as possible • Ballots must be postmarked by Election Day • If Election Day is close, drop your ballot in an official ballot drop box to ensure it is received on time. If you cannot personally return your ballot, only let people you trust deliver it for you. If You Cannot Sign Your Ballot: • Mark an “x” on the signature line in the presence of two witnesses; • The two witnesses must sign in the provided spaces.
