## What the office does
Port districts may build and run terminals, airports, industrial and manufacturing facilities and related improvements (S2). Engelke's statement names the airport, the Reimann Industrial Center, Big Pasco, the AIMS Center and rural support as the port's work (S1). The seat is a six-year term (S1). From 2026 the Port of Pasco elects commissioners by district: only District 3 voters vote in this race, in both primary and general (S4). In 2025 the District 3 seat was still elected port-wide (S5); state law otherwise has the whole port vote in the general (S6). The Port of Pasco does not cover the whole county; the east end is in the Port of Kahlotus (S5).

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Port Commission President Hans-Joachim Engelke, appointed in January 2025 and elected to a one-year term in November 2025, is the only candidate. Only District 3 voters see this race, the first by-district port election. The pamphlet marks the race "Unopposed" (S1).
