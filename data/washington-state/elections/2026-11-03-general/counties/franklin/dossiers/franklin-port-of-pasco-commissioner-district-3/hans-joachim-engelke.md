## Background
Engelke was appointed to the Port of Pasco commission in January 2025, elected to a one-year term in November 2025, and has been president of the commission since January 1, 2026 (S1). Born and raised in West Germany, he served with Allied NATO command forces in Central Europe from 1978 to 1984, immigrated legally in 1984 and became a U.S. citizen in 1989 (S1). Since 1989 he has owned a diversified crop, orchard and custom farming operation in Basin City (S1). He is a graduate, board member and chair of the Washington Agriculture and Forestry Leadership Program (S1). The seat is nonpartisan.

## Positions
- Supports "the path of diversified, responsible growth" through the airport, Reimann Industrial Center, Big Pasco, the AIMS Center, the Ag Symbiosis Project and support for rural communities (S1).
- Aims to identify "beneficial economic options and opportunities for growth, job creation, income and tax base" (S1).
