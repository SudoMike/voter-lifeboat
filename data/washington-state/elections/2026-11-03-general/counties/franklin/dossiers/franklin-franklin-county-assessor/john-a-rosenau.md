## Background
Rosenau is the incumbent Franklin County Assessor, elected in 2021 and re-elected in 2022 "with over 98% of the vote". He chaired the Southeast Washington Assessors for three years and sits on the Washington State Association of County Assessors executive board (S1). He reports more than 40 years in business, finance and public service, including work as a first vice president and senior regional manager over 500+ employees and 26 financial centers. He also lists law enforcement service from patrol officer to chief of police, and pastoral leadership. He holds an executive MBA and an M.Min. (S1). He was raised in Franklin County and prefers the Republican Party (S1).

In the August 4 primary he received 4,979 votes (44.7%) to Hollingsworth's 6,032 (54.1%) (S5).

## Positions

### Assessments and property taxes
- Says that since 2021 his office has "strengthened training, improved consistency in valuations, and implemented clear standards that promote fairness and accountability", and built "one of the most professional and highly trained assessor offices in Washington State" (S1).
- Says he has worked with the state assessors' association for "fair and sustainable property tax relief" and "improved exemption programs for seniors, individuals with disabilities, farmers, and small business owners" (S1).
- His campaign videos stress that the assessor "does not set taxes—only valuations, levy math, exemptions, and records", and that taxing districts and voters set the dollar amounts collected (S2).
- In 2023 he described the senior and disability exemption as meant to keep people from losing property to tax foreclosure, and said his office walks applicants through it (S3).

### Outreach
- In June 2026 he met with farmers in Connell with his agricultural appraisal team on current-use programs, assessments, exemptions and how voter-approved levies affect taxes (S2).
