## Background
Hollingsworth is a state-certified residential real estate appraiser with a supervisor accreditation. He says he has more than 20 years of appraisal experience, more than 925 hours of appraisal-specific education, and owns an appraisal practice working in Franklin, Benton, Yakima and Walla Walla counties (S1). His campaign site says he has been licensed since 2004 and has lived in Pasco for more than 15 years (S2). He was born and raised in the Tri-Cities. He is a Franklin County Republican precinct committee officer (2024, 2026) and the county GOP's state committeeman, and a graduate of Leadership Tri-Cities Class XXIX (S1). He prefers the Republican Party (S1).

In the August 4 primary he received 6,032 votes (54.1%) to Rosenau's 4,979 (44.7%) (S5).

## Positions

### Assessments and property taxes
- He says he decided to run after helping "many residents with Board of Equalization hearings" and talking with farmers, residents, Realtors and officials, and that resolving "issues with property assessments" needs someone with real-world appraisal experience (S1).
- "My focus will be to ensure that every property owner only pays their fair share of taxes and that assessments are fair, equitable, consistent, and transparent" (S1). His site: "No Franklin County resident should pay more than their fair share of the property tax burden" (S2).
- Promises "improved appraisal practice training, strict adherence to professional appraisal standards, and open communication with property owners", and says his goal is "to restore confidence in the Assessor's office" (S1).

### Office management
- His site lists three pillars: professional oversight (applying appraisal expertise to systems and accuracy), equity in assessment, and supporting staff "without micromanagement". It quotes him: "I'm not running to criticize—I'm running to lead" (S2).

## Campaign finance
PDC summary: $10,703.40 raised, $14,330.01 spent (S3). About $5,972 of the itemized contributions are his own; the largest outside contribution is $1,200 from the Washington Association of Realtors Public Affairs Council (S4).

## Scoring notes
- Experience: challenger with long private appraisal experience but no tenure in the office; runs on fixing assessment practice.
- Spending/accountability: frames the race around standards, training and accuracy in an office he says needs restored confidence.
- Taxes: "fair share" language concerns accuracy of individual assessments, not tax levels; the assessor does not set levies.
