## Background
Orosco is the presiding judge of Franklin County District Court, seeking a second term (S1). She has served as judge pro tempore in Benton County District Court and Pasco Municipal Court (S1). Before the bench she was a defense attorney for 14 years in Franklin and Benton Superior and District Courts and the municipal courts of Pasco, Prosser, Toppenish and Sunnyside (S1). Earlier she worked as a paralegal, a legal intern at Fluor Hanford and in Gonzaga Law School's Legal Assistance Clinic (S1, S2). She earned her J.D. at Gonzaga University School of Law in 2008 and graduated cum laude from Washington State University in 2005 (S1). Her campaign site puts her time on the bench at more than three and a half years (S2).

## Positions

### Courts and case management
- "I believe every person who comes before the court deserves patience, respect, and a fair hearing free from bias or personal agendas. Justice requires accountability and respect" (S1).
- Says the Therapeutic Court program she created "relieves burdens on law enforcement and taxpayers while holding participants accountable and providing opportunities to improve their lives" (S1). It now takes referrals from both District and Superior Court (S2).
- Next goals: a Veterans Therapeutic Court track with the Veterans Center (S1), courthouse security improvements through grants, and a driver's license reinstatement program to reduce repeat criminal filings (S2).
- Her campaign site says she expanded the work crew program as an alternative to incarceration and reduced incarceration costs through accountability programs (S2).

## Record
- District Court judge, first term; presiding judge (S1).
- Says she has brought more than $1 million back to Franklin County through state funding requests and grants for courthouse security, therapeutic court programs and training (S1, S2).
- Member of the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association education and therapeutic court committees (S1).
- Community outreach through school partnerships and educational court programs (S1).
