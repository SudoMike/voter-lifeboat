## Background
Nguyen is a public defense attorney. He reports five years in Benton and Franklin County Superior, District and Juvenile courts and Pasco Municipal Court. He also reports five years as Franklin County's preliminary appearance attorney, "making hundreds of bail arguments and handling thousands of first appearances" (S1). Earlier he worked for World Relief Legal Services (2017) and interned with the WSU Police Department and the WSU Department of Criminal Justice (S1). He earned a J.D. from Georgia State University College of Law (2015-2018) and graduated magna cum laude from Washington State University in criminal justice and political science (S1). He attended Tri-Cities Prep, Chiawana High School and Columbia Basin College, and says he is running to "give back to the community that raised me" (S1). He lists 380+ hours of pro bono legal work (S1).

The campaign website named in his statement had expired when checked (S3). He has registered with the PDC and reports no contributions (S2).

## Positions

### Courts and case management
- Says working daily with prosecutors, court staff, judges, the Office of Public Defense, jail staff and defendants gives him "an innate ability to hear criticism from each perspective and make appropriate changes when necessary" (S1).
- Pledges to "appropriately weigh outcomes for victims and defendants", "manage the court as a neutral officiant of the law rather than imposing my beliefs from the bench", and "prioritize judicial efficiency by ensuring the Court respects jail staff and attorneys' time" (S1).
- Pledges to "focus on rehabilitation by actively participating in therapeutic courts" and to "respect defendants' dignity while requiring accountability for offenses" (S1).
- Would "impose cost parity with courts such as Benton County and Pasco Municipal" (S1).
