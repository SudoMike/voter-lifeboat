## What the office does
A public utility district may build, buy and run electric generation and distribution and water systems (S2). Franklin PUD's commissioners govern it and oversee its general manager (S1). The seat is a six-year term (S1). The commissioner must live in District 2, but the whole PUD votes in the general (S5); in 2024 the District 3 seat appeared on all 123 of the county's precincts (S4).

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: former Franklin PUD general manager Tim Nies is the only candidate. The pamphlet marks the race "Unopposed" (S1).
