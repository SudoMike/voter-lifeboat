## Background
Nies began his career as the Washington State Auditor's audit manager for the Tri-Cities, then served at Franklin PUD as director of administrative services and general manager, and finished with five years at Energy Northwest on regional energy (S1). He holds a B.S. in accounting from Metropolitan State College in Denver (S1). He has served on local boards including the Lourdes Hospital Foundation, United Way, the Boys and Girls Club, TRIDEC and the Franklin PUD Rate Advisory Committee (S1). The seat is nonpartisan.

## Positions
- Names Franklin PUD's key challenges as "maintaining the Snake River Dams, ensuring dependable power availability at a reasonable cost, and safeguarding local control" (S1).
- Wants District 2 ratepayers to "continue to have a strong advocate representing them" (S1).
