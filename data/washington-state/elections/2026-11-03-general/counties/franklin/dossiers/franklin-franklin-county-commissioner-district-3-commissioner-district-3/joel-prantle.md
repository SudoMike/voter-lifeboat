## Background
Prantle has lived in Franklin County for more than 50 years and graduated from Pasco High School in 1987 (S1). He is vice president of PF&S Railway Supply, Inc., a family-owned business his parents founded in 1979, and a volunteer member of the Franklin County planning commission (S1). He and his wife have raised eight children in the county (S1). He prefers the Republican Party (S1). In the August 4 primary he received 1,924 votes (40.0%) to Didier's 2,764 (57.5%) (S4). He did not respond to NonStop Local's primary interview request (S5).
