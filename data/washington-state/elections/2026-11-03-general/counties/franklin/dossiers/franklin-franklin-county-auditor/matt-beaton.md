## Background
Beaton has been Franklin County Auditor since 2011 and was a Franklin County Republican precinct committee officer from 2020 to 2022 (S1). Before that he owned Sage Appraisal Services through 2010. He holds a BA in business administration from the University of Washington, has been a certified real estate appraiser for 31 years and an inactive real estate broker for 36 years (S1). He prefers the Republican Party (S1).

## Positions
- Says his team has been "stopping fraud and malfeasance while delivering superior elections, recording, and licensing services" and consolidated the Elections Division at 404 W Clark St, Pasco (S1).
- "I will continue to publicly press for voter roll integrity and improved election processes." He criticizes state leadership for refusing to cooperate with "our federal partners" on voter-roll checks: "Trust but verify" (S1).
- Promises "an efficient, cost-effective government in Franklin County" (S1).
