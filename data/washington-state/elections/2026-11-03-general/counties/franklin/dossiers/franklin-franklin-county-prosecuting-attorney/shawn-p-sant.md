## Background
Sant has been Franklin County Prosecuting Attorney since 2011 (S1). He was earlier a deputy prosecuting attorney in Franklin County (2004-2006) and an associate attorney at Mendoza Law Office (2006-2010). He served in the Air Force and is a U.S. Army Reserve civil affairs officer. He was a police officer in Prosser and a reserve officer in Richland (S1). He holds a J.D. from the University of Idaho (2004) (S1). He prefers the Republican Party (S1).

## Positions
