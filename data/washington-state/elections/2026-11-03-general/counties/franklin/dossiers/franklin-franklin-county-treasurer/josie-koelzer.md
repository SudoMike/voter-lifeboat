## Background
Koelzer has been Franklin County Treasurer since 2011 (S1). She was earlier a financial manager for an insurance firm and a large agricultural business and has managed her family farm for more than 30 years (S1). She has served as secretary of the state treasurers' association executive board, as a treasurer trustee on the board of WSCO, and on the State Treasurer's Local Government Investment Pool advisory board (S1). She holds a BA in psychology from Washington State University (S1). She prefers the Republican Party (S1).

## Positions
