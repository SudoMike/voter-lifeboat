## Background
Ochoa has been Franklin County's Chief Deputy Clerk for 12 years, after 25 years as a paralegal and administrative leader at private law firms, and owns a local business (S1). She has an AA in paralegal studies from Columbia Basin College and is a member of the Washington State Association of County Clerks (S1). She is board president of Benton Franklin Legal Aid and has been a Tri-Cities Sunrise Rotary member since 2014 (S1). She prefers the Republican Party (S1).
