## What the office does
The sheriff is the county's chief executive law-enforcement officer and conservator of the peace. The sheriff and deputies enforce state law, make arrests, execute court orders and warrants, serve process, and work with other agencies under mutual aid (S2). The Franklin County Sheriff's Office patrols the unincorporated county and Mesa through a sub-station there (S1).

## Race dynamics
The seat is open: Sheriff J.D. Raymond announced his retirement in April 2026 after 45 years in law enforcement in Pasco and the county (S3). Both finalists prefer the Republican Party (S1). Undersheriff Monty Huber, a 23-year veteran of the office, led the August primary with 5,932 votes (47.8%). Connell Police Chief Chris Lee had 3,791 (30.6%), and Kohl St. Peter, who did not advance, had 2,638 (21.3%) (S4). Huber reports $32,055 raised and Lee $38,670 (S5).

Huber runs as the inside candidate. He cites his part in accreditation, deputy training, the Mesa sub-station and the new behavioral health recovery center, and says that center is the issue he would spend political capital to fund. Lee runs as an outside leader from another agency. He promises to support deputies, "keep dangerous criminals off our streets", and bring transparency and fiscal discipline, and he has said the sheriff's office needs to "realign" with its mission (S1; candidate dossiers).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience/record:** continuity from the undersheriff's chair against a police chief from a small city who has not served in the county office.
2. **Safety:** both are enforcement-oriented. Huber puts more weight on mental-health and addiction response and officer wellness. Lee puts more on law and order, victims and incarceration of dangerous offenders.
3. **Spending:** Lee pledges "fiscal discipline". Huber talks of investing in people through training and wellness to cut costly turnover.
4. Immigration: Lee said he would spend political capital on a pathway to citizenship for DACA recipients. This is recorded in his dossier; `immigration` is not scored for county contests.
