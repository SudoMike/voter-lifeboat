Statement
The Auditor is responsible for multiple highly important duties such as reviewing and recording all expenditures of the county, assisting the Board of County Commissioners with monitoring and reporting the budget, financial reporting, recording and preserving documents, issuing marriage licenses, processing vehicle licensing transactions, voter registration and overseeing the election process.
During my time as your County Auditor, I have installed cameras in the elections processing room with a live feed that can be accessed through the county’s website creating increased access for public observation of ballot processing during an election. I have implemented online access to recorded documents, improving public access. Recently, I was involved in implementing a new accounting system to improve the financial tracking and reporting capabilities of the county.
