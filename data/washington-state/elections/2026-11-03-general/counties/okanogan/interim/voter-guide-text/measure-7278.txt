# CITY OF BREWSTER | City of Brewster, Proposition 1 | EMERGENCY MEDICAL CARE OR EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES CONTINUATION LEVY

## BallotTitle
Will the City of Brewster be authorized to continue to impose regular property tax levies in the amount of $0.50 or less per one thousand dollars of assessed valuation for each of six consecutive years beginning in 2027, to be used for emergency medical care or emergency medical services within the City of Brewster as provided in RCW 84.52.069?

## ExplanatoryStatement
If approved by voters, Proposition 1 would authorize the City of Brewster to continue to levy a regular property tax previously approved by the voters in 2020 to provide revenue to continue contracting with Douglas-Okanogan Fire District 15 for emergency medical services. The purpose of the levy is to fund emergency medical services, including related personnel costs and training for such personnel and related equipment, supplies, vehicles and structures needed for the provision of emergency medical services in the City, as authorized by RCW 84.52.069. The renewed levy would be imposed at the same rate of $0.50 per $1000 of assessed valuation as the expiring levy on all taxable property within the City and would continue for six consecutive years beginning in 2027.

Julie Norton - City Attorney - Ogden, Murphy, Wallace
