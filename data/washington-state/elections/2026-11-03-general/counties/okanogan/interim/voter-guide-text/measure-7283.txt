# PUBLIC HOSPITAL DISTRICT NO. 1 | Public Hospital District No. 1, Proposition 1 | BONDS FOR HOSPITAL RENOVATION AND IMPROVEMENT

## BallotTitle
The Board of Commissioners of Public Hospital District No. 1, Okanogan and Douglas Counties, Washington, adopted Resolution No. 2026-10 concerning a proposition for the construction of improvements to Three Rivers Hospital. If approved, this proposition authorizes the District to construct, equip, remodel, renovate, and furnish improvements to Three Rivers Hospital and carry out other capital improvements deemed necessary or advisable by the Commission; issue no more than $48,000,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 30 years; and levy annual excess property taxes to repay the bonds, all as provided in Resolution No. 2026-10. Should this proposition be:

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Board of Commissioners of Public Hospital District No. 1, Okanogan and Douglas Counties, Washington (Three Rivers Hospital) has determined that it is necessary to submit to the voters of the District a proposition authorizing the District to issue general obligation bonds for the purpose of paying costs of renovating and remodeling Three Rivers Hospital in Brewster, Washington.

The Board of Commissioners determined that the existing condition of health care facilities requires that the District construct and equip improvements to the existing hospital facility and carry out other capital improvements.

If approved, this proposition would authorize up to $48,000,000 of general obligation bonds that would mature within a maximum of 30 years and be repaid from annual excess property tax levies at an estimated rate of 73 cents per $1,000 of assessed value, or an estimated $18 per month for a $300,000 home. Exemptions from taxes may be available to certain homeowners. For more information, please call the Assessor’s Office in Okanogan County (509-422-7190) or Douglas County (509-745-8521).

Bradley J. Berg, Special Counsel, Foster Garvey P.C.

## ArgumentAgainst
Before voters commit $48 million for hospital construction in Hospital District #1, they deserve solid county-wide information to confirm how best to meet the medical needs of the residents of Okanogan County.
We don’t have that information, and the current proposal asks voters to commit to 30 years of increased property taxes and $48 million for new construction without the county-wide data showing that this is the best way to spend the money. Committing money, without studying county-wide data, locks in the current system, and stands in the way of designing what might be better.
Perhaps a county-wide study would support a central facility with outlying 24-hour urgent care centers. Maybe something else...but good information is the way to find the best use of limited health care tax dollars.
Without that information, voters should Vote No on the current proposal. Good information can later support funding what’s best.
Written by Melvin Sorensen

## RebuttalAgainst
Okanogan County’s three small hospitals have few overnight patients and compete with each other for patients and staff.
Three Rivers is fragile. New Medicaid reductions mean even less financial stability. This bond doesn’t help. It funds construction. If Three Rivers Hospital fails, residents get decades of increased taxes, but no hospital.
Before additional taxes, we need county-wide data to inform best options for improving hospital care in the county.
Vote No!
Written by Melvin Sorensen
