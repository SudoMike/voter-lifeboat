# FIRE DISTRICT 01 | Fire District No. 1, Proposition 1 | Levy Lid Lift

## BallotTitle
The Board of Okanogan County Fire Protection District No. 1 adopted Resolution No. 97 concerning a proposition to establish its regular property tax levy.
This proposition authorizes the District to establish its regular property tax levy at $.50 per $1,000 of assessed value to be assessed in 2026 and collected in 2027. The funds will maintain and improve fire protection, and life safety services, replace apparatus and equipment, and provide for firefighter safety. The dollar amount of the 2026 levy collected in 2027 shall serve as the base for computing subsequent levy limitations as provided by chapter 84.55 RCW

## ExplanatoryStatement
Okanogan County Fire Protection District No. 1 provides emergency fire protection and emergency medical services to its citizens. Due to the statutory 1% limitation on property tax increases, the Fire District’s current levy has dropped to approximately $.22 per thousand in 2026. The maximum regular levy rate for Washington fire districts is $1.50 per $1,000 of assessed value.

If approved by the voters, this proposition authorizes the fire district to establish its regular real property tax levy rate at $.50 per $1,000 of assessed valuation in 2026 for collection in 2027.

The Board of Commissioners has not requested a levy adjustment in many years and has determined that this proposition is critical to be able to continue operating your fire department.

The funds generated by the levy are necessary to provide an effective level of services given the constantly increasing demand for services and the increasing costs for insurance, equipment and supplies. Approval of this measure will allow the District to provide for fire fighter safety as the District responds to new challenges including electronic car fires, lithium battery fires and the increasing drug epidemic within our community.

Brian Snure - Fire District Attorney - Snure Law

## ArgumentFor
I am writing to express my unwavering support for the Oroville Fire District No. 1 levy lid lift on the November 2026 ballot.
As a resident of the Oroville community, I have seen firsthand how much we rely on our local volunteer firefighters. Every day, our volunteers respond within minutes to life-threatening emergencies, whether battling wildfires, providing critical medical assistance, or responding to serious highway accidents.
The most recent fire season has been among the worst in Washington State's history, placing unprecedented demands on local fire districts. Rising costs for fuel, equipment, vehicle maintenance, and training continue to strain the district's budget.
Voting YES ensures the Oroville Fire District No. 1 keeps pace with inflation, maintain essential equipment, and continue providing the timely emergency response that protects our community. Investing in our fire district is an investment in the safety and well-being of Oroville, both now and for future generations.
