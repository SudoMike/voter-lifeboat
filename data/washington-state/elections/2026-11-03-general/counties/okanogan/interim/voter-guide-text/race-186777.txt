Statement

During my first term as County Clerk, I prioritized modernization, accountability, and the reevaluation of long-standing practices. I encourage you to visit the Clerk’s page on the county website to review my annual correspondence and learn more about all the progress we have made!
I also had the honor of serving as Vice-President, President and Past President of the Washington Association of County Clerks (WSACC). This organization joins the Clerks of each county to aid in impactful changes and continuity across our state of Washington’s court system. In June of 2025, I was awarded Clerk of the Year for WSACC, an award that is voted on by County Clerks across the State of Washington.
As your County Clerk, I will remain committed to addressing difficult issues directly and implementing necessary changes to maintain full compliance and efficiency within our county. Our community is growing, and with growth comes change and new challenges. You can rest assured that I will meet each challenge with diligence, transparency, and a continued dedication to serving you, the public. I look forward to another term of effective change and proudly serving the people of Okanogan County as your County Clerk, thank you!
