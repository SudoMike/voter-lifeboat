Statement

For more than 16 years, I have proudly served the communities of Okanogan County and North Central Washington in a variety of law enforcement roles. My focus has always been on building trust, strengthening relationships, and ensuring our Sheriff’s Office remains connected and responsive to the people we serve. Throughout my career, I have worked in patrol, investigations, supervision, and leadership roles, including patrol officer, K9 handler, Sergeant, Field Training Officer, Chief Criminal Deputy, and Sheriff. No matter the position, I have believed that effective law enforcement starts with strong community partnerships and open communication.

I have worked closely with local schools, tribal communities, protective services, and regional partners to address challenges facing our families, neighborhoods, and businesses. I believe public safety is strongest when community members and law enforcement work together with mutual respect and shared goals.
My commitment is to keep Okanogan County safe while maintaining a Sheriff’s Office that is visible, approachable, transparent, and dedicated to serving every community with integrity, professionalism, and compassion.
I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue building a safe, connected, trusted leadership, community partnerships, and a Sheriff’s Office committed to serving every citizen with integrity and professionalism.
