Statement
As a lifelong resident of Okanogan County and dedicated public servant, I am honored to run for Okanogan County Coroner. Guided by my Christian faith and a commitment to serving others with compassion, integrity, and respect, I bring more than 28 years of law enforcement and investigative experience serving our community.
My career began with the United States Forest Service and continued with the Okanogan County Jail, Winthrop Marshal’s Office, and Okanogan County Sheriff’s Office. I served as a K-9 handler, Sergeant, Detective Sergeant, and investigated numerous homicide cases, suspicious deaths, and complex investigations. With hundreds of death investigations completed during my career, I have the experience and leadership to serve our citizens professionally and compassionately.
Since 2023, I have served as Chief Deputy Coroner for Okanogan County and am a state-certified Medicolegal Death Investigator. In this role, I work closely with families, medical professionals, law enforcement, and emergency responders, providing compassionate support and professionalism during life’s most difficult moments.
The citizens of Okanogan County deserve a Coroner who prioritizes families with compassion and professionalism. My experience, leadership, and commitment to public service will ensure a seamless transition within the Coroner’s Office and continued dedicated service to our community.
