Statement
Judge Short has been a dependable leader on the District Court bench for over 13 years. He’s been retained by voters in 2014, 2018, and 2022. He serves as the Presiding Judge and supervises the judicial business of the Court. In 2024, his peers from the District & Municipal Court Judges Association honored him with the David A. Steiner Judicial Leadership Award.
He is dedicated to serving the public with integrity in an impartial and ethical manner. He is committed to following the rule of law and the constitution, and to making sure everyone is treated fairly and with respect.
Judge Short is trusted and respected by his colleagues. He is Dean of the Washington State Judicial College, helping plan and deliver the education all new judges from all levels of courts across the state are required to complete. He also serves as faculty for the National Council of Juvenile & Family Court Judges, the Center for Justice Innovation, and the National Judicial Institute on Domestic Violence.
He has deep roots in Okanogan County. Born in Omak, he grew up on a cattle ranch his grandfather established near Nespelem. He is a husband and a stepfather to two teenagers.

Statement

Nothing matters more to me than seeing families, friends, and neighbors who live, work, and raise children in Okanogan County prosperous and free from the destructive effects that substance abuse, domestic violence, and impaired driving have on our community. I respectfully ask for your vote, so together, we can restore confidence, efficiency, and justice in the Okanogan County District Court.
