## What it does

The Board of Okanogan County Fire Protection District No. 1, the Oroville-area fire district, adopted Resolution No. 97 asking voters to set its regular property tax levy at $0.50 per $1,000 of assessed value, assessed in 2026 and collected in 2027 [S1][S4]. The 2027 dollar amount then becomes the base for computing later levy limits under chapter 84.55 RCW [S1][S2].

- **What it pays for.** Maintaining and improving fire protection and life safety services, replacing apparatus and equipment, and firefighter safety [S1]. The district provides fire protection and emergency medical services [S1].
- **Who votes.** District voters (1,176 registered in November 2025) [S3].
- **Vote needed.** A simple majority (RCW 84.55.050) [S2].

## Cost

From about $0.22 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2026 to $0.50 per $1,000 for 2027 collection [S1]. The ballot materials give no dollar amount per home [S1]. The legal maximum for a fire district's regular levy is $1.50 per $1,000 [S1].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Because of the 1% annual limit on growth in regular property tax collections, the district's levy rate has fallen to about $0.22 per $1,000 [S1]. The board says it has not asked for a levy adjustment "in many years" and that the proposition is "critical to be able to continue operating your fire department" [S1]. After 2027 the levy again grows by at most the statutory limit factor, from the new base [S1][S2].

## Arguments for

Explanatory statement, by district attorney Brian Snure: the money is needed for rising demand and rising costs of insurance, equipment and supplies, and for firefighter safety as the district responds to electric-car fires, lithium battery fires and "the increasing drug epidemic" [S1].

Statement for (committee: Denise Dhane): Oroville relies on volunteer firefighters who respond within minutes to wildfires, medical emergencies and highway accidents; the latest fire season was among the worst in state history; rising costs for fuel, equipment, vehicle maintenance and training strain the budget; voting yes ensures the district "keeps pace with inflation" [S1].

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted [S1], and no PDC committee for or against was found [S5]. No press coverage of the measure was found.
