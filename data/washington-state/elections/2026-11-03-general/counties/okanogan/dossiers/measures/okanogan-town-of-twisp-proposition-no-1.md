## What it does

The Town of Twisp asks its voters for a one-year excess property tax levy of $0.10 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027, to prevent the reduction of emergency medical care and ambulance services as call volumes and personnel and equipment costs rise (RCW 84.52.052) [S1][S6]. The money may be used only for operations and maintenance of emergency medical and ambulance services in the Twisp EMS district; Aero Methow Rescue Service, located in Twisp, is the contracted provider [S1].

- **What it pays for.** It is Twisp's share of Aero Methow's request to staff a second advanced-life-support ambulance year-round with three more paramedics; the town, Winthrop and the rural Methow Valley EMS District each vote on a matching levy [S2][S3][S5].
- **Who votes.** Town of Twisp voters (732 registered in November 2025) [S7].
- **Vote needed.** A 60% supermajority, with the turnout conditions of Article VII, Section 2(a) of the state constitution [S1].

## Cost

$0.10 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 only [S1]. Twisp's levy would raise about $25,000 [S2][S3]. Aero Methow estimates about $47 a year on a $500,000 house [S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

This is new money on top of Twisp's regular EMS levy, which town voters renewed in February 2026 with 77.8% at 50 cents per $1,000 for six years [S2][S4]. Excess operations levies last one year; Aero Methow expects to ask every year until it finds a more sustainable funding source [S2]. Aero Methow says its regular levies cover about 60% of operations, and calls rose from 531 in 2015 to 911 in 2025, more than half of them without a reimbursed transport [S2][S3].

## Arguments for

No statement for was submitted [S1]. The Twisp Town Council endorsed placing the levy on the ballot before Winthrop and the county did [S3][S5]. Aero Methow says it has become a de facto urgent care provider for the valley and must provide advanced life support because the nearest hospitals are more than an hour away [S2][S3].

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted [S1], and no PDC committee for or against was found [S8].
