## What it does

The Town of Winthrop asks its voters for a one-year excess property tax levy of $0.10 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027, to prevent the reduction of emergency medical care and ambulance services as call volumes and personnel and equipment costs rise (RCW 84.52.052) [S1][S6]. The money may be used only for operations and maintenance of emergency medical and ambulance services within the town; Aero Methow Rescue Service is the contracted provider [S1].

- **What it pays for.** Winthrop's share of Aero Methow's request to staff a second advanced-life-support ambulance 24 hours a day all year, with three more paramedics; Twisp and the rural Methow Valley EMS District vote on matching levies [S2][S3][S5].
- **Who votes.** Town of Winthrop voters (455 registered in November 2025) [S7].
- **Vote needed.** A 60% supermajority, with the turnout conditions of Article VII, Section 2(a) of the state constitution [S1].

## Cost

$0.10 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 only [S1]. Winthrop's levy would raise about $20,000 [S2][S3]. Aero Methow estimates about $47 a year on a $500,000 house [S3].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

This is new money on top of Winthrop's regular 50-cent EMS levy, which town voters renewed for ten years in 2022 [S3][S4]. Excess operations levies last one year, so Aero Methow expects to ask every year until it finds a more sustainable source [S3]. Aero Methow told the council its regular levies cover about 60% of operations, calls grew from 531 in 2015 to 911 in 2025, and in 2026 a seasonal second ambulance (April to October) created a cost overrun of about $310,000 [S2].

## Arguments for

No statement for was submitted [S1]. The Winthrop Town Council backed the request after a presentation by Aero Methow Executive Director Derek Van Marter, who said "We see the demand in the community" and that staff and board "feel obligated to prepare to respond" [S2]. He said the valley lacks the population to support an urgent care center, so Aero Methow has become a de facto one [S2].

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted [S1], and no PDC committee for or against was found [S8].
