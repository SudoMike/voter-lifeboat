## What it does

The City of Brewster asks its voters to continue its regular property tax levy for emergency medical services at $0.50 or less per $1,000 of assessed value for each of six consecutive years beginning in 2027, under RCW 84.52.069 [S1][S2]. The levy was first approved by voters in 2020; its revenue pays for the city's contract with Douglas-Okanogan Fire District 15 for emergency medical services, including personnel, training, equipment, supplies, vehicles and structures [S1].

- **Who votes.** City of Brewster voters (707 registered in November 2025) [S6].
- **Vote needed.** The explanatory statement does not state a threshold [S1]. Under RCW 84.52.069, the initial imposition of a six-year EMS levy needs a three-fifths (60%) vote, but a subsequent approval of a six-year levy needs a simple majority [S2]; the statement describes this one as a continuation [S1].

## Cost

$0.50 or less per $1,000 of assessed value a year, 2027 through 2032, the same rate as the expiring levy [S1]. The ballot materials give no dollar total [S1]. In 2020 the Chronicle estimated a 50-cent EMS levy at about $50 a year on property assessed at $100,000 [S3].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

It renews an expiring levy at the same rate [S1]. In April 2020 Brewster, Pateros and Douglas-Okanogan Fire District 15 asked for linked six-year levies of up to 50 cents for shared ambulance service; Brewster voters approved 115-80 (58.97%), but all three failed because Pateros voters said no [S3]. The explanatory statement says the current levy was approved by voters in 2020 [S1]. The other two partners renewed their own EMS levies in the August 4, 2026 primary: Pateros with 63.12% and Fire District 15 with 67.95% [S4].

## Arguments for

No statement for was submitted [S1]. In 2020 the fire district warned that without the levies ambulance costs and response times could rise, services could be downgraded, or no emergency medical services might be available [S3].

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted [S1]. No PDC committee for or against the measure was found [S5].
