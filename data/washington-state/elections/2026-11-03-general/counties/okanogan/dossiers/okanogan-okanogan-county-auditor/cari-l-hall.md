## Background

- Cari L Hall (Prefers Republican Party) was elected Okanogan County Auditor in 2018 and 2022 [S1].
- She joined the Auditor's office as finance manager in 2015; before that she was chief financial officer of a local nonprofit for 13 years [S1].
- She is certified in vehicle licensing (2018) and as a Certified Elections Administrator (2020) [S1].
- Education: Okanogan High School, 1988; studied at Eastern Washington University [S1].
- Community service: member of Washington State Association of County Auditors committees on elections, recording, legislation, finance and licensing [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in the 2022 general (11,102 votes, 97.5%) [S2] and in 2026; 97.11% in the August 4, 2026 primary [S3]. Her 2026 PDC record reports no contribution totals [S5].
