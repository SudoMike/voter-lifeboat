## What the office does
The Auditor records and preserves documents, reviews and records county expenditures, helps the commissioners monitor and report the budget, issues marriage licenses, handles vehicle licensing, and runs voter registration and elections in Okanogan County [S1][S4].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Cari L Hall (Prefers Republican Party), the incumbent, is the only candidate [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary Hall received 97.11% against write-ins [S3]. In the 2022 general, Cari Hall was also unopposed, with 11,102 votes to 285 write-ins (97.5%) [S2].
