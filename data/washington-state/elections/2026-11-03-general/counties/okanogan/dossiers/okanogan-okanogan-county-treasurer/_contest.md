## What the office does
The Treasurer receives all money due the county, collects property taxes, and pays out county funds on warrants the Auditor issues; the office also acts as treasurer for local taxing districts [S4].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Pam Johnson (Prefers Republican Party), the incumbent, is the only candidate [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary Johnson received 97.62% against write-ins [S3]. In the 2022 general, Pam Johnson was also unopposed, with 10,858 votes to 249 write-ins (97.8%) [S2].
