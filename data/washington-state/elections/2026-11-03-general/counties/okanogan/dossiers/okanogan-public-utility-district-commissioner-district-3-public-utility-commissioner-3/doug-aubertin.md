## Background

Doug Aubertin of Keller is the incumbent Ferry County PUD No. 1 commissioner for Position 3 and is seeking a fourth term; he says his first term began in 2009 [S1][S3]. His statement also describes 18 years "as Ferry County Commissioner in District #3" and lists his community service as "Serving Ferry County as your PUD Commissioner" [S1]. In 2020 he was unopposed in this race; Okanogan County's share of the vote was 146 [S2]. In 1987 he and his father started Aubertin Logging & Lumber; in 1994 he started ABN Inc., and in 2001 he rebuilt his grandparents' ranch [S1]. He attended colleges in Yakima and Eastern Washington University [S1].

## Positions

### Rates, service and state mandates
- Says the PUD has faced challenges remaining solvent while maintaining 700 miles of power lines [S1].
- Criticizes state mandates such as electric-powered service vehicles: "Electric vehicles may work well in Olympia, but they do not fit well in our rural environment" [S1].
- Says reduced power availability to public utilities through Bonneville Power Administration outsourcing "greatly influences our local power rates" [S1].
- Says he helped the PUD obtain $5 million in grants to extend service, serve the reopening of the Kinross mine and unserved areas, and install 30 miles of underground line to reduce outages [S1].
