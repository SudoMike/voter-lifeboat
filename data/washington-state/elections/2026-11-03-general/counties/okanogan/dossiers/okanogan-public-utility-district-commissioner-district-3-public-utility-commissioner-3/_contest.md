## What the office does
This is Ferry County PUD No. 1's Commissioner #3 seat (PDC jurisdiction "FERRY CO PUD 01", Position 3) (S4). The PUD is the electric utility for Ferry County, and the candidate statements describe 700 miles of power lines and service extensions (S1). A board of three commissioners, elected to six-year terms, governs it. On Okanogan County's ballot it appears only for the "PUD (COUNTYWIDE)" district, 325 registered voters in the northeast of the county (S2); in the 2020 and 2024 generals these Ferry PUD races were on eight northeastern Okanogan precincts (Bodie, Chesaw, San Poil, Wauconda, Toroda, Sourdough, Buckhorn Mtn, Myers Creek) (S3). Those voters are not in Okanogan County PUD (S2).

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Doug Aubertin, seeking a fourth term (his first term began in 2009, by his account), was unopposed in 2020 (S1, S3). Challenger Andrew Pooler is a retired mining executive and former chief operating officer of Colville Tribal Enterprise Corporation (S1). Both are from Keller (S4). Nonpartisan; no primary.

Aubertin cites $5 million in grants for line extensions and underground line, and opposes state electric-vehicle mandates for rural utilities (S1). Pooler pledges dependable, low-cost power and cites power-line construction and budget management experience (S1).
