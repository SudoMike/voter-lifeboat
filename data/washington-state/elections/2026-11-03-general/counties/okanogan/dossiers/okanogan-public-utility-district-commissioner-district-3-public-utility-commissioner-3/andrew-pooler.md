## Background

Andrew Pooler of Keller is challenging the incumbent for Ferry County PUD No. 1's Position 3 seat [S1][S2]. He reports no elected experience [S1]. He recently retired after 35 years as a mining executive responsible for the production performance or construction of 16 large-scale mines in North, Central and South America, and was for three years chief operating officer of Colville Tribal Enterprise Corporation, overseeing a sawmill, a plywood plant, three casinos, a logging operation and a construction company [S1]. He has served on the boards of several international corporations [S1]. He graduated with honors in 1984 from the University of Idaho with a B.S. in mining engineering, took four electrical engineering courses there, and attended Keller Grade School and Wilbur High School [S1]. He says he and his wife returned to live in Ferry County after he retired [S1].
