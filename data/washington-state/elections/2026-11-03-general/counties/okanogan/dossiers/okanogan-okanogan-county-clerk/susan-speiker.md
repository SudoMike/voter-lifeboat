## Background

- Susan Speiker (Prefers Republican Party) has been Okanogan County Clerk since 2023 [S1].
- She joined the county in 2009 as a criminal justice billing specialist, then worked as a court facilitator, judicial assistant and scheduler, and chief deputy clerk [S1].
- Education: Bachelor of Business Administration, cum laude, Siena Heights University; Master of Science in Executive Leadership, Champlain College [S1].
- She has served as vice-president, president and past president of the Washington State Association of County Clerks, which named her Clerk of the Year in June 2025 [S1].
- Community service: Oroville Housing Authority commissioner [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in the 2022 general (10,948 votes, 97.3%) [S2] and in 2026; 97.18% in the August 4, 2026 primary [S3]. Her 2026 PDC record reports no contribution totals [S5].
