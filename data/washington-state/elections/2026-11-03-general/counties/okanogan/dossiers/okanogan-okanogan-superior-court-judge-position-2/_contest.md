## What the office does
Okanogan County Superior Court is the county's general-jurisdiction trial court, hearing felony cases, civil disputes, land-use matters, family and child-dependency cases (S3). The county has two Superior Court judges, elected county-wide (S2).

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Judge Rachel Hong, appointed by Gov. Bob Ferguson in July 2025 after Judge Robert Grim resigned, is the only candidate for the remaining two years of the term (S1, S2). Appointed judges must stand at the next general election (S2).
