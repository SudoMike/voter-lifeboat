## Background

Rachel Hong, of Winthrop, has been an Okanogan County Superior Court judge since July 3, 2025, appointed by Gov. Bob Ferguson to the seat Robert Grim left to run for mayor of Winthrop [S1][S2][S3]. She was one of six applicants [S4][S5]. By law she must stand for election at the next general election; she is the only candidate for the two-year unexpired term [S1][S6].

Before the appointment she clerked for U.S. District Judge Barbara J. Rothstein (Western District of Washington) for more than a decade, in three stints totalling almost 15 years, drafting hundreds of rulings and supporting trial proceedings from jury selection through verdict [S1][S3][S4]. She was a litigation attorney handling civil and criminal matters in state and federal courts, including at the firms Yarmuth Wilsdon Calfo PLLC and Heller Ehrman White & McAuliffe LLP, and in-house counsel for businesses [S1][S3]. She volunteered as an attorney with the Northwest Immigrant Rights Project [S1][S2].

Mid-career she co-founded and ran Alder Falls Farm, an organic vegetable farm in Shelton, and later worked at Booth Canyon Orchard in Carlton [S1][S3]. She holds a J.D., cum laude and Order of the Coif, from the University of Michigan Law School (2000) and a B.A. from the University of Virginia (1994) [S1]. She serves on the TwispWorks Foundation board [S1][S2].

## Positions

### Judicial approach
- Says she wants everyone before the court, "regardless of their means or background," to be "genuinely heard and treated with dignity," and to be "a judge who listens carefully, thinks rigorously, explains the law and her decisions clearly, and applies the rule of law fairly" [S1].
- On her appointment: "I turn now to earning the trust of the people of Okanogan County by working tirelessly to ensure that all who come into court are treated with fairness and respect" [S2][S3].

## Record

- Superior Court judge since July 2025; she and Judge Kathryn Burke alternate every four months between presiding over criminal trials and hearing criminal motions [S4].
