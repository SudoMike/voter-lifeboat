## What the office does
The three-member Board of County Commissioners is Okanogan County's legislative and executive body: it adopts the budget, sets the county property-tax levy and puts levy measures to voters, and oversees county departments and land-use rules (S4, S5). District 3 covers the northern part of the county; candidates are nominated by District 3 voters in the primary and elected by the whole county in the general (S7). In the 2022 general the District 3 race was on all 248 Okanogan precincts (S3).

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Jon Neal of Oroville, elected in 2022, faces Trinity Hightower Stucker of Tonasket; both prefer the Republican Party (S1). Stucker edged Neal among District 3 primary voters, 51.0% to 47.7% (S2), and won the Okanogan County Republican Party's endorsement, which Neal contested as tainted by a conflict of interest (S6).

The race turns on the county's roughly $2-million deficit. The commission, Neal included, unanimously put a levy lid lift on the August ballot; voters rejected it about 70-30 and the commission pulled it from November, accepting cuts (S4). At the July forum both candidates said they did not support the lid lift, Neal saying the finances left few alternatives and Stucker saying "My plan is for that levy to fail" and pointing to more than $3 million in cuts a volunteer budget team proposed (S5). Both blame unfunded state mandates (S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
- `taxes`: both oppose the lid lift in their statements at the forum, but Neal voted to place it on the ballot; Stucker campaigns on "lower taxes" and opposed it outright.
- `spending`: Stucker proposes cuts first, including 10% pay cuts for commissioners and department heads, and efficiency audits; Neal stresses careful use of tax dollars, protecting staffing levels, and expanded behavioral-health partnerships within limited funds.
- `experience`: a sitting commissioner and former Oroville mayor vs. a first-time candidate with Farm Bureau and Senate-aide experience.
- `reform`: Stucker says the commission ignored citizen budget proposals and calls for transparency and permitting reform; Neal runs on continuity.
- `housing`, `climate`: no differentiating evidence.
