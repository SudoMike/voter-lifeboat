## Background

Steven Gadd of Okanogan is challenging the incumbent for the nonpartisan PUD District 1 seat [S1][S2]. He served four years on the Okanogan City Council [S1]. He has worked for 14 years as an assistant state auditor and earlier was chief operating officer of two nonprofit corporations for a total of 12 years [S1]. He served in the U.S. Navy during the Persian Gulf War [S1]. He holds an MBA from the University of Washington and is a Certified Management Accountant and Certified Financial Manager [S1]. He belongs to the Okanogan American Legion and the Okanogan Eagles [S1].

## Positions

### Governance and oversight
- "It's time for a change. Twelve years is long enough in any elected position!" [S1].
- Says commissioners "can become complacent and satisfied with the status quo" and that, as a government auditor, he has seen boards work "by rote" and shoot down new ideas [S1].
- Pledges "thorough oversite" so the utility runs "as efficiently and effectively as possible," to seek input from the public and employees, and to attend PUD-related trainings and organizations [S1].
