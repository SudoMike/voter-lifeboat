## Background

Scott Vejraska of Omak is the incumbent Okanogan County PUD commissioner for District 1 and says he has served 11 years and is currently board president [S1][S4]. In 2020 he was re-elected unopposed, with 14,074 votes [S2]. He describes himself as a generational rancher and a lineman with 27 years of experience [S1]. He graduated from Omak High School (1995) and holds an associate degree from the utility line program at Northeast Community College in Norfolk, Nebraska [S1]. He sits on advisory boards for the Omak FFA and P.S.I.S. career and technical education programs [S1].

## Positions

### Rates and PUD finances
- Says he has "supported efforts to keep rates low and have opposed unnecessary rate increases, with a firm NO VOTE, whenever possible" [S1].
- Says that despite wildfire damage to utility infrastructure the board "strengthened our financial reserves without placing additional burdens on ratepayers" [S1].
- "Public funds belong to the people we serve. Every dollar saved is a dollar that remains in the pockets of our ratepayers" [S1].
- Pledges to "keep rates affordable, strengthen our electrical infrastructure, and provide transparent, responsible leadership" [S1].
