## Background

- Larry D Gilman (Prefers Republican Party) has been Okanogan County Assessor since 2019 [S1].
- He has worked 31 years in the Assessor's office, hired as a residential appraiser before being elected; earlier he was a surveyor for North Star Land Surveying for two years and a U.S. Forest Service civil engineer for five years [S1].
- Education: two years at Spokane Community College (forestry and hydrology); Okanogan High School, 1988 [S1].
- Community service: Kiwanis of Okanogan; 20-year member of Okanogan County Search & Rescue; Washington State football official for more than 20 years; youth and high school baseball coach [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in the 2022 general (10,950 votes, 96.9%) [S2] and in 2026; 96.03% in the August 4, 2026 primary [S3]. His 2026 PDC record reports no contribution totals [S5].
