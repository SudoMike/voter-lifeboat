## What the office does
The Assessor lists all taxable property in Okanogan County and appraises it at 100% of true and fair market value, calculates property tax levies, and runs personal property tax, current use (open space, agriculture, designated forest land) and the senior citizen and disabled person exemption under state tax law [S1][S4].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Larry D Gilman (Prefers Republican Party), the incumbent, is the only candidate [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary Gilman received 96.03% against write-ins [S3]. In the 2022 general, Larry D Gilman was also unopposed, with 10,950 votes to 350 write-ins (96.9%) [S2].
