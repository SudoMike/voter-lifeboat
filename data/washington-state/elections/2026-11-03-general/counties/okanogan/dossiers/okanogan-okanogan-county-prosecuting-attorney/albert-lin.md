## Background

- Albert Lin (Prefers Republican Party) has been Okanogan County Prosecuting Attorney for more than four years [S1]. The county commissioners appointed him on Feb. 23, 2022, after Melanie Bailey resigned; he had most recently worked for Lincoln County [S6].
- Earlier experience: 10 years as a Grant County deputy prosecutor, 5 years as a Franklin County deputy prosecutor and 2 years as Okanogan County chief civil prosecutor [S1].
- Education: B.A., University of Washington Jackson School of International Studies (1991); J.D., Syracuse University College of Law (1997) [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in the 2022 general (10,718 votes, 97.2%) [S2] and in 2026; 95.93% in the August 4, 2026 primary [S3]. His 2026 PDC record reports $2,034.05 raised and $1,959.05 spent [S5].

## Positions

- Says the office "re-established professionalism and community trust" after there "was much to fix," and works with law enforcement to prosecute violent and property crimes and hold offenders accountable [S1].
- Says the office works to "safely and responsibly promote growth and development, without infringing upon the rights of the individual" and to protect the county's agricultural and tourist economy [S1].
