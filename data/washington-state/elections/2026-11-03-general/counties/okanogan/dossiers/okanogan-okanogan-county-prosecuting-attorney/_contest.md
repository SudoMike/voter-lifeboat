## What the office does
The Prosecuting Attorney prosecutes crimes on behalf of the state in Okanogan County, represents the county in civil cases, and is legal adviser to the county commissioners, county officers and school districts [S4].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Albert Lin (Prefers Republican Party), the incumbent, is the only candidate [S1]. In the August 4, 2026 primary Lin received 95.93% against write-ins [S3]. In the 2022 general, Albert H Lin was also unopposed, with 10,718 votes to 310 write-ins (97.2%) [S2].
