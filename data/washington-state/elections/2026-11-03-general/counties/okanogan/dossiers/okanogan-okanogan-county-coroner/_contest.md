## What the office does
The coroner determines the cause and manner of deaths in the county (natural, accident, suicide, homicide or undetermined), investigating death scenes and working with families and law enforcement (S2). It is a partisan county office elected countywide for four years (S1). State law does not require the coroner to have a law-enforcement background (S2).

## Race dynamics
Open seat: Coroner Dave Rodriguez is retiring (S3). Chief Deputy Coroner Gene Davis (prefers Republican), a retired sheriff's sergeant and detective, faces nurse practitioner Cindy Jensen (prefers Independent) of Oroville (S1, S3). Davis led the August primary 63.6% to 36.0% (S4). The forum debate turned on qualifications: Davis says the job is investigation, Jensen says medical and end-of-life experience and compassion matter too and proposes more deputy coroners to shorten rural response times (S2).
