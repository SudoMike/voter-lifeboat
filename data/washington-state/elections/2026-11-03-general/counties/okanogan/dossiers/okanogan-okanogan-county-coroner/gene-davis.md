## Background

Gene (Eugene) Davis of Okanogan is the county's chief deputy coroner, running for the open coroner's seat being vacated by retiring Coroner Dave Rodriguez (S1, S2, S4). He worked for the U.S. Forest Service (1991-1993), the Okanogan County jail (1994), the Winthrop Marshal's Office (1995-1998) and the Okanogan County Sheriff's Office (1998-2023), where he was a K-9 handler, sergeant and detective sergeant and investigated homicides and suspicious deaths (S1). He has been chief deputy coroner since 2023 and is a state-certified medicolegal death investigator (S1). He reports no prior elected office (S1). He was a reserve deputy and a volunteer firefighter in Brewster and Winthrop, and served on The Log Church Christian School board (2021-2022) (S1).

## Positions

### The coroner's office
- Says the job is fundamentally investigative: "You don't have to be a doctor, nurse or EMT. The primary thing is to be an investigator and investigate the scene" (S3).
- Says careful scene work protects victims and innocent people who could be overlooked if a homicide were classified as natural, and stresses certified death investigators working with younger deputies (S3).
- "When somebody dies, they don't tell me who they voted for. So, we're here to do a thorough death investigation"; families are victims too and deserve compassion (S2).
- Promises "a seamless transition within the Coroner's Office" (S1).
