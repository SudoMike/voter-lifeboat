## Background

Cindy Jensen of Oroville, who prefers the Independent Party, is a nurse practitioner running for the open coroner's seat (S1, S4). She has been a registered nurse since 1989 and a licensed nurse practitioner in Washington for 24 years, owns a small medical practice in Oroville, and retired from the Veterans Affairs system in January 2025 (S1). Her site says she was a primary care provider for VA Puget Sound Home Based Primary Care for more than 11 years (S2). She told a candidate forum she is a former hospice nurse (S3). She holds a B.A. in anthropology (Western Washington University), a B.S. in nursing (UW Tacoma) and a master's in nursing (UW Seattle) (S1). She reports no elected office; she served on the AUWS legislative committee (S1). Her site lists membership in the International Association of Coroners and Medical Examiners (S2).

## Positions

### The coroner's office
- Argues medical experience and compassion matter as much as investigation: "I don't think you have to be a deputy to be a coroner," noting state law does not require law enforcement experience (S3).
- Acknowledged "I will freely admit I don't investigate deaths. I just attend deaths," and said advanced education in anatomy, physiology and anthropology is also a qualification (S3).
- Suggested more deputy coroners or volunteers to shorten response times in rural areas, saying families sometimes wait more than two hours (S3).
- Registered for IACME medicolegal death investigation training (S1).

### Taxes and budget
- "I am fiscally conservative," citing the county's two-million-dollar deficit; says she has "a plan that will control costs in the coroner's office while maintaining services" (S1). Her site: "committed to careful spending and ensuring that every tax dollar is managed with integrity" (S2).
