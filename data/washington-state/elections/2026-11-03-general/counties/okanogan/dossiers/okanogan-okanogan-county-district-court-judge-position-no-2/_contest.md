## What the office does
Okanogan County District Court hears misdemeanor and gross-misdemeanor criminal cases, traffic infractions, limited civil cases, small claims and protection orders. Its two judges are elected county-wide to four-year terms; in 2022 this seat was on every precinct in the county (S4).

## Race dynamics
Presiding Judge Charles Short, on the bench since 2013 and unopposed in 2022 (S1, S4), faces Okanogan County felony deputy prosecutor John Andrew Bangerter (S1). Nonpartisan; with two candidates there was no primary (S5).

Short runs on 13 years as a judge, statewide judicial-education leadership (dean of the Washington State Judicial College) and a 2024 judicial leadership award; he lists endorsements from both county party central committees, the Farm Bureau and more than 100 judicial officers, including Robert Grim (S1, S2). Bangerter runs on daily courtroom experience as a prosecutor, "fresh perspective," efficiency and holding convicted offenders accountable; his endorsers are mainly Chelan County prosecutors, victim advocates and corrections staff (S1, S3).

## Differentiating issue axes
- `experience`: the clearest contrast. Short is a 13-year incumbent and presiding judge; Bangerter has never been a judge and runs explicitly on change.
- `safety`: Bangerter's prosecutor background and accountability language for convicted offenders versus Short's general rule-of-law statement; no bail or sentencing record for Short was found in the sources.
- `judicial`: both describe impartial application of the law; neither sets out a distinct interpretive philosophy beyond that.
