## Background

Joshua C. Harris describes himself as an attorney, Army veteran, Puget Sound business owner, and parent, born and raised in Western Washington and living in Kent [S1]. He served in the Washington Army National Guard and deployed to the Middle East attached to the 5th Special Forces Group [S1]. After his military service he founded a King County-based security company [S1]. He holds a J.D. from Villanova University, a B.S. from the University of Maryland, an A.A. from Cochise College, and an A.A.S. from the Defense Language Institute [S1]. He states he is appointed to the WSBA Board of Bar Examiners and was appointed by the Governor to the Pierce County Boundary Review Board [S1]. Neither appointment was independently confirmed; the official rosters found were not publicly accessible or did not load.

Prior candidacy: PDC records under the same filer ID (HARRJ--638) show a 2022 campaign by "Joshua C. Harris (Josh Harris)" for Pierce County Council Position 7, with a Republican party preference and a Tacoma committee address [S3]. That campaign raised $29,985 and lost in the primary [S3]. The identity link rests on the shared PDC filer ID. His pamphlet reference to a Pierce County board appointment is consistent with it [S1][S3]. This is recorded as a fact about a prior partisan candidacy, not as evidence of judicial philosophy.

His campaign website did not load at access time, so no campaign-site material was available [S2].
