## Background

Martinez lists a JD (2001) from Seattle University School of Law and a B.A. in Law and Diversity (1995) from Western Washington University [S1].
He lists his occupation as King County District Court Judge [S1].
His statement says the King County Council unanimously appointed him in 2023 and that he was elected to retain the position in 2024 [S1].
It reports 21 years of legal experience before the bench as a King County Senior Deputy Prosecutor, civil litigator, appellate lawyer, and legal advisor to businesses and government entities [S1].

## Positions

His statement names a historic opioid epidemic, an underfunded mental health system, and unaffordable civil representation as challenges facing the courts [S1].
It says he is dedicated to increasing access to justice [S1].

## Record

His statement says he serves on judicial committees that update court processes, forms, and public information so non-attorneys can understand and access the courts [S1].
It lists past board service with the Northwest Justice Project and the Latina/o Bar Association [S1].

## Endorsements

His statement lists endorsements from more than 50 trial and appellate judges, the King County Democrats, the 32nd District Democrats, and the 1st District Democrats [S1].
