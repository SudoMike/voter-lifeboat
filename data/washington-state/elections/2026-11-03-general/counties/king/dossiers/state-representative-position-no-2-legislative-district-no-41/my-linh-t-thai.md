## Background
My-Linh T Thai (Prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Representative for LD 41, Position 2, and House Deputy Majority Leader [S1]. She sits on the Appropriations, Civil Rights & Judiciary, Community Safety, Education, Finance, Healthcare & Wellness, Housing and Rules committees [S1]. She is a pharmacist, a former Bellevue School District board president and a former vice president of the Washington State School Directors' Association [S1]. She describes herself as the first refugee elected to the Washington Legislature [S1][S4]. She has served in the House since 2019 [S2].

In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 66.32% (24,035 votes) to Michael Rosen's 33.40% (12,106 votes) [S5].

## Positions
- **Healthcare:** "protecting access to affordable and high-quality care, supporting scientific innovations, and taking meaningful action on climate change are my top priorities"; also "expanding behavioral health services" [S1]. Her general statement is unchanged from the primary [S1][S2].
- **Climate:** lists "meaningful action on climate change" as a top priority [S1].
- **Affordability and taxes:** "I have worked to put money back in working families' pockets by expanding tax credits, eliminating sales tax on everyday essentials, and ensuring no child goes hungry at school lunch" [S4].
- **Local investment:** cites securing $1.2 million for Mercer Island's water supply pipeline, $1 million for the community center HVAC and $1.9 million for the Ebright Creek conservation project [S4].
- **Immigration and civil rights:** says she has "fought to defend immigrant families and workers, secure housing and shelter funding, and expand behavioral health resources for kids"; focus on "protecting people's civil rights" [S4].
- **Democracy:** cites legislation to protect voter rolls, safeguard voter privacy and expand ballot access [S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no stated position found; her 2026 endorsers include WA Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates and WA Stonewall Democrats [S3].
- **Parents and schools:** no stated position found beyond the ESHB 1296 vote under Record [S7].

## Record
- **ESSB 6346 (2026 tax on household income over $1 million, which I-645 would repeal):** voted yea on House final passage, 2026-03-09 (51-46) [S8].
- **ESHB 1296 (2025 amendments to the parents' rights law that I-1 would undo):** voted yea on House third reading (2025-03-12) and final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, 59-39) [S7].
- **EHB 1217 (2025 rent stabilization):** voted yea on House final passage as recommended by the conference committee, 2025-04-27 [S9].
- **ESHB 2266 (2026):** voted yea on House passage and final passage [S10]. The law requires cities to allow transitional and permanent supportive housing and indoor emergency shelters in specified urban zones [S11].
- No floor votes since July: the 2026 session "adjourned sine die on Thursday March 12, 2026" [S12], and no special session has been found.
