## What this office does
One of two House members for LD 41 (Eastside King County: Mercer Island, Newcastle and parts of Bellevue, Issaquah, Renton and Sammamish), elected to a two-year term. Representatives vote on state law, budgets and taxes.

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Democrat My-Linh Thai, House Deputy Majority Leader since her 2019 start in the House, faces Independent Michael Rosen, a Mercer Island attorney and nutritionist making his first run for office. Thai took 66.32% in the August primary to Rosen's 33.40% (my-linh-t-thai.md [S5]). Rosen reports slightly more raised than Thai this cycle: $60,425 vs. $58,626 in contributions, per PDC summaries (michael-rosen.md [S5]; my-linh-t-thai.md [S6]).

## Differentiating axes
- **Spending:** Rosen's platform is fiscal discipline: state spending "more than doubled", reserves "lowest in the nation". Thai cites securing local project funding and expanding tax credits and behavioral health services.
- **Taxes:** Thai voted for the 2026 high-earner income tax that I-645 would repeal. Rosen criticizes rising taxes but has not addressed the income tax or I-645.
- **Reform:** Rosen says he takes no special-interest money and is bound to neither party.
- **Experience:** House leadership incumbent vs. first-time candidate.
- **Parental rights and social:** only Thai has evidence: her ESHB 1296 vote and an endorsement list that includes Planned Parenthood and Stonewall Democrats. Rosen has stated nothing on either.
- Neither candidate has stated a position on I-645, I-1 or I-638.
