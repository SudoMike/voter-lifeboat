## Background
Michael Rosen (Prefers Independent Party) is an attorney, investor and Washington-licensed nutritionist with no elected experience [S1]. He is a fourth-generation Washingtonian born and raised on Mercer Island, where his family has run a business since 1913 [S1][S3]. Education: BBA, George Washington University; JD, University of Miami School of Law; MS in Nutrition, National University of Natural Medicine [S1]. He volunteers with Democracy Postcarders, the Mercer Island Rotary Club and area chambers of commerce [S1].

In the August 4, 2026 primary he received 33.40% (12,106 votes) to My-Linh Thai's 66.32% [S4]. His campaign website had no substantive policy content when checked during primary research [S2].

## Positions
- **Spending:** "Olympia keeps spending more and delivering less, treating your tax dollars as a blank check. State spending has more than doubled in two decades while accountability, affordability, public safety, and health outcomes have gotten worse" [S1]. "According to our state treasurer, Washington's reserves are the lowest in the nation. We need real fiscal discipline — demanding results from every dollar we spend before asking taxpayers for more" [S3]. His general statement is the same as his primary statement [S1][S2].
- **Affordability and taxes:** "Electric bills, health premiums, housing costs and taxes keep climbing while Olympia keeps asking families for more. I'll focus on bringing costs down" [S3].
- **Homelessness:** "People struggling with addiction or serious mental illness need real treatment and recovery, not simply a roof without adequate support. And decisions about our neighborhoods should include the communities affected" [S3].
- **Campaign money and independence:** "That's why I take no money from special interests. My loyalty is to you, not the industries or lobbyists that fund campaigns in Olympia. As an independent, I'm not bound by either party" [S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found [S1][S3].
- **Parents and schools:** no position found [S1][S3].
