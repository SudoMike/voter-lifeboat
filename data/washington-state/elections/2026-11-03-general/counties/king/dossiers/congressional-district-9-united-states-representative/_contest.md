## What the office does

A U.S. Representative is one of 435 voting members of the House. Representatives write and vote on federal law, control federal spending through appropriations, oversee the executive branch through committees, and provide casework with federal agencies. Terms are two years with no term limit. The 9th District includes all or parts of Mercer Island, Kent, Renton, Federal Way, Des Moines, SeaTac, Tukwila, Auburn, and parts of Seattle and Bellevue [S4].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Adam Smith (D), in the House since 1997 and the top Democrat on the Armed Services Committee, faces Republican Doug Basler for the sixth time; Smith won their 2014-2022 general-election matchups [S5]. In the primary Smith took 47.13% and Basler 21.83%; left-flank challengers Kshama Sawant (17.27%) and Melissa Chaudhry (12.51%) did not advance [S3]. Smith's 2026 record shows him breaking with past patterns on defense: he voted against the FY2027 NDAA over its cost and voted to block $3.3 billion in Israel military aid [S6].

## Issue axes that genuinely differentiate these candidates

1. **Taxes.** Smith calls for taxing capital gains like wages; Basler urges a yes vote on I-645 to repeal the state income tax.
2. **Climate and energy.** Basler says the Climate Commitment Act raises energy costs while doing little for the environment; Smith lists protecting Puget Sound and voted against overturning California emissions standards.
3. **Social policy.** Smith prioritizes abortion rights and opposes the transgender military ban; Basler is pro-life but opposes a federal abortion ban and supports IVF, and backs IL26-638.
4. **Healthcare.** Smith supports a "path to" single-payer; Basler does not address healthcare structure.
5. **Experience.** Incumbent since 1997 versus a perennial challenger with no elected record.
6. **Defense.** Only Smith has a defense record; it is mixed in 2026 (NDAA no vote on cost, Israel aid vote, continued stated support for Israel). Basler has no stated defense position in the sources reviewed.

Parents-and-schools positions (Basler supports IL26-001) are recorded in the dossiers but the `parental-rights` axis does not apply to federal contests.
