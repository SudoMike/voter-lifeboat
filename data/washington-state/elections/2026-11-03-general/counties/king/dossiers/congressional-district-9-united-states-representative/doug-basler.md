## Background

- Founder and president of Prologue Digital Inc., an advertising and video production company; media and marketing professional since 1990; sales trainer and small-business owner [S1].
- Former King County Republican Party vice chairman; former Eastside Republican Club president and current vice president; longtime Republican precinct committee officer [S1].
- Attended Multnomah University; former youth pastor and worship pastor; homelessness outreach with rescue missions, Union Gospel Mission and the Salvation Army [S1].
- Has run against Adam Smith in the 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022 general elections, losing each time [S7]; his 2020 and 2022 shares were 25.7% and 28.2%, and he lost the 2023 King County Director of Elections race to Julie Wise with 15.9% (Ballotpedia, as of July 2026) [S2].

## Positions

All items are campaign statements; he has held no public office [S1].

**Economy, taxes and regulation**
- Calls his platform an "opportunity revolution": "big government policies are making it harder to build, hire, afford, and stay" [S1]. Told Seattle Weekly that government "can and should protect your freedom, reward work, remove unnecessary barriers," and: "Before we subsidize the price, let's attack the cost" [S5].
- Lists taxes among the costs squeezing families [S1]. Urges a yes vote on IP26-645 "to repeal Washington's new income tax" [S5].

**Housing**
- "Making housing more attainable by reducing unnecessary barriers to building" [S5].

**Energy and climate**
- "The Climate Commitment Act may sound good, but in practice it is driving up the cost of energy while doing little to directly improve the environment"; also cites "one of the highest gas taxes in the country" [S1]. Supports "reliable and affordable energy" [S5].

**Public safety**
- "We can show compassion to people in crisis while still holding people accountable for crime, addiction-fueled disorder, and policies that make neighborhoods less safe" [S3].

**Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy**
- "I'm pro-life and hope people choose life, but I do not support a federal abortion ban. I strongly support IVF and contraception" [S5].
- Urges a yes vote on IL26-638 "to protect girls' sports and safe spaces" [S5].

**Parents and schools**
- Urges a yes vote on IL26-001 "for parents' rights" [S5]. Campaign site says "We need to make life affordable again, support parents" [S3].

**Relationship to the administration**
- "When President Trump is right, I'll work with him. When I believe he's wrong, I'll say so" [S5].
- Says he will be "a voice of common sense, moderation, and practical solutions" [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

From his statement to Seattle Weekly (Oct. 2, 2026): "I also urge a YES vote on all three statewide initiatives: IL26-001 for parents' rights, IL26-638 to protect girls' sports and safe spaces, and IP26-645 to repeal Washington's new income tax." [S5]
- **I-645 (IP26-645):** Yes [S5].
- **I-1 (IL26-001):** Yes [S5].
- **I-638 (IL26-638):** Yes [S5].
