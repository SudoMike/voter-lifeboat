## Background
Lisa Callan (Prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Representative for Legislative District 5, Position 2, in office since 2019 [S2]. She is Vice Chair of the House Capital Budget Committee and a member of Appropriations and K-12 Education, a former Issaquah School Board director, and a former Boeing lead engineer and project manager [S1][S2]. Her caucus page lists her as co-chair of the Children & Youth Behavioral Health Work Group [S2]. In the King County count of the August 4 primary she received 23,812 votes (57.66%) to Patrick Peacock's 17,449 (42.25%) [S8].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** will "work for a tax code that doesn't squeeze working people" [S1]. At the October 5, 2026 North Bend forum she said she favors moving the B&O tax to net income, calling it a "big, huge tax structure change" worth pursuing [S9].
- **Housing:** "workforce housing for the families who serve our communities" [S1].
- **Education and youth:** "great public schools" and "mental health care for kids"; affordable childcare [S1][S2].
- **Climate and environment:** "protect forests and clean air" [S1].
- **Guns:** "gun safety that saves lives" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "I'll defend reproductive freedom" [S1]; endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S1][S10].
- **Parents and schools:** no campaign statement found; see her ESHB 1296 votes under Record.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1M):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: she voted yea on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S3]. This is a vote, not a stated initiative position.
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: she voted yea on ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments to the I-2081 parent and guardian rights list that I-1 would undo [S4][S5].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- ESSB 6346 (2026, 9.9% tax on income over $1 million): yea on House final passage, 2026-03-09, 51-46 [S3].
- ESHB 1296 (2025, safe and supportive schools; amended the I-2081 parental-rights list): yea on House passage 2025-03-12 and on final passage as amended by the Senate 2025-04-24 [S4][S5].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S6].
- PDC (as of the 2026-10-05 update): $84,888.79 raised and $52,699.23 spent for 2026 [S7].

## Endorsements
- Pamphlet: educators, State Labor Council, grocery workers, firefighters, nurses, Sierra Club, Washington Conservation Action, Planned Parenthood, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, building trades, Housing Alliance, Rep. Kim Schrier, local mayors and councilmembers [S1].
