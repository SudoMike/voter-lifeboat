## Background
Patrick Peacock (Prefers Republican Party) of Hobart describes himself as "not a career politician" [S1]. He served 23 years in the U.S. Army as a Human Intelligence Warrant Officer and Counter-Intelligence Agent, with two Afghanistan combat tours, a Bronze Star and a Combat Action Badge; he is Vice Chair of the King County GOP and a Senior Industrial Security Specialist at Boeing [S1][S2]. He holds an MBA (finance) from Webster University and a B.A. in Philosophy from the University of Washington [S1]. He ran against Callan for this seat in 2024 [S7]. In the King County count of the August 4 primary he received 17,449 votes (42.25%) [S4].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "introducing a state income tax would only worsen the situation"; campaign site: "No state income tax. Roll back fuel taxes that drive up the price of everything" [S1][S2].
- **Housing:** "Cut the red tape blocking new construction" [S2].
- **Public safety:** "Back our officers, hold repeat offenders accountable, and restore safe streets" [S2].
- **Business climate:** wants to keep employers in Washington by lowering the cost of doing business [S1][S2]; at the October 5, 2026 forum said "You need a doctorate degree in government regulations in order to start a small business" [S5].
- **Education and parents:** "Focus our schools on reading, math, and real-world skills — and respect parents as partners in their kids' education" [S2].
- **Governance:** frames the race against "Washington's one-party rule" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
