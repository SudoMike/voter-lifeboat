## What the office does
One of two state representatives for the 5th Legislative District in the Washington State House, elected to a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including school funding, the tax structure, housing rules and criminal-justice statutes.

## District and race dynamics
A rematch of 2024: Democratic incumbent Lisa Callan (in office since 2019, Capital Budget vice chair) against Republican Patrick Peacock, an Army veteran and King County GOP vice chair (see lisa-callan.md S2, patrick-peacock.md S1, S7). Both advanced automatically; in the King County primary count Callan had 57.7% and Peacock 42.3% (lisa-callan.md S8). Callan has raised about seven times as much (lisa-callan.md S7, patrick-peacock.md S3). Both appeared at the October 5 SnoValley Chamber forum (lisa-callan.md S9).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Callan voted for the 2026 tax on income over $1 million and wants a tax code "that doesn't squeeze working people"; Peacock opposes any state income tax and wants fuel taxes rolled back (lisa-callan.md S1, S3; patrick-peacock.md S1, S2).
2. **Record vs. renewal:** four-term incumbent with budget leadership vs. a first-time officeholder candidate.
3. **Reproductive policy:** Callan pledges to "defend reproductive freedom"; Peacock has no stated position (lisa-callan.md S1).
4. **Parents and schools:** Callan voted for ESHB 1296 (2025), which I-1 would undo; Peacock asks schools to "respect parents as partners" (lisa-callan.md S4; patrick-peacock.md S2).
5. **Public safety and housing:** Peacock's platform is enforcement-first and cut-red-tape housing; Callan emphasizes workforce housing and youth mental health (patrick-peacock.md S2; lisa-callan.md S1).
