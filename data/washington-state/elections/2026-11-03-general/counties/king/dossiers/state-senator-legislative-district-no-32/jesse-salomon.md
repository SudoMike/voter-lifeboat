## Background
Jesse Salomon has been the 32nd District's State Senator since 2019 [S1]. He chairs the Senate Local Government Committee, has chaired the Joint Legislative Audit and Review Committee and the Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee, and sits on Law and Justice [S1]. He was a Shoreline deputy mayor and councilmember, a child welfare prosecutor, and a public defense attorney; he attended Western Washington University and UW Law School [S1]. In election-night primary returns he was second with 37.6% to Ryu's 44.1% [S8].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** States he "proudly supports the 'Millionaire's Tax'" [S3]. In the KUOW debate he said the final law sent too much of its revenue to the operating budget rather than tax relief and that "we could have done better," while still supporting it [S5]. He called Ryu's proposed $250,000 threshold "incredibly out of touch" (on KIRO radio, as reported by the Lynnwood Times) and floated a $750,000 threshold only if traded for senior property-tax relief [S6]. Cites closing corporate tax loopholes, ending sales tax on nonprescription medicines and hygiene products, expanding low-income tax rebates, and cutting small-business taxes [S1].
- **Spending:** Says "government needs to be more responsible with your tax dollars" and pledges to "audit spending" [S1].
- **Housing:** Says he wrote and passed SB 5818 and SB 5412 to cut red tape for new housing and supported rent stabilization [S3]; pamphlet cites capping rent increases and blocking AI-powered rent-setting [S1].
- **Public safety:** Describes "a balanced solution of enforcement and support"; supports treatment and housing for people in crisis alongside law enforcement, officer decertification, and criticizes the 2021 Blake decision's effects as an overcorrection [S3]. Pamphlet pledges to address Aurora Avenue "with compassion and accountability" by connecting people to treatment [S1].
- **Immigration:** Says he banned ICE face masks and co-sponsored bills requiring judicial warrants for sensitive locations and notice of federal immigration audits [S1][S3].
- **Health care:** Priorities are holding for-profit health systems accountable on prices and consolidation and coordinating science-based public-health guidance with other states [S3].
- **Climate:** Says he sponsored the Climate Commitment Act and supported a fracking ban and clean-energy investment [S2][S3].
- **Technology:** Cites leading the fight to block AI-powered rent-raising schemes [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Says he is "100% pro-choice," sponsored SB 5400 protecting providers' licenses from out-of-state convictions for care legal in Washington, and supports a state constitutional right to reproductive care [S3].
- **Parents and schools:** No campaign statement found; see the ESHB 1296 vote under Record.
