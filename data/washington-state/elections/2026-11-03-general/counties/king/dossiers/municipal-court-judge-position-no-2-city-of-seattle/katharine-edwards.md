## Background

Katharine Edwards lists Macalester College and Lewis and Clark Law School [S1].
She lists her occupation as Supervising Public Defender [S1].
She says she has spent 16 years in Seattle Municipal Court and other King County courtrooms representing people facing misdemeanors and serious felonies, including homicide trials, and representing children in foster care [S1].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she says she would hear both sides in full and "hold the prosecution and the defense to the same standard" [S1].
- Diversion and treatment: she says that before ordering anyone into a program she wants to see the research and data on whether it works, and that the people who run programs should have a say in how they change [S1].
- She says accountability "should fit the offense" and notes that a person can sit in jail on a misdemeanor while someone charged with a felony goes home the same afternoon [S1].

## Record

- She has not served as an elected judge; her statement describes a public defense career that included negotiating treatment alternatives [S1].

## Endorsements

- Her statement lists endorsements from Seattle Municipal Court Judges Anita Crawford-Willis, Damon Shadid, and Pooja Vaddadi; former Supreme Court Justice Mary Yu; King County Superior Court Judges Rothrock, Larranaga, Hecklinger, Vargas, Keenan, and McKee; King County District Court Judges Shotwell, Dhillon, Peaquin, and Paglisotti; the King County Democrats and the 32nd, 34th, 36th, 37th, 43rd, and 46th LD Democrats; the National Women's Political Caucus; Machinists Local 751; Ironworkers Local 86; and UAW 4121 [S1].
