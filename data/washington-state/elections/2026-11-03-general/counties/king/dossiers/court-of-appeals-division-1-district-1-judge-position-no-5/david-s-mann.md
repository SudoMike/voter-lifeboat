## Background

David S. Mann lists a J.D. from Northwestern School of Law, Lewis & Clark College, and a B.A. from the University of California, Santa Barbara [S1]; his court biography says he graduated from law school in 1991 [S2].
Before law school he worked as a professional geophysicist, for six years according to his court biography [S1][S2].
Before his appointment he had a private practice for about 25 years emphasizing environmental law and policy, civil rights, public records, land use, and open government litigation [S1][S2].
He argued a Freedom of Information Act case before the U.S. Supreme Court, in 2011 according to his court biography [S1][S2].
He lists prior board service with Conservation Northwest, the Northwest Fund for the Environment, the Washington Environmental Council, and the Marymoor Velodrome Association [S1].

## Positions

- Criminal-justice formation: his stated pre-bench career was civil practice; neither source lists prosecution or public-defense work [S1][S2].
- Court administration: he is a member of the State Board for Judicial Administration and, per his court biography, the Judicial Information Systems Executive Committee [S1][S2].

## Record

- Governor Jay Inslee appointed him to Division I of the Court of Appeals in 2016; he was retained by election in 2017 and re-elected in 2020 [S1][S2].
- He served as Chief Judge of Division I (2020-2022 per his court biography) and is the most senior judge on the court [S1][S2].
- His pamphlet entry says he has authored more than 500 opinions [S1].

## Endorsements

- His statement says he is endorsed by a majority of current appellate judges and justices in the state, without naming them [S1].
