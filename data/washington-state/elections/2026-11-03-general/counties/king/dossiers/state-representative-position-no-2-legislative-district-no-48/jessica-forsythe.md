## Background

Jessica Forsythe (Prefers Democratic Party) has been a Redmond City Councilmember since 2020, Council President 2022-23 and Vice President 2024-25; she chaired the Alternative Crisis Response Subcommittee and served on the Tenant Protections Subcommittee, as Eastrail co-chair, and on the Growth Management Policy Board [S1]. She has been a small business owner for 20 years, an SEIU board delegate, a nonprofit executive director (Emerge WA) and art director for the Seattle Symphony; she holds a BFA in Visual Communication and Design from Kent State University [S1][S3]. She is an LD 48 precinct committee officer and League of Women Voters King County treasurer [S1].

Her general-election pamphlet statement is the same text she filed for the primary [S1][S2]. In the August primary she "narrowly edged Walen ... by 17 votes," per The Urbanist [S6].

## Positions

**Housing**
- Pamphlet: "won tenant protections, secured $7.5M for housing and human services" [S1].
- Site: "I have advocated for increased levels of deeply affordable housing and renter protections, and I will continue that fight in Olympia," and calls for "more housing options that provide affordable housing at all levels of affordability" [S3].

**Climate**
- Co-wrote Redmond's Climate Emergency Declaration and shaped its Environmental Sustainability Action Plan toward net zero; says she will "shift the conversations on climate change legislation from the price tag of immediate costs to the price we are already paying" [S3].
- Media kit: supports "a clean energy transition built with strong labor standards" [S4].

**Education and spending**
- Says Washington ranks 40th nationally in K-12 investment as a share of the economy; calls for "sustainable and equitable funding models that fully-fund education and reduce reliance on local levies" [S3].

**Public safety and behavioral health**
- Media kit: "added three Mental Health Professional positions to the city's crisis response team" [S4]; she chaired Redmond's Alternative Crisis Response Subcommittee [S1].
- Site: "expand mental, behavioral, and disability services and ensure that we respond with evidence-based practices" [S3].

**Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy**
- Pamphlet: "fought for LGBTQIA+ and immigrant communities" [S1].
- Media kit: "In Olympia, she will fight for the 2SLGBTQIA+ community, immigrant rights, reproductive freedom" [S4]; site: "we must safeguard reproductive rights" [S3].

**Parents and schools**
- No statement on parental notification, records access or opt-outs found [S1][S3][S4].

**Immigration**
- Pamphlet and media kit cite fighting for immigrant communities and immigrant rights [S1][S4].

## Record

- Municipal record only: passed billion-dollar Redmond budgets, tenant protections, the Climate Emergency Declaration, and crisis-response staffing [S1][S3][S4].
- Media kit says she "championed legislation that became state law (SB 5452 and SB 6002)" [S4].

## Endorsements

- Pamphlet: 48th District Democrats, King County Democrats, Washington State Labor Council, Insurance Commissioner Kuderer, Senator Slatter, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Redmond Firefighters, 60+ elected officials [S1].
- Site adds former Governor Jay Inslee, King County Executive Girmay Zahilay, several state legislators and Redmond, Kirkland and Bellevue officials [S5]. The Urbanist reports endorsements from The Urbanist, WA Bikes and Sierra Club [S6].
