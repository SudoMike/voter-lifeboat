# State Representative Position No. 2, Legislative District No. 48 — contest overview

## What this office does

State representatives serve two-year terms in the Washington House, voting on state budgets, taxes and all state law. The 48th District is an Eastside King County district (Redmond, Bellevue, Kirkland and the Points communities).

## Race dynamics

- Democrat-vs-Democrat general: incumbent Amy Walen (House since 2019, former Kirkland mayor) against Redmond City Councilmember Jessica Forsythe.
- Forsythe "narrowly edged Walen in the primary by 17 votes," per The Urbanist (amy-walen.md S8). The Urbanist expects one of the most expensive legislative races of the fall.
- The central dispute is the 2026 millionaires' tax: Walen voted no on ESSB 6346 (amy-walen.md S4) and says she supports a high-earners income tax but opposed this one as "unconstitutional, not progressive enough" and a risk to the middle class (amy-walen.md S3). The Urbanist also reports progressive criticism of her vote against statewide middle-housing reform.
- Walen lost the November 2025 LD 48 Senate election to Vandana Slatter, 40.7% to 57.97%; Slatter endorses Forsythe (amy-walen.md S7; jessica-forsythe.md S1).
- Endorsements split: Forsythe has the 48th and King County Democrats, the State Labor Council and former Gov. Inslee; Walen lists the Bellevue Police Guild, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Gov. Gregoire and Rep. Adam Smith.
- Fundraising: Walen about $398,000 raised, Forsythe about $167,000 (PDC, early October).
- Neither has a statement naming I-645, I-1 or I-638. Walen voted for ESHB 1296 (2025); Forsythe has no legislative record.

## Differentiating axes

- **Taxes:** Walen's no vote on the millionaires' tax and her revenue-offset approach vs. Forsythe's labor-backed challenge (Forsythe has no direct tax statement in her dossier).
- **Housing and local-control:** Walen's supply-side record (conversions, permitting, historic designation limits) with reported opposition to the statewide middle-housing mandate vs. Forsythe's emphasis on deeply affordable housing and renter protections.
- **Experience:** seven-year legislator and committee chair vs. municipal record.
- **Climate:** both pro-climate; Forsythe frames it more urgently (climate emergency declaration), Walen cites her CCA vote and an all-sources clean-energy list.
- Both are progressive on reproductive and LGBTQ+ policy; Walen has the more detailed written position and the ESHB 1296 vote relevant to parental-rights.
