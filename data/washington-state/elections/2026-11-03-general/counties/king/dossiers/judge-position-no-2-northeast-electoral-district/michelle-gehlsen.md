## Background

Gehlsen lists a JD from Southern Methodist University and a BA, cum laude, from Texas State University [S1].
She lists her occupation as King County District Court Judge [S1].
Her statement says she has served as a judge for more than 16 years, including 10 years as Presiding Judge of Bothell Municipal Court before joining King County District Court in 2019 [S1].

## Positions

Her statement says she is committed to treating every person in her courtroom with dignity, fairness, and respect while applying the law equally to all [S1].
She states that strong courts promote both public safety and public confidence [S1].

## Record

Her statement says she has presided over Redmond Community Court, which it describes as addressing the underlying causes of criminal behavior while reducing repeat offenses [S1].
She reports founding Bothell Youth Court [S1].
She reports that her judicial colleagues elected her President of the Washington District and Municipal Court Judges' Association [S1].
She reports receiving the Washington Women Lawyers Vanguard Award and the Judge Steiner Leadership Award [S1].

## Endorsements

Her statement reports endorsements from judges at every level of Washington's judiciary, without naming them [S1].
