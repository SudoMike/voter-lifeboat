## Background

O'Toole lists a JD, cum laude, from Seattle University School of Law and a BA from Whitman College [S1].
She lists her occupation as King County District Court Judge and states she was first elected in 2014 [S1].
Her statement reports more than 30 years of practicing law and trying cases, as a former prosecuting attorney and as a civil attorney in private practice [S1].
She states she is a lifelong Washington resident living in east King County [S1].

## Positions

Her statement says she is committed to administering justice fairly, efficiently, and equally, with everyone treated with respect and courtesy [S1].

## Record

Her statement says she volunteers on judicial committees and in the community, without naming specific committees [S1].

## Endorsements

Her statement lists endorsements from Supreme Court, appellate, superior, district, and municipal court judges [S1].
It also lists King County Councilmembers Balducci and Dunn; State Representatives Goodman, Walen, and McBride (ret.); Bellevue Mayor Malakoutian; Redmond Mayor Birney and Redmond Councilmembers Robinson, Nieuwenhuis, and Barksdale (ret.) [S1].
Organizational endorsements listed are the King County Democrats; the 1st, 5th, 41st, and 48th LD Democrats; and the National Women's Political Caucus [S1].
