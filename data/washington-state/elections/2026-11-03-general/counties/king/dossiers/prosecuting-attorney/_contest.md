## What the office does

The Prosecuting Attorney serves as King County's lawyer and prosecutes misdemeanor and felony criminal cases; the office is elected countywide for a four-year term, with a 2026 salary of $264,745 [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Leesa Manion, first elected in 2022, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1][S2]. The contest is uncontested.
