## Background

Cat McDowall (Catherine McDowall on the court's website) lists a J.D. from the University of Virginia School of Law and a B.A. from Northwestern University [S1][S2].
She began her career in civil litigation in Washington, D.C., then joined the King County Prosecuting Attorney's Office as a deputy prosecutor in 1998 and was later promoted to senior deputy prosecutor [S2].
Before her appointment she was a Judge Pro Tempore in Seattle Municipal Court (from 2013) and King County District Court, and a pro tem in King County Superior Court's Juvenile Division [S1][S2].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she says she strives to ensure decisions are "guided by the law, careful preparation, and impartial judgment" [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: her pre-bench career was in civil practice and prosecution [S1][S2].
- She presides over the Domestic Violence Intervention Project, which she describes as combining accountability with individualized treatment to reduce repeat violence [S1].

## Record

- She was appointed to the Seattle Municipal Court in September 2020 and elected to her first full term in 2022; her current term runs January 2023 to January 2027 [S2].
- She serves on the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association Board of Governors and co-chairs its Rules Committee [S1][S2].
- She co-directs Seattle Youth Traffic Court, a restorative justice program for teens with minor traffic violations [S1][S2].
