## Background

Suzan DelBene, of Medina, has represented Washington's 1st Congressional District since 2012 and sits on the House Ways and Means Committee [S1][S2]. She chairs the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC), House Democrats' campaign arm, for the 2026 cycle and is a past chair of the New Democrat Coalition [S2][S18]. Before Congress she spent about 20 years in business, including as a Microsoft executive and at local tech startups, and served as Director of the Washington State Department of Revenue [S1]. She holds a B.A. in biology from Reed College and an M.B.A. from the University of Washington [S1].

## Positions

**Cost of living and taxes**
- General-election pamphlet: supports policies to "lower the cost of living, reduce taxes for working families and ensure the wealthy pay their fair share"; says Republicans passed "massive tax cuts for the wealthiest few" paid for with health care and food-assistance cuts [S1].
- Says she has led on permanently expanding the Child Tax Credit with monthly payments to families [S1].
- On July 20, 2026 she introduced the CHEATERS Act with Reps. Sewell, Chu and Khanna, which would restore $83 billion in IRS funding for taxpayer services and "enforcement focused on high-income tax evasion"; her release says the goal is that "the wealthy and well-connected pay their fair share of taxes" [S6].
- Wants to "end President Trump's reckless tariffs" [S1].

**Health care**
- Pamphlet: fighting to "lower health care premiums" and "roll back the cruel cuts to Medicaid"; committed to "strengthening Social Security and Medicare" [S1].
- On Oct. 5, 2026 she filed a Freedom of Information Act request for records on WISeR, a Medicare program that pays private companies to use AI to review claims [S8].

**Housing**
- Says she introduced bipartisan legislation "to spur the creation of two million affordable homes" [S1].

**Immigration**
- Pamphlet: "It's past time to reform our immigration system and rein in out-of-control ICE agents who have spread fear and violence in our communities" [S1].

**Climate and energy**
- Pamphlet criticizes Republicans for taking away "critical investments in green energy" [S1].
- Sponsored the Clean Competition Act (H.R. 6787, 119th Congress), a carbon-based trade measure (per the primary dossier) [S2].
- Voted no on H.J.Res. 210 and H.J.Res. 213 (Sept. 15-16, 2026), resolutions disapproving EPA decisions on California's nonroad engine pollution standards for ships at berth and commercial harbor craft; both passed 216-211 and 214-208 [S13][S14].

**Technology and privacy**
- Recurring sponsor of the Email Privacy Act and other digital-privacy bills (per the primary dossier) [S2].

**Foreign policy and defense**
- July 15, 2026 statement on the Massie amendment to the State/foreign-operations appropriations bill: "The American relationship with Israel must change." She wrote that Netanyahu's government "has isolated Israel internationally" and "killed and displaced civilians," and that "Americans should not be subsidizing this reckless and dangerous behavior." She voted against the amendment because it also cut humanitarian and diplomatic aid, and against the underlying bill [S7]. The House Clerk record shows her voting no on Massie Amendment No. 8 (failed 104-314) [S9]; her statement does not give the amendment number.
- Voted no on final passage of the FY2027 National Defense Authorization Act (passed 216-212, mostly along party lines) [S10].
- Voted no on H.R. 4795, which bars colleges receiving federal student aid from boycotting Israel (passed 237-169) [S11][S12].
- Told reporters on Oct. 5 that "curbing the war in Iran" and health care costs would be at the top of a Democratic House majority's agenda [S18].

**Gender and reproductive policy**
- Pamphlet: "I strongly support reproductive rights and will do everything I can to fight back against Republican extremists' attempts to pass a nationwide abortion ban" [S1].
- Voted no on H.R. 28, the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act (Jan. 14, 2025), which passed the House [S15].

**Parents and schools**
- Voted no on H.R. 2616, the PROTECT Kids Act (May 20, 2026; passed 217-198). The bill would require public elementary and middle schools receiving certain federal funds to obtain parental consent before changing a minor student's gender markers, pronouns or preferred name on school forms, or sex-based accommodations [S16][S17].

- Since July 2026, House votes include those listed above, plus "Present" on the Sept. 15 motion to table an impeachment resolution against President Trump (the motion passed 232-147; 46 Democrats voted Present) [S19].

## Scoring notes

- Fourteen-year incumbent on Ways and Means with enacted legislation, running against a first-time candidate with no elected office [S1][S2].
- As DCCC chair she leads her party's national House campaign effort; about 42% of her 2026 receipts through mid-July came from political committees [S4][S18].
- Tax position combines middle-class tax cuts (Child Tax Credit) with higher enforcement on high earners (CHEATERS Act) [S1][S6].
- Her Israel position mixes criticism of the Netanyahu government and U.S. "subsidizing" it with a vote against cutting aid via the Massie amendment [S7][S9].
