## Background

Mary Silva, of Everett, is a clinical audiologist running with a "Prefers Republican Party" preference [S1][S7]. Her pamphlet lists 20 years as a clinical audiologist, seven as a managing partner; a Doctor of Audiology (Salus University, 2009), an M.A. in audiology/speech and hearing science (Washington State University, 2006) and a B.A. (Western Washington University, 2003) [S1]. She has been a Republican precinct committee officer in Snohomish County since 2022 and earlier in King County, and was a 2024 Republican state convention delegate [S1]. The "Elected Experience" section of her pamphlet lists only these party roles; she has not held public office [S1]. Her website's biography says she holds "no special official qualification" and is running because "no one is coming to save us" [S5]. Identity check: the pamphlet, campaign site and Herald profiles all describe the same CD 1 candidate and campaign website [S1][S3][S7].

## Positions

**Economy, jobs and taxes**
- Pamphlet: incentivize domestic production to reverse offshoring and "return high-quality jobs to Washington"; lower regulatory barriers and expand access to capital for small and medium-sized businesses [S1].
- Pamphlet: shift economic focus "away from Wall Street financiers and billionaires"; says offshore tax havens and "corporate welfare dependents" are "costing our country trillions in lost revenue" [S1].
- Told the Herald in July she would offer small-business tax incentives and press states such as Washington to reduce the overall tax burden [S2][S7].
- Platform: reshore semiconductor, IT, AI and cybersecurity operations; restructure the H-1B visa program to favor local hiring; expand apprenticeships and reskilling [S4].

**Federal spending and oversight**
- Platform: "a comprehensive, real audit of the Pentagon"; overhaul the "$70 Billion Intelligence Community" and curb contractor spending; "Crack Down on the Grift" from local program fraud to "billion-dollar military contractor payouts"; a "complete accounting" of Wall Street executives who profited from bailouts [S4].
- Pamphlet: prioritize "robust federal investments" in securing grids, communications and private data [S1].

**Technology, privacy and surveillance**
- Platform: end "warrantless mass surveillance" including "widespread Flock license-plate camera systems"; require warrants before agencies or vendors collect location data, video or communications; bar foreign-linked police technology [S4].
- Supports stricter cybersecurity requirements for corporations with taxpayer-funded contracts [S2][S7].

**Immigration and national security**
- Platform: "Fortify border security" and overhaul security-clearance procedures; investigate "deep foreign penetration" of U.S. institutions [S4]. Told the Herald she supports controlled immigration and opposes open borders [S2][S7].
- Platform: a "full, transparent investigation into the involvement of foreign intelligence agencies" in the Sept. 11 attacks [S4][S8].

**Foreign policy**
- Her campaign site features a video titled "How Does Netanyahu Get Away With War Crimes?" and says it shows the need to wrest control "away from the Hydra Network of criminals from the City of London Financial District" [S6].
- The "Meet Mary" page says the country has been "undermined by elements from within our own borders" who "meet at Davos and Bilderberg" and dedicates her campaign to two people she calls whistleblowers, Katherine Pollard Griggs and Brendon Lee O'Connell [S5]. The Herald reported in July that she cited authors who have promoted debunked claims about 9/11, aliens and HIV as influences [S2][S7].

**Climate and energy**
- Told the Herald in July she is "not a big believer in the climate change narrative" while supporting land stewardship [S2][S7].

**Elections**
- Platform: require voter ID and create a midweek national voting holiday [S4]. The Herald reported that her priorities include ending mail-in voting [S8].

**Health**
- Platform: "Medical Freedom & Informed Consent," opposing government mandates and "digital tracking"; strip corporate conflicts of interest from health agencies [S4]. Endorsed by Stand for Health Freedom, which the Herald describes as opposing vaccine mandates, school health centers and water fluoridation [S2][S3].

**Gender and reproductive policy**
- No statement on abortion, gender-affirming care, sports eligibility or anti-discrimination law found. Her site refers to "rights to retain the ultimate control over your body, your medical decisions" in the context of the Stand for Health Freedom endorsement [S3].

**Parents and schools**
- Platform: "Guarantee parents retain absolute authority over all health, medical, and wellness decisions for their children" [S4].
