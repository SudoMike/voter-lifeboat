## Background
Maggie Wang is a Republican-preference candidate with no elected experience listed [S1]. She immigrated from Taiwan in 1989, operated a hair salon for eight years beginning in 1981 and owned a downtown Redmond restaurant for twenty-six years [S1]. She has been a volunteer election observer at King County Elections since 2022 [S1]. In the August 4 primary she received 25.1% to Davina Duerr's 74.7% in the count reported by the Everett Herald; both advanced [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes and affordability:** "I will fight to stop new tax increases" and make the state more affordable for working families, seniors and small businesses [S2]; pamphlet focus is "lower taxes, safer neighborhoods, and an economy where small businesses thrive" [S1].
- **Spending and accountability:** supports "auditing state spending, cutting wasteful programs, increasing transparency" and reducing unnecessary government spending [S2]; wants leadership "prioritizing your budget over bureaucratic expansion" [S1].
- **Public safety:** supports law enforcement and first responders [S2].
- **Parents and schools:** names parental rights as one of three priorities [S1]; says schools should focus on education, parental involvement and preparing students for success [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no specific position found. Her site says she opposes "divisive political ideologies in government and education" without naming them [S2].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no public position on the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. Her site pledges to "stop new tax increases" [S2]. The King County Republican Party, which endorses her, recommends YES [S4]; that is the party's position, not hers.
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** no public position on the initiative found as of 2026-10-08.
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
