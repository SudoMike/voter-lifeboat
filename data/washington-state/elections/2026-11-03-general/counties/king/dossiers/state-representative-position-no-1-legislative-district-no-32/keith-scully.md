## Background
Keith Scully has served on the Shoreline City Council since 2016, as deputy mayor (2020-22) and mayor (2022-24) [S1]. He was a paramedic (1994-98), King County deputy prosecutor (1999-2005), United Nations war crimes prosecutor (2005-06), legal director of the environmental nonprofit Futurewise (2006-08), and has been an intellectual property attorney since 2008 [S1]. He holds a J.D. from NYU, an M.A. from Tufts and a B.A. from UCLA [S1]. Community roles include the Regional Crisis Response Agency board and chairing the Shoreline Planning Commission [S1]. He led election-night primary returns for this open seat with 26.6% [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** Calls the Millionaire's Tax "a step in the right direction" and "the start, not the end" of tax reform; wants regressive sales, B&O and property taxes replaced with progressive ones, "not just layering new taxes on top" [S3]. Earlier said he wants the code to mirror the federal progressive structure and to tax business profit rather than revenue [S9].
- **Spending:** Says "Olympia needs to rein in spending" and the budget has grown dramatically every year [S3]; earlier proposed a small across-the-board cut except education [S5][S9].
- **Education:** Wants dedicated school revenue, including a share of the millionaires tax, and more local freedom for districts to raise funds [S3].
- **Housing:** Proposes tax credits and subsidies for extremely low-income rentals, streamlined permitting, and community land trusts; says the state "has taken important steps to mandate needed density" [S3].
- **Climate:** Supports 100% sustainable energy, faster permitting for solar and wind, research on geothermal and small modular nuclear, and maintaining the Climate Commitment Act [S3].
- **Technology:** Favors "limited, responsible regulation of AI": disclosure when interacting with AI, no AI in schools or with children, human oversight for dangerous uses, and data centers only with clean energy in place [S3].
- **Federal government and rights:** Supports banning masked law enforcement and barring out-of-state National Guard deployment without the governor's authority [S3]. Calls for vigorous enforcement of the hate-crimes law [S3].
- **Public safety:** Says that in Shoreline he brought mental health professionals into the field [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** No statement found.
- **Parents and schools:** No statement found on notification or opt-out rights.
