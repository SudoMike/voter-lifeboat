## Background
Danica Noble is an attorney who spent about 20 years at the Federal Trade Commission, most recently in the Bureau of Competition's Technology Enforcement Division, and was a technical advisor on competitive markets for USAID in Ukraine [S1][S2]. She worked on the FTC's antitrust case against Meta and left the agency in 2026 to run [S4][S10]. She chairs the Washington State Bar's Antitrust, Consumer Protection and Unfair Business Practices Section, has served on the Washington State PTA Advocacy Committee, and was a director of NORML Women of Washington (2011-2021) [S1]. She attended Whitman College and Georgetown Law [S1]. This is her first campaign; she was second in election-night primary returns with 24.2% [S4].

## Positions
- **Cost of living and competition:** Would strengthen the Attorney General's antitrust powers and the Insurance Commissioner's office, prohibit surveillance pricing, and cut red tape and insurance costs for childcare centers [S3].
- **Technology:** Campaign centers on "common-sense guardrails" on AI and technology targeting children and workers; opposes harvesting student data and backs smartphone-free classrooms [S1][S3][S9].
- **Housing:** Wants faster permitting for more housing at every income level, including starter homes and missing-middle housing, plus stronger renter protections, including against AI rent-setting and junk fees [S3].
- **Education:** Prioritizes stable, fair school funding and language access for families [S3]; says she will "fully fund education and spend wisely, with real accountability" [S9].
- **Health care:** Would bar insurers from using AI to deny coverage, empower state regulators, invest in public hospitals and train more health workers [S3].
- **Refugees and immigration:** Opposes federal deportation of refugees to dangerous countries and supports state help for refugees with English classes, housing and jobs [S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** No campaign statement found; Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates endorses her [S6].
- **Parents and schools:** No statement found on notification or opt-out rights.
