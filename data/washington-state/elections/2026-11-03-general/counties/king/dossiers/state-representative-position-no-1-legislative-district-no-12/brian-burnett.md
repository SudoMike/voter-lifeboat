## Background
Brian Burnett (Prefers Republican Party) of Wenatchee is the first-term incumbent for the 12th Legislative District, Position 1, elected in 2024 [S1][S9]. He was Chelan County Sheriff from 2011 to 2022 after more than 25 years in law enforcement, is a past president of the Washington Association of Sheriffs & Police Chiefs and the Washington State Sheriffs Association, and is Director of Law Enforcement Services for Seattle's Finest Security [S1][S14]. The 12th District covers all of Chelan County plus parts of Snohomish and King counties [S9]. Certified primary results showed him at 50.2% to Stacy Willoughby's 49.8%, a 174-vote gap districtwide [S9]; in the King County portion Willoughby led 59.25% to 40.67% [S10].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** "state spending has doubled over the past decade, taxes have been increased at record levels, and now a new state income tax has been added on top of it all"; pledges budgeting that "reins in spending" [S2]. On ESSB 6346: "The way this bill is written, it is an income tax on everyone with a $1 million exemption" [S3].
- **Public safety:** "Washingtonians reject the 'Defund the Police' movement"; will "fight for policies that hold offenders accountable, support those who serve" [S2]; pamphlet cites "victim-centered justice that holds criminals accountable" [S1].
- **Local control:** pamphlet cites "returning decision-making to local communities" [S1]; at the October 5, 2026 forum said the state should let cities decide what is best for them on housing [S11].
- **Parents and schools:** pamphlet says he works to protect "your role as a parent" [S1]; his legislative site says "Washington parents want their rights and their children protected" [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** says that if the girls' sports initiative to the Legislature had come to the floor he "would have supported" it [S2].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1M):** No explicit statement on I-645 found as of 2026-10-08. He voted nay on ESSB 6346 and issued a release criticizing its passage [S3][S4].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** Supports. His legislative site says of the two 2026 initiatives to the Legislature on parental rights and girls' sports: "If these measures had come to the floor, I would have supported both" [S2]. He also voted nay on ESHB 1296 (2025), the law I-1 would undo [S5][S6].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** Supports, per the same statement [S2].

## Record
- ESSB 6346 (2026): nay on House final passage, 2026-03-09 [S4].
- ESHB 1296 (2025): nay on House passage 2025-03-12 and on final passage 2025-04-24 [S5][S6].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S7].
- PDC (as of the 2026-10-05 update): $148,449.99 raised and $48,137.28 spent for 2026 [S8].
