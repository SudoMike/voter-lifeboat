## Background
Stacy Willoughby (Prefers Democratic Party) of Chelan is a first-time candidate with 25 years in financial services, including as a financial advisor and bank branch manager [S1][S10]. She is board president of Chelan Valley Hope (rent assistance, fuel, firewood, food and eviction prevention), founder of Chelan Business Women Connect and a Lake Chelan Chamber of Commerce board member [S1]. Certified primary results showed her at 49.8% to incumbent Brian Burnett's 50.2% districtwide [S5]; she led the King County portion 59.25% to 40.67% [S6].

## Positions
- **Taxes and budget:** "a state budget that prioritizes tax fairness for rural families and small businesses" [S1]; supports "balanced budgets that invest in priorities like education, healthcare, and infrastructure" with an approach to "fund what works, cut waste" [S2].
- **Housing:** "increase housing supply, crack down on predatory lending practices, and invest in rural and workforce housing"; "cutting through excessive building codes and permitting burdens" [S2].
- **Local control:** at the October 5, 2026 forum: "we need to make sure that we are letting each community decide what they need for housing" and "The communities do know what's best for them" [S7].
- **Workforce and schools:** trades pathways for young people; fully fund public schools [S1][S2].
- **Governance:** "Government should work for everyday people, not special interests or party insiders"; supports requiring the Legislature to report small-business cost impacts of mandates before voting [S2][S7].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found.
