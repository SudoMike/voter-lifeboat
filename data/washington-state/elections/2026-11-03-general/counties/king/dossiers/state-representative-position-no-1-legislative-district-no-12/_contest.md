## What the office does
One of two state representatives for the 12th Legislative District in the Washington State House, elected to a two-year term. The district covers all of Chelan County plus parts of Snohomish and King counties (in King: Carnation, Fall City, Snoqualmie Ridge and North Bend), so King voters are a minority of its electorate (see brian-burnett.md S9, S11). Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets.

## District and race dynamics
One of the closest legislative races in the state: certified primary results had first-term Republican Brian Burnett at 50.2% and Democrat Stacy Willoughby at 49.8%, a 174-vote gap, with Willoughby winning the King County portion 59% to 41% (brian-burnett.md S9, S10). Burnett, a former Chelan County sheriff, has raised $148k; Willoughby, a Chelan banker and nonprofit president, $118k (brian-burnett.md S8, stacy-willoughby.md S4). Both spoke at the October 5 SnoValley Chamber forum (brian-burnett.md S11).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes and spending:** Burnett voted against the 2026 income tax and campaigns on reining in spending; Willoughby talks about "tax fairness for rural families and small businesses" and balanced investment, without a stated position on the income tax (brian-burnett.md S2, S4; stacy-willoughby.md S1, S2).
2. **Parents and schools / girls' sports:** Burnett voted against ESHB 1296 and says he would have supported both 2026 initiatives on parental rights and girls' sports; Willoughby has no stated position (brian-burnett.md S2, S5).
3. **Public safety:** Burnett's sheriff background and enforcement-first platform; Willoughby has no stated safety platform.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** one-term incumbent and 12-year sheriff vs. first-time candidate.
5. **Local control:** both say communities should decide their own housing needs, so this axis does not separate them (brian-burnett.md S11; stacy-willoughby.md S7).
