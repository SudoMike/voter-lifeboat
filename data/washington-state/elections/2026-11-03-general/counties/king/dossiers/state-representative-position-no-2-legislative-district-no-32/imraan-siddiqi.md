## Background
Imraan Siddiqi is Executive Director of CAIR-Washington, a civil rights advocate and former small business owner (a coffee shop in Arizona); he has not held elected office [S1][S2]. He holds a BA from the University of Texas Arlington and an MBA from Arizona State, and moved to Washington in 2020 [S1][S2]. He says he was one of the original plaintiffs against the Trump administration's Muslim ban [S1]. He took 26.2% in election-night primary returns against incumbent Lauren Davis [S6].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "Make the wealthiest pay their fair share"; supports "progressive revenue" to fund affordability [S3].
- **Housing:** More homes near jobs and transit, including missing-middle and family-sized units, plus stronger renter protections and anti-displacement measures [S3].
- **Cost of living:** Supports policies reducing out-of-pocket costs for childcare, health care, transportation and utilities and expanding access to services [S3].
- **Immigration:** Protecting immigrant communities is a core plank: due process, state-funded legal defense and know-your-rights resources, language access, and keeping state and local institutions from facilitating unconstitutional federal enforcement [S1][S3].
- **Privacy:** Supports strong privacy protections so state data systems "cannot be weaponized against communities" [S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Says he "will be on the front lines of fighting back against any harmful legislation aimed at marginalized communities," naming LGBTQIA+ communities among them [S3]. Endorsed by Washington State Stonewall Democrats [S4].
- **Parents and schools:** No statement found.
