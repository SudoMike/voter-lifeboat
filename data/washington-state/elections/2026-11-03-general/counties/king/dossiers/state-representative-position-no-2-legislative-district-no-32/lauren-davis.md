## Background
Lauren Davis has been the State Representative for this seat since 2019 [S1]. Her background is in behavioral health: founder of the Washington Recovery Alliance, UW School of Social Work lecturer, and adolescent mental health program director; she was also a Gates Foundation analyst and Fulbright Scholar, and holds a BA from Brown [S1]. She took 71.7% in election-night primary returns against fellow Democrat Imraan Siddiqi [S10].

## Positions
- **Behavioral health and addiction:** Wants prevention and early intervention for youth, "treatment on demand," and recovery support [S2].
- **Public safety:** Cites passing Ricky's Law (involuntary treatment for life-threatening addiction), creating the Recovery Navigator Program, treatment programs in jails and ERs, the Lynnwood Crisis Care Center, and victim advocates who respond with police [S1][S14].
- **Gun violence:** Supports "evidence-based" gun laws [S1][S2].
- **Housing:** Lists affordable housing, preventing family homelessness and supportive housing for people with behavioral health conditions [S2].
- **Education:** Calls for "game-changing investments" from early learning through higher education [S2].
- **Immigration:** Says she has "championed healthcare for immigrants and protections against ICE" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Pamphlet: "I'm completely pro-choice" [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** No campaign statement found; she was an original sponsor of ESHB 1296 (see Record).

## Record
- Voted yea on House final passage of ESSB 6346 (9.9% tax on income over $1 million), 2026-03-09 [S5].
- Original sponsor of HB 1296 and yea on House final passage, 2025-03-12 and 2025-04-24; the bill made additions, changes and deletions to the I-2081 parental-rights list [S6][S7].
- Voted yea on final passage of EHB 1217 (rent stabilization) as recommended by the conference committee, 2025-04-27 [S8].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S9].
- PDC (data updated 2026-10-04): $227,424.22 raised, $75,885.51 spent, $39,488.58 carried forward [S4].

## Endorsements
- Pamphlet: Govs. Ferguson and Gregoire; Reps. DelBene, Larsen, Smith; local councilmembers; firefighter, nurse, teacher, police and grocery-worker unions; State Labor Council; 32nd District Democrats [S1].

## Scoring notes
- Eight-year incumbent with a behavioral-health record that combines involuntary treatment (Ricky's Law) and diversion (Recovery Navigator) [S1].
- Endorsed by both police organizations and progressive groups, and by both AWB and labor [S3][S12].
- Voted for the millionaires tax and sponsored ESHB 1296 [S5][S7].
- Her challenger campaigns mainly on immigrant protection and affordability; Davis also cites immigrant health care and ICE protections [S1].
