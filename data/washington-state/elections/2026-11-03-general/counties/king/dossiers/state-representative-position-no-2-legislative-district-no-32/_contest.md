## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members per legislative district, elected to a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets.

## District and race dynamics
LD 32 spans the King/Snohomish line: Shoreline, Lynnwood, Mountlake Terrace, Edmonds, Woodway and part of north Seattle (lauren-davis.md S10). The general is a rematch of the two-candidate primary: incumbent Democrat Lauren Davis (71.7% on election night) against Democrat Imraan Siddiqi, executive director of CAIR-Washington (26.2%) (lauren-davis.md S10). The 32nd District Democrats dual-endorsed both (lauren-davis.md S13). Davis has raised about three times as much (lauren-davis.md S4; imraan-siddiqi.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience:** an eight-year incumbent with a behavioral-health record versus a first-time candidate from civil-rights organizing.
2. **Immigration:** both stand with immigrants; it is Siddiqi's central theme, while Davis cites immigrant health care and ICE protections (imraan-siddiqi.md S3; lauren-davis.md S1).
3. **Taxes:** Davis voted for the 2026 millionaires tax; Siddiqi campaigns on making the wealthiest pay (lauren-davis.md S5; imraan-siddiqi.md S3).
4. **Coalitions:** Davis is backed by police guilds, AWB, labor and Planned Parenthood; Siddiqi by progressive groups, Stonewall Democrats and progressive members of Congress (lauren-davis.md S3, S11, S12; imraan-siddiqi.md S4).
5. **Parental rights:** Davis was an original sponsor of ESHB 1296 (lauren-davis.md S7). Siddiqi has no stated position.
