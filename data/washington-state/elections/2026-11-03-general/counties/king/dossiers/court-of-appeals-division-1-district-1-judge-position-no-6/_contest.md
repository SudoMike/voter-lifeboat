## What the office does

Court of Appeals judges serve six-year terms [S1]. The pamphlet notes that most cases appealed from Superior Courts go directly to the Court of Appeals, and that the Court must accept all appeals filed [S1]. Division I is a ten-member court [S1].

## Race dynamics

The state pamphlet lists Bill A. Bowman as unopposed [S1]. He is the incumbent: Governor Jay Inslee appointed him to the Court of Appeals in January 2020 [S1].
