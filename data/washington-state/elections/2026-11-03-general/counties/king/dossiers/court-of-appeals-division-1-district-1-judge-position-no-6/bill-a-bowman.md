## Background

Bill A. Bowman lists a B.A. from the University of Washington and a J.D. from California Western School of Law [S1][S2].
Before taking the bench he was a King County public defender, a King County deputy prosecuting attorney, and an attorney in private practice for nearly two decades [S1][S2].
He was an adjunct trial advocacy professor at the University of Washington School of Law (2014-2026) and Dean of the Washington State Judicial College (2017) [S1].

## Positions

- Criminal-justice formation: his pre-bench career included both public defense and prosecution [S1][S2].
- His pamphlet statement says that on the King County Superior Court he worked with colleagues and community groups to make resources available to underserved parts of King County and to "safely reduce pretrial incarceration" [S1].
- Court administration: he chairs the Court of Appeals Legislative Committee and, on the Superior Court, chaired the Technology and Budget committees and served on the Executive Committee [S1][S2].

## Record

- He was Black Diamond Municipal Court Presiding Judge (2011) and a King County Superior Court judge (2011-2020), where he presided over criminal, civil, family law, and dependency trials [S1][S2].
- On the Superior Court he served as Assistant Presiding Judge, Chief Judge of the Maleng Regional Justice Center, Chief Judge of the Civil Department, and Assistant Chief Judge of the Criminal Department [S1][S2].
- Governor Jay Inslee appointed him to the Court of Appeals in January 2020, and he is Acting Chief Judge for Division One [S1][S2].
