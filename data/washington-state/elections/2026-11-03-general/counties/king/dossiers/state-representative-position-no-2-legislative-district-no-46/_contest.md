# State Representative, Position 2, Legislative District 46 — contest overview

- depth: light

## What this office does

A Washington State Representative serves a two-year term in the state House, one of two representatives per legislative district, writing and voting on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets (housing, education funding, health care, taxes, public safety, transportation). The 46th LD covers northeast Seattle.

## Race dynamics

- The same two candidates as in the primary. Incumbent Darya Farivar (Prefers Democratic Party) won about 89.4%; first-time candidate Rodney 'Star' Thornley (Prefers Independent Party) won about 10.0% (see darya-farivar.md, rodney-star-thornley.md).
- Farivar reports about $66,000 raised; Thornley has no PDC financial reports on file.

## Issue axes that differentiate these candidates

1. **Experience:** sitting legislator with committee leadership and enacted bills versus a first-time candidate.
2. **Taxes:** Farivar voted for ESSB 6346 and her site backs a wealth tax; Thornley wants B&O cuts for small businesses and says the capital gains tax should be "reconsidered by voters."
3. **Housing:** Farivar calls rent stabilization the most important housing bill; Thornley would modify HB 1110 to preserve single-family neighborhoods while capping rent in mixed-income housing.
4. **Reform:** Thornley runs explicitly against party-line politics and proposes a 50% assessment on political contributions.
5. **Immigration:** both lean pro-immigrant. Thornley proposes a state resident-registration credential for undocumented residents with limits on ICE transfers.
6. **Parental rights:** only Farivar has evidence (yes vote on ESHB 1296). Neither has stated a position on I-1 or I-638.
