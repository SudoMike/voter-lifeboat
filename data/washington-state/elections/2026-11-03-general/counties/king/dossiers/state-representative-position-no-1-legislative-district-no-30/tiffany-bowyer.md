## Background
Tiffany Bowyer (prefers Republican Party) is a first-time candidate from Federal Way; her pamphlet entry lists no elected experience [S1]. She is a small-business owner with experience in budgeting, marketing, customer service, and community engagement, and has training in massage therapy, herbalism, and yoga instruction [S1]. Her community service includes her daughter's co-op, fundraising for Seattle Children's Hospital, church volunteering, and childcare and postpartum support for local families [S1]. In the August primary she received 35.93% of the vote to incumbent Jamila Taylor's 63.95%, per final results reported by the Federal Way Mirror [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes and affordability:** cites rising property taxes, gas prices, and utility bills while "government spending continues to grow" [S1]; says "I will vote no on new taxes that burden working families and small and local businesses" and that government "should live within its means" [S2].
- **Spending and accountability:** cites state audits of childcare funding and grant awards and calls for "independent audits with real follow through," transparent reporting, and performance standards [S2].
- **Small business:** cites B&O taxes, payroll costs, and regulatory burdens; says she will "reduce unnecessary barriers" [S2].
- **Public safety:** will support "fully staffing our police, holding repeat offenders accountable, and ensuring behavioral health teams work alongside law enforcement" [S2].
