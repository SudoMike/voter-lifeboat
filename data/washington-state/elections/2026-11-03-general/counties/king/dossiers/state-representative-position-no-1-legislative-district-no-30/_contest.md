## What the office does
One of two State Representatives for the 30th Legislative District (Federal Way and nearby south King County communities), elected to a two-year term in the 98-member House. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital, and transportation budgets, which set K-12 funding, state taxes, housing rules, behavioral-health programs, and criminal statutes.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates: incumbent Democrat Jamila E. Taylor, chair of the House Civil Rights & Judiciary Committee, and Republican first-time candidate Tiffany Bowyer, a Federal Way small-business owner (see jamila-e-taylor.md S1; tiffany-bowyer.md S1). They were also the only two candidates on the primary ballot.

Taylor runs on gun-violence, police-training, housing, childcare and tax-credit legislation, with Democratic, labor and reproductive-rights endorsements, including SEIU 775 and Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates (jamila-e-taylor.md S1, S11, S12). Bowyer runs on voting no on new taxes, independent audits of state programs, and full police staffing; the King County Republican Party lists her as its "BEST CHOICE", one tier below a formal endorsement (tiffany-bowyer.md S2, S4). Taylor has raised about $87k against Bowyer's about $16k (PDC; jamila-e-taylor.md S10, tiffany-bowyer.md S3).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Taylor voted for ESSB 6346 (the 2026 tax on income over $1 million that I-645 would repeal) and cites targeted credits; Bowyer pledges to vote no on new taxes.
2. **Spending and accountability:** Bowyer centers independent audits and performance standards; Taylor runs on delivering resources for housing and childcare.
3. **Public safety:** Taylor says excessive policing and incarceration should not be the community solution; Bowyer proposes full police staffing and repeat-offender accountability, both with behavioral-health components.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** a sitting committee chair vs. a first-time candidate.
5. **Parental rights and social policy:** Taylor's record includes votes for ESHB 1296 and ESSB 5599; Bowyer states no position on either topic.
