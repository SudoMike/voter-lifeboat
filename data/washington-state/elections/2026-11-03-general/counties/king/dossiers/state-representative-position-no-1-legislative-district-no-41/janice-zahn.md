## Background
Janice Zahn (Prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Representative for LD 41, Position 1 [S1]. The King County Council appointed her in January 2025 to succeed Tana Senn, and she won the November 2025 special election to keep the seat [S2]. She served on the Bellevue City Council from 2017 to 2025 [S1]. She is Chief Engineer and Public Works Construction Manager at the Port of Seattle and was earlier a structural engineer at Berger/ABAM [S1]. She holds B.S. and M.S. degrees in civil engineering and an Executive MPA from the University of Washington [S1]. She describes herself as an immigrant from Hong Kong who attended Federal Way public schools and has lived in Bellevue for more than 30 years [S4]. In the House she serves on Finance, Housing and Transportation, is vice chair of Local Government, and is an assistant majority whip [S1][S4].

In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 72.04% (26,578 votes) to Elle Nguyen's 26.76% (9,871 votes), per final King County results reported by the Mercer Island Reporter [S5].

## Positions
- **Affordability and investment:** says she is "focused on improving affordability and delivering investments in housing, education, childcare, and transportation, while addressing our state's budget challenges responsibly" [S1]. Her general-election statement is the same as her primary statement [S1][S2].
- **Budget:** says she is "focused on supporting workers and small businesses, protecting essential services" [S4]; her campaign site emphasizes "practical solutions, fiscal responsibility, and delivering results" and "accountability as we address Washington's budget challenges" [S3].
- **Climate:** "Washington is not on track to meet our 2030 climate goals — we need to accelerate investments in clean energy and community resilience" [S4].
- **Priorities listed for the general:** making housing more affordable, strengthening public education, improving transportation and infrastructure, economic and workforce opportunity [S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no stated position found; her pamphlet endorsement list includes Planned Parenthood [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** no stated position found beyond the ESHB 1296 vote under Record [S7].

## Record
- **ESSB 6346 (2026 tax on household income over $1 million, the tax I-645 would repeal):** voted yea on House final passage, 2026-03-09 (51-46) [S8].
- **ESHB 1296 (2025 amendments to the parents' rights law that I-1 would undo):** voted yea on House third reading (2025-03-12) and on final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, 59-39) [S7].
- **EHB 1217 (2025 rent stabilization):** voted yea on House final passage as recommended by the conference committee, 2025-04-27 (54-44) [S9].
- **ESHB 2266 (2026):** voted yea on House passage (2026-02-16) and final passage (2026-03-09) [S10]. The enacted bill requires cities to allow transitional housing and permanent supportive housing in urban-growth-area zones where homes or hotels are allowed, and indoor emergency shelters where hotels are allowed [S11].
- First-term bills reported by her campaign include public-works contracting reforms and HB 2228 (building-code stair flexibility) [S2]; her site cites work to "reform public works contracting to save taxpayers money" [S3].
- No floor votes since July: the 2026 session "adjourned sine die on Thursday March 12, 2026" [S12], and no special session has been found.

## Endorsements
- Pamphlet: nurses, teachers, Planned Parenthood, labor, 41st and King County Democrats, Conservation Action, Gun Responsibility, Sen. Wellman, Rep. Thai, Congressmembers Smith and DelBene, King County Councilmembers Balducci and Perry [S1].
- Her campaign site quotes the Seattle Times editorial board's primary endorsement: "Janice Zahn is exactly the kind of lawmaker voters should want to be part of what will be an arduous 2027 legislative session" [S3].
- Sierra Club Washington endorsed her in the primary [S2].
