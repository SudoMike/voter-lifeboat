## Background
Elle Nguyen (Prefers Republican Party) is a Mercer Island candidate who has not held elected office [S1][S2]. She ran for the Mercer Island School Board in 2021 (advancing past the primary) and for LD 41 Position 2 in 2022 [S1]. Her pamphlet lists community advocacy, legal research and drafting, youth and family advocacy, and small-business and real-estate investment experience, including chairing Connect Care Alliance [S1]. Education: B.A. in theology/religious studies, Master of Divinity, and J.D. [S1]. She describes herself as "a widowed mother, real estate investor and community advocate" [S4].

In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 26.76% (9,871 votes) to Janice Zahn's 72.04%, advancing to the general [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "opposes a state income tax and believes taxpayers deserve greater accountability for how their money is spent" (Renton Reporter's summary of her statement) [S4]. Her site lists "protecting taxpayers from unnecessary tax increases" [S3].
- **Spending:** "Government must exercise fiscal discipline, protect taxpayers, support small businesses" and "Olympia has been fiscally irresponsible" [S4]; her site lists "Stopping wasteful spending, balancing the budget" [S3].
- **Public safety:** her site lists "Supporting law enforcement, holding criminals accountable, and keeping our neighborhoods safe" [S3]; her pamphlet lists "improving public safety" [S1].
- **Local control and housing siting:** "adamantly opposes House Bill 2266": "Olympia should not substitute statewide mandates for the judgment of local communities. Housing and homelessness require compassionate solutions; however, communities must retain a meaningful voice in decisions affecting their neighborhoods, schools, public safety, and quality of life" [S4]. ESHB 2266 requires cities to allow transitional and permanent supportive housing and indoor emergency shelters in specified urban zones [S7]. She says she is committed to "meaningful local control" [S4].
- **Health:** says she will advocate for "accessible healthcare for the disabled and mental-health treatment emphasizing prevention, early intervention, intensive counseling, and comprehensive care rather than relying primarily on medication" [S4].
- **Governing philosophy:** "protect constitutional rights, restore accountability, respect families and local communities, strengthen economic opportunity, and apply common sense to government" [S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no specific position found. Her general pamphlet statement cites "traditional values" without naming a policy [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** "I am equally committed to public safety, parental rights, protecting children and meaningful local control" [S4]. Her primary-era site and statement also emphasized parental rights [S2]. No specific position on notification, curricula or opt-outs was found [S3][S4]. Her general pamphlet statement no longer uses the phrase "parental rights"; it cites "protecting children, strengthening families" [S1].
