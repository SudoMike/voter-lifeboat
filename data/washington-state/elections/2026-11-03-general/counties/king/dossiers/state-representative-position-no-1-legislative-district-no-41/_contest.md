## What this office does
One of two House members for LD 41, elected to a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: taxes, K-12 funding, housing and land use, healthcare, transportation and public safety.

## The district and this race
LD 41 is an Eastside district in King County: Mercer Island, Newcastle and Beaux Arts Village plus parts of Bellevue, Issaquah, Renton and Sammamish (janice-zahn.md [S3]; elle-nguyen.md [S2]). Incumbent Democrat Janice Zahn, appointed in 2025 and elected in a 2025 special election, faces Republican Elle Nguyen. Zahn took 72.04% in the August primary to Nguyen's 26.76%, with a third candidate eliminated (janice-zahn.md [S5]). Zahn reports about $54,000 raised this cycle; Nguyen files under PDC mini reporting and shows no contributions (janice-zahn.md [S6]; elle-nguyen.md [S6]).

## Differentiating issue axes
- **Taxes:** Zahn voted for the 2026 tax on household income over $1 million that I-645 would repeal; Nguyen says she opposes a state income tax.
- **Local control:** Nguyen "adamantly opposes" ESHB 2266, the 2026 law requiring cities to allow supportive housing and shelters in specified zones, as a state mandate. Zahn voted for it.
- **Spending:** Nguyen centers fiscal discipline. Zahn pairs investment priorities with "fiscal responsibility" language.
- **Parental rights:** Zahn voted for ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments I-1 would undo. Nguyen states a general commitment to "parental rights" with no specifics.
- **Experience:** sitting legislator and former councilmember vs. a candidate who has not held office.
- Neither candidate has stated a position on any of the three statewide initiatives.
