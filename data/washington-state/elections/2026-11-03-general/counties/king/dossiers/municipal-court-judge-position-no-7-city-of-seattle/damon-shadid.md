## Background

Damon Shadid lists a Juris Doctor as his education and Seattle Municipal Court Judge as his occupation [S1].
Before taking the bench he took part in community groups including the Race and Social Justice Community Roundtable, the Racial Justice Report Card, and the Task Force on Race and the Criminal Justice System [S2].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: he says the criminal legal system should have two complementary objectives, keeping the community safe and helping people obtain the services they need to exit the system [S1].
- Diversion and specialty courts: he says colleagues tapped him to work with the City Attorney and the Department of Public Defense on a new Connection Court, built on his work in the former Community Court, which offers participants quick connection to community services [S1].
- He says the Municipal Court should play a central role in improving access to housing, substance abuse treatment, and mental health services [S1].

## Record

- He joined the Seattle Municipal Court in January 2014 and is its Assistant Presiding Judge; his current term runs January 2023 to January 2027 [S2].
- He presided over the Seattle Mental Health Court for four years and started the court's Consolidated Calendar for people who have community case workers [S1][S2].
- He has served on the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association Board of Directors and the Washington State Legal Financial Obligations Stakeholder Consortium [S2].
