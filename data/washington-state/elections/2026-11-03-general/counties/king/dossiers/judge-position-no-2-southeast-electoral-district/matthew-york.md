## Background

York lists a BA in Communication from the University of Nevada, Reno, and a Juris Doctorate and Certificate of Dispute Resolution from Willamette University [S1].
He lists his occupation as King County District Court Judge and says he has served as a judge for the past nine years [S1].
His statement does not describe his practice before the bench [S1].

## Positions

His statement says complicated rules and procedure can alienate people unfamiliar with them and that he takes time to make sure questions are answered and expectations are clear [S1].
It says a judge has a responsibility to serve outside the courtroom as well as in it [S1].

## Record

His statement says colleagues elected him at times as South Presiding Judge and Chief Presiding Judge over his last two terms [S1].
He reports chairing the Executive Committee and Rules Committee and currently sitting on the Personnel Committee [S1].
He states he volunteers with several south King County non-profit organizations [S1].
