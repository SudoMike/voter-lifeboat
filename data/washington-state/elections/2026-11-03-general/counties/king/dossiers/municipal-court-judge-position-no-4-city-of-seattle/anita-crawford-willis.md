## Background

Anita Crawford-Willis lists a J.D. from Seattle University School of Law and a B.A. from Seattle University [S1].
Before her appointment she spent about 30 years in public service as a public defender, Administrative Law Judge, and Judge Pro Tem at Seattle Municipal Court [S1][S2].
Her court biography says she was assistant deputy chief at the Office of Administrative Hearings, where she ran the Seattle office [S2].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she says "accountability and compassion are complementary values" and that her focus has been equal access to justice [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: her pre-bench career included public defense and administrative law judging [S1][S2].
- Court programs: as Presiding Judge she says she is helping launch the court's Connection Court and expand its Community Resource Center [S1].

## Record

- She was appointed to the Seattle Municipal Court in 2017 and re-elected in 2018 and 2022 [S1][S2].
- She is the court's Presiding Judge [S1][S2].
- Her pamphlet statement says she served as President of the District and Municipal Court Judges Association; her court biography describes her as chair-elect of that association [S1][S2].
- She has served on the Washington Supreme Court's Gender and Justice Commission and serves on the Seattle University Board of Trustees [S1][S2].
- She received the Washington State Bar Association's Outstanding Judge award (2022) and the Loren Miller Bar Association Lifetime Achievement Award (2025) [S1][S2].
