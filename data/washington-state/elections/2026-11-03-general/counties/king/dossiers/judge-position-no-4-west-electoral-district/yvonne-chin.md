## Background

Yvonne Chin lists a J.D. from DePaul University College of Law and a B.A. from Vassar College [S1].
Before joining the bench she was a civil legal aid and public interest attorney, including as Legal Director of the Sexual Violence Law Center and Senior Staff Attorney at the ACLU of Washington [S1].
She describes experience in civil, criminal, constitutional, and administrative law, representing clients in trial and appellate courts in Washington, Illinois, and federal court [S1].
She also served as a Judge and Magistrate Pro Tempore in Seattle Municipal Court [S1].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she cites "independence, careful preparation, and respect for every person's dignity" as qualities that help keep courts fair and accessible [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: her stated background is mainly civil legal aid and public interest litigation; the statement does not describe a bail or diversion posture [S1].

## Record

- The King County Council unanimously appointed her to the District Court in 2026 [S1].
- Her statement reports "Exceptionally Well Qualified" ratings from the King County Bar Association, King County Women Lawyers, the Latina/o Bar Association, and the Loren Miller Bar Association; these ratings are self-reported and were not independently checked for this entry [S1].
