## Background

Shantrice Anderson lists a B.A. from Gonzaga University and a J.D. from Seattle University School of Law [S1].
She lists her occupation as Civil Attorney, Pro Tem Judge/Magistrate [S1].
She reports more than a decade as a public defender in King County and Colorado, handling misdemeanors, juvenile cases, and felonies, and says she now practices civil law in state and federal courts [S1].
She serves as a Pro Tem Judge in Seattle Municipal Court, presiding over pretrial hearings, motions, pleas, and sentencings [S1].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she says her decisions would be based on "careful listening, sound judgment, accountability, and rehabilitation" and that rulings "should be firmly rooted in the law" [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: her stated background is public defense followed by civil practice; she says she worked with specialty and community courts in and out of Washington and saw benefits they can bring [S1].
- Court access: she lists expanding meaningful access to justice and reducing barriers for underrepresented communities as priorities [S1].

## Record

- She has not served as an elected judge; her judicial experience is as a pro tem judge and magistrate [S1].
- Her statement reports ratings of "Exceptionally Well-Qualified" from the Joint Asian Evaluations Committee and the Latina/o and Loren Miller Bar Associations, and "Well-Qualified" from QLaw and the King County Bar Association; these ratings are self-reported and were not independently checked for this entry [S1].

## Endorsements

- Her statement lists endorsements from retiring Judge Faye Chess, Judge Haydee Vargas, District and County Democrats, and Justice Mungia [S1].
