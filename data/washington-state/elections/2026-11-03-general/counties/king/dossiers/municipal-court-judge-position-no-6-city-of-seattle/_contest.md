## What the office does

Seattle Municipal Court judges hear and decide misdemeanor cases for the City of Seattle [S1]. Judges are elected by voters in the City of Seattle and serve four-year terms; the 2026 salary is $214,791 [S1].

## Race dynamics

Shantrice Anderson is the only candidate on the ballot and runs unopposed [S2]. She lists her occupation as Civil Attorney, Pro Tem Judge/Magistrate, and lists an endorsement from "retiring Judge Faye Chess"; no sitting judge appears on the ballot for this position and the seat appears to be open [S2].
