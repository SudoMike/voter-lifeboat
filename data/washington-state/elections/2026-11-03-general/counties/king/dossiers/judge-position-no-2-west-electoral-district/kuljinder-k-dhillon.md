## Background

Kuljinder K. Dhillon lists a J.D. from Gonzaga University School of Law (2002) and a B.A. in Communications from Washington State University (1995) [S1].
She lists her occupation as King County District Court Judge and says she served King County for more than 30 years before her 2021 appointment to the court [S1].
Before law school she was a Domestic Violence Victim Advocate at the King County Prosecutor's Office [S1].
As a lawyer she represented indigent defendants as a public defender and later worked as a private defense attorney [S1].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she says everyone who comes before the court "should be treated with respect, fairness, and impartiality" [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: her stated background spans victim advocacy and criminal defense; the statement does not describe a bail or diversion posture [S1].

## Record

- She was appointed to the King County District Court in 2021 [S1].
- She says she hears criminal, small claims, and protection order cases in Seattle and presided over the Regional Domestic Violence Court for two years [S1].
- She served on the advisory board of The Judicial Institute (2021-2022) and serves on the court's Personnel Committee [S1].
