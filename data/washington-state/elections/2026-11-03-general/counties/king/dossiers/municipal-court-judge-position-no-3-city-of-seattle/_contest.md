## What the office does

Seattle Municipal Court judges hear and decide misdemeanor cases for the City of Seattle [S1]. Judges are elected by voters in the City of Seattle and serve four-year terms; the 2026 salary is $214,791 [S1].

## Race dynamics

Pooja Vaddadi is the only candidate on the ballot and runs unopposed; she submitted no pamphlet statement [S2]. She is a sitting Seattle Municipal Court judge, elected in November 2022 [S3].
