## Background

Pooja Vaddadi submitted no statement to the local voters' pamphlet; the pamphlet prints "No statement submitted." with her name and contact information [S1].
Before joining the court she practiced for seven years as a public defender with the Northwest Defenders Division of the King County Department of Public Defense, in Seattle Municipal and King County Superior Courts [S2].
She also practiced as a public defender in Snohomish County, as a private criminal defense attorney in the Seattle area, as a judicial extern with Justice Alison Tucher of the California First Appellate District, and as corporate in-house counsel at DocSend [S2].
She is an adjunct professor at Seattle University School of Law, where she teaches Legal Writing II [S2].
