## What this office does
One of two House members for LD 43 (central Seattle: Capitol Hill, University District, Wallingford, Fremont, Eastlake and downtown), elected to a two-year term. Representatives vote on state law, budgets and taxes.

## Race dynamics
Two Democrats. Nicole Macri, in office since 2017 and a DESC deputy director, has a housing- and homelessness-centered record: 2025 rent stabilization and sponsoring ESHB 2266 (2026), which requires cities to allow supportive housing and shelters (nicole-macri.md [S8][S10]). Her opponent, Alby Clendennin, a UW resident director and first-time candidate, reports no contributions (alby-clendennin.md [S4]). Macri's pamphlet statement says she is voting no on all three statewide initiatives (nicole-macri.md [S1]). The Stranger endorsed Macri in the primary (nicole-macri.md [S4]).

## Differentiating axes
- **Experience:** nine-year incumbent with an enacted housing record vs. a candidate with no government record.
- **Reform and tech:** Clendennin's stated priorities are reducing corporate influence in politics and regulating AI pricing. Macri has stated no position on either.
- **Social and parental rights:** Macri states positions on abortion access, gender-affirming care and the statewide initiatives (no on I-1 and I-638), and voted for ESHB 1296. Clendennin has stated no policy position on these.
- On taxes, housing and climate only Macri has evidence, so these axes show her positions rather than a contrast.
