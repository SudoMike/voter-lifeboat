## Background
Alby Clendennin (Prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate who lists no elected experience [S1]. He is a Resident Director at the University of Washington, overseeing administrative operations, student leader teams and safety for nearly 10,000 residents, and serves as a hearing officer for student conduct cases [S1]. Education: MA in Student Affairs Administration, Appalachian State University; BA in International Studies, University of West Florida [S1]. He describes himself as "a gay man, the son of a teacher, and a resident director" [S1]. Primary coverage in The Daily (UW) reported that he grew up in northwest Florida and took the UW job in 2024 [S2]. The pamphlet lists only an email contact, with no campaign website [S1].

## Positions
- **Political reform:** "My priorities include reducing corporate influence in politics" [S1].
- **Technology:** "regulating artificial intelligence to prevent unfair pricing" [S1].
- **Public services:** "strengthening public services so every Washingtonian can succeed. Every Washingtonian deserves a great school, a walkable neighborhood, an affordable home, and a government that prioritizes people over profit" [S1].
- Self-describes as "a pragmatic progressive Democrat" and says he "will support bold ideas" [S1].
- Primary coverage listed housing affordability, transit and protecting higher-education and research funding as focuses [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no policy position found [S1][S2].
- **Parents and schools:** no position found [S1][S2].

His general-election statement is the same as his primary statement [S1][S2].
