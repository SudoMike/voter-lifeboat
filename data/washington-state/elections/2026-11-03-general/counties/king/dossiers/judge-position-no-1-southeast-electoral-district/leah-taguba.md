## Background

Taguba lists a JD from Southwestern University School of Law and a BA from Washington State University [S1].
She lists her occupation as King County District Court Judge, Southeast Division [S1].
Her statement says the King County Council appointed her in 2021 and she was elected in 2022; it also says she has served the community since 2022 [S1].
Her statement does not describe her practice before the bench [S1].

## Positions

Her statement says justice balances accountability proportionate to the harm committed, community safety, and rehabilitation [S1].
It says she strongly supports treatment courts or other alternatives to jail where appropriate [S1].

## Record

Her statement says she has presided over thousands of criminal and civil cases at the Auburn Justice Center and Community Court [S1].
She reports serving as a Washington Supreme Court Minority and Justice Commissioner and an Advisory Board member of the Judicial Institute of Washington [S1].
