## What the office does

King County District Court judges hear and decide misdemeanor criminal cases, small claims, traffic cases, and protection orders; the 2026 salary is $226,096, and judges are elected by voters in the electoral district to four-year terms [S2].
This seat is Position 1 in the Southeast Electoral District of King County District Court [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Leah Taguba is the only candidate on the ballot for this position and is unopposed [S3].
Her statement says the King County Council appointed her in 2021, voters elected her in 2022, and she asks voters to reelect her, so she is running as the incumbent [S1].
