## Background

Jorge L. Barón is the incumbent King County Councilmember for District 4, elected in November 2023 for a term running January 2024 to December 2026 [S2]. From 2008 to 2023 he was Executive Director of the Northwest Immigrant Rights Project, after two years as a staff attorney there [S1][S2]. He immigrated from Bogotá, Colombia, at age thirteen, holds a JD from Yale Law School and an economics and history degree from Duke University, and clerked for Ninth Circuit Judge Betty B. Fletcher [S1][S2]. He lives in Queen Anne [S2].

## Positions

His general-election pamphlet statement lists the priorities he says District 4 residents gave him:
- Making criminal and civil legal systems work "effectively and fairly so we can enhance community safety" [S1].
- Stabilizing the homelessness response system to reduce the number of people living unhoused [S1].
- Protecting public health clinics and investing in Harborview Medical Center [S1].
- Improving the safety and reliability of Metro transit [S1].
- Says a second Trump administration is "threatening the safety and well-being of so many, especially our immigrant and transgender neighbors," and that state and local leaders should be "uncompromising in defending and advancing our community's values" while pursuing "progressive policies" [S1].

## Record

- Served on the transition committees of Governor Bob Ferguson and Seattle Mayor Jenny Durkan, the Joint Legislative Task Force on Deadly Force in Community Policing, and Governor Gregoire's New Americans Policy Council [S2].
- Has testified before the U.S. House Judiciary Committee [S2].
