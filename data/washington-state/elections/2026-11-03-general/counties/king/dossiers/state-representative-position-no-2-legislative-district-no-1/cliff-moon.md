## Background
Cliff Moon is a Republican-preference candidate whose only elected experience is as a Precinct Committee Officer in the 1st Legislative District [S1]. He reports sixty-three years in the construction trades and fifty-four years of small-business ownership [S1]; the Everett Herald describes him as owning a construction company for 54 years and living in Lake Forest Park [S5]. He holds a B.S. in Physical Oceanography and an M.S. in Civil Engineering from the University of Washington and has taught entrepreneurship and business classes as a visiting lecturer in Kazakhstan, India and at UW-Bothell [S1]. He finished second in the four-candidate primary, 23.1% on primary night per the Everett Herald, to advance against the incumbent [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** says "Layers of taxes are driving up costs" of gas, groceries and utilities [S1]; supports "meaningful property tax reform, senior exemptions, and caps" for long-term homeowners and lowering the B&O tax burden on small operators [S2]; says government should "resist the impulse to solve every problem by raising taxes on the people who can least afford it" [S2].
- **Spending:** says the state should "spend it wisely, audit programs that aren't delivering results" [S2].
- **Public safety:** supports "fully funding local law enforcement, restoring accountability in the courts, and investing in mental health crisis response as a complement to — not replacement for — police services" [S2]; "I support law enforcement, public safety, and legal immigration" [S1].
- **Housing and local control:** supports "removing barriers to housing production, opposing one-size-fits-all mandates from Olympia, and empowering local communities to shape their own development" [S2].
- **Environment:** supports "practical environmental policies" that protect natural resources "without destroying jobs or making energy unaffordable for working families" [S2].
- **Parents and schools:** "Parents should raise their children. Schools should focus on academics." [S1]; supports "parental involvement, transparency in curriculum, teacher accountability, and funding models that follow students rather than systems" [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found [S1][S2].
