## Background

Nirav Sheth (prefers Republican Party) was born and raised in India and came to the U.S. in 2008 to study computer and electrical engineering [S3][S10]. He served in the U.S. Marines from 2011 to 2014 (honorable discharge) as an aircraft mechanic and military police officer, and volunteered as an intelligence liaison with U.S. Border Patrol in Yuma, Arizona during his enlistment [S1][S3]. He joined the Lakewood Police Department in 2017 and resigned due to partial hearing loss [S1]. He is CFO of Sheth Groups (Meineke franchises, 2017 to present) and co-owned Bistro Baffi restaurant from 2018 to 2025 [S1]. His website says he worked his way from homelessness to owning a business in Pierce County [S3]. He holds an associate degree in electrical engineering and computer science [S1]. He has held no elected office [S1]. He ran for Congress in the 10th District in 2024 [S2][S10].

## Positions

**Public safety**
- Pamphlet: goal of "bringing law & order back to Seattle" [S1]. Ballotpedia survey: "We must make crime illegal in Washington State again" and fund law enforcement and fire departments [S9].
- Campaign site: supports funding to hire more police; keeping county sheriffs elected; letting cities refuse state-funded supportive housing "linked to drug activity or repeat crime"; dedicating cannabis and opioid settlement revenue to local law enforcement and treatment; "mandatory treatment for repeat offenders" under the state's drug-possession law; and "coordination with federal efforts to remove violent illegal offenders" [S4]. Cites I-2113 (police pursuit) approvingly [S4].

**Housing and homelessness**
- Pamphlet: "My goal is to create sustainable & affordable housing to solve homelessness" and "Real Solutions to help homeless people" [S1].

**Taxes, spending and economy**
- Campaign site: describes a "tax tsunami"; criticizes the state gas tax, the Climate Commitment Act (called "a hidden tax"), the sales tax on services, the estate tax, efforts to lift the 1% property-tax cap, the "millionaires tax," and the state's "new income tax" [S6].
- Promises "military-grade accountability" in Washington, D.C. and to "treat your tax dollars with that same sacred respect" [S1]. Wants to audit the Climate Commitment Act's impact on prices [S6].
- Supports subsidizing trade-school and apprenticeship training, and infrastructure investment in transportation and grid resilience [S3][S6].

**Energy and climate**
- Pamphlet: "Energy Independence"; cites "higher cost of living & energy" [S1]. Calls the Climate Commitment Act "a hidden tax" adding to fuel, grocery and utility costs [S6].

**Technology**
- Campaign site: "Restore a business environment that supports technology including AI innovation" [S6].

**Immigration**
- Campaign site: "a balanced policy that upholds the nation's sovereignty and ensures safety by securing the borders, while also recognizing the essential role of immigrants in the U.S. workforce"; proposes streamlining legal immigration [S7]. Ballotpedia survey lists legal immigration and border security among his policy interests [S9]. A Daily UW column (July 2026) reported he had posted content opposing birthright citizenship [S2].

**Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy**
- Campaign site: "Fairness in Sports: Pass the Protecting Fairness in Girls' Sports initiative to preserve opportunity and safety for female athletes" [S5]. "Protecting Fairness in Girls' Sports" is the name the Senate Republican Caucus uses for IL26-638 [S12].
- Campaign site: says federal guidance "defines 'sex' under Title IX as biological and immutable" and that Washington's laws and "OSPI gender-inclusive policies defy federal guidance" [S5].
- No position on abortion found.

**Parents and schools**
- Campaign site: "Parental Exclusion: Current law allows districts to socially or medically transition children in secret. Support the Strengthen Communication Between Parents and Schools initiative"; notes "all Democrat legislators voted to abolish the Parents Bill of Rights in 2025" [S5]. Also proposes refocusing curriculum on reading, writing, math, civics and early literacy and limiting social-emotional learning mandates [S5].
- Ballotpedia survey: "My aim is to restore authority over schools to the very communities that they cater to" [S9].
- Supports opting Washington into the federal scholarship tax credit (HB 2444) [S5].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (IP26-645, income-tax repeal):** His campaign site criticizes the state's "new income tax" and "millionaires tax" [S6]. It does not name I-645.
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** His site supports a "Strengthen Communication Between Parents and Schools initiative" and criticizes the 2025 repeal of parts of the Parents' Bill of Rights [S5]. IL26-001 would re-enact that 2024 law; the site does not use the IL26-001 number, so the match is by subject only.
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' sports):** Supports "the Protecting Fairness in Girls' Sports initiative" [S5], the name used for IL26-638 by its legislative supporters [S12].
