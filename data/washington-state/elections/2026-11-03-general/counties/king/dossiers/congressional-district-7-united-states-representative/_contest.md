## What the office does

A U.S. Representative is one of 435 voting members of the House. Representatives write and vote on federal law (taxes, healthcare, immigration, defense), control federal spending through appropriations, oversee the executive branch through committees, and provide casework with federal agencies. Terms are two years with no term limit. The 7th District covers most of Seattle plus parts of Burien, White Center, Shoreline and Vashon Island [S2].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Pramila Jayapal (D), in the House since 2017, faces Republican Nirav Sheth, a Marine veteran, former Lakewood police officer and restaurant/franchise owner who ran in the 10th District in 2024 [S1]. In the August primary Jayapal took 84.36% and Sheth 9.82% (Secretary of State count as of Aug. 17) [S3]. Through the pre-primary reports Jayapal had raised about $2.5 million and Sheth about $11,800 [S4][S5].

## Issue axes that genuinely differentiate these candidates

1. **Taxes.** Jayapal sponsors a federal wealth tax and opposes I-645; Sheth's campaign site opposes Washington's new income tax, the "millionaires tax," the Climate Commitment Act and other state taxes.
2. **Public safety.** Sheth runs on "law & order," police hiring and mandatory treatment for repeat drug offenders; Jayapal's record (Housing not Handcuffs Act, ICE limits) points toward services and constraints on enforcement.
3. **Immigration.** Jayapal (Immigration Subcommittee ranking member) seeks to constrain ICE and CBP; Sheth, an immigrant himself, pairs legal-immigration streamlining with border security and cooperation on removals.
4. **Social policy.** Jayapal co-chairs the Trans Equality Task Force and supports abortion rights; Sheth backs the girls' sports initiative (IL26-638) and a biological definition of sex under Title IX.
5. **Experience.** Ten-year incumbent with caucus leadership versus a first-time officeholder candidate.
6. **Climate and tech.** Jayapal proposes a federal AI charter regime; Sheth favors "AI innovation" and calls the Climate Commitment Act a hidden tax.

Parents-and-schools positions (Sheth supports a parental-notification initiative) are recorded in the dossiers but the `parental-rights` axis does not apply to federal contests.
