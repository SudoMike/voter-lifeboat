## Background
Ashley Fedan (Prefers Democratic Party) is a registered nurse of more than 25 years and a Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist who owns a small business "providing anesthesia care, including gender affirming care, abortion, and reproductive care" [S1]. She is a Navy veteran who served as a Lieutenant Commander in the Nurse Corps [S1][S2]. Her elected experience is party office: PCO in the 11th LD, 2024 Democratic National Committee delegate, and former chair of the 28th LD Democrats [S1][S10]. She holds a BSN from Case Western Reserve, an MS from York College of Pennsylvania and an EdD from Creighton [S1]. Community service includes membership in the Pro-Choice Washington PAC and the APRNs of Washington State board [S1]. In the August 4 primary she received 8,298 votes (31.95%) to David Hackney's 10,998 (42.35%) [S6].

## Positions
- **Healthcare:** "I believe healthcare is a human right, not a privilege"; will push for "transparency and accountability from insurance companies and hospital systems" and for training enough nurses and healthcare workers [S3]. Notes that no nurses serve in the Legislature [S1].
- **Housing:** would "cut through bureaucratic delays that slow down affordable housing projects" and push for stronger state partnerships with nonprofits that build affordable housing [S3].
- **Working families:** wages "that actually keep up with the real cost of living" and support for workers seeking fair treatment and safe conditions [S3].
- **Federal government:** campaign homepage cites basic human dignity "stripped away by our federal government" [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** her practice provides gender-affirming care and abortion care, and she is a Pro-Choice Washington PAC member [S1]; endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S7].
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found.
