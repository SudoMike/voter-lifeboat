## What the office does
One of two state representatives for the 11th Legislative District (Renton, Tukwila, parts of Kent and south Seattle) in the Washington State House, elected to a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including school funding, the tax structure, housing rules, healthcare programs and criminal-justice statutes.

## District and race dynamics
A Democrat-vs-Democrat general election. Incumbent David Hackney (in office since 2021) led the August primary with 42.4%, Ashley Fedan (nurse anesthetist, Navy veteran) took 32.0%, and Republican Christian Rombough was eliminated with 25.6% (see david-hackney.md S9). The King County Republican Party makes no recommendation in this race (https://kcgop.org/votersguide/, accessed 2026-10-08). King County Democrats and the Fuse Progressive Voters Guide list both candidates; Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates endorses Fedan; the governor, the State Labor Council, SEIU 775 and The Seattle Times are with Hackney (david-hackney.md S3, S10, S11, S12; ashley-fedan.md S7). Hackney has raised $207k to Fedan's $90k (david-hackney.md S8, ashley-fedan.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** the main contrast. Hackney runs on his legislative record (energy-performance, affordable-housing tax exemption, committee leadership); Fedan runs on frontline healthcare experience and the absence of nurses in the Legislature (david-hackney.md S2; ashley-fedan.md S1).
2. **Reproductive and gender policy:** Fedan's practice provides abortion and gender-affirming care and she is endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates; Hackney has no stated position on these topics (ashley-fedan.md S1, S7).
3. **Taxes and parental rights:** Hackney's votes for ESSB 6346 and ESHB 1296 give him a record on the two laws that I-645 and I-1 target; Fedan has no stated position (david-hackney.md S4, S5).
4. **Healthcare:** both emphasize cost; Fedan frames healthcare as "a human right" with insurer accountability (ashley-fedan.md S3).
