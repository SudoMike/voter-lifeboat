## Background
David Hackney (Prefers Democratic Party) has represented the 11th Legislative District, Position 1, since 2021 [S1]. He is a former Assistant U.S. Attorney, former UN war crimes prosecutor, former senior counsel at The Nature Conservancy and former consultant to the Seattle City Attorney's Office; he holds a BS from Cornell, an MPA from Harvard and a JD from Harvard Law School [S1]. He is Vice Chair of the House Consumer Protection & Business Committee [S14] and has chaired the Legislative Black Caucus [S15]. In the August 4 primary (King County count; the district lies in King County) he received 10,998 votes (42.35%) to Ashley Fedan's 8,298 (31.95%) and Republican Christian Rombough's 6,639 (25.57%) [S9].

## Positions
- **Cost of living:** "My singular focus is finding direct ways to lower costs for everyday people, from housing to healthcare" [S1][S2].
- **Housing:** says mechanisms for funding affordable housing are necessary; cites his passed bill creating a real estate excise tax exemption for sales of property designated as affordable housing, and support for transit-friendly and multifamily building [S2].
- **Climate and environment:** cites his passed bill requiring building owners to meet green energy performance standards, co-chairs the bipartisan Hydrogen Caucus, and says the state must do "everything we can to combat the climate crisis" [S2].
- **Public safety:** as a former federal prosecutor lists "accessible mental health resources, punishment with a focus on rehabilitation, better resources for jails and prisons, and smart data driven policies that enable law enforcement"; says crime can be reduced "without over policing of marginalized communities" by addressing root causes and funding youth mentoring groups such as Choose180 and Community Passageways [S2].
- **Guns:** has pursued legislation such as banning high-capacity magazines [S2].
- **Healthcare:** wants to lower healthcare costs and grow the healthcare workforce [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found. His endorsement list includes the Victory Fund [S3].
- **Parents and schools:** no campaign statement found; see his ESHB 1296 vote under Record.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1M):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: he voted yea on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S4]. This is a vote, not a stated initiative position.
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: yea on final passage of ESHB 1296 as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24; excused on the earlier House vote), the law I-1 would undo [S5][S6].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- ESSB 6346 (2026, 9.9% tax on income over $1 million): yea on House final passage, 2026-03-09, 51-46 [S4].
- ESHB 1296 (2025): excused on the 2025-03-12 House vote; yea on final passage as amended by the Senate, 2025-04-24 [S5].
- Prime sponsor of HB 1149 (2025), against organized animal fighting; passed 96-0 and 47-1 [S13].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S7].
- PDC (as of the 2026-10-06 update): $207,078.34 raised and $118,288.06 spent for 2026 [S8].
