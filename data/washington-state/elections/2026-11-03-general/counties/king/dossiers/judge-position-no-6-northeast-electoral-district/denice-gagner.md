All content below is self-reported in the candidate's King County local voters' pamphlet statement and was not independently verified [S1].

## Background

Gagner lists a Juris Doctor (2007) and a BA (2000) from the University of Washington [S1].
She lists her occupation as Judge Pro Tem for King County District Court and various municipal courts [S1].
Her statement reports 20 years of legal experience as a victim advocate, a prosecutor, a criminal defense attorney, and a judge pro tem [S1].
She states she serves as a Planning Commissioner for the City of Redmond and received the Golden Acorn Award in 2022 for volunteering at her children's public school [S1].

## Positions

Her statement says that as a prosecutor she held offenders accountable while advancing solutions that upheld public safety [S1].
It says that as a defense attorney she represented clients facing addiction and mental health issues [S1].

## Record

Her statement reports ratings of "Exceptionally Well Qualified" from all 7 independent bar associations; this is self-reported and was not independently checked for this entry [S1].

## Endorsements

Her statement lists 50+ sitting judges, 100+ attorneys, and 30+ local leaders, without naming them [S1].
Organizational endorsements listed are the Machinists Union (IAM 751), the King County Democrats, all five Eastside LD Democrats, and the National Women's Political Caucus of Washington [S1].
