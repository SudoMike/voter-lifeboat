## Background
Claire Wilson (prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Senator for the 30th Legislative District and served on the Federal Way School Board from 2011 to 2019 [S1]. She describes 35 years as an educator and administrator in early learning, family engagement, and governance, plus work as a WIC project director and small-business owner [S1]. She holds a BS in food and nutrition / child and family studies from Washington State University and an MA in adult education from the University of Northern Colorado [S1]. Her campaign site describes her as vice chair of the Senate Early Learning and K-12 Committee [S3]. She describes herself as a lesbian career education professional [S5]. In the August primary she received 62.12% of the vote to Republican Michael Rutland's 37.82%, per final results reported by the Federal Way Mirror [S18].

## Positions
- **Children, families, education:** says she leads Senate efforts to expand services for young people and passed investments in affordable childcare and early learning [S1]; says education "is still critically underfunded" and cites passing the Fair Start for Kids Act, which she says funds childcare with a capital gains tax [S3].
- **Taxes:** says she worked to reduce the tax burden on workers and small businesses while securing K-12 funding [S1]; her site says she "will work on and support legislation that holds large corporations and millionaires accountable, and demands they pay their fair share" to fully fund education [S3].
- **Housing:** says she delivered resources for affordable housing for seniors, low-income families, working people, and people leaving homelessness [S1].
- **Public safety:** says she voted for pay raises and additional training for law enforcement officers and for drug treatment and recovery funding [S1].
- **Climate and environment:** describes the transition to a "greener and cleaner economy" as a job-creation opportunity and supports transit funding to reduce air pollution [S7].
- **Health care:** says she is working to guarantee every family in the region access to affordable coverage and to lower premiums and prescription costs [S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** her site describes the Supreme Court's abortion ruling as an "inhumane attack" and says she is fighting it [S4]; says she sponsored the Washington State LGBTQ Commission and the designation of June as LGBTQ pride month [S5].
- **Parents and schools:** her site states her Senate focus has included "empowering the voice of parents in their children's schools" [S2]; she describes "the Wilson bill for comprehensive sexuality health education," with curricula selected by the local school district [S5].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1M):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: she voted yea on Senate final passage of ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S8].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: she voted yea on ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments to the I-2081 parental-rights list that I-1 would undo [S9][S10].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- Voted yea on ESSB 6346 (9.9% tax on income over $1 million) at Senate third reading (2026-02-16) and on final passage as amended by the House (2026-03-11) [S8].
- Voted yea on ESHB 1296 in the Senate (2025-04-11) [S9]; the bill amended the parental and guardian rights enacted by Initiative 2081 [S10].
- Original co-sponsor of SB 5599 (2023) and voted yea on its final passage (2023-04-19) [S11][S12]; the enacted bill lets licensed youth shelters treat a minor's seeking or receiving protected health care (gender-affirming or reproductive care) as a compelling reason not to contact parents [S12].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since the 2026 regular session adjourned sine die on 2026-03-12 [S13].
- Campaign finance (PDC, data updated 2026-10-05): $109,709.03 raised and $79,150.93 spent for 2026 [S14].
