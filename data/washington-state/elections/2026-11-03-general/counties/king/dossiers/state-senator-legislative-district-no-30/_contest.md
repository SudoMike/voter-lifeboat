## What the office does
The 30th Legislative District elects one State Senator to a four-year term in the 49-member Washington State Senate. Senators write and vote on state law and the operating, capital, and transportation budgets, and confirm gubernatorial appointments. The district covers Federal Way, Auburn, Algona, Pacific, and parts of Milton and Des Moines in south King County (see michael-rutland.md S3).

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates: incumbent Democrat Claire Wilson, a former Federal Way School Board member, and Republican first-time candidate Michael Rutland, a retired Air Force senior master sergeant and former Boeing manager (see claire-wilson.md S1; michael-rutland.md S1). Both also appeared on the primary ballot, which had only these two candidates.

Wilson runs on an early-learning, childcare, and K-12 funding record and carries a long labor, education, and reproductive-rights endorsement list, including WEA-PAC, SEIU 775 and Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates (claire-wilson.md S6, S15-S17). Rutland runs on cost of living, opposing new income taxes, parent involvement in schools, and local control, and is endorsed by the King County Republican Party (michael-rutland.md S1, S3, S6). Wilson has raised about $110k against Rutland's about $15k (PDC; claire-wilson.md S14, michael-rutland.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Wilson voted for ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on income over $1 million that I-645 would repeal, and says millionaires should pay their fair share; Rutland says he will oppose new income taxes.
2. **Parental rights:** Wilson voted for ESHB 1296 (the 2025 amendments I-1 would undo) and sponsored comprehensive sexuality education; Rutland emphasizes parent voice and keeping "political agendas" out of schools.
3. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Wilson co-sponsored SB 5599 (2023) and runs on reproductive and LGBTQ+ rights; Rutland states no position.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** a sitting senator and former school board director vs. a first-time candidate.
5. **Local control and spending:** Rutland calls for fewer state mandates and for budgeting instead of new taxes; Wilson emphasizes expanding investment in children and schools.
