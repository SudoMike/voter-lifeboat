## Background
Michael Rutland (prefers Republican Party) is a first-time candidate from Federal Way [S1][S2]. He is a retired U.S. Air Force Senior Master Sergeant with 24 years of service, including Desert Shield and Desert Storm, and a former Boeing leader with four decades of management experience [S1]. He holds a BS in management (magna cum laude) from the University of Phoenix [S1]. He is a 41-year Knights of Columbus member and volunteers with Coats for Kids and Washington Special Olympics in Federal Way [S1]. In the August primary he received 37.82% of the vote to incumbent Claire Wilson's 62.12%, per final results reported by the Federal Way Mirror [S7].

## Positions
- **Taxes and affordability:** says he will "oppose new income taxes," repeal policies "driving up fuel and grocery prices," and "demand responsible budgeting instead of higher taxes on working people" [S1]; his issues page says state government "should live within its means" [S3].
- **Public safety:** supports law enforcement, addressing crime and repeat offenses, and "investing in prevention and intervention programs" [S3].
- **Local control:** says local leaders should have "a stronger voice in decision-making," listing "protecting local decision-making" and "reducing one-size-fits-all mandates" [S3].
- **Housing:** lists "supporting affordable housing solutions" without naming a mechanism [S3].
- **Parents and schools:** says "every parent deserves a voice" and supports parental involvement, high academic standards, safe schools, and career and technical education; says schools should focus on student success, "not political agendas coming from Olympia" [S1]; site lists "empowered parents" and "parental engagement" [S2][S3].
