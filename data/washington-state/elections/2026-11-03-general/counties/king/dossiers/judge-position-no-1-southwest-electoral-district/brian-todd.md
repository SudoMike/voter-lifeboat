## Background

Brian Todd lists a B.A. in Spanish from Arizona State University, a B.A. in Child and Family Studies, and a J.D. from the University of Wyoming [S1].
He lists his occupation as Judge, King County District Court, and former Administrative Law Judge [S1].
Before joining the bench he represented clients with criminal charges, family law concerns, protection orders, and mediations, according to his statement [S1].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: he describes his philosophy as treating people fairly "regardless of where they come from and regardless of their circumstances" [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: his statement describes representing clients facing criminal charges; it does not describe prosecution work or a bail or diversion posture [S1].

## Record

- He states he was appointed to the King County District Court by unanimous vote of the King County Council; the statement does not give the appointment date [S1].

## Endorsements

- His statement says he has the support of justices on the Washington Supreme Court and judges on the Court of Appeals, Superior Court, District Court, and Municipal Courts, without naming them [S1].
