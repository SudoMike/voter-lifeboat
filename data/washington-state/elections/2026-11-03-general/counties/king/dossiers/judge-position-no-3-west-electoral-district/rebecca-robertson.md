## Background

Rebecca Robertson lists a B.A. in Political Science from the University of Washington (1996) and a Juris Doctorate from Seattle University Law School (2000) [S1].
Before joining the judiciary she was a prosecuting attorney and a judge pro tem in a variety of courts [S1].
She chaired the Washington State Bar Association's Criminal Law Section and has published and taught courses on domestic violence and DUI issues [S1].

## Positions

- Criminal-justice formation: her stated pre-bench background is in prosecution; the statement does not describe a bail or diversion posture [S1].
- Court administration: she says she teaches court budgeting, judicial independence, and law and procedure to judges, lawyers, executive and legislative branch leaders, and students [S1].

## Record

- She has been a judge since 2010 and previously served as a municipal court judge in Federal Way [S1].
- The King County Council unanimously appointed her to the District Court in 2021, and she was elected in 2022 [S1].
- She is the King County District Court's Chief Presiding Judge [S1].
- She serves on the statewide Board for Judicial Administration, is a past President of the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association, formerly chaired the BJA Policy and Planning Committee, co-chaired the Trial Court Security Task Force, and serves on the DMCJA's Council on Independent Courts [S1].
