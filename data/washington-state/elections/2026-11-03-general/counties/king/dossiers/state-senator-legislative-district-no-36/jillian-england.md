## Background
Jillian England (Prefers Republican Party) has been an elected Precinct Committee Officer since 2007 and is Vice Chair of the 36th Legislative District Republican Party [S1]. Her career is in computer science and health-care business operations; she holds a BS in Computer Science and Business from the University of Puget Sound and has been an active member of a Missouri Synod Lutheran church since 2006 [S1]. She took 12.1% of the King County primary vote (5,876) to Noel Frame's 87.7% [S5].

## Positions

### Taxes and spending
- Repealing the 9.9% "Millionaires Tax," which she says "opens the door to broader income taxation on working families," is her "top priority"; she also targets the sales tax and the B&O tax [S1].
- Site: "Repeal the New State Income Tax ... They promised it would only hit millionaires. History says it won't stay there." [S3]
- Calls for cutting "fraud and wasteful spending" and says "government force cannot create prosperity, safety, or abundance" [S2][S3].

### Climate and energy
- Would repeal the Climate Commitment Act, which she says adds 50-65 cents per gallon to gas; supports an "all-of-the-above" strategy of accelerating nuclear, expanding natural gas and preserving hydro and baseload power [S1][S3].

### Data centers and technology
- Calls Seattle's data-center moratorium "Luddite symbolism" and says the region should capture AI infrastructure growth rather than export it [S3].

### Homelessness and public safety
- Calls for practical solutions that "clear encampments" and "restore public safety"; frames homelessness as an "addiction + open tolerance + migration inflow" feedback loop and calls for funding "exit" programs rather than housing alone [S1][S3].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- In a July 29, 2026 campaign post she writes that sex is "binary and defined by reproductive biology" and that "there is no medical category called 'trans'," and calls gender-affirming interventions for minors "elective and irreversible interventions that sterilize and disrupt healthy sexual development" [S4].
- No statement on abortion found [S3][S4].

### Parents and schools
- Pamphlet: schools should "educate students rather than indoctrinate them" [S1].
- In the same July 29 post she says she will "oppose any expansion of Seattle or state policy ... that weakens parental rights over minor children" and that parents who refuse gender-affirming interventions "are exercising ordinary parental authority" [S4].
- An August 14 post criticizes ESSB 5599 (2023), which lets licensed youth shelters notify the Department of Children, Youth, and Families instead of parents in some cases, saying more shelter capacity "means more situations in which parents are delayed or sidelined" [S4].
- A June 21 post supports "the fundamental right of parents to raise their own children" [S4].
