# State Senator, Legislative District No. 36 — contest overview

- depth: light
- derived_from: data/washington-state/elections/2026-08-04-primary/counties/king/dossiers/state-senator-legislative-district-no-36/_contest.md, candidate dossiers in this directory
- General election: incumbent Noel C. Frame (Prefers Democratic Party) vs. Jillian England (Prefers Republican Party).

State senators serve four-year terms writing state law and the state budget. The 36th LD lies entirely in King County, covering northwest Seattle: Ballard, Magnolia, Queen Anne and the Greenwood area. In the August primary Frame took 87.7% to England's 12.1% (King County final results).

This is the clearest partisan contrast among King's legislative races. Frame co-sponsored and voted for the 2026 millionaires' tax (ESSB 6346) and has been the Legislature's lead wealth-tax sponsor. England's top priority is repealing that tax, which is the I-645 question. Climate policy is also contested: England would repeal the Climate Commitment Act, and Frame is endorsed by Washington Conservation Action but states no specific climate positions. On homelessness, England calls for clearing encampments. In the general campaign England has also written explicitly on gender policy and parental authority. Frame states no positions on those topics, but she voted for ESHB 1296 (2025), the parental-rights amendments I-1 would undo. Differentiating axes: taxes, spending, climate, experience, and, from England's side and Frame's vote, parental-rights.
