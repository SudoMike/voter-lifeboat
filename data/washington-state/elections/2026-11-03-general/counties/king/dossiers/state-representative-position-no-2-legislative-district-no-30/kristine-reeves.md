## Background
Kristine Reeves (prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent, serving as State Representative for the 30th District in 2017-2019 and from 2023 to the present [S1]. She chairs the House Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee and founded the bipartisan Veterans & Military Families Caucus [S1]. She is a Federal Way small-business owner in organizational development consulting, a former Director of Economic Development for the military and defense sector at the state Department of Commerce, former Executive Director of the Washington Military Alliance, and former statewide veterans coordinator for U.S. Senator Patty Murray [S1]. BA, Washington State University; MA, Gonzaga University [S1]. Her issues page says she was raised in poverty and foster homes and was homeless for a time [S2]. In the August primary she received 63.72% of the vote to independent Paul McDaniel's 35.83%, per final results reported by the Federal Way Mirror [S12].

## Positions
Her issues page is undated and refers to COVID-19 reopening, so it may predate this campaign [S2].
- **Economy and taxes:** cites helping pass paid family and medical leave and a college-tuition reduction "paid for by big companies like Amazon and Microsoft" [S2].
- **Public safety:** says she invested $100 million in local public safety [S1]; supports expanding "community policing and community mental health officer programs" and giving first responders "the tools and resources to do their jobs" [S2].
- **Health care:** says "health care is a right for every person, not a privilege" and supports the state public option to "force insurance companies to compete" [S2].
- **Food, farms, wildfire:** cites laws expanding senior access to nutritious food, mitigating wildfire risk, and protecting homeless students [S1].

## Record
- Voted nay on ESSB 6346 (9.9% tax on income over $1 million), House final passage 2026-03-09 (bill passed 51-46) [S3].
- Voted yea on ESHB 1296 at House passage (2025-03-12) and final passage (2025-04-24) [S4]; the bill amended the Initiative 2081 parental-rights list [S5].
- Voted yea on ESSB 5599 (2023), which lets youth shelters treat a minor's seeking gender-affirming or reproductive care as a compelling reason not to notify parents [S6][S7].
