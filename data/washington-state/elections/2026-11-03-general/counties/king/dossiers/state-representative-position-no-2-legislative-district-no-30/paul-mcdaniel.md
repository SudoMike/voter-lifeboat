## Background
Paul McDaniel (prefers Independent Party) is a former Federal Way City Councilmember who was appointed by the council to Position 2 [S1][S8]. He ran for that seat in the August 2025 primary and did not advance to that general election [S9]. He owns 7th Power, Inc., a technology consulting firm, and is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran of the Iraq War [S1]. He attended Federal Way High School and Highline College, volunteered at St. Francis Hospital for nine years, coached middle-school wrestling, and teaches Jiu-Jitsu [S1]. His campaign site posts summaries of public comments he gave at Federal Way City Council meetings in 2026 [S2]. In the August primary he received 35.83% of the vote to incumbent Kristine Reeves's 63.72%, per final results reported by the Federal Way Mirror [S12].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** says he will fight for "reducing taxes" [S1]; his fiscal page says "I will oppose efforts to create a state personal income tax for anyone," supports eliminating the sales tax on services, reducing "sin taxes" where appropriate, and reviewing B&O and property taxes [S3].
- **Spending:** says he will "eliminate wasteful spending" and that responsible government is about "spending smarter," not spending less for its own sake [S3].
- **Public safety:** describes his approach as "not about being tougher... not about being softer... about being smarter"; supports recruiting and retaining officers alongside expanded behavioral-health, drug-treatment, and crisis-intervention services [S4].
- **Housing:** supports increased housing supply, middle housing, accessory dwelling units, and streamlined permitting [S5][S7].
- **Parents and schools:** says parents "deserve meaningful involvement" in their children's education and supports "open communication between schools and families" and "transparency regarding academic progress" [S6]; supports "increase[d] educational choice" [S7].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** his pamphlet statement says he will "protect girls' sports" [S1].
