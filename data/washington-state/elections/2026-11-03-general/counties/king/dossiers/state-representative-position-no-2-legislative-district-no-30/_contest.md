## What the office does
One of two State Representatives for the 30th Legislative District (Federal Way and nearby south King County communities), elected to a two-year term in the 98-member House. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital, and transportation budgets.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates: incumbent Democrat Kristine Reeves (in the House 2017-2019 and since 2023, chair of the House Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee) and Paul McDaniel (prefers Independent Party), a former appointed Federal Way City Councilmember, Marine Corps veteran and technology consultant (see kristine-reeves.md S1; paul-mcdaniel.md S1, S8). They were also the only two candidates on the primary ballot.

Reeves is a Democrat who voted against ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on income over $1 million, at House final passage (kristine-reeves.md S3). McDaniel opposes any state personal income tax, pledges to "protect girls' sports," and is listed as "BEST CHOICE" by the King County Republican Party (paul-mcdaniel.md S1, S3, S11). Reeves has raised about $194k against McDaniel's about $5.6k (PDC; kristine-reeves.md S9, paul-mcdaniel.md S10).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a multi-term incumbent committee chair against a former appointed city councilmember.
2. **Taxes:** less of a split than the party labels suggest, since both oppose the 2026 income tax: Reeves through her vote, McDaniel through his platform. McDaniel adds broader tax cuts, and Reeves cites business-funded college aid.
3. **Gender and sports policy:** McDaniel pledges to protect girls' sports; Reeves voted for ESSB 5599 (2023).
4. **Parental rights:** Reeves voted for ESHB 1296; McDaniel emphasizes parent involvement, transparency and educational choice.
5. **Public safety:** both pair police support with behavioral-health services: Reeves through community policing and mental-health officers, McDaniel through "smarter" enforcement plus treatment.
