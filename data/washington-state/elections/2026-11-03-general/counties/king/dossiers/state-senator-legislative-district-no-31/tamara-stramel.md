## Background
Tamara Stramel (prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate; she has been a Democratic precinct committee officer since 2017 and is in her eighth year on the board of the 31st LD Democrats [S1]. She was a field organizer for the 2024 Sumner-Bonney Lake school bond, serves on its advisory committee and as an executive PTA board member, and previously worked as a program manager in residential treatment facilities [S1]. BA in psychology with a minor in religious studies, California State University, Bakersfield [S1]. She describes herself as a mom, organizer, and veteran spouse who has lived near Lake Tapps for eight years [S1]. In the August primary she had 46.02% to incumbent Phil Fortunato's 53.86% in the election-night results from King County Elections reported by the Auburn Reporter [S8].

## Positions
- **Cost of living:** says she will support laws "to prohibit surveillance and surge pricing that take advantage of customers" and will focus on the shortage of affordable homes for smaller households [S3].
- **Infrastructure and transportation:** wants the district prioritized in the state transportation budget, funding for the Fairfax Bridge "with good union wages," an inventory of critical routes, and flood-mitigation funding [S1][S3].
- **Education:** says schools are "asked to do more with less"; will work to change "our broken funding model" because "unfunded regulations are hurting our local schools," and to expand free school meals to all schools [S1][S3].
- **Governance:** pledges to represent "the whole 31st district" with "honesty, compassion, and determination" [S1].
