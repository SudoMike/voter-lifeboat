## What the office does
The 31st Legislative District elects one State Senator to a four-year term in the 49-member Senate. Senators write and vote on state law and budgets and confirm gubernatorial appointments. Per the primary overview's Ballotpedia citation, LD31 spans southeast King County and northeast Pierce County (Auburn, Bonney Lake, Sumner, Buckley and Orting areas), so Pierce County voters also vote in this contest.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates: incumbent Republican Phil Fortunato (senator since 2017, earlier a House member) and Democratic first-time candidate Tamara Stramel, a PTA and school-bond organizer from near Lake Tapps (see phil-fortunato.md S1; tamara-stramel.md S1). They were also the only two candidates on the primary ballot.

Fortunato runs on spending restraint, Second Amendment rights, and "defending parental rights," and is endorsed by the Association of Washington Business and the King County Republican Party (phil-fortunato.md S1, S2, S12, S13). Stramel runs on infrastructure and transportation-budget priority, school funding and free school meals, and consumer-pricing regulation, with Democratic, labor, WEA-PAC and Planned Parenthood endorsements (tamara-stramel.md S3, S4, S6, S7). Fortunato has raised about $78k against Stramel's about $46k (PDC; phil-fortunato.md S11, tamara-stramel.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Spending and taxes:** Fortunato proposes zero-based budgeting and voted against ESSB 6346; Stramel says schools are underfunded and wants free school meals expanded, with no stated tax position.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** about a decade in the Senate plus earlier House terms, against a first-time candidate.
3. **Parental rights and social policy:** Fortunato voted against ESHB 1296 and ESSB 5599 and cites parental rights; Stramel states no position, though she is endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates.
4. **Housing and homelessness:** Fortunato proposes housing deregulation and moving people off sidewalks into treatment; Stramel names the shortage of smaller affordable homes without a mechanism.
5. **Consumer regulation:** Stramel would prohibit surveillance and surge pricing.
