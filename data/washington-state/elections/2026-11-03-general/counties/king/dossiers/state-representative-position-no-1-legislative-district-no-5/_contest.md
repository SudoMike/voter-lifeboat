## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members elected from each legislative district to two-year terms. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, which set K-12 funding, the tax structure (including the 2026 tax on income over $1 million), housing and land-use rules for cities, behavioral-health funding, transportation projects such as SR 18, and criminal-justice statutes. Because the incumbent was appointed, this seat is on the November 3, 2026 ballot as a short term and a full two-year term (see zach-hall.md S1).

## District and race dynamics
Legislative District 5 is an eastern and southeastern King County district (Issaquah, Snoqualmie, Maple Valley, Black Diamond, Enumclaw and nearby areas). Appointed Democratic incumbent Zach Hall faces Republican Michelle Bennett, Mercer Island's police chief. The August primary was close: in the King County count Hall had 44.5% and Bennett 43.0%, with a progressive Democrat and a no-party candidate splitting the rest (see zach-hall.md S7). Hall has raised about four times as much as Bennett (see zach-hall.md S6, michelle-bennett.md S6). Both took part in an October 5 SnoValley Chamber forum focused on SR 18, the B&O tax and housing under the Growth Management Act (see zach-hall.md S8).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Hall voted for the 2026 tax on income over $1 million; Bennett campaigns against "the largest tax increase in state history" and the new income tax (see zach-hall.md S4, michelle-bennett.md S1, S3).
2. **Public safety:** Bennett, a police chief, says police lack "clear tools" and wants to make drugs illegal again with pretrial diversion; Hall frames safety around first responders, gun-violence prevention and behavioral-health interventions (michelle-bennett.md S1, S3; zach-hall.md S2).
3. **Record vs. renewal:** a one-session appointed legislator against a first-time candidate with a long law-enforcement executive career.
4. **Local control:** Bennett says state housing and zoning mandates do not work for every community (michelle-bennett.md S2); Hall has no comparable statement.
5. **Climate:** Hall commits to emissions reductions and a faster transition from fossil fuels (zach-hall.md S2); Bennett has no stated climate position.
