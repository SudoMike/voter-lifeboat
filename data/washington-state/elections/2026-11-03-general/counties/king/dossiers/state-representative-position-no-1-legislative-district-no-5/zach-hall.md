## Background
Zach Hall (Prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Representative for Legislative District 5, Position 1; the seat appears on the general ballot as a "short & 2-year term" [S1]. The King County Council appointed him in June 2025 to fill the House seat vacated when Victoria Hunt moved to the Senate [S12]. He is Vice Chair of the House Environment & Energy Committee and a member of the Local Government and Transportation committees, a former Issaquah City Councilmember, a former legislative assistant in the House, and former chair of the Eastside Fire & Rescue Board; he holds an MPA from the UW Evans School [S1]. In the King County count of the August 4 primary he received 18,294 votes (44.53%) to Michelle Bennett's 17,681 (43.04%) [S7].

## Positions
- **Taxes and cost of living:** says essentials such as groceries, housing, healthcare and childcare must stay affordable while protecting "the essential services our communities rely on" [S2]. At an October 5, 2026 forum he said he voted to double the small business tax credit this year and wants it to keep growing [S8].
- **Housing:** says supply is low and calls for "more affordable housing and reforms that make it easier to finance and build new homes faster" [S2]; pamphlet cites support for "reforms to fix our housing crisis" [S1].
- **Transportation:** prioritizes finishing State Route 18 and near-term safety and congestion improvements; said the Legislature secured $1 million for SR 18 safety work and that he is in "protecting mode" for its funding [S2][S8]. Also supports transit access, bike and pedestrian grants [S2].
- **Public safety:** describes safety as equipping first responders, wildfire and flood response, "preventing gun violence," and "investing in behavioral health and substance use interventions that meet people where they are" [S2].
- **Climate and energy:** commits to "reducing greenhouse gas emissions by transitioning to cleaner, more renewable energy resources" and to grid and transmission investment so the state can "transition away from fossil fuels faster" [S2].
- **Education:** fully fund schools [S1][S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found; endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates and the WA State Stonewall Democrats [S3][S10].
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1M):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: he voted yea on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S4]. This is a vote, not a stated initiative position.
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. He was not a member when the House voted on ESHB 1296 (2025) [S4][S12].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- Voted yea on House final passage of ESSB 6346 (9.9% tax on income over $1 million), 2026-03-09, passed 51-46 [S4].
- Campaign cites sponsoring and passing bipartisan bills to lower energy costs in his first session [S1][S2]; the primary dossier recorded these as HB 2296 (on-bill financing of residential energy-efficiency upgrades) and HB 2575 (environmental reporting streamlining) [S13].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S5].
- PDC (as of the 2026-10-05 update): $130,914.26 raised and $61,958.53 spent for 2026 [S6].
