## Background
Michelle Bennett (Prefers Republican Party) is Mercer Island's police chief; her pamphlet lists chief posts in Maple Valley, Sammamish and Mercer Island, service as a Planning and Operations Major and North Bend deputy in the King County Sheriff's Office, and presidencies of the King County Police Chiefs/Sheriffs and the WA State FBI National Academy Associates [S1]. Mercer Island's city manager appointed her permanent chief after an interim period [S5]. She holds a Doctorate in Education from Seattle Pacific University and an M.S. and B.A. from Central Washington University, and co-created a "Take a Stand Against Bullying" curriculum [S1]. She has not held legislative office [S1]. In the King County count of the August 4 primary she received 17,681 votes (43.04%) to Zach Hall's 18,294 (44.53%) [S7].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** says "Olympia recently voted for the largest tax increase in state history" and pledges to "ensure state spending delivers measurable results" [S1]. Primary-season coverage reported that she opposes an income tax [S4]. Seattle Red reported she named the new income tax, a services tax and the use of the law-enforcement/firefighter pension fund among her reasons for running [S3].
- **Public safety:** says "police have also been left without clear tools to protect victims and neighborhoods" [S1]. In a Seattle Red interview she said "we need to make drugs illegal again" while pairing it with pretrial diversion, and called herself an "extremely moderate candidate" [S3].
- **Local control and housing:** at the October 5, 2026 North Bend forum she said that for Olympia to dictate a city's housing zoning or affordable housing "simply doesn't work for every community" [S2].
- **Education:** pledges "stronger schools," citing her education doctorate [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found.
