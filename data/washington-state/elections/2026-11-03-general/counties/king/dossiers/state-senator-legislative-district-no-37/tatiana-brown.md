## Background
Tatiana Brown (Prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate [S1]. She is co-chair and community representative on the Washington Environmental Justice Council, a collaborative governance strategist at People's Economy Lab, former program lead of UW Seattle's Campus Sustainability Fund, and a Distinguished Fellow at the UW Evans School [S1]. She holds an MPA and MPH from the University of Washington and a BS in anthropology from Virginia Commonwealth University [S1]. She lives in Judkins Park [S5]. Seattle Gay News describes her as "an unapologetically Queer woman" [S6]. She took 25.4% of the primary vote (9,729) to Chipalo Street's 73.8% [S7].

## Positions

### Taxes and revenue
- Pamphlet: "ensure our tax system is structured so the wealthiest pay their fair share" [S1].
- Platform: "champion progressive revenue sources such as HB 2100, the Well Washington Fund" [S4].
- Told the International Examiner she would close the sales-tax exemption for new data centers and raise the top real-estate excise tax bracket for homes over $3.025 million [S5]; Seattle Gay News reports she supports adding brackets to the capital gains tax and closing corporate loopholes [S6].

### Climate and data centers
- Helped shape advocacy around the Climate Commitment Act and HEAL Act; co-chairs the Environmental Justice Council [S1].
- Platform: "pass a statewide moratorium on new hyper-scale AI data centers" and a Senate version of HB 2515 "requiring data center developers to pay for their energy use" [S4].

### Housing
- Affordable housing and anti-displacement are top priorities ("House our community and combat displacement") [S1][S4].

### Public safety
- Lists "safer neighborhoods" among priorities and "Prioritize public safety by delivering community what they need" [S1][S4]; the primary dossier recorded support for "alternative public safety approaches" from her site [S2].

### Immigration
- Platform: champion SB 5906 (SAFE Act) "to ICE-proof our schools, and community spaces" [S4].
- Told the International Examiner the state should formally refuse ICE cooperation, revisit the automatic license plate reader law, and decommission the Tacoma detention center [S5].

### Governance and campaign finance
- Wants government-funded community assemblies; helped lead some of the first in the nation [S1].
- Rejects donations from corporate CEOs and PACs, climate polluters, fossil-fuel companies and "war profiteers"; small-donor funded [S3].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- No specific policy position found in her pamphlet statement or platform [S1][S4].

### Parents and schools
- Platform: defend Running Start and the Washington College Grant, ban exclusionary discipline, fully fund school counselors and social workers [S4]. No position on parental notification, records or opt-outs found [S4].

## Endorsements
Her site lists Converge Media, Hacks and Wonks, Through the Static, The Urbanist, Seattle Gay News, The Washington Bus, Sage Leaders and Mountlake Terrace Mayor Steve Woodard [S3]. The primary dossier recorded the Transit Riders Union's endorsement [S2].
