## Background
Chipalo Street (Prefers Democratic Party) has been State Representative for the 37th District since 2023 and is running for the Senate seat vacated by Sen. Rebecca Saldaña [S1][S2]. He says he holds positions in Democratic leadership and on tax and budget committees [S1]. He works at Microsoft on technology safety and security, is a small-business owner and former professional soccer referee, and holds bachelor's and master's degrees in computer science from Brown University [S1]. Seattle Gay News reports he works with Microsoft's chief technology officer as an emerging-technologies advisor [S12]. He took 73.8% of the primary vote (28,196) to Tatiana Brown's 25.4% (9,729) [S9].

## Positions

### Taxes and budget
- Says he "earned positions in Democratic leadership and on tax and budget committees to champion the millionaires tax, which funds education, expands healthcare access, and cuts taxes for working families" [S1].
- Called the millionaires' tax "a one in one hundred year bill" in an International Examiner interview [S5].

### Housing
- Says he "battled the landlord lobby to pass tenant protections like rent stabilization and reformed zoning laws to increase home building" [S1].

### Public safety
- His site says "investment in our communities is the only sustainable path to real public safety" and lists partnerships with the WA Coalition for Police Accountability and the ACLU of Washington [S3].
- Lists an assault weapons ban among his accomplishments [S2][S3].

### Immigration and surveillance
- Pamphlet cites "maintaining food assistance for immigrants, banning ICE from wearing masks, and stopping Flock camera surveillance in our communities" [S1].
- Told the International Examiner he supports a bill barring ICE from courthouses and is working on a state commercial driver's license for immigrants after the federal government revoked immigrant CDLs [S5].

### Healthcare
- Site: "Healthcare is a human right"; seeks to expand access [S3].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Pamphlet cites "restoring the abortion funding [Trump] eliminated" [S1].
- Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Advocates Washington, Pro-Choice Washington and the Stonewall Democrats [S4].

### Parents and schools
- Voted yes on ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments to the I-2081 parents' rights list that I-1 would undo, on House final passage [S7].

## Record
- Voted yes on ESSB 6346 (2026 millionaires' tax) on House final passage [S6].
- Voted yes on EHB 1217 (2025 rent-increase limit) on House final passage [S8].
- Says he secured $131 million for district projects, including Garfield Super Block, Skyway Homeownership, Tubman Health Center, Rainier Valley Food Bank, Childhaven, and $300,000 for a reparations study [S1].
- No floor votes since July: the 2026 regular session ran January 12 to March 12, 2026, and the Legislature's session-documents page lists no 2026 special session [S11].

## Endorsements
His site lists Sen. Rebecca Saldaña, Speaker Laurie Jinkins, Rep. Sharon Tomiko Santos, King County Executive Girmay Zahilay, many other state legislators and local officials, The Stranger, The Urbanist, the King County and 37th District Democrats, the Washington State Labor Council, SEIU locals, building-trades unions, Washington Conservation Action, Sierra Club, Planned Parenthood Advocates Washington, Pro-Choice Washington, the Alliance for Gun Responsibility, and OneAmerica Votes [S4].
His general-election pamphlet statement closes: "With unmatched endorsements from labor and environmental advocates, I ask for your vote to drive progressive wins" [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (income tax repeal):** No public statement on I-645 found (campaign site, pamphlet, news search) [S1][S3][S5]. He voted for ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal, and campaigns on it [S1][S6].
- **I-1 (parental rights in schools):** No public statement found [S1][S3][S5]. He voted for ESHB 1296, the 2025 law I-1 would undo [S7].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** No public position found (campaign site, pamphlet, news search) [S1][S3][S5].
