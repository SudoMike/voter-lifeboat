# State Senator, Legislative District No. 37 — contest overview

- depth: light
- derived_from: data/washington-state/elections/2026-08-04-primary/counties/king/dossiers/state-senator-legislative-district-no-37/_contest.md, candidate dossiers in this directory
- General election: Chipalo Street vs. Tatiana Brown, both preferring the Democratic Party. Open seat (Sen. Rebecca Saldaña did not seek reelection).

State senators serve four-year terms writing state law and the state budget. The 37th LD lies entirely in King County: southeast Seattle (Rainier Valley, Beacon Hill, Central District, Chinatown-International District), Skyway and part of Renton. In the August primary, Rep. Chipalo Street took 73.8% to 25.4% for first-time candidate Tatiana Brown (King County final results).

The two agree on most issues: progressive revenue, affordable housing, protections for immigrants, climate. Street himself said he had "not heard any differences between our platforms." The differences are mostly experience, theory of change and how far each goes. Street has a House record: he voted for the millionaires' tax, rent stabilization and the 2025 parental-rights amendments, and he has broad labor and establishment backing. Brown is an environmental-justice organizer who refuses corporate and PAC money. She proposes community assemblies, a statewide moratorium on hyperscale AI data centers, and formally refusing ICE cooperation. Differentiating axes: experience, reform (campaign finance and participatory governance), tech (data centers), and to a lesser degree immigration. Neither candidate has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives.
