## Background
Hannah Sabio-Howell (Prefers Democratic Party) has held no elected office [S1]. She was communications director at Working Washington, a labor advocacy organization, and earlier a legislative assistant and communications specialist in the state House and Senate [S1]. She holds a BA from Whitworth University in political science and Spanish [S1]. She serves on the Pro-Choice Washington PAC board and The Urbanist Elections Committee [S1]. Her primary dossier records that she is a renter and describes herself as a queer person of color from a mixed-status immigrant family [S2]; FOX 13 describes her as a renter in First Hill [S6].

In the August 4, 2026 primary she received "around 23%" to Jamie Pedersen's "around 66%," per KUOW [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "fight to ensure the ultra-wealthy pay their fair share" [S1]. She calls the millionaires tax "a super important first step" but says it is not enough and wants more corporate taxation where "the wealth really sits in Washington state" [S5]. FOX 13 reports she advocates "a statewide payroll tax" [S6]. Her issues page lists "Establishing the Well Washington Fund, a tax on massive corporations to support healthcare access, higher education", "Fighting for a corporate income tax on mega corporations" and "undoing recently-passed tax cuts for the ultra-wealthy" [S4].
- **Housing:** "Ending apartment bans statewide and ensuring more housing can be built everywhere, especially near transit centers"; "Cutting bureaucratic red tape that slows housing development"; "Investing in social housing developers at the local level" [S4]. Primary coverage recorded support for a statewide social housing program and a tighter rent-stabilization cap than the enacted 10% [S2].
- **Childcare:** "Building a universal pre-K program statewide and establishing a flexible home-care cash benefit for families seeking care for 2- and 3-year-olds"; restoring Working Connections Childcare enrollment [S4]. Her statement commits to "build toward universal childcare" [S1].
- **Public safety:** "Establishing programs that send social workers and mental health professionals to non-violent emergency calls"; "Investing in community-based programs that mentor youth, provide alternatives to incarceration"; funding public-defender caseload standards [S4]. Primary coverage recorded that she wants to revisit rolled-back police use-of-force and pursuit standards [S2].
- **Immigration:** "Protecting our sensitive data from access by immigration enforcement and stripping incentives from companies that contract with ICE"; "Banning state and local law enforcement from hiring former ICE officers" [S4].
- **Technology:** "Regulating and restricting the use of AI and surveillance by law enforcement, including the expansion of data centers" [S4].
- **Healthcare:** her primary dossier records support for the state backstopping federal cuts and taking steps toward universal healthcare [S2]. Her statement cites work to "expand healthcare access" [S1].
- **Campaign money:** "we're the only campaign in this race not taking corporate PAC money" [S1]; FOX 13 describes her as "the only one in the race refusing corporate money" [S6].
- **Process:** her primary dossier records support for even-year local elections and a full-time Legislature [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** says she "organized alongside grassroots workers' rights, immigrants' rights, and queer rights organizations"; serves on the Pro-Choice Washington PAC board, described as working "to advance reproductive justice" [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** no position found [S1][S3][S4].
