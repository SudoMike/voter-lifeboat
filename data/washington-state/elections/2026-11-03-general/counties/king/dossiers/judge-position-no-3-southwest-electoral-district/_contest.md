## What the office does

King County District Court judges hear and decide misdemeanor criminal cases, small claims, traffic cases, and protection orders [S1]. Judges are elected by voters in the electoral district and serve four-year terms; the 2026 salary is $226,096 [S1].

## Race dynamics

Mitch Greene is the only candidate on the ballot and runs unopposed [S2]. He lists his occupation as Attorney, Judge pro tem, so no sitting judge appears on the ballot for this position and the seat appears to be open [S2]. His statement refers to the seat as "Burien Division No. 3" [S2].
