## Background

Javier Valdez (Prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Senator for the 46th Legislative District and Chair of the Senate State Government, Tribal Affairs and Elections Committee; he is a former State Representative [S1][S4]. His legislative service began in the House in 2017 and he moved to the Senate by 2023 [S2]. He has worked for the City of Seattle for over two decades, leading programs to expand opportunities for women- and minority-owned businesses [S1]. He holds a bachelor's degree in business administration from the University of Washington and a master's in public administration from Baruch College (CUNY) [S1]. He has served on the board of the Washington State Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, as president of Hispanic Seafair, and as president of AFSCME Local 21C [S1]. His general-election pamphlet statement is materially the same as his primary statement [S1][S2].

In the August 4, 2026 primary he received 40,980 votes (about 90.6% of votes cast in the race) to Sandra Stephens's 4,153 (about 9.2%) [S12].

## Positions

### Immigration and federal relations
- Says he is "leading the charge to ban ICE agents from wearing masks," and that he helped ban federal agents from using cameras to surveil immigrants and opposed federal access to state voter data [S1].

### Taxes and spending
- Says he "voted to tax millionaires and the very wealthy to pay for free school lunches, tax relief for small businesses, and healthcare investments" [S1].

### Housing
- Says he helped reform zoning laws to allow more affordable housing and supported a cap on rent increases [S1].

### Public safety and guns
- Says he wrote laws banning ghost guns and high-capacity magazines and wants to pass legislation prohibiting carrying guns in parks and fairgrounds [S1].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Says he helped backfill state funding for Planned Parenthood after federal cuts; endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S1].
- As a House member he sponsored HB 1732, expanding hate-crime classifications [S2].

### Parents and schools
- No campaign statement on parental notification, records access, or curricula opt-outs found [S1][S3]. See his ESHB 1296 vote under Record.

## Record

- Committee chair, State Government, Tribal Affairs and Elections; member of Transportation and Law and Justice committees (as of July 2026) [S4].
- Prime sponsor of SB 5855 (2026), which prohibits local, state and federal law enforcement officers, including ICE agents, from wearing face-concealing masks while interacting with the public; it received final Senate approval on March 9, 2026 and went to the governor [S6]. He introduced it in December 2025 in response to masked immigration raids [S7].
- Prime sponsor of SB 5098, restricting weapons at park facilities, county fairs and certain public buildings; it passed the Senate in 2025 but did not receive a House vote [S5].
- As a House member: HB 1739 (ghost guns), HB 1732 (hate crimes), HB 2632 (false emergency reporting) [S2]. His campaign site still describes his House service and the ghost-gun law [S3].
- Voted yes on ESHB 1296 (2025), which amended the parents' rights law enacted by I-2081 (Senate final passage 2025-04-11, 30-19) [S8].
- Voted yes on ESSB 6346 (2026), the tax on household income over $1 million (Senate final passage 2026-03-11, 27-21) [S9].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not been in session since the 2026 regular session adjourned on March 12, 2026 [S11].

## Endorsements

Per his general-election pamphlet statement: 46th District Democrats, Washington State Labor Council, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Washington Conservation Action, One America Votes, Washington State Nurses Association, "and more" [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (IP26-645, income tax repeal):** No public statement on I-645 found on his campaign site or in his pamphlet statement [S1][S3]. Voted yes on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S9], and cites voting to tax millionaires as a campaign accomplishment [S1].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** No public statement found [S1][S3]. Voted yes on ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments I-1 would undo [S8].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** No public position found on his campaign site, in his pamphlet statement, or in news searches [S1][S3].

## Campaign finance

PDC reports, as of a record updated 2026-10-07: $133,275.00 in contributions, $38,149.80 carried forward, and $66,289.97 in expenditures for the 2026 cycle [S10].

- Record on the 2026 ballot questions: yes votes on ESSB 6346 (the tax I-645 would repeal) and ESHB 1296 (the amendments I-1 would undo) [S8][S9].
- Signature record areas: constraining federal immigration enforcement (SB 5855 mask ban), gun regulation, and the millionaires' tax [S1][S6][S9].
