## Background

Sandra Stephens (Prefers Republican Party) is a retired registered nurse running for State Senator in the 46th Legislative District [S1]. She graduated in 1978 with a Bachelor of Science in nursing from Oregon Health Sciences University [S1]. She worked as a medical-surgical nurse in hospitals, mental health home care, stroke centers, clinics and community health centers, and for her last sixteen years was a charge nurse at UW Northwest Hospital [S1]. Her only listed elected experience is two terms (1988-1992) in the National Association of Pro-Life Nurses [S1]. She describes herself as a grandmother who raised seven children; her community service includes hospital boards, feeding the homeless, volunteering at youth prisons and Salvation Army emergency response teams [S1]. Her pamphlet statement lists no campaign website [S1]. Her general-election statement is materially the same as her primary statement [S1][S2].

In the August 4, 2026 primary she received 4,153 votes (about 9.2% of votes cast in the race) [S4].
