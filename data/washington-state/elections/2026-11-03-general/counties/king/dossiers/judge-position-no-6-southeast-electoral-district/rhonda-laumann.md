## Background

Laumann lists a J.D. (1992) from Seattle University and a BA (1989) from Colorado Mesa University [S1].
She lists her occupation as King County District Court Judge, Southeast division, position 6 [S1].
Her statement says she was elected to the bench in 2018 and is seeking a third term [S1].
Her statement does not describe her practice before the bench [S1].

## Positions

Her statement says she communicates with many self-represented litigants on the civil bench and explains proceedings and rulings in understandable terms [S1].

## Record

Her statement says she is currently responsible for thousands of civil cases and has handled criminal, small claims, and protection order cases over the past eight years [S1].
She reports training pro tempore judges and teaching for the National Institute of Trial Advocacy [S1].
She states she serves on the court's Diversity and Inclusion Committee and chairs the Small Claims Forms subcommittee [S1].
