## Background
John Bielka (prefers Democrat Party) is a first-time candidate ("First time public servant") [S1]. He is a civil engineer and public works director with more than 30 years managing infrastructure, budgets, and operations, has owned an excavation business, and raises cattle on a farm outside Orting [S1]. Bachelor's in civil engineering, University of Saskatchewan, with graduate coursework in engineering economics and accounting [S1]. He volunteers as a career and technical education advisor at Chief Leschi Schools and as a grant writer for the Orting Senior Center and Orting Food Bank [S1]. In the August primary he had 44.18% to incumbent Joshua Penner's 55.66% in the election-night results from King County Elections reported by the Auburn Reporter [S7].

## Positions
- **Taxes and budget:** "I will not support increased taxes until the Legislature prioritizes its spending choices," citing Governor Gregoire's line that Washington "has a spending problem, not a revenue problem"; says he will tell voters "honestly whether any new proposed taxes meets this standard" [S3].
- **Spending:** says the state budget has grown 116% against 13% population growth over 11 years and calls for measuring results and ending programs "that fail to deliver" [S3].
- **Housing:** supports ADUs, duplexes, townhomes, small multifamily housing, nonprofit-led tiny-home communities, condo-style division of larger homes, and "clear, timely, and predictable" permitting [S3].
- **Public safety:** supports recruiting and retaining officers, holding repeat offenders accountable, and stronger behavioral-health and crisis-response systems "so police are not the only option" [S3].
- **Health care:** supports wider access to primary, mental-health, and senior care, plus price transparency and fewer surprise bills [S3].
- **Climate and flooding:** supports using Climate Commitment Act funding "where appropriate" to fix flooding and drainage [S3].
- **Guns:** supports "the Second Amendment and responsible gun ownership" [S2][S3].
- **Regulation:** supports permitting reform with firm review timelines and concurrent agency review [S3].
