## Background
Joshua Penner (prefers Republican Party) is the incumbent State Representative for the 31st District, Position 2, in his first term since January 2025; he is Assistant Ranking Member on Appropriations and serves on the Finance and Early Learning & Human Services committees [S1][S8]. He was Mayor of Orting for eight years, an Orting councilmember for six years, and a planning commissioner for two, and formerly chaired the Pierce County Regional Council [S1]. He is a Marine Corps Iraq War veteran (radio operator), works in executive strategy at Penner & Co, co-founded the veteran support network PNW Vets, and is a crisis intervention trainer for WA-CJTC [S1]. Harvard University ALM; BA, St. Martin's University [S1]. In the August primary he had 55.66% to Democrat John Bielka's 44.18% in the election-night results from King County Elections reported by the Auburn Reporter [S12].

## Positions
- **Cost of living and accountability:** pledges to "keep fighting to lower living costs, demand accountability, protect our most vulnerable, and secure our neighborhoods" [S1]; his site's tagline is "Pragmatic Leadership. Compassionate Outcomes. Real Accountability." and calls for "data-driven oversight" [S2].
- **Child welfare:** says he led floor efforts to change the Keeping Families Together Act's child-removal standard, which he says kept caseworkers from removing infants from fentanyl-exposed homes [S2].
- **Disability care:** opposed SB 5167's closure of Residential Habilitation Centers without replacement care in place; pamphlet cites saving Buckley's Rainier School [S1][S2].
- **Sound Transit and local zoning:** opposed SB 6309, which he says lets Sound Transit sidestep local zoning laws and financial accountability [S2].
- **State overreach:** opposed SB 5925's expansion of the Attorney General's investigative powers over small businesses and local governments, and offered amendments to shield local budgets [S3].
