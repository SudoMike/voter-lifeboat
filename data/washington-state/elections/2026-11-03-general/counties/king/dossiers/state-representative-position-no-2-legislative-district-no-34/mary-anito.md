## Background
Mary Anito (Prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate challenging House Majority Leader Joe Fitzgibbon; she lists no prior elected office other than service as a fiduciary on REI Co-op's Retirement Plan Committee [S1]. Her career is in operations and supply-chain management (REI, Wayfair, Caterpillar, Cisco Systems, Hum Nutrition, Shelf Engine) and home renovation through Jenito Homes in Seattle, and she describes herself as a public school mom of three [S1]. Education: BS in Materials Science and Engineering, Johns Hopkins University; MBA and MS as an MIT Leaders for Global Operations Fellow [S1]. Community service includes secretary and treasurer of the ROOTS Young Adult Shelter board, co-op preschool boards, and PTA enrichment coordination [S1]. She took 24.3% of the King County primary vote (9,999 votes) to Fitzgibbon's 74.5% [S4]. In July her campaign site could not be retrieved [S2]; it was reachable on 2026-10-08 [S3].

## Positions

### Budget and taxes
- Argues "fiscal responsibility and strong public investment are not mutually exclusive" and wants to balance the budget while funding infrastructure, education and healthcare [S1].
- Campaign slogan: "Fiscal responsibility. Families first. Every voice heard." [S3]
- No stated position on specific taxes (the millionaires' tax, sales or B&O taxes) found [S1][S3].

### Education
- Supports fully funding schools, including special education, materials and staff such as counselors, nurses and paraeducators; wants to "fix the levy and equalization structure" and invest in educator recruitment [S3].

### Housing
- Supports building and preserving affordable housing and preventing evictions, including through a centralized data dashboard [S3].

### Healthcare
- Supports efforts toward universal healthcare "such as The Washington Health Trust," restoring Medicaid funding, mental-health beds and maternal care [S3].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Her site says she will "ensure access to reproductive healthcare (including restoring funding for abortion access ...)" [S3].

### Public safety
- Supports universal background checks, Extreme Risk Protection Orders, secure-storage requirements and community violence intervention programs [S3].

### Small business
- Supports streamlined licensing and permitting, access to funding and apprenticeship-based workforce development [S3].

### Parents and schools
- No position on parental notification, records access or curricula opt-outs found [S1][S3].

## Endorsements
Her site shows Moms Demand Action, the Northwest Progressive Caucus of WA and The Seattle Times under "Endorsed and Recognized by," alongside a quote attributed to The Seattle Times: "It's time for a new sensibility and set of experiences" [S3]. The Seattle Times piece itself was not located, so whether this is a formal editorial endorsement is unverified.
