# State Representative Position No. 2, Legislative District No. 34 — contest overview

- General election: two candidates, both preferring the Democratic Party.

State representatives serve two-year terms writing state law and the state budget. The 34th LD lies entirely in King County: West Seattle, Vashon and Maury islands, White Center and parts of Burien. In the August primary, House Majority Leader Joe Fitzgibbon took 74.5% of the vote to 24.3% for first-time candidate Mary Anito (King County final results).

Fitzgibbon has a long legislative record: he led on the Climate Commitment Act and Clean Fuel Standard, voted for and championed the 2026 millionaires' tax, co-sponsored the 2025 parental-rights amendments (ESHB 1296), and has publicly opposed I-645. Anito, an operations engineer and small-business owner, runs on "fiscal responsibility" combined with public investment, universal-healthcare efforts and education funding. She has no stated position on specific taxes or the initiatives. The axes that clearly separate them are experience (long-tenured leader vs. newcomer) and, more weakly, fiscal emphasis. On taxes, climate and parental rights only Fitzgibbon has documented positions; on those axes the evidence is one-sided, not opposed.
