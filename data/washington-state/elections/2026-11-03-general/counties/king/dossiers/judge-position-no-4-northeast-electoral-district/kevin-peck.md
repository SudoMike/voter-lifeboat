All content below is self-reported in the candidate's King County local voters' pamphlet statement and was not independently verified [S1].

## Background

Peck lists a J.D. from the University of Oregon and a B.A., summa cum laude, from Northern Illinois University [S1].
He lists his occupation as King County District Court Judge [S1].
His statement says the King County Council unanimously appointed him in 2019 and that he was elected to the position in 2020 and 2022 [S1].
It reports that before his appointment he served as a Judge Pro Tem in King County District Court for more than 25 years [S1].
It reports more than three decades as a King County trial lawyer representing clients in employment discrimination, civil rights, and federal criminal cases [S1].

## Positions

His statement names transparency, integrity, and fairness as central to his work [S1].
It says he aims to ensure that people appearing before him understand the legal process, their choices, and the accountability expected of them [S1].

## Record

His statement says he pursued a case through the U.S. Supreme Court to uphold free speech rights, without naming the case [S1].
It reports more than 30 years as a board member of the Washington Community Action Network [S1].

## Endorsements

His statement lists more than 100 judges; Councilmember Claudia Balducci; Attorney General Nick Brown; and former Attorney General Rob McKenna [S1].
Organizational endorsements listed are IAM District Lodge 751, ATU 587, Teamsters JC 28, the King County Democrats, and the 1st, 5th, 41st, 45th, and 48th Legislative District Democrats [S1].
