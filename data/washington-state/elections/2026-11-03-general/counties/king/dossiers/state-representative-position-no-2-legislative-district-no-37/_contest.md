# State Representative Position No. 2, Legislative District No. 37 — contest overview

- General election: Jaelynn Scott (Prefers Democratic Party) vs. Evon McCorkle (Prefers Independent Party). Open seat (Rep. Chipalo Street is running for the Senate).

One of two House seats for the 37th LD, which lies entirely in King County (southeast Seattle, Skyway, part of Renton). State representatives serve two-year terms.

Scott took 88.3% of the August primary vote to McCorkle's 10.9% (King County final results). Scott runs the Lavender Rights Project and would be Washington's first openly transgender legislator. Her platform is detailed: wealth, corporate and high-earner taxes, a 5% rent-increase cap and social housing, universal healthcare, defense of abortion and gender-affirming care, and community-based public safety. Her endorsers range from Bernie Sanders to the House Speaker. McCorkle is a college student whose platform is general (education funding, housing, healthcare, "fair tax reform" with financial responsibility, representation for working-class residents). He has no endorsements and almost no campaign money. Evidence is uneven: Scott has documented positions on most axes, McCorkle on few. Differentiating axes: experience/outsider status, and taxes, housing and social only where Scott's positions stand alone. Neither has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives.
