## Background
Evon McCorkle (Prefers Independent Party) is a college student studying mathematics and physics and a first-time candidate with no prior elected office [S1]. He describes experience in leadership discussions, policy development, community engagement and independent research on housing, education and state policy, including proposals on tax reform, housing access and public accountability [S1]. His site describes lived experience with housing instability and reliance on public assistance [S3]. The International Examiner could not reach him for comment [S5]. He took 10.9% of the primary vote (4,143) to Jaelynn Scott's 88.3% [S6]. In July his campaign website could not be reached [S2]; it was reachable on 2026-10-08 [S3].

## Positions
- **Education:** Consistent, responsible education funding, treated "as investment, not expense"; funding affects class sizes, support staff and program access [S1][S4].
- **Housing:** Affordability and access to safe shelter; expand capacity and create pathways to stability [S1][S4].
- **Taxes:** Lists "fair tax reform" that balances "financial responsibility with investment," acknowledges high tax burdens in Washington, and says "meaningful progress requires financial responsibility" [S4]. No position on specific taxes found [S4].
- **Healthcare:** Affordable, reliable coverage that does not depend on income [S4].
- **Representation:** "I'm not backed by powerful insiders or political machines"; wants to restore trust between residents and elected officials and give minority and working-class residents a stronger voice [S1][S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** No statement found [S1][S3][S4].
- **Parents and schools:** No position on parental notification, records or opt-outs found [S1][S4].
