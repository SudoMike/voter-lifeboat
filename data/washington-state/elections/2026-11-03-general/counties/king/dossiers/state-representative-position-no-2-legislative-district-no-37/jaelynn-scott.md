## Background
Jaelynn Scott (Prefers Democratic Party) is Executive Director of the Lavender Rights Project, a nonprofit serving Black trans and gender-diverse communities, and has led it for seven years [S1][S6]. She is an ordained family minister with a Master of Divinity, a former union member, and a consultant in mindfulness-based organizational management; she has no prior elected office [S1]. Her site describes her as a "proud Democratic Socialist" [S3]. If elected she would be the first openly transgender person in the Washington Legislature [S6][S7]. The seat is open because Rep. Chipalo Street is running for the Senate [S2]. She took 88.3% of the primary vote (33,678) to Evon McCorkle's 10.9% [S8].

## Positions

### Taxes
- Pamphlet: "a fairer tax code" [S1].
- Platform: "pass corporate accountability legislation by closing loopholes and taxing the rich," including a corporate income tax, capital gains and wealth taxes, a high-earner payroll tax, and taxes on AI/data centers and private prisons [S4].

### Housing
- Platform: "Housing is a human right, not a commodity"; public and cooperative ownership, a 5% cap on rent increases, public banking, social housing, tenant and community opportunity-to-purchase (TOPA/COPA), and a ban on junk fees [S4].

### Public safety
- Pamphlet: "compassionate public-safety" and a "community-based public safety model" [S1].
- Platform: community-based crisis response, independent police oversight, ending qualified immunity, gun-responsibility legislation, and ending mass surveillance [S4].

### Healthcare
- Pamphlet: "universal healthcare" [S1]; platform: "universal coverage, no deductibles, no debt" and reinvestment in the Washington Health Trust [S4].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Platform defends abortion and gender-affirming care against federal attacks [S4].
- Her organization serves Black trans communities, and endorsers include Gender Justice League, the WA Stonewall Democrats chair and Surge Reproductive Justice [S5][S6].

### Immigration
- Platform: "build a real sanctuary for Washington state" [S4].

### Schools and students
- Platform: decriminalize students, "fully fund our public schools," end the school-to-prison pipeline [S4]. No position on parental notification, records access or curricula opt-outs found [S4].

### Childcare and tech
- Supports universal childcare with a dedicated revenue stream [S4][S6].
- Platform: end mass surveillance; regulate and tax data-center energy and water use [S4].

## Record
- Says she redirected $16 million from jails into supportive housing and helped establish a community-based public-safety model [S1][S6].
