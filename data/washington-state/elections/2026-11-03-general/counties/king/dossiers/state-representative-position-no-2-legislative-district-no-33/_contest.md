## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members per legislative district, elected to a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets.

## District and race dynamics
LD 33 is in south King County: SeaTac, Burien, Des Moines, Normandy Park and parts of Kent (primary _contest.md). Incumbent Democrat Mia Su-Ling Gregerson (53.30% in the certified primary) faces Republican Yuri Marinchik (24.41%), who edged out Burien Councilmember Alex Andrade, a Democrat, by 600 votes for the second slot (mia-su-ling-gregerson.md S10). Gregerson has raised about $58k; Marinchik under $2k (mia-su-ling-gregerson.md S2; yuri-marinchik.md S3). The King County Republican Party endorses Marinchik (yuri-marinchik.md S4).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience:** a 13-year incumbent and Appropriations vice-chair versus a first-time candidate who runs against "career politicians."
2. **Spending:** Gregerson runs on delivered state programs (school meals, food aid, free youth transit); Marinchik says the state spends more than it earns (mia-su-ling-gregerson.md S1; yuri-marinchik.md S2).
3. **Taxes:** Gregerson voted for the 2026 millionaires tax; Marinchik rejects new taxes (mia-su-ling-gregerson.md S3; yuri-marinchik.md S2).
4. **Reform:** Marinchik signed a congressional term-limits pledge (yuri-marinchik.md S6).
`parental-rights` and `social` evidence exists only on Gregerson's side (ESHB 1296 vote; PPAA endorsement). Marinchik has no stated position on either.
