## Background
Mia Su-Ling Gregerson has represented the 33rd District since 2013 and is Vice-Chair of House Appropriations; she co-chairs a statewide poverty-reduction task force and says she helped establish Washington's first Office of Equity [S1]. She served on the SeaTac City Council and as mayor from 2008 to 2015, when SeaTac passed the first $15 minimum wage in the country [S1]. Before the Legislature she spent nearly 20 years in the dental field; she has a history degree from UW [S1]. She led the certified primary with 53.30%; Republican Yuri Marinchik was second with 24.41%, ahead of Democrat Alex Andrade [S10].

## Positions
- **Cost of living:** Frames the race around grocery, gas and rent costs and points to programs she worked on: free school meals for every student, emergency food assistance when SNAP benefits dropped, free transit for kids [S1].
- **Consumer protection:** Campaigns on the Right to Repair law [S1][S11].
- **Transportation:** Cites funding for the 224th Street corridor and Kent RapidRide [S1].
