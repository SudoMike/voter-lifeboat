## Background
Yuri Marinchik is a first-time candidate who describes himself as a working-class immigrant working full time while building a small business with his wife; he lists no elected office or formal education credentials [S1]. He finished second in the certified primary with 24.41%, 600 votes ahead of Democrat Alex Andrade [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "I do not believe the same politicians who are ruining our state when they say that new taxes will make things better. It will not." [S2] Pamphlet calls for "fair taxes" and lower housing and energy costs [S1]. Cites voters' approval of $30 car tabs being ignored and Sound Transit RTA taxes paid by people who do not use transit [S2].
- **Spending:** Says the state faces a budget shortfall and is "spending more than we earn" [S2].
- **Regulation:** Criticizes "endless rules that make life harder and increase government control" [S1].
- **Public safety:** Calls for "stronger public safety"; cites drug overdoses, homelessness, car break-ins and property crime and says he has been robbed twice [S1][S2].
- **Term limits:** Signed the U.S. Term Limits pledge supporting congressional term limits [S6].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** No statement found.
- **Parents and schools:** No statement found.
