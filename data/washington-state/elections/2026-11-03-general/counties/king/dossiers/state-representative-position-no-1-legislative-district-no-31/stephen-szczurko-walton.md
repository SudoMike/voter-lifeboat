## Background
Stephen Szczurko-Walton (prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate with no elected experience [S1]. He was a U.S. Navy nuclear reactor operator from 2015 to 2024, with three deployments aboard the USS Theodore Roosevelt, and now works as a commercial HVAC technician serving schools, hospitals, and small businesses; earlier jobs included retail and campus security [S1]. B.A. in political science, UC Santa Barbara (2014); Master of Public Administration (2025) [S1]. In the August primary he had 42% to incumbent Drew Stokesbary's 57.88% in the election-night results from King County Elections reported by the Auburn Reporter [S5].

## Positions
- **Campaign finance:** pledges to "pass SB 6358 and end corporate election spending in Washington" [S1]; his site says SB 6358 "would bypass Citizens United" by removing status from any non-human entity, "corporation, union, non-profit," that spends to sway an election [S2].
- **Taxes:** calls for "a progressive tax structure that ends Washington's status as the second-most-regressive tax state in the country" [S2]; pamphlet pledges to bring down tax costs "crushing working families" [S1].
- **Health care:** supports "universal healthcare — more effective than the private insurance market" [S2].
- **Housing:** supports "state preemption to cut red tape on housing construction" [S2].
- **Climate and energy:** will "defend the Climate Commitment Act and modernize our grid" [S1]; his site calls cap-and-invest "already law" and says he will "defend it against raids on its funding," while keeping "every safe, clean power source on the table" [S2].
