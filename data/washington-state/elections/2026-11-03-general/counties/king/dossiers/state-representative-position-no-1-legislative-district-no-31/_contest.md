## What the office does
One of two State Representatives for the 31st Legislative District, elected to a two-year term in the 98-member House, voting on state law and the operating, capital, and transportation budgets. LD31 spans southeast King County and northeast Pierce County (see the primary overview for state-senator-legislative-district-no-31), so Pierce County voters also vote in this contest.

## District and race dynamics
November 3, 2026 general election, two candidates: incumbent Republican Drew Stokesbary, the House Minority Leader, and Democratic first-time candidate Stephen Szczurko-Walton, a Navy veteran and HVAC technician (see drew-stokesbary.md S1; stephen-szczurko-walton.md S1). They were also the only two candidates on the primary ballot.

Stokesbary runs on cost of living, crime, and school outcomes ("Let's fix Washington"), opposed the 2026 tax on income over $1 million, and has broad law-enforcement, business, and some labor endorsements (drew-stokesbary.md S1, S3, S4, S5). Szczurko-Walton runs on campaign-finance reform (SB 6358), a progressive tax structure, universal health care, and defending the Climate Commitment Act (stephen-szczurko-walton.md S1, S2). The fundraising gap is wide: about $387k against about $16k (PDC; drew-stokesbary.md S11, stephen-szczurko-walton.md S4).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Stokesbary voted against ESSB 6346 (the tax I-645 would repeal) and called it a "betrayal"; Szczurko-Walton calls for a progressive tax structure.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** the House Republican leader with a legislative career dating to 2014, against a first-time candidate.
3. **Climate:** Szczurko-Walton pledges to defend the Climate Commitment Act; Stokesbary's materials do not address it.
4. **Money in politics:** Szczurko-Walton's signature issue is ending corporate election spending.
5. **Health care:** Szczurko-Walton supports universal health care.
6. **Parental rights and social policy:** Stokesbary voted against ESHB 1296 and ESSB 5599; Szczurko-Walton states no position, though Stonewall Democrats endorse him.
