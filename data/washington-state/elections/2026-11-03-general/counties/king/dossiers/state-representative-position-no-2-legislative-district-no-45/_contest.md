# State Representative Position No. 2, Legislative District No. 45 — contest overview

## What this office does

A state representative serves a two-year term in the 98-member House, voting on state budgets, taxes and all state law, and shaping policy through committee work. This is one of the two House seats for the 45th District (Kirkland, Redmond, Woodinville, Sammamish, Duvall).

## Race dynamics

- Open seat: Rep. Larry Springer did not seek reelection (primary contest overview).
- Redmond City Councilmember Vanessa Kritzer (Prefers Democratic Party) led the three-way primary with 62.75% (results as of August 10, per the Snoqualmie Valley Record; vanessa-kritzer.md S7), eliminating Democrat Chandler Torbett. Her general-election opponent is John P Gibbons (Prefers Republican Party).
- Gibbons submitted no statement, biography or photo to either pamphlet, lists no website, and had no PDC financial reports on file as of September 17 (john-p-gibbons.md S1, S3). Kritzer had raised about $150,000 by October 6 (vanessa-kritzer.md S6).
- Neither candidate has a public position on I-645, I-1 or I-638.

## Differentiating axes

- Only Kritzer has documented positions. Her record and statements support scores on taxes (progressive revenue), housing (tenant protections, inclusionary zoning), climate, local-control (state sets the floor), immigration and social (reproductive and LGBTQIA+ rights), plus experience (municipal record).
- Gibbons offers a party-preference contrast only; no axis can be scored for him.
