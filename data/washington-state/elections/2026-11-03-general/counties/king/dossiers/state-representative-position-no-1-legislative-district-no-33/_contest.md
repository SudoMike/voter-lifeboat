## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members per legislative district, elected to a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets.

## District and race dynamics
The 33rd District is in south King County (SeaTac, Burien, Des Moines, Normandy Park, and parts of Kent and Renton); Democrats hold all three seats (primary _contest.md). Incumbent Democrat Edwin Obras, appointed in December 2024 and elected in a close 2025 special election against a fellow Democrat, faces Republican Chris Martinez, a first-time candidate endorsed by the King County Republican Party (edwin-obras.md S1, S11; chris-martinez.md S4). Obras has raised about six times as much as Martinez in 2026 (edwin-obras.md S3; chris-martinez.md S3).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Obras voted for the 2026 millionaires tax; Martinez calls it an "unconstitutional income tax" and wants lower B&O taxes (edwin-obras.md S4; chris-martinez.md S2).
2. **Climate:** Martinez would reduce or eliminate the Climate Commitment Act; Obras is endorsed by Washington Conservation Action (chris-martinez.md S2; edwin-obras.md S1).
3. **Housing:** rent caps and limits on investor buying (Obras) versus deregulating home builders (Martinez).
4. **Public safety:** Martinez wants more police and "law and order"; Obras points to background-check laws and jail reform.
5. **Parental rights:** Obras sponsored ESHB 1296, which amended the I-2081 parental-rights list; Martinez says parents have the right to know and guide what children learn and opposes "woke ideologies" in schools (edwin-obras.md S6; chris-martinez.md S2).
6. **Experience:** a short-tenure incumbent with six enacted bills versus a political newcomer.
`social` evidence is thin on both sides: a PPAA endorsement (Obras) and "traditional family values" language (Martinez).
