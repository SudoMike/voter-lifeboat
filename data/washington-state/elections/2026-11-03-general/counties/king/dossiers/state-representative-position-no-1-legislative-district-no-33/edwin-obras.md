## Background
Edwin Obras was appointed to the state House in December 2024, when Rep. Tina Orwall moved to the Senate, and won the November 2025 special election [S1][S11]; that race was a 641-vote win over Burien Mayor Kevin Schilling, a fellow Democrat [S11]. He came from the Philippines as a child, has lived in SeaTac for 23 years, and was the first in his family to graduate from college (BA, UW; MPA, Northeastern) [S1]. He spent 18 years at the Seattle Human Services Department, rising to division director [S1]. He is Assistant Majority Whip and serves on the Labor, Health Care and Community Safety committees [S2].

## Positions
- **Housing:** "Your rent went up $400. Your paycheck didn't." He has pushed for rent-increase caps and limits on "excessive home buying that prices out first-time buyers" [S2].
- **Workers:** Cites three worker-protection laws and pending bills on tip-skimming and asbestos training [S2].
- **Public safety:** Cites his background-check and peace-officer false-identification laws; working on jail system reform and community safety liability standards [S2].
- **Health care:** Wants to expand statewide access to mental health resources; working on nursing-assistant regulation [S2].
- **Immigration:** "I'll continue fighting for immigrant rights" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** No campaign statement found; endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S2][S9].
- **Parents and schools:** No campaign statement found; he was an original sponsor of ESHB 1296 (see Record).

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

## Record
- Six bills signed in the 2025-26 biennium: ESHB 1332 (rideshare), SHB 1490 (background checks), 2SHB 1524 (isolated employees), HB 2155 (nursing titles), EHB 2156 (AG investigators), ESHB 2165 (falsely identifying as a peace officer) [S2].
- Says he secured over $30 million for the district, including housing and health investments [S1].
- Voted yea on House final passage of ESSB 6346 (9.9% tax on income over $1 million), 2026-03-09 [S4].
- Original sponsor of HB 1296 and yea on House final passage, 2025-03-12 and 2025-04-24 [S5][S6].
- Voted yea on final passage of EHB 1217 (rent stabilization) as recommended by the conference committee, 2025-04-27 [S7].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S8].
- PDC (data updated 2026-10-05): $39,542.41 raised, $22,804.55 spent for 2026 [S3].

## Scoring notes
- Incumbent of under two years with six enacted bills, against a first-time Republican candidate [S1][S2].
- Voted for the millionaires tax and sponsored ESHB 1296; his opponent lists ESSB 6346 among bills he opposes (see chris-martinez.md) [S4][S6].
- Rent caps and worker protections are his affordability approach, the inverse of his opponent's tax- and regulation-cutting approach [S2].
- His opponent lists HB 2165, which Obras's site describes as his peace-officer false-ID law, among bills he calls constitutional violations (see chris-martinez.md) [S2].
