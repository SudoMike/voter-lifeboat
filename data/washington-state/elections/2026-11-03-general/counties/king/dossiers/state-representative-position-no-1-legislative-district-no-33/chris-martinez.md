## Background
Chris Martinez (Christopher Elpidio Quisora Martinez Jr. in PDC filings) is an appointed Republican precinct committee officer with no prior elected office [S1][S3]. He describes himself as a lifelong blue-collar worker (automotive technician, industrial painter, handyman, laborer), graduated high school in 2010 and earned a computer-based accounting certificate in 2012 [S1]. His site describes a troubled youth, including juvenile detention and jail at 23, followed by marriage in 2019, a Christian conversion, homeownership and paying off debt [S2].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** Wants lower taxes, "especially B&O taxes," and lists "HB 6346 (Unconstitutional Income Tax)" among bills he opposes [S2]. Pamphlet pledges to "abolish energy-killing taxes" [S1].
- **Spending and size of government:** Cut wasteful spending, reduce unnecessary regulations on businesses and home builders, and "reduce the size of Washington Government" [S2].
- **Climate and energy:** Would reduce or eliminate the Climate Commitment Act, which he blames for energy costs [S2].
- **Public safety:** "Restore law and order," support law enforcement, hire more officers, hold criminals accountable; opposes SB 5974, which he describes as removing elected sheriffs [S1][S2].
- **Constitutional rights:** Emphasizes Second Amendment and religious liberty; lists HB 2165, SB 5925 and HB 2320 as constitutional violations he opposes [S2].
- **Housing:** Would reduce regulations on home builders to make housing more affordable [S2].
- **Parents and schools:** "Fight against woke ideologies being taught in our schools without parents knowing or consenting. Parents have the right to know and guide what their children learn." [S2]
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Campaigns on "traditional family values" and "protecting the traditional family unit" [S1][S2]; no specific statement found on sports eligibility, gender-affirming care or abortion.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645):** No statement naming I-645 found as of 2026-10-08; he lists the income tax it would repeal among bills he opposes [S2].
- **I-1 (IL26-001):** No statement naming I-1 found as of 2026-10-08; his site states parents have the right to know and guide what their children learn [S2].
- **I-638 (IL26-638):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- The King County Republican Party, which endorses him, recommends yes on all three; that is the party's position, not his [S4].

## Scoring notes
- First-time candidate with an explicit limited-government platform: lower taxes, repeal of the Climate Commitment Act, more police [S1][S2].
- Opposes the income tax his opponent voted for, and the peace-officer false-ID bill (HB 2165) his opponent's site says he passed [S2].
