# State Representative Position No. 1, Legislative District No. 45 — contest overview

## What this office does

A state representative is one of two House members for the district, serving a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state laws and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, covering K-12 funding, housing, taxes, public safety, health care and transportation. The 45th District is an Eastside King County district including Kirkland, Redmond, Woodinville, Sammamish and Duvall (per the primary contest overview).

## Race dynamics

- Two-person general between incumbent Roger Goodman (Prefers Democratic Party), in the House since 2007 and chair of the Community Safety Committee, and JoAnn Tolentino (Prefers Republican Party), a retired music teacher and compliance professional making her first legislative run (roger-goodman.md S1, S4; joann-tolentino.md S1).
- Goodman led the August primary with 72.87% (results as of August 10, per the Snoqualmie Valley Record; roger-goodman.md S9).
- Both candidates filed the same pamphlet statements as in the primary.
- Goodman voted for the 2026 millionaires' tax (ESSB 6346) and the 2025 parental-rights amendments (ESHB 1296), the two laws that I-645 and I-1 target; neither candidate has a public position on any of the three statewide initiatives (roger-goodman.md S5, S6).

## Differentiating axes

- **Experience:** nearly 20-year incumbent and committee chair vs. a first-time candidate running as "not a politician".
- **Parental rights:** Tolentino says "parents, not politicians, should guide their children's education and healthcare" and rejects "CRT and gender-ideology debates in classrooms"; Goodman voted for ESHB 1296.
- **Public safety:** police-accountability reform and behavioral-health services (Goodman) vs. "full support for our law enforcement" plus treatment-focused recovery (Tolentino).
- **Housing and energy:** affordable-housing investment and climate action (Goodman) vs. deregulation, zoning reform and an all-sources energy policy including fossil fuels (Tolentino).
- **Taxes and spending:** Goodman voted for the millionaires' tax and cites school meals and childcare funding; Tolentino calls for auditing "every dollar" and balanced budgets.
- **Social:** Goodman pledges to champion reproductive rights; Tolentino has no stated position on reproductive or LGBTQ+ policy beyond classroom content.
