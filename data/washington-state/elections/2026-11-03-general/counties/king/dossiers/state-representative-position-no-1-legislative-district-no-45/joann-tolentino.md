## Background

JoAnn Tolentino (Prefers Republican Party) is a Woodinville resident challenging incumbent Rep. Roger Goodman [S1][S3]. She taught private music lessons for over three decades (35 years per the pamphlet, 34 per her site) and worked in Governance, Risk, and Compliance software sales before retiring [S1][S3]. Her elected experience is five years as a precinct committee officer [S1]. She attended Wenatchee Valley College and Seattle Pacific University as a Music Therapy major [S1]. She has been married 46 years, raised four children and adopted a child from foster care [S3]. In the primary record she was a 2025 candidate for the Northshore School District board, District 4 [S2].

Her general-election pamphlet statement is the same text she filed for the primary [S1][S2]. In the August primary Goodman led with 72.87% against her, per results as of August 10 [S5].

## Positions

**Elections**
- Supports voter ID, paper ballots and bipartisan oversight, framed as election integrity [S1][S3].

**Parents and schools**
- Pamphlet: "We must ... empower parents, and prioritize academic excellence" [S1].
- Site: "Parents, not politicians, should guide their children's education and healthcare. Let's restore local control and open dialogue in schools." [S3]
- Site: "Focus on proven curricula, involve parents, and keep politics out of classrooms," and "Reject CRT and gender-ideology debates in classrooms" [S3].

**Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy**
- No statement found on sports eligibility, gender-affirming care, abortion or anti-discrimination law [S1][S3]. Her "gender-ideology" statement is about classroom content and is listed under Parents and schools.

**Housing**
- "I will fight the regulations driving up housing costs" [S1]; site: "Streamline permitting and reform zoning to build more affordable homes" [S3].

**Energy and environment**
- Pledges to "champion affordable energy" [S1]; site: "Support reliable energy — fossil fuels, nuclear, hydro — to keep bills low and lights on" [S3].

**Public safety, homelessness and addiction**
- Calls for "proven recovery solutions for homelessness and full support for our law enforcement" [S1].
- Site: "We need treatment, not just punishment. Fund recovery programs and hold systems accountable" [S3].

**Fiscal policy**
- Site: "waste and fraud have no place in government. Audit every dollar and pass balanced budgets" [S3]; pamphlet stresses accountability and transparency [S1].

**Immigration**
- Site: "Support legal immigration and the rule of law, while ensuring humane processing and border security" [S3].
