## Background
Kelabe Tewolde (Prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate who says he is running "to bring needed generation change to Olympia" [S1]. He has been Associate Director of Leadership Development at Rainier Scholars since 2018 and is a Rainier Scholars alumnus; earlier he did UX research and casework and legislative internships for U.S. Sen. Patty Murray [S1][S5]. He has served on the Seattle Planning Commission since 2020 and holds a BA in political science from Colgate University [S1]. He was raised by a single mother who came to the U.S. as a refugee; his family experienced housing insecurity, medical debt and gun violence [S1]. He is a renter and says he has moved four times in six years [S5][S6]. He won the August primary with 51.5% (19,902 votes) to incumbent Sharon Tomiko Santos's 47.9% (18,517) [S8].

## Positions

### Housing
- Pamphlet priority: "housing affordability for struggling families" [S1].
- Supports rent stabilization; says he would have voted for the 2025 rent-increase cap that Santos opposed, and that if the incumbent "isn't willing to see how harmful it is for everyday folks, then I think it's time for a fresh voice" [S5][S7].
- Site: prevent displacement and keep South Seattle affordable for working families [S3].

### Education and childcare
- Pamphlet priorities: "quality childcare for working parents" and "early interventions that keep kids learning" [S1]; site: fully fund early education [S3].
- Criticized the Legislature for cutting about $300 million in education spending while giving up about $270 million in estate-tax revenue: "that doesn't make sense" [S7].
- Supports a school cellphone ban, citing results at Rainier Scholars [S5].

### Healthcare and debt
- Pamphlet: "interest limits on medical debt" [S1].

### Public safety
- Site: "safety built on community investment," including gun-violence prevention through community investment [S3].

### Taxes
- No stated position on the millionaires' tax found [S5]. His criticism of the estate-tax rollback is noted under education [S7].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- No policy statement found; endorsed by the Washington State Stonewall Democrats [S3][S4].

### Parents and schools
- No position on parental notification, records or opt-outs found [S1][S3].
