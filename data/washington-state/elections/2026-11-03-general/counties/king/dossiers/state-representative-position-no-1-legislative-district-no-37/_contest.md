# State Representative Position No. 1, Legislative District No. 37 — contest overview

- General election: incumbent Sharon Tomiko Santos vs. Kelabe Tewolde, both preferring the Democratic Party.

One of two House seats for the 37th LD, which lies entirely in King County (southeast Seattle, Chinatown-International District, Central District, Skyway, part of Renton). State representatives serve two-year terms.

This is among the most contested legislative races in King County. Tewolde, a first-time candidate, won the August primary 51.5% to 47.9% (King County final results) over Santos, the House Education Committee chair and the state's longest-serving House member. The sharpest policy split is rent stabilization: Santos cast the only Seattle-area vote against the 2025 rent-increase cap, and Tewolde says he would have voted for it. They also differ on a statewide school cellphone ban (Tewolde for it; Santos prefers local board control) and on Santos's education leadership, which the Seattle Times editorial board criticized when it endorsed Tewolde. Both are broadly progressive on taxes and services. Santos voted for the millionaires' tax and chaired the committee that advanced the 2025 parental-rights amendments (ESHB 1296). Tewolde has no stated position on those questions or on the three statewide initiatives. Differentiating axes: experience (27-year incumbent vs. generational change), housing (rent regulation), and local-control (school cellphone policy).
