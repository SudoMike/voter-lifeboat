## Background
Ted Cooke (Prefers Republican Party) is a senior software tester, team lead and certified project manager from Covington with a Bachelor of Theology from Beacon University [S1]. He was King County GOP 2nd Vice Chair (2020-2022), has been a precinct committee officer since 2009, and served on the Timberlane HOA board in Covington (2010-2011) [S1]. He has not held public office and lost this seat to Chris Stearns in the 2024 general election [S2]. His volunteer history includes Portable Academic Lapdesk (an education nonprofit), Vine Maple Place Women's Shelter, Union Gospel Mission and the Maple Valley Food Bank [S1].

In the August 4 primary's election-night returns he received 40.19% to Stearns's 59.61%; both advance [S6].

## Positions

### Taxes and spending
- "I'll freeze all new taxes and eliminate existing taxes on income, property, and fuel. I'll order audits of every state office and program and cut waste" [S1].
- His priorities page calls for stopping increases in government spending and lowering or eliminating the state gasoline tax [S4].

### Climate and energy
- His priorities page calls for ending cap-and-trade to reduce energy costs and lowering or eliminating the gas tax [S4].

### Housing and homelessness
- "I'll prioritize starter homes with yards over high-density apartments forced on us by Olympia" [S1].
- Proposes cost-effective shelters that are "sober, sanitary" and safe, with job counseling, and expanded state mental health services [S3][S4].
- To encourage building affordable houses: "Ease the Growth Management Act, streamline permitting & inspection, lower excise and B&O taxes and eliminate the capital gains tax" [S4].

### Public safety
- Says public safety comes first [S1]. His priorities page: police should "show up and be allowed enforce the law"; "Allow police to act on reasonable suspicion once again, not be held to the unworkable standard of probable cause"; and no one "who [has] shown anti-police bias" on use-of-force review boards [S4].

### Government
- Would "end unaccountable public-private partnerships and legislative commissions" and "replace DEI with merit in government hiring" [S1].
- His priorities page proposes precinct-level ballot storage, domestically made voting machines, bipartisan audits, and in-person voting with ID [S4].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- Pledges to "uphold the biological definition of gender" [S1].

### Parents and schools
- Pledges to "restore full parental rights in education" and put "a classical American education" first [S1].
- His priorities page says parents should always have oversight of children and education, that school curricula should be publicly available for parental review, and that schools should not "present sexually encouraging material nor seek to replace parents' values" [S4].
- His home page says education should focus "on the success of our kids, not on indoctrination" [S3].

## Endorsements
No organizational or elected-official endorsements found; his site carries a testimonial from the late Donald Tilly, a retired PACCAR audit manager and Marine [S3].
