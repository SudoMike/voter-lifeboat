## Background

Andrea Samonica Jarmon lists a J.D. from the University of Washington School of Law and B.A. degrees in English and Women Studies from the University of Washington [S1].
She lists her occupation as Judge, King County District Court [S1].
She reports fifteen years of legal experience as a prosecutor, an Assistant Attorney General, an administrative law judge, and an attorney representing families and individuals in criminal and civil matters [S1].

## Positions

- Judicial approach: she says courts "must be accessible, accountable, trauma-informed, and human-centered" and that every person should feel their matter was "fully, carefully, and fairly considered" [S1].
- Criminal-justice formation: her stated background includes both prosecution and representation of individuals in criminal matters; the statement does not describe a bail or diversion posture [S1].
- Court administration: she says that as a judge she reduced her dockets to allow more meaningful hearings, streamlined pre-trial hearing calendars, expanded the court's community engagement, and created civic engagement programs introducing young people to legal careers [S1].

## Record

- She is a sitting King County District Court judge seeking to continue in the position; the statement does not give the date she joined the court [S1].
