## Background

Claudia Balducci is the incumbent King County Councilmember for District 6 in East King County [S1][S2]. Her pamphlet lists earlier roles as Bellevue Mayor and Councilmember, jails director, and labor attorney [S1]. She holds a JD from Columbia University School of Law and a BA from Providence College, and lives in Bellevue [S1][S2]. She chaired the Council during the COVID-19 pandemic [S1].

## Positions

Her general-election pamphlet statement:
- Says she chairs the Regional Affordable Housing Committee and worked to plan for more than 200,000 new affordable homes across King County [S1].
- Cites transit-oriented development near light rail, Health through Housing, the Doors Open arts and culture funding, and light rail across the I-90 bridge [S1].
- Says she worked on homelessness and behavioral health services, public safety, parks and open space, "reproductive freedom," and defending immigrant communities [S1].
- Says she sponsored the charter amendment moving county elections to even-numbered years [S1].
- Says she will continue expanding affordable housing, "improving public health and safety through smart investments with accountability," completing the regional transit network, and protecting the environment [S1].

## Record

- Established and co-led the Regional Affordable Housing Taskforce and chairs the King County Affordable Housing Committee, which set countywide affordable-housing targets [S2].
- Proposed the early opening of the Eastside 2 Line "starter line" and championed Vision Zero policies [S2].
- Chairs the Regional Water Quality Committee [S2].
- The county's page credits her "Doors Open" legislation with nearly $800 million for arts, science and heritage programs, and her work implementing the Puget Sound Taxpayer Accountability Account for education [S2].
