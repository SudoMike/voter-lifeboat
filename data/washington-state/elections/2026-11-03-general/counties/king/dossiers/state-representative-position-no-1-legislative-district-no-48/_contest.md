# State Representative Position No. 1, Legislative District No. 48 — contest overview

## What this office does

State representatives serve two-year terms in the Washington House, voting on state budgets, taxes and all state law. The 48th District covers parts of Redmond, Bellevue and Kirkland plus Clyde Hill, Yarrow Point, Hunts Point and Medina (per the incumbent's campaign site; osman-salahuddin.md S3).

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Osman Salahuddin (Prefers Democratic Party) was appointed in January 2025 and won the November 2025 election for the seat with 70.08% (osman-salahuddin.md S2, S6). Challenger Jeffery Poppe (Prefers Republican Party) is a retired CPA running on no new taxes and fraud audits.
- Poppe's general-election statement adds attacks absent from his primary statement: "My opponent voted for 6 new taxes" and "received 72% of his campaign money from PACs, unions, and businesses" (jeffery-poppe.md S1).
- Salahuddin voted for the 2026 millionaires' tax (ESSB 6346) and the 2025 parental-rights amendments (ESHB 1296) (osman-salahuddin.md S7, S8). Neither candidate has a stated position on I-645, I-1 or I-638.
- Fundraising: Salahuddin about $82,000 raised plus $74,000 carried forward; Poppe under $1,000 (PDC).

## Differentiating axes

- **Taxes and spending:** voted for the millionaires' tax and cites "equitable revenue measures" (Salahuddin) vs. "No more new taxes!" and fraud audits (Poppe).
- **Experience:** short legislative tenure plus city council (Salahuddin) vs. first-time candidate (Poppe).
- **Reform:** Poppe pledges to accept only individual contributions.
- **Parental rights:** Salahuddin's ESHB 1296 vote; Poppe has no stated position.
- **Immigration, safety and climate:** only Salahuddin has a record or statements (immigration-proceedings sick leave, behavioral-health co-response, just transition).
