## Background

Jeffery (Jeff) Poppe (Prefers Republican Party) is a retired CPA and financial systems consultant with 40 years of experience; he says he audited a billion-dollar bank fraud for the FDIC, conducted audits for the Los Angeles County Grand Jury, and consulted for five Fortune 500 companies, the federal government, the State of California and large California counties [S1][S3]. He also owned and operated a Greek restaurant [S1][S3]. He was a director of the San Diego Taxpayers Association and of a homeowner association, and a delegate to Washington and national Republican conventions [S1]. He holds a BS from the University of Cincinnati and an MBA in Finance from San Diego State University [S1]. He has lived in the 48th District for 16 years [S3]. He has held no elected public office [S1].

## Positions

**Taxes and spending**
- "No more new taxes! Washington has an outrageous spending problem, not an income problem." He pledges to "reject any new or unnecessary taxes" [S1][S3].
- Pledges to "spearhead fraud audits" and make government "effective and efficient" [S1][S3].
- Wants state spending and performance metrics made publicly accessible [S3].

**Campaign finance**
- Pledges to accept only individual contributions [S1][S3].

**Change from the primary statement**
- The general-election statement adds two claims about his opponent: "My opponent voted for 6 new taxes" and "My opponent received 72% of his campaign money from PACs, unions, and businesses" (the candidate's characterizations) [S1]. The primary statement made the same pledges without those lines [S2].

**Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy; parents and schools**
- No statement found on either topic [S1][S3].

## Endorsements
