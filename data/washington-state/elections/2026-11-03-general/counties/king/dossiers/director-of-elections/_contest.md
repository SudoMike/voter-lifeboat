## What the office does

The Director of Elections runs King County's elections, including voter registration, ballot counting and results; the office is elected countywide for a four-year term, with a 2026 salary of $241,096 [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Julie Wise, director since 2015, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1][S2]. The contest is uncontested.
