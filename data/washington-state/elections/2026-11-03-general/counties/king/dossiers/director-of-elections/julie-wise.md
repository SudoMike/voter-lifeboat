## Background

Julie Wise is the incumbent King County Director of Elections, first elected in 2015 [S2]. She joined King County Elections in 2000 answering voter questions in the phone bank and later became Deputy Director [S2]. Her pamphlet lists her occupation as Director of Elections, 26-year election administrator, and farmer [S1]. She holds national and Washington State election-administrator certifications and an AA from Green River College [S1][S2].

## Positions

Her general-election pamphlet statement:
- Says that during her tenure the department added more than 80 ballot drop boxes, in-person voting centers, election materials in four new languages, ballot alerts, and "new layers of physical and cybersecurity" [S1].
- Names the Voter Education Fund, a Civic Engagement Internship and a Bipartisan Observer Program [S1].
- Says she will keep the department "steadfast, transparent, and nonpartisan" and will advocate "from Olympia to D.C." for access to voting [S1].

## Record

- Under her leadership King County went from 10 ballot drop boxes to more than 80 [S2].
- Pushed for prepaid ballot postage in King County, which the department says led to statewide prepaid postage in 2018 [S2].
- Added four non-English languages; the department now provides ballots and materials in seven languages [S2].
- Started the Voter Education Fund, a public-philanthropic partnership funding nonpartisan voter registration and education by community organizations [S2].
