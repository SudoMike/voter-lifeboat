## Background

Harry S. Steinmetz is a Des Moines City Councilmember running for King County District Court, Southwest Position 4, against the incumbent judge [S1][S2]. He holds a J.D. from Santa Clara University School of Law (1989) and a B.A. from San Diego State University (1985) [S1].

His pamphlet statement lists 36 years as an Administrative Law Judge, Judge Pro Tem, prosecutor and defense attorney [S1]. His campaign site gives more detail:

- criminal defense attorney and Deputy Prosecuting Attorney;
- Administrative Law Judge at the Washington State Board of Industrial Insurance Appeals, hearing workers' compensation cases;
- Judge Pro Tem;
- investigator for the Commission on Judicial Conduct;
- staff attorney to the Washington State Senate Judiciary Committee [S2].

These career details are self-reported. No independent source checked for this dossier confirmed the employers, dates or the jurisdiction of his prosecution work [S1][S2]. He does not currently appear on the King County District Court judges roster [S13].

His Des Moines council service has independent coverage. The Waterland Blog's first-round 2021 returns showed him leading for a Des Moines council seat [S5]. In January 2024 the council chose him as Deputy Mayor for 2024-25 on a 4-2-1 vote [S4]. In November 2025 he ran as the incumbent for Council Position 1 and led challenger David Denino 53.57% to 45.94% in the second count [S3]. His pamphlet describes him as "a twice-elected Des Moines City Councilmember" [S1].

## Positions

Judicial approach (self-described):

- Says he has drawn "on experience from every side of the courtroom" and has a "balanced understanding of our legal system and the need to apply the law fairly, impartially, and with respect for every person" [S1].
- Stresses judicial conduct: "Character matters. The conduct of a judge—both on and off the bench—either earns or loses the public's trust," and says he has treated court staff "with dignity and respect" [S1].
- Says District Court is often the public's first contact with the justice system and that every person should be treated "with dignity, respect, and fairness" [S2].

Criminal-justice formation, bail and diversion:

- He describes both defense and prosecution experience plus administrative adjudication [S1][S2]. His statement and site do not address bail, diversion or therapeutic courts [S1][S2].

Court administration and access:

- No specific court-administration proposals appear in the pamphlet or on the campaign site [S1][S2].

## Record

- Des Moines City Council: elected 2021 and re-elected 2025 to Position 1, and Deputy Mayor in 2024-25 [S3][S4][S5]. His site says he worked on public safety, municipal budgeting and long-term planning there [S2].
- No judicial rulings by him were located. His Administrative Law Judge and pro tem service is self-reported [S2].

## Endorsements

- His pamphlet quotes King County District Court Chief Judge Rebecca Robertson: "Harry is extraordinarily well qualified to serve on the court" [S1].
  - The sources reviewed do not say why Robertson, or the other sitting KCDC judges on his list, endorsed against a colleague.
  - The only documented context is the Commission on Judicial Conduct's April 2026 reprimand of his opponent [S6]. A "My Opponent" section of Steinmetz's campaign site describes that reprimand and links to the order [S2].
  - No endorser statement connecting the two was found.
