## What the office does

A state senator is one of 49 members of the Washington State Senate, serving a four-year term. Senators write and vote on state laws, set the operating, capital and transportation budgets, and confirm gubernatorial appointments. The 47th Legislative District covers parts of Kent and Auburn and all of Covington in South King County (Kent Reporter, cited in kristina-soltys.md S7).

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Democrat Claudia Kauffman (in the seat since 2022, and 2007-2011) faces Republican Kristina Soltys, a Covington City Councilmember since 2019. Only these two ran, and election-night primary returns had Kauffman at 58.72% and Soltys at 41.10%. Soltys has raised slightly more than Kauffman this cycle (about $208.9K vs. $196.0K in PDC-reported contributions as of early October). Both candidates run on affordability, housing supply and public safety, so the differences are mostly about means.

## Differentiating axes

- **taxes:** Kauffman voted for the 2026 tax on household income over $1 million (ESSB 6346) and writes that low-income residents pay far more of their income in taxes than billionaires. Soltys opposes any income tax and says Olympia has "a spending problem."
- **housing / local-control:** Kauffman pairs housing supply with renter protections against "predatory price gouging." Soltys favors permitting reform and market incentives and says the state should empower local communities rather than impose mandates.
- **spending:** Kauffman emphasizes the safety net, childcare and capital investments. Soltys emphasizes budget accountability.
- **experience:** Two Senate stints and committee leadership vs. city council service.
- **parental-rights:** Kauffman's only evidence is her 2025 yes vote on ESHB 1296. Soltys has no stated position, so this axis does not separate them on evidence.
- **Less differentiating:** Both fund police and pair it with treatment. Neither has stated a position on gender or reproductive policy, or by name on the three statewide initiatives. Soltys's radio comments on the income tax repeal are the closest either comes.
