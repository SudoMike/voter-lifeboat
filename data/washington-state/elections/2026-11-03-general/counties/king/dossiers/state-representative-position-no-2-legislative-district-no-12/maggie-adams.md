## Background
Maggie Adams (Prefers Democratic Party) of Snoqualmie is a first-time candidate; she is a Business Representative and Job Steward for IATSE Local 887, a delegate to the Martin Luther King Jr. County Labor Council, and a member of Actor's Equity Association; earlier she worked at Microsoft and Amazon [S1]. She attended Coastline Community College [S1]. Certified primary results: Adams 33.9% for second place behind incumbent Mike Steele's 41.1% [S4]; she led the King County portion with 50.77% [S5]. She missed the October 5, 2026 North Bend forum due to illness, and a statement was read for her [S6].

## Positions
- **Healthcare:** "Accessible, comprehensive healthcare is a fundamental human right"; would "work to bring access to full, comprehensive healthcare to every corner of the state" [S2].
- **Taxes:** pamphlet lists "tax fairness and inflation relief" [S1]; primary-season coverage reported she supports a millionaire's tax and higher taxes on high-grossing businesses [S7].
- **Workers:** living wages, worker protections and on-the-job safety standards [S1].
- **Education:** fund educators' wages and facilities; access to higher learning "regardless of economic status or origin" [S2].
- **Environment, water and data centers:** prioritize water conservation in drought and give communities and farmers "top priority for water resources—especially with data centers and out-of-state energy companies launching soon" [S2].
- **Housing:** primary-season coverage reported she would regulate vacation rentals and mandate affordable units in new construction [S7].
- **Public safety:** primary-season coverage reported she emphasizes community policing and de-escalation [S7].
- **Democracy reform:** supports constitutional term limits for elected offices and federal judges, and lowering the voting age to 16 for state and local elections [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found.
