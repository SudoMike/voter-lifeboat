## What the office does
One of two state representatives for the 12th Legislative District in the Washington State House, elected to a two-year term. The district covers all of Chelan County plus parts of Snohomish and King counties, so King voters (Carnation, Fall City, Snoqualmie Ridge, North Bend) are a minority of the electorate (see mike-steele.md S10, S12). Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets.

## District and race dynamics
Five-term Republican Mike Steele (capital-budget ranking member) beat back a challenge from his right in the primary, finishing first with 41.1% while Republican Adam James (24.9%) was eliminated; Democrat Maggie Adams, an IATSE union representative from Snoqualmie, advanced with 33.9% and led the King County portion with 50.8% (mike-steele.md S10, S11). Steele has raised $147k to Adams's $9k (mike-steele.md S9, maggie-adams.md S3). Steele's endorsements include the state labor council, WEA-PAC and AWB as well as the county GOP (mike-steele.md S3, S13, S14, S15); Adams lists none.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Steele voted against the 2026 income tax and his caucus site calls for banning one; Adams has said she supports a millionaire's tax (mike-steele.md S4, S5; maggie-adams.md S7).
2. **Record vs. renewal:** ten years in office and budget seniority vs. a first-time candidate.
3. **Healthcare:** Adams calls healthcare a fundamental human right to be extended statewide; Steele emphasizes choice, transparency and cost containment (maggie-adams.md S2; mike-steele.md S2).
4. **Housing:** Steele says taxes, fees and regulation make housing less affordable; Adams would mandate affordable units in new construction (mike-steele.md S12; maggie-adams.md S7).
5. **Political reform and data centers:** Adams alone proposes term limits and voting at 16, and puts community water needs ahead of data centers (maggie-adams.md S2).
6. **Parents and schools:** Steele voted against ESHB 1296 (2025), the law I-1 would undo; Adams has no stated position (mike-steele.md S6).
