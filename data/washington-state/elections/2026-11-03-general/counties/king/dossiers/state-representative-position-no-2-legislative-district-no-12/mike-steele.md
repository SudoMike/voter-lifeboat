## Background
Mike Steele (Prefers Republican Party) of Chelan has represented the 12th Legislative District, Position 2, since 2017 and is seeking a sixth term; he is a past Deputy Minority Leader and the ranking member of the Capital Budget Committee, and serves on K-12 Education and Consumer Protection & Business [S1][S2]. He served two terms on the Chelan City Council, is Executive Director of the Lake Chelan Chamber of Commerce and CFO of his family's apple and cherry orchard, and is a trustee of the Association of Washington Business [S1]. Certified primary results: Steele 41.1%, Maggie Adams 33.9%, Republican Adam James 24.9% [S10]; in the King County portion Adams led with 50.77% to Steele's 30.31% [S11].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** campaign priorities include "lowering tax burdens" and "responsible budgeting and practical fiscal policies that protect taxpayers" [S2]; primary coverage quoted him saying the state has a spending problem, not a revenue problem [S16]. His House Republican caucus site calls for action to "ban a state income tax" [S4].
- **Housing:** at the October 5, 2026 forum: "You cannot make affordable housing more affordable by taxing it more or increasing the fees or creating a regulatory environment that is unfavorable" [S12].
- **Transportation:** wants projects prioritized and finished "before we start something new and shiny" [S12]; opposes "policies that would significantly increase transportation and energy costs" [S2].
- **Healthcare:** "Expanding affordable healthcare options while improving transparency, choice, and cost containment" [S2].
- **Education:** supports STEM, career and technical programs, and clear academic standards with accountability for spending [S2]. His caucus site supports expanding school choice and "empowering parents" [S4].
- **Public safety:** primary coverage reported he opposes decriminalization [S16]; endorsed by the Washington State Fraternal Order of Police [S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1M):** No explicit statement on I-645 found as of 2026-10-08. He voted nay on ESSB 6346 [S5]; his caucus site calls for banning a state income tax [S4].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: nay on ESHB 1296 (2025), the law I-1 would undo [S6][S7].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- The King County Republican Party, which endorses him, recommends yes on all three; that is the party's position, not his [S15].

## Record
- ESSB 6346 (2026): nay on House final passage, 2026-03-09 [S5].
- ESHB 1296 (2025): nay on House passage 2025-03-12 and on final passage 2025-04-24 [S6][S7].
- No floor votes since July: the Legislature has not met in session since sine die on 2026-03-12 [S8].
- PDC (as of the 2026-10-05 update): $147,444.00 raised and $109,715.18 spent for 2026 [S9].

## Endorsements
- Campaign site: Washington State Labor Council, House Republican Organizational Committee, Washington State Medical Association, Associated General Contractors, APRNs of Washington State, WA State Building & Construction Trades Council, Children's Campaign Fund, Mainstream Republicans of Washington, Washington Farm Bureau, Washington State Fraternal Order of Police, PSE SEIU Local 1948, Retired Public Employees Council, WA State School Retirees' Association and others [S3].
- WEA-PAC (general) lists Steele, not his Democratic opponent [S13]. Also endorsed by the Association of Washington Business and the King County Republican Party [S14][S15].

## Scoring notes
- Ten-year incumbent and capital-budget ranking member against a first-time candidate [S1].
- Voted against the 2026 income tax and against ESHB 1296 [S5][S6].
- Endorsements cross the usual lines: the state labor council, the teachers' union PAC and a public-school employees union alongside business groups and the county GOP [S3][S13][S15].
- Lost the King County portion of the primary to Adams while leading districtwide [S10][S11].
