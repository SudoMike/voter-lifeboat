## Background

Hawkins lists a law degree ("Jurist Doctorate") from the University of Washington Law School and a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Washington [S1].
She lists her occupation as Judge and asks for re-election to Shoreline District Position 2 [S1].
Her statement does not give the year she joined the bench or describe her practice before it [S1].

## Positions

Her statement says she is dedicated to keeping the court independent and treating everyone with dignity and respect [S1].

## Record

Her statement says she has presided over the Shoreline/Kenmore Community Court program and worked with community organizations and the University of Washington to create a free life skills course [S1].
She reports creating the "Devon's Closet" program to provide personal care products, coats, shoes, and other necessities to justice-involved people without resources [S1].
She states she currently presides over the King County District Court Regional Mental Health Court and Regional Veterans Court at the Seattle and Kent locations [S1].
Her statement says she was elected King County District Court West Division Presiding Judge [S1].
It reports service on the court's GR 36 Committee and, at the state level, on the DMCJA Board of Governors and its Diversity, Therapeutic Courts, and Education Committees [S1].
