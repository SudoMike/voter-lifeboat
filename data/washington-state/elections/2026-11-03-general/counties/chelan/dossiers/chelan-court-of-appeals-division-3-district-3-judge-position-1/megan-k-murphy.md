## Background

- Judge of the Washington Court of Appeals, Division III, since January 2025, and the only candidate for this seat [S1].
- Gov. Jay Inslee announced her appointment on Jan. 13, 2025, to replace Judge Rebecca Pennell, whom the U.S. Senate had confirmed as a federal district judge for Eastern Washington. She was one of four applicants [S2].
- She was unopposed in the Nov. 4, 2025 general election for the seat and won 98.8% of Chelan County's votes, the rest write-ins [S3].
- Began her career in 2001 as a Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney, then moved to civil litigation in private practice [S1]. The Yakima Herald-Republic reported that she spent about five years as a prosecutor, then had a Yakima practice specializing in medical-malpractice defense before becoming a partner at Fox Ballard in Seattle, and had appeared before Division III in numerous cases [S2].
- Education: Eisenhower High School (1993); Claremont McKenna College (1997); University of Colorado School of Law (2001) [S1].
- Community: Yakima Town Hall and Downtown Yakima Rotary [S1]; board of the Yakima Symphony Orchestra since 2015, including terms as president and vice president; member of the Yakima chapter of Washington Women Lawyers [S2].
