## What the office does

The Court of Appeals hears appeals from trial-court decisions. Division III has five judges. Its District 3 (Chelan, Douglas, Kittitas, Klickitat and Yakima counties) elects two of them [S2]. Every voter in those five counties votes on this seat, so in Chelan County it is a countywide race [S1]. This election is for a six-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Appointed incumbent.** Judge Megan K. Murphy joined the court in January 2025 [S1], appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee to replace Judge Rebecca Pennell [S5]. A governor's appointee serves until a successor is elected at the next general election, and the winner of that election serves the rest of the unexpired term [S3]. Murphy was unopposed for the seat in the Nov. 4, 2025 general election, winning 98.8% of Chelan County's votes [S4].
- **Uncontested.** She is the only candidate for the full term this year [S1].
