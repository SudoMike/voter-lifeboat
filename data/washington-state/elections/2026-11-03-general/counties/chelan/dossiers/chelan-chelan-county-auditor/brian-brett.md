## Background

Brian Brett prefers the Republican Party [S1][S3]. He lists no elected experience [S1]. He reports a 34-year fire-service career, including 8 years as Fire Chief of the Wenatchee Valley Fire Department. He also lists work as a senior accountant and CPA at Cordell, Neher & Co. and with Brett & Son Delivery Service [S1]. He graduated from Eastmont High School and holds associate degrees in automotive technology and fire service administration from Wenatchee Valley College, a bachelor's in accounting from Central Washington University, and a graduate certificate in governmental financial management from Rutgers University. He is a Washington State Certified Public Accountant [S1]. He lists no current community-service roles [S1].

In June 2026 NCWLIFE described him as the "retiring Wenatchee Valley Fire Chief" [S5]. It reported the same month that incoming Chief Jon Perry was sharing duties with Brett and would succeed him when Brett retires at the end of the year [S6]. In September 2026 NCWLIFE called him "former WVFD Chief Brian Brett" when he served as master of ceremonies at the 25th-anniversary 9/11 remembrance ceremony in Cashmere [S7].

He led the certified August primary with 12,073 votes (60.92%) to Brandt Cappell's 7,746 (39.08%) [S4]. NCWLIFE lists the race among the area's featured general-election contests [S9].
