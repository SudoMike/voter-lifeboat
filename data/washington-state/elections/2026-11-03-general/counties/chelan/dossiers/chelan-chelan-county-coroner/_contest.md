## What the office does

The county coroner has jurisdiction over the remains of people who die suddenly without recent medical attendance, by unnatural, unlawful or violent means, under suspicious circumstances, from unknown causes, in a jail or prison, or whose bodies are unclaimed [S2]. The Chelan County coroner's office conducts 100 to 150 death investigations a year [S4]. Coroner is a partisan county office [S4].

## Race dynamics

- **Appointed incumbent.** Wayne Harris, coroner since 2006, announced in September 2024 that he would retire at the end of 2025 with a year left in his term [S4]. Chelan County commissioners appointed Chief Deputy Coroner Earl J. Crowe to replace him on Dec. 22, 2025 [S5]. Because the coroner is a partisan office, state law required the commissioners to choose from a list put forward by the Chelan County Republican Party [S4].
- **Why it says "Short and Full Term."** An appointee holds a vacated county office only until the next general election [S3]. The ballot therefore fills both the remainder of Harris's term and the next four-year term [S1].
- **Uncontested.** Crowe (Prefers Republican Party) is the only candidate [S1]. He received all 16,275 counted votes in the Aug. 4 primary, apart from unresolved write-ins [S6].
