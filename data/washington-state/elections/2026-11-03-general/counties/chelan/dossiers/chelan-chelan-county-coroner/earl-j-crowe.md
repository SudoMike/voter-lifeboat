## Background

- Earl J. Crowe (Prefers Republican Party) is the appointed Chelan County Coroner and the only candidate for the office [S1].
- Chelan County commissioners appointed him on Dec. 22, 2025 to replace retiring coroner Wayne Harris [S3]; his statement says he took office in January 2026 [S1].
- He joined the coroner's office in 2013 and was Harris's chief deputy coroner [S4][S5]. His statement lists 22 years in death investigations, 13 of them as Chelan County chief deputy coroner [S1].
- He is board certified by the American Board of Medicolegal Death Investigators and by the State of Washington, and served eight years on active duty in the U.S. Navy [S1].
- Education: master's degree in criminal justice [S1].
- Community: 10 years in the Rotary Club of Wenatchee Sunrise, including as club president and treasurer [S1].

## Positions

- His statement describes the job as working with families "during some of the worst times in their lives" and says he believes in "serving everyone with dignity and respect no matter their social and/or economic background" [S1].
- No other policy positions found as of 2026-10-08.
