## Background

Doug Miller prefers the Democratic Party [S1][S3]. He describes himself as a 50-year resident of the valley and a Wenatchee High School graduate [S1]. He has an associate degree from Wenatchee Valley College, attended Gonzaga University and earned a bachelor's degree in public fire and EMS administration from Eastern Oregon University [S1]. He has worked as a fire department administrator and assistant fire chief, regional airline pilot, college instructor in fire science, aviation and EMS, and small business owner [S1]. His community service includes volunteer firefighting, the WHS Band Boosters, Civil Air Patrol and Chelan County Search and Rescue [S1].

His elected experience includes the Wenatchee School District board, the Wenatchee City Council and a county fire commission [S1]. PDC records for the same filer (Douglas H. Miller) show him winning a Wenatchee City Council seat in 2009 and a Chelan County Fire Protection District 1 (Malaga) commissioner seat in 2017 [S6]. They also show him losing an at-large council race in 2021 and a Port of Chelan County commissioner race in 2023, and winning a Wenatchee City Council seat in 2025 [S6]. In January 2024 NCWLIFE described him as a "former Wenatchee city councilmember and Chelan County fire district commissioner" when he applied for a council vacancy [S11]. The email address on his candidate statement is "wencouncilrep4" [S1].

He placed second in the certified August primary with 10,636 votes (47.39%) to Rick Viall's 11,809 (52.61%) [S4].

- Cites his record in public office: "I have served you, the citizens of Chelan County, and protected the taxpayer dollars" [S1].
- Says investment and the interface with local and county agencies are "extremely important" and that public and private finance rules are needed "to safe guard the integrity of tax dollars" [S1].
- Quotes the state-law requirement that public funds in public depositaries be protected against loss [S1].
