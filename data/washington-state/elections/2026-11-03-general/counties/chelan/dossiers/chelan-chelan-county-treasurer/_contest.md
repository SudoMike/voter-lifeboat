## What the office does

The treasurer collects property taxes, safeguards and invests public funds and accounts for them publicly. In Viall's words, the treasurer "does not decide what taxes should be" [S6]. Miller's statement cites the state-law duty to protect public funds held in depositaries against loss [S1]. The county's web page lists David Griffiths as Chelan County Treasurer [S5]. He is not on the 2026 ballot, so the seat is open [S1][S3]. It is a partisan county office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Rick Viall (prefers GOP Party) is a Chelan wealth advisor and business owner who has not held elected office [S1][S6]. Doug Miller (prefers Democratic Party) is a former fire department administrator and assistant fire chief. He has served on the Wenatchee City Council and as a county fire commissioner, and won a Wenatchee City Council seat again in 2025 [S1][S7]. Viall led the certified August primary 11,809 (52.61%) to 10,636 (47.39%) [S4]. Both candidates emphasize safeguarding public funds, accountability and modernization. Viall stresses fiduciary and compliance experience, coordination with the Auditor, audit preparation and updated financial software. Miller stresses his years in public office, investment, and cybersecurity and "accountability upgrades" [S1][S2]. Viall's campaign site also offers property-tax lookup tools and research on the Chelan-Douglas Regional Port Authority's land holdings and Malaga tax-increment plan [S6].

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Record vs. renewal:** a long-serving local elected official against a private-sector financial advisor running for office for the first time.
2. **Spending (audit and restraint):** both use accountability language. Viall is more specific about audit preparation and scrutinizes the port's borrowing plan. Neither proposes program changes.
3. **Taxes, housing, safety, climate, reform:** no differentiating evidence. The office collects taxes but does not set them.
