## Background

- Jon Volyn is the presiding judge of Chelan County District Court and the only candidate for Position 1 [S1].
- He was a part-time judge from 2018 to 2022 and was elected to Position 1 in 2022 [S1]. He was then a Wenatchee attorney, the first to announce for the seat being vacated by retiring Judge Roy Fore [S3]. He won with 52.29% to Allen F. Blackmon's 47.37% [S4] and took office Jan. 1, 2023 [S5].
- Says he has more than 25 years of legal experience, tried about 100 jury trials, and presided over more than 30 as trial judge [S1].
- Says he has served on state judicial leadership committees on education, therapeutic courts, public outreach and court rules [S1].
- Lists recognition as one of the state's top 100 litigation attorneys, an outstanding service award, several pro bono service awards, a leadership award, and service as district bar president [S1].
- Education: B.A., Walla Walla College; J.D., University of Idaho. Member of the Washington and Idaho bars [S1].
- Community: Wenatchee Rotary Club member [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- "A Judge is a public servant. Everyone is equal before the law and must be treated fairly with respect and dignity." [S1]
- Says he "strictly follows the law and upholds the Constitution and Bill of Rights" [S1].

## Record

- **Therapeutic court (2024).** The Wenatchee World reported in August 2024 that Chelan County District Court judges would be able to divert a small number of defendants into a community-monitored program for untreated mental-health or substance-use problems. Volyn said it was "a way not only to help someone overcome a difficult issue in their life but also avoid a conviction" [S6].
- **Grace City Church noise citation (2023).** In July 2023 Volyn signaled he was inclined to agree with the church's attorney that the city of Wenatchee's noise citation had incomplete paperwork. He asked for more written argument before ruling [S7]. In August 2023 the city and church entered a stipulated order of continuance with Volyn, which sets the ticket aside if the church has no further noise violations for six months [S8].
