## What the office does

District court hears misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors (sentences of up to a year in jail and a $5,000 fine unless a statute says otherwise), violations of city ordinances, traffic infractions and preliminary hearings [S2]. This year's ballot elects Chelan County District Court Positions 1 and 2, each for a four-year term [S1]. The office is nonpartisan [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Presiding Judge Jon Volyn, elected in 2022, is the only candidate [S1]. In 2022 he won the open seat with 52.29% to Allen F. Blackmon's 47.37% [S3]; Blackmon is now the incumbent in Position 2 and is also the only candidate for that seat [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
