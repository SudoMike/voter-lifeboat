## Background

Aaron Young is running for the PUD board from Malaga in south Chelan County; PUD races are nonpartisan and he lists no party preference on the ballot [S1][S3][S4]. He graduated from Wenatchee High School in 1995, is an Eagle Scout, and graduated from the Ag Forestry leadership program (Class 28) [S1][S3]. He has worked 15 years as a tree-fruit grower, packer and shipper in Malaga, 15 years as a part-time EMT for Ballard Ambulance, three years as a U.S. Forest Service wildland firefighter in Leavenworth, and as a general contractor [S1][S3].

He lists eight years on the executive board of the Chelan County Republican Central Committee, past board seats on the North Central Homebuilders Association and the Wenatchee Farmers Market, 18 years as a volunteer with Chelan County Fire District 1 in Malaga, and current membership on the Chelan County Boundary Review Board [S1]. His campaign site adds 12 years on the Chelan County Planning Commission [S3].

In June 2026 NCWLIFE reported that Chelan County commissioners chose Brandt Cappell for the interim county auditor appointment "over former Chelan County Republican chairman Aaron Young" [S5]. NCWLIFE did not mention the PUD race. This dossier treats the two as the same person because the Chelan County Republican Central Committee role matches; no other source links them. (He is not Marty Young, the Chelan County Clerk candidate.)

## Positions

### Rates
- "I will protect the rate payers of Chelan County" [S3].
- Says he "will stand firm against rate hikes that burden families, farms, and businesses" and "will scrutinize every decision that could raise costs for the people who depend on the PUD" [S3].

### Data centers and large loads
- Names "increased demand on the power grid, data center growth, and pressure on local infrastructure" as the county's challenges [S1].
- "Chelan County is an attractive target for data centers due to low-cost hydropower. Aaron will ensure that large-scale commercial users don't shift the cost burden onto local residents and agriculture" [S3].
- Would "prioritize reliable power for residents and agriculture first" [S3].

### Grid planning
- Says growing demand "requires smart, long-range planning" and cites his land-use, construction and emergency-services experience [S3].

### Representation and governance
- Says he is "the only candidate in this race who lives south of downtown Wenatchee," and that Malaga, Squilchuck, Stemilt and South Wenatchee deserve a commissioner who lives there [S1][S3].
- "The PUD belongs to the people of Chelan County"; says the utility "must be run with full transparency and accountability to ratepayers" [S1][S3].

### Agriculture
- Calls tree fruit, orchards and local industry "the backbone of Chelan County's economy" [S3].
