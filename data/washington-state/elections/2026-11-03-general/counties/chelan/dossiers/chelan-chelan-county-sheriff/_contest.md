## What the office does
The sheriff runs the Chelan County Sheriff's Office. Its units named in this race include patrol, marine patrol, traffic, U.S. Forest Service deputies, the drug task force and a behavioral health unit. The office also responds to wildfires, floods and other critical incidents with partner agencies [S1]. It is a partisan county office with a four-year term [S1][S4].

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Mike Morrison, first elected in 2022 over Brian Burnett (17,300 to 14,358), is seeking a second term [S5][S7]. His challenger is Stan Langlow, a patrol deputy in Morrison's own office. Langlow came to Chelan County as a lateral hire from the Kitsap County Sheriff's Office [S1][S6][S7]. Both prefer the Republican Party [S1][S4]. Morrison took 16,168 votes (75.94%) to Langlow's 5,123 (24.06%) in the certified August primary. With only two candidates, both advanced [S3].

Morrison runs on his record: staffing and retention, new equipment and facilities, training above state requirements, grant funding and incident response [S1]. He has also promoted less-lethal tools and body-worn cameras paid for with federal funds [S8]. Langlow runs on leadership, deputy support and community connection. He stresses accountability, transparency and support for deputies and staff, and cites his patrol, SWAT and training background along with a business degree [S1][S7].

No candidate forum, debate coverage or organizational endorsement for either candidate was found as of 2026-10-08.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a first-term incumbent with 20+ years in law enforcement and a seat on the state sheriffs' association executive board, against a deputy with about 10 years in law enforcement who joined the office recently.
2. **Safety:** Morrison cites proactive units, a behavioral health unit and de-escalation tools. Langlow's platform talks about "proactive, visible service" and training but makes no specific enforcement or diversion proposals. The difference is mostly one of emphasis.
3. **Spending:** Langlow pitches "fiscal responsibility" and "value and accountability to taxpayers." Morrison cites stewardship through county budget pressures and securing grants. Both statements are general.
4. **Taxes, housing, climate, reform:** no evidence in this race.
