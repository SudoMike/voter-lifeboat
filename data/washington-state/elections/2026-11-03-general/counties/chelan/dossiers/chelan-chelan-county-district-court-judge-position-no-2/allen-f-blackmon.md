## Background

- Allen F. Blackmon is the incumbent judge in Chelan County District Court Position 2 and the only candidate [S1].
- He was a Chelan County deputy prosecutor from 1996 to 2022 [S1]. NCWLIFE reported that he spent 26 years in that office, mostly handling juvenile cases [S3].
- He ran for District Court Position 1 in 2022 and lost to Jon Volyn, 47.37% to 52.29% [S3][S8]. NCWLIFE reported that he had also sought a District Court judgeship in 2006 and 2018 [S5].
- In November 2022 the Chelan County commissioners chose him to fill the seat of Judge Kyle Mott, who resigned to join a private law firm [S3][S4]. NCWLIFE described the appointment as filling a two-year vacancy [S5].
- In the November 2024 election, the first vote on keeping him in the seat, he defeated Wenatchee attorney Jay Eyestone with 60.27% to 39.34% [S5][S6][S7].
- Says he has served Chelan County for almost 30 years combined as a judge and deputy prosecutor, and that he was raised in the county [S1].
- Education: J.D., Syracuse University College of Law; B.A., University of Washington College of Architecture and Urban Planning; Wenatchee Valley College; Wenatchee High School [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says he "strives to guarantee all decisions are firmly rooted in the law and the Washington State Constitution and the Constitution of the United States" [S1].
- Says he "ensures every voice is heard, including victims," and "imposes accountability with fairness" [S1].
- Says he has "created a culture of education and fairness in his courtroom" [S1].
