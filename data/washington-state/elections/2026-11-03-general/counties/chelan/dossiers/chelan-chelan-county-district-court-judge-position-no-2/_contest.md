## What the office does

District court hears misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors (sentences of up to a year in jail and a $5,000 fine unless a statute says otherwise), violations of city ordinances, traffic infractions and preliminary hearings [S2]. NCWLIFE describes Chelan County District Court as the court for traffic tickets, infractions, small-claims lawsuits and misdemeanor charges [S3]. Judges are nonpartisan and this seat carries a four-year term [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Judge Allen F. Blackmon is the only candidate [S1]. The county commissioners appointed him in 2022 to the vacancy left by Judge Kyle Mott [S3]. In 2024, the first vote on keeping him, he defeated Wenatchee attorney Jay Eyestone with 60.27% to 39.34% [S3][S4]. This year's election is for a full four-year term [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
