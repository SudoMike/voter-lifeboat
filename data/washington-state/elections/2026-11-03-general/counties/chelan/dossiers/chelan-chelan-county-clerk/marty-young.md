## Background

Marty Young (listed as Marty (Martin) Young on the 2022 ballot) is the Chelan County Clerk and prefers the Republican Party [S1][S3][S5]. He was elected in 2022 and has been a Republican Precinct Committee Officer in Precinct 120 since 2018 [S1]. In 2022 NCWLIFE described him as a DSHS program specialist and secretary of the Chelan County Republican Party [S6]. Before that he worked in business management, engineering operations and consulting, including as an operations and production manager responsible for department administration, budgeting and quality control [S1]. He holds an MBA from the University of Oregon and a B.S. in metallurgical engineering from the South Dakota School of Mines & Technology [S1]. He and his wife Jackie have nine children. They moved to Blewett Pass more than 28 years ago after living in four other states for his corporate job [S1][S9]. He worked the 2010 and 2020 Census, attends Grace City Church and is a regular Red Cross blood donor [S1].

He beat Sandra Arechiga in 2022, 16,456 (50.64%) to 15,978 (49.17%) [S5]. In the four-candidate August 2026 primary he placed second with 6,916 votes (30.72%), behind Arechiga's 8,553 (37.99%) [S4]. (He is not Aaron Young, the PUD District 1 candidate and former county Republican chairman.)

## Positions

### Office administration
- Says the office ensures court-record confidentiality, record-retention standards and legal reporting requirements. He says it processes grants "(reducing tax payer burden)" and produces monthly quality-control reports on its electronic document management systems [S1][S2].
- Values close connections with the other 38 county clerks and the office's subject-matter experts, saying "One cannot be so prideful to think you know it all" [S1].
- Says teamwork is a focus and that he has "great rapport with the union representative to build morale and efficiency" [S1].
- His campaign site asks voters to back "common-sense conservative experience & retained knowledge" and says he serves on the statewide clerks' legislative and education committees [S9].
