## Background

Sandra Arechiga prefers the Independent Party [S1][S3]. She has worked in the Chelan County Clerk's Office for 12 years [S1][S8]. She is a Family Law Court Facilitator. She prepares and clerks the non-contested dissolution calendar, works the front counter and supports passport processing [S1]. She was Jury Administrator for six years [S1]. Her campaign site adds Spanish interpreting and translation for Spanish-speaking residents [S8]. In 2022 NCWLIFE described her as a deputy clerk [S6]. She has lived in Chelan County for nearly 26 years, where she and her husband Omar raised three children [S1].

She lost the 2022 race for clerk to Marty Young, 15,978 (49.17%) to 16,456 (50.64%) [S5]. In the four-candidate August 2026 primary she placed first with 8,553 votes (37.99%), ahead of Young's 6,916 (30.72%) [S4][S11].

## Positions

### Office administration
- Says "the Clerk's Office is not about party politics; it is about the people," and pledges "transparent, reliable, and efficient service while ensuring accountability in every function of the office" [S1][S2].
- Calls her close 2022 result evidence of "strong support for experienced, nonpartisan leadership focused on serving the public" [S1].
- Her campaign site lists four commitments: transparency "not only within the Clerk's Office, but within all of Chelan County"; using her bilingual ability "to better address the diverse needs of Chelan County citizens"; respect and professionalism; and "working in good faith with Chelan County Commissioners" [S8].
- Her stated goals are to promote transparency, improve communication, make information and services more accessible, support staff and build "a positive workplace culture" [S8].
