## Background

- Kelly Allen is the incumbent Chelan PUD commissioner for at-large District B and the only candidate [S1][S3].
- She was elected in 2022, unopposed, with 98.79% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S3][S5], to the seat Dennis Bolz held for 16 years before deciding not to seek re-election [S4][S6]. She was sworn in in January 2023 for a four-year term [S6][S7].
- The PUD's commissioner page listed her as board president as of May 2026 [S3].
- She served on the Chelan City Council from 2016 to 2020 [S3].
- She owns the Kelly Allen Agency, an insurance agency in Chelan, and with her husband owns Allen Construction Services, Inc., a residential construction company [S3].
- Community: past president of Chelan Rotary Club; member of the steering committee of the Wenatchee chapter of Braver Angels, which the PUD describes as a group bringing Democrats and Republicans together on difficult issues [S3].
