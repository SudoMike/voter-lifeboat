## What the office does

A public utility district may generate, buy and distribute electricity and set the rates for it [S3]. Chelan PUD is run by five commissioners. Three represent commissioner districts and serve six-year terms; two represent at-large districts A and B and serve four-year terms. The commission sets policy, approves plans, budgets and expenditures, reviews operations and sets rates and charges [S4]. Only voters in a commissioner district vote in its primary, but voters of the entire PUD vote in the general election [S2]. District B is an at-large seat, so every Chelan County voter sees this race [S1][S4].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Kelly Allen, elected in 2022 and board president as of May 2026, is the only candidate, for a four-year term [S1][S4]. She was also unopposed in 2022, winning 98.79% with the rest write-ins [S5]. Her pamphlet entry thanks voters for their trust and gives no other statement [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
