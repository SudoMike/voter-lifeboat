## Background

- Robert W. Sealby (Prefers Republican Party) is the incumbent Chelan County Prosecuting Attorney and the only candidate [S1].
- Chelan County commissioners appointed him in 2021 to replace retiring prosecutor Doug Shae [S1][S4]. The Wenatchee World reported his swearing-in on May 27, 2021 [S3]. At the time he was a 59-year-old attorney who had practiced in Wenatchee for 30 years [S4].
- He was elected to the office in 2022, unopposed, with 98.23% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S1][S5].
- Licensed by the Washington State Bar Association since 1991; admitted in all Washington state courts, the federal Western and Eastern Districts of Washington and the 9th Circuit Court of Appeals [S1].
- Former active-duty U.S. Army; honorably discharged in 1986 [S1].
- Education: B.A., Columbia College (summa cum laude, 1988); J.D., University of Puget Sound School of Law (1991) [S1].
- Community: member of First United Methodist Church; past president and member of the Wenatchee School Board [S1].

## Record

- **Police use-of-force reviews (2023).** Sealby reviewed North Central Washington Special Investigation Unit findings on two fatal 2022 shootings by Wenatchee police. In a March 2023 report he found the officers' actions in the May 7, 2022 shooting outside Living Hope Community Church "necessary and performed in good faith" and legally justifiable [S6]. In April 2023 he announced that force used in the June 2022 Western Avenue shooting appeared legally justified and that no officers would face criminal charges [S7].
- In the Aug. 4, 2026 primary he received all 15,561 counted votes for the office, with 314 unresolved write-ins [S8].
