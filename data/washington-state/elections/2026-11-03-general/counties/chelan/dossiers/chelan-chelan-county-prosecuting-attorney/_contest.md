## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes criminal and civil actions in which the state or county is a party, defends suits against the county, and is the legal adviser to the county commissioners, county and precinct officers and school directors [S2]. It is a partisan county office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Robert W. Sealby (Prefers Republican Party), appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022, is the only candidate [S1]. He was also unopposed in 2022, winning 98.23% with the rest write-ins [S3], and received all 15,561 counted votes in the Aug. 4, 2026 primary, apart from unresolved write-ins [S4]. The contest is uncontested.
