Beginning October 27, voters registering or updating their existing registration in time for the November 3, 2026 General Election will need to appear in-person at any election’s office no later than 8:00 p.m. on Election Day, November 3. Notice of November 3, 2026 General Election A General Election will be conducted in Chelan County on November 3. Voters will receive their ballot with local issues based on the districts they reside in. The following offices will appear on the General Election ballot: State Measures Initiative to the People Initiative Measure No. IP26-645 concerns state and local taxes. Initiatives to the Legislature Initiative No. IL26-001 concerns parental rights relating to their children in public school. Initiative No. IL26-638 concerns participation in athletics at K-12 schools.
