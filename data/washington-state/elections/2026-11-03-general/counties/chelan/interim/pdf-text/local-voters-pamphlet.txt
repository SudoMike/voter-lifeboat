Vote Center Vote Center 3 3 Chelan County Elections is committed to ensuring that all voters have the opportunity to vote a private and secure ballot. An AVU is a voting assistance unit that enables voters with visual impairments, cognitive/learning disabilities, illiteracy, or limited mobility to vote independently and privately. An AVU presents your ballot on a monitor, you select choices by touching a screen, using a select wheel, or by using a tool. You can use headphones to listen to the text and prompts on your ballot. AVU’s can also be fitted with sip-and-puff tools. AVU’s are wheelchair accessible and screened from others to maintain privacy.
