## BallotTitle
PUBLIC SAFETY AND GOVERNMENT SERVICES LEVY LID LIFT

The City Council for the City of Cashmere has called for a
ballot proposition to provide for continual operation of essential government
services. If approved, this proposition would increase the City's regular
property tax rate to $1.5737 per $1,000 of assessed value for collection in
2027, and authorize a maximum levy rate increase of 9% each year from 2028 to
2032. The 2032 levy rate will be used to determine the maximum levy rates under
84.55 RCW.
