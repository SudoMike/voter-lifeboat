Statement

In 2013, I moved to Wenatchee with my wife Joy and our three daughters. Since then, I have had the honor of serving Chelan County as Chief Deputy Coroner and most recently as the appointed Coroner.
I hold a Board Certification through the American Board of Medicolegal Death Investigators Inc., something that only 297 individuals in the world hold. I work with families in Chelan County during some of the worst times in their lives, and I believe in serving everyone with dignity and respect no matter their social and/or economic background.
As the incumbent Chelan County Coroner, I speak for those who cannot speak for themselves while giving respect to the living. It is a privilege to serve the community I live in, and I would be honored to receive your vote.
