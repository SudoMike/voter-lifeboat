## Sandra Arechiga | Chelan County CLERK | party: (Prefers Independent Party) | website: sandraforclerk.com | email: votesandraarechigaforclerk@gmail.com
Biography

For the past 12 years, I have proudly served Chelan County through my work in the Clerk’s Office. I currently serve as a Family Law Court Facilitator, prepare and clerk the non-contested dissolution calendar, assist at the front counter, and support passport processing. I served six years as Jury Administrator, helping maintain a fair and efficient judicial system. Throughout my career I have supported court operations, financial obligations, records management, and daily office functions. This hands-on experience has given me a strong understanding of the Clerk’s Office and the importance of professionalism, accountability, efficiency, and public service every single day.

Statement

Chelan County has been my home for nearly 26 years. It is where my husband, Omar, and I have raised our three children and where I have built my career in public service.
I ran for this office with a clear belief: the Clerk’s Office is not about party politics; it is about the people. It is about providing accurate, respectful, and dependable service to every individual who walks through our doors.
My experience has given me a strong understanding of how the Clerk’s Office functions and how it must remain consistent, efficient, and accountable. Every day in this role requires professionalism, attention to detail, and a commitment to serving the public with integrity.
I want to thank the community for the support I received in my last race for Clerk, which was decided by less than one percent. That close result reflected strong support for experienced, nonpartisan leadership focused on serving the public.
When elected, I will continue providing transparent, reliable, and efficient service while ensuring accountability in every function of the office. I humbly ask for your support and your vote for Sandra Arechiga as your next Chelan County Clerk.
