## Background

Ashley White is a U.S. Army veteran and lifelong Cowlitz County resident who states a preference for an Independent party [S1]. She says she spent five years in the Cowlitz County Clerk's Office, the last two as a Lead Deputy Clerk "helping oversee daily operations," and that she now works at Cowlitz County Juvenile Court [S1]. No campaign website was listed in the pamphlet or found. The pamphlet lists no phone number [S1].
