## What the office does

The County Clerk keeps the records of the Cowlitz County Superior Court, protecting "the integrity and accuracy of the Courts records," and manages the deputy clerks who handle filings for the public [S1]. Both candidates frame the job as nonpartisan court administration, and both state an Independent preference [S1]. Four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Staci Myklebust, clerk for about 12 years, faces Ashley White, an Army veteran who worked five years in the clerk's office (the last two as a lead deputy clerk) and now works at Cowlitz County Juvenile Court [S1]. Myklebust's stated goal is an electronic filing system and more public services. White promises modernization, customer service and transparency without naming specific changes [S1]. In the initial primary count Myklebust led with about 64% to White's 35% [S2].

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Record vs. renewal:** a long-serving incumbent against a former deputy who promises modernization.
2. Taxes, spending, housing, safety and climate do not arise in this administrative race.
