## Background

Staci Myklebust is the incumbent Cowlitz County Clerk. Her statement says she has served as clerk "for the last 12 years," with "a combined 23 years of experience," and that she is a lifelong Cowlitz County resident [S1]. She states a preference for an Independent party (printed "Independant" on the ballot) and says she is running as an Independent "because I believe that this position is not about politics" [S1].

## Positions

### The clerk's office and the courts
- Describes her role as "protecting the integrity and accuracy of the Courts records" and supporting the deputy clerks who run day-to-day operations [S1].
- "It is my goal to implement an electronic filing system and increase public services, while continuing to be responsible and honest with the taxpayer's money." [S1]
- Wants to "promote access to the Courts and make the legal system easy and accessible" [S1].
