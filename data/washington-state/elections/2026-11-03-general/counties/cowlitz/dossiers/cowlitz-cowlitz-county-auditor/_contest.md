## What the office does
The Auditor runs Cowlitz County elections and voter registration, records documents and keeps the county's accounts.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Auditor Carolyn Fundingsland (prefers Independent), formerly the county's elections manager, is the only candidate. The pamphlet marks the candidate "Unopposed" [S1].
