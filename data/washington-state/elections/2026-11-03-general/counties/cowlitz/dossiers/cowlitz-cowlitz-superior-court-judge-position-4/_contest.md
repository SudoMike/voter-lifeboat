## What the office does

Superior Court is the county's general-jurisdiction trial court: felony criminal cases, civil suits, family law, probate and juvenile matters. Cowlitz County has five Superior Court judges (jill-karmy.md S2). The whole county votes. This seat is on the ballot for a two-year unexpired term because its previous holder retired and the governor filled it by appointment [S1][S2] (jill-karmy.md S4).

## Race dynamics

Judge Jill Karmy, appointed by Governor Ferguson in September 2025 after three years as a Superior Court commissioner, faces Senior Deputy Prosecuting Attorney Jacob Lervold, a career felony prosecutor [S1] (jill-karmy.md S4). Karmy runs on judicial experience and temperament. She has the endorsement of every sitting Cowlitz Superior Court judge, and in a 2026 evaluation poll of 65 respondents, published on her own site, 87.7% chose her for appointment (jill-karmy.md S1, S3, S5). Lervold runs on trial and appellate experience and a "follow the law, not make it" philosophy. He is endorsed by several elected prosecutors, police officers and Rep. Ed Orcutt; one endorser criticizes the governor's appointments (jacob-lervold.md S2). Karmy has raised about twice as much (jill-karmy.md S6; jacob-lervold.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Record vs. renewal:** a sitting appointed judge against a first-time judicial candidate.
2. **Judicial philosophy:** only Lervold states an interpretive philosophy ("apply the law as written"). Karmy speaks of fairness and impartiality in general terms.
3. **Professional formation (safety, narrowly):** Lervold's career is all prosecution. Karmy's mixes civil practice, municipal prosecution and a therapeutic court she co-created.
