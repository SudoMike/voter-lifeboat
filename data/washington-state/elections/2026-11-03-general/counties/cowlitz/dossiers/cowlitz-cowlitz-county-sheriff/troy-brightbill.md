## Background

Troy Brightbill, 51, of Kalama, states a preference for the Republican Party and is unopposed for the open sheriff's seat; Sheriff Brad Thurman announced in February 2026 that he would not seek reelection [S1][S2]. Brightbill has served 27 years with the Cowlitz County Sheriff's Office as deputy sheriff, patrol sergeant, detective sergeant, chief criminal deputy and undersheriff, and for the last seven years managed operations while commanding the Lower Columbia Major Crimes Team and serving as an assistant commander of Lower Columbia SWAT [S1][S2]. He says he has "worked to modernize our operations and increased our staffing" and will "provide deputies with the training, tools, and support they need" [S1]. PDC summary (updated 2026-09-17): $5,935.37 raised, $5,444.96 spent [S3].
