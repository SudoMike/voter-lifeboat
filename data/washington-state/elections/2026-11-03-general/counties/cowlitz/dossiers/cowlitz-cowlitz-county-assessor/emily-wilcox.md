## Background

Emily Wilcox is the incumbent Cowlitz County Assessor and states a preference for an Independent party, and is unopposed [S1]. The statement says "Fair and equitable property assessments, grounded in accuracy, consistency, and compliance with Washington law, remain my top priority" and that since taking office the Assessor has focused on "improving efficiency, transparency, and responsible use of taxpayer resources" through modernized operations and technology [S1]. The office has expanded outreach, including advance notice when appraisers will be working in an area [S1]. The PDC registration uses mini reporting, so no contribution totals are filed [S2].
