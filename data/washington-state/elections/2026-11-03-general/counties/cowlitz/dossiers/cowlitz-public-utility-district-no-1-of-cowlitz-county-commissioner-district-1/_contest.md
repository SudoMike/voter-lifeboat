## What the office does
The three Cowlitz PUD commissioners set the public electric utility's budget, rates and power-supply strategy; each is nominated by district but elected by the whole PUD, which covers all of Cowlitz County.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Commissioner Bruce Pollock, vice president of the Washington PUD Association, is the only candidate for a six-year term. The pamphlet marks the candidate "Unopposed" [S1].

None: uncontested, information only.
