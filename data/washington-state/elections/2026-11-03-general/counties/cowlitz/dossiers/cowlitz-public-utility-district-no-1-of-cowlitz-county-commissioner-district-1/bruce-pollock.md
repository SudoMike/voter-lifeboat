## Background

Bruce Pollock is a Cowlitz County PUD commissioner seeking another six-year term for Commissioner District 1; he is unopposed and the office is nonpartisan [S1]. His statement says he has represented Cowlitz PUD on the Energy Northwest Board of Directors and served five years with the Washington Public Utility Districts Association, now as vice president of its executive board and "slated to serve as President in 2027-2028" [S1]; KLOG reported his election as the association's vice president in May 2026, after serving as its secretary [S2]. He cites 45 years of business leadership and frames the coming period around "forecasted energy shortages and Washington State regulatory policies" [S1]. In September 2026 the PUD board adopted a 2027 budget with no rate increase, covering a projected deficit from reserves and citing a new Bonneville Power Administration contract starting in October 2028 [S3]. His PDC registration uses mini reporting [S4].
