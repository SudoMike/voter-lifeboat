## Background

Chelsea Chandler teaches science and STEM at Monticello Middle School in Longview and states a preference for the Democratic Party [S1][S2][S8]. She grew up in Montesano, earned a biology degree and teaching certification at the University of Montana, and served two years with her husband in the Peace Corps in rural Jamaica on agroforestry, sanitation and environmental education projects [S2]. She moved to Cowlitz County in 2014, worked on community health initiatives with Cowlitz County Health and Human Services, and managed the Longview School District's Teen Parent Program [S2]. Neither her pamphlet statement nor her campaign biography lists prior elected office [S1][S2]. She filed on the last day of filing week, giving Dahl his only opponent [S5].

## Positions

### County budget, grants and taxes
- Says the board should be "focused on creating economic opportunities, bringing investments into the county, ensuring public safety and affordability, being good stewards of our tax dollars" [S1].
- "Refusing outside funding does not keep money in taxpayers' pockets. We've already paid those taxes. If we don't compete for grants and other available investments, those dollars simply go to other communities while Cowlitz County gets left behind." [S3]
- "We can bring our tax dollars home and make them work for the people who paid them. That's not about bigger government. It's about smarter government." [S3]
- Says elected officials must "invest wisely, plan for the future, and make sure our tax dollars deliver real results" [S3].
- No statement found for or against a specific county tax, including the 2026 public safety sales tax.

### Housing and jobs
- Calls affordable housing and family-wage jobs "among the biggest challenges facing our county" and wants a county "where families can afford a home, where businesses can thrive, where workers can earn family-supporting wages" [S3]. She names no specific housing policy (zoning, permitting, subsidies or tenant rules) [S3].

### Governing approach
- "I'll listen to experts. I'll study the data. I'll ask hard questions. Finally, I'll make decisions based on facts and what's best for our community—not on political ideology." [S3]
- Pamphlet: "We deserve leaders who listen, bring people together, and focus on real solutions instead of partisan politics." [S1]

### Public safety
- Lists "strong public safety" and "ensuring public safety" among her goals without specific proposals [S1][S3].
