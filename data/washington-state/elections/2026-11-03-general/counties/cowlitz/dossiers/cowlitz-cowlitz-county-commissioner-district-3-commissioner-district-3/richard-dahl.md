## Background

Richard "Rick" Dahl is the incumbent Cowlitz County Commissioner for District 3 and states a preference for the Republican Party [S1][S6]. He won the seat in the November 2022 general election, defeating John Jabusch 23,115 votes to 20,772 [S7]; KLOG calls Jabusch his predecessor and describes Dahl as now the board's longest-serving commissioner [S6]. He retired from Fibre Federal Credit Union in January 2022 after a 39-year banking career, latterly as Senior Vice President and Chief Retail Officer over the branch network, consumer lending and the call center [S1][S3]. He holds a Bachelor of Science from Central Washington University and served for many years on the state recreation grant board now known as the Recreation and Conservation Office [S3]. He describes himself as a 30-year Castle Rock resident [S1].

## Positions

### Taxes and the county budget
- Website: "I have opposed every suggestion of a tax increase on the public - and I have been successful. Your hard-earned money belongs to you, not to government." [S2]
- Pamphlet: "I have refused to take more from your wallet," and says he "supported the sunset of a tax returning that money to the citizens of Cowlitz County" (the statement does not name the tax) [S1].
- When the board approved a 0.1% public safety sales tax for unincorporated Cowlitz County in September 2026, Dahl argued the county should reduce spending rather than raise taxes, and said governments seem to be "taking more and more, hindering business" [S4].
- Reelection announcement: he would "continue the goal of keeping government out of your pocket and out of your day-to-day lives!" [S6]

### Role of government and property rights
- "I believe the primary role of government is to protect your individual rights and freedoms." [S2] He describes his model as "Citizen led government, not the Government led citizen model currently in use." [S1]
- Website: "I have opposed any attempt to implement new restrictions or conditions on what you can do with your property. I have been successful in amending or blocking any such action at the county level." [S2]
- Pamphlet: says he opposed a new flood control ordinance that placed restrictions on property owners "having little to do with flood control," and that the result was "a much smaller document eliminating the harmful aspects" [S1].

### Homelessness and human services
- In a July 2025 meeting on the state Consolidated Homeless Grant, Dahl said, "The more we spend, the more they come. If that isn't the definition of enabling, I don't know what is," and he repeated that stance at the September 30, 2025 meeting [S5]. The board, Dahl included, then voted unanimously to accept the $11.7 million grant, which keeps distribution of the money under county control [S5].

## Record

- Elected District 3 commissioner in November 2022 [S7]; seeking a second four-year term [S1][S6].
- September 2026: voted against a 0.1% sales tax for law and justice services (courts, sheriff, jail, juvenile services, public defense, coroner) in unincorporated Cowlitz County. It passed 2-1 with Commissioners Steve Rader and Steve Ferrell in favor. It takes effect January 1, is expected to raise about $3.3 million in 2027, and is meant in part to address a projected $5.9 million county shortfall [S4].
- September 2025: voted with the board to accept the $11.7 million Consolidated Homeless Grant after questioning homelessness spending [S5].
- Says he opposed a flood control ordinance until it was narrowed, and supported the sunset of a county tax [S1] (no independent record of either was found).
