## What the office does

Cowlitz County is governed by a three-member Board of County Commissioners (Steve Rader, District 1; Steve Ferrell, District 2; Richard Dahl, District 3) [S4]. The board adopts the county budget, sets county taxes and fees, and passes county ordinances. It approved the county's new 0.1% public safety sales tax in 2026 [S3]. Commissioners serve four-year terms [S1]. Candidates are nominated by district in the primary, but the whole county votes in the general election: the 2024 District 2 race drew 56,821 votes from the 59,822 ballots cast county-wide [S2].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Richard Dahl (prefers Republican), a retired credit-union executive elected in 2022, faces Monticello Middle School teacher Chelsea Chandler (prefers Democratic), a first-time candidate [S1] (richard-dahl.md S3, S7; chelsea-chandler.md S2). The Daily News frames the race around what role county government should play in residents' lives (richard-dahl.md S10). Dahl runs on his opposition to tax increases and to new property restrictions. In September 2026 he cast the lone vote against the 0.1% public safety sales tax, saying the county should cut spending instead (richard-dahl.md S1, S2, S4). Chandler runs on attracting outside investment and grants, affordable housing and family-wage jobs, and a non-partisan, data-driven approach (chelsea-chandler.md S1, S3). In the initial primary count Dahl led with about 56% to Chandler's 44% (richard-dahl.md S8). Chandler has raised about five times as much as Dahl (chelsea-chandler.md S7; richard-dahl.md S9).

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Taxes:** Dahl opposes any tax increase and voted against the 2026 sales tax. Chandler takes no explicit tax position.
2. **Spending:** Dahl wants spending cuts and calls homelessness spending "enabling." Chandler favors pursuing grants and outside investment.
3. **Record vs. renewal:** a sitting commissioner against a first-time candidate.
4. Housing and safety are named by Chandler only as goals, without specific policies, so they separate the candidates weakly. Climate does not arise.
