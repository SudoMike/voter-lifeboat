## What the office does
The Treasurer collects property taxes and holds and invests the money of the county and its junior taxing districts (schools, ports, fire districts).

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Treasurer Debra Gardner (prefers Independent), in office eight years, is the only candidate. The pamphlet marks the candidate "Unopposed" [S1].
