## Background

Debra Gardner has been Cowlitz County Treasurer "over the last eight years," states a preference for an Independent party and is unopposed [S1]. The statement describes the office as "the custodian of all money for the County including our junior taxing districts, schools, ports, fire districts" and says that over the past four years the Treasurer's team "launched the Cowlitz County Investment Pool, increasing interest earnings while maintaining our top priority of safeguarding public funds" and implemented enhanced fraud-prevention measures [S1]. The PDC registration uses mini reporting [S2].
