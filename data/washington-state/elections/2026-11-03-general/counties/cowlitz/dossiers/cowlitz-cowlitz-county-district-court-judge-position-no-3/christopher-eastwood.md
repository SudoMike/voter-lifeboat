## Background

Christopher Eastwood is a Longview attorney who has served as a pro tem judge in Cowlitz County District Court since 2022 [S1][S2]. He says he has practiced law for nearly two decades in nine counties, as a prosecutor, a defense attorney and a civil litigator [S1][S2]. His campaign lists these roles: Kelso city prosecutor, Cowlitz County public defender, Thurston County deputy prosecutor, Longview senior city prosecutor, and private civil and criminal practice in Central and Eastern Washington [S2]. The pamphlet says he has six years of prosecution experience, almost all in Cowlitz County, and more than a decade in private practice [S1]. He taught First Amendment law as an adjunct at Gonzaga Law School (his site gives both 2018 and 2019) [S1][S2][S3]. He is a Brigham Young University law graduate, grew up in Central Washington, and moved to Longview in 2020 [S3]. The race is nonpartisan [S1].

## Positions

### Judicial approach
- "Good judges are firm, fair, patient, and impartial." [S1]
- "As your District Court Judge, I will faithfully and fairly apply the law, protect constitutional rights, and bring accountability with compassion." [S1]
- "Everyone who enters that courtroom deserves to be treated with dignity and respect, to be heard, and to receive due process under the law." [S1]
- Campaign: "The foundation of our justice system is the principle that the law applies equally to everyone, regardless of background, politics, or status." [S2]
- Stresses that he has been "on both sides" of criminal cases [S2].
