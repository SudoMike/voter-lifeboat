## Background

- **County work.** Has served as chief deputy treasurer in the Garfield County Treasurer's Office [S1][S2]. The guide does not give dates, and it does not say whether she still holds the post.
- **Banking.** Previously worked at the Bank of Eastern Washington [S2]. Holds leadership and customer service certifications through the Oregon Bankers Association; has taken courses in fraud prevention, risk management, records management, BARS (the state's local-government accounting rules), ethics and cybersecurity [S1].
- **Personal.** Grew up in Clarkston and graduated from Clarkston High School; has lived in Garfield County for eight years, where she and her husband are raising three children [S1][S2].
- **Community.** Concessions and market sale committee; Wine and Stein volunteer; hog barn superintendent; Pomeroy School District volunteer [S1].
- **Party.** Prefers the Republican Party [S1][S5].

## Positions

**Running the office**
- Says her banking background gives her "a fresh perspective, along with the ability to adapt to new systems, processes, and challenges" [S1].
- Would "maintain a consistent presence in the office" and lead with "integrity, transparency, dependability, and a commitment to putting people first" [S1].
- Wants a treasurer "who is accessible, engaged, accountable, and committed to serving the people" [S1].

## Record

- Ran in the primary as a write-in [S2][S4]: write-ins drew 230 votes to Summers's 593 [S3]. She was added to the general candidate list on Aug. 19, 2026 [S5].
- PDC mini-reporting option [S6].
