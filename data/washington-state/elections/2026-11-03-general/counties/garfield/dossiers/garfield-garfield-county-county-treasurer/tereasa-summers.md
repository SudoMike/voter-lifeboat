## Background

- **Office.** Garfield County Treasurer; her statement says "elected 2019" [S1]. The SOS results show her winning the November 2018 general, 940 to 386 over Karen S. Roosevelt [S3], and being re-elected unopposed in 2022 (975 votes) [S4]. She prefers the Democratic Party [S1].
- **Earlier county work.** Chief deputy treasurer, 2009-2019; 911 dispatcher for the Sheriff's Office, 2001-2009; 25 years in county government in all [S1].
- **Family.** Her mother, Cathy Summers, worked 30 years in the Treasurer's Office, 18 of them as county treasurer [S1].
- **Education.** Pomeroy High School; attended Walla Walla Community College; pursuing an advanced degree in accounting and business at the University of Phoenix; state continuing education in public funds investing, ethics, cash handling and fraud prevention [S1][S2].
- **State roles.** Representative to the Washington State Association of County Treasurers and the Washington Association of County Officials; serves on the newly elected officials, treasurer's education and nominating committees; chairs the County Finance Committee [S1].

## Positions

**Running the office**
- Says she "implemented a new tax system providing taxpayers online access to property tax information" [S1].
- Is working to offer "a monthly property tax payment option for taxpayers without escrow accounts" [S1].
- Says the treasurer "must be a careful steward of public funds while providing excellent service," and commits to "transparency, accountability, honesty, and integrity" [S1].
