## What the office does

District courts hear misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor criminal cases [S2], along with civil and traffic matters. Garfield County has one district court, in the courthouse in Pomeroy, with one judge [S3]. The seat is nonpartisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Judge Thomas W. Cox is the only candidate [S1]. He was re-elected unopposed in 2022 (1,029 votes, on all 11 precincts) [S4].
- **Electorate.** The court is county-wide: the 2022 race was on every precinct's ballot [S4].
