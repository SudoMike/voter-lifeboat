## What the office does

The auditor records deeds and other documents and keeps the county's accounts [S2]. In Garfield County the office also runs elections, financial reporting, payroll, vehicle and vessel licensing, budget preparation and accounts payable [S1]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Auditor McKenzie Lueck (States No Party Preference) is the only candidate [S1]. She was re-elected unopposed in 2022 (1,118 votes) [S3] and received 825 votes in the August primary [S4].
