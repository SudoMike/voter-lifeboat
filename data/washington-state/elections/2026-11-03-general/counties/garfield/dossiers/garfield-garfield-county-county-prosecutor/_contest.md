## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney is the legal adviser to the county's officers and boards, prosecutes criminal cases for the state in the county, and represents the county in civil actions [S2]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Prosecuting Attorney Matt L. Newberg (Prefers Republican Party) is the only candidate [S1]. He was re-elected unopposed in 2022 (1,036 votes) [S3] and received 772 votes in the August primary [S4].
