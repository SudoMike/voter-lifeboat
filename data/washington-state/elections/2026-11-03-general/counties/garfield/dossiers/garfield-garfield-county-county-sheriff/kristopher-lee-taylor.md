## Background

- **Current role.** Garfield County Sheriff's Office deputy since 2022; the office's armorer, aerosol irritant projector instructor, field training officer and search and rescue coordinator [S1]. President of the Garfield County Sheriff's Deputy Union [S1]. He states no party preference [S1].
- **Earlier career.** EMT with Garfield County Fire District 1 and EMT instructor with Lewis-Clark State College [S1].
- **Training and education.** Pomeroy High School (2007); EMT certification, Lewis-Clark State College (2017); Basic Law Enforcement Academy, Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission (2022) [S1].
- **Family.** Born and raised in Garfield County; he and his wife are raising four children there. His grandfather, William "Bill" Taylor, was Garfield County Sheriff from 1981 to 1993 [S1][S2].

## Positions

**Staffing the Sheriff's Office**
- Calls "retaining qualified deputies" one of the biggest challenges facing the office [S1].
- Would meet it by "focusing on opportunities for professional growth, building stronger local recruitment efforts, and creating an environment where law enforcement professionals feel supported and valued" [S1].

**Public safety**
- Commits "to keep our communities safe" and "strengthen our sheriff's office" [S1]. No statement on enforcement priorities, drugs, the jail or alternatives to arrest was found [S1][S2].
