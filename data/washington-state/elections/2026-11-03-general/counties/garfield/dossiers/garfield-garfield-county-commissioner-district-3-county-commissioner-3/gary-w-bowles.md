## Background

- **Public role.** In his second term as a commissioner of Garfield County Fire District 1 [S1]. He states no party preference [S1].
- **Emergency medical service.** Volunteer EMT for Fire District 1 since 2010; former EMT instructor for Lewis-Clark State College [S1][S2].
- **Education.** Pomeroy High School (1982); Walla Walla Community College, agricultural mechanics (1984); EMT certificate, Lewis-Clark State College (2010) [S1].
- **Community.** County events through labor, music, donations and cooking; the community Christmas parade [S1]. Born and raised in Garfield County; son of Virgil and Juyne Bowles [S1].

## Positions

**Openness and county government**
- If elected, will be "committed to transparency, accountability, and open communication," saying residents "deserve honest leadership and a government that keeps them informed and involved" [S1].
- Says "the best decisions come from listening to one another and working together," and wants to foster "collaboration and respect among county employees and the public" to "move our county forward in a positive direction" [S1].
- Says "leadership is about being present when your community needs you most" [S1].

No statement on the county budget, taxes, land use or energy was found [S1][S2].

## Record

- PDC mini-reporting option [S4].
