## Background

- **Office.** Garfield County Commissioner since 2015 [S1]. He won the District 3 seat in 2018, 848 to 470 over Bennie W. Keller [S2], and was re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S3]. He prefers the Republican Party [S1]. He chairs the board in 2026 [S5].
- **Regional and state roles.** Member of the Snake River Salmon Recovery Board, the Washington Counties Risk Group executive board (as he names it) and the legislative steering committee of the Washington State Association of Counties; board chair of the Voluntary Stewardship Program [S1].
- **Community.** Pomeroy Spinners Club; former Garfield County Fire District 1 volunteer; Sheriff's Office search and rescue; past president of the Pomeroy Gun Club; market sale committee [S1].
- **Personal.** Lifelong county resident; operates a farm and ranch with his family [S1][S6].

## Positions

**County finances and operations**
- Says "fiscal responsibility, transparency, and accountability are essential to maintaining the services and infrastructure our community depends on" [S1].
- Says he has stayed "actively involved in county operations, budgeting, and long-term planning," and is "committed to improving county departments, supporting employees," and finding "practical solutions" [S1].
- Says effective leadership "requires listening, involvement, and the willingness to have difficult conversations when necessary" [S1].

## Record

- As chair in March 2026 he presided over the board's flood emergency proclamation (Resolution 2026-09), which let department heads act on rising water and exceed budgeted expenses without prior board approval, along with a $30,000 levy shift to solid waste and two inter-fund loans [S5].
- Sat on the board that appointed Mulrony to District 1 in February 2026 [S7].
- Primary (District 3 voters only): 165 votes to Bowles's 171 [S4].
- Files full PDC reports and reports $0 raised and spent [S8].
