## What the office does

The three-member Board of County Commissioners is the county's legislative authority: it adopts the county budget, sets county policy and ordinances, and oversees county roads, property and services [S2]. This seat is for the two years left in the term Jim Nelson won in 2024 [S1][S5][S8].

## Race dynamics

- **Vacancy and appointment.** Commissioner Jim Nelson resigned for personal and health reasons in December 2025. The Garfield County Republican Party put forward names and the remaining commissioners appointed one, to serve until this election [S6]. They chose Vonda (Vonni) Mulrony on Feb. 23, 2026; she is the first woman to serve as a Garfield County commissioner [S7].
- **Primary by district, general county-wide.** District 1 is the Mayview, Ping, Tucannon and Ward 3 precincts [S8]. Only district voters voted in the primary: Mulrony 186, Blachly 119, in 4 of 4 units [S4]. Every county voter votes in the general [S3]; in 2024 the District 1 seat was on all 11 precincts [S5].
- **Candidates.** Mulrony, the appointed incumbent, prefers the Republican Party; Beau Blachly, a farm-supply sales manager and county planning commissioner, states no party preference [S1].
- **County finances.** The county is under a county-wide hiring freeze; in July the board granted the sheriff an exemption for two deputy positions [S10]. At a June 28 forum both candidates were asked about the county budget, alternative energy and county-city cooperation; the paper did not report their answers [S9].
- **Money.** Both campaigns chose the PDC's mini-reporting option [S11].

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: Mulrony is the sitting (appointed) commissioner and has served on the school, hospital district, health and equalization boards; Blachly has not held county office and runs on a private-sector agribusiness career, with service on the planning commission and the conservation district board [S1].
- `spending`: Mulrony stresses "strong financial oversight"; Blachly pairs a "long term balanced budget" with keeping road and emergency services at a high standard [S1].
- Property rights: Blachly states "a firm commitment to the rights of property owners large and small" [S1]; no rubric axis covers it directly.
