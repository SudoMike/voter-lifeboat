## Background

- **Current role.** Appointed Garfield County Commissioner for District 1 [S1]. The board selected her on Feb. 23, 2026, after interviews with the board and the Garfield County Republican Party, to serve until this election; she is the first woman to serve as a Garfield County commissioner [S2]. She prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- **Other boards.** Lists the Pomeroy School District board, the Garfield County Hospital District board, the Board of Equalization and the Board of Health, and is the Republican precinct committee officer for Tucannon precinct [S1].
- **Career.** Clinical dental hygienist; public health oral health program coordinator; program manager and professor of dental hygiene at Lewis-Clark State College; insurance agent at Obenland & Low Insurance Agency [S1].
- **Education.** M.H.R.D. in organizational leadership and performance; B.A. in social science; associate degree in dental hygiene [S1].
- **Community.** Pomeroy Pony Punchers, Blue Mountain Artisans Guild, 4-H Pirate Ponies, Second Harvest food distribution; a Garfield County resident since 1980 [S1].
- **Earlier race.** Ran for Commissioner 1 in 2020 and lost to Jim Nelson, 928 to 503 [S5].

## Positions

**County finances and governance**
- Her vision includes "a community where trust, transparency, and engagement thrive," and she says "strong financial oversight, and steady, informed leadership are essential for our county's future" [S1].
- Promises "integrity, collaboration, and practical experience" and "thoughtful, community-focused decision-making" [S1].
- Describes Garfield County as a place "where a work ethic and traditional values remain strong" and says she is running to retain her appointed seat [S4].

**Alternative energy and FEMA**
- During the appointment process the board asked each finalist about the commissioner's role, managing the county budget, alternative energy and Federal Emergency Management Agency participation [S3]. The primary dossier, reading the county's appointment record, recorded that she named financial responsibility and balancing the budget as central duties, raised landowner rights in alternative-energy decisions and was skeptical of county FEMA participation [S8]. That record could not be re-read for this refresh.
