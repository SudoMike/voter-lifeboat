## Background

- **Public roles.** Vice chair of the Pomeroy Soil and Water Conservation District board, on which he has served since 2016; member of the Garfield County Planning Commission; director of the PNW Canola Association [S1]. He states no party preference [S1].
- **Career.** 15 years as a sales area manager for WinField United (Land O'Lakes), including training company staff and customers' employees, after 14 years as agronomy manager at Pomeroy Grain Growers [S1].
- **Education.** Pomeroy High School; Walla Walla Community College (agricultural business); bachelor's degree from Purdue Global [S1][S2].
- **Community.** Youth projects, including cleaning and maintaining pioneer cemeteries; former Cub Scout and Boy Scout leader [S1].
- **Roots.** Lifelong Garfield County resident; his family's farm has been in the county continuously since 1879 [S1].

## Positions

**County finances and services**
- Says his corporate career taught him "to be results focused, with experience in management of large budgets" [S1].
- Will "work to build long term balanced budget funding to ensure continuation of the high standards of our community services such as our county road department and emergency services" [S1].

**Property rights**
- States "a firm commitment to the rights of property owners large and small" [S1].

## Record

- No county office held. Primary (District 1 voters only): 119 votes to Mulrony's 186 [S3].
- PDC mini-reporting option [S4].

## Scoring notes

- Challenger to the appointed incumbent; his public experience is on the conservation district board and the planning commission, and his pitch rests on a private-sector agribusiness career [S1].
- Pairs a balanced budget with keeping road and emergency services at their current standard [S1].
- Names property owners' rights in his statement; Mulrony's statement does not [S1].
