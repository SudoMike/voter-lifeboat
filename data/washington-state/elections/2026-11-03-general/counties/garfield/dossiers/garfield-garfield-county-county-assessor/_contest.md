## What the office does

The assessor values real and personal property for property taxation, as of January 1 each year [S2]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent, uncontested.** Assessor Brian Bartels (Prefers Republican Party) is the only candidate [S1]. He won the office in 2018 over Keshia Gilbert, 915 to 396 [S3], was re-elected unopposed in 2022 (1,010 votes) [S4], and received 757 votes in the August primary [S5].
