Statement

It is an honor to serve as your Garfield County Treasurer and give back to the community that has given so much to me. Born and raised in Garfield County, I have spent 25 years serving our citizens and understand the importance of experienced, dependable local government.
Public service has always been close to my heart. My mother, Cathy Summers, dedicated 30 years to the Treasurer’s Office, including 18 years as County Treasurer. Watching her serve our community with honesty, compassion, and integrity inspired me to follow in her footsteps.
Since becoming Treasurer, I have worked to modernize our office while preserving the friendly, personal service Garfield County deserves. I implemented a new tax system providing taxpayers online access to property tax information. I am also working to provide a monthly property tax payment option for taxpayers without escrow accounts, giving families another way to budget for their taxes.
I believe your Treasurer must be a careful steward of public funds while providing excellent service to every citizen. I remain committed to transparency, accountability, honesty, and integrity. Serving Garfield County is more than my job; it is a privilege, and I respectfully ask for your continued support.
