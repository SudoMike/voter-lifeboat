Statement

Being born and raised right here in Garfield County, I have a deep love for our community and the people who make it special. My wife and I chose to raise our four children here because we believe in the values that define our county - safety, connection, and that small-town spirit that makes this such a great place to call home.

I’m committed to working hard for the people of Garfield County to keep our communities safe, strengthen our sheriff’s office, and ensure that this continues to be a place where families, like mine, can thrive for generations to come.
