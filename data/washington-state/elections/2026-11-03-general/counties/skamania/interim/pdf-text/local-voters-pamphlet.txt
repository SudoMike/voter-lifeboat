Local Voters’ Pamphlet 37 Welcome to the 2026 General Election Voters’ Pamphlet. This pamphlet includes candidate statements and details about the state and local issues on the November 3, 2026 General Election ballot. Why Your Vote Matters: Your participation in elections is crucial as many important state and local races and issues directly impact your life. Your vote helps shape the future of our communities. Key Dates to Remember: • Ballots Mailed: By October 16th. If you don’t receive your ballot, contact the Auditor’s Office for a replacement. Please return your ballot as soon as possible. • Postmark Deadline: Mailed ballots must be postmarked by November 3rd to be counted. • Drop Box Deadline: Ballots must be deposited in drop boxes by 8:00 PM on November 3rd. Drop box locations in Skamania County are listed in this pamphlet and on our website. Need Help? For information on voter registration deadlines, correcting mistakes on your ballot, or any other questions, contact our elections department at 509-427-3730. Election Night Results: The Auditor’s Office will be open until approximately 8:30 PM to answer questions. Unofficial results will be posted on our website at www.skamaniacounty.org/elections as soon as they are available. Review the Pamphlet: Please take time to read through this pamphlet for valuable information on candidates, levies, ballot drop locations, and more. Your vote is an important way to contribute to our community’s future. Thank you for participating in the election! 38 Local Voters’ Pamphlet Voting information Accessible voting The Skamania County Elections Office is committed to accommodating voters with disabilities. Accessible formats of the voters' pamphlet (including audio MP3’s and a video voters’ guide) are available on the WA Secretary of State’s website. If you have questions or need help accessing the website, please contact the voter hotline at 800-448-4881 or email voterspamphlet@sos.wa.gov. Skamania County voters with disabilities have the option of casting their ballot independently using an Accessible Voting Units (AVU). AVUs are available in the Auditor's Office starting 18 days before Election Day, until 8 p.m. on Election Day. AVUs in Skamania County are wheelchair- accessible, screened from other voters to maintain privacy, and can be outfitted with headphones or sip-and-puff tools. If you have questions or concerns regarding accessibility, please contact the Skamania County Elections Office at 509-427-3730. Register to vote Registering to vote only takes a few minutes, and can be done in person, by mail, or online at VoteWA.gov. A Washington state driver's license, permit, ID, or the last four digits of your Social Security number are required to register online. If you need to update your address or name, please contact Skamania County Elections at 509-427-3730. Voting information Replacement ballots If you lose your ballot or it becomes damaged, you can obtain a replacement by contacting Skamania County Elections at 509-427-3730. Replacement ballots can also be printed online at VoteWA.gov. Returning your ballot You must sign and date your return envelope in order for your ballot to be counted! • Sign on the signature line below the "Voter's Declaration" • Do NOT sign someone else’s name, even if you have power of attorney. • Voting over the phone by having someone else fill out and sign your ballot is against the law. Voters can call us at 509-427-3730 to learn about the options for voting while they are out of town. • Drop boxes are available if you don’t want your signature to go through the mail. A complete list of ballot deposit locations in Skamania County can be found on the following page. Military and overseas voters As a military or overseas voter you have several options for registering to vote, receiving your ballot and returning it. If you are a military spouse or dependant, and you are away from home because of the military service, you also qualify as a military voter. Military and overseas voters are exempt from the regular voter registration deadlines. The last day to register and request a ballot is Election Day. Even if you are not registered, you may still vote and send in a ballot because your signature on the declaration attests to your eligibility and constitutes registration. If you register close to Election Day, or for some other reason did not receive your ballot, please request one as soon as possible to ensure enough time to return your ballot. Specify whether you would like to receive your ballot by mail, email or fax. Voters registered as military and overseas citizens may return ballots by mail, email or fax.
