## Background

- Incumbent Skamania County Sheriff, running as a Republican; in her fourth year in office [S1]. Her campaign site cites 22 years in law enforcement, nearly 20 of them in Skamania County [S2].
- Won in 2022 by 25 votes, 2,997 to 2,972 over David Waymire [S3]. The only candidate in 2026 [S1].

## Record

- Said in February 2025 that the loss of federal Secure Rural Schools money forced the office to cut two patrol deputies, after earlier cuts to animal control, detectives, dispatchers and corrections officers [S4].
- Proposed a public safety sales tax to restore an animal control officer [S5]; county voters rejected it in November 2024, 3,750 to 3,297 [S6].
- In 2025 proposed bringing the Search and Rescue, dive and other volunteer teams under sheriff's office oversight and expanding a general volunteer auxiliary [S7].

## Positions

- Says there is "more work to be done" and asks to continue her efforts [S1]; pledges to uphold the Constitution [S2].
