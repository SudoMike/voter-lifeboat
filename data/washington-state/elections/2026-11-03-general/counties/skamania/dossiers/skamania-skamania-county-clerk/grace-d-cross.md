## Background

- Incumbent Skamania County Clerk and Clerk of the Superior Court, seeking re-election as a Democrat; 20 years in the Clerk's Office, ten of them as the elected clerk [S1].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 (4,141 votes) [S4]. Led the 2026 primary 2,153 to 2,098 [S3].
- Raised $8,429.37 and spent $6,494.88 as of the PDC summary updated Sept. 30, 2026 [S5]; most of it is her own money, plus $1,500 from the Skamania County Democratic Party [S6].

## Positions

### Court records and service

- Says she modernized the office in her last term: more public records online, record preservation, electronic filing and electronic responses to jury summons [S1][S2].
- Prioritizes a well-trained staff, continuing education and technology for accurate, consistent service; staff give procedural information and referrals but not legal advice [S2].
- Says the office does not make economic policy but will keep working with the courts, county departments, businesses and community partners on accessibility, responsiveness and transparency [S2].

## Record

- Elected clerk since 2016 (ten years by her account) after about a decade on the office's staff [S1].

## Endorsements

- No endorsement list found. The Skamania County Democratic Party contributed $1,500 [S6].
