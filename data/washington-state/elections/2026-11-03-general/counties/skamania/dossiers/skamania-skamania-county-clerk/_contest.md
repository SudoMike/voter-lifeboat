## What this office does

The county clerk is the clerk of the Superior Court: the office keeps the court's case files and records, takes filings and fees, manages the jury pool and gives the public access to court records; it cannot give legal advice. It is a partisan, county-wide office with a four-year term [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Democrat Grace D. Cross (20 years in the office, ten as elected clerk) faces Republican Olivia D. Munsch, who has worked 14 years in the same office [S1].
- The primary was close: Cross 2,153, Munsch 2,098 [S3].
- Both are full PDC reporters and had raised similar sums by late September: Cross $8,429, Munsch $7,529 [S4].
- Both promise accessible, accurate, courteous service. Cross points to e-filing, online records and electronic jury summons responses added in her last term; Munsch to published policies and fee schedules, plain-language help, a patron feedback system and outreach to employers about jury service [S1][S2].

## Differentiating issue axes

- `experience`: the elected incumbent against a long-serving staff member of the same office who asks to lead it. Both cite long service; the difference is who has run the office.
- The office is ministerial; neither candidate states positions on taxes, spending, housing, safety, climate or political money, so those axes stay unscored.
