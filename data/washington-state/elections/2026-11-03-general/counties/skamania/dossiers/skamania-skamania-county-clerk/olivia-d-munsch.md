## Background

- Lifetime Skamania County resident from Carson, running as a Republican [S1].
- Eighteen years working for the county, 14 of them in the Clerk's Office [S1]. Her current position in the office is not stated in the sources.
- First run for office. Finished 55 votes behind the incumbent in the primary, 2,098 to 2,153 [S3].
- Raised $7,529.20 and spent $5,105.51 as of the PDC summary updated Sept. 27, 2026 [S4]; about $4,000 is her own money, and her largest outside contributors are Dave and Kathy McKenzie ($750 each) and Patrick Robinson Law PLLC ($500) [S5].

## Positions

### Court records and service

- Would publish clear policies, fee schedules and court-procedure information online and as handouts, to cut the time and cost of real estate transactions, disputes and civil filings [S2].
- Would help people understand procedures in plain language while staying clear of legal advice, and train staff to respond with compassion and equal care for all parties [S2].
- Proposes a feedback system for patrons on streamlining common transactions, and education for local businesses and employers on jury service to limit its economic impact [S2].
- Stresses the clerk's statutory independence and separation of powers [S2], and collaboration with other county offices [S1].
