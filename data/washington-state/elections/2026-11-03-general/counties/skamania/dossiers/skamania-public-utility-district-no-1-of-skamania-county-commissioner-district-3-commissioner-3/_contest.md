## What this office does

Skamania County PUD provides electric service across the county and water service in Carson and Underwood; a three-member elected board sets its rates, budget, borrowing and major projects and meets twice a month [S2][S3]. Commissioners serve six-year terms, are nominated by district and are elected by every PUD voter in the general [S1][S4][S5]. The PUD's 2026 residential electric rate is a $60 monthly basic charge plus $0.1045 per kWh [S3]. The race was not on the August primary ballot.

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Liz Green, the board's president, has held the District 3 seat for 12 years (unopposed in 2020) [S1][S2][S5]. Her challenger is Addison Dillon of Carson, a Skamania County native from the service industry on his first run [S1][S6].
- The race is about the cost of electricity. Green points to the rebuilding of a 90-year-old system (substations, water treatment plants, reservoirs) financed by bonds, grants and loans spread over decades, and to rising regulatory and power-supply costs [S1][S6]. Dillon says the residential basic charge has risen from $8.45 in 2009 to $60, the highest in the state, and calls for scrutiny of major projects before rate increases, a freeze on commissioner pay raises and a plain-language "Ratepayer Report Card" on every bill [S1][S6].
- The office is nonpartisan; neither candidate states a party preference [S1].
