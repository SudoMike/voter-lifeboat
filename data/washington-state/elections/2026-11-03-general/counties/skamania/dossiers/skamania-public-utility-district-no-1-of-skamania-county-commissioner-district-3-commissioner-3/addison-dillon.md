## Background

- Skamania County native and PUD customer living on Shipherd Falls Road, Carson; career in the service industry; first run for office [S1][S2][S4].
- Raised $2,559.56 and spent $2,470.15 as of the PDC summary updated Sept. 17, 2026 [S7]. His largest contribution is $500 from the Skamania County Democrats (June 15), although the office is nonpartisan [S8].

## Positions

### Rates and spending

- Says the residential monthly basic charge rose from $8.45 in 2009 to $60 today, about $619 more a year per household before any power is used, and that it is the highest basic charge in Washington; the PUD's 2026 rate schedule confirms the $60 charge [S2][S6].
- "Ratepayer Protection Before New Spending": before major projects, new debt or large expenses, a public explanation of costs, benefits and the effect on bills [S2][S5].
- Would support rate increases "only when they are clearly justified, fully explained, and demonstrably necessary," as a last resort [S4][S5].
- Would oppose any commissioner pay raise until rates stabilize [S2].
- Wants less exposure to "outside energy costs that can be unpredictable" and careful evaluation of major projects, while protecting reliability [S1].

### Transparency

- Proposes a monthly one-page "Ratepayer Report Card" printed on bills: basic charge and its change, average bill, comparison with neighboring utilities, revenue, expenses, debt, reserves, outage minutes, projects and votes affecting rates [S3].
- Would hold public listening sessions and meet business owners before major decisions [S5].

## Endorsements

- No endorsement list found. The Skamania County Democrats contributed $500 [S8].
