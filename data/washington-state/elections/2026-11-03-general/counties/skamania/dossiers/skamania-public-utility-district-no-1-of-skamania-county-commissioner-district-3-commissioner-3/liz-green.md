## Background

- Incumbent PUD commissioner for District 3 for 12 years and president of the three-member board; lives on Willard Road, Cook [S1][S3].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2020 (4,840 votes) [S4]. Files with the PDC as a mini reporter [S5].

## Positions

- Says rebuilding the PUD's 90-year-old system has been challenging and is "laying a solid foundation for the next 90 years"; the PUD is rebuilding substations and water treatment plants and replacing water reservoirs [S1][S2].
- Explains the financing: because the PUD sells at cost, it secured bonds, grants and loans and chose a rate structure that spreads the cost over decades so current and future customers both pay [S2].
- Says the regulatory environment is raising costs and the risk of power shortages, and that she has advocated for the PUD locally, statewide and regionally [S1].
- Supports coordinating projects with the county and other public agencies through a quarterly meeting so work is done together, reducing planned outages and costs, and says ratepayers should be "aware and involved" in PUD discussions [S2].
