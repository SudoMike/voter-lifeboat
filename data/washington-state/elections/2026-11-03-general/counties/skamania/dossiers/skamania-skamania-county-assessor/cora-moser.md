## Background

- Raised in Mill A and now lives in Carson; runs as a Republican [S1][S3].
- Twenty years as a paralegal, including real estate law, and more than eight years working for Skamania County [S1][S5]. She is not a certified appraiser; she says state law does not require one, that the office already employs certified appraisers, and that she would start the certification process if elected [S2].
- First run for office. Led the August primary 2,157 to 2,130 over the incumbent [S7].
- Files with the PDC as a mini reporter (no itemized contributions) [S8].

## Positions

### Property taxes and exemptions

- Wants the assessor's office to pursue property tax relief for homeowners: says she will join the Washington State Association of County Assessors' exemption committee and advocate for a state homestead exemption [S2][S5].
- "I will work hard to make our property taxes more affordable for local residents and businesses" [S3]. Says many owners struggle with "ever-increasing property taxes" and need "an advocate who brings a fresh perspective and puts property owners first," while acknowledging the assessor does not set tax policy [S5].

### Transparency and service

- Would explain valuations in plain language, keep an open-door policy, return calls and emails promptly and attend community meetings [S1][S5].

### County fairgrounds

- Opposes what she describes as the incumbent assessor's proposal to sell the county fair parking lot to a private industrial investor, arguing that the county's greater need is housing, that the county and the Port of Skamania already have vacant industrial land, and that relocating the county uses of the lot would cost millions [S2]. Her campaign site posts records she cites on the matter (a CERB presentation and grant application) and the Department of Revenue's 2025 levy audit [S6]. No independent news account of the proposal was found.

### Small business and tourism

- Says local businesses depend on tourism and that county land should be used to add events and visitors [S2].

## Endorsements

- Skamania County Republican Party (unanimous vote) [S4].

## Scoring notes

- Challenger on her first campaign, running explicitly for "change" and "fresh perspective" against a two-decade incumbent [S2][S5].
- Tax position is relief through exemptions (homestead), stated as an advocacy goal for the office [S2][S5].
- Contrast with Spencer on the fairgrounds parking lot is her own account; the incumbent's side was not found in any source.
