## Background

- Incumbent Skamania County Assessor, seeking re-election as a Democrat [S1]. He dates his start to 2002 and describes himself as "the assessor for the last 20 years" [S2].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 with 4,233 votes [S5]. Finished 27 votes behind Cora Moser in the 2026 primary, 2,130 to 2,157 [S4].
- Lives on Loop Road, Stevenson [S1].

## Positions

### Taxes and exemptions

- Has backed, through the state assessors' association, raising Washington's business personal property tax exemption: it was $3,000 when he began, the association won an increase to $15,000, and he would raise it to $50,000 "or potentially even higher" so small rural businesses keep more capital and spend less time on reporting [S2].

### Valuation practice

- Uses the sales-comparison, cost and income approaches and, for commercial property, takes "the lower middle of the data" as the final value so it is defensible and within statute; notes the county has fewer than 200 commercial properties and few comparable sales [S2].
- Says the appeal process is explained on every value notice and the forms are on the office website [S2]. His pamphlet statement stresses "accurate, equitable property assessments," integrity and his staff [S1].

## Record

- The Department of Revenue's 2025 levy audit of Skamania County (August 2025) reviewed about 40 percent of the county's taxing districts and found two requirements: the office used a $1.00 rather than $1.50 statutory maximum rate for fire districts in its levy-limit calculations, which caused no under-levy, and it did not certify total local and state assessed values to the taxing districts. It also recommended the assessor educate the county commission on certifying levy requests district by district. A follow-up review is set for 2026 [S3].
- His opponent says he proposed selling the county fair parking lot to an industrial investor [S6]. No account of the proposal from Spencer or from independent news was found.

## Scoring notes

- Long-tenured incumbent running on continuity, staff and statutory compliance [S1][S2].
- His tax-relief position is a business exemption increase pursued through the assessors' association [S2].
- The DOR audit is a documented record item; its findings are procedural [S3].
