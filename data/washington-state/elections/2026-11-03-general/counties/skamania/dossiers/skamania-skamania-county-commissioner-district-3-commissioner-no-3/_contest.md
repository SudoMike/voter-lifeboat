## What this office does

The three-member Board of County Commissioners is Skamania County's legislative body: it adopts ordinances and the annual budget, sets land-use and zoning policy, and through appointed department heads runs community development, public works and roads, community health, senior services and recreation; members also sit on outside boards. Terms are four years [S2]. Candidates are nominated in a District 3 primary (the eastern part of the county) and elected by all county voters in November [S2][S3][S4][S5].

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Republican Asa Leckie, the board's chair and an Army Special Forces veteran elected in 2022 (3,340 to 2,766 over Robert J. Hamlin), faces Democrat Paul H. Mosbrucker of Underwood, a former school board member on his first run for county office [S1][S4][S6].
- Leckie led the District 3 primary 775 to 629 [S3].
- Leckie runs on no new taxes, timber revenue and resource management, and pushing back on state mandates. He says he has never voted for or supported the county's annual 1% property tax increase [S1][S6]. Mosbrucker runs on housing scarcity and cost, insurance premiums, school cuts and slow job growth, and calls for "new leadership and innovative ideas" [S1].
- The county's fiscal context: a small taxable land base hemmed in by the national forest and Scenic Area, the lapse of federal Secure Rural Schools payments, and a 2024 sheriff's sales tax proposal that voters rejected (see candidate files).

## Differentiating issue axes

- `taxes`: Leckie's explicit no-new-taxes stance and his opposition to the 1% levy increase; Mosbrucker states no tax position.
- `spending`: Leckie's "fiscal discipline" and core-services budgeting.
- `housing`: Leckie's vote to open Underwood forest land to 10-acre residential lots and his stress on developable land; Mosbrucker names housing scarcity as a top problem without a stated remedy.
- `experience`: a sitting chair against a challenger running on "new leadership."
- `climate`: Leckie's push for more logging and lower energy costs, against state legacy-forest set-asides.
