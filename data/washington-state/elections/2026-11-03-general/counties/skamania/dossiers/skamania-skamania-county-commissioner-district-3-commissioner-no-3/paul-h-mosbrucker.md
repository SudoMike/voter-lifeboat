## Background

- Underwood resident of 22 years, running as a Democrat; first run for county office [S1].
- Cites "management experience" (not described further) and school board service [S1]. A 2009 White Salmon Enterprise report lists Paul Mosbrucker among the five directors of the White Salmon Valley School District board, whose district includes Underwood [S2].
- Trailed in the District 3 primary, 629 to 775 [S3].
- Raised $4,970.54 and spent $2,180.54 as of the PDC summary updated Sept. 28, 2026 [S4]; contributions include $1,500 from the Skamania County Democrats and his own money [S5].
- No campaign website was found, and the Chamber of Commerce's candidate Q&A printed no answer from him [S6].

## Positions

- Lists the county's problems as scarce and unaffordable housing, rising home insurance premiums, years of school cuts, slow job creation and a county budget "at the mercy of politicians who don't live here" [S1].
- Says the county has "the people with the skills to find solutions, but we need new leadership and innovative ideas to make it happen" [S1].
- States no specific proposal on taxes, spending, housing or land use in any source found.

## Endorsements

- No endorsement list found. The Skamania County Democrats contributed $1,500 [S5].

## Scoring notes

- Challenger running for "new leadership" against the sitting chair [S1].
- Names housing cost and school funding as problems but gives no remedy, so the housing and spending axes have no direction from him.
- Evidence beyond the pamphlet is biographical (school board, PDC); his positions come from the pamphlet alone.
