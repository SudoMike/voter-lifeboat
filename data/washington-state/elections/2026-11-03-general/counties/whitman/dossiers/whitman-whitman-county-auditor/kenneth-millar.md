## Background

Kenneth Millar, 47, of Colfax, works in Whitman County Information Technology. He describes this as supporting systems "where accuracy, security, and accountability are essential to daily operations" [S1][S3]. He holds a bachelor's degree in conservation ecology and biology and an associate degree in accounting, and has taken coursework in business finance [S1][S3][S4]. He was a recruiting manager for the 2010 U.S. Census in Brevard County, and has worked as a poll worker in a large voting precinct on many occasions [S1]. In the Gazette profile he lists himself as a U.S. Army veteran with past work for the U.S. Census Bureau and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, and as currently an EMT/firefighter and local business owner [S3]. He volunteers with local church and community groups and is married with four children [S3]. His printed pamphlet entry lists "No information submitted" under elected experience, other professional experience, education and community service; his background appears only in the statement text [S1]. He has not held elected office [S1].

The Gazette reported his candidacy in December 2025 and described him then as a Republican [S4]. He filed for the office in May 2026 stating no party preference [S2][S8].

In the fully reported August 4, 2026 primary he received 1,794 votes, to Crystn Guenthner's 6,041 [S6]. On primary night he said, "I am not throwing it away, as of yet. But whoever does win, I hope they can shake things up a little bit and make the changes that need to be made." [S5]

## Positions

### Auditor's office administration
- Says government "should operate with clarity and consistency so that citizens can have full confidence in how processes are carried out and decisions are made" [S1].
- Says the office "should continuously strengthen accuracy, transparency, and staff development"; he would prioritize reliable election information, "investing in the current staff's knowledge and training," and "open, honest communication with the public" [S3].
- "He views public service as a duty, not a career" [S1].

### Elections
- On concerns about ballot mailing errors under the current administration: says he "will strengthen the quality control procedures implementing multiple verification checkpoints, continuous process reviews, with a culture of accountability" so every eligible voter receives the correct ballot on time [S3].
- Commits to ensuring "elections, records, and financial responsibilities are handled with precision and fairness" [S1].
