## What the office does

The county assessor determines the value of all taxable real and personal property in the county, calculates the property tax levies that raise revenue for local governments, and administers property tax exemptions [S2]. The assessor is a partisan office with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent.** Wraylee Flodin, appointed in July 2021 and elected since, is seeking another term [S1].
- **Uncontested.** The Gazette reported that only one of the nine county positions on the 2026 ballot drew more than one candidate; the assessor race drew only Flodin [S3]. The pamphlet lists her as unopposed [S1].
