## Background

- **Appointment.** County commissioners appointed Flodin assessor on July 6, 2021, after the death of Assessor Robin Jones in June. She had been chief deputy treasurer after four years in the treasurer's office [S3]. Her statement lists chief deputy treasurer 2018-2021 and assessor from 2021 [S1][S2].
- **Elections.** She was the only candidate on the November 2022 general-election ballot for assessor, receiving 10,059 votes to 555 write-ins [S7]. The Gazette listed her as the incumbent, R-Colfax, when filing closed in May 2026 [S5].
- **Community.** Lists 4-H leader, treasurer of WSACA, and Rotary and Kiwanis membership; says she has lived in Whitman County for almost 30 years [S1].

## Record in office

- **2025 valuation notices.** In November 2025 Flodin addressed an error in value notices mailed to property owners in Endicott, Farmington, LaCrosse, Lamont, Malden, Oakesdale, Palouse, Rosalia, St. John and Tekoa; she said the system had written over a 15% increase applied the year before, with another increase applied that year [S4].
- **Exemption outreach.** The assessor's office page promotes a screening tool for the senior and disabled-person property tax exemption and notes the household income limit rose to $50,000 from $40,400 [S6].

## Positions (from own statement)

- Says she focuses on accurate and transparent assessments and that updating assessment ratios to meet state requirements brings property values "more in line with today's market" [S1][S2].
- Says she manages the yearly review of more than 40,000 tax parcels, has worked to modernize property records, and started outreach on tax-relief programs for seniors, people with disabilities and qualifying veterans [S1][S2].
