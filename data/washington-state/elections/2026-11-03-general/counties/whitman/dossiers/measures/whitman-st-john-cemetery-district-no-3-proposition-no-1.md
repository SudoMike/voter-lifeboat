## What it does

Proposition No. 1 asks for a special property tax levy of $20,000 in total, collected in 2027, "to be used for one year of maintenance and operation of the St. John Cemetery District #3" [S1][S2]. No explanatory statement was submitted, and no volunteers came forward to write statements for or against [S1][S2].

## Cost

- $20,000, an estimated $0.16 per $1,000 of assessed value (2026 valuation), collected in 2027 [S1][S2].
- Computed from the estimated rate: about $48 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- The district has a small regular levy ($0.07808 per $1,000, $16,731, for taxes due in 2025) [S5].
- Voters approved a St. John cemetery maintenance and operation levy in November 2024, 473 to 143 (76.8%) [S3]; DOR shows a $16,000 special levy ($0.07507 per $1,000) for taxes due in 2025 and no special levy in 2024 [S5][S6]. No St. John cemetery levy appears in the November 2025 general results [S4], and whether one is being collected in 2026 was not found.
- This request is $4,000 (25%) more than the 2025 special levy, at an estimated rate about twice the 2025 special-levy rate [S1][S5].
