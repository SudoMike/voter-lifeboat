## What it does

Proposition No. 1 asks for a special tax levy of $75,000 "for the operating fund, swimming pool, capital outlay, and/or cumulative reserve" of St. John Park and Recreation District No. 3, collected in 2027 [S1].

- No explanatory statement and no statements for or against were filed; the guide notes a hardship waiver on file with the county [S1], and the printed pamphlet lists the district among jurisdictions "waivered out of this pamphlet due to financial hardship" [S2].

## Cost

- $75,000, an estimated $0.2506 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1].
- Computed from the estimated rate: about $75 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- The district asks voters each year. Approvals: November 2022 pool operation levy, 457 to 146 (75.8%); November 2023, 315 to 114 (73.4%); November 2024 operation levy, 491 to 187 (72.4%); November 2025 pool operation levy, 342 to 112 (75.3%) [S3][S4][S5][S6]. In November 2025 a separate capital levy for a splash pad drew 264 yes and 177 no (59.9%) [S6].
- The DOR levy detail shows the district's special levy at $75,000 for taxes due in both 2024 ($0.30303 per $1,000) and 2025 ($0.29021) [S7][S8]. This request is the same amount.
