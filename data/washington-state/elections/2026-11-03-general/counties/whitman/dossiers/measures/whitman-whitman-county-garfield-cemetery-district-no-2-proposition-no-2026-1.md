## What it does

The measure asks for a levy of $105,000 by Whitman County Cemetery District No. 2, collected in 2027, "to help defray the costs of equipment, improving the sprinkler system, and for other operation and maintenance of the Cemetery" [S1][S2]. The printed pamphlet heads it "Proposition No. 2026-1" [S2]; the VoteWA record lists it as "Proposition No. 1" [S1], and the explanatory statement calls it "proposition 2027-1" [S1][S2].

- **What it pays for.** The explanatory statement covers operating and maintenance costs for the district's cemetery (WCCD2) and Silver Creek Cemetery: sprinkler updates and installation, tree removal and safety projects, grant searches for a veterans' memorial, columbarium development and headstone reset corrections, turf work (fertilizer, spraying), grounds equipment, shop interior updates for secure records space, and putting the website and burial records online [S1][S2].

## Cost

- $105,000, an estimated $0.82 or less per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1][S2].
- Computed from the estimated rate: up to about $246 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- The district asks voters each year. Approvals: November 2022, 247 to 117 (67.9%); November 2023, 175 to 82 (68.1%); November 2024, 259 to 181 (58.9%); November 2025, 174 to 110 (61.3%) [S3][S4][S5][S6].
- The DOR levy detail shows the district's special levy at $140,000 ($1.53194 per $1,000) for taxes due in 2024 and $120,000 ($1.26244) for 2025 [S7][S8]. The board's statement says "the board has been able to reduce the requested amount this year" [S1][S2].

## Arguments for

The statement for, by Linda Kildew for the board, says the levy would fund "continued employee funding, fuel, water, electricity, and maintenance costs," grounds and sprinkler work, tree removal, turf management and veterans' memorial plans, and the upkeep and planned revival of Silver Creek Cemetery, including mapping and headstone updates [S1][S2].

## Arguments against

None filed: "After repeated recruitment attempts, no volunteers came forward to write a statement against the ballot measure" [S1][S2].

## Endorsements and campaign

The district board supports it [S1][S2]. No committee for or against was found in PDC filings [S9].
