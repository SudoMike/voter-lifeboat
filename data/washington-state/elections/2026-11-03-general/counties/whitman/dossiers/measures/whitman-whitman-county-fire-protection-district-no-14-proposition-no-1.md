## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would set Whitman County Fire Protection District No. 14's regular property tax levy at $1.08 per $1,000 of assessed value, assessed in 2026 for collection in 2027. The money would "maintain and improve fire protection, and life safety services, replace apparatus and equipment, and provide for firefighter safety." The 2027 dollar amount becomes the base for later limits under chapter 84.55 RCW. The board adopted Resolution No. 26-03 [S1].

- **The district.** The county assessor's subfund for the district is named "FIRE DIST. #14 COLTON REG" [S3].
- No explanatory statement and no statements for or against were filed; the guide notes a hardship waiver on file with the county [S1], and the printed pamphlet lists the district among jurisdictions "waivered out of this pamphlet due to financial hardship" [S2].

## Cost

- $1.08 per $1,000 of assessed value for 2027 collection [S1].
- **Current rate.** The Department of Revenue's levy detail shows the district's regular levy at $0.63305 per $1,000 ($121,183) for taxes due in 2025 and $0.67815 ($119,983) for 2024 [S4][S5]. A 2026 rate was not found in a readable source. Against the 2025 rate, $1.08 is about 71% higher.
- Computed from those two rates (not a district figure): about $324 a year on a $300,000 property at $1.08, against about $190 at the 2025 rate, roughly $134 more.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- This is a levy lid lift on the district's existing regular fire levy. The DOR table lists a 1% levy-limit increase for the district and a statutory maximum rate of $1.50 per $1,000; the rate fell from $0.67815 to $0.63305 between 2024 and 2025 while the dollar levy rose about 1% [S4][S5].
- No Fire District 14 measure appears in the November 2022, 2023, 2024 or 2025 Whitman County general election results [S6][S7][S8][S9].
