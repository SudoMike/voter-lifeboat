## What it does

A one-year excess levy in 2026, for collection in 2027, to raise $30,000 "for General Operations, Improvement of the Town Streets and the Water/Sewer Systems." The explanatory statement says it "would continue the current level of support" for those purposes [S1][S2].

## Cost

$30,000. The ballot title gives the rate as "approximately .53¢ per $1000.00 of assessed value," which, read with the town's earlier levies, means about $0.53 per $1,000 (about $53 a year per $100,000) [S1][S2][S5][S6].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Colton has asked for a $30,000 levy each year, and voters have approved it each time:

| Election | Measure | Est. rate per $1,000 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 2022 | Levy for general operations, streets and water/sewer | n/a | 169-54 [S8] |
| Nov. 2023 | Proposition No. 815 | n/a | 114-29 [S4] |
| Nov. 2024 | Proposition No. 853 ($30,000) | $0.80 | 179-71 [S5][S7] |
| Nov. 2025 | Proposition No. 872 ($30,000) | $0.71 | 95-34 [S3][S6] |

The dollar amount is unchanged and the estimated rate is lower than in 2024 and 2025 [S1][S5][S6].
