## What it does

A one-year excess property tax levy of $72,000, collected in 2027, "to finance street maintenance and repair and street improvements in the Town of Garfield" [S1][S2][S3]. The explanatory statement lists powering streetlights, grading and oiling gravel roads, repairing potholes, roadside weed control, snow plowing and sanding, and public works salaries for street maintenance [S1][S2]. The council adopted Resolution No. 2026-13 on July 8, 2026, finding "a great need" that "requires a special levy" [S3].

## Cost

$72,000, an estimated $2.24 per $1,000 of assessed value (about $224 a year per $100,000) [S1][S2][S3].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

The town says "The Street Fund is the only fund that does not generate its own revenue; all street-related operations are solely supported through the passage of the Street Levy" [S1][S2]. The levy has gone to voters each year since at least 2023:

| Election | Amount | Est. rate per $1,000 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov. 2023 | n/a | n/a | 129-58 [S7] |
| Aug. 2024 | n/a | n/a | 118-57 [S6] |
| Nov. 2025 | $72,000 | $2.50 | 140-77 [S4][S5] |
| Nov. 2026 | $72,000 | $2.24 | [S3] |

The amount is unchanged from last year and the estimated rate is lower [S3][S4].

## Arguments for

Statement for, by Rebecca Pfaff, the town's clerk/treasurer [S1][S3]: street improvements and maintenance "depend on these levy funds to perform essential services. If you value well-maintained and snow plowed streets within the Town of Garfield, please support and vote Yes for the 2027 Street Levy."

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted; no volunteers came forward [S1][S2].
