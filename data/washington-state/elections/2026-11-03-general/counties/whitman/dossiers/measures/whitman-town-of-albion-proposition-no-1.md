## What it does

A one-year excess property tax levy of $20,000, collected in 2027, "for fire protection, law enforcement services, emergency response, and other lawful public safety purposes" in the Town of Albion [S1][S2]. Funds may also go to public safety equipment and training [S1][S2].

## Cost

$20,000, an estimated $0.459 per $1,000 of assessed value, about $45.90 a year on a property assessed at $100,000 [S1][S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Albion asked for the same amount for the same purpose in 2023: Resolution No. 23-04, a one-year levy for police, fire and code enforcement services, $20,000 at about $0.86 per $1,000 [S4]. Voters approved it 107 to 38 (74%) [S3]. No Albion levy appeared on the November 2024 or November 2025 general election ballots [S5][S6]. The 2026 rate estimate ($0.459) is about half the 2023 estimate ($0.86) for the same dollar amount [S1][S4].

## Arguments for

Statement for, by Corina Anderson (spelled "Andersen" in the printed pamphlet) [S1][S2]: approval "will provide dedicated funding to help maintain fire protection, law enforcement services, emergency response, and other essential public safety services for the Town of Albion."

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted; no volunteers came forward [S1][S2].

## Endorsements and campaign

None found.

## Lean notes

- `taxes`: a yes vote adds a one-year $20,000 excess levy; Albion's last such levy was approved in 2023 and none was on the 2024 or 2025 general ballots, so it is not a continuation of a yearly levy [S1][S3][S5][S6]. Direction +2.
- `safety`: the levy funds existing fire, law enforcement and emergency services; no source shows an expansion [S1]. Not mapped.
