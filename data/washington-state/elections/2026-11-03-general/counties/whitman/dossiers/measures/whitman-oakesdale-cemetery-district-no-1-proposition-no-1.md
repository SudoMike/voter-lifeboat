## What it does

Proposition No. 1 asks for a special levy of $70,000, collected in 2027, "for the continued improvements and maintenance of the Oakesdale Cemetery District #1" [S1].

- No explanatory statement and no statements for or against were filed; the guide notes a hardship waiver on file with the county [S1], and the printed pamphlet lists the district among jurisdictions "waivered out of this pamphlet due to financial hardship" [S2].

## Cost

- $70,000, an estimated $0.35 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1].
- Computed from the estimated rate: about $105 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- The district asks voters each year. Approvals: November 2022, 227 to 122 (65.0%); November 2023, 211 to 79 (72.8%); November 2024, 269 to 153 (63.7%); November 2025, 186 to 75 (71.3%) [S3][S4][S5][S6].
- The DOR levy detail shows the district's special levy at $70,531 ($0.46051 per $1,000) for taxes due in 2024 and $70,000 ($0.45167) for 2025 [S7][S8]. This request is the same amount.
