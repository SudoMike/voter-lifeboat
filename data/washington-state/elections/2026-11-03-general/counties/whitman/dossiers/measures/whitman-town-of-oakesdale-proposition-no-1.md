## What it does

A one-year special property tax levy of $14,000 "for public safety to fund fire protection and emergency medical services for the Town of Oakesdale," levied on 2026 assessed value for collection in 2027 [S1]. The measure does not appear in the printed local voters' pamphlet; the VoteWA record says no explanatory statement or statements for or against were submitted and that a hardship waiver is on file with the county [S1].

## Cost

- $14,000, an estimated $0.46 per $1,000 of 2026 assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1].
- Oakesdale's Proposition No. 2 on the same ballot asks for a separate $60,000 street levy at an estimated $1.93 per $1,000 [S1]; together the two would be about $2.39 per $1,000.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

MRSC describes excess levies under RCW 84.52.052 as separate from the regular levy, expiring after one year for all agencies except fire protection districts, and requiring a 60% majority [S6].

- **Prior Oakesdale levies.** Oakesdale voters approved a "Public Safety Levy" in November 2024 (207 yes, 46 no, 81.8%) along with two street levies [S2], and approved town Propositions No. 1 and No. 2 in November 2023 (89.1% and 78.8% yes) [S3]. The November 2025 general ballot carried no Oakesdale town levy [S4].
- For 2025 collection the Department of Revenue lists an Oakesdale "Special" levy of $124,000 at $4.19 per $1,000, in addition to the town's regular levy of $56,925 at $1.85 per $1,000 [S5].
- The sources found do not say whether the 2024 public safety levy had the same amount or purpose, or how the town provides fire and EMS service.
