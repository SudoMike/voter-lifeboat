## What it does

A one-year excess property tax levy under RCW 84.52.052 "for the purpose of funding the operation and maintenance of the infrastructure", levied on 2026 assessed value for collection in 2027, as provided in Resolution 2026-08 [S1][S2]. The council adopted the resolution on July 28, 2026; its title describes "a one-year excess property tax levy" [S4]. The explanatory statement says the city "is seeking voter support for the continued operation and maintenance of City infrastructure" [S1].

## Cost

- $55,000, collected in 2027 [S1][S2].
- **The two official texts give different rates.** The ballot title (and the resolution) estimate $0.73058 per $1,000 of assessed value [S1][S4]; the explanatory statement gives $0.5912 per $1,000 [S1][S2]. Both give the same dollar amount. The sources found do not explain the difference.
- The ballot-title rate matches the per-$100,000 cost the Daily News reported for the same $55,000 levy in 2024 ($73 per $100,000) [S5].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Under RCW 84.52.052, a city or town may levy property tax above its statutory limits when voters authorize it at an election held in the year the levy is made [S10]. MRSC describes these excess levies as separate from the regular levy, expiring after one year for all agencies except fire protection districts, and requiring a 60% majority [S9].

- **A recurring annual levy.** Palouse put infrastructure, pool and street levies on the November ballot in both 2024 and 2025, and three city propositions in 2023. In 2024 the city asked for $55,000, $50,000 and $50,000 [S5]; the "Operation & Maintenance Levy" passed with 424 yes, 238 no (64.0%) [S3]. In 2025 the corresponding measure passed with 261 yes, 138 no (65.4%; "Maintenance and Infrastructure Levy") [S7], and in 2023 city Proposition No. 1 passed with 317 yes, 184 no (63.3%) [S6].
- For 2025 collection the Department of Revenue lists a Palouse "Special" levy of $155,000, the total of the three 2024 levies, at a combined $1.75 per $1,000, below the roughly $2.05 the 2024 estimates added up to [S8][S5].
- Propositions No. 2 (pool, $50,000) and No. 3 (streets, $50,000) are on the same ballot [S1][S2].
