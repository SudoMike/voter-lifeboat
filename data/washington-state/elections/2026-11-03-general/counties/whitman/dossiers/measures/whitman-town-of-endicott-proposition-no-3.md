## What it does

A one-year excess property tax levy of $35,000, collected in 2027, "for street maintenance and public safety." Funds may be used for street repairs, preservation, drainage, street lighting, traffic control devices, snow and ice response, liability insurance for the street system, and other lawful street-related public safety purposes [S1][S2].

## Cost

$35,000, an estimated $1.14 per $1,000 of assessed value; the county assessor sets the final rate [S1][S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

Endicott places three one-year town levies (fire and EMT, streets, parks) on the ballot each year; the town had three levies on the 2025 ballot [S6]. Mayor Dean Marty said of the 2024 set that they would not fund any major new projects [S5]. The 2023 results list three Endicott propositions by number only, which all passed (96-15, 81-29, 74-35) [S4]; the export does not name their subjects.

- In 2024 the street work and maintenance levy was $30,000 at an estimated $1.14 or less per $1,000 [S5]. This year's request is $5,000 more at the same estimated rate [S1][S5].
- Results of the "Street Work & Maintenance Levy": 100-51 in 2022 [S8], 108-58 in 2024 [S7], 65-31 in 2025 [S3].

## Arguments for

Statement for, by Dean Marty [S1][S2]: approval "provides dedicated funding to maintain safe streets and related public safety infrastructure for residents, visitors, and emergency responders."

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted; no volunteers came forward [S1][S2].
