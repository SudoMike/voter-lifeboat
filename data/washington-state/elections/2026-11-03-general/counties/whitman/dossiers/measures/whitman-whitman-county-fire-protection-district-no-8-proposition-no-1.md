## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize Whitman County Fire District No. 8 to levy an excess property tax on all taxable property in the district to raise $75,000 a year for four consecutive years, collected in 2027, 2028, 2029 and 2030, for a maintenance and operation levy. The commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2026-1 [S1][S2].

- **The district.** The pamphlet's jurisdiction list calls it "LaCrosse Fire Dist. No. 8" [S3], and the county assessor's subfunds are named "FIRE DIST. #8 LACROSSE REG" and "FIRE DIST. #8 LACROSSE SPEC" [S4].
- The ballot title does not list specific purchases or programs, and no explanatory statement was submitted [S1][S2].

## Cost

- $75,000 a year for four years, an estimated $0.36 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2026 [S1][S2].
- Computed from the estimated rate (not a district figure): about $108 a year on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- The district has two property tax levies: a regular levy ($0.28537 per $1,000, $60,530, for taxes due in 2025) and a voter-approved special levy of $75,000 ($0.35398 per $1,000 in 2025; $0.36909 in 2024) [S5][S6].
- Voters approved a Fire District 8 maintenance and operation levy in November 2022, 113 to 54 (67.7%) [S7], and a "Resolution Requesting Special Levy" in November 2025, 74 to 36 (67.3%) [S8].
- This measure keeps the special levy at the same $75,000 a year collected in 2024 and 2025 [S5][S6], and covers four collection years in one vote [S1].
