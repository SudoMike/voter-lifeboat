## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize Whitman County (Endicott) Cemetery District No. 4 to levy an excess property tax of $40,000 on all taxable property in the district, collected in 2027, to finance a maintenance and operations levy. The commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2026-1. "This levy renews an existing levy that expires in 2026" [S1][S2]. No explanatory statement was submitted, and no volunteers came forward to write statements for or against [S1][S2].

## Cost

- $40,000, an estimated $0.37 per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1][S2].
- Computed from the estimated rate: about $111 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- It renews the levy approved in November 2025 [S1]. The Gazette described that 2025 measure (Resolution No. 2025-1) as "a renewal of an existing levy expiring this year," $40,000 at an estimated $0.38 per $1,000 for collection in 2026 [S8]; it passed 98 to 43 (69.5%) [S5].
- Earlier approvals: November 2022, 153 to 72 (68.0%); November 2024, 177 to 73 (70.8%) [S3][S4].
- The DOR levy detail shows the special levy at $20,000 ($0.20379 per $1,000) for taxes due in 2024 and $40,000 ($0.38234) for 2025, alongside a small regular levy ($0.03894 in 2025) [S6][S7].
