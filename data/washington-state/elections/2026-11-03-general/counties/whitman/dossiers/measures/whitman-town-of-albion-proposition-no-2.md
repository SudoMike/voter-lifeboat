## What it does

A one-year excess property tax levy of $5,000, collected in 2027, "to maintain and preserve the Town Cemetery." Funds may be used for hazardous tree removal, tree trimming, cemetery maintenance, grounds improvements, public safety and other lawful cemetery purposes [S1][S2].

## Cost

$5,000, an estimated $0.114 per $1,000 of assessed value, about $11.40 a year on a property assessed at $100,000 [S1][S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

No Albion cemetery levy appears in the November 2022, 2023, 2024 or 2025 Whitman County results; Albion's only levy in that period was the 2023 protective-services levy [S3][S4][S5][S6]. This appears to be a new levy purpose for the town. No source describing the cemetery's condition or the trees involved was found.

## Arguments for

Statement for, by Corina Anderson (spelled "Andersen" in the printed pamphlet) [S1][S2]: approval "will provide dedicated funding to remove hazardous trees, improve public safety, and preserve the Town Cemetery for current and future generations."

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted; no volunteers came forward [S1][S2].
