## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize the Endicott Parks & Recreation District No. 7 to impose a special tax levy of $65,000 "for the operating fund, swimming pool, capital outlay and/or cumulative reserve," collected in 2027 [S1].

- No explanatory statement and no statements for or against were filed; the guide notes a hardship waiver on file with the county [S1], and the printed pamphlet lists the district among jurisdictions "waivered out of this pamphlet due to financial hardship" [S2].

## Cost

- $65,000, an estimated $0.42 or less per $1,000 of assessed value, collected in 2027 [S1].
- Computed from the estimated rate: up to about $126 on a $300,000 property.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- Voters approved an Endicott park district "Operating Fund Levy" in November 2024, 206 to 105 (66.2%) [S3]. No Endicott park district levy appears in the November 2025 general results [S4].
- The DOR levy detail shows a $65,000 special levy ($0.42568 per $1,000) for taxes due in 2025 and no special levy for the district in 2024 [S5][S6]. Whether a levy is being collected in 2026 was not found. This request is the same amount as 2025.
