## What the office does

The sheriff runs county law enforcement. The office's listed responsibilities include patrol and crime investigation, the jail, civil process, court services, narcotics investigation and the Quad Cities Drug Task Force, search and rescue, and concealed pistol licenses [S2]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Incumbent.** Sheriff Brett Myers, in office since 2003, is seeking a seventh four-year term [S3].
- **Uncontested.** The pamphlet lists him as unopposed [S1].
