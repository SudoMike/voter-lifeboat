## Background

- **Sheriff.** Elected sheriff and in office since January 2003 [S4][S6]. He was the only candidate on the November 2022 general-election ballot, receiving 10,564 votes to 487 write-ins [S7].
- **Earlier career.** Whitman County patrol deputy 1996-2000 and patrol/investigations sergeant 2000-2003 [S5]; his site also lists earlier service as a reserve deputy [S6].
- **Office scope (as described on his 2022 site).** Describes overseeing a 60-bed jail, about 45 employees and a $4.5 million budget, and commanding a multi-jurisdictional narcotics task force [S5].
- **Education.** B.A. in criminal justice, Washington State University, 1994; M.S. in public administration, Andrew Jackson University, 2009; graduate of the National Sheriffs' Institute (2007), FBI Northwest Command College and DEA Drug Unit Commander Academy (2004) [S5]. Grew up and attended school in Pullman, where he lives [S6].
- **Party preference.** The VoteWA guide and the sample ballot list him as "Prefers Republican Party" [S2][S3]; the printed pamphlet lists "States No Party Preference" [S1].
