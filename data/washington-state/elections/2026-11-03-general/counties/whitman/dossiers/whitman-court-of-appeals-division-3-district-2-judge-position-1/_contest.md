## What the office does

The Court of Appeals reviews decisions from the trial courts to check that the law was followed [S1]. Division III's District 2 (Adams, Asotin, Benton, Columbia, Franklin, Garfield, Grant, Walla Walla and Whitman counties) elects one judge [S2].

## Race dynamics

- **Appointed incumbent.** Gov. Bob Ferguson appointed Grant County Superior Court Judge Tyson R. Hill to the court effective Jan. 1, 2026, filling the seat Judge George Fearing vacated when he retired that day [S4].
- **Why it is on the ballot.** An appointee to the Court of Appeals serves until a successor is elected at the next general election, and the winner serves the rest of the unexpired term [S3]. This race is therefore for a two-year unexpired term [S1].
- **Uncontested.** Hill is the only candidate [S1].
