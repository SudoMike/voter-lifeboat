## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney's office provides civil and criminal legal services for the county [S2]: it prosecutes crimes and advises county officials [S1]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Open seat.** Prosecuting Attorney Denis Tracy, elected in November 2002, is retiring at the end of 2026; the winner takes office Jan. 1, 2027 [S3].
- **Uncontested.** Chief Deputy Prosecutor Dan LeBeau is the only candidate [S1].
