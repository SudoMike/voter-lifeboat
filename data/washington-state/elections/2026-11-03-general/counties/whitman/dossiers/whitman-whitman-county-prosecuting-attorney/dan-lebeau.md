## Background

- **Prosecutor's office.** Whitman County deputy prosecutor for more than 20 years and chief deputy since 2016, prosecuting homicide, sexual assault, domestic violence and other serious crimes; also lists juvenile, contract, civil and family law and advising county officials [S1][S3].
- **Other legal roles.** Executive committee of the Washington State Bar Association Criminal Law Section since 2020 (chair since 2025); adjunct faculty at the University of Idaho College of Law (2026); faculty for prosecutor trial-skills training since 2024; Colton Municipal Court judge 2016-2022 [S1]. His site says he taught criminal law at the University of Idaho in spring 2026 [S3].
- **Open seat.** Prosecuting Attorney Denis Tracy, first elected in November 2002, is retiring at the end of 2026 and is not seeking another term [S4].
- **Earlier candidacy.** Ran for Whitman County District Court judge in 2018 and placed third in the primary behind John Hart and Rob Rembert in the count reported Aug. 10 [S5].
- **Education.** J.D., University of Idaho College of Law; B.S. in political science, University of Wisconsin-Stevens Point [S1].
- **Community.** Kiwanis of Colfax since 2023; Neill Public Library board of trustees 2013-2022 [S1].
- **Party preference.** Prefers Republican Party [S1][S2].

## Positions (from own statement)

- Says prosecution "is not about political decisions" but about upholding the rule of law, and that legal advice to county officials should focus on legal rather than political questions [S1].
- Says the office will keep building cases against "the most dangerous and violent criminal offenders" while working with therapeutic courts when appropriate; his site says he helped establish two therapeutic courts in the county [S1][S3].
- Says the office will continue to support crime victims through the criminal process [S1].

## Endorsements

- Says he has the support of all members of the current office [S1]. His site lists Prosecutor Denis Tracy, current and former deputy prosecutors, and prosecutors from other counties, including Latah County Prosecuting Attorney Bill Thompson [S3].
