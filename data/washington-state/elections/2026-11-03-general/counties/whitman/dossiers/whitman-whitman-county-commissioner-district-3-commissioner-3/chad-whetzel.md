## Background

- **Appointment.** Commissioners Tom Handy and Art Swannack appointed Whetzel, of Pullman, to the seat left open by Michael Largent, effective March 17, 2025 [S3]. Largent had held the post since 2006 and resigned in January 2025 [S5].
- **2025 election.** Whetzel kept the seat in the November 2025 election, 4,594 votes to 4,116 for Cody Finch [S5]. He was the only candidate in the August 2026 District 3 primary (2,611 votes to 162 write-ins) [S7].
- **Work.** Has run a farrier business since 1997 [S1]; the Gazette described him as owner of Whetzel Horse Shoeing [S4].
- **Civic roles.** 4-H leader and council member for 25 years; joined Whitman County Fire District 12 in 1999 and has been an EMT for 25 years and captain for 15; Whitman County Planning Commission member for 12 years and chair for 8; member of the state and county cattlemen's associations [S1][S4].
- **Education.** B.S. in animal science, Washington State University, 1997 [S1].
- **Party preference.** Prefers Republican Party [S1][S2]. The board page lists him as District 3 commissioner [S6].

## Positions (from own statement)

- Says his planning commission service gives him insight into the county's challenges and that he wants to balance "responsible growth" with "preserving our rural heritage" [S1].
- Says his experience as a small business owner guides him "when balancing budgets" [S1].
