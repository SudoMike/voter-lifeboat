## What the office does

The three-member Board of County Commissioners is the county's executive and legislative authority. It adopts, amends and repeals county ordinances, including zoning, traffic, planning and public safety ordinances, and appoints advisory boards and non-elected department heads [S2]. The seat is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Who votes.** Voters in Commissioner District 3 nominated the candidates in the August primary [S3][S5]; under RCW 36.32.050(1) the commissioner is then elected by the voters of the whole county in the general election [S4].
- **Incumbent.** Chad Whetzel has been the District 3 commissioner since March 2025 [S1]; the board page lists him in that seat [S2].
- **Uncontested.** Whetzel was the only name in the District 3 primary, receiving 2,611 votes to 162 write-ins [S5], and the pamphlet lists him as unopposed [S1].
