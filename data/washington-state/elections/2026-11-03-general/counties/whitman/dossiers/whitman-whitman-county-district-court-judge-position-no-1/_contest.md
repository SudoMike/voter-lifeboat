## What the office does

Whitman County District Court hears misdemeanor criminal cases, traffic infractions, anti-harassment and domestic violence protection petitions, name changes, small claims, and civil disputes of up to $100,000. It also runs misdemeanor probation [S2]. The position is nonpartisan with a four-year term [S1].
