## Background

- **District Court judge.** Sworn in as Whitman County District Court judge in December 2018 after defeating Rob Rembert in the November election for the seat of retiring Judge Doug Robinson [S3][S4]. He was the only candidate on the 2022 general-election ballot, receiving 10,174 votes to 199 write-ins [S7]. The court's page names him as judge [S6].
- **Before the bench.** Public defender in the area for about 15 years and Colfax Municipal Court judge; also filled in as a temporary District Court judge and temporary Whitman County Superior Court commissioner [S3][S4]. In 2018 he said he was the only candidate in that race who had graduated from the Washington Judicial College [S3].
- **Judicial associations.** Received the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association Presidential Award in 2024 for service to the association, having served on several of its committees, and was dean of the Washington Judicial College in 2023 [S5].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- He submitted no 2026 pamphlet or VoteWA statement [S1][S2]. In 2018 he described a judge's job as balancing accountability with rehabilitation, noting that punitive measures on their own often do not seem to work [S3].

## Endorsements

- In 2018 he said he had the endorsements of Judge Robinson and retired Judge Frazier and more than 60 judges statewide, including four state Supreme Court justices [S3]. No 2026 endorsements were researched for this uncontested race.
