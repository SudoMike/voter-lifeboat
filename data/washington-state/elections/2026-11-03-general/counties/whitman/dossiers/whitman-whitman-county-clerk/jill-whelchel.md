## Background

- **County clerk.** Says she has served as Whitman County Clerk since 2015 [S1][S2]. She was the only candidate on the November 2022 general-election ballot for clerk, receiving 11,021 votes to 234 write-ins [S4].
- **Earlier career.** Court Appointed Special Advocate (CASA) volunteer from 2000 to 2005 while managing direct marketing for the Washington State University Creamery; Whitman County staff guardian ad litem and CASA program manager from 2005 to 2014 [S1][S2].
- **Education.** B.A. in hospitality business management, Washington State University [S1].
- **Party preference.** States no party preference [S1].
