## What the office does

The coroner investigates sudden and unexpected, accidental, suspicious and unattended deaths in the county, establishes cause and manner of death, completes death certificates, and coordinates with the health officer when a cause of death threatens public health [S2]. The office is partisan with a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Open seat.** Coroner Annie Pillers, in the office since 2018, is not seeking a third term [S3].
- **Uncontested.** Chief Deputy Coroner Cassie Richardson is the only candidate [S1].
