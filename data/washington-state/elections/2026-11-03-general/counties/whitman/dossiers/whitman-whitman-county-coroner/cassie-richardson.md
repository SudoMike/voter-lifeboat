## Background

- **Coroner's office.** Joined the Whitman County Coroner's Office as a deputy coroner in November 2022 and became chief deputy coroner in January 2025 [S3]. Says she has three years of experience as deputy and chief deputy [S1].
- **Accreditation.** Says she was "heavily involved" in the office earning accreditation from the International Association of Coroners & Medical Examiners in 2025 [S3]; her statement says she contributed to the office achieving national accreditation [S1].
- **Earlier career.** More than a decade in regulated laboratories as a bench scientist and quality assurance manager [S1].
- **Credentials and education.** Washington state certification as a medicolegal death investigator [S1][S3]; M.S. in medical sciences (forensic medicine), University of Florida, 2025; bachelor's degrees in chemistry and Spanish, Colorado State University, 2012 [S1].
- **Community.** Three years as a youth fastpitch softball coach on the Palouse [S1]. Has lived in Pullman since 2018 [S3].
- **Party preference.** "Prefers Democrat Party" [S1][S2].

## Positions (from own statement)

- Says she will build on the foundation set by Coroner Annie Pillers and maintain the office's accreditation standards [S1][S3].
- Says families deserve "clear, timely, and compassionate communication" and that she will ensure thorough investigations [S1].

## Endorsements

- Coroner Annie Pillers [S1][S3]. Pillers, who is retiring, joined the office in 2008, became chief deputy in 2013 and coroner in 2018 [S4].
