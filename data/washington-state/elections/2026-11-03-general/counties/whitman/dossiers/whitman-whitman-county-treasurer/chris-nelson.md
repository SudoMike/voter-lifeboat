## Background

- **Before office.** Chris (Christina) Nelson of Endicott was Whitman County's IT director from September 2009 and had worked 20 years in Washington county government, including Island and Cowlitz counties, when she filed in January 2018 to challenge Treasurer Mark Clinton [S3].
- **Elections.** In the November 2018 count reported after election week she led Clinton, 4,012 votes (51.75%) to 3,740 (48.25%); both ran as Republicans that year [S4]. She was the only candidate on the 2022 general-election ballot, receiving 10,043 votes to 449 write-ins [S6]. The county lists her as treasurer [S5].
- **Party preference.** States no party preference on this ballot [S1][S2].
