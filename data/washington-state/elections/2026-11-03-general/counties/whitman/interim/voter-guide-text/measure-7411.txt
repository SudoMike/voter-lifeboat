## JurisdictionID

## ShortDescription
Proposition No. 1

## MeasureName
Operation and Maintenance Levy

## BallotTitle
Shall a special levy of $75,000, an estimated $0.31 per $1,000 of assessed valuation, be imposed on all real property in Lacrosse Parks and Recreation District 1, to be collected in 2027, for the operation, maintenance, and capital improvements of the Swimming Pool and existing buildings?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Proposed Special Levy of $75,000 will continue to support the operation, maintenance, and capital improvements for the swimming pool and existing buildings. Operation costs include lifeguard salaries, pool chemicals, utilities, and office supplies. Maintenance costs include professional services, repairs, construction, and painting supplies. Recent improvements include resealing the expansion joints, installing a second new pool filter, and painting. This levy also supports the numerous youth sports programs that the Parks and Recreation District sponsors, including: swim team, soccer, football, basketball, and baseball.

## ArgumentFor
Please support the Parks and Recreation levy. This levy makes it possible to continue operating our pool. It also supports the youth programs we sponsor. Our pool is the lifeblood of our community in the summer months, providing healthy recreation for children, youth, and families. Having a pool allows Lacrosse children to participate in an organized swim team through the Tri-County Swim League. Patrons utilize the pool for swim lessons and water aerobics. The pool gives our children and youth a positive, healthy recreational choice when there are so few choices in our small, rural community. In addition, maintaining a pool in our community offers employment opportunities for many of our youth. Thank you for supporting this important levy.
