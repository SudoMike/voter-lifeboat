## ExplanatoryStatement
Proposition 2 would replace an existing capital levy expiring in 2027. This replacement levy will be used to pay costs of making technology, safety, security and infrastructure improvements throughout school facilities, including: (1) acquiring, installing, implementing and modernizing instructional technology equipment, infrastructure, systems and facilities; (2) acquiring, installing, implementing and modernizing security cameras, entry/access controls, door locks and other safety and security systems; (3) upgrading and/or improving other infrastructure for safety (including bleachers, locker rooms, heating and cooling systems and equipment, electrical, plumbing, roofing, lighting and fencing). Visit: www.cheneysd.org.

The proposed three-year replacement levy would authorize collection of $880,000 in 2028, $1,350,000 in 2029 and $1,900,000 in 2030. The levy rate per $1,000 of assessed value required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $0.10 in 2028, $0.15 in 2029, and $0.20 in 2030. Exemptions from taxes may be available, contact your county assessor.

## ArgumentFor
Cheney School District is asking the voters to support the Replacement Capital Levy on November 3rd, 2026 and I urge you to vote "yes" in support of this levy. All students deserve access to a quality education, regardless of background or family income. Vote Yes to renew the technology, security and infrastructure levy. This is a replacement levy, not a new tax.

Preparing students to succeed after graduation requires access to modern technology. Technology funds include critical technology infrastructure such as digital whiteboards, and training to help students excel.

Proposition 2 also funds critical facility maintenance, including code compliance; improvements such as roof repairs, HVAC, fire alarms systems; and health, safety and security upgrades across the district. Safety and security improvements such as security cameras, access controls, and door locks help keep our students and staff safe.

Failure to pass Proposition 2 would result in deferred maintenance and would only cost more money down the road. This is a fiscally responsible measure to protect our investments in our schools. Renewing this levy keeps our students warm, dry, and safe and provides necessary technology leading to improved student learning and success. Let’s renew our commitment and vote to approve the Replacement Capital Levy for critical technology, security for our schools, and building maintenance.
