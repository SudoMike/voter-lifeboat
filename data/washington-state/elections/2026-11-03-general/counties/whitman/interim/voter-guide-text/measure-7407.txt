## Jurisdiction

## JurisdictionID

## ShortDescription
Proposition No. 1

## MeasureName
Street Levy

## BallotTitle
Shall the City of Tekoa impose a special property tax levy upon taxable property within the City of Tekoa in the amount of $50,000.00, an estimated $1.23 per $1,000.00 of assessed valuation in 2026 for collection in 2027, for ongoing oiling of gravel streets, chip sealing roads, replacing asphalt or repairing and replacing sidewalks?

## ExplanatoryStatement
We are asking the voters to approve a street levy for $50,000. The levy will pay for sidewalk replacement or matching money for a sidewalk grant, matching money for a street grant, chip sealing paved roads, or oiling gravel streets. We will be seal coating a majority of the streets this summer with a grant from TIB. We applied for a TIB grant to replace the sidewalk on Main Street for 2027. We have a large number of sidewalks that need replaced that this levy would pay for or provide matching funds.

## ArgumentFor
I am in favor of the street levy in Tekoa. Any improvements that will improve the city streets and sidewalks will benefit all the citizens in Tekoa. This keeps our city safe!
