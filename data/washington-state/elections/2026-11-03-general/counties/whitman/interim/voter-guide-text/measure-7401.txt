## BallotTitle
For the purpose of funding the operation and maintenance of the infrastructure, the City Council of the City of Palouse proposes, pursuant to RCW 84.52.052, to impose an excess property tax levy upon all taxable property within the City in the amount of $55,000.00, an estimated $0.73058 per $1,000.00 of assessed value in the year 2026 for collection in 2027, as provided in Resolution 2026-08.

Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The City of Palouse is seeking voter support for the continued operation and maintenance of City infrastructure. The City Council requests that voters approve Proposition 1, as authorized by Resolution 2026-08.
The proposed levy rate is $0.5912 per $1,000 of assessed property value. If approved, the levy is estimated to generate approximately $55,000 to help maintain and preserve the City's essential infrastructure.
