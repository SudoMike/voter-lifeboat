## MeasureName
Street Maintenance and Public Safety Excess Levy

## BallotTitle
Shall the Town of Endicott levy a one-year excess property tax of $35,000 for collection in 2027 to fund street maintenance and public safety?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Town of Endicott requests voter approval of a one-year excess property tax levy of $35,000 for street maintenance and public safety. Funds may be used for street repairs, preservation, drainage improvements, street lighting, traffic control devices, snow and ice response, liability insurance associated with the street system, and other lawful street-related public safety purposes. The estimated levy rate is $1.14 per $1,000 of assessed valuation. The final levy rate will be determined by the Whitman County Assessor.

## ArgumentFor
Approval provides dedicated funding to maintain safe streets and related public safety infrastructure for residents, visitors, and emergency responders.
