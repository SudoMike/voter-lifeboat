## MeasureName
Street Maintenance and Repair Levy

## BallotTitle
The Town Council of the Town of Garfield adopted Resolution 2026-13 concerning a proposition to finance street maintenance and repair and street improvements in the Town of Garfield. This proposition would authorize the Town of Garfield to collect an excess property tax levy upon all taxable property within the town in the amount of $72,000, an estimated $2.24 per $1,000.00 of assessed value in the year 2026, for collection in 2027.

Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The funds collected from the street levy are used to support the maintenance and upkeep of all streets within the town limits. This includes a variety of essential services such as powering streetlights, grading and oiling gravel roads, repairing potholes, controlling roadside weeds, snow plowing and sanding, and covering salaries related to public works projects associated with street maintenance.

## ArgumentFor
The Street Fund is the only fund that does not generate its own revenue; all street-related operations are solely supported through the passage of the Street Levy. Street improvements and ongoing maintenance depend on these levy funds to perform essential services. If you value well-maintained and snow plowed streets within the Town of Garfield, please support and vote Yes for the 2027 Street Levy.
