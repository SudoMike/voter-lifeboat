## MeasureName
Cemetery Maintenance and Hazardous Tree Removal

## BallotTitle
SHALL THE TOWN OF ALBION LEVY A ONE-YEAR EXCESS PROPERTY TAX OF $5,000, AN ESTIMATED $0.114 PER $1,000 OF 2026 ASSESSED VALUATION, FOR CEMETERY MAINTENANCE, HAZARDOUS TREE REMOVAL, GROUNDS IMPROVEMENTS, AND OTHER LAWFUL CEMETERY PURPOSES FOR THE TOWN OF ALBION FOR COLLECTION IN 2027?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Town of Albion requests voter approval of a one-year excess property tax levy of $5,000 to maintain and preserve the Town Cemetery. Funds may be used for hazardous tree removal, tree trimming, cemetery maintenance, grounds improvements, public safety, and other lawful cemetery purposes. The estimated levy rate is $0.114 per $1,000 of assessed valuation, or approximately $11.40 annually for a property assessed at $100,000.

## ArgumentFor
Approval of this proposition will provide dedicated funding to remove hazardous trees, improve public safety, and preserve the Town Cemetery for current and future generations.
