## BallotTitle
The Commissioners of Whitman County (Endicott) Cemetery District No. 4 adopted Resolution No. 2026-1, concerning a proposition to finance a maintenance and operations levy for the District. This proposition would authorize the District to levy an excess property tax on all taxable property within the District at an estimated rate of $0.37 per $1,000.00 of assessed value in 2026, to raise $40,000, to be collected in 2027. This levy renews an existing levy that expires in 2026.
