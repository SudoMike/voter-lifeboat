## BallotTitle
The Board of Whitman County Fire Protection District No. 14 adopted Resolution No. 26-03 concerning a proposition to establish its regular property tax levy.

This proposition authorizes the District to establish its regular property tax levy at $1.08 per $1,000 of assessed value to be assessed in 2026 and collected in 2027. The funds will maintain and improve fire protection, and life safety services, replace apparatus and equipment, and provide for firefighter safety. The dollar amount of the 2026 levy collected in 2027 shall serve as the base for computing subsequent levy limitations as provided by chapter 84.55 RCW.
