## MeasureName
Restoring Regular Property Tax Levy for Library Services

## BallotTitle
The Board of Trustees of the Whitman County Rural Library District adopted a Resolution concerning the Rural Library District’s regular property tax levy rate. If approved, this Proposition would restore the Rural Library District’s regular property tax levy rate to $0.45 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2026, for collection in 2027 and maintain that rate authorization for each of the nine succeeding years. (the actual levy rate may be reduced below the authorized maximum in any year based on annual budget requirements) and use the 2036 levy amount to calculate subsequent levy limits under chapter 84.55 RCW.

Should this proposition be approved

## ExplanatoryStatement
Whitman County Rural Library District serves 14 communities, offering free access to resources, programs, and welcoming spaces where people come together to learn, connect, and grow. Rising costs and state tax limitations have reduced the District’s levy rate over time, creating a gap between revenue and the cost of services. This proposition would increase the annual cost for a $275,000 home by $14.30 and establish a self-imposed maximum levy rate of $0.45 per $1,000 for ten years. This rate is below the state limit and reflects the District’s commitment to requesting only funding needed to provide stable, predictable library services.

## ArgumentFor
We face a critical decision about the future of the Whitman County Rural Library District.
The district serves 14 Whitman County communities, with each branch reflecting its town's unique character, history, and culture. For many, the library is more than a place to borrow books. It is the only free, welcoming public space where people can gather to learn, connect, attend programs, and build community. In 2025, the district hosted 2,264 programs and circulated 57,173 physical items, in addition to thousands of digital resources.
The district benefits all county residents, not just frequent library visitors. District libraries are community anchors that drive downtown activity, expand access to free wireless internet, and improve residential and commercial property values. Libraries strengthen Whitman County's workforce with technology access, resume assistance, and digital skills training. Library programs reduce crime by engaging youth outside of school. In short, strong libraries contribute to strong, stable, and informed communities.
This proposition requests to increase the library district's property tax rate from $0.39 to $0.45 of $1,000 of assessed property value. While state and federal library funding is unpredictable and may come with requirements that do not reflect our communities' values, this is a local tax. The library district is governed by a resident board, with public meetings and published budgets, agendas, and minutes. Local taxation preserves local control. Establishing this rate allows the district to maintain a balanced budget and keep revenue aligned with the cost of providing library services.
Please Vote "YES" for Proposition 1.
