## MeasureName
Fire Protection and Emergency Service Excess Levy

## BallotTitle
Shall the Town of Endicott levy a one-year excess property tax of $13,500 for collection in 2027 to fund fire protection and emergency services?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Town of Endicott requests voter approval of a one-year excess property tax levy of $13,500 to help fund fire protection and emergency services. Funds may be used for contractual fire protection, emergency response, equipment, training, and other lawful public safety purposes.
The estimated levy rate is $.44 per $1,000 of assessed valuation. The final levy rate will be determined by the Whitman County Assessor.

## ArgumentFor
Approval provides dedicated funding to help maintain reliable fire protection and emergency services while protecting residents, businesses, and property.
