## BallotTitle
SHALL THE TOWN OF ALBION LEVY A ONE-YEAR EXCESS PROPERTY TAX OF $20,000, AN ESTIMATED $0.459 PER $1,000 OF 2026 ASSESSED VALUATION, FOR FIRE PROTECTION, LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICES, EMERGENCY RESPONSE, AND OTHER LAWFUL PUBLIC SAFETY PURPOSES FOR THE TOWN OF ALBION FOR COLLECTION IN 2027?

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Town of Albion requests voter approval of a one-year excess property tax levy of $20,000 to support protective services, public safety equipment, training, and other lawful public safety purposes. The estimated levy rate is $0.459 per $1,000 of assessed valuation, or approximately $45.90 annually for a property assessed at $100,000.

## ArgumentFor
Approval of this proposition will provide dedicated funding to help maintain fire protection, law enforcement services, emergency response, and other essential public safety services for the Town of Albion.
