## BallotTitle
The Commissioners of Whitman County Fire District No. 8 adopted Resolution No. 2026-1, concerning a proposition to finance a maintenance and operation levy for the District. This proposition would authorize the District to levy an excess property tax on all taxable property within the District at an estimated rate of $0.36 per $1,000.00 of assessed value in 2026 to raise $75,000 per year for four consecutive years, to be collected in 2027, 2028, 2029, and 2030.
