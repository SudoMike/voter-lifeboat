## Jurisdiction

## ShortDescription
Proposition No. 1

## MeasureName
Operation and Maintenance Levy

## BallotTitle
Shall a levy by the Whitman County Cemetery District No. 2 of $105,000, an estimated $.82, or less, per one thousand dollars of assessed property valuation, be collected in 2027, to help defray the costs of equipment, improving the sprinkler system, and for other operation and maintenance of the Cemetery be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
Garfield Cemetery District No. 2, has proposed proposition 2027-1 to finance the operating and maintenance costs for WCCD2 and Silver Creek Cemeteries including: maintenance programs: sprinkler updates and installation, tree removal and safety projects, grant search and implementation: Veteran's memorial, columbarium development, headstone reset corrections grounds improvements: fertilizer, spraying, turf management, and purchases: purchase of grounds equipment, updates/construction of shop interior: secure records maintenance space, website and burial records in digital/online system, and other beautification projects for the year 2027.

## ArgumentFor
We as a board are in support of the Garfield Cemetery Levy for 2027. The board will continue with maintenance strategies for improving the grounds, sprinkler systems updates, tree removal and safety projects, turf management which includes seed, weed and fertilizer, Veterans memorial plans and other beautification projects. Your support of the levy will provide much needed funding for continued employee funding, fuel, water, electricity, and maintenance costs.
Additionally, these funds also support the Silver Creek Cemetery upkeep and maintenance. With these funds, the board plans to implement plans to update and revive the Silver Creek Cemetery. This includes turf management, updates, and tree grooming, as well as mapping and headstone updates.
With your support, the board has been able to reduce the requested amount this year. We kindly ask for your continued support for this very important and necessary establishment for our town and community members.
The WCCD2 board greatly appreciate your continued support!
