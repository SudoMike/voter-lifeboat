## What the office does
The three county commissioners are the county's legislative and executive body: they adopt the budget and property tax levies, set land-use and road policy and oversee county departments. Commissioners are nominated by district in the primary and elected by the whole county in the general (S3); the 2022 District 2 race was on all 27 precincts (S4).

## Race dynamics
Two Republicans. Incumbent Robert Rosencrantz, appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022, led small-business owner Brandy Parker Warren by one vote, 670 to 669, in the district-only primary (S2). Rosencrantz runs on opposing data centers, holding taxes down and his road and housing record (S1). Warren runs on collaboration, responsible growth and infrastructure (S1). The Spokesman-Review reported that Warren's husband works as a manager at Merkle Standard, which is seeking power for a data center at the former Usk paper mill (S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
- `experience`: incumbent with a record against a first-time candidate.
- `taxes` and `spending`: Rosencrantz states positions (hold taxes down, spend carefully, no road levy shift); Warren speaks of responsible budgeting without specifics.
- Data centers are the race's sharpest issue, but the `tech` axis does not apply to county contests; it is recorded in the dossiers and the race blurb.
