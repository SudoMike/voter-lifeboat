## Background

- Brandy Parker Warren, of Newport, prefers the Republican Party and is a first-time candidate for county office. She owns Lucky Bee Variety Store in Newport and Squatchee's Scoops & Swirls in Priest River, Idaho, and reports more than 20 years of small business management [S1][S4].
- Her listed elected experience is president and treasurer of the Pend Oreille Youth Soccer Association and membership on the Newport Business Advisory Committee; she attended Spokane Community College [S1]. She has lived in Newport for more than 20 years [S4].
- In the August 2026 primary, which only District 2 voters could vote in, she received 669 votes to Rosencrantz's 670 [S9].
- Full PDC reporting: $7,920.16 raised and $6,159.16 spent as of October 5, 2026 [S10].
- The Spokesman-Review reported that her husband, according to election records, works as a manager at Merkle Standard, the company seeking up to 225 megawatts of power to convert its Usk crypto-mining site into a data center [S7]. The paper could not reach her for comment [S8]. Her campaign materials do not mention the Usk proposal or data centers [S1][S3][S4][S6].

## Positions

### County priorities

- Says she will focus on "strengthening infrastructure, supporting public safety, encouraging responsible growth, and protecting the quality of life" [S1].
- Describes her approach as community-driven, transparent, proactive and collaborative: listening to residents, bringing people to the table and focusing on "solutions, not division" [S1][S3].
- Says her business experience taught her "responsible budgeting" and that county government should stay focused on local families, businesses and taxpayers [S1].
- Says she is "not running to represent a party or a select group" [S3].

## Endorsements

- The mayors of Newport (Keith Campbell), Metaline Falls, Cusick and Metaline; County Auditor Marianne Nichols; Commissioner John Gentle (District 1); Port of Pend Oreille Commissioners Kim Gentle and Keith Peterson and Port Executive Director Kelly J. Driver; an Ione council member, a fire chief and residents [S5].

## Scoring notes

- First-time candidate running against the incumbent on collaboration and responsible growth, with no specific tax, spending or land-use proposals in her materials [S1][S3][S4].
- Endorsed by local mayors, a fellow commissioner and port officials [S5].
- Has not stated a position on the Usk data center; news coverage reports a family employment tie to the developer [S7][S8].
