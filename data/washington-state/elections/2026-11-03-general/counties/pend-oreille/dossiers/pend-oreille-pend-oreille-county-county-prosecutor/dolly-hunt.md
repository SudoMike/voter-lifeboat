## Background

- Dolly Hunt, of Usk, prefers the Republican Party. She has been a prosecutor for more than 22 years and the elected Pend Oreille County Prosecutor for three terms; she joined the office in 2006 after three years as an Okanogan County deputy prosecutor [S1].
- She sits on the boards of the Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys, the Municipal Research and Services Center and the National District Attorneys Association; J.D., Santa Clara University School of Law, 2002 [S1].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 (5,078 votes) [S3]; 3,285 votes (97.1%) in the August 2026 primary [S4]. Mini PDC reporting [S5].

## Positions

- Says she will protect constitutional rights while holding lawbreakers accountable, and work with law enforcement, victims and community partners [S1].
- Calls herself a career prosecutor focused on victims' rights, community safety and offender accountability [S6].
