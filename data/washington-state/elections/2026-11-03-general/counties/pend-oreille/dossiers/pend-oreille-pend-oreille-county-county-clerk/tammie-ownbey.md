## Background

- Tammie Ownbey, of Newport, prefers the Republican Party and has been Pend Oreille County Clerk since 2007; she was president of the Washington Association of County Officials in 2016 and of the Washington State Association of County Clerks in 2023 [S1].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 (5,162 votes) [S3]; 3,381 votes (97.3%) in the August 2026 primary [S4]. Mini PDC reporting [S5].

## Positions

- Describes the office's work: Superior Court records, jury service, protection orders, legal financial obligations and passports [S1][S2].
