## What the office does
The three-member commission of Public Utility District No. 1 of Pend Oreille County sets rates, budgets and policy for the county's public electric, water and broadband utility and hires its general manager; commissioners serve six-year terms. They are nominated by district and elected by the whole PUD, which is the whole county (S2); the 2020 race for this seat was on all 27 precincts (S6).

## Race dynamics
Open seat: Commissioner Curt Knapp did not seek re-election (S3). Retired technology executive Ernie Hood and Newport teacher and former KUBS FM manager Gerry David Bradbury advanced from the district-only primary, Bradbury first (S1, S3). Both pledge low rates and reliable service. Bradbury centers the 2030 renegotiation of the Boundary Dam power deal with Seattle City Light and praises current management; Hood stresses fiscal discipline, transparency on spending and rate pressure from state mandates and data centers. A proposed 225-megawatt data center at the former Usk mill is seeking PUD power (S4); the PUD says large loads pay their own upgrades and wholesale power (S5).
