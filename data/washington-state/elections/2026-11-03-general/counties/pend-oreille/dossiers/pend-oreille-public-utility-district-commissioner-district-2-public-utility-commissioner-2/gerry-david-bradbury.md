## Background

- Gerry David Bradbury, 52, of Newport, is a lifelong county resident and Newport High School graduate. He reports 31 years as a teacher, coach and administrator in Washington, Oregon and Idaho, including 23 years teaching and coaching in the Newport School District, nearly 20 years managing the school's KUBS FM station and service as principal of Priest River Junior High in the West Bonner County School District [S1][S3].
- He served on the Newport City Council in the early 2000s and as a planning and zoning commissioner [S1]. He holds a bachelor's degree in education and a master's in leadership and administration from Eastern Washington University [S3].
- He worked for the PUD as a seasonal engineering assistant in college and took part in its Teachers Interested in Public Power program [S1][S3]. Outgoing Commissioner Curt Knapp reported a breakfast meeting with him in April 2026 [S6].
- Other community roles include Newport volunteer firefighter, president of Pend Oreille Valley Friends of the NRA and hunter-education instructor [S1].
- He placed first in the August 2026 primary, which only District 2 voters could vote in, with 758 votes to Ernie Hood's 407 and Tracy Rutt's 263 [S4][S7]. The seat is open: Commissioner Curt Knapp did not seek re-election [S7]. Mini PDC reporting [S5].

## Positions

- Calls the renegotiation of the 48-megawatt Boundary Dam power agreement with Seattle City Light, due by 2030, the most important issue for ratepayers, and wants a new agreement that keeps rates among the lowest possible [S3].
- Names state and federal environmental mandates, many "unfunded," as a cost risk for ratepayers, along with infrastructure upgrades and the eventual relicensing of Box Canyon Dam [S3].
- Praises the current board for hiring a general manager and management team he describes as excellent [S3].
- Sees a commissioner's main role as representing residents and acting as the link between ratepayers and PUD management [S3].
- Pledges to keep the PUD's tradition of "Quality Service at Low Cost" [S1][S3].
