## Background

- Ernie Hood (Ernest Hood Jr.), 68, of Newport, is retired and moved to the county nearly eight years ago. He served 10 years in the U.S. Navy as an electronics and cryptologic technician, then spent more than 35 years in technology and manufacturing with Intergraph, Compaq, Hewlett-Packard, Unirac (solar), Keytronic and Jetseal, including leadership roles [S1][S3].
- He holds an MBA and a bachelor's in management and has not held elected office [S1].
- He is vice president of the Pend Oreille County Radio Group, treasurer of the Community Emergency Response Team, an Amateur Radio Emergency Services participant, a church elder and a member of the VFW and American Legion [S1].
- He spoke during public comment at the PUD board's April 21, 2026 meeting, and Commissioner Knapp reported meeting with him [S6].
- He placed second in the district-only August 2026 primary with 407 votes [S4][S7]. Mini PDC reporting [S5].

## Positions

- Calls the biggest challenge balancing reliability and resilience with affordable rates for families and seniors, and names aging infrastructure, rising costs, "data center proliferation" and state mandates such as the Clean Energy Transformation Act as pressures on rates [S3].
- Wants "fiscal discipline," efficiency, caution about "unnecessary spending" and "independent oversight"; asks of every major decision whether he would make it with his own family's money [S3].
- Credits the current board with reliable service and broadband expansion, but wants more proactive communication and "greater transparency in how major spending decisions are made" [S3].
- Wants the PUD to meet the CETA 2030 carbon-neutral requirement and renew the Boundary power assignment with Seattle City Light while limiting the effect on ratepayers [S3].
- Pledges honesty, transparency and keeping rates "as affordable as possible" [S1].
