## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would levy a one-year excess property tax for the Sacheen Lake Water and Sewer District's general fund: about $0.75 per $1,000 of assessed value, raising $113,827, levied in 2026 for collection in 2027 (S1, S2). The district says the money pays for aquatic weed control (milfoil and water shield, applied by a contractor), lake-level management including access for volunteers who clear beaver dams and culverts on the West Branch of the Little Spokane River, upkeep of the Myers/Harter sanctuary where the Sacheen Lake Association picnic is held, and insurance for volunteers, the park and the board (S1).

The district surrounds Sacheen Lake, west of Newport; only voters in the Sacheen precinct have voted on its levies (S4, S5, S6; WA DOR SEW2025 polygon `3`, builder notes).

## Cost

- About $0.75 per $1,000 of assessed value, $113,827 in total, for 2027 only (S1).
- The 2025 levy was $0.73487 per $1,000 ($88,376 on $120.3 million of assessed value) (S3), so the rate is close to the current one; the statement for calls it "no additional tax cost to you" (S1).
- An excess levy needs 60% approval and turnout validation under Article VII, section 2(a) of the state constitution (S7).

## Arguments for

- Grant Miller, president of the Sacheen Lake Association, writes that the district runs on user fees (after loan payments) plus this levy, and that without weed treatment invasive weeds "choke off portions of the lake" (S1).

## Arguments against

- "A concerned citizen" writes: "I should not be taxed to provide revenue for a government organization. It should be my choice." (S1)

## Record

- Voters approved the district's one-year M&O levy at each recent general: 168-68 in 2020, 134-64 in 2022 and 140-73 in 2024 (S4, S5, S6).

## Lean notes

- `taxes`: a yes vote renews a one-year excess levy at about the current rate ($0.75 vs. $0.73) (S1, S3); no new or higher tax in substance.
