## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would let Riverside School District No. 416-62 replace its educational programs and operations levy, which expires at the end of 2027, with a three-year levy for 2028 through 2030, under Resolution No. 6-26-03 (S1, S2). The money pays for programs, services and staff the state does not fund: elective and advanced courses, vocational education, nurses, counselors, technology support, safety, security, performing arts, athletics and extracurricular activities (S1). The district says the levy is 13% of its budget and pays for all athletics (S3).

The district is based in Spokane County (Chattaroy and Elk); its Pend Oreille County part is the strip along the southern county line (WA DOR SCH2025 polygon `62`; builder notes). Only voters there see this copy; Spokane County voters see Spokane's.

## Cost

- Estimated $1.58 per $1,000 of assessed value: $3,996,811 in 2028, $4,116,715 in 2029 and $4,240,217 in 2030 (S1, S2).
- Taxpayers now pay $1.52 per $1,000 for the expiring levy, the superintendent told the Spokesman-Review (S3). DOR's 2025 detail shows $1.48497 per $1,000 on $113.9 million of assessed value in the Pend Oreille part (S4).
- Needs a simple majority.

## Arguments for

- The statement for (Community for the Riverside Replacement Levy: Jeni Vineyard, Kevin Koesel, Jennifer Gaffaney) says the measure replaces the current levy rather than adding a second one, and that the programs it funds "are not 'extras'" to students (S1).
- Superintendent Ken Russell says the district has run the same levy every three years for decades, and that the board decided not to seek another capital levy now because voters have said they "just can't afford it" (S3).

## Arguments against

- No statement against was submitted; the pamphlet says no volunteers came forward (S1).
- Voters turned down the district's attempt to renew a separate capital levy in 2025 (S3).

## Lean notes

- `taxes`: a yes vote continues an existing school levy at a slightly higher estimated rate ($1.58 vs. $1.52) (S1, S3). It is a replacement, not a new tax.
