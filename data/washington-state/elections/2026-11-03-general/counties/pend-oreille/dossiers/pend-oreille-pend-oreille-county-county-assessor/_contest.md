## What the office does
The county assessor values every taxable property in the county each year and keeps the assessment rolls that property taxes are figured from.

## Race dynamics
Open seat: Assessor Jim McCroskey did not file for re-election, and Chief Deputy Assessor Nathan Longly is the only candidate (S1, S3).
