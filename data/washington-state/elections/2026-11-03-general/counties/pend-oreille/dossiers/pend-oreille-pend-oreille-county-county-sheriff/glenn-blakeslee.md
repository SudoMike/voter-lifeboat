## Background

- Glenn Blakeslee, of Newport, prefers the Republican Party and is seeking a third term as sheriff; he was hired as a deputy in 1999 and reports more than 2,200 hours of law enforcement training, 550 of them in leadership and supervision [S1].
- Selkirk High School graduate (1987) with earlier military, medical and construction work [S1].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 (5,188 votes) [S3]; 3,384 votes (96.6%) in the August 2026 primary [S4]. Mini PDC reporting [S5].

## Record

- In 2026 he and the sheriffs of Stevens, Ferry and Spokane counties sued to block Senate Bill 5974, which set age, experience, background and certification requirements for sheriffs; they called it undemocratic and a "loyalty oath" [S6]. A Thurston County judge enjoined parts of the law in their case in late April 2026 [S7].

## Positions

- His statement lists experience only and no policy positions [S1][S2].
