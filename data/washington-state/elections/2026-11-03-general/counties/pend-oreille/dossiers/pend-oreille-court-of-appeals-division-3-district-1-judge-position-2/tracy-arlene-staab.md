## Background

- Tracy Arlene Staab submitted no statement, experience or education to the 2026 voters' guide, and the county pamphlet carries no entry for her [S1].
- She won this open seat in 2020 against attorney Marshall Casey, replacing retiring Judge Kevin Korsmo [S2]. In Pend Oreille County she received 4,160 votes to Casey's 2,826 [S3].
- Before that she was a Spokane Municipal Court judge from 2009, and earlier an appellate attorney for the Spokane public defender's office and the Federal Defenders of Eastern Washington and a Court of Appeals law clerk; she was admitted to the bar in 1993 [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- In 2020 she stressed her criminal-law background, since many of the court's cases are criminal, and called the written briefs the most important part of an appeal [S2].
