## Background

- Marianne Nichols, of Newport, prefers the Republican Party and has been Pend Oreille County Auditor since 2007; she has been a Washington certified election administrator since 2009 and was president of the Washington Association of County Auditors in 2018-2019 [S1].
- In 2022 she won a fifth term over Tamara Newman, a Republican precinct committee officer who questioned election security and had the county Republican Party's endorsement; Nichols had the endorsement of the other elected county officials [S4][S5]. The SOS count was 4,354 to 1,699 [S3].
- Received 3,444 votes (97.5%) unopposed in the August 2026 primary [S6]; mini PDC reporting [S7].

## Record

- In 2022 she said the county's ballot tabulators cannot be reached over the internet and that her office had found one ballot from a deceased voter in 2020, which it reported to the sheriff and the FBI [S4].
- Says her office prepares the county's financial and accountability audits and has had successful audits every year [S4].

## Positions

- States a commitment to secure and fair elections, responsible financial management and customer service across licensing, recording, elections and financial services [S1][S2].
