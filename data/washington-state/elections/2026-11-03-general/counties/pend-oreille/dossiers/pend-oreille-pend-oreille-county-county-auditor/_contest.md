## What the office does
The county auditor runs elections and voter registration, records documents, licenses vehicles and keeps the county's financial records.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Auditor Marianne Nichols, in office since 2007, is the only candidate (S1). In 2022 she beat a Republican challenger who questioned election security, 70.7% to 27.7% (S4, S5).
