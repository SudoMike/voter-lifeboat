Statement
Serving the citizens of Pend Oreille County has been one of the greatest honors of my life. Since first being elected County Auditor in 2007, I have worked hard to provide professional, dependable, and transparent service to our community. I believe local government works best when it is accessible, accountable, and focused on the people it serves.
As County Auditor, I have remained committed to secure and fair elections, responsible financial management, and excellent customer service in every division of the Auditor’s Office, including vehicle licensing, recording, elections, and financial services. I am grateful to work alongside an incredible team dedicated to serving our citizens with integrity and respect.
Pend Oreille County is my home, and I feel blessed to have been born and raised here. My husband Jeff and I were blessed to raise our two boys, Cody & Luke, here and are grateful to have our daughter-in-law, Laura, as part of our family. I graduated from Newport High School and stay involved through volunteer service, ministry, youth activities, and community organizations.
Thank you for your trust, support, and confidence.
