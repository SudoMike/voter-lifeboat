Community Service
For nearly eight years in Newport, I have supported emergency preparedness, communications, veterans, and church ministry in the community. I serve as Vice President of the Pend Oreille County Radio Group (POCRG), Treasurer of the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT), and participate in Amateur Radio Emergency Services (ARES) organization. Statement
I will serve with honesty, integrity, and transparency. My leadership, technical, and operations experience—combined with an MBA focused on budgeting and long-range planning—prepare me to make thoughtful, responsible decisions for our utility district. Nearly eight years ago, my family chose Pend Oreille County for our forever home. Since then, I have invested in our community through emergency preparedness, communications, veterans’ organizations, and church leadership. I believe in practical problem-solving, listening to the community, maintaining reliable utility services, planning responsibly for future needs, and keeping rates as affordable as possible.
