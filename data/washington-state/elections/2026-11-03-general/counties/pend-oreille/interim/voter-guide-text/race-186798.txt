Statement
As a lifelong resident of Pend Oreille County, I truly love this community and the people who call it home. My husband, Forrest, and I raised our two sons here, and we are proud to have built our lives in this wonderful county. It has been both a privilege and an honor to serve as your County Clerk for the past 20 years.
The Clerk’s Office maintains all Superior Court records and provides essential services to both the Court and the public. Our office handles a wide range of responsibilities, including court operations, jury service, protection orders, legal financial obligations, and passports.
I am especially grateful for my dedicated staff whose compassion, professionalism, and knowledge make this service possible each day. Thank you for your continued support, trust, and vote.
