### Statement
Elected Experience
Dolly Hunt has been a prosecutor for over twenty-two years and has served as the elected prosecutor for three terms. Prior to joining the Pend Oreille County Prosecutor’s Office in 2006, she served three years as a deputy prosecutor in Okanogan County and has served as a special deputy prosecutor for Lincoln County.
Other Professional Experience
Dolly Hunt is a board member of the Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys, Municipal Research and Services Center (MRSC) and the National District Attorneys Association.
Education
Newport High School (1995); Gonzaga University, B.A. (1999); Santa Clara University School of Law, J.D. (2002).

Community Service
Dolly Hunt has served her community in many different areas. Please see www.dollyhunt.com for a complete biography.
Statement
With more than 22 years of prosecutorial experience, I have served with integrity and a steadfast commitment to protecting the rights of every citizen. Raised in Pend Oreille County, I understand and share the values of the people in our community.
I bring proven experience, a strong work ethic, and a deep commitment to serving our community. I will ensure the constitutional rights guaranteed under our State and Federal Constitutions are protected while holding those who violate the law accountable for their actions.
As your Prosecutor, I will continue to work closely with law enforcement, victims, and community partners to promote public safety and pursue justice fairly and responsibly. I believe in treating every person with professionalism, respect, and impartiality while making decisions based on the law and the facts of each case.
My experience in the courtroom, dedication to ethical service, and commitment to our community have prepared me to continue serving the citizens of Pend Oreille County with honesty, fairness, and accountability.
