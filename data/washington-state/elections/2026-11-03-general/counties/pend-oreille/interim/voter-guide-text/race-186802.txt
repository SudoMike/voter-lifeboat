I’ve worked every day to keep those promises and have done so by securing additional funding for road maintenance and snowplowing, helping bring nearly $5 million in housing support to Metaline Falls, and fighting to restore local property rights impacted by unnecessary state regulations. It is the honor of a lifetime to serve as your County Commissioner, and I respectfully ask for your vote so I can keep working hard for you.
