Statement
The most important function of the Treasurer's office is safeguarding public funds.
With the help and hard work of my dedicated, professional staff we have and will continue to strive to provide the best service to you, our taxpayers. Another function of the Benton County Treasurer’s office is that we are the Bank for the county and other groups that fall under the County umbrella. With the addition of several new software packages within the county the efficiency of these packages will help us to achieve office efficiencies. These programs were developed to make ours a more efficient, and more automated office.
We are working to make the investments of the funds that are entrusted to us earn the best and safest return. I have the desire and resolve to see the Treasurer's office achieve greater efficiencies and enhance the services we provide to the taxpayers as well as the other government offices that we serve. My goal is to build on these recent achievements and continue to strive for Benton County to have one of the most efficient county treasurer operations in the country.
Endorsed by the Benton County Republican Party.
