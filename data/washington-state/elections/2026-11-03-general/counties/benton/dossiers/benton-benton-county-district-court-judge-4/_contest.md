## What the office does

Benton County District Court (its jurisdiction is described in the Position 1 contest file, benton-benton-county-district-court-judge-1) has five judges and a commissioner [S2], and each judge is elected countywide to a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge John Ziobro, who has held Position 4 since his 2017 appointment and won it in 2022, is not on the 2026 ballot [S1][S2][S3]. District Court Commissioner Jennifer Johnson is the only candidate [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
