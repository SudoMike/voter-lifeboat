## Background

- Benton County District Court commissioner since January 2025 [S1][S2].
- She began her legal career in 2007 as a Benton County deputy prosecuting attorney and later worked as a criminal defense attorney, including one year as a contract defense attorney for Benton County [S1][S2]. She was a judge pro tempore for 10 years in Benton and Franklin County District Courts and Pasco Municipal Court [S1][S2].
- Education: graduated from Seattle Pacific University with a double major in communications and Latin American studies, and from Gonzaga Law School in 2007 [S2]. (Her voters'-guide entry gives graduation years of 2024 for Seattle Pacific and 2027 for Gonzaga, which do not match the court's bio or her stated 2007 start as a prosecutor [S1][S2].)
- Community service: 10 years on the board of the Children's Reading Foundation of the Mid-Columbia [S1].
- Not an incumbent judge: Position 4 is held by Judge John Ziobro, who is not on the 2026 ballot [S1][S2][S3].
