## What the office does

A State Representative writes and votes on Washington law and the state's operating, capital and transportation budgets for a two-year term [S1]. The House roll calls on ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on income over $1 million, and ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments to the parents' bill of rights, are examples of the votes members cast [S2][S3]. The 8th District ballot covers parts of Benton and Franklin counties [S1].
