## Background

- April Connors (Prefers Republican Party) is the 8th District's Position 2 state representative [S1].
- Her statement lists her as House Republican Floor Leader, assistant ranking member on the Appropriations Committee, and a member of the Housing and Rules committees [S1].
- She is a real-estate managing broker with 25 years in the Tri-Cities, holds a BA from WSU, and serves on the boards of Habitat for Humanity and Visit Tri-Cities [S1].

## Positions

- Focuses on affordability, public safety, "expanding housing opportunities," protecting local jobs and "fighting for Hanford" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found in the sources reviewed.
- **Parents and schools:** No statement found in the voters' guide. Record: voted against ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S3].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S2].
- **I-1 (parental rights in schools):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted against ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments I-1 would undo [S3].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- Voted Nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on household income over $1 million (2026-03-09, passed 51-46) [S2].
- Voted Nay on ESHB 1296 on House passage (2025-03-12, passed 56-37) and on final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, passed 59-39) [S3].
