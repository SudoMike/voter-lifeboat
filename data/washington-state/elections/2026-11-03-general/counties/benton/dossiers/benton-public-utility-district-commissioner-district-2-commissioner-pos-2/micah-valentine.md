## Background

Micah Valentine is in his second four-year term on the Kennewick School Board [S1]. His site lists him as the board's vice president [S5]. He owns or has owned several businesses, including Ascend, LLC, Ramp, LLC, a lawn aerating company and 4 Peaks Restoration. He has been sales and marketing manager for Pro Roofing NW and held sales roles with Social Rocketship and Complete Merchant Solutions [S1]. He graduated from Elma High School (1997), attended Clark College, and holds a B.A. in business management and Spanish from Southern Virginia University [S1]. He is an Eagle Scout and served an LDS mission [S1]. His filing lists a Kennewick address [S7].

## Positions

**Energy and the grid**
- Says "Olympia is pushing destructive political energy policies onto Eastern Washington" and that these mandates "threaten our grid, raise costs, and ignore the engineering realities" [S1][S2].
- Supports "protecting our dams, expanding nuclear energy, and keeping rates low" [S1]. Opposes "forcing our region to rely on wind and solar that weaken the grid" [S1][S2].
- Says he is "not against any technology that can stand on its own," but taxpayers should not pay for sources that need mandates or subsidies [S2]. Would be "on board" with a natural gas plant or a small nuclear reactor [S2].
- Says he would "advocate for Benton County in Olympia" for affordable power, local decision-making and grid reliability [S2].

**Rates, spending and growth**
- Pledges to keep rates low by "asking hard questions before spending money." Supports reliability investments, but not "spending millions on projects driven by politics instead of practical need" [S2].
- Sees reliable power as a driver of growth, "especially in South Kennewick." Lists "data infrastructure" among the users the system should be built to serve [S2].
- Says Benton PUD "has done a strong job" and that his goal "is not to dismantle that success" [S2].

**Governance philosophy**
- Describes his platform as "Common Sense Conservative Governance": fiscal responsibility, local control, individual liberty and strong families [S3]. His home page says, "Get conservatives back in local government!" [S3]
- Supports term limits [S3].

**Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy**
- His site says "Boys shouldn't be in girls' locker rooms" [S3]. His school board page says he defended "Title IX to protect girls' sports and locker rooms from biological males." It lists a board "Title 9 defense (boys in girls' sports & locker rooms) resolution" and a "Title IX complaint vs WA State" [S5].
- His school board page lists a "Pro Family. No (LGBTQIA+) agenda in school curriculum resolution" [S5].

**Parents and schools**
- Says "Parents deserve a meaningful voice in their children's education" and "Schools should educate, not parent" [S3].
- As a Kennewick School Board member, he lists these actions [S5]: a "Not permitted to teach CRT" policy, reduced sex-education and health curricula "with no hassle waivers," SEL curriculum limited to elementary schools, a classroom display policy, and safety officers in every school. He says the district offers parents "'no-questions-asked' opt-out options" for sex education.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-638 (girls' sports):** No statement on I-638 itself was found. His campaign site states positions on the same subject: sex-based eligibility for girls' sports and locker rooms [S3][S5].
- **I-1 (parental rights):** No statement on I-1 itself was found. His site records support for parental opt-outs and parental voice in schools [S3][S5].

## Record

His site credits the board with buying down a bond in 2025 to save taxpayers $4 million [S5]. These are his own characterizations; no independent district record was retrieved.
