## Background

- Stephanie Barnard (Prefers Republican Party) has been the 8th District's Position 1 state representative since 2022 [S1].
- Her statement lists legislative roles including ranking member of the Technology, Economic Development and Veterans Committee and service on the Capital Budget, Environment and Energy, Rules, Civic Health and Public Safety committees, the Nuclear Energy Caucus and the Women's Caucus [S1].
- She has worked in government affairs for the Tri-City Regional Chamber of Commerce, studied at Columbia Basin College and WGU, and founded ANGELS Network, a nonprofit providing car seats and training to low-income parents of newborns [S1].

## Positions

- Says she is committed to quality education, "protecting parental rights," and affordable gas, food, energy and housing [S1].
- Pledges to keep "fighting for lower taxes," public safety, workforce development and economic growth [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found in the sources reviewed.
- **Parents and schools:** Lists "protecting parental rights" among her commitments [S1]. Record: voted against ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S3].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives

- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted Nay on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S2].
- **I-1 (parental rights in schools):** No statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-08. Her statement lists protecting parental rights [S1]; record: voted against ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments I-1 would undo [S3].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** No public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record

- Voted Nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on household income over $1 million (2026-03-09, passed 51-46) [S2].
- Voted Nay on ESHB 1296 on House passage (2025-03-12, passed 56-37) and on final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, passed 59-39) [S3].
