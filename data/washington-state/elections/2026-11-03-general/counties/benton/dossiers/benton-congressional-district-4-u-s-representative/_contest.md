## What the office does

The U.S. Representative for Washington's 4th District votes on federal law, taxes, spending and oversight for a two-year term [S1]. The district runs from the Canadian border to Oregon and covers all of Benton, Douglas, Grant, Klickitat, Okanogan and Yakima counties and parts of Adams and Franklin counties [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

An open seat: six-term Republican Rep. Dan Newhouse is retiring and has said he will not endorse either finalist [S2]. Republican Amanda McKinney, a Yakima County commissioner endorsed by President Trump, took 34.9% in the 11-candidate August primary; Democrat John Duresky, a retired Air Force major and former Hanford Field Office manager from West Richland, took 29.9% [S2]. The Cook Political Report rates the district "solid R" [S2], and Trump carried it by nearly 21 points in 2024 [S4]. McKinney's campaign and allied outside groups outspent Duresky's by a wide margin in the primary [S5]. At the Sept. 30 debate in Richland they split on whether tariffs and the Iran war drive prices, on the administration's deportation program and state sanctuary law, and on how far Congress should check the president [S3].

## Differentiating issue axes

- immigration: McKinney backs the administration's deportations and opposes amnesty; Duresky calls the deportation program "immoral and un-American" and supports a path to legal status. Both back an expanded H-2A guest-worker program.
- climate: McKinney opposes clean-energy mandates, the Horse Heaven wind project and state fuel taxes; Duresky says climate change is real and backs wind, solar and small modular nuclear.
- reform: Duresky's Take BAC Congress pledge (term limits, stock-trading ban, lobbying moratorium, overturning Citizens United, Supreme Court ethics) against McKinney's party-leadership and super PAC backing.
- healthcare and spending: Duresky would restore ACA subsidies, fund rural hospitals and move toward universal coverage; McKinney campaigns on fiscal discipline and fraud prevention and states no health plan.
- social: McKinney's girls'-sports and pro-life positions; Duresky has no stated position in the sources found.
- defense: McKinney backs a rebuilt military and congressional deference on the Iran war; Duresky calls the war unconstitutional.
- experience: a sitting county commissioner against a first-time candidate with a federal military and civil-service career.
