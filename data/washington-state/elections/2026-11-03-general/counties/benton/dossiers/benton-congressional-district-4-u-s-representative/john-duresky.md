## Background

John Duresky, of West Richland, is a retired Air Force major who served 24 years, first as an avionics technician and then as a cyberspace operations officer, with three combat deployments [S1][S3]. He then worked in federal civil service, including five years as a project control officer at the Department of Energy's Hanford Field Office, where he says he managed technology and contractor-oversight programs budgeted at more than $100 million a year [S1][S3][S14]. He took an early retirement offer in 2025 during the administration's DOGE workforce cuts [S8][S16]. He has never held elected office [S1]. He finished second in the August primary with 29.9% [S14].

## Positions

- **Taxes:** would "reform the tax code to close loopholes like stepped-up basis and carried interest" [S2]; describes his affordability agenda as including "tax reforms that favor working people" [S8].
- **Spending and the economy:** would invest in roads, bridges and broadband, raise the federal minimum wage, and "rebuild trust in labor unions" [S1][S2]. Would protect Hanford cleanup jobs and federal employees from "politically motivated mass firings" [S2]. Says working families face high costs and "we need to focus on lowering these costs at home instead of wasting taxpayer dollars abroad" [S1]. Wants to remove tariffs he calls harmful and says tariffs should be set by Congress, not the president [S1][S7]. Said he sees promise in the housing package recently passed by Congress [S11].
- **Healthcare:** would keep rural hospitals open, restore and expand ACA subsidies, protect Medicare and Medicaid, and lower drug costs; "Ultimately, moving toward universal healthcare coverage should be our goal" [S1][S2]. Says rural hospitals are "in genuine danger" unless Congress fixes Medicaid and Medicare reimbursement rates [S9].
- **Immigration:** calls the administration's deportation program "immoral and un-American" [S7][S10]. Says ICE "does have a function" but Congress should define its mission [S10], and "we have to get those armed, masked, badge-less people off our streets" [S8]. Supports a "fast, fair and affordable path to legal status" for long-term undocumented residents without criminal records [S10], while saying he does not believe in open borders and that asylum needs reform [S7][S8]. Would reform the H-2A visa program [S1].
- **Energy and climate:** "Climate change is real ... we have to do everything we can to fix it: windmills, solar, small modular nuclear" [S9]. Endorsed by the Washington State Democratic Party's Environment and Climate Council [S6]. Would protect irrigation rights and oppose sales of public lands [S1][S2].
- **Defense and foreign policy:** says "I know what war costs" [S1] and calls the Iran war "a pretty plain-label violation of our Constitution" [S5]. Blames the war and tariffs for higher fuel and fertilizer prices [S7].
- **Government and reform:** signed the Take BAC Congress pledge: congressional term limits, a lobbying moratorium after leaving office, a ban on congressional stock trading, overturning Citizens United, and an enforceable Supreme Court ethics code [S4][S7][S15]. Would protect Social Security from cuts or privatization [S2] and strengthen whistleblower protections [S2].
- **Veterans:** would reduce VA wait times, improve mental-health services and support veterans' transition to civilian life [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found in the sources reviewed. His platform describes itself as "not culture wars" [S2].
- **Parents and schools (recorded, not scored for a federal race):** no position found in the sources reviewed. The Washington Education Association endorses him [S4].
- **Technology:** no AI, data-center or platform-regulation position found in the sources reviewed.
