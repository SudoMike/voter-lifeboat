## What the office does

The three-member Board of County Commissioners is Benton County's legislative authority. It fixes county taxes, approves county spending and audits county accounts, manages county property, and oversees county roads and public buildings [S3]. District 2 includes Prosser, Benton City, and parts of Richland, Kennewick, Plymouth and the Horse Heaven Hills area [S4].

## How it is elected

- **Nominated in the district.** Under RCW 36.32.040, only the voters of a commissioner district nominate candidates for that district's seat, "to be voted for at the following general election" [S2]. In the Aug. 4, 2026 primary, only District 2 voters had the race: 12,329 votes were cast, and Michael Alvarez took 12,051 [S6].
- **Elected countywide.** In the general, the race appears on every Benton County ballot. The voters' guide lists it as "Countywide Commissioner District #2" [S1]. In the 2022 general, the District 2 race drew 66,710 votes, more than the 57,567 cast in the uncontested countywide auditor race on the same ballot [S5].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent Michael Alvarez (Prefers Republican Party), chair of the board and a former Richland mayor, is the only candidate [S1]. He won the seat in 2022 over William "Bill" Jenkin, 65.1% to 33.39% [S5].
