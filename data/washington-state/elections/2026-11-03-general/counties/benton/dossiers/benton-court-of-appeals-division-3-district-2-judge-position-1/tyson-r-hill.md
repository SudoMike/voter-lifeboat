## Background

- **Appointment.** Gov. Bob Ferguson appointed Hill to Division III on Nov. 19, 2025, effective Jan. 1, 2026. He fills the seat of retiring Judge George Fearing. It was Ferguson's first appellate appointment [S2][S3]. The Columbia Basin Herald reported that Hill is the first Division III judge from Grant County [S3]. Because he was appointed, he must stand at this general election for the remaining two years of the term [S1][S4].
- **Trial bench.** Grant County Superior Court judge from 2021, when Gov. Jay Inslee appointed him; he then ran for a full term beginning in 2024 [S2][S3]. Before that he served more than seven years on the Grant County District Court bench [S2]. He co-founded and presided over Grant County's first therapeutic drug court and regularly served as a pro tem judge on Division III [S2].
- **Earlier career.** Four years as lead appellate attorney in the Grant County Prosecutor's Office, arguing before Division III and the state Supreme Court and prosecuting felonies including murder, arson and assault [S1][S2]. Law clerk to Washington Supreme Court Justice James M. Johnson. Consultant and staff assistant to the U.S.-China Economic & Security Review Commission. Senate Finance Committee intern [S1][S2].
- **Associations.** Has served on the Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys' appellate committee, the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association board of governors, and the Superior Court Judges' Association ethics and rural courts committees [S2].
- **Education.** J.D., University of Washington School of Law (2008); B.S., Brigham Young University (2004) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Describes his role as reviewing trial-court decisions "to ensure the law is followed," adding, "I have no agenda beyond that" [S1].
- Says parties, law enforcement, victims and the community "want a judge who is impartial, takes their case seriously, and correctly applies the law" [S1].
- On his appointment he said he looked forward "to transitioning from a referee on the field to a replay official in the booth" [S2].
