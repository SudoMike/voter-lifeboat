## What the office does
The 16th District elects two State Representatives, each to a two-year term; this is Position 1 [S1][S2]. Representatives sponsor and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including K-12 funding, the tax structure, housing and land-use rules and criminal-justice statutes [S2]. The 16th District covers Walla Walla County and parts of Benton, Franklin and Grant counties [S2].

## Race dynamics
Republican Mark Klicker, a three-term incumbent, farm-and-ranch realtor and timber producer, faces Democrat Kyle Palmer, a Richland real-estate professional and former Richland Planning Commission chair running for the first time [S1][S2]. Klicker led the primary 60.36% to 39.55% (mark-klicker.md S13). Palmer reported no campaign contributions to the PDC (kyle-palmer.md S6).

The candidates differ most on taxes. Klicker voted against the 2026 tax on income over $1 million and the 2025 B&O and services tax bills and said at a July forum that the income tax will eventually reach everyone unless the repeal initiative passes (mark-klicker.md S3, S4, S5, S12). Palmer said he is not troubled by the 9.9% rate on income above $1 million but wants a lean budget before new taxes (kyle-palmer.md S3). Both call for spending restraint: Klicker for cutting waste and shrinking government, Palmer for outcome-based budgeting and no new long-term commitments without a funding plan [S2]. On data centers, Klicker says existing state rules are already strict; Palmer would require transparency on power and water and make companies pay for infrastructure and local benefits [S2]. Klicker's record also includes votes against the 2025 clean fuels bill, the 2025 tenant housing-stability bill and ESHB 1296 (mark-klicker.md S6, S7, S8). Palmer has not stated positions on those subjects or on the three statewide initiatives.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Klicker's recorded no votes and opposition to the income tax vs. Palmer's mixed answer on the same tax.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** three-term incumbent vs. a first-time candidate with planning-commission experience.
3. **Technology:** Klicker sees data centers as adequately regulated; Palmer would add conditions.
4. **Climate and energy:** Klicker backs natural gas, hydro and nuclear and voted against the clean fuels bill; Palmer has no stated climate position beyond reliable and advanced energy.
5. **Parents and schools:** Klicker voted against ESHB 1296; Palmer has no stated position.
