## What the office does

The county treasurer receives all money due the county and pays it out on warrants attested by the auditor [S2]. The Benton County Treasurer's Office describes itself as "the property tax collector for Benton County and the Cities, School Districts, Fire Departments, and all the other taxing authorities in the County." As the county's "banker," it also collects fees, licenses and bond and special-assessment revenue [S3]. The treasurer collects taxes; it does not set values or levy rates [S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent Kenneth Spencer Jr. (Prefers Republican Party), treasurer since 2017, is the only candidate [S1]. He was re-elected without opposition in 2022 [S4].
