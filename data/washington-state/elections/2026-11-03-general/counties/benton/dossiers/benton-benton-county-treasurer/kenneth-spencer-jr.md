## Background

- Kenneth Spencer Jr. (Prefers Republican Party) has been Benton County Treasurer since 2017 and a Benton County precinct committee officer since 2012 [S1][S2].
- He lists 20 years as a college professor, 55 years as a commercial appraiser, four years as a county supervisor, and roles as general manager and CFO of three private businesses [S1].
- He holds a B.S. in agricultural management from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo and lists graduate study at WSU, CWU and EWU [S1].
- He has been a Boy Scout leader for 35 years and is an Eagle Scout [S1].
- He was re-elected without opposition in 2022, with 52,499 votes (97.94%) [S3].

## Positions

- Calls "safeguarding public funds" the office's most important function [S1].
- Cites new county software packages that he says will make the office more efficient and automated [S1].
- Pledges to invest entrusted funds for "the best and safest return" and wants Benton County to have "one of the most efficient county treasurer operations in the country" [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
