## Background

Brad Klippert has been a Benton County sheriff's deputy since 1999, working in patrol and investigations and as school resource officer for the Kiona-Benton City School District. He was earlier a deputy in Pierce County (S2, S6). He represented the 8th Legislative District in the state House for 14 years, from 2008 to 2022, working on public safety, criminal justice, veterans' affairs and education (S1, S2, S4). He reports more than 30 years of military service in the Army, Army Reserve, National Guard and Washington State Guard, including 20 years as a helicopter pilot, and retired as a colonel and commander of the Washington State Guard (S1, S2). He has taught at levels from first grade through college. He holds a bachelor's degree in biblical studies and behavioral science and a master's in teaching, and trained as a paramedic and EMT (S1). He prefers the Republican Party (S1).

He won Kennewick City Council Position 4 in November 2025 with 66.84% of the vote and was sworn in that month (S4). He was one of three Republican nominees for the 2025 sheriff vacancy; commissioners appointed Mike Clark (S4, S8). In the August 4 primary he received 12,051 votes (29.2%) to Clark's 28,696 (69.5%) (S9).

## Positions

### Public safety and policing
- "I will defend the rights of citizens, support our law enforcement officers, and work every day to keep Benton County safe." He also says "We will not allow crime, fear, or government overreach to weaken the values that make this county strong" (S1).
- Called public safety the most urgent issue: "we're going to serve the citizens of Benton County" (S3).
- On deputies: "They're going to know that I, as the sheriff of Benton County, I'm going to invest in them." His statement says they "deserve leadership that supports them, equips them, and stands beside them" (S1, S3).
- As a council candidate in 2025 he named combating drug addiction as his top priority (S6).
- As a state representative in 2017 he supported the death penalty "in the interests of swift and sure justice" and called the appeals process "too long, too drawn out and ... way too expensive" (S7).

### Transparency and rights
- "Citizens deserve transparency, fairness, and a Sheriff who listens to their concerns" (S1).
- "I believe in God, the Constitution, and the responsibility we all share to protect our families and communities" (S1).
