## Background

- Benton County District Court judge since 2021. He was appointed to Position 2 in May 2021 and elected in November 2022 [S1][S2]. In 2022 he was the only named candidate and received 98.9% of the vote [S3]. He is the court's presiding judge for 2025-2026 [S2].
- Earlier career, per his statement: Franklin County deputy prosecuting attorney (1994-2002); associate at Roach & Petersen (2002-2007); his own practice (2007-2009); partner at Bell, Brown & Rio (2009-2021). He was also legal counsel to Greater Columbia Behavioral Health (2009-2021) and a judge pro tem in Benton and Franklin County District Courts [S1]. The court's bio adds that in private practice he served as prosecutor for Richland, West Richland and Pasco [S2].
- Education: J.D., University of Puget Sound School of Law (now Seattle University), 1992; B.S. in accounting, Brigham Young University, 1988 [S1][S2].
- Community service: Greater Tri-Cities YMCA board of trustees since 2000, including as board president from 2006 to 2009 [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Describes District Court as "a high-volume court" that needs a judge who "thinks quickly and decisively but also with compassion and empathy," and says most people "just want a chance to be heard and be treated fairly" [S1].
- Oversees the court's Mental Health Court [S1].
