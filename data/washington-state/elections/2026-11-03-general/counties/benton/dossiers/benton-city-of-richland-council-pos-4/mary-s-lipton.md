## Background

Mary S. Lipton is a staff scientist at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, an adjunct professor at WSU, and a small business owner [S1]. The Herald describes her as a "bioanalytic chemist" at PNNL's Environmental Molecular Sciences Laboratory and an adjunct professor at WSU Tri-Cities [S2]. She lists a Ph.D. in biochemistry, with "South Carolina" given as the school [S1]. She has lived in Richland for more than 30 years and raised her family there [S1][S2]. Her listed elected roles are professional and civic, not public office: the American Association for the Advancement of Science biology section steering committee, the American Society for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology public affairs advisory committee, and secretary of the Orchard Hills HOA [S1]. Her volunteer work includes Richland School District PTO and Bomber Boosters, Mid Columbia Ballet, youth sports coaching, Math is Cool coaching, and judging science fairs [S1]. She finished second in the August 2026 primary with 27.9% [S3].

## Positions

**Growth and infrastructure**
- Lists "managing growth and infrastructure responsibly" as a city priority. Wants to balance "responsible growth with preserving the character, quality of life, and environmental resources that make Richland unique" [S1].
- Told the Herald: "Richland is walking a fine line between growth and maintaining its identity." She said she favors a balanced approach and "lots of debate" as Richland attracts newcomers, including industry [S2].

**Economy, safety and amenities**
- Lists "supporting economic stability and workforce development" and "protecting public safety, parks, riverfront access, and community resources for future generations" [S1].
