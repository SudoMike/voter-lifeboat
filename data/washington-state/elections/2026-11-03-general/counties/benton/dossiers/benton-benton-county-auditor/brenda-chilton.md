## Background

- Brenda Chilton (Prefers Republican Party) has been Benton County Auditor since 2010 [S1].
- Before that she was chief deputy auditor (2005-2009), a supervisor in the Auditor's Office (1998-2005), and a paralegal (1987-1998) [S1].
- She chairs the Washington State Association of County Auditors' vehicle licensing committee and sits on the state Election Administration and Certification Board and the Voting Systems Review Board [S1]. She is a past president of that association [S1].
- She is a certified election administrator (State of Washington) and a Certified Elections/Registration Administrator (Election Center/Auburn University) [S1].
- She is a past president of Tri-Cities Republican Women and of the Kiwanis Club of Atomic City [S1].
- She was re-elected without opposition in 2022, with 56,435 votes (98.03%) [S2].

## Positions

- Calls "election integrity and security" among her highest priorities. Says she is committed "to ensuring every lawful vote is counted accurately and securely" [S1].
- Cites the opening of the new Benton County Elections Center, technology and modernization investments, and a multi-year project to digitize historical county records [S1].
