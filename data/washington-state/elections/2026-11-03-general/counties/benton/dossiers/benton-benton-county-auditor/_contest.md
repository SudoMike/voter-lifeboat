## What the office does

The county auditor is the recorder of deeds and other documents and keeps the county's accounts, including its warrant register and annual financial statements [S2]. The auditor is also "ex officio the supervisor of all primaries and elections" in the county [S3]. Brenda Chilton describes the Benton office's divisions as Elections, Recording, Accounting and Licensing [S1].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent Brenda Chilton (Prefers Republican Party), auditor since 2010, is the only candidate [S1]. She was re-elected without opposition in 2022 [S4].
