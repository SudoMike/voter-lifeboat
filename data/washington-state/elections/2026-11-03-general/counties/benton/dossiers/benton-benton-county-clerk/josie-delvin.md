## Background

- Josie Delvin (Prefers Republican Party) has been Benton County Clerk for 20 years [S1][S2].
- Before that she worked in Benton County District Court for 17 years [S1].
- She has lived in Benton County for 40 years and lives in Richland [S1].
- She was re-elected without opposition in 2022, with 54,913 votes (97.49%) [S3].

## Positions

The changes she cites from the past four years [S1]:
- new office space in the Justice Center, with more privacy for the public
- a larger jury assembly room and an upgraded jury system that lets jurors respond electronically
- electronic filing
- digitized superior court paper case files
- a continuing project to digitize historical records from microfilm

She notes the office processes "hundreds of passport applications each year" [S1].
