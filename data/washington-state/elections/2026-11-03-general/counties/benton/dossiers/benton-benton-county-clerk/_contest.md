## What the office does

The county clerk is clerk of the superior court. The office keeps the court's dockets and its record of filings, proceedings, verdicts, orders and judgments [S2]. Josie Delvin says the Benton office also handles jury management, electronic filing, and passport applications at its Prosser and Kennewick offices [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent Josie Delvin (Prefers Republican Party), clerk for 20 years, is the only candidate [S1]. She was re-elected without opposition in 2022 [S4].
