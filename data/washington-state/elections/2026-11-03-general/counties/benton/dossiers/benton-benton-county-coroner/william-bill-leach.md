## Background

- William "Bill" Leach (Prefers Republican Party) is the Benton County Coroner. He says he has served two terms [S1][S2].
- He was re-elected without opposition in 2022, with 55,102 votes (98.22%) [S3].
- He submitted no education or professional history to the voters' guide [S1].

## Positions

What he cites from his time in office [S1]:
- Calls for service have "nearly double[d]" as the county grew.
- The office hired a full-time forensic pathologist, so autopsies are done in-house rather than hours away.
- Benton is one of two Eastern Washington counties with a staff forensic pathologist. Contract autopsies for other counties bring in revenue that offsets county costs.
- The office earned IACME national accreditation, and all staff are state-certified medicolegal death investigators.

He pledges to keep modernizing the office [S1].
