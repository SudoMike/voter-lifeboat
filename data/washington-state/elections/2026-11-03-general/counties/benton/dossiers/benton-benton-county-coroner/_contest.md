## What the office does

The coroner investigates deaths that fall under the office's jurisdiction. The coroner may hold an inquest when a death is suspected to be unnatural, violent, the result of unlawful means, or suspicious [S2]. William Leach says the Benton office has a staff forensic pathologist who performs autopsies in-house and under contract for other counties [S1]. The office is in Kennewick [S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent William "Bill" Leach (Prefers Republican Party), coroner for two terms, is the only candidate [S1]. He was re-elected without opposition in 2022 [S4].
