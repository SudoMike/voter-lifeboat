## Background

Talesha "Tali" Sams is a Benton City attorney who has practiced law for 25 years [S1][S2]. Her 2026 statement lists her legal experience as Adult Protective Services attorney, Title 11 court visitor, District Court judge pro tem, prosecutor, public defender, private practice and county bar association trustee [S1]. She says she has practiced civil, criminal and administrative law, worked as a prosecutor in both District and Superior Courts, and has been a King County District Court judge pro tem since 2015 [S1].

- **Earlier career (2020 report).** After law school she opened a general practice in federal, state and district courts. After seven years in practice she became an Adams County deputy prosecutor, handling infractions, misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors in District Court and felonies in Superior Court and the Court of Appeals, Division III [S2]. Since 2008 she has worked for DSHS Adult Protective Services on cases of abuse, neglect and financial exploitation of vulnerable adults, and she has served as a guardian ad litem in Title 11 cases in Benton County [S1][S2].
- **Before law.** U.S. Army veteran. The 2020 report says she served four years and is a combat veteran [S1][S2]. She also worked as a nurse, respiratory therapist and emergency medical technician [S1][S2].
- **Education.** Law degree, Gonzaga University; bachelor's degree in arts and social sciences, Washington State University; associate degree in respiratory therapy/nursing, Walla Walla Community College [S1].
- **Community service (as listed in the voters' guide).** YWCA domestic-violence shelter; Benton-Franklin County Legal Aid Society; free legal clinics; Veterans Court mentor; Office of Military and Veterans Legal Assistance; county bar associations; PFLAG and Pride Foundation [S1].

## Positions

- **Judicial approach.** She says she "would uphold the law and would not legislate any personal agenda." A judge, she writes, should be "knowledgeable and experienced in all matters before them," should have "humility, a positive demeanor and temperament," and should "treat all those who come before the bench with respect and fairness regardless of their social or economic backgrounds" [S1].
- **Public service.** She says she has "always made it my priority to serve the public and advocate for the disadvantaged" [S1]. She has said she went into law because of people she met who could not get access to justice because of their social and economic backgrounds [S2].
- **Bail, diversion, therapeutic courts.** No position found in the sources reviewed. Her community-service list includes serving as a Veterans Court mentor [S1].

## Record

- No published rulings were found. Her bench experience is as a King County District Court judge pro tem since 2015 [S1][S2].
- **Prior campaigns.** She ran for Benton County District Court Position 1 in 2018 and lost to incumbent Terry Tanner, 34.5% (21,694 votes) to 65.5% [S3]. She ran for Benton-Franklin Superior Court Position 1 in the August 2020 primary and placed sixth of six in Benton County, with 8.9% [S4].
