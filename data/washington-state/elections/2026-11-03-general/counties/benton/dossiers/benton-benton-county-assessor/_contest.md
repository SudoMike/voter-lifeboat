## What the office does

The assessor lists and values property for taxation. State law requires all taxable real and personal property to be listed and assessed every year at its value as of January 1 [S2]. The Benton County Assessor's page explains that the 1% levy limit caps growth in a taxing district's total levy, not the tax on an individual home. A home's tax can rise or fall depending on how its value changes relative to others in the district [S3]. The assessor sets values; taxing districts and voters set levies.
