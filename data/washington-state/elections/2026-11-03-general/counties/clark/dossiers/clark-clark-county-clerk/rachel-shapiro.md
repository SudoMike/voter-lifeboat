## Background

Rachel Shapiro is the founder and owner of Shapiro Mediation LLC, which provides divorce mediation services, including financial forensics and asset analysis [S1]. She earned a law degree in 2000 from the University of Massachusetts School of Law in Dartmouth and a B.S. from the University of Nebraska [S1][S3]. The Columbian describes her as a legal professional and mediation business owner [S13]. Her only elected role is precinct committee officer for Precinct 681 in Clark County [S1]. She has volunteered with the Camas-Washougal Parent Co-op Preschool, the PTA and the Girl Scouts [S1]. She says she was once a self-represented litigant who needed to modify a decree, that her case reached the Court of Appeals, and that the experience led her to law school [S2]. The office is nonpartisan and the ballot lists no party preference [S1].

## Positions

### Court technology and cost
- Calls for replacing the county's separate OnBase document-imaging system with the state-supported Odyssey case-management system, which she says the county receives at no cost. "As a business owner, I am appalled by the waste of taxpayer money" [S5].
- Cites a $180,308 OnBase licensing cost for the Clerk's Office in the 2026 budget. She notes that license sales to outside users are expected to offset it. She also cites separate District Court OnBase costs of $87,744 to $97,661 a year for 2025-2027 [S5].
- Notes that Presiding Superior Court Judge Derek Vanderwood presented an Odyssey-OnBase comparison to the County Council in July 2025. She calls for a transparent review of costs, security and record preservation before any transition [S5].
- The Columbian reports that the race has become a debate over taxpayer-funded court technology, office accessibility and help for self-represented filers [S14].

### Access to the courts
- Proposes a courthouse Legal Aid Department with attorneys and trained staff for civil matters such as family law, eviction, protection orders and guardianship. She would fund it through state and federal grants, Office of Civil Legal Aid money, county appropriations, nonprofit partners and volunteers, so that local taxpayers do not bear the entire cost [S6].
- Says the clerk's office sells the forms people need to file, and wants free fillable forms online [S6].
- Proposes a "Lawyer of the Day" program, modeled on Massachusetts, connecting self-represented litigants with volunteer attorneys for limited-scope help [S7].
- Proposes a court dispute-resolution center and supports encouraging, or where court rules permit requiring, mediation in appropriate family-law cases. She would exclude domestic-violence and safety cases [S4][S8]. She argues this would free judicial time for foster-care and dependency reviews, and acknowledges that the clerk cannot order judges to send cases to mediation [S8].
- Would fund dispute resolution through existing dedicated funds, grants, partnerships and sliding-scale fees [S9].

### Transparency and service
- Supports "greater transparency in government budgeting and spending, including easier online access to financial information" [S1].
- Would hold a monthly "Lunch with the Clerk" open office hour on the first Wednesday at noon [S1][S10].
- Says a courthouse "should operate like a well-managed business" [S4].

## Endorsements

Her campaign says she is recommended by The Columbian's Editorial Board and the Clark County Democratic Party [S10]. The Columbian's recommendation was for the primary, where it named either Weber or Shapiro [S12]. For the general election the editorial board recommended Weber [S15].

## Scoring notes

- Experience: a first-time candidate for county office (her only elected role is party precinct committee officer) against a 16-year incumbent [S1][S13]. She frames her case on legal and mediation experience rather than on newness [S3][S4].
- Spending: her platform is mixed. She wants to eliminate what she calls duplicative technology spending [S5], and she proposes new courthouse programs (legal aid, Lawyer of the Day, dispute resolution) that she would fund mainly through grants, partnerships and fees [S6][S7][S9].
- Transparency: proposes online budget access and monthly open office hours [S1].
