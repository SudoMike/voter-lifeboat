## Background
Abigail E. Bartlett has been a Clark County District Court judge since 2021, after serving as a District Court commissioner (2017-2021) and as a senior deputy prosecutor in the Clark County Prosecutor's Office (2006-2017) [S1]. She holds a J.D. from Seattle University School of Law [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
- Says she presides over Treatment Alternative Court and DUI Court, which aim to "reduce criminal recidivism and increase community safety by addressing the underlying substance abuse disorders" [S1].
