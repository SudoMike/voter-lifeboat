## What the office does
The treasurer collects property taxes for the county and its taxing districts, invests public funds, and manages the county investment pool and debt.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: incumbent Treasurer Alishia Topper, in office since 2019, is the only candidate. The race is on every Clark County ballot [S1].
