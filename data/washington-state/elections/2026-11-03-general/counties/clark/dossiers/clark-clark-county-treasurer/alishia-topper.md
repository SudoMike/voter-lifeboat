## Background
Alishia Topper has been Clark County Treasurer since 2019 and served on the Vancouver City Council from 2014 to 2018 [S1]. She is immediate past president of the Washington State Association of County Treasurers and a Vancouver Housing Authority commissioner [S1]. She holds an MPA from Portland State University and is a certified public funds investment manager [S1].

## Positions
- **Spending and accountability:** pledges to "safeguard tax dollars, invest wisely for positive returns, and ensure we follow financial best practices" [S1].

## Record
- Says her office manages an investment pool portfolio exceeding $1 billion and last year allocated $47 million in interest earnings, the highest in the pool's history [S1].
- Says the office lent short-term to two school districts for cash flow and received clean financial and performance audits with no findings [S1].
