## Background
Ben Christly is the Democratic candidate for Position 1 in the 17th Legislative District [S1]. He is a Navy veteran and a registered nurse with 24 years in health care, including emergency and critical care, who now works as a flight nurse; he is a former small-business owner [S1][S3]. He holds a bachelor of science in nursing and has done graduate study in leadership [S1]. He coaches youth football and has volunteered with Meals on Wheels [S1][S3]. He announced his campaign on January 19, 2026 [S6].

He is an elected Clark County Charter Review Commissioner, one of three from County Council District 3 on the 2026-2027 commission [S5][S1]. He describes the role as nonpartisan and focused on accountability [S1]. He has not held legislative office [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** says "working families and small businesses should not be carrying a disproportionate share of the tax burden while billion-dollar corporations find ways to avoid paying theirs," and calls a "fair and responsible tax structure" essential [S2]. No statement on the 2026 tax on income over $1 million was found.
- **Spending and budget:** calls for "responsible budgeting" that invests in schools, affordable health care and economic opportunity [S2].
- **Education:** wants to fully fund public schools, expand early learning including preschool and pre-kindergarten, and expand trade schools, apprenticeships, vocational programs and community colleges [S2][S1].
- **Housing:** pamphlet priority is "building more housing" [S1]; his site calls for housing policies that "support working families, stabilize costs" [S2].
- **Health care:** wants affordable, accessible health care across the district [S2][S1].
- **Small business:** says he will work to make Washington "more small-business-friendly," citing red tape and rising costs [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no direct statement found. Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates and Pro-Choice Washington [S4].
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found on parental notification, record access or opt-outs. Endorsed by the Washington Education Association PAC and the American Federation of Teachers [S4].
- **Approach:** says he rejects the "us versus them" mindset and wants to work across differences [S2][S1].
