## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House, for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The 17th District ballot appears in Clark, Klickitat and Skamania counties [S1].

## Race dynamics
Republican Rep. Kevin Waters is seeking a third term [S1]. He won the seat in 2022 with 53.15% over Democrat Terri Niles [S3] and had no ballot opponent in 2024 [S4]. He voted against the 2026 tax on income over $1 million (ESSB 6346) [S2]. His challenger is Democrat Ben Christly, a Navy veteran and flight nurse who sits on the elected Clark County Charter Review Commission [S1][S5].

Waters has a full House voting record: no votes on ESSB 6346, the 2025 capital gains, excise and B&O tax bills, the 2025-27 operating budget, rent-increase limits and ESHB 1296, plus an amendment to expire the 2025 services taxes (kevin-waters.md S3-S11). Christly has no legislative record; he campaigns on school funding, early learning, health-care access and a tax structure that asks more of large corporations (ben-christly.md S2). Waters reports about $399k raised to Christly's $164k (kevin-waters.md S20; ben-christly.md S7).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Waters votes against new state taxes and has proposed cutting them; Christly wants large corporations to carry more of the tax burden.
2. **Spending:** Waters voted against the operating budget and says the state has a "results problem"; Christly emphasizes fully funding schools and early learning.
3. **Record vs. renewal:** a two-term incumbent and former port commissioner vs. a first-time legislative candidate who holds a county charter-review seat.
4. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Christly is endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates and Pro-Choice Washington; Waters' campaign site says he will not oppose voter-approved abortion rights. Evidence on both sides is thin.
5. **Parents and schools:** Waters voted against ESHB 1296's changes to the parental-rights law; no Christly position found.
