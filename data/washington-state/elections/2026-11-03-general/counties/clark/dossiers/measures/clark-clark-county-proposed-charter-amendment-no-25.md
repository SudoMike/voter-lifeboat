## What it does
The ballot title says that after January 1, 2027 the amendment "would require any new councilmanic tax assessed, levied, or increased to have a two-third affirmative vote by the Council," and would not apply to fees, rates and charges, special assessments, or existing taxes and their renewals that do not seek a higher rate [S2]. The explanatory statement says it defines "new councilmanic tax" and aims to "limit tax increases without broad council support" [S1]. With five councilors, opponents note, two-thirds means four votes [S1].

**Who proposed it.** The Clark County Charter Review Commission, fifteen commissioners elected in November 2025 (three per council district) for 2026-2027 terms, proposes charter amendments; any amendment must be approved by Clark County voters at a general election [S4]. The adopting resolution is No. 26-08 [S2][S3].

## Cost
No tax or rate in the ballot title; it changes the vote needed for future council-imposed taxes [S1][S2].

## Arguments for
The statement in favor (John Jay, chair; Paul Harris; Brian Lewallen) says [S1]:
- Tax increases "should require broad support" and a supermajority makes councilmembers build consensus.
- It "does not change any current tax or fee" or reduce any service.
The rebuttal says "just three councilors can impose a new tax on 550,000 Clark County residents" [S1].

## Arguments against
The statement against (Janet Landesberg, chair; Dorothy Gasque; Julie Koepp) says [S1]:
- It will not cut taxes; most taxes are not set by the Council, and the Council skipped the allowed 1% property-tax increase in 7 of the last 14 years.
- Law and justice is 73% of the county budget; requiring four of five votes lets two councilors block funding for services, which "would make Clark County the most restrictive in Washington."

## Endorsements and campaign
The commission's members are Patrick Adigweme, Ann Donnelly, Eric LaBrant (District 1); Brandon Erickson (chair), Cathie Garber, Kim Hamlik (District 2); Ben Christly, Janet Landesberg, John Jay (District 3); Liz Cline, Dorothy Gasque, Morgan Holmgren (District 4); Brad Benton, Duncan Hoss, Peter Silliman (District 5) [S4]. Jay, Landesberg and Gasque sit on the commission [S4].
