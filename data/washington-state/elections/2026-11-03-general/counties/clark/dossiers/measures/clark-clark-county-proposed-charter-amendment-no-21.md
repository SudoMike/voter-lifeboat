## What it does
The ballot title says the amendment "would give the County Council the concurrent authority with the County Manager to nominate members to all boards and commissions, with the exception of the Ethics Review Commission" [S2]. The explanatory statement says it revises charter Section 2.4.H and that the Council retains final appointment authority [S1].

**Who proposed it.** The Clark County Charter Review Commission, fifteen commissioners elected in November 2025 (three per council district) for 2026-2027 terms, proposes charter amendments; any amendment must be approved by Clark County voters at a general election [S4]. The adopting resolution is No. 26-23 [S2][S3].

## Cost
No tax or rate in the ballot title; no fiscal statement was published [S1][S2].

## Arguments for
The statement in favor (Patrick Adigweme, chair; Eric LaBrant) says the Council can now nominate only to the Planning Commission, Historic Preservation Commission and Board of Equalization; extending nomination to other boards could reduce the burden on staff and help keep volunteer seats filled [S1].

## Arguments against
None filed: "After recruitment attempts, no person within the jurisdiction volunteered to write a statement against" [S1].

## Endorsements and campaign
The commission's members are Patrick Adigweme, Ann Donnelly, Eric LaBrant (District 1); Brandon Erickson (chair), Cathie Garber, Kim Hamlik (District 2); Ben Christly, Janet Landesberg, John Jay (District 3); Liz Cline, Dorothy Gasque, Morgan Holmgren (District 4); Brad Benton, Duncan Hoss, Peter Silliman (District 5) [S4]. Adigweme and LaBrant sit on the commission [S4].
