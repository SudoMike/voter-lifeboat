## What it does
The ballot title says the amendment "would permit the County Council to conduct, or cause to be conducted, performance and program audits to review the effectiveness and efficiency of the programs and operations of the County" and require the Council to establish by ordinance "an independent county auditing process" within the legislative branch [S2]. The explanatory statement says it creates charter Sections 2.7 and 2.8, that audits may be done by qualified independent contractors under generally accepted government auditing standards, and that annual state-mandated audits continue unchanged [S1].

**Who proposed it.** The Clark County Charter Review Commission, fifteen commissioners elected in November 2025 (three per council district) for 2026-2027 terms, proposes charter amendments; any amendment must be approved by Clark County voters at a general election [S4]. The adopting resolution is No. 26-14 [S2][S3].

## Cost
No tax or rate in the ballot title [S2]. The statement against says the amendment's fiscal analysis estimates at least $100,000 more a year; the rebuttal for the measure says it requires no audit and so adds no expense by itself [S1].

## Arguments for
The statement in favor (Peter Silliman, chair; Liz Cline; Ann Donnelly) says [S1]:
- "Performance auditing has been shown to improve the efficient use of funds"; it gives the body that approves department budgets the power to audit them.
- The Council would choose the scope and whether staff, the auditor or a contractor does the work; other charter counties allow this.
The rebuttal says the Council can now only request an audit, which the auditor need not perform [S1].

## Arguments against
The statement against (John Latta, chair; Dijana Katan; Janet Landesberg) says [S1]:
- Councilors and residents can already request audits through an Audit Oversight Committee that includes a councilor; the State Auditor offers outside review free.
- The county pays the State Auditor $350,000-$400,000 a year for required audits; Pierce County spends about $600,000 a year on performance audits.

## Endorsements and campaign
The commission's members are Patrick Adigweme, Ann Donnelly, Eric LaBrant (District 1); Brandon Erickson (chair), Cathie Garber, Kim Hamlik (District 2); Ben Christly, Janet Landesberg, John Jay (District 3); Liz Cline, Dorothy Gasque, Morgan Holmgren (District 4); Brad Benton, Duncan Hoss, Peter Silliman (District 5) [S4]. Silliman, Cline, Donnelly and Landesberg sit on the commission [S4]. Silliman is a candidate for County Council District 5 [S1].
