## What it does
The ballot title says the amendment "would establish a new timeline for the submission of budget information, presentation, and adoption of the Clark County budget earlier than those dates set in Chapter 36.40 RCW" [S2]. The explanatory statement says it sets earlier deadlines for departments' budget estimates, requires the County Manager to give preliminary budget information to the Council and the public, and mandates public meetings before final adoption [S1]. The statement in favor describes the schedule as a first public presentation in the first week of September, a second in October, and the adoption hearing in November [S1].

**Who proposed it.** The Clark County Charter Review Commission, fifteen commissioners elected in November 2025 (three per council district) for 2026-2027 terms, proposes charter amendments; any amendment must be approved by Clark County voters at a general election [S4]. The adopting resolution is No. 26-10 [S2][S3].

## Cost
No tax or rate in the ballot title; no fiscal statement was published [S1][S2].

## Arguments for
The statement in favor (Peter Silliman, chair; Liz Cline; Ann Donnelly) says [S1]:
- In 2025 the first public budget presentation was October 29 and the budget was adopted December 2 "with almost no public involvement."
- Earlier presentations would give residents visibility into the county's "almost $900-million budget" and time to comment.
Its rebuttal says the charter already modifies budget requirements and is an appropriate place for transparency principles [S1].

## Arguments against
The statement against (John Latta, chair; Julie Koepp; Janet Landesberg) says [S1]:
- The county already follows state budget rules, and county leaders can set earlier deadlines without a charter change.
- Process details do not belong in the charter and could make the budget process harder to improve.

## Endorsements and campaign
The commission's members are Patrick Adigweme, Ann Donnelly, Eric LaBrant (District 1); Brandon Erickson (chair), Cathie Garber, Kim Hamlik (District 2); Ben Christly, Janet Landesberg, John Jay (District 3); Liz Cline, Dorothy Gasque, Morgan Holmgren (District 4); Brad Benton, Duncan Hoss, Peter Silliman (District 5) [S4]. Silliman, Cline, Donnelly and Landesberg sit on the commission [S4]. Silliman is also a candidate for County Council District 5 [S1].
