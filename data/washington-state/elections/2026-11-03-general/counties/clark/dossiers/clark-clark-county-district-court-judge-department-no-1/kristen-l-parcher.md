## Background
Kristen L. Parcher has been Clark County District Court judge in Department 1 since 2018, after serving as a District Court commissioner from 2011 to 2018 [S1]. Before that she provided indigent defense with Vancouver Defenders in District Court and practiced family law at Green & Ritchie [S1]. She holds a J.D. from the University of Oregon [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
- Pledges to serve "with intelligence, patience and empathy, never forgetting the importance of due process owed to court participants" [S1].
