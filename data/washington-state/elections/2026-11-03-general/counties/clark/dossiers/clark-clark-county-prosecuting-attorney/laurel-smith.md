## Background
Laurel Smith has worked in the Clark County Prosecuting Attorney's Office since 2012 and is a senior deputy prosecutor with supervisory experience leading attorneys and staff in specialized units, including domestic violence and child abuse [S1]. She has served as a judge pro tempore in Clark County District Court since 2022 and reports no prior elected office [S1]. She holds a J.D. from Lewis and Clark Law School and a B.A. from San Diego State University [S1].

## Positions
- **Public safety:** says she has prosecuted homicides, sexual assaults and crimes against children, and that as prosecutor she will "hold offenders accountable and use smart, evidence-based approaches that make our community safer" [S1].
- **Courts and fairness:** commits to a justice system that is "fair, effective, and rooted in integrity" [S1].
