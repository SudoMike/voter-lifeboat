## What the office does
The prosecuting attorney runs the Clark County Prosecuting Attorney's Office, which charges and prosecutes felonies countywide and misdemeanors in unincorporated areas, and is the county government's civil lawyer.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Senior Deputy Prosecutor Laurel Smith is the only candidate for this open office. The race is on every Clark County ballot [S1].
