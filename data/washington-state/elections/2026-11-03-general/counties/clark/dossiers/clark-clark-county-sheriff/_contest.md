## What the office does
The sheriff runs the Clark County Sheriff's Office, which patrols unincorporated Clark County, investigates crimes, serves civil process and warrants, and operates the county jail.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: incumbent Sheriff John Horch, elected in 2022, is the only candidate. The race is on every Clark County ballot [S1].
