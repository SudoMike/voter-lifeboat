## Background
John Horch is the elected Clark County Sheriff and has served for the past four years [S1]. Before that he served in the Sheriff's Office as enforcement chief, drug task force commander, and in major crimes and internal affairs, and on an FBI organized crime task force; he is a bomb technician [S1]. He holds a B.S. in organizational leadership and is a graduate of the FBI National Academy [S1].

## Positions
- **Public safety:** says the office increased staffing for patrol, investigations and support services, introduced body-worn cameras, and, with regional partners, opened the SW Washington Police Academy [S1].
- **Staffing:** says the office "remains among the lowest-funded agencies in the state based on the officer-to-citizen ratio" and that increasing staffing is his top priority, to create dedicated traffic, retail theft, major crimes, child victim, drug, warrants, civil and evidence units [S1].
- **County Propositions 12 and 13:** John Horch is listed on the committee for the statements in favor of County Proposition No. 12 (jail, courts and Sheriff headquarters bonds) and No. 13 (public safety levy lid lift) [S2].
