## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House, for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The 49th District is in Vancouver [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics
Democratic Rep. Monica Stonier, House Majority Floor Leader and an Evergreen Public Schools access coach, has held the seat since 2016 and seeks re-election [S1][S2]. Her Republican challenger, Derek Thompson, a retired cosmetology instructor and former Nike supervisor, lost a 2025 bid for Vancouver City Council [S1][S2]. The Columbian frames the race as an incumbent citing accomplishments and goals against a challenger focused on school funding [S2]. Stonier voted Yea on the 2026 tax on income over $1 million (passed the House 51-46) [S3]. Primary vote shares were not verified for this refresh.

The record is one-sided in depth. Stonier has a long voting and sponsorship record: Yea votes on the 2026 income tax and 2025 capital gains, excise and B&O tax increases, rent-increase limits, and 2023 reproductive and gender-affirming care protections, and prime sponsorship of the 2025 law that rewrote Initiative 2081's parents' rights (monica-jurado-stonier.md S5-S11, S14). Thompson's platform is brief: fix the school funding formula, protect families from "unnecessary taxes," use tax dollars efficiently, and protect legacy businesses during infrastructure projects (derek-thompson.md S1, S2). He reports $2.1k raised to Stonier's $140k (PDC, both dossiers). Neither candidate has a public position on I-1 or I-638 on record; Stonier's legislative record bears on both topics.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Stonier voted for and defends the tax on income over $1 million and voted for the 2025 revenue package; Thompson opposes "unnecessary taxes" in general terms.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** ten-year incumbent and floor leader vs. a first-time legislative candidate.
3. **Parents and schools:** Stonier sponsored the 2025 rewrite of the Initiative 2081 parents' rights; Thompson's school focus is the funding formula, with no stated position on parental rights.
4. **Spending:** Stonier frames the budget as a contract with the people and seeks more special-education and childcare funding; Thompson stresses efficient use of tax dollars and classroom over "bureaucracy."
