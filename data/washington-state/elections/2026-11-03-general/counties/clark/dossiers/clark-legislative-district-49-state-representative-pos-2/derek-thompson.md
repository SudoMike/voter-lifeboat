## Background
Derek Thompson (Prefers Republican Party) is a retired cosmetology instructor licensed in Washington and Oregon, a former department supervisor at Nike and a DoorDash delivery driver [S1]. His campaign says he has worked as a cosmetologist and instructor since 1991 [S2]. He lists business administration and entrepreneurship coursework, and community service as a Bible education superintendent, at the Fourth Plain Adventist food bank, as a Clark Transition Center volunteer since 2006, and as a supporter of MLK Elementary and Fort Vancouver High School [S1]. He submitted no elected experience [S1]. The Columbian reports this is his second run for public office after losing a 2025 bid for Vancouver City Council [S3]. In that November 2025 general election, "Derek O Thompson" received 6,176 votes (15.66%) for Vancouver City Council Position 2 to Erik Paulsen's 33,013 (83.7%) [S5].

## Positions
- **Schools:** names a "broken school funding formula" as a top concern and pledges to "fix school funding at the source" [S1]; the Columbian's subhead says school funding would be his focus [S3]. Says education funding "should focus on students, teachers, and classroom success—not bureaucracy" [S2].
- **Taxes and spending:** says "fiscal accountability starts with protecting hardworking families from unnecessary taxes" [S2] and pledges to "insure your tax dollars are used efficiently" [S1].
- **Business and infrastructure:** would "protect our legacy businesses from being sidelined by major infrastructure projects" [S1].
- **Public safety:** supports "safer neighborhoods and effective community partnerships" [S2].
