## Background
Gordon Matthews is a licensed engineer (B.S. in mechanical engineering, Georgia Tech) who reports 44 years in the electric utility industry: 12 years at the Georgia Power Research Center, rising from craftsman to senior research engineer, and 32 years at the Bonneville Power Administration in 14 assignments including laboratories, technology innovation, corporate strategy, business practices and rate design [S1]. His site gives the BPA tenure as 31 years and the total as 43 [S2]. He lists his elected experience as a former precinct committee officer and has lived in Vancouver since 1992 [S1].

## Positions
- **Energy supply:** says the BPA hydro system "can no longer deliver our full requirements" because no new dams are being built and snowpack is diminishing, and that decarbonizing and electrifying pose new challenges [S1][S2].
- **Solar:** proposes partnering with eastern Washington public utilities to build solar there, which he says yields 150% of the generation from the same investment [S1] (his site says 200% [S2]), and redesigning community solar so ratepayers share in the dividends [S1][S2].
- **Rates:** says the utility is "well run, providing affordable services reliably" and that he would pursue "sound rate design" [S1][S2].
