## Background
Jane A. Van Dyke is the incumbent Clark Public Utilities commissioner for District 3 [S1]. She is an attorney (Washington State Bar), an active and past president of the Washington Public Utility Districts Association, and a member of the American Public Power Association Policy Makers Council [S1]. She was executive director of Clark County Volunteer Lawyers, the Clark County Bar Association, Columbia Springs Environmental Education Center and the Columbia Slough Watershed Council [S1]. She holds a J.D. from Lewis and Clark Northwestern School of Law [S1].

## Positions
- **Rates:** will advocate for "power and water at nonprofit rates" [S1][S2].
- **Energy and conservation:** says conservation and efficiency let the utility avoid costly investments and that the utility exceeded its regional conservation goals; supports rooftop and community solar; says the Integrated Resource and Water System plans will drive future conservation and resource acquisition [S1]. Her site adds that the utility has invested in two community solar facilities and several regional wind projects [S2].
- **Assistance programs:** cites Operation Warm Heart and the Senior Rate Credit for income-limited customers [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
No public position on I-645, I-1 or I-638 found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- Says the utility has had one electric rate increase in 15 years and one water rate increase in 13 years, and (per her site) three bill credits in that period [S1][S2].
- Cites a J.D. Power customer-satisfaction award and Reliable Public Power Provider recognition [S1][S2].
- Her site says the utility's annual operation and maintenance costs are the lowest per customer of any electric utility in Washington [S2].
