## Background
Diana H. Perez is the Democratic candidate for Position 2 in the 17th Legislative District and has served on the Vancouver City Council since 2022 [S1][S7]. She spent more than 30 years in public lands and natural resource management with the USDA Forest Service as a biologist, deputy monument manager and acting district ranger, and is a small-business owner [S1]. She holds an M.S. in fisheries from Oregon State University and a B.S. in wildlife management from Texas Tech University [S1]. She has served as a Washington State Parks and Recreation commissioner (appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee) and a Washington State Housing Finance Commission commissioner, and on the boards of the Clark County Elder Justice Center and Clark County FISH [S1][S4]. She founded a local council of the League of United Latin American Citizens [S4].

She announced her campaign in the week ending January 2, 2026 [S7]. Her endorsers quote her pledge to give up her council seat if elected, and her pamphlet asks to represent voters "full-time" [S5][S1].

## Positions
- **Affordability and housing:** would incentivize workforce and middle-income housing, increase state investment in affordable housing, encourage "responsible density near transit and jobs," reduce "unnecessary permitting delays and regulatory barriers," and support homelessness prevention including behavioral health services [S3]. Wants better access to affordable child care [S3][S1].
- **Taxes:** cast the only vote against Vancouver's 0.1% public-safety sales tax (passed 6-1 on 2026-08-24), citing its effect on lower-income residents and "the cumulative tax burden that continues to be placed on all of us"; she said she supports public-safety investment but the council should "pause and hear what voters have to say" [S8]. No statement on the 2026 state tax on income over $1 million was found.
- **Spending:** says "one of our overriding objectives is living within our budgetary means and making every dollar count" and pledges "fiscal responsibility" [S3][S1], alongside proposals for more state investment in housing, schools and infrastructure [S3][S1].
- **Public safety:** wants adequate staffing and training for law enforcement and emergency responders, alongside prevention programs, behavioral health and victim support: "Public safety requires both immediate response and long-term prevention" [S3].
- **Energy and climate:** "Climate change is real, and transitioning to sustainable energy is essential," but the transition "must be done responsibly without jeopardizing grid reliability or driving up utility costs"; supports grid and transmission investment, a "balanced energy portfolio," and continuing to evaluate the energy code [S3][S2].
- **Health care:** pamphlet commits to improving access to health care and child care [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no individual statement found. On 2026-10-05 the Vancouver City Council, of which she is a member, voted unanimously to oppose I-638 [S9]; her individual vote and attendance could not be confirmed past the paywall [S9].
- **Parents and schools:** the same unanimous council resolution opposed I-1 [S9]. No individual statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **I-1 (parental rights in schools):** no individual statement found as of 2026-10-08. The Vancouver City Council, on which she sits, unanimously adopted a resolution opposing it [S9].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no individual statement found as of 2026-10-08. The same council resolution opposed it [S9].

## Record
