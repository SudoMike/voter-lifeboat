## What the office does
The five-member Clark County Council is the county's legislative body under its home rule charter: it adopts the county budget and property-tax levies, sets councilmanic taxes and fees, adopts the Growth Management Act comprehensive plan and land-use rules for unincorporated areas, and decides which county propositions go to voters [S3][S4]. The seat is nonpartisan [S1]. Three of the five council seats (Districts 1, 2 and 5) are on the November 2026 ballot [S2].

## Race dynamics
First-term incumbent Glen Yung led the four-way primary with 36.65% in the election-night count; Bryan Shull, a Vancouver brewery and small-business owner and president of the Uptown Village Association, was second with 29.03% [S2]. Yung runs on his record: a 120-bed shelter with treatment, protections for mobile-home-park residents, middle-housing rules and public-safety investment (glen-yung.md S1, S4). He voted for the 1% property-tax levy increase in the 2026 budget and for a 0.1% councilmanic sales tax for affordable housing and behavioral health (glen-yung.md S5, S6). Shull asks voters to adopt a "tax-last" approach, lower fees and faster permitting, and to replace housing-first and harm-reduction strategies with treatment and accountability (bryan-shull.md S1, S2). The full council, Yung included, voted to place the Proposition 12 jail bond and Proposition 13 levy lid lift on this ballot [S4]; neither candidate's separate position on them was found. The Columbian's editorial board recommends Yung (glen-yung.md S16).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Yung's votes for the 2026 levy increase and the housing sales tax vs. Shull's "tax-last" platform.
2. **Spending:** Yung's investment in shelter, treatment and housing vs. Shull's call to address "out of control spending" first.
3. **Public safety and homelessness:** Yung's "balanced" crime-and-root-causes framing vs. Shull's treatment-and-accountability critique of housing first and harm reduction.
4. **Housing:** both cite permitting; Yung adds mobile-home-park protections and public funding, Shull emphasizes fees and land-use deregulation.
5. **Record vs. renewal:** a first-term incumbent with a voting record vs. a business owner who has not held public office.
