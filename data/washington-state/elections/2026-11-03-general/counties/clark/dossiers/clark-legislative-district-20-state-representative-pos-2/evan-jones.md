## Background
Evan Jones, 42, of Kelso is the Democratic candidate for Position 2 in the 20th Legislative District [S3][S1]. He was elected a Port of Longview commissioner in 2024, chairs the Southwest Washington Regional Transportation Planning Organization, and sits on the boards of the Cowlitz Economic Development Council and the Cowlitz-Wahkiakum Council of Governments [S1]. He is a U.S. Army combat veteran and a heavy-rigging tradesman: a journeyman with IBEW Local 48 and shop steward at Omega Morgan in Portland, commuting about 110 miles round trip [S1][S3][S2]. He is married with two daughters and serves as his union's veterans representative [S3]. This is his first legislative race [S3].

He said that a year earlier he would have called himself an independent and runs as a Democrat because he thinks an independent cannot win the seat [S3].

In the August 4 primary he trailed Rep. Ed Orcutt 37.56% to 62.26% in the election-night count [S4][S5].

## Positions
- **Why he is running:** as a port commissioner he cast the lone vote in January 2025 against awarding the Port of Longview's $44 million rail-corridor contract to Rotschy, Inc., a contractor with a record of safety incidents, including a 2023 trencher injury to a 16-year-old worker [S3][S5]. He says the 2025 child-labor bill (HB 1644) that followed was "watered down," that Orcutt voted against it, and that Orcutt's reply to his email put "in perspective where his priorities were" [S3][S7]. He pledges to "close those loopholes" so public money goes to "safe, accountable contractors" [S2].
- **Permitting and jobs:** says permitting "gridlock" is driving family-wage jobs in timber, shipping and construction to Oregon and Idaho and pledges to "fix permitting" and "cut the red tape" [S1][S2]. He told The Daily News the process is "really onerous" and that environmental groups can stop projects; "It's not that I disagree with the environmental lobbyists ... but I do want people to be able to go to work. There has to be a balance" [S3].
- **Taxes:** platform plank "More Taxpayers, Not More Taxes": "We don't need to raise taxes. We need more businesses and workers paying into the system" [S2].
- **Representation:** says he would be in the majority caucus and would "stop the policies that don't fit our district — on guns, on permitting, on Seattle decisions that land wrong here" [S2].
- **Guns:** pledges to defend the Second Amendment and called assault-weapons bans a "pretty major violation" of it [S2][S3].
- **Trades training:** would expand vocational training and apprenticeships [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
