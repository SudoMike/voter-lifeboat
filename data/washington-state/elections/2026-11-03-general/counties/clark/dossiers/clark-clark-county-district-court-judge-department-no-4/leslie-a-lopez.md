## Background
Leslie A. Lopez has been a Clark County District Court judge since 2025, after serving as a District Court commissioner (2024-2025) and pro tempore judge in Superior and District Courts (2023-2024) [S1]. She was chief civil deputy (2021-2024) and a deputy prosecuting attorney (2018-2021) in the Clark County Prosecutor's Office, and a trial lawyer for municipalities from 2009 to 2018 [S1]. She holds a J.D. from Lewis & Clark Law School [S1]. The sample ballot lists the seat for a short and full four-year term [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
- Commits to District Court's mission of "exceptional justice services, ensuring equitable access to the court and its programs, treating everyone with fairness and respect" [S1].
