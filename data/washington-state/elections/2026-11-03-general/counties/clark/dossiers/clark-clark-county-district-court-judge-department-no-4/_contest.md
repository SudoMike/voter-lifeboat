## What the office does
District Court judges hear misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor criminal cases, traffic infractions, civil suits up to $100,000, small claims, protection orders and felony first appearances for Clark County. Clark County District Court judges are elected countywide.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Judge Leslie A. Lopez is the only candidate for Department 4, on the ballot for both the short and the full four-year term. [S1]
