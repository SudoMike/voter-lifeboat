## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House, for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The 18th District lies in Clark County; the general election decides the seat between the two candidates who advanced from the August primary [S1].

## Race dynamics
First-term Republican Rep. John Ley, 70, a retired Delta Air Lines captain, Air Force veteran and former Clark County Today reporter, is defending the seat against Democrat Deken Letinich, assistant political and legislative director of the Washington/Northern Idaho District Council of Laborers, who has not held elected office [S1][S2]. The Columbian editorial board recommends Letinich [S4]. This contest is separate from the district's Position 1 race.

The Interstate 5 Bridge replacement is the most visible split: Ley opposes the current plans, including tolls and light rail, while Letinich's pamphlet says "It's time to build the bridge" and The Columbian reports he cites the economic infusion the project would bring [S1][S2]. On taxes, Ley voted against ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on income over $1 million (passed 51-46) [S5], and urges a yes vote on I-645 to repeal it; Letinich supports taxing income above $1 million and opposes lowering the threshold [S3] (john-ley.md S4). Ley proposes capping state budget growth and voted against both operating budgets of the biennium, while Letinich calls for more school, early-learning and infrastructure funding (john-ley.md S3, S10, S11; deken-letinich.md S3). Ley co-sponsored bills restricting gender-transition care for minors and allowing sex-based eligibility in girls' school sports and campaigns to "restore parental control over children's education"; Letinich's pamphlet says he is pro-choice (john-ley.md S16, S17; [S1]). PDC summaries show Letinich with $188k raised and Ley with $97k (deken-letinich.md S9; john-ley.md S21).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** recorded votes against the tax on income over $1 million and support for I-645 vs. support for the tax and its current threshold.
2. **Spending:** a cap on budget growth and program audits vs. expanded education, childcare and infrastructure funding.
3. **Transportation / I-5 Bridge:** opposition to tolls, light rail and the current replacement plan vs. stewardship of the project as it breaks ground.
4. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** co-sponsorship of HB 1038 and HB 1699 vs. a pro-choice statement.
5. **Parents and schools:** Ley campaigns on parental control, voted against ESHB 1296 and backs education savings accounts; Letinich has no stated position on notification, records or opt-outs.
6. **Climate:** Ley would end the carbon tax; Letinich has no stated position on climate policy.
7. **Record vs. renewal:** first-term incumbent vs. a labor organizer who has not held public office.
