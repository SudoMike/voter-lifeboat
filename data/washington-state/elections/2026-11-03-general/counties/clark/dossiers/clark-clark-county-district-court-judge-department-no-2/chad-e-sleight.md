## Background
Chad E. Sleight has been a Clark County District Court judge since March 2017; he was appointed by the Clark County Council and re-elected in 2018 and 2022 [S1]. He was a pro tem District Court judge from 2013 to 2017 and a prosecutor for Battle Ground, Ridgefield and La Center from 2009 to 2017, and is a past president of the Clark County Bar Association [S1]. He holds a J.D. from Willamette University [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
