## Background
Andy Zahn is the Democratic candidate for Position 1 in the 20th Legislative District [S1]. He is 35, lives in Toutle, has lived in the district his whole life, and works as a freelance writer, photographer and videographer; this is his first bid for any elected office [S9]. His campaign site says he grew up on a small working farm near Mount St. Helens, attended Lower Columbia College, and has worked hauling hay, as a public-lands steward, and as a writer for technology outlets including Digital Trends, Android Police and SlashGear [S3][S2]. His pamphlet statement lists no elected experience [S1]. He volunteers with conservation groups including Friends of Willapa National Wildlife Refuge and Wild Fish Conservancy Northwest [S9].

Identity: the Andy Zahn in this race is the Toutle resident whose PDC registration (as Andrew Zahn) is for LD 20 House Position 1 [S11]; he is not Rep. Janice Zahn, a Democratic House member from Bellevue who appears in 2025-26 House roll calls.

In the August 4 primary, Rep. Peter Abbarno led Zahn with 65.04% of the vote in the election-night count [S10].

## Positions
- **Platform pillars:** expanding industrial hemp production and processing for union manufacturing jobs, creating large parks near rural towns, and concentrating growth in town centers while "defending our fields and forests from further suburban sprawl" [S2][S4]. He describes the approach as a "steady-state economy" [S4].
- **Taxes and revenue:** calls Washington's tax structure "deeply flawed and regressive" and "in need of a complete overhaul," and writes that "we could raise more money in state taxes, while also reducing the burden on middle and lower income Washingtonians if we were to reform our broken and regressive tax structure" [S6]. He argues tax reform alone will not fix the budget and proposes borrowing for infrastructure that pays back its cost, recovering federal tax dollars, and revenue from self-sustaining state projects [S6]. He favors higher taxes on data centers' power and water use [S5][S8].
- **Spending:** says the state is "not so much of a bind financially" when spending has "a clear path towards recouping that investment" [S6]; he told The Daily News he would also like to see the state budget balanced [S9].
- **Housing and land use:** wants affordable housing built in community centers rather than on farm and forest land [S1][S4]; proposes a publicly built rent-to-own housing model whose monthly fee repays construction cost [S6].
- **Transit:** proposes publicly owned hourly passenger rail on the I-5 corridor between Tacoma and Portland with stops in small towns including Ridgefield and Woodland, restored Tacoma-Morton rail, more Seattle-Spokane service, expanded rural bus service and walkable mixed-use zoning, with the goal of making car ownership optional [S7][S8][S4].
- **Climate and energy:** says fossil-fuel extraction and consumption must cease and climate change is urgent; prioritizes cutting electricity demand, subsidizing efficiency, and rooftop and parking-lot solar over utility-scale solar and wind farms, which he says have severe ecological impacts [S8].
- **Data centers and AI:** calls data centers "an existential threat"; proposes a moratorium on new data centers, a ban on running generative AI and cryptocurrency in them, higher taxes on their power and water use, and a ban on private-sector and government use of generative AI in the state, plus labeling of AI-generated material [S5][S4][S1]. He also says he wants to protect residents from excessive surveillance and protect privacy [S9].
- **Health care:** pamphlet pledges to "improve access to healthcare" [S1]; his campaign site says "paying for healthcare through the government in a universal healthcare system would save us an immense amount of money" [S6].
- **Education:** proposes free community college and more degree programs at community colleges so young people can stay in rural towns [S6].
- **Money in politics:** says corporate influence has "corrupted our institutions" and "we've got to evict big money interests from our government" [S1]; he has vowed not to accept corporate contributions [S9].
- **Environment (local):** opposes raising the Toutle River Sediment Retention Structure spillway [S9][S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
