## Background
James B. Smith has been a Clark County District Court judge since his 2021 appointment and 2022 election; he is the assistant presiding judge and oversees the Veterans Therapeutic Court program [S1]. Before the bench he spent 16 years as a deputy prosecutor, as chief criminal deputy in Cowlitz County and as a senior deputy and Children's Justice Center team leader in Clark County [S1]. He holds a J.D. from the University of Mississippi [S1].
