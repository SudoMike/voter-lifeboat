## BallotTitle
Concerning Revised Council Powers Regarding Boards and Commissions

The Clark County Charter Review Commission adopted Resolution No. 26-23 proposing an amendment to the Clark County Home Rule Charter, concerning revised Council powers regarding boards and commissions. If approved, this amendment would give the County Council the concurrent authority with the County Manager to nominate members to all boards and commissions, with the exception of the Ethics Review Commission.

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Clark County Charter Review Commission proposes an expansion of the County Council’s authority to concurrently nominate members to certain boards and commissions—excluding the Ethics Review Commission. The amendment revises Section 2.4.H of the Home Rule Charter to allow the council to share nomination power with the County Manager, while retaining final appointment authority.

## ArgumentFor
Amendment 21 gives our County Council the ability to submit community members for consideration to the volunteer boards and commissions that help keep our County running.

Currently, the Council can only nominate volunteers to the Planning Commission, Historic Preservation Commission, and the Board of Equalization. This amendment expands that ability to include other boards and commissions, now and in the future, with the important exception of the Ethics Review Commission.

This could potentially reduce the burden on county staff to fill spots on these commissions and increase the Council's ability to keep these important volunteer roles filled. Councilors hear from the community often and they can help ensure that those who want to contribute can.

A yes vote will make this common-sense adjustment to our Charter.
