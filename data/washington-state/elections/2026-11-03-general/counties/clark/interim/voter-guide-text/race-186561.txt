Statement

It has been an honor to serve as your Sheriff
over the past four years. The Sheriff’s Office has made significant progress in
keeping our community safe. We increased staffing for patrol, investigations,
and support services, which help reduce response times. The Body Worn Camera
program was introduced to enhance transparency and accountability while
protecting deputies. Investments in tools such as the Grappler have improved
our ability to safely apprehend offenders and protect victims. In collaboration
with other regional law enforcement partners, we opened the SW Washington
Police Academy to raise training standards and efficiency. CCSO also expanded
community engagement through public forums, neighborhood outreach,
communications, and social media.

Even with these improvements, the Sheriff’s
Office remains among the lowest-funded agencies in the state based on the
officer-to-citizen ratio. Increasing CCSO staffing will remain my top priority.
Additional deputies will enable dedicated positions for traffic enforcement,
retail theft, major crimes, child victim support, drug investigations,
warrants, civil, and evidence units.

I ask for your vote to build a safer, stronger
community.
