Statement
I am truly honored to have been appointed by the Clark Council to be the Department 5 judge for Clark County District Court. It is where I began my legal career as an intern and where I learned how to be an attorney. I strongly believe that district court is a reflection of the issues and struggles our community faces on a daily basis. My goal everyday is to provide a courtroom that is impartial, fair and informative while also recognizing those issues and struggles of every community member that comes in to my courtroom.
