Statement

Affordability must be part of every decision, because wages aren't keeping up. Randi will support policies to attract higher paying jobs, make housing more affordable, and oppose policies that shift costs onto families while protecting the wealthy and big corporations. Affordability is about keeping people in their homes and planning for the future.

Randi is an Autism Mom and advocate for families navigating education systems, healthcare, and public services that too often fail. That perspective has shaped how she approaches policy: thoughtfully, inclusively, ad with deep respect for how decisions affect families day to day.
