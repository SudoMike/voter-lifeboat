Statement
It is my honor to serve Clark County as a Senior Deputy Prosecutor. For more than a decade, I have prosecuted some of the most serious and complex cases, including homicides, sexual assaults, and crimes against children. I have dedicated my career to seeking justice for victims and ensuring accountability for those who commit crimes.
My career as a trial attorney and as a supervisor has required not only legal expertise, but strong judgment, a sense of fairness, and the ability to lead through difficult and high-stakes work. I have a proven record of achieving justice. I train prosecutors and work closely with law enforcement, victim advocates, and community partners. And I advocate for laws that make our community a safer place to live.
I care about how we at the Prosecutor’s Office serve the public. The safety of our community is of the utmost importance. As Prosecutor, I will continue to work tirelessly to hold offenders accountable and use smart, evidence-based approaches that make our community safer.
Clark County is my home. I am committed to ensuring our justice system is fair, effective, and rooted in integrity. I respectfully ask for your vote.
