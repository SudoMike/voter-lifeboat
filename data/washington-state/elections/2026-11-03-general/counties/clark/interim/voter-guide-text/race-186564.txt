Statement
It has been an incredible honor and privilege to serve as a District Court Judge in the place that I was born and raised. I was appointed to the Bench by the Clark County Council in 2017, was re-elected in both 2018 and 2022, and hope to continue to serve this community as a judge for many years to come. The role of a judge is not only to preside over cases and make rulings, but also to treat people fairly and with respect. Judges have the ability to make a real difference in people's lives. Our Court is facing new challenges every day, and I am proud of the work we do in District Court to meet those challenges head on find solutions.
