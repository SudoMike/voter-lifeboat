## Background

Timothy (Tim) Garrison, a Republican, is a Skagit County small business owner with experience in accounting and federal and state tax matters [S1][S2]. He holds a B.A. in business administration with an accounting concentration and an M.B.A. in finance [S1]. The SkagitResidents.com home page names the schools as the University of Washington and "Seattle International University." The same blurb calls him a Burlington accountant and tax professional with "more than 50 years of public and private financial experience," and says he founded "a civic members group dedicated to government accountability" [S4]. Neither his pamphlet statement nor his own slate page repeats those details [S1][S3]. He lists work in commercial fishing and cattle ranching and volunteer work with Skagit Valley Church of Christ, Shriners fundraising and burn-center programs [S1]. He calls himself "a political outsider" [S1]; no source found lists a public office he has held. He filed as a write-in candidate for the August 4 primary, when Treasurer Jackie Brunson was the only name on the ballot. He qualified for the general with 1,155 write-in votes, about 5% [S2]. He is one of four write-in candidates promoted together on SkagitResidents.com as "constitutional candidates" [S2][S4].

## Positions

- Says that "in today's fraud-rich environment" taxpayers need responsible stewardship, and that he brings "the financial discipline to review and challenge how public money is handled" [S1].
- Says his focus "will be eliminating fraud and protecting every taxpayer dollar by thoroughly reviewing and scrutinizing transactions that come through the Treasurer's Office" [S1].
- Describes himself as "a defender of the Constitution" who "supports constitutionally lawful, transparent operations and will not allow improper or unlawful transactions under his watch" [S1].
- Promises transparency, customer service and "practical innovation" to make treasurer services easier to understand [S1].
- Before the primary he joined the other three write-in candidates in demanding that the county correct a primary voters' pamphlet that labeled the clerk, coroner and treasurer races "Unopposed" [S5].
- No public statement found on tax rates, levies or the 2026 state income tax.
