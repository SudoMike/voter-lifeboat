## What the office does
The county treasurer is a regional office serving about 80 local government districts. It collects property and excise taxes, holds and invests public funds (including through the Skagit County Investment Pool) and pays the districts' debt (S2). The treasurer collects taxes but does not set tax rates or decide county spending. It is a four-year, partisan office (S1).

## Race dynamics
Treasurer Jackie Brunson, who lists her party preference as "Democrat Party," was first elected in 2018. She is seeking another term after nearly 34 years in county government (S1). Republican Timothy Garrison, an accountant and small business owner, qualified for the general with 1,155 write-in votes (about 5%) in a primary where Brunson was the only printed name (S3). He is one of four write-in candidates promoted as a "constitutional" slate on SkagitResidents.com (S3; timothy-garrison.md S3). Garrison runs as "a political outsider" focused on "eliminating fraud" by scrutinizing transactions. Brunson cites office modernization and her work with legislators on tools for delinquent property owners (S1). The Skagit County Democrats endorse Brunson (jackie-brunson.md S4). The county Republican Party's general-election list makes no endorsement or recommendation in this race (timothy-garrison.md S7). Both campaigns registered for PDC mini reporting, and neither had filed reports as of September 17 (jackie-brunson.md S5; timothy-garrison.md S6). Coverage is thin. The League of Women Voters forum for this race was October 5, but the Herald had not run a preview story on it as of 2026-10-08, and neither candidate has a dedicated campaign website.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a long-serving county finance official against a self-described political outsider. This is the clearest axis.
2. **Spending (oversight side):** Garrison's platform is scrutinizing public transactions to root out fraud. Brunson cites cost savings in her office. Neither speaks to program spending levels, which the treasurer does not control.
3. No evidence on taxes, housing, safety or climate for either candidate.
