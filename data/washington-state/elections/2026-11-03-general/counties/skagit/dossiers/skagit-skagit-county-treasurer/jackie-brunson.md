## Background

Jackie Brunson, who lists her party preference as "Democrat Party," is the incumbent Skagit County Treasurer [S1][S2]. She was elected in 2018 and took office in 2019 [S1]. Before that she was chief of administrative services at the Skagit County Sheriff's Office (2002-2018), an accountant there (1996-2002), and an employee of the Skagit County Auditor's Office (1992-1996), nearly 34 years of county service in all [S1]. She holds a bachelor's degree in accounting from the University of Houston and is a 2013 Leadership Skagit graduate [S1]. She is legislative chair of the Washington State Association of County Treasurers and secretary/treasurer of the Washington Association of County Officials. She also lists board roles with Youthnet and Chinook Enterprises and volunteer work with 4-H and Special Olympics [S1]. She has no campaign website listed in the pamphlet or the VoteWA guide [S1]. Her opponent, Republican Timothy Garrison, qualified for the general as a write-in candidate [S2].

## Record

- Treasurer since 2019. She says that in that time she implemented electronic excise filing, digitized records, cross-trained staff, and reduced banking costs [S1]. These are her own claims. No independent audit or news account of them was found.
- In 2026 the treasurer's office joined the assessor's and auditor's offices in closing on five days between August and December to cut costs during the county's budget shortfall [S3].
- The office she runs serves about 80 local taxing districts: it collects property and excise taxes, holds and invests their funds through the Skagit County Investment Pool, and makes their debt payments [S6].
