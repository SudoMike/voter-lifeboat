## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes criminal cases for the state and county, defends lawsuits brought against the county, and is legal adviser to the county commissioners, county officers and school directors [S2]. The office is a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Erik Pedersen (Prefers Democratic Party), the office's chief civil deputy, is the only candidate; the pamphlet marks him "Unopposed" [S1]. The incumbent, Rich Weyrich, was re-elected without an opponent in 2022 with 97.83% of the vote (the rest write-ins) [S3] and is not on the 2026 ballot [S1]. The Skagit Valley Herald's filing-week story listed Pedersen as a prosecuting attorney candidate, not among the filing incumbents [S4]. The contest is uncontested.
