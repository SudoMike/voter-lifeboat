## Background

- Jenifer Howson has been a Skagit County District Court judge since January 2021, after serving as the court's commissioner from 2017 to 2020 [S1]. The county's court page lists her as one of the court's three judges [S3].
- She was re-elected unopposed in 2022 with 98.4% of the vote [S5]. She filed for re-election on May 4, 2026 [S6] and is unopposed [S1].
- She was the court's presiding judge for four years [S1], 2021 to 2025 according to her statement on the county's Community Court page [S4].
- Before the bench she practiced law for 27 years in the District and Superior Courts of Skagit, Whatcom, Island and Snohomish counties and served as judge pro tem for Sedro-Woolley Municipal Court and Skagit County District and Superior Courts [S1].
- Education: J.D., University of the Pacific McGeorge School of Law (1989); B.A. in political science, University of Washington (1984) [S1].
- Recognition: Skagit Women's Alliance and Network "Woman of the Year" for Transformational Leadership (2022); Skagit Legal Aid Community Partner Award for the Licensing Clinic (2025); Skagit Bar Association Hugh Ridgway award for Professionalism and Integrity (2007) [S1].
- The VoteWA online voters' guide lists the race as a countywide nonpartisan office and no campaign website, only a campaign email address [S2].

## Judicial experience and approach

- Says she led the team that developed a network of Community Courts throughout Skagit County, which she describes as "nationally recognized" [S1].
- The county describes Community Court as a specialty court that holds participants accountable while connecting them to services such as housing, treatment, mental health care and employment, with sessions in Mount Vernon, Anacortes and Burlington [S4].
- In a statement on the county page she says that since 2020 the District and Municipal Court network has referred nearly 1,000 participants to resources, and that treatment courts reduce incarceration costs and re-arrests [S4].
