## Background

- Patrick Eason has been a Skagit County District Court Commissioner since July 2021 [S1]; the county's court page lists him as a court commissioner [S6].
- Before the bench he was Assistant City Attorney for Mount Vernon for eight years, a deputy prosecuting attorney in Skagit and Mason counties, and in private practice for ten years handling civil, criminal, family-law and personal-injury matters [S4].
- He is a member of the Board of Governors of the statewide District and Municipal Court Judges' Association and was legal counsel for the Skagit Valley Tulip Festival [S1].
- Education: J.D. cum laude, Seattle University School of Law (1998); B.A. in political science, Western Washington University (1994); A.A., Skagit Valley Community College (1992) [S1].
- His campaign says he is running "to replace a retiring member of the bench" [S4]. Position 1 is held by Judge Warren M. Gilbert, re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S7][S6]; Gilbert is not on the 2026 ballot [S1]. Eason filed on May 4, 2026 [S8] and is unopposed [S1].
- The VoteWA online voters' guide lists the race as a countywide nonpartisan office and his campaign website as easonforjudge.com [S2].

## Judicial experience and approach (from own statements)

- As commissioner, he says he has presided over criminal jury trials, guilty pleas and sentencings, criminal and civil pretrial dockets, protection orders, small claims and infraction hearings [S1].
- Says the court also serves as the municipal court for Anacortes, Burlington and Mount Vernon [S3].
- Says "the principal mission of any judge or other judicial officer is to be fair and impartial to all parties who come before the Court" [S3].
