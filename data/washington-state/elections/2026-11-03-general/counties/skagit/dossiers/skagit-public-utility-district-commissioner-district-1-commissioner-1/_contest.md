## What the office does
Skagit Public Utility District No. 1 is a water utility. Its board of commissioners sets policy, rates and the budget, approves capital projects, and oversees the general manager. Commissioners serve six-year terms (S1; andrew-miller.md S5). The PUD runs the Judy Reservoir water treatment plant (S8) and holds significant Skagit River water rights (S6). Since 2024 it has leased those rights to irrigators during the dry season under a pilot program that ends after 2026 (S5). Miller's campaign says it serves about 80,000 residents, including Burlington, Mount Vernon, Sedro-Woolley, Guemes Island and Alger, and offers broadband (SkagitNet) up-valley (andrew-miller.md S3, S5). Commissioners are nominated by commissioner district, but in the general election the whole PUD votes for each position (S4). Skagit PUD covers the county, so the seat appears on every Skagit ballot (S2).

### Position number discrepancy
- The local voters' pamphlet (pages 5 and 17) and VoteWA call the seat **Commissioner Position 1** / "Commissioner 1" (S1, S2).
- The PDC registers both candidates for position 1 of "SKAGIT PUD 01 - WATER" (andrew-miller.md S6; spencer-roozen.md S4).
- The Herald and Miller's campaign call it the **District 1** seat (andrew-miller.md S4, S8).
- **The county's sample ballot prints "Public Utility District No. 1 Commissioner Position 3"** with Miller and Roozen as the candidates (S3).
- There is only one PUD contest on the Skagit ballot, so the sample ballot's "Position 3" appears to be a labeling error on that document. The voter's actual ballot label could not be checked here. Treat this as Position 1 / District 1 and flag it to the Auditor if needed.

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Andrew Miller, first elected in 2020, is seeking a second six-year term. He runs on experience, stakeholder relationships and the board's record: the strategic plan, the farm water-sharing agreement and infrastructure work (andrew-miller.md S1, S4, S5, S7). Challenger Spencer Roozen, a Windermere real estate broker with a WSU agriculture degree, filed on the fourth day of filing week (spencer-roozen.md S1, S5). He runs on rate restraint and transparency. His campaign says rates rose 34% over six years and proposes line-item cost breakdowns before rate votes, a public dashboard, public hearings before major water deals, farmer input, and fire-flow upgrades (spencer-roozen.md S3). Both oppose committing Skagit water to data centers. Miller testified against large-scale data centers at the county's July hearing (S6; spencer-roozen.md S3). The Skagit County Democrats recommended Roozen on October 3. Roozen also lists County Commissioners Browning and Wesen and the Realtors association. Miller lists the Washington Farm Bureau (spencer-roozen.md S3, S6; andrew-miller.md S3). Roozen reports about $22k raised to Miller's $0 in 2026 contributions (spencer-roozen.md S4; andrew-miller.md S6). The League of Women Voters of Skagit County scheduled a PUD candidate forum for October 12 (S7). skagitpud.org refused automated access (HTTP 403) on 2026-10-08, so the PUD's own pages were not used. As of 2026-10-08 the Herald had not yet published a preview story on this race.

## Differentiating issue axes
No rubric axis applies to the PublicUtility category, so neither candidate is scored. The substantive contrasts, for the candidate cards:
1. **Incumbent record vs. challenger:** six years on the board and its president, against a first-time candidate (the substance of the `experience` axis, which is not applied to this category).
2. **Rates:** both promise to pursue grants to limit costs to ratepayers. Roozen frames past rate increases as a problem and makes rate hikes a "last resort"; Miller says the board has held "the line on affordability."
3. **Process and transparency:** Roozen proposes formal public hearings, notice and comment, and cost dashboards. Miller stresses building relationships with farmers, tribes, cities and conservation interests.
4. **Data centers:** no real difference. Both oppose them, and Miller also opposes battery storage siting.
