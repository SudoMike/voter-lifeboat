## Background

Spencer Roozen is a third-generation Skagit County resident and grandson of Dutch immigrants. He graduated from Mount Vernon High School and from Washington State University with a degree in Agriculture Technology and Management [S1]. He has been a broker with Windermere Real Estate in Mount Vernon for 12 years [S1]. He has served on the boards of Camp Fire Samish and the North Puget Sound Association of Realtors, and has been a Skagit Rotary member for 10 years [S1]. He filed on May 7, 2026, to challenge Commissioner Andrew Miller for the District 1 seat [S5]. This is his first run for the PUD board; he lists no prior elected office [S1][S3]. The office is nonpartisan [S1].

## Positions

### Rates and transparency
- His pamphlet statement promises "a strong work ethic, rate transparency, and a practical approach to decision-making" and "responsible stewardship of public resources" [S1].
- His campaign says "Water rates are up 34% over six years — far outpacing inflation" and that he "will treat rate hikes as a last resort, not routine" [S3]. (This rate figure is the campaign's claim; no independent source was found to check it.)
- Proposes looking into whether increases are necessary and pursuing state and federal grants instead. He would publish a line-item cost breakdown before every rate vote and create a "plain-language public dashboard" on where ratepayer money goes [S3].

### Water allocation ("Not One Drop")
- "Skagit water should serve Skagit first — not outside special interests like data centers" [S3].
- Proposes public hearings before any major new water deal and public notice and comment before any commission vote on water-use changes ("End backroom deals"). He would put existing users (farms, families, instream flows) ahead of new industrial customers and require large new users to fund their own infrastructure [S3].
- Supports "tribal fishing rights, and treaty protected flows" [S3].

### Farmers
- Proposes standing farmer input on water decisions and expanding and speeding up the seasonal water-transfer program for farmers outside the Anacortes system. He also wants multi-year water outlooks and public tracking of water shortfalls [S3].

### Fire protection
- Proposes a district-wide review of hydrant capacity and fire flow and funding and timelines to fix deficient areas. He would require fire-flow verification before new development is approved, add backup power for pumps and wells in high-risk areas, and coordinate with fire departments [S3].
