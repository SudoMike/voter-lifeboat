## What the office does

The assessor values property for property-tax purposes. State law requires each county assessor to run a continuous revaluation program: all taxable real property is revalued every year and physically inspected at least once every six years [S2]. The office is a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Assessor Danny Hagen (Prefers Independent Party) is the only candidate; the pamphlet marks him "Unopposed" [S1]. He filed on the first day of filing week, May 4, 2026 [S4]. He won the seat in 2022 over Karie Storle (Prefers Republican Party), 54.76% to 45.11% [S3]. The contest is uncontested.
