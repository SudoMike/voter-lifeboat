## Identity note

The pamphlet prints his website as "www.CodyHart." (truncated) and his email at codyhartinfo.com [S1][S2]; codyhart.com and codyhartinfo.com resolved only to parked pages on 2026-10-08. His PDC registration lists info@codyhart.org [S7], and the voters' guide PDF hosted on that site links to "Cody Hart for Government Accountability (CodyHart.ORG)" [S6]. So codyhart.org is treated as his site. That site refers to the 2nd Congressional District and hosts "23-cv-832 HART et al V JANICKI et al" (Janicki was a Skagit County commissioner) [S3][S5]. The Skagit Valley Herald identifies the Cody Hart who filed 2026 recall petitions against county officials as the Hart who ran against U.S. Rep. Rick Larsen in 2024 [S10]. On that basis, the recall-petition coverage below is attributed to this candidate.

## Background

Cody Hart (prefers Republican Party) is a U.S. Navy veteran from Sedro-Woolley [S1][S7]. His pamphlet biography describes him as "an independent investigator, public corruption expert, civil rights advocate" with state and federal court experience. It says he earned a Bachelor of Science degree with coursework through the Navy, FEMA and Washington Emergency Management's Incident Command System [S1]. His website says he was stationed at Naval Air Station Whidbey Island and used the GI Bill, starting at Skagit Valley College, to earn a civil engineering degree. It says he became a licensed Professional Engineer who owns an engineering firm in Skagit County [S3]. His statement describes him as "a Christian, a sworn defender of the U.S. Constitution" and a father of three daughters [S1]. The Herald reports that he ran against Rep. Rick Larsen in the 2024 general election for the 2nd Congressional District and identified himself on that ballot as a MAGA Republican [S10]. He lists no law enforcement employment [S1][S3].

He did not appear on the August 4 primary ballot. He received 1,018 write-in votes (about 4%) in a race that otherwise had only Chris Baldwin, which met the 1% threshold for the general-election ballot [S8]. The Herald describes him as one of four self-proclaimed "constitutional" candidates who qualified as write-ins. All four are featured on SkagitResidents.com [S8].

## Positions

### Public safety and the office
- Says he is "volunteering to serve as a Constitutional Sheriff for Skagit County" [S1].
- Calls himself "a change-in-leadership candidate, not someone who has been part of the current problems in the Sheriff's Office" [S1].
- "I place resident's rights over protecting government every time. Constitutional rights must be defended. I'm asking for your support to bring accountability and law and order back to Skagit County" [S1].
- His website supports "enforcement of our nations immigration laws" and says, "As a gun owner, I am an advocate for gun rights ... and will oppose anyone who infringes upon you right to bear arms" [S3].

### Spending and government accountability
- His statement says he has "proven experience confronting the misuse of public funds that has fueled the worst public corruption our county has ever seen." It also cites "reporting millions of dollars of waste, fraud, and abuse in government" [S1].
- His website says: "As a independent investigator, I have already identified hundreds of millions of dollars of waste, fraud, and abuse in Washington state, including dramatic findings in Skagit County" [S3].

### Elections
- His statement cites experience "restoring election integrity" [S1]. His website posts his own election audit reports on the 2020 and 2021 elections, a 2019 Skagit election review, "SUSPECTED TAMPERED 2020 BALLOTS COLLECTED IN ANACORTES," and 2022 HAVA complaints [S5].
- His website's "Lawfare" page says he "has exposed massive election fraud, police corruption, judicial misconduct" and that "the deep state is escalating its attack on Cody by using lawfare against Cody in his personal life" [S4].

### Parents and schools
- His website's "Parental Rights" plank reads: "Activist teachers and school administrators have no place in our school system. I stand for parental rights and will fight to stop the sexualization of children hold those responsible accountable!" [S3]. The site's footer and "About" text refer to the 2nd Congressional District, so this plank may date from his congressional campaign [S3].

### Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy
- No explicit statement on gender identity, LGBTQ+ policy, sports eligibility or abortion found as of 2026-10-08. (His website also has a "Religious Freedom" plank [S3].)

## Record

He has not held public office [S1][S3]. His record of civic action, as reported:
- **Recall petitions.** In December 2025 a recall petition he filed was denied. On January 2, 2026, he filed another petition to recall Commissioners Peter Browning, Ron Wesen and Lisa Janicki and Prosecuting Attorney Rich Weyrich. The petition argued they should have acted against Superior Court Judge Heather Shand, who Hart alleged had vacated her office by not filing her oath on time [S9]. On January 15, Snohomish County Superior Court Judge Marybeth Dingledy ruled the petition insufficient and fined him $5,348.22. In her judgment she wrote that the petitioners do "not care about the merits; they only care about harassing Prosecuting Attorney Weyrich, the Commissioners, and the County" [S9].
- Hart wrote to the Herald that the fines mean "there is a 100% certainty sanctions will be pursued against anyone who files a recall against Prosecutor Weyrich." He described his motivation as using "my right to petition to have the problems I come across addressed through constitutionally defined right to recall public officials for misconduct" [S10].
- Chief Civil Deputy Prosecuting Attorney Erik Pedersen told the Herald the same group had filed 16 recall petitions against county officials. He estimated the staff time spent defending them at over $100,000 and said he believes the petitioners' motives are political [S10]. The Herald reports that many of the January petitioners, including Hart, also filed lawsuits against other county elected officials and employees that month [S10].
- His website posts federal court filings, including "23-cv-832 HART et al V JANICKI et al ORIGINAL COMPLAINT" and an emergency motion in case 23-cv-00503, plus "CODY HART FERGUSON IMPEACHMENT DOCUMENTS 7-30-24" [S5].
