## What the office does

District Court has criminal jurisdiction over misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors (sentences of up to one year in jail and a $5,000 fine unless a statute says otherwise), hears traffic infractions, conducts preliminary hearings, and sets bail [S3]. Skagit County District Court also hears civil claims up to $100,000, small claims, protection orders, name changes and dangerous-dog hearings [S4]. The court has three elected judges [S4]; the position is a four-year nonpartisan term [S1] and is on every ballot in the county [S2].

## Race dynamics

Judge Paul W Nielsen, appointed by the county commissioners in February 2024 to fill a vacancy, is the only candidate; the pamphlet marks him "Unopposed" [S1]. Position 2 was won in 2022 by Dianne Edmonds Goddard, unopposed, with 98.1% of the vote [S5]. The county's court page lists Nielsen as presiding judge [S4]. The contest is uncontested.
