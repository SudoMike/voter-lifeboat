## Background

- Paul W Nielsen has been a Skagit County District Court judge since March 2024; the county commissioners selected him in February 2024 to fill a vacant judicial position [S1]. Position 2 had been won in 2022 by Dianne Edmonds Goddard [S4].
- The county's court page lists him as the court's presiding judge [S3].
- He was the court's commissioner from January 2021 to February 2024, selected by the District Court judges [S1].
- He was a senior deputy prosecutor in the Skagit County Prosecuting Attorney's Office from 2007 to 2020, with a felony caseload that included computer-facilitated crimes, crimes against children and serious violent crimes [S1].
- From 2001 to 2006 he was in private practice (Rusing & Nielsen), handling criminal, domestic relations, personal injury and L&I cases [S1]. He attended Seattle University School of Law [S1].
- He filed for the seat on May 4, 2026, as an incumbent [S5] and is unopposed [S1].
- The VoteWA online voters' guide lists the race as a countywide nonpartisan office and no campaign website, only a campaign email address [S2].
