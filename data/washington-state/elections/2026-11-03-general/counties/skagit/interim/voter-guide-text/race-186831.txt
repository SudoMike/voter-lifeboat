## Melissa Beaton | Countywide Clerk | party: (Prefers Democratic Party) | ballot counties: Skagit | website: melissabeatonforclerk.com | email: voteforbeaton@gmail.com
Biography
Raised in Skagit County, I began my career in high school working for local law firms. I then spent two decades in Skagit County Superior Court before being elected County Clerk in 2019. I also serve on the Mount Vernon City Council and the Skagit County Law & Justice Council. I am a Certified Court Manager through the National Center for State Courts and a past president of both the Washington State Association of County Clerks and Skagit Rotary. A Leadership Skagit graduate, I am active in community service through Skagit Rotary, Community Action, the YMCA, and previous school committees.
Statement
I am honored to serve as your County Clerk and would be privileged to continue in that role. My career has been dedicated to the legal community, and public confidence in our justice system is more important than ever. The Clerk’s Office operates within the executive branch, independent of the judiciary, serving as the administrative and financial office of Superior Court. Responsible for a $3 million budget and 19 staff, I’ve worked to modernize services, implement e-filing, and build a strong, effective team.
This work is highly technical and vital—ensuring the integrity, accuracy, and accessibility of court records. Yet it is also deeply human. We serve people during some of the most difficult moments of their lives, whether navigating family matters, grieving loss, or facing a legal system that can feel overwhelming.
I believe the Clerk’s Office is about more than records and procedures. It is about helping people navigate a complex system with dignity, compassion, and respect. Skagit County deserves a Clerk invested in the community and working every day to make the justice system more accessible.
Thank you for the opportunity to serve. I would be honored to earn your vote again.

## Amanda Toscano | Countywide Clerk | party: (Prefers Independent Party) | ballot counties: Skagit | website:  | email: ar.toscano@yahoo.com
Biography
I am a lifelong Skagit County resident and real estate agent, raised in a rural working-class family and proud graduate of Concrete High School. I later earned my degree in Vancouver, Washington, and built a career in corporate customer service with an emphasis on management and leadership. In every industry where I’ve worked, I’ve been trusted with leadership roles. Today, I successfully manage millions of dollars in property contracts, tight deadlines, and a diverse clientele from many income levels and backgrounds. I am a results-oriented person who believes in honest, transparent collaboration with my community.
Statement
The County Clerk is the guardian of the Superior Court record and a vital link between the public, the courts, and other branches of county government. This office is responsible for receiving, processing, and preserving case filings, maintaining accurate dockets, and handling court-related financial transactions with care and accountability. The work demands precision, confidentiality, and a steady focus on fairness so that every person engaging with the court system can trust that their documents and payments are handled correctly.
As your County Clerk, and citizen representative to the courts, I will listen to anyone who wants to be heard to ensure that our procedures are clear, consistent, and customer-friendly at the counter and online. Drawing on my experience managing complex contracts, strict deadlines, and diverse clients, I will prioritize efficiency, accuracy, and accessibility in every aspect of the office. I will be a careful steward of public resources, prioritizing needs over wants and working daily to improve transparency and reduce barriers to justice. Together, we can strengthen trust in our courts and protect the integrity of the public record for our beautiful Skagit County.
