## Keith L. Wagoner | Countywide Commissioner District 3 | party: (Prefers Republican Party) | ballot counties: Skagit | website: wagonerforcc.com | email: wagonerkl@msn.com
Biography
Senator 39th Legislative District 2018-present (Minority Whip), Mayor City of Sedro-Woolley 2016-18, Sedro-Woolley City Council 2010-15
Served United States Marine Corps and U.S. Navy for 23 years as a Naval Aviator flying helicopters, including 13 years in overseas locations conducting resupply and Search and Rescue missions. Retired with the rank of Commander. Diplomatic assignment to U.S. Embassy Tokyo, Japan.
Washington State University (freshman year); United States Naval Academy, Annapolis MD. (BS, Physical Oceanography); University of San Diego (MS, Global Leadership)
Fraternal Order of Police 2020 Legislator of the Year, Sedro-Woolley Rotary; Fraternal Order of Eagles #2069, American Legion Post 41
Statement
My twenty-three-year military career as a naval officer coupled with executive experience as mayor and legislative experience as a state senator has provided a unique perspective on leadership and working with others to accomplish mutual goals. In an increasingly divisive political environment, now more than ever, we need people who reflect the values and needs of our rural communities and can provide steady, pragmatic leadership. Growing up in rural Skagit County, working in the fields, instilled a deep and personal connection to our county and its people. My Skagit roots run deep; my wife and I raised our children here. No state senator in the history of the legislative district has brought more of your hard-earned tax dollars back to east Skagit County for projects that improved our community. I understand the importance of promoting our farms and businesses and the need to reduce regulatory and tax burdens at all levels of government. No other candidate has my depth of experience or record of achievements for Skagit County. I humbly ask for your vote that I may continue to serve and improve our communities. I look forward to connecting with you and hearing your ideas for a better Skagit County.

## Joe Burns | Countywide Commissioner District 3 | party: (Prefers Democratic Party) | ballot counties: Skagit | website: electjoeburns.com | email: joe@electjoeburns.com
Biography
Current Skagit County Commissioner for District 3 and former Sedro-Woolley City Council member. Before public service, I spent two decades in the automotive industry, starting in trade school and serving nine years as shop foreman at Doyle Guffie’s Rallye Auto Sales. I then taught auto shop at Sedro-Woolley High School as a union member, helping prepare students for the future. Studied Gas and Diesel Automotive Service Technologies at the College of Southern Idaho and earned the AWC Certificate of Municipal Leadership. Current Sedro-Woolley Lions Club member and former president, Loggerodeo Parade Foreman, and former Helping Hands Food Bank board chair.
Statement
Like any parent, I care about whether my children will be able to afford to live, raise a family, and build a future in Skagit County 20 years from now. Those concerns guide my work as your Skagit County Commissioner.
Before public service, I spent two decades in the automotive industry, where I learned the value of accountability, problem-solving, and doing the job right. Trust and respect are earned through integrity and diligence, a principle I bring to county government every day.
Skagit County should be a place where hard work pays off, local businesses can grow, and young families have a reason to stay. That means protecting taxpayer dollars, planning responsibly for growth, and focusing on solutions to affordability. I believe people deserve local leaders who listen, stay focused on the work, and follow through.
As commissioner, I have helped secure grant funding, supported local agriculture, and strengthened flood protection for our communities. I’ve traveled across the county, meeting residents where they are and hearing directly from the people I serve.
Endorsed by Rick Larsen, Lisa Janicki, Julia Johnson, and many others. I would be honored to earn your vote to continue serving as your Skagit County Commissioner.
