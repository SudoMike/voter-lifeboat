40 Local Voters’ Pamphlet Welcome Message Dear Skagit County Voters, Your vote is your voice! That's why having information about candidates is important. We hope this printed pamphlet is helpful in making your voting decisions. An online version of the pamphlet is also available on our website, www.skagitcounty.net/elections This election, candidates will be running for federal, state, and county positions. As a voter, here are ways to ensure that your ballot is counted: • Return your voted ballot quickly • Use a ballot drop box whenever possible • If you return your ballot via US Postal Service, make sure it is postmarked by Election Day, November 3, 2026 • Sign your return ballot envelope • If you receive a letter from our office regarding your signature, return it to our office as soon as possible. The sooner voters return their ballots, the more complete the results will be when we post them on election night! Thank you! Instructions Vote for one in each contest Fill in the box to the left of your choice using black or blue ink. You may leave a contest blank; your ballot will still count. If you vote for more than one, no votes will be counted for that race. Correcting a mistake Draw a line through the incorrect candidate name or ballot. You may then you may make another choice. Optional write-in To vote for a candidate not listed in that contest, fill in the box to the left of the dashed line and print the name on the line. Who donates to campaigns? Use a blue or black ink pen to mark your ballot. You may leave a contest blank, your ballot will still count. Put your voted ballot in the security sleeve. Put the security sleeve in the return envelope. Seal the return envelope. Sign and date the declaration on the return envelope. If you are unable to sign, make your mark and have it witnessed by TWO people. Ensure that both people sign the envelope. A power of attorney cannot be used to sign a ballot. Deposit your ballot in a drop box. Drop boxes are picked up by Elections department staff, not the USPS. See drop box locations on the next page. Drop boxes open 20 days before Election Day. They remain open 24 hours a day until 8:00 PM Election Night. Skagit County Elections strongly encourages using drop boxes. Library service provided to Sedro-Woolley residents is based on an agreement between the City and the Central Skagit Library with fees tied to the Central Skagit Library levy rate. Voter approval of Proposition 1 will result in Sedro- Woolley becoming a part of the Central Skagit Library. The City would continue paying for library services through 2027. The City estimates that in 2028 the library district levy rate would be $0.2320 per $1,000 of assessed value and that the district would collect approximately $517,255 from property within the City.
