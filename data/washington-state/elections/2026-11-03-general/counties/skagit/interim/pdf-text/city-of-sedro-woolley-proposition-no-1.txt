City of Sedro - Woolley | Proposition No. 1 Proposition No. 1 Annexation Into Central Skagit Rural Partial - County Library District The City Council of Sedro - Woolley adopted Resolution Number 1191 - 26 concerning annexation into the Central Skagit Rural Partial - County Library District. The Central Skagit Rural Partial - County Library District (Central Skagit Library) covers the surroundin g rural areas within the Sedro - Woolley School District boundaries but does not include Sedro - Woolley city limits. Library service provided to Sedro - Woolley residents is based on an agreement between the City and the Central Skagit Library with fees tied to the Central Skagit Library levy rate. Voter approval of Proposition 1 will result in Sedro - Woolley becoming a part of the Central Skagit Library. Should the City of Sedro - Woolley be annexed to and become a part of the Central Skagit Rural Partial - County Library District? YES . . . . . . . . . □ NO . . . . . . . . . □ Explanatory Statement City of Sedro - Woolley If approved, City residents would remain eligible for library services and, beginning January 1, 2027, would be eligible to vote on library district ballot propositions. Because taxing district boundaries for the following tax year are set on August 1, the library district could begin levying property taxes within the c ity limits in 2028. The City would continue paying for library services through 2027. The City estimates that in 2028 the library district levy rate would be $0.2320 per $1,000 of assessed value and that the district would collect approximately $517,255 from property within the City. The refore, the City shall reduce its budgeted property tax levy by up to $474,115 in 2028 to offset most of the new library district property tax. Based on current estimates, a home with an assessed value of $519,450 would pay approximately $11.00 more in property tax in 2028. If the proposition is rejected, the City would remain outside the library district and library services would continue to be governed by the interlocal agreement, unless changed by the City and the district. If rejected, City residents would be ineligible to vote on library district ballot propositions, including library district property tax propositions (levy lid lifts).
