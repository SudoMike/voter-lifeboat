## Background
Anthony E. Howard (Nonpartisan) has been a District Court judge since 2015, after serving as a District Court commissioner (2011-2015), judge pro tem (2007-2011) and trial attorney (2000-2011) [S1]. He was presiding judge of the District Court (2016-2018) and Mental Health Court judge (2019-2023), and lists Youth Traffic Court judge and assistant presiding judge roles in 2026 [S1]. He holds a J.D. from Seattle University School of Law (2001) and a B.A. from the University of Washington (1998) [S1].

## Positions
- Describes himself as believing in common sense, accountability and public safety, while saying some cases call for addressing the underlying problems [S1].
- Says he founded Youth Traffic Court to reach teen drivers early [S1].
