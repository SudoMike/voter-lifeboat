## Background
Jeffrey D. Goodwin (Nonpartisan) was unanimously appointed to the District Court by the County Council in 2004 and has been re-elected six times [S1]. He has twice served as presiding judge and is president of the statewide District and Municipal Court Judges' Association [S1]. Before the bench he worked as a prosecutor, a public defender and in civil and criminal practice; he holds a J.D. from Seattle University (1992) [S1]. The pamphlet's text layout places the 'South District Court Judge Position 2' heading after his statement; the official ballot lists him for Position 2 [S1].
