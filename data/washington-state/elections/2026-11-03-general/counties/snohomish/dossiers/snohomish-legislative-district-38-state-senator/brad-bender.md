## Background
Brad Bender (Prefers Republican Party) is a first-time candidate who says he has "over thirty years of experience in the electrical field, both as a small business owner and a blue-collar worker," including bidding, budgeting and scheduling jobs [S1]. He states he has "no previous elected experience" [S1]. He submitted no other professional, education or community-service information for the pamphlet and lists only an email contact, no website [S1]. In the August 4 primary he took 33.6% to incumbent June Robinson's 66.2% in the count the Everett Herald reported [S3]. No campaign website or news profile of his platform was found as of 2026-10-08.

## Positions
- **Taxes:** wants Washington to "become a reasonable, economical, and low-tax state" and to help "lower taxes and make Washington an affordable place to live" [S1].
- **Spending:** says "the out-of-touch Democrats in Olympia continue to raise taxes to cover their own spending" [S1].
- **Business climate:** cites an Association of Washington Business figure that "nearly 1 in 4 small businesses are considering moving out of state" [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** no position found.
