## What the office does
A Washington State Senator is the single Senate member elected from each legislative district to the 49-member State Senate, for a four-year term. Senators write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, and confirm gubernatorial appointments. The November 3, 2026 general election decides the seat between the two candidates who advanced from the August primary.

## District and race dynamics
The 38th Legislative District covers most of Everett, Marysville and the Tulalip Reservation (june-robinson.md S5, S12). Democrat June Robinson, in the Legislature since 2013 and chair of Senate Ways & Means, faces Republican Brad Bender, an electrician and small-business owner running for office for the first time (june-robinson.md S1, S5; brad-bender.md S1). Robinson took 66.2% to Bender's 33.6% in the primary as reported by the Everett Herald (june-robinson.md S12). Robinson reports about $225k raised to Bender's about $2k (june-robinson.md S11; brad-bender.md S2).

Robinson presents the 2026 tax on income over $1 million as her tax-reform achievement and co-sponsored it; she was also prime sponsor of the 2021 capital gains tax (june-robinson.md S1, S7, S8). Bender's platform is a "low-tax state" and criticism of Democratic spending (brad-bender.md S1). Neither candidate has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Robinson co-sponsored the millionaires tax I-645 would repeal and prime-sponsored the capital gains tax; Bender wants lower taxes.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** Senate budget chair with 13 years in the Legislature vs. a first-time candidate.
3. **Spending:** Robinson cites record investments in housing, behavioral health and child care; Bender says Democrats raise taxes to cover their spending.
4. **Social policy:** Robinson pledges to protect abortion rights and LGBTQ+ neighbors; no position found for Bender.
5. **Parents and schools:** Robinson voted for ESHB 1296 and says she helped pass SB 5395, the comprehensive sex-education law (june-robinson.md S3); no position found for Bender.
