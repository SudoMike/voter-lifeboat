## Background
Jennifer Rancourt (Nonpartisan) has been a Snohomish County District Court judge since 2019 and was presiding judge in 2022-23 [S1]. She was Dean of the Washington State Judicial College in 2024, spent 17 years as a trial attorney in Snohomish County and 9 years as a judge pro tem in Marysville and Everett, and is a member of the Tulalip Tribal Bar [S1]. She chaired the Washington State Clemency and Pardons Board (2012-2018) and was Auditor of Defense Services for Mount Vernon and Burlington (2017-2018) [S1]. She holds a B.A., magna cum laude, from Western Washington University and a J.D. with honors from the University of Washington [S1].
