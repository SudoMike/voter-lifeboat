## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members elected from each legislative district to the 98-member House, for a two-year term, writing and voting on state law and the state budgets (local-voters-pamphlet page 30).

## District and race dynamics
Democrat Lillian Ortiz-Self, House Majority Caucus Chair and an Everett School District counselor, faces Libertarian Bruce Guthrie, a retired executive and teacher from Edmonds in his seventh Libertarian campaign (lillian-ortiz-self.md S1; bruce-guthrie.md S1, S2).

Ortiz-Self runs on education, behavioral health, child care, immigrant protections and reproductive rights, and voted for the 2026 income tax on millionaires and the 2025 capital gains and estate tax changes (lillian-ortiz-self.md S1, S2, S5, S7). Guthrie's first issue is repealing the state income tax; he also calls for cutting state spending and gas taxes, arresting shoplifters, school vouchers, term limits and barring "genetic males" from girls' high school sports (bruce-guthrie.md S1, S2). Ortiz-Self reports $49.9k raised; Guthrie's PDC summary shows none (lillian-ortiz-self.md S11; bruce-guthrie.md S3).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Ortiz-Self voted for ESSB 6346 and ESSB 5813; Guthrie's top issue is repealing the income tax.
2. **Spending:** Ortiz-Self calls for state investment to backfill federal cuts; Guthrie calls for spending cuts.
3. **Immigration:** Ortiz-Self sponsored detention-oversight and ICE-notice bills; Guthrie would help ICE deport only those convicted of crimes.
4. **Gender and sports eligibility / reproductive policy:** Guthrie supports sex-based eligibility in girls' sports; Ortiz-Self campaigns on abortion rights.
5. **Safety and climate:** Guthrie favors arrests for theft and opposes the Climate Commitment Act; Ortiz-Self cites behavioral health, gun safety and clean-economy votes.
