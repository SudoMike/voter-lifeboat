## Background
Lillian Ortiz-Self is the incumbent Democratic State Representative for the 21st District, Position 2, and House Majority Caucus Chair; she sits on the Education; Children, Youth and Families; Labor and Workplace Standards; and Rules committees [S1]. Her campaign site says she has been Majority Caucus Chair "for eight years" [S3]. She is a school counselor in the Everett School District and a former mental health counselor and mental health center director, with master's degrees in public administration and counseling from Drake University [S1]. She co-chairs the Washington State Latino Democratic Caucus and the Legislative Education Opportunity and Accountability Gap committee [S1].

## Positions
- **Cost of living / spending:** says she has supported "affordable health care, expanding child care opportunities, and addressing food and housing insecurity," and that "As the federal government continues to cut access to vital services, our state needs leadership that is ready and willing to invest in Washington" [S2].
- **Taxes:** no campaign statement found. Record: voted for ESSB 6346 [S5] and for ESSB 5813 (2025), which made capital gains and estate taxes more progressive [S7].
- **Public safety:** pamphlet cites "expanding behavioral health care, strengthening and diversifying law enforcement, and advancing gun violence prevention" [S1]; her site recounts arguing for a limit on firearm magazine capacity [S2].
- **Immigration:** "states must serve as the first line of defense against these attacks"; says she introduced legislation "increasing oversight on our private detention centers and to require notice of federal requests of employment documentation" to protect immigrants from ICE enforcement [S2].
- **Climate:** pamphlet cites "an infrastructure that supports a clean economy" [S1]; site lists "climate action" and "protecting our environment" [S3]; voted for 2SHB 1409, the 2025 clean fuels bill [S10].
- **Housing:** co-sponsor of and voted for EHB 1217 (2025), limiting rent and fee increases [S8]; voted for ESHB 2266 (2026), supportive housing and shelter siting [S9].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** site: abortion protection is "a protection that I and many other Democrats have long fought for in Olympia ... This is something we must continue fighting for" [S2].
- **Parents and schools:** a school counselor on the Education Committee who cites "equitable investments in early learning, education, and technical and career training" [S1]. Record: voted for ESHB 1296 (2025), which amended the I-2081 parental-rights list [S6]. No campaign statement on notification or opt-outs found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
