## Background
Bruce Guthrie, who states a Libertarian Party preference, says this is his "seventh campaign as a Libertarian in Washington since 2002"; he has not held elected office [S1]. He is a retired sales and marketing executive with a second career in teaching (Masters in Teaching, Seattle University), and holds a BA from Cornell and an MBA from Northwestern [S1][S2]. He has lived in Edmonds since 2008 [S2]. He reports collecting about 200 signatures for the six 2024 Let's Go Washington initiatives [S1].

## Positions
Top issues as listed on his site [S2], with pamphlet themes [S1]:
- **Taxes:** "Oppose/Repeal State Income Tax"; "Reduce other state taxes, including gas taxes"; calls the new income tax "unconstitutional" and predicts it "will soon be modified to affect the middle class" [S1][S2].
- **Spending:** "Reduce State Spending to help Reduce Inflation and consumer prices"; "a vote to fight the irresponsible spending in Olympia" [S1][S2].
- **Housing:** says rent is high "because property taxes are high and over-regulation of land reduces the supply of housing" [S2].
- **Public safety and homelessness:** "Fewer homeless by arresting shoplifters and bike thieves. Stop encouraging homelessness by the current policy of subsidizing it." [S2]
- **Climate and energy:** wants lower gas taxes and says the Climate Commitment Act and cap-and-invest "have not improved the environment" and have raised gas prices [S2].
- **Transportation:** "building more highway lanes on I-5 and I-405" [S2].
- **Immigration:** "Help ICE deport ONLY people convicted of crimes other than illegal immigration. Stand aside peacefully without cooperating when they try to deport all others." [S2]
- **Government reform:** "Term Limits. Four terms in the WA Legislature or in the House of representatives is plenty." [S2]
- **Technology and surveillance:** "Eliminate Flock Cameras." [S2]
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "Protect women's sports by precluding genetic males from competing against genetic females in grades 9 and above." [S2]
- **Parents and schools:** "Implement a voucher system so parents have the choice of where to send their kids to school" [S2]. This is school choice; no position on parental notification, records or opt-outs found. His three children attend private schools [S2].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
