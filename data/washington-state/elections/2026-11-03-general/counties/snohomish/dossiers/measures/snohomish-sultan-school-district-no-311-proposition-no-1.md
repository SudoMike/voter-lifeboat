## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would authorize the district to "modernize Gold Bar Elementary (pre-K; grades K-2), Sultan Middle (grades 3-5) and Sultan High (grades 6-8) sites; construct a new high school (grades 9-12, expanded career/technical education); repurpose Sultan Elementary (Parent Partnership, educational supports, turf field for student/community use); issue $160,000,000 of general obligation bonds maturing within 21 years; and levy annual excess property taxes to repay the bonds" [S1].

- The explanatory statement says the bonds would improve safety and security, "reduce or eliminate the use of portable classrooms for instruction," and move "the District's youngest learners out of the floodplain" at Gold Bar [S1].
- As a bond, it needs 60% approval [S2].

## Cost

- $160,000,000 in bonds over a maximum 21 years, repaid by annual excess property taxes "sufficient to pay bond debt service" [S1].
- The pamphlet gives no rate or per-home estimate [S1]. The district website could not be read in this pass (it renders only with JavaScript), and web search was unavailable, so no rate estimate is recorded.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- New bond debt; the resolution's text could not be read (the filed PDF is a scanned image).
- Prior attempt: in November 2024 a $79 million Sultan bond to build a new elementary school and modernize Gold Bar Elementary had 46.9% on election night, short of the 60% required [S2].
