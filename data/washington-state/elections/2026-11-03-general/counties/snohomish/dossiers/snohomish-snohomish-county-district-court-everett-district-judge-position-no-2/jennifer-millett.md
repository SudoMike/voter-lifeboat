## Background
Jennifer Millett (Nonpartisan) has been a District Court judge in the Everett Division since January 2026 and was a District Court commissioner from 2022 to 2026 [S1]. She was a judge pro tem in district and municipal courts in Snohomish, San Juan and King counties for eight years, prosecuting attorney for the City of Marysville, a Snohomish County deputy prosecutor and law clerk to Superior Court Judge Thomas J. Wynne [S1]. She holds a J.D. (2000) and B.A. (1998) from Gonzaga University [S1]. The ballot lists this position as a short and full term [S1].
