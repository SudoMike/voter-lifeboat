## Background
Edwin H. Feller is the Republican-preference candidate for the 2nd Congressional District and has never held public office [S1][S3]. He grew up on a Whatcom County dairy farm, graduated from Lynden Christian High School and attended WSU [S1]. He reports 60 years in the HVAC industry, including work for the U.S. State Department servicing sensitive U.S. facilities in 60 countries [S1]. His filing lists a Lynden mailing address [S3]. Community service: The Firs Bible & Missionary Conference [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** "the affordability crisis is self-inflicted due to taxes being too high, and the government growing too large" [S1]; supports "balanced budgets" [S1] and lists "Fiscal Responsibility" among his priorities [S2]. Proposes "repealing federal carbon taxes" [S2].
- **Public safety:** supports "robust law enforcement" and respect for first responders [S1]. Proposes a federal "Safe Streets mandate tying grants to local cooperation, designating cartels as foreign terrorist organizations, and ending sanctuary policies" [S2].
- **Immigration:** supports "secure borders" [S1]; proposes "Overhauling immigration to prioritize nuclear families and a merit-based points system" and ending chain migration [S2].
- **Energy and climate:** proposes "Dismantling radical environmental mandates, streamlining energy permits, expanding nuclear and hydroelectric capacity, and repealing federal carbon taxes" [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** supports Title IX protections for women and girls [S1]; proposes "Codifying that 'sex' in Title IX refers exclusively to biological sex" for collegiate and high school athletics [S2], and a "federal ban on irreversible medical procedures for minors" [S2].
- **Parents and schools:** frames the proposed ban on irreversible medical procedures for minors as "prioritizing long-term psychological health and parental rights over ideological experimentation" [S2]. No other position on parental notification, record access or curriculum opt-outs found. (Recorded only; not scored for a federal contest.)
- **Elections:** supports "fair elections" [S1].
- **Health care, defense and technology:** no position found.
