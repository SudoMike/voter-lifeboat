## What the office does
A U.S. Representative serves a two-year term in the House, writes and votes on federal law and appropriations, conducts oversight of the executive branch, and provides constituent services. The 2nd District includes portions of Snohomish County (including Everett) and all of Skagit, Whatcom, Island and San Juan counties (rick-larsen.md S5).

## District and race dynamics
Democratic incumbent Rick Larsen, in his thirteenth term and the lead Democrat on the Transportation & Infrastructure Committee, faces Republican Edwin H. Feller, a Lynden-area HVAC veteran who has never held office (rick-larsen.md S5; edwin-h-feller.md S1). Both advanced from a four-candidate August primary in which two candidates running to Larsen's left were eliminated (primary _contest.md). Larsen runs on cost of living, health care access, ICE accountability, abortion rights and infrastructure funding (rick-larsen.md S1, S2). Feller runs on lower taxes and smaller government, enforcement-first immigration and public safety, repeal of federal carbon taxes and sex-based athletics eligibility (edwin-h-feller.md S1, S2). Neither has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Immigration:** Larsen conditions DHS/ICE funding on oversight and voted against the Laken Riley Act; Feller would end sanctuary policies and move to merit-based immigration.
2. **Climate:** Larsen voted for the Inflation Reduction Act and calls climate mitigation a top priority; Feller would dismantle environmental mandates and repeal carbon taxes.
3. **Taxes and spending:** Feller blames taxes and government size for the affordability crisis; Larsen voted against the 2025 tax-and-spending reconciliation law and supports lifting the Social Security tax cap.
4. **Social policy:** Larsen backs the Women's Health Protection Act; Feller backs a biological-sex definition in Title IX and a ban on irreversible medical procedures for minors.
5. **Record vs. renewal:** a thirteen-term incumbent versus a first-time candidate.
