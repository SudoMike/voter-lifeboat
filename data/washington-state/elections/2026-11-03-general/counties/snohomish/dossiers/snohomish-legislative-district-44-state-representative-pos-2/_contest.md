## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House, for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules for cities, health and behavioral-health funding, and criminal-justice statutes.

## District and race dynamics
Democrat April Berg, in office since 2021 and chair of the House Finance Committee, faces Republican Tonya Stadlman, a housing-industry manager for 30 years and secretary of the 44th LD Republican organization, running for office for the first time (april-berg.md S1; tonya-stadlman.md S1). As Finance chair, Berg says she helped lead the 2026 Millionaires Tax (ESSB 6346), which I-645 would repeal (april-berg.md S3, S5). Stadlman's "Six to Fix" platform starts with cutting the gas tax and "Audit Before Ask," calls for permitting reform, and pledges to "restore and protect parental rights" (tonya-stadlman.md S2).

Neither candidate has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives by name. Berg reports $250k raised to Stadlman's $37k (april-berg.md S13; tonya-stadlman.md S3).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** a lead author of the Millionaires Tax vs. a candidate pledging lower taxes starting with the gas tax.
2. **Spending:** Berg's platform of public investment (universal childcare, housing trust fund) vs. Stadlman's "audit programs, measure outcomes."
3. **Housing:** public and supportive housing funding and a vote for the 2025 rent cap vs. permitting reform and the view that regulation raises housing costs.
4. **Parents and schools:** Berg voted for ESHB 1296 (2025 amendments to the I-2081 list); Stadlman pledges to "restore" parental rights and parents' access to information.
5. **Climate:** Berg supports clean fuel standards; Stadlman would start cost relief with the gas tax.
6. **Record vs. renewal:** Finance Committee chair vs. a first-time candidate.
