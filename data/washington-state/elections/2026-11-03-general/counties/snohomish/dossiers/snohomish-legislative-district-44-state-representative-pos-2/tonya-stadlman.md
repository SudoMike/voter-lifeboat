## Background
Tonya Stadlman is the Republican candidate for the 44th District, Position 2. Her pamphlet statement lists 30 years in Washington's housing industry managing affordable and conventional residential communities, and service as a PCO and secretary of the 44th Legislative District Republican organization [S1]. She is a certified health and wellness coach and yoga teacher, serves on a Snohomish County community advisory board and is "Crime Free Housing trained" [S1]. She has not held public office [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes and affordability:** wants "safer communities, lower taxes, and a business-friendly climate" [S1]; her "Six to Fix" platform says: "First, reduce costs that hit household budgets directly, starting with the gas tax. Before asking Washingtonians for more, audit where the money is going" [S2]. She would "examine reforms to the B&O tax" [S2].
- **Spending:** platform standard "Audit Before Ask. Measure the Results. Put People Over Politics"; "Spending more money or creating another program doesn't automatically solve the problem"; "Audit programs, measure outcomes, and invest in approaches that demonstrate results" [S2]. Says Olympia spends "more every year while public safety, affordability, and our basic systems have gotten worse" [S2].
- **Housing:** "you cannot make housing more affordable by making housing more expensive to build, own, and operate"; would "reduce unnecessary barriers, speed up permitting, encourage more starter homes and diverse housing choices, and protect the housing we already have" [S2]. Says cost of living, public safety and homelessness are "all driven by the laws Olympia passed" [S1].
- **Public safety:** "Be SMART, not simply soft or hard. That means accountability for dangerous behavior, support for law enforcement, consequences for repeat offenders, and treatment and recovery when addiction or behavioral health is driving the cycle" [S2]. Notes the state "ranks 51st in police officers per capita" [S1]. Would "pair housing with treatment and recovery when needed, and make independence the goal" [S2].
- **Climate and energy:** her only energy position is reducing the gas tax [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** "Parents should never feel excluded from their child's education"; "I will fight to restore and protect parental rights and ensure parents have access to important information about their children's education and well-being" [S2]. Calls for "strong academics, safe classrooms, transparency, accountability" [S2].
