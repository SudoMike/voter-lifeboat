## Background
Jason Moon is Mukilteo City Council President and a Democrat challenging the Democratic incumbent [S1]. He lists service on the Puget Sound Regional Council Economic Development Board, the Snohomish County Law and Justice Council and the Snohomish County Steering Committee [S1]. He is a partner at Zeolite Composites and previously worked in account management at Microsoft and Amazon and for nonprofits; he holds an MBA and bachelor's degree from the University of Washington and attended Kamiak High School [S1]. He is the son of immigrants who owned a Lynnwood dry cleaner [S1]. The pamphlet's votemoon.com address redirects to hellojasonmoon.com, whose contact details match the pamphlet [S1][S2]. (Not to be confused with Cliff Moon, a Republican candidate in the 1st District.)

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "We need common-sense leadership that protects taxpayers from higher taxes and keeps government accountable. It's not a Revenue problem, it's a spending problem." [S2] Pamphlet: "accountable budgeting protecting taxpayers and essential services" [S1].
- **Spending:** "without a balanced budget and real accountability, we can't fund our state or the essential programs and services people rely on" [S2].
- **Local control and housing:** "Poorly planned state mandated density threatens the character of our communities. Local governments need more control because communities have different needs." [S1] Site: "No Cookie Cutter Housing ... our cities understand their needs far better than any top-down mandate ever will" and "State-level decisions have overridden local control" [S2].
- **Public safety:** "Washington has the lowest law-enforcement staffing per capita in the country and cases are being left on the table— that has to change." Also calls mental health, addiction and homelessness "system failures," not "moral failings" [S2].
- **Full-time representation:** pledges to resign from the Mukilteo City Council if elected, "because it's impossible to hold two elected offices," and criticizes the incumbent for "simultaneously holding two elected positions" [S1][S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** says "Schools are underfunded" [S1]; lists Endeavour Elementary PTA among community service [S1]. No position on notification, records or opt-outs found.
