## Background
Robert (Chili) Hicks is a Republican Stanwood City Councilmember, elected in 2024 [S1]. He retired as a Navy Master Chief Petty Officer after a 21-year career, then worked as an HR manager at Boeing, a telecommunications HR executive and a small business owner for 30 years [S1]. He holds an MBA from Saint Leo University and a BA from Columbia College, and lists service on the Stanwood Planning Commission, the Stanwood-Camano YMCA board, the Stanwood Salary Commission, veterans' posts and the Boeing Employee Veteran Association [S1]. His campaign site describes him as a father of three and grandfather of six who has lived in Washington nearly 30 years [S2]. He has not held state office [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "I will fight to lower taxes, slash government waste, protect your family budget, and restore accountability" [S1]. Proposes to "lower the state sales tax and stop new taxes and fees" and to eliminate the estate tax [S3]; headline plank "REDUCING TAXES & ELIMINATING RED TAPE" [S2].
- **Spending:** says one-party control has produced "reckless spending, waste, and failed policies" and calls for "common-sense fiscal discipline" [S1]; proposes "public review of every major budget," "independent audits to expose waste" and "performance-based funding" tied to results [S3]; "REINING-IN OUT OF CONTROL SPENDING" [S2].
- **Public safety:** supports restoring police pursuit authority (citing Initiative 2113), police that are "fully funded, properly trained, and empowered," holding "judges and prosecutors accountable," and to "crack down hard on fentanyl dealers and open-air drug markets," alongside expanded "access to drug treatment for those ready to turn their lives around" [S3]. Site plank: "ALLOWING PEOPLE TO BE VICTIMIZED & DIE AT THE HANDS OF CRIMINALS AND ILLICIT DRUGS ISN'T COMPASSION" [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** "GUTTING THE VOTER-APPROVED PARENTS BILL OF RIGHTS IS A BRIDGE TOO FAR" [S2]. Proposes restoring the Parents' Bill of Rights protections, requiring each school to "post its curriculum openly," that parents "be informed before any non-emergency health services," and giving parents "clear access to curriculum, test scores, and performance measures" [S3]. Also supports expanded "school choice options—including more charter opportunities and education savings accounts" and a focus on reading, math and life skills [S3].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-08; his platform calls for lower taxes and no new taxes [S1][S3].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** no statement naming I-1 found as of 2026-10-08. His site objects to "gutting the voter-approved parents bill of rights" and proposes restoring its protections [S2][S3], which is what I-1 would do by repealing the 2025 amendments.
- **I-638 (IL26-638):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.

## Record
- Stanwood City Council, elected 2024; previously Stanwood Planning Commissioner [S1]. No legislative voting record.
- PDC (updated 2026-10-05): $81,556.03 in contributions and $67,011.86 in expenditures for 2026 (committee filed as Robert D. Hicks) [S5].

- Platform centers lower and fewer taxes (sales tax cut, estate tax repeal, no new taxes or fees) and audits and performance-based budgeting [S1][S3].
- Specific parental-rights proposals: curriculum posting, prior notice of non-emergency health services, restoring the original parents' bill of rights [S3].
- Enforcement-first public-safety platform with a treatment component for those "ready" [S3].
- Local elected experience (Stanwood council since 2024) but no state legislative record, against a sitting representative [S1].
