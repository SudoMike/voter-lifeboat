## Background
Clyde Shavers is the incumbent Democratic State Representative for the 10th Legislative District, Position 1, and is Vice Chair of the House Education Committee and a member of the Washington Artificial Intelligence Task Force [S1]. He is a U.S. Naval Academy graduate who served in the Navy in the Middle East and Southeast Asia, a Yale Law School graduate and practicing attorney, and lives on Whidbey Island [S1]. His campaign site says he served nearly a decade in the Navy [S2]. Community service listed in his statement: Skagit Friendship House board; former boards of Goosefoot and the Whidbey Veterans Resource Center [S1]. The Everett Herald noted that with two candidates filed, both he and Robert (Chili) Hicks advanced automatically to the general [S17].

## Positions
- **Taxes and costs:** "I do not support raising taxes – and instead, we need to get money back into the hands of working people" [S3]. His pamphlet statement says he voted no on a tax that would have made gas cost more, has worked for years on expanding property-tax exemptions for seniors and veterans, co-sponsored legislation to reduce pressure on food prices and "fought unfair rate hikes" on water bills [S1]. His site says he "fought to lower property taxes for more seniors, veterans, and the disabled" and to "cap medication costs" [S2].
- **Housing:** "The state legislature needs to break down barriers and remove obstacles so that non-profits and other developers are incentivized to build affordable housing" [S3]; says he "funded affordable housing for Langley, Coupeville, Stanwood, and Arlington" [S2].
- **Public safety:** "I will always support our law enforcement ... We must give our law enforcement family the resources and tools to do their job right" [S3].
- **Climate and energy:** "Washington can lead the way in developing a strong clean energy economy that provides high-wage jobs without leaving our current workforce behind" [S3]; lists HB 1924 on fusion energy among his accomplishments [S4]; describes himself as "an environment advocate" [S2]. See Record for his votes on the 2025 clean fuel standard and gas-tax bills.
- **Veterans:** passed legislation for more Veteran Service Officers [S1][S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "I am 100 percent pro-choice. I believe that women – not politicians – should make their own health care decisions" [S3]; "100% pro-choice" [S2]. Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S16] and the Washington Stonewall Democrats [S5].
- **Parents and schools:** Vice Chair of House Education [S1]; lists HB 2236 on career learning opportunities for every child [S4]; voted for ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S8]. No campaign statement on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted no on ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S7].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted yes on ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments to the I-2081 list that I-1 would repeal [S8].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
