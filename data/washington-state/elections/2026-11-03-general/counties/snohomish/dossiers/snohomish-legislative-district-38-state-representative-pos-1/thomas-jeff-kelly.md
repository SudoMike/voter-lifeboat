## Background
Thomas "Jeff" Kelly (Prefers Cascade Party) is a first-time candidate, a U.S. Army veteran and combat medic, and a registered nurse [S1][S3][S4]. He chairs the Delta Neighborhood Association in north Everett and is a board member of the Cascade Party of Washington [S1][S3]. The Herald describes the Cascade Party as a centrist party created in 2024 by Krist Novoselic and reports Kelly is 49, retired from nursing and lives in Everett [S4]. He describes growing up with a single father and his family relying at times on welfare, food stamps, Section 8 housing and Medicaid [S3]. He finished second in the primary with 28.4% [S5].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** pamphlet lists "lowering taxes" as a priority [S1]. His website calls for reducing "the current State gas tax burden at the pump" and a "proportional tax plan that decreases burdens on working families" [S2]. He told the Herald the tax system is regressive ("People are just getting fleeced") and pledged to bring forward a fairer tax plan in his first term without yet knowing its shape; he also suggested tax holidays [S4].
- **Spending:** said the Legislature will have to cut spending and that he would run a cost-benefit analysis of state programs, while avoiding "cavalier" cuts [S4]; listed cutting back excessive government spending as a goal [S5].
- **Public safety:** website: "Fully fund law enforcement," fund police units against organized retail theft, "Treat drug addiction, do not enable it," and "Protect our public spaces from harmful and illegal activities" [S2]. Pamphlet: fund police and behavioral health; "Services should heal, not enable, through clear expectations, real treatment, and incentives" [S1]. Opposes tapping police and fire pension surplus for the state budget [S4].
- **Housing:** "Increase housing through builder-friendly policies" [S2]; would streamline permit timelines [S4]; supports more wraparound services for homelessness [S4].
- **Economy:** expand trade programs, keep maritime, aviation and timber industries local, business-friendly living-wage job creation, free day-use entry to state parks [S2][S4].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** no position found.
