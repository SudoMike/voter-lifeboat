## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House, for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health and behavioral-health funding, and criminal-justice statutes. The November 3, 2026 general election decides the seat between the two candidates who advanced from the August primary.

## District and race dynamics
The 38th Legislative District covers most of Everett, Marysville and the Tulalip Reservation (julio-cortes.md S11). Democrat Julio Cortes, in office since 2022 and an Everett economic-development manager, faces Thomas "Jeff" Kelly, an Army veteran and nurse running for the centrist Cascade Party (julio-cortes.md S1; thomas-jeff-kelly.md S1, S4). In a three-way primary Cortes took 51.1%, Kelly 28.4% and progressive Democrat Annie Fitzgerald 19.3%, so the general is a Democrat vs. a third-party candidate (julio-cortes.md S12). Cortes reports about $84k raised to Kelly's about $18.6k (julio-cortes.md S13).

Both name affordability as the top issue (julio-cortes.md S11). Cortes voted for the 2026 millionaires tax, rent stabilization and ESHB 1296, and stresses diversion and prevention (julio-cortes.md S1, S7, S8, S9, S11). Kelly runs on lower taxes and a gas-tax cut, fully funding law enforcement, "builder-friendly" housing and spending review, while also calling the tax system regressive (thomas-jeff-kelly.md S1, S2, S4). Neither has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Public safety:** Cortes emphasizes juvenile diversion and reentry support; Kelly emphasizes funding police and treatment that does "not enable" addiction.
2. **Taxes:** Cortes voted for and promotes the millionaires tax; Kelly wants lower taxes and a smaller gas tax but an undefined fairer plan.
3. **Housing:** Cortes co-sponsored rent-increase limits as well as faster permitting; Kelly favors builder-friendly supply policies.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** two-term incumbent vs. a first-time third-party candidate.
5. **Spending:** both call for program review; Kelly expects cuts, Cortes wants to protect early learning and child care.
