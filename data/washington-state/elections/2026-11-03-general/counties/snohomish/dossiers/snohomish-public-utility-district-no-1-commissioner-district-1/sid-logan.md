## Background
Sid Logan is the incumbent Snohomish County PUD No. 1 commissioner for District 1, serving since 2017; he is a member of the APPA Policy Makers Council and a director of Energy Northwest [S1]. He reports 14 years in engineering, 10 years as a public-schools operations director, 5 years as a school bus driver and 2 years as a commercial fisherman, and holds a B.S. in engineering from the University of Alaska [S1]. Community service includes VP and treasurer of the Arlington Smokey-Point Chamber and PTA/PTSA leadership in Arlington [S1]. The PUD serves the residents of Snohomish County and Camano Island, with electric service to 380,000 homes and businesses and water service to over 23,000 homes [S3].

## Positions
- **Rates:** says rates under his leadership have stayed "well below that of our neighboring utilities," that five of his nine years had no general rate increase, and that he anticipates no rate increase for 2027 [S1].
- **Reliability and risk:** expects excellent storm service, investments to minimize outages and preparation for cyber-attacks [S1]. Was "a strong advocate" for the recent change to the PUD's Bonneville Power Administration contract, which he says requires BPA to supply enough power to meet the PUD's needs and removes exposure to extreme market prices [S1].
- **Clean energy:** supports "further integration of renewable energy," notes the PUD's electricity is 97% carbon free, and supports the PUD's 20-year plan to provide for growth with clean energy [S1].
- **Customer assistance:** advocates PUD financial-assistance programs for struggling families and seniors and the Community Energy Fund [S1].
