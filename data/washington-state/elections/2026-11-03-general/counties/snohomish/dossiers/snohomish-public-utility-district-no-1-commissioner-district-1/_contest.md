## What the office does
A Snohomish County PUD commissioner sets policy for the customer-owned utility that provides electricity to 380,000 homes and businesses and water to over 23,000 homes in Snohomish County and on Camano Island: rates, power supply contracts (including with the Bonneville Power Administration), resource planning, reliability and customer programs (sid-logan.md S3, S1). The term is six years (local-voters-pamphlet page 79).

## Electorate
Commissioner District 1 candidates are nominated in the primary by District 1 voters only, but under RCW 54.12.010(3) "Voters of the entire public utility district may vote at a general election to elect a person as a commissioner of the commissioner district." In the general, every Snohomish County voter (and Camano Island voters in Island County) votes in this race.

## District and race dynamics
Incumbent Sid Logan, commissioner since 2017 and an Energy Northwest director, faces former Island County Commissioner Janet St Clair (sid-logan.md S1; janet-st-clair.md S1). Bruce King, the third primary candidate, was eliminated in August (primary _contest.md). Both finalists promise low rates and reliable service. Logan runs on his rate record (no general increase in five of nine years, none expected for 2027), the revised BPA contract, and a 97% carbon-free supply (sid-logan.md S1). St Clair argues the PUD must do more on climate and economic challenges and grow water and energy investments, and stresses collaboration and climate resilience (janet-st-clair.md S1, S2). Logan's endorsers include local officials of both parties and IBEW Local 77; St Clair's include Democratic legislators, the Snohomish County Democrats and Washington Conservation Action (sid-logan.md S1; janet-st-clair.md S1, S3). St Clair has raised $18.8k to Logan's $4.1k in 2026 (janet-st-clair.md S4; sid-logan.md S2).

## Differentiating issue axes
The rubric has no axes for PublicUtility contests, so neither candidate is scored. What differentiates them for voters:
1. **Record vs. renewal:** nine-year utility incumbent versus a county-government and nonprofit leader new to the utility.
2. **Climate emphasis:** Logan stresses the existing clean supply and rate stability; St Clair says more needs to be done on climate challenges.
3. **Coalitions:** bipartisan local and labor endorsements versus Democratic-legislator and environmental-group endorsements.
