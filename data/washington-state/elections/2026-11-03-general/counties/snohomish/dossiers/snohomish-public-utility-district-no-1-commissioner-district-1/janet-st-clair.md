## Background
Janet St Clair served eight years as an Island County Commissioner and was elected four times as chair of the Board of Health [S1]. She was a nonprofit executive for 30 years, sits on the Washington Association of Counties board, and holds a BA and an MSW from UC Berkeley [S1]. She served six years on the state Community Economic Revitalization Board and on the Puget Sound Partnership's Ecosystem Coordination Board, and led efforts to expand internet access to unserved areas [S1]. Her campaign site lists a Camano Island mailing address [S2], which is part of the PUD's service area with Snohomish County [S5].

## Positions
- **Rates:** "As your SnoPUD Commissioner, I will keep your energy bills low and your service reliable" [S1]; promises "low rates and investing in affordable plans for small businesses, seniors and working families" [S2].
- **Reliability:** "Investing in our energy grid and protecting our infrastructure in the face of climate threats and cyber attacks" [S2].
- **Climate and resources:** says the PUD "has taken important steps" but "there is more we need to do to address today's climate and economic challenges"; wants to "protect our water and energy resources and grow those investments" [S1]. Lists "diversification, strategic capital planning and a balanced energy portfolio" and "climate resiliency, flood mitigation and managing water pollutants" as priorities [S2].
- **Collaboration:** "Building a stronger voice for PUD customers through collaboration" serving municipalities, tribes and rural communities [S2].
