## What the office does
A Washington State Senator is the single Senate member from each of the 49 legislative districts, elected to a four-year term. Senators write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, and the Senate alone confirms the governor's appointments (local-voters-pamphlet page 30).

## District and race dynamics
The 21st District is in southwest Snohomish County, and both candidates have served on the Mukilteo City Council (marko-liias.md S1; riaz-khan.md S1). Democrat Marko Liias, in the Senate since 2014 and chair of the Senate Transportation Committee, faces Republican Riaz Khan, a Boeing employee and former one-term Mukilteo councilmember (marko-liias.md S1; riaz-khan.md S1).

Liias runs on housing, transportation jobs and protection from federal policy, and has recorded votes for the 2026 tax on income over $1 million (ESSB 6346) and the 2025 capital gains and estate tax changes (marko-liias.md S5, S7). Khan runs on lower taxes, reduced spending, public safety and parental rights, stated without specific proposals (riaz-khan.md S1, S2). Liias reports about $411k raised; Khan's PDC summary shows none (marko-liias.md S12; riaz-khan.md S3). Neither has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives.

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Liias voted for two progressive tax bills; Khan campaigns on lower taxes.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** twelve-year senator and committee chair vs. a former city councilmember.
3. **Spending:** Liias cites transportation and school investment; Khan cites cutting unnecessary spending.
4. **Parents and schools:** Liias voted for ESHB 1296; Khan names parental rights as a priority without detail.
5. **Climate:** Liias's record includes clean fuels and building-emissions laws; Khan states no climate position.
