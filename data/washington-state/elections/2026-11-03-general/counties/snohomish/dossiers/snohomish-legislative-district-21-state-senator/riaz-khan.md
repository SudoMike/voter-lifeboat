## Background
Riaz Khan (PDC filer name Mohammed Riaz Khan) is a Republican candidate and former Mukilteo City Councilmember (2019-2023) [S1][S3]. He has worked at Boeing since 1999, was a Boeing SPEEA council representative 2019-2024 and a Snohomish County and Washington State Labor Council delegate 2020-2024, and says he founded a financial company [S1][S2]. He holds an MBA in leadership from Webster University and a Certified Public Manager credential from Texas State University [S1]. He has not held state office [S1]. Identity checked against the pamphlet contact details, which match his campaign site and PDC filing [S1][S2][S3].
