## Background
Rick Leo (Nonpartisan) has been an Evergreen District Court judge since 2023, after serving as a District Court commissioner (2015-2023) [S1]. He was a Snohomish County deputy prosecuting attorney and public defender, a private criminal defense attorney and owner of a solo practice [S1]. He served as president, president-elect, vice president and secretary/treasurer of the state District and Municipal Court Judges' Association, and sits on the Ethics Advisory Committee [S1]. He holds a J.D. from the Columbus School of Law and an M.A. and B.A. in criminal justice from SUNY Albany [S1].
