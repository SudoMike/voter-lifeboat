## Background
Dave Paul is the incumbent Democratic State Representative for the 10th Legislative District, Position 2, and chairs the House Postsecondary Education & Workforce Committee; he also serves on the Technology, Community & Economic Development & Veterans and Transportation committees [S1]. The Everett Herald reported in July 2026 that he is seeking a fifth term and works as Director of Community Relations at Skagit Valley College [S15]. He holds a BA from Seattle University, an MA from Miami University (Ohio) and a doctorate from the University of Illinois [S1]. His campaign site says he has served Skagit Valley College for over 15 years [S2]. Community service listed in his statement: Island County Economic Development Council, Oak Harbor Educational Foundation (past president), Rotary, and formerly the Oak Harbor School District Budget Committee and Sustainable Whidbey Coalition [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** pamphlet says he "helped eliminate taxes for small businesses and is fighting to fix our upside-down tax code" [S1]. Told the Herald he would protect Workforce Education Investment Act funding for college grants [S15]. See Record for his tax votes.
- **Costs and health care:** says he passed legislation that lowered the cost of asthma inhalers and EpiPens, and reduced college costs and fixed tuition loopholes affecting veterans and military families [S1].
- **Housing:** "Affordable housing is critical because our communities are stronger when residents can afford to live where they work" [S2]; lists housing affordability as a community priority [S1].
- **Ferries and transportation:** prioritizes funding for three unbudgeted electric ferries, warning that without them many ferries may need to come out of service around 2035 [S15]; lists improved ferry service as a priority [S1].
- **Public safety:** "works closely with law enforcement to improve public safety and mental health services" [S1]; told the Herald he is working to improve Designated Crisis Responder programs, especially on Whidbey Island [S15].
- **Jobs:** "Workers in the 10th District deserve to be paid a living-wage" [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "Dave is pro-choice and endorsed by Planned Parenthood" [S1]; Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates lists him [S14].
- **Parents and schools:** education is his campaign's lead issue ("Everyone deserves the opportunity to receive quality education") [S2]; endorsed by WEA, AFT and the Washington State PTA [S3][S13]; voted for ESHB 1296 (see Record) [S6]. No campaign statement on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
