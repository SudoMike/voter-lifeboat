## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including ferry funding, which dominates this island district's agenda (dave-paul.md S15).

## District and race dynamics
Democratic incumbent Dave Paul, seeking a fifth term and chair of House Postsecondary Education & Workforce, faces Republican Tim Hazelo, a Navy veteran and contractor from Oak Harbor and former Island County Republican chair (dave-paul.md S1, S15; tim-hazelo.md S1, S6). Republican Carrie Kennedy also ran in the August primary and did not advance (dave-paul.md S15).

Ferries are the clearest policy split: Paul prioritizes funding three more electric ferries, while Hazelo opposes electric ferries as less dependable and costlier and favors diesel-electric boats built locally (dave-paul.md S15; tim-hazelo.md S6). On taxes, Paul voted for the 2026 tax on income over $1M and the 2025 gas-tax package and says he is fixing the "upside-down tax code," while Hazelo opposes any income tax and taxes not approved by voters (dave-paul.md S1, S5, S7; tim-hazelo.md S3, S6). Paul states he is pro-choice; no Hazelo position on abortion was found (dave-paul.md S1). The Herald reported Hazelo's 2025 conviction, under appeal, related to 2024 ballot-counting observation (tim-hazelo.md S6). PDC: Paul $137.3k raised, Hazelo $20.3k (dave-paul.md S4; tim-hazelo.md S7).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** income tax over $1M supporter vs. opponent of any income tax.
2. **Climate/energy:** electric ferries and the clean fuel standard (Paul voted yes) vs. diesel-electric ferries.
3. **Spending:** Hazelo's audits and skepticism of social-program spending vs. Paul's votes for the operating budget.
4. **Record vs. renewal:** four-term incumbent and committee chair vs. a first-time state candidate.
5. **Safety:** Hazelo would end cashless bail; Paul emphasizes crisis responders and mental health alongside law enforcement.
