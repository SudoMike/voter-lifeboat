## Background
Mary Fosse (Prefers Democratic Party) is the incumbent State Representative and Deputy Majority Floor Leader, and previously served on the Everett City Council [S1]. She lists experience as a former Everett small business owner, event coordinator, State House staff member and co-chair of the Underground Economy Taskforce, and a BA in History from Whitman College [S1]. Community service listed includes chairing the Delta Neighborhood Association, serving as an Everett Districting Commissioner and on the Everett Public Schools Fiscal Advisory Council, and founding the Delta Kids Program [S1].

## Positions
- Says many residents are being priced out by rising costs of gas, housing, childcare and healthcare, and cites her own childhood poverty [S1].
- Lists her priorities for the next two years as lowering costs of living, supporting working families, affordable housing, childcare, improving healthcare access, protecting consumers, and building safe, resilient communities [S1].
