## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House, for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules for cities, health and behavioral-health funding, and criminal-justice statutes.

## District and race dynamics
Democrat Brandy Donaghy, in office since 2021 and Vice Chair of House Transportation, faces Republican Chris Elder, a 20-year Navy aviation veteran, farmer and realtor seeking office for the first time (brandy-donaghy.md S1; chris-elder.md S1). Both are Navy veterans (brandy-donaghy.md S1; chris-elder.md S1).

Donaghy's record centers road safety, emergency preparedness and consumer protection, and her site states explicit support for reproductive rights and gender-affirming care (brandy-donaghy.md S2, S3). She voted for the 2026 Millionaires Tax and the 2025 tax increases (brandy-donaghy.md S7-S10). Elder runs on cutting "wasteful spending rather than raise taxes," citing the growth of state budgets, and on easing housing regulation and adding police (chris-elder.md S3). Neither candidate has stated a position on the three statewide initiatives. Donaghy reports $94k raised to Elder's $40k (brandy-donaghy.md S13; chris-elder.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Donaghy's votes for the 2025-26 tax increases vs. Elder's pledge to fight tax increases.
2. **Spending:** Elder centers spending restraint; Donaghy favors childcare, healthcare-workforce and behavioral-health investment.
3. **Record vs. renewal:** five-year incumbent and committee vice chair vs. a first-time candidate.
4. **Housing:** Donaghy pairs supply (transit-oriented development, co-housing) with a vote for the 2025 rent cap; Elder would ease regulations.
5. **Public safety:** Donaghy's prevention and early-intervention framing vs. Elder's support for more police plus crime prevention.
