## Background
Chris Elder is the Republican candidate for the 44th District, Position 1. He is a 20-year Navy veteran in naval aviation, a business owner, farmer and realtor, and the founder of the Mil2Vet podcast for veterans leaving the military [S1][S2]. His pamphlet statement lists PCO as his elected experience and says this is his first time seeking office [S1]. He has lived in Snohomish County for over 30 years [S2] and teaches transition classes for service members at Naval Station Everett [S1].

## Positions
- **Spending:** his site says state spending rose from about $82 billion in the 2013-15 budget to more than $173 billion in 2025-27, "a staggering increase of more than 110%" against roughly 35-36% inflation; he "will fight to reduce wasteful spending rather than raise taxes, demand full transparency of how the government spends your money" [S3]. The pamphlet says he "will fight to cut waste, lower taxes" [S1].
- **Taxes:** "High taxes and regulations now make it hard to raise families and run businesses" [S1]; he will "work tirelessly to let you keep more of your earnings" [S3] and lists "lower taxes" among his priorities [S2].
- **Housing:** "will fight to lessen regulations that hinder affordable housing" [S3].
- **Public safety:** will "support our first responders and law enforcement, address Washington's low police-per-capita levels, and will invest in proven strategies that prevent crime" [S3].
- **Economy and education:** pro-growth policies for jobs and small businesses; "skills-based education and family-wage jobs" through college and the trades [S1][S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no statement on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found as of 2026-10-08; his education positions concern skills-based and trades education [S1][S3].
