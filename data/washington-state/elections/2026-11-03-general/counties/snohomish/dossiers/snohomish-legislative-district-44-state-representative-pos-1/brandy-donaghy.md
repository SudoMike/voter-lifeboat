## Background
Brandy Donaghy is the incumbent Democratic State Representative for the 44th District, Position 1, in office since 2021 [S1]. She is Vice Chair of the House Transportation Committee and serves on Technology, Economic Development & Veterans; Consumer Protection & Business; and Education, plus joint committees on veterans' and military affairs and civic health [S1][S4]. She is a Navy veteran (aviation ordnance), a former real estate agent and project coordinator, and holds a B.A. in business administration from UW Bothell [S1]. Her community service centers on emergency response and disaster preparedness [S1].

## Positions
- **Housing:** "committed to working with stakeholders to build the housing supply needed to make housing and homeownership achievable for all," through "a variety of equitable housing models, from transit oriented development to co-housing communities" [S2]. Voted for EHB 1217 (2025 rent and fee increase limits) [S11].
- **Taxes:** no campaign statement on taxes found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted for the 2026 Millionaires Tax and the 2025 capital gains, excise and B&O tax bills (see Record) [S7][S8][S9][S10].
- **Public safety:** her 2026 session update says "public safety is strongest when communities have access to prevention, early intervention, and the resources needed to keep people stable," and that community safety means "building systems that are proactive, compassionate, and effective"; it cites funding for behavioral health and crisis response [S6]. She sponsored the 2024 expansion of traffic safety cameras (ESHB 2384) and the 2025 young-driver education requirement (ESHB 1878) [S3]. She supports "common-sense gun legislation" [S2].
- **Emergency preparedness:** lists the statewide resiliency program (HB 1728, 2023) among her bills and says the state, cities and individuals need resources to be resilient [S2][S3].
- **Climate:** committed to "advancing policies that reduce carbon emissions, promote renewable and clean energy sources, and protect our natural resources," without leaving workers behind [S2].
- **Childcare and healthcare:** childcare should be "sufficient, accessible, and affordable" with fair wages for workers; healthcare "should be accessible, equitable and affordable," including behavioral health [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "Reproductive health care is health care"; "Protecting gender-affirming care is also essential ... Gender affirming care is healthcare" [S2]. Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates [S15].
- **Parents and schools:** supports "improved access to quality, equitable education" [S2]; sponsored the 2026 school albuterol access bill (SHB 2360) [S3][S6]. Voted for ESHB 1296 (2025), which amended the I-2081 list of parent and guardian rights [S12][S17]. No campaign statement on parental notification or opt-outs found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (IP26-645, repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no public position on the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted for ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S7].
- **I-1 (IL26-001, parental rights):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08. Record: voted for ESHB 1296, the amendments I-1 would repeal [S12][S17].
- **I-638 (IL26-638, girls' school sports):** no statement on the initiative found as of 2026-10-08. Her site says gender identity "should not be used as a weapon to limit their human or civil rights" [S2].

## Record
- Sponsored bills listed on her campaign site include HB 2033 (2022, emergency vehicle lights), SHB 1893 (2022, EMT provisional certificates), HB 1728 (2023, resiliency office), ESHB 2384 (2024, traffic safety cameras), ESHB 1878 (2025, young driver education), SHB 1081 (2025, solicited real estate transactions) and SHB 2360 (2026, albuterol in schools) [S3].
- ESSB 6346 (tax on income over $1 million): yea on House final passage, 2026-03-09 (51-46) [S7].
- ESSB 5813 (2025, higher capital gains and estate tax rates): yea, 2025-04-23 (53-45) [S8].
- ESSB 5814 (2025, excise taxes, including sales tax on more services): yea, 2025-04-23 (50-47) [S9].
- ESHB 2081 (2025, business and occupation tax increases): yea, 2025-04-22 (50-48) [S10].
- EHB 1217 (2025, rent and fee increase limits): yea on 3rd reading 2025-03-10 (53-42) and on final passage as recommended by the conference committee 2025-04-27 (54-44); nay on concurring in the Senate amendments 2025-04-23 (48-50) [S11].
- ESHB 1296 (2025, amendments to the I-2081 parents' rights list): yea on final passage as amended by the Senate, 2025-04-24 (59-39) [S12][S17].
- PDC (data updated 2026-10-04): $93,763.68 raised and $76,594.60 spent for 2026 [S13].
