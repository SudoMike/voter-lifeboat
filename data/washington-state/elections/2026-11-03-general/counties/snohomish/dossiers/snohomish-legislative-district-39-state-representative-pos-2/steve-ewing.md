## Background
Steve Ewing (Prefers Republican Party) served on the Lake Stevens City Council from 2019 to 2025 and earlier on the Lake Stevens Planning Commission, the Snohomish Health District board and the Sno-Isle Library board [S1][S2]. He has worked for the State of Washington since 2002 as a mental health counselor, community corrections officer and, currently, Department of Corrections administrative hearings officer, and holds a BS from BYU [S1][S2]. He is running for the seat Rep. Carolyn Eslick is leaving [S2].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** pamphlet says that on the council he "passed balanced budgets, opposed higher taxes," and calls for "normal affordable taxes" [S1]. Site: "Taxes that are spent wisely and kept low," and "Olympia is charging us European-level taxes while delivering third-world services" (quoting "one observer") [S2].
- **Public safety:** "Experiments that started in Seattle with unreasonable limits on police are now being pushed statewide, and all of us are suffering from this extremism" [S2]; pamphlet says he "prioritized public safety" [S1]. Endorsed by the Washington Council of Police and Sheriffs [S1][S2].
- **Transportation:** "Public road ways that actually work"; worked for better roads on the council [S1][S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no specific statement found. His pamphlet says "people are leaving Washington because state government is getting weird" without naming policies [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** calls for "Public schools that focus on teaching," citing reading results [S2]. No statement found on parental notification, record access or opt-outs.
