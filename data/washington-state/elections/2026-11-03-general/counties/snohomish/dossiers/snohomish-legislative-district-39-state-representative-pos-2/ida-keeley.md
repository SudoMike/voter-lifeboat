## Background
Ida Keeley (Prefers Democratic Party) is a first-time candidate for the open Position 2 seat; Rep. Carolyn Eslick is not seeking reelection [S1]. She is a consultant and former juvenile court administrator, Superior Court program manager, DCYF state area administrator, crisis residential center program manager and founding member of Dawson Place child advocacy center, with a BA in sociology and criminal justice from WSU [S1][S5]. Her campaign says she has managed public programs and multimillion-dollar budgets for more than 25 years and describes her as "an Independent Democrat" [S3]. She lives in Lake Stevens [S5].

## Positions
- **Affordability and housing:** will "expand access to affordable housing and childcare, lower healthcare and prescription drug costs, and protect programs that give working families greater economic security," while stressing "responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars" [S2].
- **Public safety:** "lasting public safety requires both accountability and prevention"; supports "adequately staffed, well-trained law enforcement" and investments in behavioral health and substance-use treatment, victim services and youth intervention [S2]; "strong communities require both accountability and support" [S5].
- **Data centers and AI:** supports "a pause on new high-capacity data center development until stronger safeguards are in place to protect ratepayers, natural resources, and surrounding communities," and says Washington "needs enforceable standards and real accountability" for artificial intelligence [S2]. Says "large corporations—not local families and small businesses—should bear the costs of the infrastructure their developments require" [S2].
- **Labor and jobs:** supports workers' right to organize and bargain collectively, apprenticeships and family-wage jobs [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found.
- **Parents and schools:** will "fully fund our public schools and special education," expand career and technical education, and support early learning [S1][S2]. No statement found on parental notification, record access or opt-outs.
