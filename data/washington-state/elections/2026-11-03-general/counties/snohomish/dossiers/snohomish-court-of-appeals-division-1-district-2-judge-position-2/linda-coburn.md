## Background
Linda Coburn (Nonpartisan) has been a Court of Appeals judge since 2021, after being elected in 2020 [S1]. She was an Edmonds Municipal Court judge until 2021, a Snohomish County public defender, and a law clerk to Judge Stephen Dwyer (Court of Appeals) and Judge George Bowden (Snohomish County Superior Court) [S1]. She lists service on the Supreme Court Minority and Justice Commission, the Gender and Justice Commission Advisory Committee and the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association board, and Judicial College faculty [S1]. Before law she was a journalist at The Seattle Times; she holds a J.D., cum laude, from Seattle University, an M.S. from Ohio University and a B.A. from the University of Washington [S1].
