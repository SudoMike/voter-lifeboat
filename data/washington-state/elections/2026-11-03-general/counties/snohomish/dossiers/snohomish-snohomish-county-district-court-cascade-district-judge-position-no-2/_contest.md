## What the office does
A Snohomish County District Court judge hears misdemeanor and gross-misdemeanor criminal cases, traffic infractions, small claims, civil cases up to the district-court limit, and protection orders, and is elected by the voters of the court's electoral district to a four-year term.

## District and race dynamics
Uncontested: Judge Rachelle Francis, appointed in 2023 and retained in 2024, is the only candidate. Snohomish County District Court judges are elected by the voters of their electoral district (RCW 3.38), not county-wide. Cascade Division is one of four electoral districts (Cascade, Everett, Evergreen, South).
