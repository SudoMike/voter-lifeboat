## Background
Rachelle Francis (Nonpartisan) was appointed to the Cascade Division of the District Court in 2023 by a unanimous vote of the County Council and retained by voters in 2024 [S1]. She was a Snohomish County public defender for 22 years, with experience in criminal law and involuntary behavioral health treatment [S1]. She holds a B.A., cum laude, from the University of Washington (1996) and a J.D., cum laude, from Seattle University School of Law (2000) [S1].

## Positions
- Says she seeks a balance between compassion and accountability in criminal cases and that access to services in the community is often key to reducing recidivism [S1].
- Says she prioritizes procedural fairness and the rule of law [S1].

As a judicial candidate, no positions on social or parents-and-schools topics are researched or recorded (RESEARCH-GUIDE.md).
