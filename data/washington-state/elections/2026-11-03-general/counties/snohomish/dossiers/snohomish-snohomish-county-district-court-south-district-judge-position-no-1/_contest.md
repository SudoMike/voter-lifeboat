## What the office does
A Snohomish County District Court judge hears misdemeanor and gross-misdemeanor criminal cases, traffic infractions, small claims, civil cases up to the district-court limit, and protection orders, and is elected by the voters of the court's electoral district to a four-year term.

## District and race dynamics
Uncontested: Judge Elizabeth Fraser, a District Court judge since 2013, is the only candidate. Snohomish County District Court judges are elected by the voters of their electoral district (RCW 3.38), not county-wide. South Division is one of four electoral districts (Cascade, Everett, Evergreen, South).
