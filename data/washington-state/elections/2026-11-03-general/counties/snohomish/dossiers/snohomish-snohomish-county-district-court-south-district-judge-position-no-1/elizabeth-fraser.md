## Background
Elizabeth Fraser (Nonpartisan) has been a Snohomish County District Court judge since 2013 and has twice served as presiding judge [S1]. She was a trial attorney at the Snohomish County Public Defender Association (1988-2001) and attorney administrator at the Snohomish County Office of Public Defense (2001-2012) [S1]. She holds a J.D. from Catholic University of America (1988) and a B.A. from the University of Notre Dame (1985) [S1].
