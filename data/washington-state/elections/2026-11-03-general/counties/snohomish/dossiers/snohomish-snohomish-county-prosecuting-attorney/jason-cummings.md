## Background
Jason Cummings (Prefers Democratic Party) has been Snohomish County Prosecuting Attorney since 2023 [S1]. He was the county's Chief Civil Deputy Prosecuting Attorney (2007-2022) and a Deputy Prosecuting Attorney (1999-2007), after work as a deputy prosecutor in Kitsap (1997-1999) and King (1996) counties [S1]. He holds a B.A. in Political Science from the University of Washington (1993) and a J.D., cum laude, from Seattle University (1996) [S1].

## Positions
- Says he will prioritize the safety of every neighbor, ensure the office provides accurate and unbiased legal services, and exercise prosecutorial discretion to best serve the community [S1].
- Names his priorities as safe, livable communities; transparency; and integrity [S1].
- Says his support comes from Democrats, Republicans and nonpartisan local officials [S1].
