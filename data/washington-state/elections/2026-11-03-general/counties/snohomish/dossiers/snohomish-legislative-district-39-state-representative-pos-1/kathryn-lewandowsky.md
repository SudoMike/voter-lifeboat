## Background
Kathryn Lewandowsky (Prefers Democratic Party) is a registered nurse since 1991 (LPN from 1987) whose pamphlet lists oncology, ICU, hospice and community-hospital work [S1]. Her elected experience is in the Washington State Nurses Association: local unit chair and grievance officer at St. Joseph's Hospital in the 1990s [S1]. She is board vice-chair of Whole Washington (since 2019) and board treasurer of One Payer States (since 2019), and holds a BSN (WWU, 2017) and a master's in legal studies (Purdue Global, 2024) [S1]. She has not held public office [S1].

## Positions
- **Health care:** supports "moving Washington toward a universal, publicly accountable single-payer health care system that guarantees comprehensive care" [S2]; pamphlet calls health care a human right and calls for "real healthcare choice for all" and protecting rural hospitals [S1].
- **Housing:** "Housing is a human right — not merely a speculative investment"; supports "expanding affordable housing, protecting tenants from predatory practices" [S2]; pamphlet says housing should not be a yield-generating asset for hedge funds [S1].
- **Affordability and revenue:** supports "fair taxation, consumer protections, lower prescription drug costs" and a public bank modeled on North Dakota's [S2]. No specific tax proposal found.
- **Data centers, AI and electricity:** says public dams "shouldn't shift electricity costs from massive data centers onto families" [S1]; writes that "the continued progression of Data Centers across America must stop until there can be put in place laws that guarantee our constitutional protections," and that "the true costs be borne by the corporations and not shifted to taxpayers" [S3].
- **Environment:** "clean air, clean water, and climate stability are rights that belong to everyone"; supports accelerating the clean-energy transition and protecting forests, salmon and farmland [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement of her own found; endorsed by Washington State Stonewall Democrats [S4].
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found.
