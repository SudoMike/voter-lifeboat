## ArgumentFor
Vote YES to help preserve South Whidbey's public waterfronts, fairgrounds, and small business spaces. This six-cent levy lid lift will help the Port address growing maintenance needs, compete for grant funding, and invest in infrastructure that benefits the entire community.
The Port of South Whidbey serves approximately 16,000 residents and businesses by maintaining public beaches, the working harbor, boat launches, the Whidbey Island Fairgrounds & Events Center, and facilities that support local small businesses and economic development.
This measure would increase the Port's levy by six cents per $1,000 of assessed value, providing funding to preserve aging facilities and secure the local matching funds needed to compete for state and federal grants. Those grant dollars help stretch local tax dollars further while supporting projects that benefit the entire community.
The Port has a long history of responsible financial management, operating under balanced budgets, and successfully completing independent state audits.
A yes vote will help preserve public access, maintain important community facilities, and invest in the infrastructure South Whidbey depends on today and for years to come.
Join us in supporting this important investment in South Whidbey's future.

## ExplanatoryStatement
The Port District of South Whidbey Island Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 26-06 requesting an election to authorize an increase to the regular real property tax levy to be collected beginning in 2027. As a result of state limitations on property tax increases, the port district must seek voter approval to increase the regular property tax levy. The additional tax revenue is necessary for renovation and modernization of port facilities including at the historic fairgrounds and waterfront, and to support the port economic development activities and expansion of the small business incubator programs.

If approved by the voters, this levy would raise the regular property tax levy rate by six cents (from $0.109 to $0.169) per one thousand dollars of assessed value upon all the taxable property within the port district for collection in tax year 2027 and to increase the levy as allowed by law for each year thereafter.
