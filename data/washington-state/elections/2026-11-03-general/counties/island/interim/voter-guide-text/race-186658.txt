Statement
Growing up in Island County, Langley Police Chief Tavier Wasser has carried the lessons of transparency, honesty, and togetherness with him throughout his law enforcement career. After graduating, he joined the US Marine Corps and was one of three Marines selected for the Presidential Security detail, where he honorably protected President Barack Obama.

After returning home, he served as a sheriff’s deputy and today serves Langley as Police Chief and Disaster Response Coordinator. He also trains community members in emergency response (CERT). Tavier has the experience Island County residents want to build a strong, fair, and compassionate sheriff’s office. Tavier has a proven record of responsible public budgeting and community-focused policing.

Everyone deserves to feel safe in our town, cities, and rural areas. As Sheriff, he will increase communications with the public (PIO), support deputies (officer wellness & training), and build trust between law enforcement and the communities.

Tavier, his high school sweetheart wife, and their child are long time Whidbey Island residents. Tavier was honored as the Police Officer of the Year in 2024, employee of the month Island County 2016, and was appointed as the Washington Association of Sheriff and Police Chiefs’ Co-Chair of Public Trust Committee.
