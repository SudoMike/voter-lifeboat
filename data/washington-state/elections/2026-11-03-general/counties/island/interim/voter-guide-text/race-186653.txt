Statement

The Island County Assessor’s Office is responsible for far more than appraising property. The office administers property tax laws, manages valuation systems, maintains accurate tax rolls, oversees exemption programs, and ensures fairness and uniformity for taxpayers countywide.

During my first term as Assessor, we modernized public property tools, improved land valuation systems, strengthened appraiser training, expanded taxpayer services on Camano Island, significantly reduced appeals, established a Farm & Agriculture Advisory Committee, improved equity in assessments, and operated under budget.

As an Island County resident, homeowner, father, and licensed real estate broker, I understand the importance of fair and accurate assessments, transparent government, and public trust.

My background includes thirty years of public service and leadership, including executive management experience in county government and law enforcement. I currently hold the designation of Accredited Washington State Real Property Appraiser. I also serve on the Washington State Association of County Assessors Education and Appraisal Committees, working with assessors statewide to improve professional standards, training, and uniformity in property assessment practices.

Island County deserves experienced, steady leadership committed to fairness, professionalism, and service to all residents. I respectfully ask for your support to continue that progress as your Island County Assessor.
