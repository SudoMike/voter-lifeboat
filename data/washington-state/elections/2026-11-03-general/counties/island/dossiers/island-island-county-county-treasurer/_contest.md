## What the office does

The county treasurer receives and disburses county money, keeps the fund accounts, pays bonded debt, and invests idle funds for the county and the special districts it serves [S2]. Tony Lam describes the office as "the bank" for county departments and local districts such as school, fire, port and water districts, and notes that Island County runs an investment pool for those entities [S1]. The office also bills and collects property taxes [S3]. The treasurer is elected countywide to a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Uncontested.** Incumbent Tony Lam (Prefers Independent Party) is the only candidate [S1].
- **2022.** Lam, then chief deputy treasurer, won the open seat after Treasurer Wanda Grone retired. He beat Republican Richard MacQuarrie 57.2% to 42.7% [S3][S4]. He ran in 2022 as "Prefers Democrat Party" and in 2026 lists "Prefers Independent Party" [S1][S4].
- **Primary.** He was the only name on the Aug. 4, 2026 primary ballot and received 20,880 of 21,045 votes; the rest were write-ins [S5].
