## Background

- Tony (Anthony) Lam (Prefers Independent Party) is the Island County Treasurer, in office for four years [S1][S8].
- He lists 18 years in the treasurer's office, including six as treasury accountant and eight as chief deputy treasurer, and 10 years in private-sector accounting in Hawaii [S1].
- In 2022 the Whidbey News-Times reported that he was born in Vietnam, came to the U.S. after the Vietnam War, grew up in Hawaii and moved to Whidbey Island for a job in the treasurer's office [S3].
- **Education.** BBA in accounting, University of Hawaii [S1][S3].
- **Community service.** Volunteers at Island County events; HOA treasurer for five years [S1].
- He ran in 2022 as "Prefers Democrat Party" [S5][S6]; his 2026 filing lists "Prefers Independent Party" [S1][S8].

## Positions

What he says about the job [S1][S2]:
- The treasurer is a "key position of public trust" that receipts, disburses, invests and accounts for funds of the county and local districts, and collects taxes for them.
- The treasurer, as fiduciary, must follow the RCW and WAC rules that govern the office, and the county's investment pool "requires an experienced Investment Officer as manager."
- The treasurer "must be present daily to train, direct, and support staff."

In 2022 he said he would run the office as a hands-on boss and wanted more staff, because one or two absences strained the office [S3].
