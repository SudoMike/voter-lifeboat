## What the office does

The coroner investigates deaths that fall under the office's jurisdiction and may hold an inquest, with a jury, when a death appears unnatural, violent, the result of unlawful means, or suspicious [S2]. The Island County coroner is elected countywide to a four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

- **Uncontested.** Incumbent Shantel Porter (Prefers Independent Party) is the only candidate [S1].
- **How she got the office.** In 2022 Porter, then the deputy coroner, filed to replace Coroner Robert Bishop, who did not seek reelection [S3]. She ran unopposed that November and received 30,202 votes (98.98%) [S4].
- **Primary.** She was the only name on the Aug. 4, 2026 primary ballot and received 21,478 of 21,668 votes; the rest were write-ins [S5].
