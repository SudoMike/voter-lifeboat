## Background

- Shantel Porter (Prefers Independent Party) is the Island County Coroner and says she has held the office for four years [S1][S4].
- Before that she was the deputy coroner; she filed in 2022 to replace Coroner Robert Bishop, who did not seek reelection [S3].
- She describes a 28-year career in dentistry that began after high school [S1].
- **Education.** High school graduate (1992); certified medicolegal death investigator (2022) [S1].
- She describes herself as a lifelong Island County resident [S1].

## Positions

What she says about the job [S1][S2]:
- Families deserve "transparency, honest communication, grace, and compassion" on what is often "the most difficult day of their lives."
- She pledges to keep "advocating for truth and speaking for those who can no longer tell their own story," with "careful attention to the facts" in every case.

Her statement does not address office budget, staffing, autopsy arrangements or inquests [S1].
