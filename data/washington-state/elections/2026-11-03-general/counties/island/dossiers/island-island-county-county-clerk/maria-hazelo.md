## Background

Maria Hazelo (PDC name Maria L Hazelo) lives in Oak Harbor and is the Island County Juvenile Court Services office manager (S5, S6). The News-Times reports she has worked for the county for 20 years (S5). Her statement says she manages daily operations, records, filings, public access and financial transactions, including grants, payroll and purchasing. She is also the liaison between Superior Court and the state Administrative Office of the Courts on data integrity and user security. She previously worked as a Court Appointed Special Advocate (CASA) program assistant, volunteer coordinator, notary public and juvenile detention officer (S1). She studied computer information systems at Skagit Valley College and has taken Administrative Office of the Courts, de-escalation and CASA training (S1). She belongs to the Elks, the VFW, the Grange and the Oak Harbor Garden Club. She has been married 38 years, has three sons and three grandsons, and owns a small farm on Whidbey Island (S1, S3).

She was the first clerk candidate to file with the PDC and announced in April 2026 as an independent (S6). She filed for the ballot as a Republican, and the Island County Republican Party endorsed her (S1, S8). Her husband, Tim Hazelo, is a Republican candidate for state representative in the 10th Legislative District (S6). This dossier does not attribute his positions to her. In the August 4 primary she finished second with 7,551 votes (26.3%), 185 votes ahead of Amber Sewell (S9, S10).

## Positions

### Court records and operations
- Promises to focus on "modernizing court records systems, reducing wait times, improving public access to records while safeguarding confidentiality, and maintaining the highest standards of integrity and accountability" (S1, S2).
- Says "the transition will be seamless because I am already doing this work" (S2).
- Acknowledges the high staff turnover at the Clerk's Office. Describing a "coaching-style of leadership," she says she would make sure staff are trained in the office's procedures and policies so they feel confident in their jobs (S5).
- Proposes de-escalation training for staff and a resource list staff can draw on to answer the public's questions (S5).

### Spending and budget
- Cites her experience managing grants, payroll and purchasing (S1). She has made no specific budget proposals in the sources reviewed.
