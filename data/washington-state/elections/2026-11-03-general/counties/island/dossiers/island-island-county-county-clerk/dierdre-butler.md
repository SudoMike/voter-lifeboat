## Background

Dierdre Butler (PDC name Eileen Dierdre Butler) lives in Coupeville. She moved to Island County from Texas in 2007 and raised two sons there with her husband (S1, S3, S11). She worked in the Island County Clerk's Office from 2014 to 2020, starting as deputy clerk and advancing to chief deputy (S1, S3). The News-Times put her Clerk's Office service at seven years (S5). Since 2020 she has worked in accounts payable in the Island County Auditor's Office (S1, S3). She prefers the Democratic Party (S1).

She ran for clerk in 2022 as an unaffiliated candidate against incumbent Debra Van Pelt. On election-night first count she had 38.3% to Van Pelt's 61.7% (S6). By late April 2026 she had filed with the PDC as a Democratic candidate, with Van Pelt not seeking reelection (S7). She led the four-way August 4 primary with 9,639 votes (33.6%) (S10).

## Positions

### Court records and operations
- Promises to "modernize operations, streamline legal record-keeping processes, and reduce administrative delays for residents and the court system" (S2, S4).
- Says she will reach out to other counties to find processes that make visiting the Clerk's Office easier for the public. She also wants to spread information about what the office does (S5).
- Says that while the clerk "cannot change the law," the clerk can change how staff interact with people at the counter. She calls making both staff and the public feel supported a "balancing act" (S5).
- Says she wants every person who interacts with the office "treated with the utmost respect, patience, and compassion" (S1).

### Spending and budget
- Says she will bring her Auditor's Office background to "carefully manage the Clerk's budget" and run the office "efficiently, transparently, and responsibly on behalf of taxpayers" (S3, S4).
