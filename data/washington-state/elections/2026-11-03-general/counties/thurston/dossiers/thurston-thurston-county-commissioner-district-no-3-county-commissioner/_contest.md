## What the office does

A County Commissioner serves as the local government and legislative authority outside city limits; Thurston's five commissioners are nominated by district and elected countywide [S2].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Tye Menser (Prefers Democratic Party), commissioner for District 3 since 2019 and the board's chair, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. Although nominated by District 3 voters, the commissioner is elected by the whole county in the general (RCW 36.32.0556).
