## Background

- Thurston County Commissioner for District 3 since 2019 and current board chair [S1].
- Represents the county on the LOTT Clean Water Alliance (president), Intercity Transit Authority, Olympic Region Clean Air Agency, Board of Health and Thurston Climate Action Collaborative; statewide county representative on the Sentencing Guidelines Commission (vice-chair) and the State Building Code Council [S1].
- Criminal defense, intellectual property and family law attorney from 1997 to 2018; A.B., Harvard University; J.D., UC Berkeley [S1].

## Positions

- Housing: says he helped form the Regional Housing Council, pass a countywide Home Fund and start several permanent supportive housing projects [S1].
- Criminal justice: cites opening a County Resource Hub, filling gaps in jail diversion and relaunching the LEAD program "to get people to needed services, not incarceration"; wants a countywide regional jail with rehabilitation and re-entry programs [S1].
- Environment: says he fought to establish a county climate program, increased buffers for salmon recovery and led an effort to preserve the county's oldest forest patches [S1].
- Says the county must "continue to resist the federal effort to impose authoritarian values over our community's values" [S1].
