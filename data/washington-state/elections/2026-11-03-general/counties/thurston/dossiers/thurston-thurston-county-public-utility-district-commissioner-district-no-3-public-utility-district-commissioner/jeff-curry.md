## Background

- Jeff Curry describes himself as a local small business owner with a two-year technology degree from South Puget Sound Community College and experience "managing complex logistics" [S1].
- His listed utility-related experience is serving on the board for the Scott Lake community and its local water district; he submitted no elected experience [S1].
- Community service he lists: the Kiwanis Club, serving at the Union Gospel Mission in Seattle and Olympia, and delivering food for food banks [S1].
- The campaign website named in the pamphlet, jeff-curry.com, showed only a hosting provider's placeholder page when retrieved on 2026-10-08, with no campaign content [S2].
- **Prior campaigns.** PDC records under the same filer ID show four earlier candidacies: Thurston County Treasurer in 2022 (lost in the general), Tumwater School District director Position 1 in 2023 (lost in the general), Governor in 2024 as an independent (lost in the primary) and Tumwater School District director Position 3 in 2025 (lost in the primary) [S4]. In the 2022 treasurer race he received 42,690 votes (36.37%, "States No Party Preference") to Jeff Gadman's 74,211 (63.23%) [S5]. In the 2023 Tumwater school board race, listed on the ballot as Jeff (Cowboy) Curry, he received 3,931 votes (37.2%) to Jill Tokarczyk Adams's 6,570 (62.17%) [S6].

## Positions

- **Affordability.** Says his top priority is utility services that are "fair, reliable, and affordable," with "common-sense leadership that protects your hard-earned money"; under "Affordability First" he pledges to keep rates reasonable and to be transparent about how utility dollars are spent [S1].
- **Solar.** Proposes "Adding practical solar resources to our local grid to increase energy independence, diversify our power supply, and improve long-term sustainability" [S1]. Thurston PUD currently describes itself as a water utility that owns and operates 271 water systems; the pamphlet statement does not say how the PUD would add solar [S1][S7].
- **Water-quality app.** Proposes a "user-friendly water quality mobile app" that would let anyone in the county check water quality in their area from a phone [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statements found.
- **Parents and schools:** no statements found in this campaign; he ran for the Tumwater school board in 2023 and 2025 [S4], but no platform from those races was retrieved.
