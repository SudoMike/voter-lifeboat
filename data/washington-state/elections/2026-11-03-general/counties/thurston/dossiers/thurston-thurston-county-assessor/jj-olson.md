## Background

- JJ Olson (Prefers Democratic Party) is the Business Applications Administrator in the Thurston County Assessor's Office, managing the appraisal and tax software and overseeing calculations on more than 120,000 parcels with $66 billion in assessed value and more than $650 million in annual property taxes [S1].
- He started as a field appraiser and has worked in the office for about a decade, including as senior appraiser and appraisal analyst [S1][S2]. He has held Washington real property assessment accreditation since 2016 and says he is the only accredited appraiser in the race [S1].
- He holds a B.A. in finance and management from Saint Martin's University; he is a Kiwanis Club of Olympia member and past treasurer, a precinct committee officer, a youth and FC Olympia soccer coach, a member of the International Association of Assessing Officers and vice president of the Olympia Waldorf School board of trustees [S1]. His pamphlet statement lists no elected experience ("No information submitted") [S1]. His campaign site describes him as a Minnesota native and Saint Martin's University alumnus [S2].
- In the certified August 4 primary he received 40,570 votes (55.10%) to Lynda Nashed Zeman's 30,960 (42.05%) [S5].

## Positions

- **Assessment administration:** says he will prioritize fair, accurate and transparent appraisals and make sure every property owner is "fairly represented and heard" [S1]. Says "accuracy isn't optional" because small errors affect families, small businesses and local services [S2].
- **Technology:** says he is leading a technology transformation to make the office more accessible and transparent, and is evaluating tools to make the work "more accurate, more equitable, and more transparent" [S1][S2].
- **Communication with property owners:** says people want clear explanations of how their value was determined, "not jargon," based on years of answering calls and emails about property values [S2].
- **Taxes, budget, housing, public safety, climate:** no positions found; his site does not state positions on property-tax levels, budget proposals or staffing plans [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** no position found on parental notification, record access or curricula opt-outs. He serves on the board of trustees of the Olympia Waldorf School, a private school [S1].
