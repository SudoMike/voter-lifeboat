## Background

- Lynda Nashed Zeman (Prefers Democratic Party) was an appointed Lacey City Councilmember in 2019 [S1][S2].
- She spent three years providing executive administration in the Thurston County Assessor's Office (human resources, budgeting, financial oversight, public communication, process modernization) and now works in an executive administration role with the Chair of the Board of County Commissioners [S1][S2].
- She has served two years on the board of, and was recently elected vice president of, the West Puget Sound Chapter of the International Association of Assessing Officers and has completed IAAO coursework toward the Assessment Administration Specialist designation (USPAP, mass appraisal, assessment administration) [S1][S2]. She holds an A.A. and EMT training [S1].
- She previously co-owned Window Genie of Olympia [S2]. Community roles include past president of the Zonta Club, former Hawks Prairie Rotary board member, Thurston County Democrats precinct committee officer, former secretary of Thurston County Democratic Women, and League of Women Voters member [S1].
- In the certified August 4 primary she received 30,960 votes (42.05%) to JJ Olson's 40,570 (55.10%) [S4].

## Positions

- **Office leadership:** describes the Assessor's Office as "an executive leadership role responsible for people, budgets, systems, and fair, accurate property valuations," and says the county needs an assessor who is "present, accessible" [S1].
- **Workforce and budget:** says experienced staff are retiring, workloads are growing and county budgets are tight; says she will strengthen training and mentorship, support the transition to modern systems and "manage resources responsibly" [S1]. Her site says she would run the office with "fiscal responsibility" [S2].
- **Communication with property owners:** says property owners "deserve clear answers before frustration turns into appeals"; would make valuation information easier to understand and be visible in the community [S1].
- **Taxes, housing, public safety, climate:** no positions found.
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** no position found on parental notification, record access or curricula opt-outs.
