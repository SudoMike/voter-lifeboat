## Background

- Judge, Court of Appeals Division II since 2021 and Acting Chief Judge since 2026 [S1].
- Thurston County Superior Court judge 2012-2021, including presiding judge 2020-2021 and chief judge of Adult Drug Court for three years [S1].
- Nineteen years in civil litigation in private practice; former chair of the WSBA Judicial Recommendation Committee; past president of the Thurston County Bar Association [S1].
- J.D. with honors, University of Washington School of Law (1993) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statement)

- Says each Court of Appeals judge decides about 200 cases a year, and commits "to continuing to decide cases fairly and consistently with the law" [S1].
