## Background

- Garrett Cady (Prefers Independent Party) works in the Thurston County Clerk's Office as a proceedings specialist, and previously as judicial support specialist and records auditor; before that he was an offender court assistant at Thurston County Juvenile Court [S1]. He describes himself as a deputy clerk who has worked at the front counter and as a courtroom clerk and trains newer employees [S1][S2].
- He lists systems administration experience with the court systems Odyssey, JIS and SCOMIS, is a Health Care Authority certified peer counselor, and attended Washington State University [S1][S2].
- The Board of County Commissioners appointed him to the Treatment Sales Tax Advisory Committee (2024-2025), which recommends how to spend a sales tax dedicated to reducing criminal-justice involvement and reliance on public assistance [S1][S2].
- He is a sixth-generation Thurston County resident from Yelm [S2].
- In the certified August 4 primary he received 39,251 votes (48.72%) to Nicole Miller's 40,864 (50.72%) [S4].

## Positions

- **Restoring services after budget cuts:** says the county commissioners' "narrow 3-2 vote" on the 2026-2027 budget imposed a "disproportionate" cut that eliminated Clerk's Office positions and forced the office to close to the public [S2]. Says the office has since been re-prioritized, positions reinstated and in-person service returned, and that he will "prioritize the retention of all our in-person services" [S2].
- **Victim services:** pledges to restore and enhance the Domestic Violence Liaison Program and in-person front-counter support [S1][S2].
- **Staff:** would retain experienced staff whose institutional knowledge he calls fundamental, and says he has the majority support of Clerk's Office staff [S1][S2].
- **Political independence:** says "there is absolutely no place for politics in the Clerk's Office" and that the clerk, like the county's nonpartisan judges, must be "free from any political influence" [S1][S2].
- **Access to justice:** calls it his highest priority and cites Article I, Section 10 of the state constitution ("Justice in all cases shall be administered openly and without unnecessary delay") [S1][S2].
- **Taxes, housing, public safety, climate:** no positions found.
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found.
- **Parents and schools:** no position found on parental notification, record access or curricula opt-outs.
