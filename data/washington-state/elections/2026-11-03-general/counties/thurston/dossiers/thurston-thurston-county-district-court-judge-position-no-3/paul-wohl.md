## Background

- Thurston County District Court Commissioner for the past 12 years, presiding over criminal, civil and infraction matters [S1].
- City of Olympia Prosecutor for 14 years; earlier prosecutor for the City of Federal Way and deputy prosecutor for Pierce County; public defender in King County [S1].
- Instructor and selected Associate Dean at the Washington State Judicial College; served on the Washington Supreme Court Public Trust and Confidence Committee [S1].
- J.D., Seattle University School of Law; B.A., University of Washington [S1].
