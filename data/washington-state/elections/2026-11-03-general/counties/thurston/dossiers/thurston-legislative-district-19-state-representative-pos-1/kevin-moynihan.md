## Background
Kevin Moynihan (D-Aberdeen) is a member of the Aberdeen City Council [S1][S3][S5]. He describes himself as a working line cook and former HVAC tradesman, and says he is attending The Evergreen State College for a master's in public policy and administration and a GIS certificate [S1]. The Chinook Observer reports he has worked as a journeyman HVAC technician and as a chef and cook [S6]. He is the only candidate who filed against Walsh [S6].

In the August 4 primary he received 19,272 votes (44.35%) to Walsh's 24,113 (55.5%) [S7].

## Positions
- **Affordability and the economy:** says he will "crack down on unfair price hikes," lower prescription drug costs, expand affordable childcare and "build more housing" [S4][S5]. He calls for investment in port infrastructure, apprenticeships, sustainable forestry and fishing jobs, and new manufacturing and clean energy jobs [S4][S5].
- **Housing:** pledges to "pass statewide rent stabilization" [S1], and his community-service listing names "rent stabilization initiatives" [S1].
- **Healthcare:** pledges to "protect rural healthcare infrastructure" [S1] and to lower prescription drug costs [S4].
- **Labor:** pledges to "expand local union apprenticeships to keep family-wage jobs in our communities" [S1].
- **Money in politics:** says "I refuse corporate PAC money" [S1]. His site says government should work for people, "not powerful interests or political insiders," and pledges to "stand up to corporate power" [S2][S3].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no position found in the sources reviewed. He criticizes "online culture wars" and "culture-war politics" without stating positions on those issues [S1][S2].
- **Parents and schools:** calls for more funding for rural and coastal schools, competitive educator pay, career and technical education pathways and modern facilities [S4][S5]; says education policy should focus on "students, teachers, and opportunity instead of political distractions" [S4]. No position on parental notification, record access or curricula opt-outs found.
- **Contrast with the incumbent:** argues that Walsh, as state Republican Party chair, has prioritized "partisan politics and national culture-war battles" over local results [S4][S5].
- **Other:** lists himself as an ally of the Chinook Indian Nation's effort to win state and federal recognition [S1].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08. (The Washington State Labor Council, which endorses him, recommends a no vote on IP26-645 [S9]; that is the endorser's position, not his.)
- **I-1 (parental rights):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no public position found as of 2026-10-08. (The WSLC recommends a no vote on IL26-638 [S9].)

## Record
- Serves on the Aberdeen City Council, where his site says he has focused on infrastructure, economic development and responsive city government [S3][S5]. No specific council votes were found in the sources reviewed.
