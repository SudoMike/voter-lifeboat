## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House for a two-year term [S1]. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The November 3, 2026 general election decides the seat between the two candidates who advanced from the August primary.

## Race dynamics
The 19th District covers Pacific and Wahkiakum counties and parts of Grays Harbor, Lewis, Cowlitz and Thurston counties [S2]. Rep. Jim Walsh (R-Aberdeen), first elected in 2016 and state Republican Party chair since 2023, is seeking a sixth term; his only challenger is Aberdeen City Councilmember Kevin Moynihan (D) [S2]. In the primary Walsh took 55.5% (24,113 votes) to Moynihan's 44.35% (19,272) [S3][S4].

Walsh runs on his record opposing the 2026 income tax and on state mandates he calls unlawful, and he filed the 2023 parents' bill of rights and income-tax initiatives (jim-walsh.md S1, S22, S26). Moynihan, a line cook and former HVAC tradesman endorsed by the state labor council, runs on affordability, statewide rent stabilization, rural healthcare and refusing corporate PAC money, and argues Walsh has put national partisan fights ahead of the district (kevin-moynihan.md S1, S4, S9). Walsh's party has backed I-645 and he appeared at the rally for the parental-rights and girls' sports initiatives; Moynihan has not stated positions on the three statewide initiatives (jim-walsh.md S23, S25).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Walsh voted against ESSB 6346 and supports its repeal; Moynihan has no stated tax position beyond affordability themes.
2. **Housing:** Moynihan pledges statewide rent stabilization; Walsh voted against the 2025 rent-increase limits in EHB 1217.
3. **Record vs. renewal:** five-term incumbent and party chair versus a city councilmember without legislative service.
4. **Parents and schools:** Walsh filed the 2023 parents' bill of rights initiative and voted against the 2025 rollback; Moynihan focuses on school funding and has no stated position on notification or opt-outs.
5. **Money in politics:** Moynihan refuses corporate PAC money; Walsh chairs the state Republican Party.
