## Background
Beth Doglio has represented the 22nd Legislative District (Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater) in the State House for eight years [S1]. Before the Legislature she was a senior advisor at Climate Solutions, political director of Pro-Choice Washington, development director of Audubon Washington and founding executive director of Washington Conservation Voters [S1]. She holds a BA from Indiana University and lists board service with New Horizon Communities and the South Sound Y and past presidency of the Roosevelt PTA [S1]. She chairs the House Environment & Energy Committee and sits on Appropriations and State Government & Tribal Relations [S2][S4].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** voted for ESSB 6346, the 2026 9.9% tax on income over $1 million, on final passage on March 9, 2026, and against Republican amendments to it [S5]. Voted for ESSB 5813 (2025 capital gains and estate tax rate increases) [S7] and for ESHB 2081 (2025 business and occupation tax surcharges, including a temporary surcharge on large companies) [S8]. Is prime sponsor of HB 1785, which would impose a surcharge on publicly traded companies with "excessive executive compensation" [S9].
- **Spending and budget:** says she worked so the state "didn't balance our state budget on the backs of public employees" and to "continue to invest" in people and communities [S2]; voted for the 2025-27 operating budget (ESSB 5167) [S18]. Her statement pledges to advocate for state employees, education funding and "a strong social safety net" [S1].
- **Housing:** says she has provided "additional dollars to house our most vulnerable" [S1]; voted for EHB 1217 (2025 rent stabilization) on final passage of the conference report [S10]. Endorsed by the Washington Housing Alliance Action Fund [S3].
- **Climate and energy:** cites passing climate legislation and the first-in-the-nation Clean Buildings standards [S1][S2]; voted for 2SHB 1409 (2025 clean fuels program changes) [S11]. Is prime sponsor of HB 2515 on "emerging large energy use facilities" (data centers), which passed the House 51-41 in 2026 with her yes vote [S12][S13]. Lists 2024 Legislator of the Year from Washington Conservation Action [S2].
- **Public safety and guns:** pledges to advocate for "responsible gun laws" and is endorsed by the Alliance for Gun Responsibility [S1][S3]; is prime sponsor of HB 1152 on secure storage of firearms in vehicles and residences [S19]. Lists a 2018 Legislator of the Year award from the Washington Council of Police & Sheriffs and is endorsed by Olympia Firefighters Local 468 [S2][S3].
- **Immigration:** voted for 2SHB 2105 (immigrant worker protections, chapter 240, Laws of 2026) and SSB 5855 (limits on face coverings by law enforcement officers, chapter 115, Laws of 2026) [S14][S15].
- **Tech:** HB 2515 (above) addresses large energy-use facilities such as data centers [S12].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** former political director of Pro-Choice Washington, endorsed by the Planned Parenthood Alliance [S1]; also endorsed by the LGBTQ+ Victory Fund and WA Stonewall Dems [S3]. Is listed as an elected official opposing I-638 [S16].
- **Parents and schools:** voted for ESHB 1296 (2025 amendments to the parental-rights law) on House passage and on final passage as amended by the Senate, and against Republican floor amendments to it [S6]. Is listed as opposing I-1 [S16]. Cites being a former PTA president [S1].
- **Election administration:** says she advanced legislation to keep elections "fair, accessible, and trustworthy" [S2]; endorsed by FairVote Washington [S3].

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no public statement found as of 2026-10-08; she voted for ESSB 6346, the law I-645 would repeal [S5].
- **I-1 (parental rights):** opposes; listed among elected officials opposing I-1 and I-638 by the NO HATE in WA State campaign [S16].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** opposes, per the same list [S16].

## Record
- ESSB 6346 (2026 income tax over $1M): yes on final passage, March 9, 2026 (51-46) [S5].
- ESHB 1296 (2025 parental-rights amendments): yes on third reading (56-37) and on final passage as amended by the Senate (59-39) [S6].
- ESSB 5813 (2025 capital gains/estate tax): yes (53-45) [S7]. ESHB 2081 (2025 B&O surcharges): yes (50-48) [S8].
- EHB 1217 (2025 rent stabilization): yes on final passage as recommended by the conference committee (54-44) [S10].
- 2SHB 1409 (clean fuels): yes on final passage (54-43) [S11].
- 2SHB 2105 and SSB 5855 (2026 immigration-related bills): yes [S14][S15].
- Prime sponsor: HB 2515 (large energy use facilities), HB 1785 (executive-compensation surcharge), HB 1152 (firearm storage) [S9][S12][S19]. Her campaign site lists Clean Buildings standards, the Washington State Women's Commission, workplace protections for domestic-violence survivors, prevailing-wage changes and part-time state worker bargaining among bills she passed or led [S2].
