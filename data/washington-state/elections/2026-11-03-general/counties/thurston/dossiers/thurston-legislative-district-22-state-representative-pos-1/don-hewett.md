## Background
Don Hewett states he "is not a professional politician" and lists no elected experience [S1]. He served as a police officer in the Air Force 96th Bomb Wing and later worked as an executive engineer leading engineering teams [S1]. He lists bachelor's degrees in mathematics, computer science and electrical engineering from the University of Washington [S1]. His listed community service is renovating homes for single mothers, supporting local charities, working at a food bank and volunteering at schools [S1].
