## What the office does
The county clerk is ex officio clerk of the Wahkiakum County Superior Court: the office attends court sessions to keep an independent record, issues court orders such as warrants and letters testamentary, collects and disburses restitution, child support and other court money, and manages Superior Court juries [S3]. Four-year term [S1].
