## What the office does
The treasurer acts as the bank for the county and its school, port, cemetery, fire, diking and other districts: collecting property taxes and real estate excise tax, investing and disbursing funds, honoring warrants, accounting for district debt and foreclosing on tax-delinquent property [S3]. Four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested open seat. Treasurer Tammy Peterson, elected in 2022 [S4], did not file; Chief Deputy Treasurer Marissa Longtain (Prefers Independent Party) is the only candidate [S1][S3].
