## What the office does
The auditor keeps the county's official financial records (warrant registers, payroll, the county budget and its supplements), audits every bill before the commissioners approve it, runs elections, serves as the Department of Licensing agent for vehicle titles and registrations, issues marriage licenses and records deeds and other documents [S3]. Four-year term [S1].
