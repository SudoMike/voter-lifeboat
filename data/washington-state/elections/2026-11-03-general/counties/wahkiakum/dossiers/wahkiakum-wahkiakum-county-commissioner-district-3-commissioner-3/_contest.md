## What the office does
The three county commissioners are the county's legislative and executive authority: they adopt the budget, set policy, approve contracts and appoint boards. A commissioner is nominated by the voters of the district [S8] and elected by the whole county [S9]: the 2022 District 3 primary ran in four precincts (Deep River, Grays River, Rosburg/Altoona, Skamokawa), the 2022 general on all eleven [S7][S6]. This election fills the rest of the current term and the next four-year term ("Short & Full") [S1].
