## What the office does
The district court judge hears misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor criminal cases, traffic and Fish and Wildlife citations, civil and small claims cases, anti-harassment orders and name changes, and oversees probation; the county has one district court judge, elected county-wide [S3][S5]. Four-year term [S1].
