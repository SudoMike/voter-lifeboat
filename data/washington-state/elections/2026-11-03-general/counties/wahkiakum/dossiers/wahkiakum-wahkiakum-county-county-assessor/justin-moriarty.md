## Background

Justin (Justin R.) Moriarty, of Cathlamet, lists "Prefers Independent Party" on the ballot [S1][S7]. The Department of Revenue lists him as an active accredited appraiser (number 1956) since October 21, 2024, accreditation valid to October 21, 2026 [S2]. In May 2025 he described himself as "a certified residential appraiser" and "someone working inside the Assessor's Office today" [S3]; the Assessor's page on 2026-10-08 lists the assessor, a chief deputy and one residential appraiser, and does not list him [S8].

He ran for the office in 2025 and lost to Drew Jenkins, 813 votes (42.52%) to 1,096 [S5]. In the August 2026 primary he received 619 votes to Jenkins's 1,052 [S6].

## Positions

All from his 2025 campaign letters; no 2026 statement was found (Wahkiakum's online voters' guide carries no county races, and the Eagle has run no 2026 candidate questionnaire as of 2026-10-08).
