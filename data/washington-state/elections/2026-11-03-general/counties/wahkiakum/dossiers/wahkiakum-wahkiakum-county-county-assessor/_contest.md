## What the office does

The assessor determines the value of all taxable real and personal property in the county so that property-tax liabilities are distributed equitably among owners in each taxing district; state law requires real property to be appraised every year at 100 percent of market value [S2]. The 2025 levy-rate sheet says the office revalues the whole county each year and physically inspects 15 to 25 percent of it [S6]. Four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics

A rematch. Drew Jenkins won a 2025 election for the rest of an early-vacated term, 1,096 votes (57.32%) to Justin Moriarty's 813 (42.52%) [S3][S5]; the 2025 rate sheet still named Bill Coons as assessor [S6]. In the August 2026 primary Jenkins led 1,052 to 619 [S4]. Both list "Prefers Independent Party" [S1].
