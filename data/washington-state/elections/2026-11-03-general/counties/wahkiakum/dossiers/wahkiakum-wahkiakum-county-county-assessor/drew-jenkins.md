## Background

Drew (Andrew M.) Jenkins, of Cathlamet, is the Wahkiakum County Assessor and lists "Prefers Independent Party" on the ballot [S1][S2][S7]. He won the 2025 election to fill an early vacancy in the office, 1,096 votes (57.32%) to 813 for Justin Moriarty, and is now running for a full four-year term [S3][S5].

He worked in the Assessor's Office from 2016 to 2023, first as a residential appraiser and then as a commercial and residential appraiser, and before that was a residential appraiser in Chicago for 13 years [S3]. He also reports 28 years with United Airlines as a flight attendant and in management, where he developed training programs [S3]. The Department of Revenue lists him as an active accredited appraiser (number 1621) since October 9, 2018, accreditation valid to September 24, 2027 [S4].

## Positions
