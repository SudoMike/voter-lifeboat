## Background

John (John M.) Mason, of Cathlamet, states no party preference on the ballot [S2][S1]. He has worked in Wahkiakum County law enforcement since 2013 and was a patrol sergeant [S4]. The commissioners appointed him sheriff on July 2, 2024, on the retirement of Sheriff Mark Howie, who had mentored him for the job [S5]. In 2025 he beat Josh Grasseth in the primary, 1,137 to 202, and in the general, 1,734 (87.44%) to 246 [S6][S7]; he ran unopposed in the August 2026 primary (1,534 votes) [S8].

## Positions

- Says he is committed to "countywide Law Enforcement, Public Safety, 911 Dispatch, and Emergency Management" while "maintaining a conservative budget within our current means" [S4].
- Says he will "continue to promote" officer wellness and resiliency, given rural minimal staffing [S4].
- Says he believes in "the Rule of Law" and that "community partnerships and transparency are key" [S4].
