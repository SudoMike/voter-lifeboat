### Statement
Biography:

Commissioner Catlin is a lifelong resident of Kittitas County. He worked on the family farm until graduation from Ellensburg High School in 1973 when he started working in the logging industry. He went back to school in 1981 and became a journeyman electrician. In 1984, he and his wife Tara started and ran their own electrical contracting business. In 2016 they sold the business to their sons, but Rick continues to work for them part time because he loves his work. Rick and Tara have four children and seven grandchildren - most of them living here in Kittitas County.
