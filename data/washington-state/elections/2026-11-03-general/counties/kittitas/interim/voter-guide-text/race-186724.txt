### Statement
Biography:

Bryan
Elliott is a lifelong Kittitas County resident, U.S. Army Veteran, and your
County Auditor. He served five years on active duty, including a Middle East
deployment, and continues his military service as an Operations Officer in the
Washington Army National Guard. Before his election, Bryan honed his experience
in the private sector as a business consultant, specializing in streamlining
operations and financial oversight for retail organizations. Bryan and his
wife, Sierra, are raising their two sons in Ellensburg,
where he also serves on the Conference of Governments, Clymer Museum Board, and
CWU Foundation Board. 

Statement

Bryan Elliott believes citizens should expect results from
their elected officials. Since taking office, he has positioned himself as a
modernizer, working across departments and offices to elevate county services.

Bryan brought enhanced transparency to elections by
installing 24/7 live-view cameras in the Election Center. He secured strategic
grant funding to harden physical and digital infrastructure, protecting your
vote without asking local taxpayers for an extra dime.

Bryan and his staff have led the county through three consecutive
years of timely audits with $0 in questioned costs. He successfully overhauled
the county’s financial software, on time and on budget, and implemented credit
card processing in the Recording Services division, giving thousands of residents
convenient service.

As state mandates grow more complex and budgets tighten,
Bryan is postured to adapt. He will continue to invest in election security
while leveraging improved financial technology to give decision-makers the
clarity needed to face budget challenges head-on.

Experience and results matter. Vote to re-elect Bryan
Elliott for County Auditor.
