### Statement
Biography:

Cori grew up on a small Ellensburg farm, where hard
work and helping neighbors shaped her values. She still honors those roots by
leading youth 4-H, volunteering at Fair, and staying active in Ellensburg Noon
Rotary. After high school, she began 35+ years of service in
Kittitas County. She and her husband, Michael, later welcomed their son, Tommy.
Recruited by Coroner Nick Henderson, she moved from KITTCOM to the Coroner’s
Office in 2019. Cori learned the work from the ground up- earning
certifications, supporting families, managing operations, training staff,
securing grants, and helping the office achieve accreditation before becoming
Coroner

Statement

Cori McKean stepped into the role of Coroner after the
passing of Coroner Nicholas Henderson — an honor and responsibility she
approached with deep respect. Known for her steady leadership and compassion,
she serves as the final guardian of dignity, accuracy, and truth during
families’ most difficult moments. Though much of a coroner’s work is unseen,
its impact is lasting: offering clarity, protecting the truth, and guiding
loved ones through profound loss.

Throughout her years in the office, Cori has worked
alongside dedicated colleagues through complex and emotional situations. What
stays with her most is not the technical work, but the people — families
seeking answers, communities needing reassurance, and the quiet moments when
compassion mattered most.

Her commitments reflect those values:

Thorough, respectful work

Support for families

Continuous learning

Transparency and trust

Every day, Cori strives to serve with professionalism,
integrity, and humanity. Every life deserves respect. Every family deserves
care. And every interaction is an opportunity to show compassion at its best.

### Statement
Biography:

My name is Charlie Divine, and I am honored to be a candidate for Coroner in Kittitas County. I currently serve as a Deputy Coroner and I am a WA State Certified Medicolegal Forensic Investigator. For the past four years, I have proudly served in the Coroner’s Office.  I have had the privilege of assisting families throughout our county, state, and country during some of the most difficult moments of their lives.  I am currently in the process of attaining my D-ABMDI Certification and I have also been a Nationally Certified Anesthesia Technician and a Nationally Certified EMT.

Statement

I have worked with grieving families, attended training seminars, and mentored interns entering the field of medicolegal death investigation. These experiences have strengthened both my understanding of the responsibilities of this office and my dedication to the people we serve. With more than 25 years of experience in healthcare, I have developed a strong foundation built on compassion, empathy, and trust. My career includes serving as a Nationally Certified Anesthesia Technician at Harborview Medical Center and University of Washington Medical Center, where I worked as a key member of the anesthesia team in high-pressure medical environments that demanded professionalism, attention to detail, and teamwork. I lead with integrity, treating others with respect, and remaining calm and compassionate during difficult situations. I believe these qualities are essential for building a collaborative and unified Coroner’s Office that serves the community with dignity and professionalism.  If elected Coroner, my priorities will be to modernize investigations, improve the accountability of the office, and rebuild a team-centered culture. I believe that through training, thorough investigations, and transparency, we can restore confidence in the office.  Thank you for considering my candidacy. It would be an honor to serve the people of Kittitas County as your Coroner.
