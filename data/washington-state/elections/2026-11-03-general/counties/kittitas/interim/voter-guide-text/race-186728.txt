Biography:

Nine years Chief Criminal Deputy Prosecuting Attorney; no prior elected offices held or sought.  Washington Association of Prosecuting Attorneys - Appellate Committee, Training Committee, and regular instructor at state-wide prosecutor trainings; Central Washington University - adjunct law professor; Tutor/Mentor for law clerk (professional legal apprenticeship). Bachelor’s of Arts, English; Bachelor’s of Arts, Law & Justice (2002 CWU, Cum Laude); Juris Doctorate (2005 Arizona State University, Cum Laude). Ellensburg Morning Rotary; Ellensburg School District volunteer - classrooms, athletics, and theater; Valley Theater Company volunteer; Bright Beginnings (Head Start) former board member; Wellspring Christian Fellowship former board member; State Mock Trial judge

Statement

Experienced; trusted; proven in the courtroom - Jodi Hammond is the advocate Kittitas County deserves. Jodi has dedicated her career to protecting the people of Kittitas County and standing up for victims of crime. With more than 20 years of prosecutorial experience, Jodi has handled thousands of cases ranging from misdemeanors to serious violent felonies, including homicide and sexual assault cases. She has earned the respect of law enforcement, colleagues, and victims throughout the county.  She is a thoughtful, committed, and experienced attorney with exceptional legal abilities.

As Chief Criminal Deputy Prosecutor, Jodi leads with integrity, professionalism, and compassion. Her experience extends beyond the courtroom through more than nine years serving on the office management team: working closely with the civil division, participating in leadership and legal advisory meetings, and spearheading changes within the office to improve productivity and efficiency.

Jodi is endorsed by Greg Zempel, the current elected prosecutor, and supported by members of law enforcement because they trust her judgment, experience, and commitment to public safety. She is also a dedicated wife, mother, mentor, and community member who understands the importance of fairness, accountability, and service.

Jodi Hammond will continue protecting Kittitas County with excellence, leadership, and integrity.

Statement

I am running as an Independent for Prosecutor because this
critical position demands fair, effective, and accountable justice that
protects our families, supports victims, and strengthens our communities, free
from partisan politics.

I was born and raised in Washington and have called
Ellensburg home since 2010. I bring 25 years of prosecutorial experience,
having handled thousands of cases, and a rare perspective as a current Public
Defender has sharpened my ability to pursue just outcomes effectively. 

The Elected Prosecutor leads an office of attorneys and
staff, whose work impacts every family in the county. My military service
taught me that the best leaders develop their people. I will bring that same
commitment to the Prosecutor’s office by mentoring and training talented
prosecutors, investing in staff, and building a team that delivers the highest
quality public service.

As your Prosecutor, I will aggressively prosecute violent
and repeat offenders; support victims and strengthen partnerships with law
enforcement; balance accountability with smart prevention, diversion, and
rehabilitation programs to reduce recidivism; and prioritize staff development,
fiscal responsibility, and transparent operations.

I respectfully ask for your vote and am committed to serving
Kittitas County with integrity and proven leadership, ensuring this office work
for everyone.
