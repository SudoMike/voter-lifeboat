### Statement
Biography:

Ben Kokjer is the Chief Deputy of Operations for the Kittitas County Sheriff’s Office and a candidate for Kittitas County Sheriff. A Central Washington University Law & Justice graduate, he began serving as a reserve officer with the Ellensburg Police Department in 1999 and has worked for our Sheriff’s Office for 21 years. Ben has served in patrol, investigations, specialty assignments, and now, since 2019, leads the day-to-day operations and countywide response. Ben and his wife, Tonieka, a public-school teacher, have raised three children in Kittitas County.

Statement

Public safety starts with trust, and trust must be earned every day. I’m running for Kittitas County Sheriff to deliver professional law enforcement service, strong investigations, and consistent leadership as our community’s needs evolve.
As Chief Deputy of Operations, I lead countywide patrol response, investigations, training, and supervision. As Sheriff, I will maintain reliable public safety response so when you call for help, a well trained, well equipped deputy shows up, wherever in the county you live. I will continue to work on practical strategies to mitigate traffic and other impacts of tourism and travel in and through our county. I will focus on effective crime fighting and thorough investigations, with timely follow up for victims and strong casework for prosecutors. I will hold offenders accountable while protecting constitutional rights and professional standards that sustain community trust. I will continue modern accountability tools, including body worn cameras and sound evidence practices.  I will strengthen readiness for major incidents by improving coordination with local partners and continuing to advance Upper County emergency capability, including progress toward an Emergency Operations Center and a fully equipped West County Precinct.
Build on our History. Lead for the future. Vote for Ben.
