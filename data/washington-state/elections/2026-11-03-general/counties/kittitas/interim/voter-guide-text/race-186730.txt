Statement

I am honored to serve as your County Treasurer, having been elected in 2018.
Serving the people of Kittitas County has been a great honor. Over the past eight years, I have worked to protect public funds, maintain strong investment practices, and ensure property taxes are collected not only accurately, but efficiently. During my time in office, we have modernized procedures, strengthened internal controls, and remained careful stewards of your tax dollars.
I care deeply about doing this job well, and I am proud of the dedicated team I work alongside. Together, we are committed to providing responsive service to you, the taxpayers, and maintaining financial stability for our local taxing districts.
I am proud of my office's work and remain committed to managing public funds with integrity, transparency, and professionalism. I am running for one simple reason: I love serving the people of this county. Kittitas County has been my home since 1995. I know my experience, leadership, and commitment will continue to make a positive difference. Vote Amy Cziske!
