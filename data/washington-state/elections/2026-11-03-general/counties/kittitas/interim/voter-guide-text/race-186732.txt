### Statement
Biography:

I was born and raised in the Upper Kittitas County.  This amazing place has always been home and now my wife, who is also born and raised here, and I have the privilege of raising our kids here.  After graduating from Cle Elum High School, I earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Washington and my law degree from Thomas M. Cooley Law School.  I spent 22 years as a deputy prosecuting attorney in several locations around the state and have spent the last four years as the Upper Kittitas County District Court Judge.

Statement

It has been my greatest honor to serve as your Upper Kittitas County Judge for the last 4 years and I am excided about the opportunity to continue in this position.  Just like our community, our court has seen a lot of changes over the last few years, including personnel, procedures, and new computer systems to name just a few.  While changes like these are not always easy, I am dedicated to facing them with openness and excitement while being determined not to let them change what this court is at its core, which is a fair and impartial judiciary that treats all people equally.  I look forward to continuing my role as a public servant to the citizens of this great community.
