## Background

Charlie Divine is a deputy coroner in the Kittitas County Coroner's Office, where he has worked since 2022, hired by the late Coroner Nick Henderson [S1][S3]; his campaign home page calls him "a recent Deputy Coroner" [S2]. He is a Washington State certified medicolegal forensic death investigator pursuing national (D-ABMDI) certification [S1][S2]. Before that he worked about 20 years as a nationally certified anesthesia technician at Harborview Medical Center and UW Medical Center, and earlier as a nationally registered EMT [S1][S3]. He states no party preference [S1] and runs as an independent [S4].

In the August 4 primary he received 4,024 votes (35.1%) to Cori McKean's 7,393 (64.5%) [S5].

## Positions

### Office direction
- If elected, his priorities would be to "modernize investigations, improve the accountability of the office, and rebuild a team-centered culture," saying that through training, thorough investigations and transparency "we can restore confidence in the office" [S1].
- Listed priorities: accurate investigations using modern forensic practice and documentation; transparency and accountability; support for families; collaboration with first responders; and updated training, equipment and procedures [S2].
