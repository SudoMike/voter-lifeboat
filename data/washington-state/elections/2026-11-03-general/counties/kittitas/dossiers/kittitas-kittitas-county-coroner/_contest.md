## What the office does
The coroner's office conducts death investigations and the related public-record reporting for Kittitas County [S2]; the coroner works with grieving families, law enforcement and medical partners [S1].

## Race dynamics
The ballot elects a coroner for both the short term (the rest of 2026) and the full four-year term [S1]. Nick Henderson, the county's first elected coroner, died in October 2025; Chief Deputy Coroner Cori McKean (prefers Republican) was named acting coroner and then appointed [S2]. Deputy Coroner Charlie Divine (states no party preference) runs on modernizing investigations, accountability and rebuilding team culture to "restore confidence" in the office [S1]. In spring 2026 the four deputy coroners raised concerns about McKean's leadership and one filed a complaint about a hire [S4]; an outside investigation found no wrongdoing [S5]. McKean led the primary 7,393 (64.5%) to 4,024 (35.1%) [S3].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience:** the appointed incumbent and long-time chief deputy versus a deputy coroner since 2022.
2. **Reform:** continuity ("retain") versus restoring accountability and office culture.
3. **Spending:** McKean proposes a county morgue; Divine wants updated training and equipment.
