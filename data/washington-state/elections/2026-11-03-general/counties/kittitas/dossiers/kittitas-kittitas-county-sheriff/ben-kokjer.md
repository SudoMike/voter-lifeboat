## Background

Ben Kokjer is Chief Deputy of Operations at the Kittitas County Sheriff's Office, which he joined in 2005 [S1][S5]. A CWU Law and Justice graduate, he was an Ellensburg Police reserve officer (1999-2001), a ranger at Lake Easton State Park and a Langley police officer from 2003 [S2][S5][S6]. At the sheriff's office he has been a patrol deputy, firearms instructor, field training officer, tactical team leader, Major Crimes Unit deputy, corporal and sergeant; he was Deputy of the Year in 2012 and was named operations commander in July 2019 [S2][S6]. From 2020 he has run day-to-day patrol, major crimes, internal investigations, search and rescue, the marine and dive units and EOC operations [S7]. He is running for the seat of retiring Sheriff Clay Myers [S5]. He prefers the Republican Party [S1].

In the August 4 primary he received 9,012 votes (77.8%) to Darryl Chepoda Jr.'s 2,510 (21.7%) [S8].

## Positions

### Public safety
- Promises reliable response "so when you call for help, a well trained, well equipped deputy shows up, wherever in the county you live," with staffing, training and deployment aimed at rural coverage [S1][S3].
- "I will hold offenders accountable while protecting constitutional rights and professional standards"; promises effective crime fighting, thorough investigations and strong casework for prosecutors [S1][S3].
- Would keep "modern accountability tools, including body worn cameras" [S1].
- Would work on traffic and other effects of tourism and travel through the county, and on major-incident readiness: an Upper County Emergency Operations Center and "a fully equipped West County Precinct" [S1].
