## What the office does
The sheriff runs county law enforcement: patrol and response across the county, criminal investigations, search and rescue, the jail and courthouse security, and county emergency management [S3].

## Race dynamics
Open seat: Sheriff Clay Myers is not running [S2]. Chief Deputy of Operations Ben Kokjer (prefers Republican), 21 years in the office, runs on reliable response, investigations and accountability [S1]. Darryl Chepoda Jr. (prefers Independent), an Upper County rancher and retired fire chief with no stated law enforcement experience, runs on transparency, constitutional rights, community engagement and wildfire evacuation [S1]. Kokjer led the primary 9,012 (77.8%) to 2,510 (21.7%) [S4].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience:** a career deputy and current chief deputy versus an outsider to law enforcement.
2. **Reform:** continuity versus a general call for the office to "better serve" through transparency and community engagement.
3. **Safety:** only Kokjer states an enforcement approach; Chepoda's statement does not address enforcement versus prevention.
4. Positions on social policy, parents-and-schools or the 2026 statewide initiatives: none found for either candidate.
