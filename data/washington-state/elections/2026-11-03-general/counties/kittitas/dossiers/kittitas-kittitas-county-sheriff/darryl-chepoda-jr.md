## Background

Darryl Chepoda Jr. describes himself as a fifth-generation Upper County resident who ranches and farms there; his statement lists federal and civil service consulting and contracting, work as a retired fire chief, a bush contractor and a "black hat miner" [S1]. His statement lists no law enforcement experience [S1]. He filed for sheriff on the first day of Filing Week as an independent [S2] and prefers the Independent Party on the ballot [S1]. His pamphlet entry gives no contact information [S1].

In the August 4 primary he received 2,510 votes (21.7%) to Ben Kokjer's 9,012 (77.8%) [S3].

## Positions

### Sheriff's office
- Names "full and open transparency, constitutional rights and community engagement" as the major issues he wants to address, saying the office "could better serve us" [S1].
- Says wildfire evacuation needs renewed focus [S1].
- Would look into an opt-in, app-based program for community information and outreach [S1].
