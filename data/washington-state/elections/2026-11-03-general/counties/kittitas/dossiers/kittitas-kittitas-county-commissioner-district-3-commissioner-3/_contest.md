## What the office does
The three commissioners are the county's governing board; commissioners are nominated by district and elected by the voters of the whole county in the general election [S6]; the District 3 race appeared on every county precinct in 2022 [S4].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Commissioner Brett Wachsmith, in office since 2019, is the only candidate. Nominated in District 3, the seat is elected county-wide. [S1][S5][S6]
