## Background

- Brett Wachsmith has been a Kittitas County Commissioner since 2019; he was county treasurer from 2015 to 2018, an Army infantry veteran (2002-2008) and a staff accountant (2007-2014), and was MRSC board president in 2025 [S1]. He prefers the GOP [S1].
- His statement cites work on mental-health resources, developmental-disability support and veterans' services [S1].
- He was unopposed in 2022 (13,804 votes) [S4] and in the district-only August 4 primary (1,643 votes) [S3], and is unopposed now [S5].
