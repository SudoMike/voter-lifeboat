## Background

- Amy Cziske has been Kittitas County Treasurer since 2019 (elected 2018) and worked in the office from 2015; she is seeking a third term [S1][S3]. She prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Before county government she spent 14 years in marketing and public relations for Solarity Credit Union and the Washington Cattlemen's Association [S3]. She has held officer posts in the Washington Association of County Officials and was vice president of the Washington State Association of County Treasurers [S1].
- Her statement cites modernized procedures, stronger internal controls and accurate, efficient property tax collection [S1]. She was unopposed in the primary (8,415 votes) [S4].
