## What the office does
The Court of Appeals hears appeals from superior courts. Division III has five judges; District 3 (Chelan, Douglas, Kittitas, Klickitat and Yakima counties) elects two of them [S3]. Every voter in those counties votes on the seat, so in Kittitas County it is a county-wide contest [S1]. The previous holder, Rebecca Pennell, was unopposed in 2020 [S4].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Judge Megan K. Murphy, appointed to the court in January 2025, is the only candidate. [S1]
