## Background

- Megan K. Murphy has been a judge of the Court of Appeals, Division III, since January 2025, appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee to replace Judge Rebecca Pennell; she was one of four applicants [S1][S2].
- She began in 2001 as a Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney (about five years), then practiced civil litigation: a Yakima practice in medical-malpractice defense, then partner at Fox Ballard in Seattle; she had appeared before Division III in numerous cases [S1][S2].
- Education: Eisenhower High School (1993), Claremont McKenna College (1997), University of Colorado School of Law (2001) [S1]. Community service: Yakima Town Hall, Downtown Yakima Rotary [S1]; Yakima Symphony Orchestra board since 2015 [S2].
