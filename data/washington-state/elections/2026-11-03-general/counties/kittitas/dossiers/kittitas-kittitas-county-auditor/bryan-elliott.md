## Background

- Bryan Elliott is the Kittitas County Auditor, seeking a second term; he defeated Democrat Jerry Pettit in 2022 [S3]. He prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- A lifelong county resident and Army veteran (five years active duty, including a Middle East deployment), he is an operations officer in the Washington Army National Guard; before office he was a business consultant [S1][S3].
- He lists 24/7 live-view cameras in the Election Center, grant-funded election security, three consecutive timely audits with no questioned costs, a financial-software overhaul and card payments for recording services [S1][S3]. He was endorsed early by all three county commissioners and all five mayors [S3].
- He was unopposed in the August 4 primary (8,305 votes) [S4].
