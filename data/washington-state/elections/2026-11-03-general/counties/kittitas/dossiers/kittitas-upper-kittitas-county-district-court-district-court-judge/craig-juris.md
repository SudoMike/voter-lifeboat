## Background

- Craig Juris has been the Upper Kittitas County District Court judge for four years; before that he spent 22 years as a deputy prosecuting attorney in several places around the state [S1][S2]. He grew up in Upper Kittitas County and graduated from Cle Elum High School, the University of Washington and Thomas M. Cooley Law School [S1].
- He won the seat in 2022 with 2,704 votes to Eileen Murphy Young's 2,463 [S3].
- His statement describes recent changes in court personnel, procedures and computer systems and commits to "a fair and impartial judiciary that treats all people equally" [S1].
- Judicial contest: experience only was reviewed; no social, parents-and-schools or partisan research.
