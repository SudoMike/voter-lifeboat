## What the office does
Kittitas County has two district court districts, each electing one judge; the Upper district covers Hyak, Easton, Ronald, Roslyn, Cle Elum, South Cle Elum and nearby precincts [S4]. Only voters in the Upper district vote on this seat [S3].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Judge Craig Juris, on the Upper Kittitas County District Court since 2022, is the only candidate. [S1][S3]
