## Background

- Mike Hougardy is the Kittitas County Assessor and says he has held the office for the past nine years [S1][S2]. He prefers the GOP [S1].
- He submitted no biography; his statement says the office prides itself on being "transparent, customer service oriented, and equitable in our appraisals" and that he is dedicated to "adhering to the statutes that govern this office" [S1].
- He was unopposed in the August 4 primary (8,026 votes, write-ins 286) [S3] and is unopposed for re-election [S4].
