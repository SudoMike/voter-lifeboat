## Background

- Paul Sander has been the Lower Kittitas County District Court judge since he was first elected in 2018; he has lived in Kittitas County since 2004 [S1][S2]. He was unopposed in 2022 (9,195 votes) [S3].
- His statement says he worked with justice-system partners to set up the first Kittitas County Department of Public Defense, and with the Upper County court to create a multi-district Behavioral Health Court (serving more than 30 people since 2022), and that the court moved to the statewide Enterprise Justice case system in May 2026 [S1][S2].
