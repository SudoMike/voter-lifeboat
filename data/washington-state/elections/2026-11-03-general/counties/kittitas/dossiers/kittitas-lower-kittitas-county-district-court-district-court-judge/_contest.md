## What the office does
Kittitas County has two district court districts, each electing one judge; the Lower district covers Ellensburg, Kittitas, Thorp, Vantage and the other lower-county precincts [S4]. Only voters in the Lower district vote on this seat [S3].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Judge Paul Sander, on the Lower Kittitas County District Court since 2018, is the only candidate. [S1]
