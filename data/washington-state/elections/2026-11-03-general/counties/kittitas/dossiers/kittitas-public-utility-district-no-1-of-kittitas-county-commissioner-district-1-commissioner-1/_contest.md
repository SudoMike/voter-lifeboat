## What the office does
Kittitas PUD No. 1 commissioners are nominated by district and elected by the voters of the whole PUD, which covers the whole county [S4]; the 2020 race for this seat was on every county precinct [S3].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Commissioner Rick Catlin, first elected in 2020, is the only candidate for a six-year term. [S1]
