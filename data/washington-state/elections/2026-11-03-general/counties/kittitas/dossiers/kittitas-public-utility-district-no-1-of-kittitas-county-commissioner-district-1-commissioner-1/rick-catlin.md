## Background

- Rick Catlin has been the PUD's District 1 commissioner for six years; he won the seat in 2020 with 12,039 votes to Patrick Kelleher's 8,692 [S1][S3].
- A lifelong county resident, he worked in logging after Ellensburg High School (1973), became a journeyman electrician, and ran an electrical contracting business with his wife from 1984 until selling it to their sons in 2016 [S1].
- His priority is "to improve communication with our customers and providing great service to them" [S1].
