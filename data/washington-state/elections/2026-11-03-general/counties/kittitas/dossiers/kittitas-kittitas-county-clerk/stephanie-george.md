## Background

- Stephanie George is the Chief Deputy Clerk and administrative supervisor in the Kittitas County Clerk's Office (2022-present), after two years as a deputy clerk; she was appointed interim county clerk from October 18 to November 30, 2022 [S1][S4]. She prefers the Republican Party [S1].
- Earlier she was a legal assistant at a private firm (2014-2020) and worked in hotel management and at the Washington State Archives' Central Region [S1]. She is a member of Ellensburg Downtown Rotary [S1].
- Clerk Karen Bowen is retiring [S3]; George is unopposed for the open seat [S4] and received 8,173 votes in the primary [S5].
