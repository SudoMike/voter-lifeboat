## What the office does
The sheriff is the county's chief executive officer and conservator of the peace. The sheriff and deputies keep the peace, make arrests, serve court process and may work with other law enforcement agencies under the mutual aid act (S2). The Lewis County Sheriff's Office also runs the Lewis County Jail. Its preliminary 2027 budget request was about $23.25 million: about $11.8 million for law enforcement and $11.5 million for the jail (S5).

## Race dynamics
Sheriff Rob Snaza is retiring at the end of his term, so the seat is open (S4). Both finalists prefer the Republican Party (S1). Gabriel Frase is the Sheriff's Office's special services chief and a 24-year employee of the office. Tracy Murphy is a former Centralia Police Department detective sergeant who led the Joint Narcotics Enforcement Team (JNET) and ran against Snaza in 2022 (S1, S4). Centralia's police chief fired Murphy on April 29, 2026. The chief found that Murphy had been dishonest during an internal investigation, and Murphy is challenging the firing through his union, with arbitration set for November 19-20. The Prosecuting Attorney has issued a potential impeachment disclosure ("Brady letter") about him (S4, S6). In the certified August 4 primary Frase received 8,158 votes (36.15%) and Murphy 7,735 (34.28%). Matt McKnight (16.51%) and Danny Riordan (12.03%) were eliminated (S3). The two finalists were the largest fundraisers among all county candidates (S7).

The office faces a budget gap. Opioid-settlement money that has paid for two deputies is shrinking, jail medical costs are rising, and one commissioner wants to end the yearly transfer of more than $1 million from the county road fund to the Sheriff's Office. A voter-approved public safety sales tax has been discussed, and the Chronicle reports that candidates for sheriff and commissioner have often argued it should wait for a new sheriff (S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience/record:** Frase is the Sheriff's Office insider, one of its three chiefs, and says the agency has a "good foundation" to build on. Murphy is an outside challenger who served 35 years in a city police department and casts the race as "the status quo and a new direction".
2. **Safety:** both are enforcement-oriented. Frase prioritizes victims "over career criminals" and re-forming a combined SWAT team and drug unit. Murphy prioritizes aggressive drug-supply enforcement and cold cases, along with jail programs to reduce recidivism.
3. **Spending:** both promise careful use of taxpayer money. Murphy says he will examine administration, staffing and programs "before asking taxpayers for more".
4. **Reform/accountability:** both promise more transparency. Murphy's firing and Brady letter, and how each candidate talks about them, are central to the race's accountability debate.
5. Positions on the 2026 statewide initiatives: none found for either candidate.
