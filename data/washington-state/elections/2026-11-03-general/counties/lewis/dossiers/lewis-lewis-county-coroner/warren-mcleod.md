## Background

Warren McLeod has been Lewis County Coroner since 2011; his statement says he was elected in 2011 and has served sixteen years. He succeeded longtime Coroner Terry Wilson and has run unopposed in every election since his first (S1, S2). Before moving to Lewis County at the end of 2008 he was an EMT in the Boston area for 14 years and then a death investigator with the Clark County Coroner's Office in Nevada for 11 years (S1, S2). He holds an AAS and a BA in criminal justice, an MA in justice administration and an MA in political science (S1). He has taught criminal justice and forensics at South Puget Sound Community College. He moved from tenured to adjunct professor when the coroner's job became full time in 2014 (S1, S2). He prefers the Republican Party (S1). He told The Chronicle this will be his last term if re-elected (S2, S3).

## Positions

### Office operations
- Three goals for a final term: keep the office's accreditation at its 2028 renewal, have all deputies certified by the American Board of Medicolegal Death Investigators when eligible, and bring more employees on as full- or part-time county staff (S2).
- On staffing he says the county's casual on-call deputies are not truly "intermittent" help. He plans to keep asking commissioners for funding for full-time positions despite tight county budgets (S2).
- His statement says the office has held accreditation from the International Association of Coroners and Medical Examiners since 2012 and that staff are certified death investigators. It lists the office's 2011 vision statements: transparency, staff certifications, public safety partnerships and reducing preventable deaths (S1).
- Describes a vote for him as a vote for continuity, with policy updates to stay compliant with state rules (S2).
