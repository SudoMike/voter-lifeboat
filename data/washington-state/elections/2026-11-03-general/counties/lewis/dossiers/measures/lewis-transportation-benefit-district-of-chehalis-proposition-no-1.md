## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would renew the Transportation Benefit District of Chehalis's existing sales and use tax of two-tenths of one percent (0.2%) for ten more years, from July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2037. The district's governing board is the Chehalis City Council, which adopted Resolution No. 17-2026 [S1][S2].

- Revenue may be used only for transportation purposes allowed by state law: street, bridge and transportation infrastructure upkeep and repair, traffic safety and operations, and projects in the city's transportation plans [S1][S2].
- The current tax expires June 30, 2027; if the measure fails, collection stops then [S1][S2].
- Projects the city names for renewed funding: Safe Routes to School pedestrian and bicycle improvements, replacement of the Chamber Way rail bridge, and later phases of the Downtown Rejuvenation Plan [S1][S4][S6].

## Cost

- 0.2% sales and use tax, 20 cents on each $100 of taxable purchases (2 cents on a $10 purchase), the same rate as now [S1][S6].
- The Chronicle notes state law would allow up to 0.3% for this purpose; the city is asking to keep 0.2% [S5].
- Because it is a sales tax, visitors who shop in Chehalis also pay it [S6][S7].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- Chehalis voters approved the tax in a February 14, 2017 special election, 639 (54.90%) to 525 (45.10%) [S3]. The renewal keeps the same rate [S1][S4].
- The city reports about $11.2 million collected since 2017 for projects including reconstruction of Northwest Pacific Avenue and Southwest Chehalis Avenue and pedestrian safety work on Southwest 13th Street [S1][S4].
- The city says the money has been used as local match for about $12.9 million in state and federal grants [S4] (The Chronicle's September roundup says more than $12 million [S5]). A downtown business owner cites a $4 million state grant for the first phase of the Downtown Rejuvenation Project matched with TBD money [S7].

## Arguments for

The statement for (no author listed) [S1]:
- "This is not a new tax, it simply renews the TBD for another ten years. It does not create a new tax or raise the current tax rate."
- It funds street reconstruction, sidewalks, pedestrian safety and accessibility upgrades, and provides the local match "often required to secure state and federal grants."

Public Works Director Lance Bunker: renewing "at the existing rate will help us keep that work moving forward" [S4]. Book 'N' Brush owner David Hartz writes that downtown depends on people being able to reach it, park and move around safely [S7].

## Arguments against

No statement against was filed [S1]. No committee for or against the measure was found in PDC filings [S8]. In 2017, 45.10% of Chehalis voters rejected the original tax [S3].

## Endorsements and campaign

The city scheduled a public Q&A on the renewal for October 15, 2026 [S6]. No campaign committee was found [S8].
