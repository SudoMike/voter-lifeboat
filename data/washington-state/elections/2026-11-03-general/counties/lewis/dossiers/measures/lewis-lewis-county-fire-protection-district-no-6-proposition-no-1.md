## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would set Lewis County Fire Protection District No. 6's regular property tax levy at $1.15 per $1,000 of assessed value, assessed in 2026 for collection in 2027. The money would "maintain and improve fire protection, and life safety services, replace apparatus and equipment, and provide for firefighter safety." The 2027 dollar amount becomes the base for later limits under chapter 84.55 RCW (101% a year). The board adopted Resolution No. 2026-2 [S1]; the Prosecuting Attorney's approval memo refers to it as Resolution 2026-03 [S6].

- **The district.** It serves rural Chehalis and Adna and has since 1956 [S3]; the district puts its area at 142 square miles and its 2025 emergency responses at 1,034 [S2]. The Chronicle describes it as straddling Chehalis and running from west of Adna to remote areas north of Onalaska [S4].
- **What the district says it would fund.** Equipment and apparatus replacement, station and infrastructure upkeep, current staffing and response times, and operating costs no longer covered after the annexation [S2]. The board says it "has not requested a levy adjustment in many years" [S1]; the flyer says the fire levy has not had a lid lift in more than 20 years, while the EMS levy was raised in 2023 [S2].
- **If it fails.** The district lists possible reduced staffing or service hours, deferred replacement of aging apparatus and facilities, and less ability to keep up with call volume [S2].
- Senior and disabled property tax exemptions (RCW 84.36.381) apply [S1].

## Cost

- $1.15 per $1,000 of assessed value for 2027 collection [S1].
- Current rate: the explanatory statement says about $0.79 per $1,000 in 2026 [S1]; the district's flyer says about $0.73 [S2]. Both say that after the Chehalis annexation the rate for the remaining district would otherwise be about $0.99 to $1.00 [S1][S2].
- District flyer table, computed from $0.73 to $1.15: $126 more a year on a $300,000 property ($345 instead of $219), $168 more on $400,000, $210 more on $500,000 [S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

- This is a levy lid lift on the district's existing regular fire levy, which the 1% limit has pushed down [S1][S2]. Fire districts may levy up to $1.50 per $1,000 [S1].
- **Chehalis annexation.** The City of Chehalis annexed its urban growth area, including parts of Fire Districts 5 and 6, effective June 2026, after the districts sued and reached a settlement in April. The city will contract with each district to serve the annexed areas through 2027, possibly through 2031 [S5]. The district says the annexation took $193,000 a year in revenue [S2].
- **Leadership.** Fire District 6 has been without a chief since January 2026 and is the lead agency in a joint search with Fire District 5 for a shared chief; the districts remain separate [S5].

## Arguments for

No statement for was filed in the voters' guide [S1]. The district's flyer says the measure "is presented to voters as a necessary step to maintain the current level of community fire and emergency medical services" [S2]. The board's resolution calls the increase "essential and necessary for the protection and health and life of the residents" (as quoted by The Chronicle) [S4].
