## Background

- Scott Tinney (Prefers Republican Party) has been Lewis County Clerk since 2016 [S1][S2].
- Before that he was a deputy clerk in the Thurston County Clerk's Office (1993-2016), a paralegal at Calbom, Pond, Falkenstein and Marshall (1992-1993), and worked for Clark County District Court (1991-1992). He was a court-appointed special advocate for Clark County Juvenile Court (1990-1991) and worked in the logging industry (1980-1988) [S1].
- Education: Castle Rock High School (1980); Associate in Applied Science (paralegal), with honors, after studying at Lower Columbia College and Clark College [S1].
- Community service: coached Chehalis Little League baseball and Napavine youth wrestling [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in 2018 (100%) [S4]; re-elected in 2022 with 57.29% over Linda Williams (41.47%) [S3]. Unopposed in 2026 [S5]. Registered with the PDC under mini reporting [S6].
