## What the office does

The assessor values all taxable property in the county each year for property taxes. The Lewis County Assessor's page describes Nielson as "an active participant in the daily duties and functions of the office" and says he works to run it "as efficiently as possible" [S2]. The assessor sets values; taxing districts and voters set levies. Recent Lewis County assessments have drawn appeals and lawsuits, including settlements with corporate property owners such as Darigold and a 2026 refund suit naming the assessor in his official capacity [S4].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Incumbent Ross W. Nielson (Prefers Republican Party) is the only candidate [S1]. The Chronicle reported at the close of filing that he would run unopposed [S5]; an earlier report had named Onalaska resident Kevin Emerson as a possible opponent based on financial disclosures [S3]. Nielson submitted no statement to the voters' guide [S1].
