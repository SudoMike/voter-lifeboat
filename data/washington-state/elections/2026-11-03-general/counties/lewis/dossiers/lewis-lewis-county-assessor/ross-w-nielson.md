## Background

- Ross W. Nielson (Prefers Republican Party) is the Lewis County Assessor, in his first term [S1][S3].
- He won the office in 2022 with 15,829 votes (52.72%) over Tom Crowson (45.75%) [S5].
- The county's biography says he is a lifelong Washington resident who has lived in the Winlock area for over 20 years. It says he ran a small business and trained and worked as an independent fee appraiser, with management experience [S2]. The Chronicle's report of his campaign announcement describes many years as a fee appraiser in public and private settings [S3].
- He submitted no biography or statement to the 2026 voters' guide [S1]. He registered with the PDC under mini reporting and reported no contributions or expenditures [S7].

## Positions

- His campaign announcement said that when he took office "there was a very large backlog of court and appeals cases," and that he worked to open communication "with the goal of having fair and equitable values for all" [S3].
- It described his approach as "hands-on": "The thought of having a figurehead leading the department without daily participation and working in the office is not acceptable to him" [S3].
- The county page says he runs the office "in an open and transparent way" and works "to save the taxpayers money" [S2].

## Record

- The assessor's office reached settlements with several corporate property owners over appraisals, most significantly Darigold, and in 2026 the county indemnified Nielson, named in his official capacity, in a landowner's suit seeking a 2025 property tax refund [S4].
