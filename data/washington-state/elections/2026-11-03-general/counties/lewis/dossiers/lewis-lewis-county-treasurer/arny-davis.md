## Background

- Arny Davis (Prefers Republican Party) has been Lewis County Treasurer since January 2015, the 28th treasurer since 1850 [S1][S2].
- He was Chief Deputy Treasurer for two and a half years before that, and served on the Lewis County Planning Commission (2007-2014) [S1][S2].
- He reports more than 30 years in business management, finance and banking, including work as a commercial lender for national and local banks [S1][S2].
- Community: Centralia College Foundation board (2013-2023), Twin City Rotary, Centralia Community Church of God [S2]. He lives in Adna [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in 2018 (100%) [S4] and 2022 (98.35%, against write-ins) [S3]; unopposed in 2026 [S6]. Registered with the PDC under mini reporting [S7].
