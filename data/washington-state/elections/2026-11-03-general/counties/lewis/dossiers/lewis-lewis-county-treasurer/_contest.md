## What the office does

The Treasurer handles the county's financial operations and banking and collects property taxes. The incumbent's statement says the office also serves the school, fire, water and cemetery districts in the county [S1]. The county page says the treasurer chairs the County Finance Committee and oversees a staff of eight [S2].
