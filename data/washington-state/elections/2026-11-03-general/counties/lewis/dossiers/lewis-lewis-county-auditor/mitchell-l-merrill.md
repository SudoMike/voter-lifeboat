## Background

Mitchell Merrill was raised in Winlock (S2). He reports nearly 10 years of public accounting experience, including auditing financial statements and managing professional standards for a staff of more than 25, and owns a business (S1). The Chronicle reports he began as an intern in 2016 at the local firm that became Mountain View CPAs, earned his CPA license and an MBA from Pacific Lutheran University, and started Merrill CPA, P.S. in July 2025 (S3). He also holds a Bachelor of Applied Science in business management and an associate degree from Centralia College (S1). His community service includes treasurer and elder roles at Winlock Seventh-day Adventist Church, president and treasurer of Generation of Youth for Christ Northwest, site coordinator for the Volunteer Income Tax Assistance program, and president and treasurer of the East Lewis County Chamber of Commerce from 2019 to 2023 (S1). He prefers the Republican Party (S1). He has not held elected office (S1).

In the certified August 4 primary he placed first with 7,630 votes (37.23%), 33 votes ahead of Mitch Townsend (S7).

## Positions

### Auditor's office administration
- Says the office "plays an important role in elections, licensing, recording, public records, and financial accountability" and that citizens "deserve an office that remains accurate, transparent, responsive, and professionally managed by a Certified Public Accountant" (S1).
- Priorities: "follow the law in an ethical and accurate manner, continue to leverage modern technology, and give power to the people through access to information." He says he "will build on the foundation laid by my predecessor's service" (S1).
- Calls the move from his CPA practice to the auditor's office a "lateral move" with many of the same responsibilities (S3).

### Elections
- "Elections must continue to be free from material error and fairly administered so the voice of the people is honored" (S1).
- His site says he is "committed to protecting the integrity of the ballot and ensuring elections are conducted fairly, accurately, and transparently" (S2).

### Spending and stewardship
- Says government "should work efficiently and responsibly for the people" and commits to "using resources wisely, promoting accountability" (S2). He says he will "keep government resources working for Lewis County citizens" (S1).
