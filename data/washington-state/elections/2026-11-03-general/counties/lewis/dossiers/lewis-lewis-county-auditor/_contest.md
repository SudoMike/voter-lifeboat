## What the office does
The county auditor records deeds and other documents, keeps the county's accounts against the treasurer's, publishes the county's annual financial statement and registers the warrants the commissioners authorize (S2). The auditor is also the officer who maintains voter registration and conducts state and local elections in the county (S3). Merrill's statement adds licensing and public records to that list (S1).

## Race dynamics
Auditor Larry Grove, in the office since 1989 and auditor since his first election in 2014, is retiring, so the seat is open (S5). Both finalists prefer the Republican Party (S1). In the certified August 4 primary, CPA Mitchell L. Merrill received 7,630 votes (37.23%), former Lewis County Republican Party chair Mitch Townsend 7,597 (37.07%), and Frank L. Corbin 4,955 (24.18%) (S4).

Merrill runs on his credentials as a certified public accountant and on continuity with Grove, who endorses him along with former Auditor Gary Zandell, Commissioners Scott Brummer and Sean Swope and Treasurer Arny Davis (S5, S6). Townsend runs on a "Contract with Lewis County": audit the auditor's office, audit the voter rolls, re-examine all county finances and press for state election reform. He argues the job is an executive post that does not require a CPA (S1; mitch-townsend.md S2, S3). Both raised under $12,000 (S7; candidate dossiers).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience/record:** a young CPA endorsed by the outgoing and former auditors, promising to build on the current office, against a longtime business owner and school-board chair promising a top-to-bottom review. Neither has run a county office.
2. **Spending/accountability:** Townsend promises a full financial re-examination of every county department and "open books"; Merrill stresses accurate, professionally managed accounting and "responsible stewardship."
3. **Reform:** Townsend's platform centers on state election reform (voter ID and citizenship proof, election-night results, no late-arriving ballots), voter-roll audits and ballot-to-voter matching. Merrill commits to fair, accurate, transparent elections run under current law.
4. Positions on the 2026 statewide initiatives: none found for either candidate.
