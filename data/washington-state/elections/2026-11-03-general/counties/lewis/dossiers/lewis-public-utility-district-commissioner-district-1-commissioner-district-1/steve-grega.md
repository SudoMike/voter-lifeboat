## Background

Steve Grega, of Chehalis (S3), is a professional engineer (since 1980) who retired after 37 years as an engineer at Lewis County PUD. He lists his PUD duties as licensing, construction and operation of Cowlitz Falls Dam, state and federal regulatory compliance and the Nine Canyon wind project (S1). Cowlitz Falls is the PUD's 70-megawatt hydroelectric project, completed in 1994 (S4). Before the PUD he worked for the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers from 1970 to 1980 on dams and civil construction. He is also a business owner, farmer and timber owner (S1). He graduated from Washington State University in 1970 with a B.S. in electrical engineering (S1). He lists "Freeholder Elect" as elected experience (S1). His community service includes the Lewis County Chehalis Basin Flood Advisory Committee, LOVE, Inc., youth soccer leadership and a church council (S1). The race is nonpartisan (S1).

He ran for the same seat in 2020 and lost in the primary (S2).

## Positions
