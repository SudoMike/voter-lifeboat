## Background

Jeff Baine, of Chehalis (S3), is a lifelong Lewis County resident and a solutions architect for Barracuda Networks. He reports work with public and private organizations on technology, communications, broadband expansion and long-term infrastructure planning (S1). The Chronicle describes him as a former long-time employee of the PUD and former president of the Lewis County Broadband Action Committee (S2). He listed no elected experience or education in the voters' guide (S1). The race is nonpartisan (S1).

## Positions

### Utility governance and rates
- "I believe local leadership and local control matter. Decisions impacting our power, infrastructure, and future should be made with transparency, accountability, and in the best interests of ratepayers" (S1).
- Names growing energy demand, resource adequacy, aging infrastructure and increasing regulation as pressures on public utilities, and says utilities "must navigate the requirements of the Clean Energy Transformation Act while balancing reliability, affordability, and responsible long-term planning" (S1).
- Priorities: dependable service, "responsible financial stewardship," open communication, broadband expansion, infrastructure investment and "protecting affordable power for families, farms, and businesses" (S1).
