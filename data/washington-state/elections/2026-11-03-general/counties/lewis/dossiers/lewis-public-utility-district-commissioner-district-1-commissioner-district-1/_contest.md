## What the office does
Public Utility District No. 1 of Lewis County, formed by voters in 1936, sells electricity to about 35,000 customers. Its three elected commissioners serve six-year terms, set utility policy and hire and oversee the general manager (S3, S4). Most of its power comes from the Bonneville Power Administration, and it owns the 70-megawatt Cowlitz Falls hydroelectric project (S5). Commissioners are nominated by their commissioner district, but voters of the entire PUD elect them at the general election (S2). The PDC lists 48,309 voters in the PUD against 58,906 county-wide (S9). The City of Centralia is not in the PUD: the PUD commissioner races of November 2020 appeared in every Lewis County precinct except Centralia #1 to #13 (S11).

## Race dynamics
The District 1 seat has had three holders since 2021. Commissioner Michael Kelly, elected in 2020, was charged with bribery, and the seat was vacated in March 2025 after he missed 60 days of meetings. Commissioners Mike Hadaller and Dave Fenn appointed former general manager Dave Muller in May 2025 (S6, S8). Muller is listed as the District 1 commissioner (S3) and is not on the ballot (S1). The ballot carries both the short term and the full six-year term (S1).

Three candidates filed: Steve Grega, former Commissioner Ben Kostick and Jeff Baine (S7). Only Grega and Baine appear on the general ballot, with no primary (S1, S9). Grega is a retired 37-year PUD engineer who worked on the licensing of Cowlitz Falls Dam and also ran for the seat in 2020 (S1, S7). Baine is a solutions architect at Barracuda Networks, a former long-time PUD employee and former president of the Lewis County Broadband Action Committee (S1, S7). The race is nonpartisan (S1). Both promise affordable, reliable power. Grega focuses on keeping rates low and on Cowlitz Falls Dam relicensing; Baine on transparency, broadband expansion, infrastructure investment and navigating the Clean Energy Transformation Act (S1). The two were scheduled to debate at the Centralia chamber's October debates (S10).
