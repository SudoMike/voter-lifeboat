## What the office does

The Prosecuting Attorney is the county's chief legal officer. The office prosecutes crimes under state law and county ordinance (RCW 36.27.020): adult felonies in Superior Court, misdemeanors in District Court, juvenile cases and appeals. It also advises the Board of County Commissioners and county departments, handles some child support and paternity cases, prepares ballot titles and sits on the canvassing board [S2].
