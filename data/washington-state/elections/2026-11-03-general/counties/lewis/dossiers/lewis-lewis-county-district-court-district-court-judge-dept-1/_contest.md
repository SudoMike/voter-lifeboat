## What the office does

Lewis County District Court is a court of limited jurisdiction for the whole county. It has two judges, R.W. Buzzard and Wade S. Samuelson [S2]. Its pages cover the case types district courts hear: misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor crimes, traffic and other infractions, civil and small claims, anti-harassment orders, name changes, and probation [S2].

## Race dynamics
