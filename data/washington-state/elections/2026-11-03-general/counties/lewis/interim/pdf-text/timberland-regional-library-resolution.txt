Attachment A EXPLANATORY STATEMENT State law allows Timberland Regional Library District (the “ Library ”) to annually collect up to $0.50 per $1,000 of assessed property value to fund Library services, operations, maintenance and improvements . Due to tax limitation laws, the Library has collected at a lower rate for many years. The Library last requested an increase in 2009, but that resolution was not adopted. Approval of Proposition 1 would authorize the Library to reset its regular property tax levy from $ 0.228924 to $0. 35 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2027 and 2028 . For example, t he estimated increase for a home valued at $ 334 ,000 is a bout $ 40 . 44 annually , or $ 3 . 37 month ly . The resulting 202 8 levy amount would be used to calculate all applicable tax limitations thereafter. If approved by voters, Proposition 1 would provide funding necessary to maintain Library services, staffing, collections, and open hours. If not approved, further reductions in staffing, books and materials, hours, online services, bookmobile services and programming will begin in 2027, and maintenance and improvements of Library facilities could also be delayed.
