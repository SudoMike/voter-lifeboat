## BallotTitle
Transportation Benefit District of Chehalis

Proposition No. 1

Sales and Use Tax Levy Renewal For Transportation Needs

The Governing Board of the Transportation Benefit District (City Council) of the City of Chehalis, Washington, adopted Resolution No. 17-2026 concerning sales and use tax to finance transportation improvements.  This proposition would renew the current sales and use tax of two-tenths of one percent (0.2%) for an additional term of 10 years beginning July 1, 2027 and ending June 30, 2037 to fund transportation improvements outlined in the City's Transportation Improvement Plan, traffic engineering and control, and the general operation, maintenance, and repair of city streets, bridges, and other transportation infrastructure

Should this proposition be approved?

## ExplanatoryStatement
Proposition No. 1 concerns the City of Chehalis Transportation Benefit District's sales and use tax.

If approved, this proposition would authorize the Transportation Benefit District to continue imposing a sales and use tax of two-tenths of one percent (0.2%), equal to 20 cents on each $100 of taxable purchases, for an additional ten-year period beginning July 1, 2027, and ending June 30, 2037.

Revenue generated by the tax may be used only for transportation improvements and related purposes authorized by state law, including preserving and maintaining city streets, bridges, and other transportation infrastructure; improving traffic safety and operations; and funding transportation projects identified in the City's transportation plans.

This proposition renews the existing voter-approved Transportation Benefit District sales and use tax, which is scheduled to expire on June 30, 2027. Approval would allow the Transportation Benefit District to continue collecting the tax at the current rate for an additional ten years. If the proposition is not approved, collection of the tax will expire on June 30, 2027.

## ArgumentFor
Please vote Yes on the Renewal of the Transportation Benefit District.
Voting Yes to renew Chehalis’s Transportation Benefit District (TBD) is a vote to maintain safe streets and sidewalks for our community without increasing taxes. This is not a new tax, it simply renews the TBD for another ten years. It does not create a new tax or raise the current tax rate.
 It extends a proven, local funding source that has helped improve the transportation infrastructure that our community depends on every day. It supports street reconstruction, sidewalk improvements, pedestrian safety projects, and accessibility upgrades that makes Chehalis safer for residents, businesses, visitors, and emergency responders. It also provides local matching funds often required to secure state and federal grants.
Originally implemented in 2017, the TBD has generated approximately $11.2 million for local projects that have improved roadways, sidewalks, accessibility, pedestrian safety and aging infrastructure. These include NW Pacific Avenue and SW Chehalis Avenue reconstructions, SW 13th Street, sidewalk and accessibility improvements, and the Downtown Rejuvenation Plan. Renewal of this funding will provide continuing support for such projects as the safe routes to school pedestrian connections, Chamber Way Rail Bridge replacement,  downtown rejuvenation plan improvements, and ADA accessibility among other projects.
The current funding will expire unless it is renewed. We are asking you to vote Yes to this existing measure for another 10 years.
