I want Lewis County to thrive again with good paying jobs by making Lewis County a place where businesses want to be. Safety, education, medical care, transportation and expedited permitting are all part of what we need. While County Commissioners don't have a final say on all these needs, we can be strong voices bringing public pressure to move our county forward. Our residents shouldn't have to be worried about being burglarized while they are at work or have to tolerate known drug dealers and drug houses. I believe we are about to get a new Sheriff who understands our safety needs. It's time for economic development throughout Lewis County. Commissioner Pollock and I will provide that leadership to make this happen. I have had the good fortune to serve as a PUD Commissioner with Dave Muller and David Fenn. They taught me that Commissioners set policy, not day to day management. No more expensive political lawsuits. Just serve the public.  
I know the people and needs of the 3rd District; I will be there at council meetings. I will be a strong voice because I am of, by, and for people too often not heard. Elect Mike Hadaller.
