Statement
I have the honor of serving as your County Clerk. I ask for your vote so that I can continue serving the people of Lewis County in that role. The County Clerk needs to have a wide range of knowledge and experience directly related to the position. That knowledge and experience can only be gained by doing the job. I have been doing the job for more than 33 years either as a Deputy Clerk or as the County Clerk. I have the knowledge and experience that uniquely qualifies me for the position.
