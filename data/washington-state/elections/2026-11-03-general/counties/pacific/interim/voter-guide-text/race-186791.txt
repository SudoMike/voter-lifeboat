Statement
I am running for Pacific County Prosecutor because our community deserves a Prosecutor’s Office grounded in strength, stability, and integrity. I have the experience and commitment to bring this vision to life. Strength means enforcing the law fairly and consistently, protecting victims, holding offenders accountable, and prioritizing public safety. It also means making difficult decisions when necessary and ensuring criminal charges and sentencing accurately reflect the conduct that occurred. Stability means building a Prosecutor’s Office that is dependable, professional, and focused on long-term success. It comes from strong leadership, clear policies, supporting the employees who serve our community, and a long-term commitment to Pacific County and its future. Integrity means doing the right thing, even when it is difficult. It means being honest, transparent, and guided by fairness, ethics, and respect for the law. Every case should be handled ethically, fairly, and with professionalism. These principles are essential to building public trust, protecting our communities, and maintaining a justice system that serves Pacific County fairly and effectively. I would be honored to serve as Pacific County Prosecutor and look forward to building a strong future for our community.
