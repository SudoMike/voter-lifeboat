## Background

Nicole Deskins, of South Bend, prefers the Independent Party and has been the appointed Pacific County Auditor since Dec. 1, 2025 [S1][S3]. Before that she was a senior accountant and then office and accounting manager in the auditor's office [S3][S2], and earlier a logistics manager for a steel tube and bar manufacturer and distributor [S1]. She graduated from South Bend High School in 2007 [S1]. The county commission chose her over Dotsi Graves by a 3-0 vote on Oct. 31, 2025 to replace Alex Gerow, who resigned [S2].

She finished second in the August primary, 4,012 to 4,034 [S5].

## Positions

- Cites a new payroll system and a new county website during her time as auditor, and says she has further plans "to strengthen the Office" [S1].
- In a September 2026 letter about federal moves to restrict mail voting, she said the county remained "confident in our County and State procedures," described chain-of-custody, signature verification and logic-and-accuracy testing, and urged voters who mail ballots to get a postmark at the post office [S4].
