## What the office does
Pacific County District Court has two judges, North District and South District, and each is elected only by the voters of its own district: in 2022 the North District race drew 3,221 votes and the South District race 5,053, out of 12,068 ballots county-wide [S3][S2]. Four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Judge Scott Harmer, elected in 2022, is the only candidate; nonpartisan office [S1][S2].
