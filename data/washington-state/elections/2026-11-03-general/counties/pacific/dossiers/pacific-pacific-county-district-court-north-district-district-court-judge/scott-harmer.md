## Background

Scott Harmer, of South Bend, is a third-generation Pacific County resident and the North District Court judge since 2022 [S1][S2]. He was elected unopposed that year with 3,168 votes in the district [S3]. His legal career: Pacific County Prosecutor's Office intern (1992-1993) and senior criminal deputy prosecutor (1994-1999), Pacific County indigent defense counsel (2000-2022), private practice (2000-present), Pacific County Superior Court commissioner (2000-present), and Lewis County indigent defense counsel (2022-present) [S1]. He says he tried multiple homicide cases as a prosecutor, including one of the state's first "3 strikes" cases, and has served as a special assistant attorney general advising the Department of Children, Youth and Families in Pacific and Wahkiakum counties [S1]. He graduated from Eastern Washington University (1991) and Gonzaga School of Law (1994) [S1].
