## Background

Michael S. Turner, of Raymond, states no party preference [S1][S2]. Born and raised in Pacific County, he returned permanently in 1996 and has practiced law there for 30 years [S1]. He has prosecuted for the cities of Raymond, South Bend, Long Beach and Ilwaco, served as their municipal judge, as city attorney for Raymond and South Bend, as a pro tem district court judge, a superior court commissioner and the county's hearing examiner [S1][S2]. The Observer reports he is a tax referee at the Washington State Board of Tax Appeals [S2]. He holds a J.D. from the University of Washington, a master's in management from Willamette's Atkinson School and B.A.s in mathematics and chemistry from Willamette [S1]. He ran for Pacific County Superior Court judge in 2019 and lost to Don Richter with 48% [S2]. He is a past president of Raymond Kiwanis, Willapa River Lions and the Pacific County Bar Association [S1].

He finished second in the August primary, 3,381 to Zorn's 4,858 [S4].
