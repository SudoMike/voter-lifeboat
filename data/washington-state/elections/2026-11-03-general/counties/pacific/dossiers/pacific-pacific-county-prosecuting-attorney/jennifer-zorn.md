## Background

Jennifer Zorn, of South Bend, prefers the Independent Party and is Pacific County's chief criminal deputy prosecutor, handling "the majority of the county's felony prosecutions" [S1][S4]. A prosecutor for more than ten years, she was previously the felony sex crimes prosecutor and then chief criminal deputy prosecutor in Grays Harbor County, and has prosecuted for Lewis County and the cities of Puyallup and Federal Way [S1][S4]. She holds a J.D. from St. Thomas University School of Law and a B.A. from The Evergreen State College [S1]. This is her first campaign [S1]. Outgoing Prosecutor Michael Rothman said he recruited her and that her candidacy "was our plan" [S3].

She led the August primary 4,858 to 3,381 [S7].

## Positions

### Prosecution and public safety
- Platform of "strength, stability, and integrity": "enforcing the law fairly and consistently, protecting victims, holding offenders accountable, and prioritizing public safety," and ensuring charges and sentencing "accurately reflect the conduct that occurred" [S1].
- Says strength "also means understanding when bail is necessary to protect the community" [S2].
- Stability: an office that is "dependable, professional," with clear policies, supported employees and no "constant turnover" [S1][S2].

## Record

- Chief criminal deputy prosecutor in Pacific County under Rothman [S1][S3].
- In April 2026 the office dismissed felony charges with prejudice because they had been filed after the statute of limitations; Zorn said a deputy caught the error, the office "acted immediately to dismiss," and training procedures were reviewed so "this clerical error does not happen again" [S5].
