## What the office does
The prosecuting attorney leads the county office that charges and prosecutes crimes, including the felony cases its chief criminal deputy handles [S1][S2]. From July 1, 2026 the salary is more than $195,000 a year [S2]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics
Open seat: Prosecutor Michael Rothman, appointed in 2021, is not running; he said he recruited his chief criminal deputy, Jennifer Zorn, and that her run "was our plan" [S2]. Zorn faces Michael S. Turner, a Pacific County attorney for 30 years who has served as city prosecutor and city attorney, pro tem district court judge, superior court commissioner and county hearing examiner [S1][S3]. Zorn prefers the Independent Party; Turner states no party preference [S1]. Zorn led the primary 4,858 to 3,381 [S4].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience and continuity:** Zorn is the office's current felony lead and Rothman's chosen successor; Turner is an outsider to the current office with a long local legal and quasi-judicial career.
2. **Safety:** Zorn's platform stresses enforcement, victim protection, bail "when necessary" and charges that "accurately reflect the conduct"; Turner's statement gives no prosecution philosophy.
3. No positions on taxes, spending or other rubric axes in either campaign.
