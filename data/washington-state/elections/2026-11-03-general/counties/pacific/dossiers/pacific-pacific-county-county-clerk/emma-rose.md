## Background

Emma Rose, of South Bend, prefers the Independent Party and has been Pacific County Superior Court Clerk since 2023 [S1][S2]. She worked in Pacific County court administration from 2020 to 2022 [S1], and was elected unopposed in 2022 with 8,698 votes [S3]. She holds a bachelor's degree from Washington State University, serves as Southwest District representative for the Washington State Association of County Clerks, sits on the South Bend School Foundation board and coaches youth girls' basketball [S1].
