## What the office does
The county treasurer runs the county treasury and its finances; Didion describes the job as daily operations, financial responsibilities and customer service [S1]. Four-year term [S1].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested open seat. Treasurer Renee Goodin, first elected in 2006 (unopposed in 2022 with 8,321 votes), is not running; Sheila Didion (Prefers Independent Party), a senior accountant in the treasurer's office, is the only candidate [S2][S3][S1].
