## Background

Bruce Walker, of Raymond, is the incumbent Pacific County Assessor and states no party preference [S1][S2]. He was first elected in 2002, defeating Lisa Olsen, and is seeking a seventh term; this is the first time he has been opposed since then [S2]. He ran unopposed in 2022 (8,468 votes) [S5]. Before becoming assessor he was a senior title officer with Pacific County Title Co. in South Bend [S2]. He submitted no statement or biography to the 2026 voters' guide [S1].

He led the August primary 5,011 to 3,366 [S6].

## Positions

No campaign platform found. His public communications as assessor:
- Explains the 2026 changes to the senior citizen and disabled persons exemption (SB 6162): higher income thresholds, a $7,500 standard deduction, a 40% disability rating threshold for veterans, a deduction for renting out space in the home [S3][S4].
- Notes that "any taxes exempted in the Senior/Disabled, Timberland, Farm and Ag or Conservation programs do not go away; nor do the taxing districts lose any monies, but these exempt taxes are shifted onto other persons in the taxing district who don't receive an exemption" [S3].
- Hosted exemption workshops at the Long Beach annex and in South Bend in October 2026 [S4].
