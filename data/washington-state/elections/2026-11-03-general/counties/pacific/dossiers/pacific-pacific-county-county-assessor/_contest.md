## What the office does
The assessor's office sets the assessed values used for property taxes and runs the exemption programs, such as the senior citizen and disabled persons exemption; the office works from the courthouse in South Bend and an annex in Long Beach [S3]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics
Incumbent Bruce Walker, first elected in 2002 and seeking a seventh term, faces his first opponent since that year: Jeff Phelan of Ocean Park [S2]. Both state no party preference [S1]. Walker submitted no statement to the voters' guide; Phelan's statement promises to "exempt, reduce, or remove your property taxes where possible" and calls for "new blood" [S1]. Walker led the primary 5,011 to 3,366 [S4].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience:** a 24-year incumbent against a first-time candidate whose background is utility accounting.
2. **Taxes:** Phelan frames the office as a way to lower taxes; Walker's public columns explain the exemption programs; one notes that exempted taxes "do not go away" but shift to other taxpayers in the district [S5].
3. **Spending:** Phelan pledges to reduce costs "without impacting personnel"; Walker has made no statement.
