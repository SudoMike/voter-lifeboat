## Background

Jeffrey (Jeff) Phelan, of Ocean Park, states no party preference [S1][S3]. He moved to the area in the late 2010s [S3]. His statement lists accounting work at Southwest Gas Corporation (a three-year capital and operations budget of about $7.5 million, billing and rate-and-tax database compliance, a business continuity plan), a B.S. in business information systems, an associate degree in accounting, honorable U.S. Air Force service and a Six Sigma Green Belt [S1]. He has not held elected office [S1]. He announced his campaign at the Pacific County Republican Party's Lincoln Day Dinner in April 2026 [S2].

He finished second in the August primary, 3,366 to Walker's 5,011 [S4].

## Positions

### Taxes
- "Are you on a fixed income? My income is fixed and I cannot afford to continue to pay an ever-increasing amount of taxes. I am running for Assessor to do my part to help fix the tax mess. I will exempt, reduce, or remove your property taxes where possible" [S1].
- Criticizes the incumbent: "Twenty years in office taxing and taxing. It's time for new blood" [S1].

### Office management and spending
- Pledges to return "accountability, transparency, and effectiveness to county government while streamlining processes, improving services, and reducing costs," without "impacting personnel" [S1].

## Public conduct reported in the press

The Chinook Observer reported in April 2026, from about 80 screenshots it was given, that Phelan had posted on Facebook that the Chinook Indian Nation is "not a nation… not a tribe… just a bunch of fraudsters," had posted in favor of ICE removals and against Democrats ("imbeciles," "spineless"), and had received a one-month trespass notice from Timberland Regional Library in 2025 for offensive language on a library Facebook page, which he called a "badge of honor." He did not respond to the paper's request for comment [S2]. In an October 2025 post he described himself as "a functioning quadriplegic" [S2].
