## What the office does
Pacific County PUD No. 2 is the county's public electric utility. Its three commissioners set rates and policy and oversee the general manager; each is nominated from a commissioner district and elected by voters in the whole PUD, which covers all of Pacific County, for a six-year term [S1][S3]. District 1 covers Ilwaco, Chinook, Naselle, Bay Center and South Bend [S2].

## Race dynamics
