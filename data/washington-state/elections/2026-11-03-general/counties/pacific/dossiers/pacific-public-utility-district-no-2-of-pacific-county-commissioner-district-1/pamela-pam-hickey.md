## Background

Pam Hickey, 79, of Ilwaco, has been a PUD No. 2 commissioner since her 2020 election [S2][S1]. She won that race county-wide, 6,168 votes (52.11%) to Michael Swanson's 5,527 (46.7%) [S3]. She was office manager for the Chinook Water District for 10 years and spent 25 years at Fortune 500 companies as an information systems project manager and merchandise/product manager [S1]. She studied accounting and biochemistry at Saint Mary-of-the-Woods and Louisiana State University New Orleans [S1].

## Positions and record

- Says the PUD has kept rates "lower than surrounding utilities," stayed debt free, secured broadband service for north county and approved a 20-year power agreement with the Bonneville Power Administration for "reliable, affordable, climate compliant electricity" [S1].
- Says she wrote the commissioner manual setting out commissioners' responsibilities [S1].
- Says 80% of the PUD's senior staff are new after retirements and a new general manager is in place, so the board must give "consistent direction and stability" [S1].
- Says she donates 10% of her base commissioner salary to help neighbors pay utility bills and has put campaign advertising money toward low-income residents' PUD bills [S1].
