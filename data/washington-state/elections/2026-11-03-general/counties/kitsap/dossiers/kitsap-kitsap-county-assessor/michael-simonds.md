## Background

- Michael Simonds (Prefers Democratic Party) is a Navy veteran who served aboard the USS Nebraska as a nuclear missile technician and settled in Kitsap County [S1].
- He joined Redfin in 2017 as its first agent based in the county and reports nearly a decade negotiating home sale values and analyzing housing data [S1].
- He lists no prior elected office and no campaign website [S1].
- PDC lists him as a declared candidate who "Qualified for general" after the August primary; no campaign finance reports were on file as of 2026-09-21 [S2].

## Positions

- **Purpose of the office:** says his mission "isn't to increase county revenue; it's to ensure that the revenue we do collect is balanced fairly and accurately across every single neighborhood" [S1].
- **Valuation equity:** says outdated assessment models mean "working-class neighborhoods often pay more than their fair share while high-value properties lag behind," calls this "an equity issue," and proposes advanced data analytics to correct neighborhood-level imbalances [S1].
- **Access and exemptions:** proposes user-friendly online tools explaining valuations and the appeals process, plus expanded outreach so seniors, veterans, disabled and lower-income residents use every exemption and deferral program allowed under state law [S1].
- **Agenda:** "accountability, transparency, and modernization" for the office [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
