## Background

- District Court judge, Department 3, elected in 2022 [S1], with 55.11% over Stan Glisson [S2]. Currently assistant presiding judge [S1].
- Member of the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association and current co-chair of the Council for Independent Courts [S1].
- Before the bench: Bremerton Municipal Court commissioner, judge pro tem, and 18 years as a trial lawyer, as a prosecutor and as a public defender [S1].
- Served as a yeoman in the Coast Guard's District 13 Seattle legal office [S1].
- Education: Gonzaga Law School [S1].
- Community service: North Kitsap Poulsbo Rotary, Poulsbo Chamber, OlyCAP board, youth coaching and theater and band volunteering [S1].
