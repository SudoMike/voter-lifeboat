## Background
Jim Pierson, a Democrat, is an Army Special Forces veteran [S1][S5]. His campaign site describes seven years in the Special Forces in the Arctic Circle and a software-industry career as a product and program manager [S5]. The Kitsap Daily News reports that after the Army he worked as a union cable installer, then in engineering, quality assurance, product management and executive advisory roles at Apple, Microsoft and GoDaddy over three decades [S2]. His only elected post is Democratic precinct committee officer; he has also been a resolution officer in the 35th District Democrats [S1][S2]. His pamphlet lists work against eminent-domain impacts in Thurston County, an HOA architecture committee seat, the Commercial Aviation Workgroup, Rotary and trail building [S1].

In the August 4 primary he finished second with just over 29% (11,809 votes) behind Rep. Dan Griffey (just over 52%), ahead of Democrat Shaena Garberich (about 18%), as reported by the Kitsap Daily News [S3].

## Positions
- **Approach:** "Government should not be an enemy of the people. It should be a steward that makes systems work for people" [S1]. Says he is running "because I believe government can work better for the people it serves" [S4]. Says he starts "with the problem, follow[s] the evidence" rather than with ideology [S2].
- **Taxes:** pamphlet promises to "work for fair taxes" [S1]. Supports "a fairer tax system that provides property tax relief for seniors and working families while asking the wealthiest individuals and corporations to contribute their share" [S2]. Platform: "keep property taxes low" and "Large corporations and the ultra-wealthy must pay their fair share" [S6]. No statement found on ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on income over $1 million, or on I-645.
- **Spending:** says representatives should "spend public dollars wisely," and that government "should work as efficiently and responsibly as the people who fund it" [S6][S5]. Wants schools and educators "properly funded" [S6].
- **Housing:** platform calls for planning housing and infrastructure "so growth lowers costs" and says "renters must be protected" [S6]. Wants to increase housing supply [S2]. Points to permitting delays and barriers to condominium construction, including construction-defect liability, as causes of high costs [S2].
- **Healthcare:** pamphlet: "When profit comes first, families pay more and get less"; will push for "patient-first healthcare solutions that lower costs and expand access without breaking what works" [S1]. Platform: healthcare "must move away from a for-profit system toward one that puts people first" [S6]. Names mental-health treatment access as a priority [S2].
- **Climate and environment:** pamphlet supports "climate policy that is practical and lowers costs for families" [S1]. Platform: protect clean water, wildlife and salmon habitat, hold polluters accountable, and invest in clean and renewable energy [S6]. Lists wildfire preparedness as a priority [S2].
- **Data centers and new industry:** names AI and data centers among the issues he hears most often; wants new industries to "pay their fair share of infrastructure costs instead of shifting those costs onto working families" [S2].
- **Public safety:** calls for "smart transportation planning, accountable policing, access to treatment and recovery, and investments that prevent harm before it happens" [S6]. Names addressing the root causes of homelessness as a priority [S2].
- **Immigration:** "Immigrants deserve a fair and expanded path to citizenship" [S6]. Lists "immigration and government overreach" among the issues voters raise with him [S2].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "Everyone should be free to live authentically, love who they love, and make their own healthcare decisions. Reproductive rights must be protected, discrimination banned, and church and state kept separate" [S6]. Pamphlet: "protect your right to be who you are" [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found on parental notification, record access or opt-outs. Lists strengthening public schools as a priority [S2].
- **Campaign money:** his campaign site says he "does not accept corporate contributions" [S5].
