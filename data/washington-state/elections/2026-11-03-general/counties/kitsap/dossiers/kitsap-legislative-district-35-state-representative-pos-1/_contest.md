## What the office does
A Washington State Representative serves a two-year term in the state House [S2]. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The 35th District takes in all of Mason County and parts of Thurston and Kitsap counties [S2].

## Race dynamics
Republican Rep. Dan Griffey, a retired Mason County firefighter first elected in 2014, faces Democrat Jim Pierson, an Army Special Forces veteran who spent three decades in the technology industry [S2][S1]. In the August primary Griffey led with just over 52%, Pierson took just over 29%, and Democrat Shaena Garberich took about 18% [S3].

Griffey's campaign centers on affordability, crime and businesses leaving the state. He opposes the 2026 tax on income over $1 million: he voted no, offered a floor amendment to exclude public pensions from it, and keeps a "No State Income Tax" plank on his site (dan-griffey.md S2, S5, S6, S7). Pierson promises "fair taxes," property-tax relief, and asking "the wealthiest individuals and corporations to contribute their share." He has not addressed the income tax or I-645 directly (jim-pierson.md S1, S2). Pierson's platform also covers reproductive rights, anti-discrimination law, an expanded path to citizenship, renter protections, clean energy, and moving healthcare away from a for-profit system (jim-pierson.md S6). Griffey has no campaign statements on those topics; his record includes votes for I-2081 and against ESHB 1296, ESSB 5599 and ESHB 1469 (dan-griffey.md S11, S12, S14, S15). Neither candidate has stated a position on I-1 or I-638. PDC reports show Griffey with $94.6k raised and Pierson with $38.4k (dan-griffey.md S17; jim-pierson.md S8).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes:** Griffey has a record and platform against income taxes and recent tax increases. Pierson wants the wealthiest and large corporations to pay more, with property-tax relief.
2. **Public safety:** Griffey's "families before felons" enforcement agenda against Pierson's "accountable policing" with treatment and prevention.
3. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Pierson states support for reproductive rights and anti-discrimination law. Griffey's evidence is his votes only.
4. **Immigration:** Pierson supports an expanded path to citizenship. No Griffey statement was found.
5. **Climate and energy:** Pierson backs renewable investment and "practical" climate policy. Griffey wants a lower gas tax and hydropower counted as renewable.
6. **Record vs. renewal:** a 12-year incumbent and former House Republican Whip against a challenger who has held no public office beyond precinct committee officer and says he takes no corporate contributions.
