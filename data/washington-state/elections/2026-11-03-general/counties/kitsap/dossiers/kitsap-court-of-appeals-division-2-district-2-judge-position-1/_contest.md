## What the office does

The Court of Appeals hears appeals from the superior courts. Division II sits in Tacoma, and its District 2 (Clallam, Grays Harbor, Jefferson, Kitsap, Mason and Thurston counties) elects three judges [S2]. Judges serve six-year terms [S3]. In his statement, the incumbent says the court's 22 judges decide about 1,500 cases a year [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Erik D. Price, on the court since 2021, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. Lisa L. Sutton held this position in 2020 [S4]. Price was on the 2022 ballot unopposed and took 98.09% of the Kitsap vote [S5]. The contest is uncontested.
