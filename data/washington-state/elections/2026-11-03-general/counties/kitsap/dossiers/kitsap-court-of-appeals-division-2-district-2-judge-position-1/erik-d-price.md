## Background

- Judge, Court of Appeals, since 2021; acting chief judge of Division II since 2026 [S1].
- Thurston County Superior Court judge 2012-2021, including presiding judge (2020-2021) and three years as chief judge of the adult drug court [S1].
- 19 years in private civil litigation practice before the bench [S1].
- Other roles: chair of the Thurston County Pre-trial Justice Improvement Team, co-chair of the Felony Leadership Improvement Project, former chair of the WSBA Judicial Recommendation Committee, and past president of the Thurston County Bar Association [S1].
- Education: J.D. with honors, University of Washington School of Law (1993); B.A. cum laude, University of Washington (1988), Phi Beta Kappa [S1].
- On the 2022 ballot unopposed, with 98.09% of the Kitsap vote [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Describes the Court of Appeals as the only chance at review for most litigants. He says each judge decides about 200 cases and writes 60-70 opinions a year [S1].
- Says he is committed "to continuing to decide cases fairly and consistently with the law" [S1].
