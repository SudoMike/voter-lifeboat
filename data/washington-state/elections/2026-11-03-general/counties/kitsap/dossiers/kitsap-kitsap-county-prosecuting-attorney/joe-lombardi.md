## Background

- Joe Lombardi (Prefers Democratic Party) has spent the past decade as a Kitsap County deputy prosecuting attorney, a Washington assistant attorney general and a Kitsap County public defender [S1][S2]. He lists no elected experience [S1].
- He says he worked his way up as a prosecutor from misdemeanor to juvenile to felony practice. At the Attorney General's Office he prosecuted in jurisdictions across the state [S4].
- He chairs the Kitsap County Public Defense Advisory Committee on Civil Rights and Social Action [S1].
- Education: J.D. and B.A. in history, University of Washington [S1].
- An endorser statement on his campaign site says he "grew up in this community" [S2].
- Both candidates went to the general without the primary narrowing the field. Lombardi says voters "overwhelmingly favored me as a challenger in the primary" [S4]. This dossier did not verify the vote shares.
- Enright says Lombardi has handled misdemeanor trials, assisted on two murder cases, and tried two felony cases without a supervisor, neither ending in conviction. He also says Lombardi has never supervised staff or managed a budget [S4]. These are the opponent's characterizations.

## Positions

### Management of the Prosecutor's Office
- Says "years of mismanagement and poor leadership" have caused experienced prosecutors to leave, mishandled cases that let violent offenders go free, and violated people's rights [S1][S2].
- Top priority: "eliminate the toxic management culture" that he says has damaged relationships with courts, law enforcement and the public and produced "erratic charging standards, case mismanagement, and abuse of power" [S4].
- Wants more transparency and accountability in the office, rigorous new training for deputy prosecutors, and rebuilt trust with the legal community [S4].
- Says he would "reestablish meaningful victim services which have been eliminated by the current Prosecutor" [S4].

### Public safety and prosecution
- Would personally prosecute the most serious violent offenses and retain experienced trial prosecutors "so dangerous people are no longer released back on our streets" [S1][S2].
- Would expand diversion for low-level and first-time offenders, which he calls "proven to reduce cycles of incarceration and crime," and connect people with mental-health issues to services [S1][S2][S4].
- Would "promote community safety by actually enforcing terms of probation" [S4].

### Immigration and federal enforcement
- "I will keep both ICE and national politics out of county cases" [S1].
- Commits to "prosecuting those who execute unlawful federal enforcement actions in our community" and to policy changes that limit ICE involvement in county cases [S2].

### Campaign money
