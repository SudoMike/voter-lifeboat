## What the office does

The county treasurer receives all money due the county and pays it out on warrants attested by the county auditor, invests county and special-district funds not needed right away, and pays the bonded debt of the county and the special districts it serves [S2].

## Race dynamics

Treasurer Pete Boissonneau (Prefers Democratic Party), in office since 2023, was the only candidate to file [S1][S3]. The contest is uncontested.
