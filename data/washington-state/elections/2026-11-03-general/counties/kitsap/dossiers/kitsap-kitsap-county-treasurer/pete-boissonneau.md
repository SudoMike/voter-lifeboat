## Background

- Pete Boissonneau (Prefers Democratic Party) has been Kitsap County Treasurer since 2023 [S1]. He was elected in November 2022 as the only candidate on the ballot, with 95.79% (the rest write-ins) [S2].
- Earlier in the Treasurer's Office he was chief deputy treasurer (2022) and investment and banking officer (2019-2022). Before that he was an institutional fixed-income broker at Multi-Bank Securities (2009-2018) [S1].
- Education: MBA (2019) and B.S. in international business and finance (2007), University of South Carolina [S1].
- Community service: Port Orchard Rotary Club Foundation chair, instructor at a youth financial-literacy camp, and drumline instructor at Peninsula High School [S1].

## Positions

- Says one of his first priorities was no-fee e-check payments for taxpayers [S1].
- Says he set up an insured cash sweep program that has earned more than $2 million for the county general fund [S1].
- Describes a five-year laddered investment strategy meant to manage interest-rate changes and reduce risk [S1].
- Says he will prioritize stewardship of taxpayer dollars, transparency and accessible customer service [S1].
