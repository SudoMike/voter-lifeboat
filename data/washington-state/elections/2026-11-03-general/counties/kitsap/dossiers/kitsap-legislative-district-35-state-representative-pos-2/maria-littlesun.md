## Background
Maria Littlesun, a Democrat, is a lifelong Shelton resident and a first-time candidate [S1]. She is a descendant of the Skokomish Tribe [S4]. She describes nearly two decades caring for vulnerable people in Mason and Thurston counties as a state-contracted in-home healthcare provider, social-services professional and crisis responder [S4]. She has also worked as a social worker, certified nursing assistant, peer counselor and care coordinator [S2][S3]. She manages the Squaxin Island Tribe's Vulnerable Adult Protection Team and Elders Day Program, among other tribal services [S1][S4]. She trained as a CNA at Centralia College [S1]. She chaired the 2026 Shelton School District levy renewal and is president of the Shelton schools' Indian Education Parent Advisory Committee [S1][S4]. She and her husband are raising two children in Shelton public schools, including an autistic, nonverbal son with Type 1 diabetes, and care for her 87-year-old grandfather, a Marine Corps veteran [S4].

She faces Rep. Travis Couture in the November 3 general [S2]. She did not respond to the Kitsap Daily News questionnaire for its general-election profile [S2].

## Positions
- **Priorities:** "making life more affordable, expanding housing, strengthening our public schools, and increasing access to healthcare" [S1].
- **Taxes and affordability:** will "prioritize tax reforms that remove the heavy burden from struggling families and seniors" and work to lower utility costs [S5]. Wants to expand property-tax relief for seniors and veterans and bolster the Working Families Tax Credit [S5]. No statement found on ESSB 6346, the 2026 tax on income over $1 million, or on I-645.
- **Spending and public investment:** will "fund transit projects that help rural residents," champion "fully funding Special Education," restore funding for Dual Language Learning programs, and increase funding for affordable-housing construction [S5].
- **Housing:** supports "zoning reform to allow for more density near transit and jobs," tenant protections, first-time homebuyer programs and more social housing with wraparound behavioral-health services [S5].
- **Homelessness and public safety:** "I don't believe that encampment sweeps are helpful to solving the root cause"; wants social housing and wraparound medical and behavioral-health services so first responders need not wait until someone is "on their last legs" [S5].
- **Healthcare:** wants insurance reforms, protection of rural communities from clinic loss in healthcare mergers, and legislation that "protects Washington Apple Health insurance and access to Medicaid"; supports telehealth and mobile care for rural areas [S5]. Wants insurers required to provide early-intervention resources for children with disabilities [S5].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "I will protect the right to all aspects of family planning in our state ... protect the right to all forms of contraception, and will ensure medical abortion is an option for families that need it" [S5]. Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates and Washington State Stonewall Democrats [S6].
- **Parents and schools:** no statement found on parental notification, record access or opt-outs. Focuses on special-education resources and school funding [S5][S4].
