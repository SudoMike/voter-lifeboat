## What the office does
A Washington State Representative serves a two-year term in the state House; the 35th District is one of the state's 49 legislative districts and covers Mason County and parts of Thurston and Kitsap counties [S2]. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets: K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The winner takes office in 2027 [S2].

## Race dynamics
Republican Rep. Travis Couture, a Navy veteran and Shelton-area contractor first seated in 2023, is the House Republican budget leader. Democrat Maria Littlesun is a first-time candidate and a social worker and crisis responder who manages Squaxin Island Tribe elder and vulnerable-adult programs [S1][S2]. Littlesun did not answer the Kitsap Daily News questionnaire; the paper reprinted her pamphlet statement [S2]. This package did not find a published August primary tally for this two-candidate race.

Couture campaigns on cost of living, public safety and schools. He opposes the 2026 tax on income over $1 million: he voted no and offered amendments to delay it and to cut a related sales tax on advertising services (travis-couture.md S5, S6, S7). He has also become a public advocate for I-1, saying he drafted the parental-notification language now in law (travis-couture.md S4). Littlesun's platform centers social housing, tenant protections, wraparound services instead of encampment sweeps, special-education funding, Medicaid protection, and contraception and medical-abortion access (maria-littlesun.md S5). She has stated no position on the income tax or the three initiatives. PDC reports show Couture with $110k raised and Littlesun with $82k (travis-couture.md S17; maria-littlesun.md S8).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes and spending:** Couture leads his caucus on the budget, campaigns on spending control and tax relief, and voted against the 2025-26 tax increases. Littlesun proposes targeted tax relief and more spending on special education, transit and affordable housing.
2. **Public safety and homelessness:** Couture backs law enforcement staffing, repeat-offender accountability and encampment cleanup. Littlesun opposes sweeps and favors social housing with services.
3. **Parents and schools:** Couture is campaigning for I-1. Littlesun has no stated position and focuses on special-education resources.
4. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Littlesun pledges to protect contraception and medical-abortion access. Couture's evidence is his 2023 votes and his I-1 remarks.
5. **Housing:** Littlesun supports social housing, tenant protections and density near transit. Couture's site speaks generally of generating more housing.
6. **Record vs. renewal:** a four-year incumbent in caucus leadership against a first-time candidate.
