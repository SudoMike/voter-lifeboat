## Background

- Kitsap County District Court judge since her 2014 election [S1]. She won that race 56.95% to 43.05% over Tracy Flood [S2], and was re-elected unopposed in 2018 and in 2022 (98.3%) [S3][S4].
- Kitsap County deputy prosecutor 1997-2014, including chief deputy prosecutor of the District/Municipal Court Division. She focused on crimes against children, sexual assault and domestic violence cases [S1].
- Former Special Assistant United States Attorney prosecuting violent felons in federal court [S1].
- Chair of the Kitsap District and Municipal Court Practice and Procedure Committee [S1].
- Community service: past chair of Kitsap SAIVS (Special Assault Investigations and Victim's Services), past board member of the Kitsap County DV Task Force, past United Way board president, past president of South Kitsap Rotary, YWCA "Woman of Achievement" [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says she helped create, and now presides over, the county's first mental health court, the Behavioral Health Court. She says it has helped dozens of people through treatment and structure, reducing recidivism [S1].
- Says she strives to consider all parties' rights, listen carefully to every litigant and preside fairly and impartially [S1].
