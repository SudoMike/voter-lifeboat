## Background

- Brien Kennedy (Prefers Democratic Party on the ballot) is a deputy clerk in the Kitsap County Superior Court Clerk's office and a Poulsbo resident; this is his first run for office [S1][S4].
- He says he has been in the clerk's office "just over a year" [S4].
- Earlier career: account executive at Amazon, project manager at Microsoft, director of e-commerce at SmartyPants Vitamins and Nuun Hydration, then seven years running his own growth-consulting practice; he stepped back after a health crisis [S1][S4].
- Education: B.A. in business administration, University of Washington Foster School of Business; first in his family to graduate from college [S1][S4].
- PDC: mini reporting option, no finance reports on file, status "Qualified for general." The PDC registration's party field reads "INDEPENDENT," while the ballot lists him as preferring the Democratic Party [S6][S1].

## Positions

- **Public audit:** pledges a full operational audit of the clerk's office (vendor contracts, staffing, technology spending, process decisions), presented publicly in plain language; says it does not "assume waste going in" and acknowledges the commissioners, not the clerk, set the budget [S3][S1].
- **No added cost:** says his changes come "without more staff or more budget," by "reprioritizing where the office puts its attention" [S2]; describes plain-language guides and video walkthroughs as a "zero-cost initiative" [S3].
- **Access for court users:** wants faster pro se filings, an easy-to-find trial request form, and public information on passports, protection orders and jury duty [S2]; proposes plain-language guides for every service [S1][S3].
- **Accountability:** monthly public Zoom office hours and an annual public report on spending and results [S3][S1].
- **Budget critique:** says the office absorbed a $201,065 cut in 2026, mostly by eliminating two positions, and that its September 2025 budget presentation named a records backlog and morale problems without proposing fixes [S5].
- **Contested elections:** says "Unopposed races must stop" and notes this is the third contested clerk race in 28 years [S2][S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no public position found as of 2026-10-08.
