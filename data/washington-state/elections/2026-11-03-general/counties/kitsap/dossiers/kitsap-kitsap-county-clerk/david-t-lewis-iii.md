## Background

- David T Lewis III (Prefers Democratic Party) was appointed Kitsap County Clerk in 2021 and elected in 2022 [S1].
- He says he has served in the clerk's office for over 41 years, including as the office's domestic violence specialist and as a deputy county clerk [S1].
- He is president of the Washington Association of County Clerks [S1].
- Education: Bremerton High School, Olympic College, Professional Development Academy [S1].
- Community: board president of Kitsap Legal Services, Coffee Oasis board member, youth and adult athletics volunteer [S1].
- PDC: mini reporting option (campaign limited to small fundraising), $100 in reported contributions, status "Qualified for general" [S2].

## Positions

- **Office role:** describes the clerk's office as maintaining official Superior Court records, assisting judges, attorneys and citizens, and providing passport services [S1].
- **Modernization and access:** says he has supported technology such as e-filing, letting people file remotely, "saving time and money and improving access to justice for everyone" [S1].
- **Service:** committed to "a professional, efficient, and customer-focused office that serves all residents with fairness and respect" [S1].
