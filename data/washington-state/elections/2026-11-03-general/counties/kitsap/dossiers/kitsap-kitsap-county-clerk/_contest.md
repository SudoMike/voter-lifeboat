## What the office does

The county clerk is the clerk of the Superior Court. The office keeps the court's dockets, filings, verdicts, judgments, probate and criminal records [S2]. It also assists judges, attorneys and the public, handles jury administration and protection-order filings, and provides passport services [S1].

## Race dynamics

Both candidates prefer the Democratic Party [S1]. Incumbent David T Lewis III, appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022, says he has worked in the clerk's office for over 41 years. He cites e-filing and is endorsed by the Kitsap County Democrats and the 26th LD Democrats [S1]. Challenger Brien Kennedy, a deputy clerk for about a year and a former Amazon/Microsoft and e-commerce executive, says this is only the third contested clerk race in 28 years [S1]. He proposes a public operational audit, plain-language guides, monthly office hours and an annual report, all without added budget (brien-kennedy.md S3). Both use PDC mini reporting, which caps their fundraising at small amounts (david-t-lewis-iii.md S2; brien-kennedy.md S6). No news coverage of this race was found.

## Differentiating issue axes

1. **Experience:** a four-decade office veteran and current clerk, against a challenger who runs explicitly on fresh eyes and private-sector management.
2. **Spending / oversight:** Kennedy proposes a public audit and zero-cost service improvements. Lewis points to e-filing and efficiency without proposing specific oversight measures.
