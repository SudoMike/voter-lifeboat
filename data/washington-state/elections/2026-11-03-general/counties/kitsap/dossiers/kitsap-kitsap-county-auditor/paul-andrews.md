## Background

- Paul Andrews (Prefers Democratic Party) was elected Kitsap County Auditor in 2018 [S1], winning 70.58% to 29.42% over fellow Democrat Richard Huddy [S2].
- He was re-elected in 2022 with 58.54% over Republican Mike Simpson [S3].
- As auditor he is the county's chief election official. He lists roles as co-chair of the State Licensing Committee, secretary of the Kitsap County Finance Committee, past president of the Washington State Association of County Auditors, and state-appointed member of the Recording Standards Commission, the Voting Systems Review Board and the VoteWA Executive Steering Committee [S1].
- Before his election he worked 29 years as an IT professional and Kitsap County data analyst [S1].
- Education: degree in building design; state-certified elections administrator; Certified Election and Registration Administrator (Auburn University); Leadership Kitsap graduate [S1].

## Positions

- Says he has prioritized transparency: making county financial information available, keeping elections open and making historical documents accessible [S1].
- Says he has cross-trained staff "to be able to do more with less" and used technology to provide more services [S1].
