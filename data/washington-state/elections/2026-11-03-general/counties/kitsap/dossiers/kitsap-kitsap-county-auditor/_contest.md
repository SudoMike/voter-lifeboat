## What the office does

The county auditor records deeds and other documents filed in the county, keeps the county's accounts with the treasurer, registers warrants, and publishes the county's annual financial statements [S2]. The auditor is also ex officio supervisor of every primary and election in the county [S3].

## Race dynamics

Auditor Paul Andrews (Prefers Democratic Party), first elected in 2018, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. In 2022 he was re-elected with 58.54% over Republican Mike Simpson [S4]. The contest is uncontested.
