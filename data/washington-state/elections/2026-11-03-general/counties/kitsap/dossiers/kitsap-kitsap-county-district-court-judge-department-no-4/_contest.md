## What the office does

Kitsap County District Court has criminal jurisdiction over misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors committed in the county (up to one year in jail and a $5,000 fine unless a statute says otherwise), hears traffic infractions, conducts preliminary hearings, and approves bail and arraigns defendants [S2]. State law sets Kitsap's minimum at four district judges [S3]. The race appears on the ballot countywide [S1].

## Race dynamics

Judge Kevin P Kelly, appointed to Department 4 in 2017, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. He was re-elected unopposed in 2022 with 98.38% (the rest write-ins) [S4]. The contest is uncontested.
