## Background

- Appointed Kitsap County District Court judge, Department 4, on June 30, 2017 [S1]. Elected unopposed in 2018 and re-elected unopposed in 2022 (98.38%) [S2][S3].
- Kitsap County deputy prosecuting attorney for 27 years. His posts included chief deputy of the District/Municipal Court Division (2015-2017), senior deputy in the Administration Unit (2010-2015) and the Felony Drug Unit (2006-2010) [S1].
- Education: B.A., Kent State University (1984); J.D., Cleveland Marshall College of Law (1988) [S1].
- Community service: Rotary member for more than 30 years and two-time president of the Rotary Club of South Kitsap; former executive board member of the Kitsap Humane Society; Leadership Kitsap graduate [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says he was the county's drug court prosecutor for many years and was on the team that created the Veterans Treatment Court and the Behavioral Health Court. He says he understands "the Therapeutic Court model" [S1].
- As a deputy prosecutor he trained as a certified handler to bring the county's first courthouse dog, Kerris, to ease anxiety for people, often children, appearing in court [S1].
