## Background

- Lists her current role as District Court judge [S1]. The date she joined the bench is not given in the voters' guide and was not found.
- Former municipal judge pro tem in Kitsap, Pierce and Mason counties; Bremerton city prosecutor and assistant city attorney; criminal defense attorney as a public defender and in private practice. She has litigated in municipal, state and federal court [S1].
- Past president of the Kitsap County Bar Association; Washington State Bar Association continuing-education instructor; chair of the Kitsap County Land Use Planning Commission [S1].
- Education: J.D., Seattle University School of Law; B.A., University of Washington; A.A., Green River College [S1].
- Community service: YMCA mock trial volunteer, Kitsap County Youth Court mentor, Central Stage Theatre board vice president, YWCA Woman of Achievement in Public Service [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Says public trust in courts rests on "accountability, equal justice, and integrity." She says she has worked to keep the system "fair, independent, and accessible to everyone" and to use technology to make courts easier to navigate [S1].
