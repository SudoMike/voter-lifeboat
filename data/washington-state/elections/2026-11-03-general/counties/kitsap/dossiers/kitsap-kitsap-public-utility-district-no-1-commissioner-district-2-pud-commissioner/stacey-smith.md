## Background

- KPUD commissioner for District 2 (South Kitsap) since a 2025 appointment; the appointed term runs through 2026 [S1][S2]. She says she is seeking election to the next six-year term and that District 2 covers South Kitsap, Gorst and West Bremerton [S3].
- In her 11th year as Kitsap County's Director of Aging and Long-Term Care. Earlier work was in mental health, children's mental health services, family therapy and private practice. She is a licensed counselor [S1][S3].
- Education: B.A. in sociology, University of Washington; M.A. in education, City University [S1].
- Former member of the Kitsap County Department of Community Development Planning Commission and the Kitsap County Youth Diversion Board; current board member of the Washington Association of Area Agencies on Aging [S1].
- As commissioner she also serves on the Kitsap Economic Development Alliance and the Public Utilities Insurance Association [S3].
- Spouse of a retired Navy veteran; Kitsap resident for more than 50 years [S1][S3].

## Positions

- Says that since her appointment she has supported water infrastructure and conservation projects, improved safe drinking water access, and secured funding to expand fiber internet in underserved areas [S1].
- Priorities: "fair publicly owned utility rates," "equitable broadband access," clean drinking water, wastewater support and community education about KPUD [S1][S3].
- Supports KPUD's open-access, community-owned fiber network and expanding broadband across District 2 with public funds [S3].
- Wants KPUD to work more closely with other water and wastewater providers "to develop regional systems as existing infrastructure ages" [S3].
- KPUD Proposition 1 (electric authority): as board secretary she signed Resolution 26-07-28A, which placed the measure on the November ballot [S4]. No personal position for or against the measure was found in her statement or on her website [S1][S3].
