## What the office does

Kitsap Public Utility District (KPUD) runs drinking water systems serving more than 35,000 customers, wastewater plants at Port Gamble and Bainbridge Island, an open-access fiber network and countywide water-resource monitoring [S4]. A three-member elected board approves the budget, sets rates and oversees operations; commissioners serve six-year terms and must live in their district [S3][S2]. This year the board also placed KPUD Proposition 1, which asks voters to authorize KPUD to build or acquire electric facilities, on the same ballot [S4].

## Race dynamics

- **Who votes.** Only District 2 voters vote in a PUD primary, but "voters of the entire public utility district may vote at a general election" [S2]. KPUD serves the whole county, so every Kitsap voter sees this race [S1][S4].
- **Uncontested.** Commissioner Stacey Smith, appointed in 2025 to a term that runs through 2026, is the only candidate [S1][S3]. Jim Civilla held the District 2 seat and was re-elected unopposed in 2020 [S5].
