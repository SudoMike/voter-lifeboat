## Chad M. Enright | Countywide Prosecuting Attorney | party: Prefers Democratic Party | ballot counties: Kitsap | website: electchadenright.com | email: electchadenright@gmail.com
Elected Experience: Kitsap County Prosecutor since 2019.
Other Professional Experience: I began practicing law in 2004, joined the Prosecutor’s Office in 2008, and was previously Chief Deputy Prosecutor - Felony & Juvenile Division. I am a WA Minority and Justice Commissioner, hold multiple roles with WA Association of Prosecuting Attorneys, and am past president of the Kitsap Bar Association.
Education: North Kitsap High School, BA from University of Washington, JD from Seattle University.
Community Service: I am a Board Member for Kitsap Community Resources and Peninsula Services. I serve on the NK Recovery Resource Center Operational Workgroup, Police and Community Together, and Substance Use Recovery Services Advisory Committee.
Statement: I have dedicated my career to prosecuting criminals, fighting for justice, and keeping Kitsap County safe. I’m running for re-election to continue this critical work.
My team is addressing crime at the source and improving our community through creation of the North Kitsap Recovery Resource Center and pre-arrest diversion program, and expansion of Kitsap THRIVE Court, Washington State’s first Girls Court, and the Tribe’s Welcome Home Program. We are saving money through our new civil litigation team, paperless office, and zero case backlogs, and all of our prosecutor positions have been filled. Currently we are creating a real time data website so the public can see cases filed and resolved. We will also become the first county in Washington to use Race Blind Charging, redacting race-identifying information from police reports.
I’m endorsed by every local Democratic elected official, the Kitsap County Democrats, and Kitsap County Deputy Prosecuting Attorney Guild.
Kitsap is the community I grew up in, raised my family, and have devoted my 22+ year legal career. My legal background, government experience, and community service uniquely qualify me to lead. I will always strive to build a better place for all and I look forward to earning your support.
