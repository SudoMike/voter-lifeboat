Professional Experience: I served our country aboard the USS Nebraska as a Navy nuclear missile technician, settling in Kitsap County. For nearly a decade, I has negotiated home sale values and analyzed complex housing data daily. My deep background in advanced data analytics and property valuation uniquely equips me to bring a modern, technical, and highly accurate approach to the Kitsap County Assessor’s office.
Statement: As your Kitsap County Assessor, my mission isn’t to increase county revenue; it’s to ensure that the revenue we do collect is balanced fairly and accurately across every single neighborhood. Outdated assessment models create an unbalanced burden, where working-class neighborhoods often pay more than their fair share while high-value properties lag behind. That is an equity issue.
I am running to bring accountability, transparency, and modernization to the Assessor’s office. My platform focuses on utilizing advanced data analytics to correct neighborhood-level imbalances, ensuring no community is overtaxed to compensate for under-assessments elsewhere.
Furthermore, I will focus on accessibility by creating user-friendly online tools so every resident can easily understand how their property was valued and navigate the appeals process. This includes expanding direct outreach for seniors, veterans, disabled, and lower-income residents to ensure they are utilizing every tax exemption and deferral program allowed under Washington State law, helping long-term residents stay in their homes.
My approach to the Assessor’s office involves ensuring the system is fair, accurate, and transparent. I ask for your vote to bring true balance and fairness to Kitsap County.
