## Paul Andrews | Countywide Auditor | party: Prefers Democratic Party | ballot counties: Kitsap | website: electpaulandrews.com | email: electpaulandrews@gmail.com
Elected Experience: Elected Kitsap County Auditor in 2018.
Other Professional Experience: Along with being the chief election official for Kitsap County, Paul is Co-Chair of the State Licensing Committee, Secretary of the Kitsap County Finance Committee, Past President of the Washington State Association of County Auditors, a state appointed member of the Recording Standards Commission, Voting Systems Review Board, and the VoteWA Executive Steering Committee. Prior to being elected, Paul’s experience included 29 years as an IT professional and Kitsap County Data Analyst.
Education: Degree in Building Design. State Certified Elections Administrator. Certified Election and Registration Administrator (CERA) through Auburn University. Leadership Kitsap graduate.
