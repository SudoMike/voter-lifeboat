Statement
Greg Nance is honored to represent Kitsap, the community that raised him. He's fighting to fix our ferries, protect Puget Sound, and support working families. He worked across the aisle to secure $895 million for ferry construction, maintenance, and workforce. He passed legislation protecting kids from fentanyl, expanding affordable childcare, and bolstering Olympic College healthcare programs. He secured $42 million to modernize West Sound Tech and helped protect 680 acres of Kitsap forest from clearcutting.
Supported by: Suquamish Tribe and Port Gamble S'Klallam Tribe; Washington State Labor Council (representing 600 unions and 500,000 members) — including teachers, nurses, and firefighters.
