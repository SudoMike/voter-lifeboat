## Background

Jack Griffin Jr., of Valley, is a first-time candidate for public office (S1, S3). He says he spent 40 years "working across unions, Fortune 500 companies, big tech, and healthcare," and that his family has always been in Valley, where he has returned "for good" (S1). He attended Auburn High School and Green River College and is an active member of the Society for Creative Anachronism (S1). He states no party preference (S1). His statement names no employers or job titles (S1).

In the district-only August 4 primary he received 779 votes (17.12%) (S2). He did not answer questions at the July candidate forum in Colville (S3). He reports to the PDC under the mini-reporting option, so no contribution totals are published (S4).
