## What it does

Proposition No. 1 would set Stevens County Fire Protection District No. 10's existing regular property tax levy at $0.75 per $1,000 of assessed value in 2026 for collection in 2027, under Resolution No. 2-2026 (S1). The 2026 maximum allowable levy then becomes the base for later levy limits under chapter 84.55 RCW (S1). The money would fund "acquisition, maintenance, and operation of fire protection equipment and facilities": emergency fire vehicles, fire stations and trained, equipped personnel (S1).

The district covers the Aladdin Road/Spirit area northeast of Colville (WA DOR FIR2025 polygon `10`; point-checked at 2785 Aladdin Rd, Colville; builder notes).

## Cost

- $0.75 per $1,000 of assessed value for 2027 collection, up from the current $0.54 (S1).
- The explanatory statement: property owners "would pay an addition $21.00 per year ($1.75 per month) per $100,000 of assessed value" (S1).

## Arguments for

No statement for was submitted (S1). The board's explanatory statement, prepared by district attorney Brian K. Snure, says the levy and its revenue are "necessary to maintain the current level of fire protection, fire suppression and emergency services while providing for the safety of our firefighters" (S1).

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted (S1). No organized opposition was found.
