## Background

- Thomas Franklin Webster was appointed by the governor to Position 2 of the Superior Court for Stevens, Ferry and Pend Oreille counties and sworn in on January 23, 2026, after Judge Jessica Taylor Reeves retired on December 31 [S1][S2]. Reeves was elected to the seat unopposed in 2020 [S3].
- He has practiced law since 2000: partner at Tingle, Knight, Webster and Juckette in Iowa (2000-2006), associate at McGrane & Schuerman in Colville (2006-2009), and owner of Webster Law Office (2009-2025), focused on personal injury and family law [S1][S2].
- Education: Cornell College (environmental science and history, 1996); J.D., Drake University Law School (2000) [S1].
- Colville Rotary member 2007-2025; lives in Evans [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Said the position is nonpartisan and he will apply "a strict interpretation of the law, not for any partisan gain," following stare decisis and statutory law until the Legislature changes it [S2].
- Named access to justice for low-income and rural residents as the biggest challenge in the three counties [S2].
- His statement says his civil-litigation background "adds a depth to the current bench" for people in court for reasons other than criminal prosecution [S1].
