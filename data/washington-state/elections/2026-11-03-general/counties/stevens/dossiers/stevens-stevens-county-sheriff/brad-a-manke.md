## Background

- Brad A. Manke, of Colville, prefers the Republican Party and has served two terms as Stevens County Sheriff [S1].
- He reports 29.5 years as a commissioned officer, 28 with the Sheriff's Office, and is vice president of the Washington State Sheriffs' Association; he has a BA in criminal justice and 20 years as a member of the Colville/Stevens County Fire District 3 department [S1].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 (97.97%) [S2]; 9,984 votes (97.53%) in the 2026 primary [S3]. Mini PDC reporting [S5].

## Positions

- Goals for the next term: more deputies (he cites 0.88 officers per thousand residents in unincorporated Stevens County, against a state average of 1.37), succession planning, and getting the new jail built and open [S1].
- One of four Eastern Washington sheriffs who sued over Senate Bill 5974 (new eligibility standards for sheriffs); a Thurston County judge preliminarily blocked several provisions, including removal of a sheriff by a governor-appointed board, which Manke said "bypass[es] the vote of the people" [S4].
