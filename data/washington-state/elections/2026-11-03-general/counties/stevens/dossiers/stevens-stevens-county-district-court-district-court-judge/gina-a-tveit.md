## Background

- Gina A. Tveit has been Stevens County District Court Judge since 2009 and reports more than 30 years of legal experience in Stevens County as a prosecutor, attorney and judge [S1].
- She graduated from Colville High School, Gonzaga University and Gonzaga School of Law, and attended the Washington State Judicial College [S1].
- Election history: won in 2010 with 70.78% over Ronnie Rae [S2], in 2014 with 62.15% over Michael E. Clay [S3], in 2018 with 61.83% over Lech Radzimski [S4], and unopposed in 2022 (98.09%) [S5].
- She reports to the PDC under the mini-reporting option; the summary shows $2,260.96 raised and spent as of September 27, 2026 [S6].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- "When voting for judges, experience matters" [S1].
- Says she has "worked tirelessly to keep our community safe by holding offenders accountable and using taxpayer resources responsibly" [S1].
- Says she helped develop the Stevens County Veterans Court, Therapeutic Drug Court, 24/7 alcohol monitoring and expanded probation services, "all aimed at protecting our citizens and supporting rehabilitation" [S1].
- Pledges to serve "with honor, independence and dedication" and "a steadfast commitment to the rule of law" [S1].
