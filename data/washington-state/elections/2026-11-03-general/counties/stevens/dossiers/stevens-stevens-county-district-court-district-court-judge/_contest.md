## What the office does
Stevens County District Court is a county-wide court of limited jurisdiction; this ballot elects its one listed judgeship (S1). It hears misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors, traffic infractions and other violations, conducts preliminary hearings and sets bail (S2); Irwin described it at the July forum as "a low level, people's court" for DUIs, low-level assaults, tickets and citations and name changes (S3).

## Race dynamics
Judge Gina A. Tveit, on the bench since 2009 and unopposed in 2022 (S1, S4), faces Colville attorney C. Olivia Irwin, who says she has practiced for 12 years (S3). The seat is nonpartisan and with two candidates was not on the August primary ballot. Irwin submitted nothing to the voters' guide (S1); Tveit did not attend the July forum, where Irwin spoke (S3).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience/record:** a 17-year incumbent who cites drug, veterans and alcohol-monitoring programs she helped build, against a challenger with no judicial experience.
2. **Safety:** Tveit pairs holding offenders accountable with therapeutic courts and probation services; Irwin's public remarks (access to justice, a "sliding scale," the justice center) do not state a sentencing or bail approach.
3. Judicial philosophy: neither candidate's sources state one.
