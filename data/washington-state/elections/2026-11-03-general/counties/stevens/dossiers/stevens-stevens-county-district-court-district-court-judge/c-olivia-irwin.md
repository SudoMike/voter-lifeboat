## Background

- C. Olivia Irwin (Christal Olivia Irwin in PDC records) of Colville submitted no statement, experience or education to the 2026 voters' guide [S1][S3].
- At the July 9 candidate forum in Colville she said she has been an attorney for 12 years, is "an older graduate of law school," and that her husband is an attorney and a deputy prosecutor [S2].
- No bar biography, employer, bar association rating or case record was found in the sources reviewed. The Washington State Bar's lawyer directory could not be read by script.
- PDC lists her campaign with no reporting option or totals as of September 22, 2026 [S3].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Described District Court as "a low level, people's court that handles DUIs (Driving Under the Influence), low level assaults, tickets and citations, name changes," and said "I'm a proponent of the sliding scale" (the article does not say what the sliding scale applies to) [S2].
- Said her experience "has given me empathy, moral fortitude, and integrity," and that she wants "to expand access to justice," with "strong advocacy for the justice center" and attention to "fiscal issues" [S2].
