## Background

- Erika George, of Colville, prefers the Republican Party and has been Stevens County Prosecuting Attorney since 2023 [S1].
- She was a deputy prosecutor from 2015 to 2022 (child support enforcement, juvenile, appeals, public disclosure, and felony prosecution exclusively from 2016) and lists earlier civil, family and criminal defense practice [S1].
- Education: Kettle Falls High School; BA, University of Washington; law degree, University of Idaho; LL.M. in criminal trial, California Western School of Law [S1].
- Won the office in 2022 with 12,370 votes (60.17%) over Geoff Kristianson [S2]; 9,595 votes (97.37%) in the 2026 primary [S3]. Mini PDC reporting [S4].

## Positions

- Says she has a reputation as "tough, but fair," and worked in her first term to "change ... culture, rebuild relationships, fight for victims and the community and bringing the focus of this office back to the prosecution of crime," holding people accountable while using tools "to combat the root of criminal behavior" [S1].
