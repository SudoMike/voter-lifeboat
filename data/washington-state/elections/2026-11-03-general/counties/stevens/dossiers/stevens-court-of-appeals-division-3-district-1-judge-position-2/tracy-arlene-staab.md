## Background

- Tracy Arlene Staab submitted no statement, experience or education to the 2026 voters' guide [S1].
- She ran for this open seat in 2020 against attorney Marshall Casey, to replace retiring Judge Kevin Korsmo [S2]. In Stevens County she received 12,829 votes to Casey's 10,202 [S3].
- Before that she was a Spokane Municipal Court judge from 2009, and earlier an appellate attorney (including attorney of record in 70 Ninth Circuit cases) and a Court of Appeals law clerk; she was admitted to the bar in 1993 [S2].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- In 2020 she stressed her criminal-law background, since many of the court's cases are criminal, and called the written briefs the most important part of an appeal [S2].
