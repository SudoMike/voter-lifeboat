## Background

Roseanne Ennis Jezerinac lives in the Suncrest area of southern Stevens County with her husband and seven children (S2). She is a deputy medicolegal death investigator with the Spokane County Medical Examiner's Office and a former Stevens County deputy coroner, and says she has investigated more than 1,600 deaths (S1, S2). Earlier she was a funeral director and embalmer and spent eight years in laboratory medicine and women's health as an operations and client-relations manager (S1, S2). She holds a mortuary science degree and is board certified by the American Board of Medicolegal Death Investigators, the Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission and the International Conference of Funeral Service Examining Boards, with Department of Justice training in signs of hidden homicide and strangulation (S1, S2). She guest lectures at Lakeside Middle School and Ridgeline High School (S1, S2). She prefers the Republican Party (S1).

In the August 4 primary she received 2,911 votes (25.61%) (S3).

## Positions

### Prevention and public health
- "As Coroner, I will bring prevention and education to the forefront"; she describes the coroner's duty as protecting "the wellbeing and lives of our community," not only exonerating the innocent and prosecuting the guilty (S1, S2).
- Names fentanyl overdose deaths, suicide prevention and mental-health services, the missing and murdered Indigenous people crisis, violence, and risky behavior as subjects for education built on death-investigation findings (S1, S2).
- Lists education initiatives on overdose prevention, impaired driving, gun and water safety and infant safe sleep (S2).

### Office management and budget
- Will "add transparency to the operations of the Coroner's Office" and better report cause- and manner-of-death statistics to families and community leaders (S1).
- Would "increase funds for autopsy services from the current budget" (S1), which her site describes as increasing "the allocation of funds for autopsy services from the currently available budget" (S2).
- Says she "will not split my time between counties or hold multiple full-time roles" (S2).
