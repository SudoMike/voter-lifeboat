## Background

- Michelle Enright, of Colville, prefers the Republican Party and says she is in her fifth year as Stevens County Clerk [S1].
- She reports more than 32 years in county government, 20 of them in the Clerk's Office, plus 911 dispatch, Auditor's Office licensing, Prosecutor's Office legal support and clerk of the board for the commissioners [S1].
- Re-elected unopposed in 2022 (98.48%) [S2]; 9,606 votes (97.84%) in the 2026 primary [S3]. Mini PDC reporting [S4].

## Positions

- Says her office has digitized decades of microfilmed records, implemented electronic filing, improved jury management and joined the State Digital Archives [S1].
- Is working with Superior Court and the Stevens County Rural Library District to expand electronic filing and video appearances for residents in remote areas [S1].
