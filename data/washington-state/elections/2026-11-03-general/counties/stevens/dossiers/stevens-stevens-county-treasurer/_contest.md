## What the office does
The treasurer collects property taxes, invests and accounts for county and district funds, and pays county warrants.

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Chief Deputy Assessor Toni Graham is the only candidate for the open seat; Treasurer Leslie Valz, elected in 2022, is not on the ballot (S1, S2).
