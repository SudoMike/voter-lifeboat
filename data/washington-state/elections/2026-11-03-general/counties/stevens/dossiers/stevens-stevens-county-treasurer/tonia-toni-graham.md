## Background

- Tonia "Toni" Graham, of Colville, prefers the Republican Party and has not held elected office [S1].
- She is Stevens County's Chief Deputy Assessor and previously worked in the Treasurer's Office as a cashier and real estate excise tax affidavit specialist; she lists work at NorthEast Washington Alliance Behavioral Health, ownership of an energy auditing company, and administrative management in the U.S. Marine Corps [S1].
- Bachelor's degree in recreation and tourism with a business minor [S1].
- The incumbent, Leslie Valz, was elected in 2022 [S2] and is not a 2026 candidate [S1]. Graham received 9,402 votes (97.93%) in the 2026 primary [S3]. Mini PDC reporting [S4].

## Positions

- Promises a treasurer who is "accessible, accurate, and committed to doing the right thing," and says the campaign is "about putting taxpayers first" [S1].
