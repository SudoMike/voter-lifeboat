Statement
I was raised in Stevens County. My father was an Army veteran who went on to work in law enforcement and retired from the Stevens County Sheriff’s Office after a career spanning over 26 years. My mother has worked as a nurse in Colville for more than 30 years.
Stevens County is my home and I joined the Stevens County Prosecutor's Office in 2017.
From day one, I have fought for the citizens of Stevens County. I have garnered a reputation of being tough, but fair. I am hard working, focused, and dedicated to my job and this community.
I have fostered a working relationship with law enforcement, victim’s advocates, defense attorneys and court staff.
During my first term, I worked hard to promote a change in culture, rebuild relationships, fight for victims and the community and bringing the focus of this office back to the prosecution of crime. I held individuals accountable while also using the tools at my disposal to combat the root of criminal behavior. I look forward to continuing the work I've started, and I ask for your vote!
