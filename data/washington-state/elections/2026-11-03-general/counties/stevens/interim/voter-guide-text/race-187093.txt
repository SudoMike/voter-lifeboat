Statement
Mr. Webster has been practicing law, primarily in civil litigation, since 2000. In 2006 he moved to Colville to be near his family who reside here. He worked as an attorney for McGrane and Schuerman from 2006-2009 handling primarily civil litigation. In 2009 he opened Webster Law Office PLLC and focused primarily on Personal Injury and Family Law litigation. In January of 2026 he was appointed to the Superior Court for the Counties of Stevens, Ferry, and Pend Oreille.
Mr. Webster is committed to serving his community with years of experience in and out of the courtroom. His emphasis in civil litigation adds a depth to the current bench which will directly affect the people within these communities who are before the court for reasons other than criminal prosecution.
Mr. Webster lives in Evans with his wife and two children, as well as several family pets. He is an avid fly fisherman and enjoys camping and hiking as often as he is able. He often remarks that he is blessed to live where he would vacation. He has been truly honored by his appointment and looks forward to continuing to serve his community as a Judge of the Superior Court.
