Statement
When voting for judges, experience matters. It has been my honor and privilege to serve as your District Court Judge since 2009. As a wife, mother, and grandmother, I care deeply about preserving the quality of life and strong values that make our county great. On the bench, I’ve worked tirelessly to keep our community safe by holding offenders accountable and using taxpayer resources responsibly. I’ve helped develop essential programs like the Stevens County Veterans Court, Therapeutic Drug Court, 24/7 alcohol monitoring, and expanded probation services—all aimed at protecting our citizens and supporting rehabilitation. Public servants should lead with integrity, strength and a steadfast commitment to the rule of law. I will continue serving with honor, independence and dedication to our community. Thank you for your trust and support.
