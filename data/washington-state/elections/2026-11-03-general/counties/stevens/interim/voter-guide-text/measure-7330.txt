## ExplanatoryStatement
Proposition 1 would allow Nine Mile Falls School District to replace an existing educational programs and operations levy expiring in 2027. The replacement levy will be used to pay expenses of educational programs and operations not funded by the State, including: staffing and supports for safety, music and fine arts, nurses, counselors, class size reduction, athletics and extracurricular activities, certificated and classified staffing and salaries, enrichment activities, transportation, summer activities, maintenance and operations, technology, nutrition services, and special education. Further information at: www.9mile.org.

The proposed three-year replacement levy would authorize the collection of up to $4,354,837 in 2028, $4,428,827 in 2029 and $4,504,075 in 2030. The levy rate required to produce these amounts is estimated to be $2.10 per $1,000 of assessed value. The exact levy rates and amounts may be adjusted based upon the actual assessed value of the taxable property within the District and the limitations imposed by State law at the time of the levy. Exemptions from taxes may be available, contact your county assessor.
