Statement
As a United States Marine Corps veteran, I built my career on administrative management, financial accountability, and serving others before personal gain. That foundation has carried me through every role since — from running my own energy auditing company to nine years of county government service — and it is the same foundation I will bring to the Stevens County Treasurer’s Office.
I have served in the Treasurer’s Office as a Cashier/Real Estate Excise Tax Affidavit Specialist, managed fiduciary payee responsibilities and accounts receivable at NorthEast Washington Alliance Behavioral Health, and currently serve as Chief Deputy Assessor — overseeing property valuation, levy administration, compliance, and office operations county wide.
Across every role, the common thread has been the same: managing public or client funds accurately, transparently, and with a deep sense of responsibility to the people I serve from Northport to Nine Mile Falls.
Stevens County deserves a Treasurer who is accessible, accurate, and committed to doing the right thing — even when no one is watching. This campaign is about putting taxpayers first and keeping the public trust this office was built on.
