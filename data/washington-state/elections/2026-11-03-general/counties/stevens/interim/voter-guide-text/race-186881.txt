Statement

I am honored to seek reelection as Auditor.

As Auditor, I carry responsibility for some of the county’s most important public functions, including elections, financial accountability, recording and preservation of official documents, vehicle and vessel licensing services, and other complex administrative operations affecting Stevens County citizens. The Auditor’s Office faithfully administers and enforces Washington law as written. Voters should carefully consider the qualifications, judgment, leadership, and experience necessary to manage these responsibilities.

Throughout my time in office, I have focused on improving services, increasing efficiency, strengthening election integrity, security, and administration, and providing responsive customer service while keeping costs low.

Under my leadership, the Auditor’s Office expanded online access to historical recorded documents, launched a notification program to help protect property owners from deed fraud, improved flexibility through cross-training, reduced licensing wait times through personalized appointments, and maintained and expanded licensing access through subagency services.

My administration emphasizes election integrity and security, clean audits, precise financial reconciliation, and voter verification procedures for every lawful ballot received.

I respectfully ask for your vote and continued confidence.

I remain committed to transparent, professional, and lawful administration that produces accurate results voters can trust and independently verify for the next four years.
