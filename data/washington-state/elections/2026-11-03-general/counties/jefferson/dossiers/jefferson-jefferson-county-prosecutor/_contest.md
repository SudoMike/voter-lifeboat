## What the office does

The prosecuting attorney prosecutes crimes in the county's courts and is legal adviser to county officials and boards [S2]; the office is partisan with a four-year term [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Prosecuting Attorney James Kennedy, in office since 2019, is the only candidate [S1].
