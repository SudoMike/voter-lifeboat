## Background

- Jefferson County Prosecuting Attorney since 2019; his whole legal career has been as a county prosecutor [S1][S2].
- Nearly six years in the U.S. Army, including two tours in Iraq; B.A. in history, Ithaca College; J.D., Seattle University School of Law [S1].
- Serves on the advisory committee of the Jefferson County Children's Advocacy Center [S1].
- Prefers the Democratic Party [S1].
- Unopposed in the primary, with 98.2% of the vote against write-ins [S3].
- His PDC registration reports no contributions or spending [S4].

## Positions

- Has focused on prosecuting crimes against vulnerable victims and helped create the county's first Children's Advocacy Center [S1].
- Says public safety "also means addressing the underlying causes of crime"; has worked to expand rehabilitative services and helped start a pilot bringing restorative justice into the therapeutic courts, to "improve accountability, reduce recidivism" [S1].
