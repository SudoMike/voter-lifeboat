## What the office does

The treasurer receives and pays out all money due the county [S2] and safeguards public funds for special purpose districts and county operations [S1]; the office is partisan with a four-year term [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Treasurer Stacie Prada, in office since 2015, is the only candidate [S1].

## Differentiating issue axes

None: there is no opponent on the ballot.
