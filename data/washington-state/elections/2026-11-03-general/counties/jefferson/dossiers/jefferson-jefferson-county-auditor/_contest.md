## What the office does

The auditor keeps the county's recorded documents and accounts and supervises elections in the county [S2][S3]; the office is partisan with a four-year term [S1][S4].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Lauralee Kiesel, who has worked 21 years in the Assessor's Office, is the only candidate and pledges 'a smooth transition' [S1].

## Differentiating issue axes

None: there is no opponent on the ballot.
