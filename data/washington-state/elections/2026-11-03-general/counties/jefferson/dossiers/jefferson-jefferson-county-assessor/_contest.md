## What the office does

The assessor lists and values all taxable real and personal property in the county for property taxes [S2]; the office is partisan with a four-year term [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Chuck Garland, a residential appraiser in the Assessor's Office who has not held elected office, is the only candidate [S1].

None: there is no opponent on the ballot.
