## What this office does

Jefferson PUD's three elected commissioners govern the public utility that provides electric, water, sewer and broadband service in the county [S1][S3]. District 2 covers Cape George, Marrowstone Island, Port Hadlock, Chimacum and Irondale [S2]; the seat is a six-year term [S6], and every county voter can vote in November [S4][S5].

## Race dynamics

- Open seat: incumbent Kenneth Collins of Marrowstone Island is not on the ballot [S2][S1].
- No primary was needed with two candidates [S1].
- Keith Kisler, a Chimacum farmer and Finnriver co-founder, and Michael Brittain, a Port Hadlock computer-business owner and Navy veteran, both first-time candidates [S1].
- At the Sept. 30 League of Women Voters forum both backed diversifying water and power sources, adding wind, solar and battery storage, treating broadband as essential, and keeping data centers off the Peninsula. Kisler stressed conservation, local generation and affordability programs; Brittain stressed faster customer service and a review of the PUD's charges [S3].
