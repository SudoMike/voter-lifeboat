## Background

- Born in Spokane and raised in Kennewick; earned an associate degree through Running Start at Columbia Basin College, studied computer engineering at the University of Washington, then served in the Navy aboard the carriers USS Abraham Lincoln and USS Carl Vinson (2003-2007) [S1].
- Moved to Port Townsend in 2007 as a wireless network engineer on government contracts; founded Hadlock Computer Services in 2012 and runs computer stores in Port Hadlock and Sequim [S1][S3][S4].
- Community service: internships and worker retraining since 2012 [S1].
- No prior elected office [S1].
- Reported $1,000 raised and nothing spent as of the PDC report updated Oct. 2, 2026 [S5].

## Positions

### Customer service and transparency

- Running because "our community deserves a representative who listens, communicates openly"; promises "reliable infrastructure, responsible planning, and transparent leadership" [S1].
- Wants faster, accurate answers when customers contact the PUD: "There's no reason with today's technology that we should be waiting a week for an answer" [S4].

### Rates and affordability

- Wants to review all of the PUD's current charges to make sure it can support low-income residents [S4].

### Power supply and data centers

- Supports looking at many energy sources, wind, solar and battery storage; singled out the ocean as "a massive and unending source of power" [S4].
- Does not see data centers as "reasonable" for the area, joking he might consider one that paid $100 million for new infrastructure [S4].
- His campaign site stresses energy efficiency and capacity [S3].

### Water and broadband

- Favors expanding the PUD's reservoir system and possibly deepening wells [S4].
- Calls broadband essential and says his computer-repair work shows him where access fails [S4].

## Endorsements

- None found; his campaign site lists none [S3].
