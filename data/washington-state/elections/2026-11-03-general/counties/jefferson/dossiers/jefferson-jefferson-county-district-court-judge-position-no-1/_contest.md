## What the office does

Jefferson County District Court hears misdemeanor and gross misdemeanor criminal cases [S2]; its single judge is elected county-wide to a four-year term [S3][S4].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Judge Mindy Walker, the court's elected judge since 2019, is the only candidate [S1].
