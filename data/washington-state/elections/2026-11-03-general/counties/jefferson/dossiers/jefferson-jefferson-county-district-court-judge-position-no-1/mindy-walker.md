## Background

- Elected Jefferson County District Court Judge since 2019 and its presiding judge [S1].
- Formerly a judge pro tem and a partner at Ramirez and Walker, PLLC, practicing in federal, tribal and Washington courts [S1].
- J.D., Gonzaga Law; studied international law and political studies at Trinity University [S1].
- Board of the District and Municipal Court Judges Association; therapeutic court committee; co-chair of a diversity committee; continuing judicial education presenter [S1].
- Her PDC registration reports no contributions or spending [S3].

## Record

- Says the court moved nearly every type of hearing to Zoom availability after COVID-19, improving public access and interpretation services [S1].
- Champions the county's Behavioral Health Court, a collaboration of mental health, housing, substance use treatment, prosecutors and law enforcement that she says reduces recidivism; wants therapeutic court services expanded [S1].
