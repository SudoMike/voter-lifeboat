## What the office does

The county clerk is clerk of the Superior Court, keeping its records and files and receiving fees and funds paid into court [S2]; the office is partisan with a four-year term [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested. Clerk Amanda Hamilton, in office since 2023, is the only candidate [S1].

None: there is no opponent on the ballot.
