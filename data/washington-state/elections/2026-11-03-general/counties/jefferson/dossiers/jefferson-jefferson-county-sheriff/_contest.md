## What the office does

The sheriff is the county's chief law enforcement officer, keeping the peace, making arrests and serving court process [S2]; the office is partisan with a four-year term [S1][S4].

## Race dynamics

Only one name on the ballot: Sheriff Andy Pernsteiner, appointed in 2025 [S1]. A write-in candidate, Crystal L. Cox, has registered with the Public Disclosure Commission for the office [S3].

## Differentiating issue axes

None: there is no opponent on the ballot.
