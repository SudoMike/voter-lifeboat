## Background

- Appointed interim sheriff by the Board of County Commissioners on Feb. 24, 2025, after Sheriff Joe Nole's retirement in January; he had been Nole's undersheriff for six years [S1][S5].
- With the Sheriff's Office for 28 years; earlier four years in the Marine Corps and two as a security police officer at Indian Island [S1][S2].
- Ran unopposed in November 2025 to keep the office [S6]; this race is for the next four-year term [S1].
- Board member of Discovery Behavioral Healthcare; president of Holiday Meals of Jefferson County; East Jefferson Rotary, Elks, American Legion [S1].
- Prefers the Democratic Party [S1].
- Unopposed in the primary, with 98.1% of the vote against write-ins [S3]. Reported $4,013.92 in spending and no contributions as of Oct. 4, 2026 [S4].

## Positions

- Says public safety means "building trust, strengthening partnerships," and working with schools, health care providers and community groups on substance abuse, homelessness and mental health "with compassion, accountability, and practical solutions" [S1].
- On taking office he named staffing as the department's biggest challenge; by August 2025 the office was fully staffed [S6].
- In February 2026 publicly opposed Senate Bill 5974, which he said "would fundamentally change how sheriffs are held accountable" [S7].
