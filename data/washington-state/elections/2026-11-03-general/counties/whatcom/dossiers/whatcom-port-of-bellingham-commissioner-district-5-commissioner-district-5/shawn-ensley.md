## Background

Shawn Ensley, 48, of Deming, is running for office for the first time [S1][S2]. He grew up in Acme and Deming and graduated from Mount Baker High School [S1][S4]. He served 21 years in the Air Force Reserve in logistics and airport logistics, retiring in 2019 [S1][S4]. He earned an associate degree in instrumentation and control from Bellingham Technical College and served on its Instrumentation Advisory Board [S1]. He has worked in the instrumentation and electrical trade since 2004, including more than 19 years at the BP Cherry Point refinery, where he is now the instrumentation and electrical shop foreman [S1][S2][S4]. He describes himself as "not a career politician" [S4].

He placed second of six in the August primary, with 21.4% in the returns CDN reported [S9].

## Positions

- **Port mission and jobs.** Wants the port to stick to its mission statement: sustainable economic development, transportation gateways and management of public land and facilities. He puts living-wage jobs and "investing in projects with clear purpose" first [S1][S3]. He calls the mission the "lifeblood of Whatcom County" and wants living-wage jobs at the Bellingham Shipping Terminal [S2].
- **Working waterfront.** Says "our working waterfront should remain exactly that, working." He wants development and public spaces that do not push out fishing, marine trades and waterfront workers [S5][S6].
- **Airport and cruise traffic.** Wants expanded and reliable air service [S5]. Suggested that small and mid-sized cruise ships at Fairhaven could help bring back air travel at Bellingham International [S2].
- **Development.** Supports "responsible development that attracts private investment, creates good jobs, strengthens our tax base, and keeps the waterfront accessible" [S6]. Wants port property decisions to balance economic development, public access, environmental responsibility and long-term value for taxpayers [S5].
- **County-wide focus and trust.** Says the port should serve "all of Whatcom County, not just a few areas influenced by special interests," and pledges transparency and restored public confidence [S1].
- **Underserved needs.** Names reliable transportation, infrastructure, living-wage jobs and well-maintained, accessible port facilities [S3].
