## Background
Joseph Segault is a stone mason on Orcas Island running for the 40th Legislative District, Position 2, with no stated party preference [S1][S2]; his PDC registration lists an Eastsound address [S5]. He is a political newcomer who says he has long been drawn to local politics [S2]. His voters' guide entry lists no elected, professional, education or community-service information [S1]. No campaign website is listed [S1].

In the August 4 primary he placed second of four with 12.9% in Whatcom County returns as of August 7, behind incumbent Alex Ramel's 68.3% [S3]; certified results confirmed he advanced [S4].

## Positions
- **Taxes and cost of living:** says he is "not alright with the economic pressure that is being forced on our local communities, through increased taxes, the rising cost of living, and a lack of representation at the State level" [S1]. Wants to stop taxes from "continuing to put upward economic pressure on everyday families" and says rising taxes push "a certain class of people out" [S2]. On the tax on income over $1 million, says "things of that nature and that levity (sic) should be taken to the vote of the people" [S2]. Cascadia Daily News described him as more skeptical of the income tax than Ramel [S3].
- **Spending:** says it is unclear "why these budgets are totally bloated" and that he has no "direct solutions" yet for state budget issues, having focused on local matters [S2].
- **Housing:** says restrictive zoning is making housing less affordable and "we need to build more homes" [S2].
- **Education funding:** describes himself as an advocate for education funding; says he does not know the long-term solution to school revenue shortfalls but believes one is possible [S2].
- **Approach:** says he would work across the political spectrum as an independent and that most people are "in the middle" [S2]; his statement asks voters who are happy with the state's direction not to vote for him [S1].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** no statement found as of 2026-10-08.
- **Parents and schools:** no statement on parental notification, record access or opt-outs found as of 2026-10-08.
