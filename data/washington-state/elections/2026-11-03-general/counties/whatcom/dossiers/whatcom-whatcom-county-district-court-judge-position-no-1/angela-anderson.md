## Background
- Angela Anderson has been a Whatcom County District Court judge since October 2021 and describes herself as the court's first female judge [S1].
- Before the bench she was a Whatcom County public defender from 2006 to 2019, then ran Anderson Legal PLLC and was the Lynden Municipal Court public defender from 2019 to 2021 [S1].
- J.D. from Washington and Lee University School of Law; bachelor's degree from Truman State University [S1].
- Serves on Whatcom Community College's Criminal Justice Program Advisory Committee [S1].
- She was the only candidate listed for Position 1 in the 2022 election [S3].

## Positions
- Cites creating Family Justice Court, which she says holds domestic-violence offenders accountable while ensuring they receive treatment [S1].
- Presides over Whatcom County's Mental Health Court, a health-department program hosted in District Court and Bellingham Municipal Court that has served 155 people in its first decade, with nearly 90 graduates having charges dismissed [S1][S2].
- Told CDN that repeated contact between people with serious mental illness and the justice system is "a health crisis," that she prefers focusing on individual participants over filling the program to capacity, and that mental health court should not be the community's only response to mental illness [S2].
- Cites Zoom appearances and prompt hearings for protection orders and small-claims trials as ways the court reduces barriers to access [S1].

## Record
- In February 2023, after the prosecutor's office filed affidavits of prejudice against newly elected Judge Jonathan Rands in 123 DUI cases, a Superior Court writ sent those cases to Judge Anderson [S4].
