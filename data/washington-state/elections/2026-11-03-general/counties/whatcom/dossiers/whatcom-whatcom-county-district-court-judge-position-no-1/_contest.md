## What the office does

Whatcom County District Court is where most criminal misdemeanors are litigated [S2]. Its judges also hear protection-order petitions and small-claims trials and run therapeutic courts such as Mental Health Court and Family Justice Court [S1][S3]. The court has three judicial officers [S3]. Judges serve four-year terms [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Judge Angela Anderson, on the bench since October 2021, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1]. The contest is uncontested.
