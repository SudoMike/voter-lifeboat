## What the office does

Whatcom County District Court is where most criminal misdemeanors are litigated [S3]. Its cases range from traffic infractions and criminal cases involving alcohol, drugs and domestic violence to civil disputes such as auto accidents and small claims [S2]. Judges serve four-year terms [S1].

## Race dynamics

Incumbent Judge Jonathan Rands, elected in 2022 and now the court's presiding judge, is the only candidate on the ballot [S1][S3]. The contest is uncontested.
