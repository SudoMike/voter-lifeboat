# City of Lynden - Proposition 2026-05 Levy Lid Lift for Public Safety and Essential Community Services

## BallotTitle
The City Council of the City of Lynden adopted Resolution No. 26-1148 concerning a levy lid lift to fund public safety and essential community services. This proposition would fund public safety (police and fire), and essential community services (including park and street maintenance, restoring eliminated staff positions, reopening City Hall to the public on Fridays, and offsetting lost funding for the Lynden Community/Senior Center) by authorizing a maximum 2026 property tax levy, for collection in 2027, at $1.54304 per $1,000 of assessed value; authorizing annual increases of 3% for 2027-2035; and using the 2035 levy to calculate future levy limits.

## ExplanatoryStatement
Proposition 2026-05 would authorize the City of Lynden to increase its regular property tax levy above the limit otherwise imposed by RCW 84.55.010. The City's costs for police and fire protection, street maintenance, park maintenance, and other essential municipal services have increased significantly due to inflation and rising personnel, equipment, and operating expenses, while the City's regular property tax revenues only increase by 1% annually. Without additional funding, the City will simply be unable to avoid further cuts to service levels. 
If approved by the voters, levy lid lift revenues would be used to fund (1) public safety (police and fire), (2) maintenance of city streets and parks, (3) the Lynden Community/Senior Center, (4) restoring staff positions cut during 2025 and 2026, and (5) reopening City Hall to the public on Fridays. 
This proposition would authorize the City to levy property taxes in 2026 (for collection in 2027) at a maximum rate of $1.54304/$1,000, an increase of $0.50/$1,000 over the 2025 levy rate. A limit factor of 100% plus 3% would be used to adjust levies in 2027 through 2035. The 2035 levy amount would be used as the basis to calculate subsequent levy limits.

## ArgumentFor
We all agree that governments at all levels should operate within their means. Belt-tightening measures have been implemented throughout all Lynden City Departments to reduce costs while minimizing the impact to services. The simple truth is that the City is unable to raise the money we need to maintain the quality of services we have come to expect and value. 
Why vote yes? All of us who live in Lynden have come to enjoy our City's green spaces including parks and trails. This levy supports the maintenance necessary to keep them beautiful. Our public safety is also an essential component of the levy. With population growth, the addition of police officers is needed. The Fire Department is also stretched thin, handling over 2,700 calls per year. 
The levy lid lift is critical for us to maintain our quality of life in Lynden. For only a small additional amount of 50 cents per $1,000 of assessed value we will be able to keep essential police and fire services, and resources to care for streets, parks, our Community Center, and other valuable assets. 
The city we love needs our investment: vote for the levy to keep us safe and well-maintained. https://www.lyndenwa.org/435/2026LevyLidLift
