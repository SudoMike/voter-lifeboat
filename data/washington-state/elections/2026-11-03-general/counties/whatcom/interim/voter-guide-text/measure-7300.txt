# Fire Protection District 1 - Proposition 2026-08 Regular Property Tax Levy Lid Lift

## BallotTitle
The Board of Commissioners of Fire Protection District 1 adopted Resolution No. 2026-07 concerning a proposition to maintain and adequately fund District operations.
This proposition would fund fire protection and emergency medical services by establishing the District's regular property tax levy in 2026 at an amount not to exceed $1.48 per $1,000.00 of assessed valuation and authorize setting a limit factor of 106% for each of the following nine years. The maximum allowable levy in the final year shall serve as the base for subsequent levy limitations as provided by Chapter 84.55 RCW.

## ExplanatoryStatement
Fire Protection District 1 provides emergency fire protection and medical services to its citizens. If approved by the voters, this proposition will authorize the Fire Department to establish its regular real property tax levy rate at $1.48 per $1,000 of assessed valuation in 2026 for collection in 2027. This measure will replace the final year of the levy lid lift approved by the voters in 2021.
The proposition will also allow the Fire Department to keep pace with inflation for the following nine years by establishing an annual limit factor of 6 % per year. At the end of the ten-year period, absent further voter approval, the Fire Department tax levy will be subject to statutory one percent limit.
The Fire Department's Board of Commissioners has determined that this proposition is necessary to maintain an effective level of services, fire fighter staffing, equipment and facilities in light of rising costs. The levy is subject to the property tax exemptions provided for qualifying seniors, veterans and others under chapter 84.36 RCW.

## ArgumentFor
Whatcom County fire district 1 covers 75 square miles, 12,700 residents, and averages 1,100 calls per year, with 10 full time paid employees, 3 part time paid employees, 8 active volunteers and 9 newly graduated volunteers. Approximately 800 of those calls are EMS calls.
Fire and life safety services are funded through a fire levy, which is capped by state law at $1.50 per 1,000.00 of assessed property value. Washington law limits fire district revenue growth to 1% annually, even though inflation, emergency call volume, and service demands increase faster. In 2021, voters approved a levy lid lift that restored the levy rate to $1.40 per 1,000.00 to help maintain fire and emergency services. Since then, the levy rate has decreased and is currently $1.12 per 1,000.00. A levy lid lift is not a new tax. It restores or adjusts the existing fire district levy rate.
WCFD 1 has done more with less for years and provided excellent care and services at the same time. Because emergency service costs continue to rise while levy rates gradually decrease over time, the fire district occasionally asks voters to reset the levy rate to help maintain emergency services, staffing, equipment and operational readiness.
