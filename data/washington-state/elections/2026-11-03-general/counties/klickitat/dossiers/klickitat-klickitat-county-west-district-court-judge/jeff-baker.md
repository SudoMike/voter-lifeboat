## Background

- Jeff Baker has been Klickitat County West District Court Judge since 2014 and has also served as Superior Court Commissioner for Klickitat and Skamania Counties [S1].
- Before the bench he was a partner at Annala, Carey, Baker, Thompson & VanKoten in Hood River, an associate at Ater Wynne LLP in Portland and a special assistant attorney general in St. Paul, Minnesota; admitted in Minnesota, Oregon and Washington. BA, Lewis and Clark College; JD cum laude, University of Minnesota [S1].
- He is the judicial representative to the statewide advisory committee on implementing the Trueblood litigation and a Trout Lake Community Council member [S1].
- He was unopposed in 2018 (3,640 votes on 15 West District precincts) and 2022 (3,527 votes on 16) [S2][S3]. The West District had 8,871 registered voters in 2025 [S4].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)

- Pledges "fair and impartial judicial services" and to uphold his ethical obligations and oath [S1].
