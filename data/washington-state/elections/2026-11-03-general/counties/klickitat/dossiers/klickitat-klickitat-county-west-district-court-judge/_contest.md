## What the office does

The West District Court in White Salmon hears infractions, misdemeanors and gross misdemeanors, civil cases up to $75,000, small claims and protection orders, and serves as the court for the Cities of White Salmon and Bingen [S5]. Its judge is elected by West District voters only (8,871 registered in 2025; the 2022 race was on 16 of 29 precincts), for four years [S1][S2][S4].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested: Judge Jeff Baker, on the West District Court bench since 2014, is the only candidate. Only voters in the West District Court district vote on this seat.
