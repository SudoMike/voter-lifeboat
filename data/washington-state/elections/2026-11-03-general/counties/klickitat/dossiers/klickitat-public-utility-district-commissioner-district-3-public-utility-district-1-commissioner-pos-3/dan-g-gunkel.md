## Background

- Dan G. Gunkel (nonpartisan office) was first elected to the Klickitat PUD board, District 3, in 1991 [S1]. He served on the Energy Northwest Executive Board from 1994 to 2014 and chaired its Nuclear Oversight Committee from 2006 to 2014 [S1].
- He owns Gunkel Orchards, a third-generation family farm that grows, packs and ships cherries, peaches and apricots, and graduated from Goldendale High School in 1974 [S1].
- In the nominating primary (11 district units) he finished second with 879 votes, behind Logan Siebert (1,220) [S3].

## Positions

### Rates and the PUD's revenue assets
- His priorities are "reasonable electric rates, high reliability, and excellent customer service"; he says KPUD's rates went from third highest in its utility group when he was elected to tenth today [S1].
- Credits the McNary hydro share, the H.W. Hill renewable natural gas plant (which he says nets $5 million to $8 million a year and is contracted to Puget Sound Energy through 2041) and transmission projects for revenue that offsets rates: "KPUD is currently applying $5.9 million a year to offset rates," about $34.49 a month less for the average residential customer [S1][S2].
- Cites a Fitch upgrade of KPUD's electric revenue bonds to an A rating (August 2025, affirmed 2026) and says the board must be willing to adjust rates to stay financially sound [S2].

### Pumped storage, regional supply and data centers
- Wants to see the Goldendale Energy Storage (pumped storage) project completed, citing projected $14 million a year in taxes and 40 to 60 permanent jobs; warns that Northwest demand growth could bring rolling blackouts in extreme weather [S1][S2].
- On data centers, worries about groundwater ("Their recharge rate is slow") and prime farmland: "It would be a crime to put a data center on that" at Maryhill [S2].

### Nonpartisanship
- Calls the county Republican Party's recommendation of his opponent "inappropriate and sad"; says he takes no campaign contributions so money does not influence his decisions [S2]. PDC lists him as a mini reporter (no financial reports) [S4].

## Record

- PUD commissioner since 1991; he and retired Commissioner Randy Knowles built the McNary, RNG and transmission assets he cites [S1][S2].
