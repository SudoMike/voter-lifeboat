## What this office does

Klickitat PUD's three elected commissioners set policy, budgets and electric rates for the county's public electric utility, which also owns generation and transmission assets (McNary hydro share, the H.W. Hill renewable natural gas plant), for six-year terms [S1][S5]. The PUD covers the whole county: its 16,421 registered voters in the Auditor's 2025 count are the county total [S2]. Commissioners are nominated by district and elected PUD-wide in the general (RCW 54.12.010(3)); the 2022 Position 2 race was on all 29 precincts [S3].

## Race dynamics

- Commissioner Dan Gunkel, first elected in 1991, faces Logan Siebert, a Goldendale meat-processing business owner and first-time candidate [S1].
- Siebert led the District 3 primary 1,220 to 879; a third candidate, Dan Christopher, had 387 [S4].
- Gunkel runs on rate offsets from PUD-owned generation and finishing the Goldendale pumped-storage project; Siebert on rate transparency and making data centers pay their share of grid costs. Both worry about data centers' water use [S1][S5].
- The county Republican Party recommended Siebert in this nonpartisan race; Gunkel called that "inappropriate and sad" [S5].

## Axes that differentiate

None: no rubric axis applies to public utility district contests, so the race is listed for information without scores. Differences are on experience (35 years vs. none), rate strategy and transparency.
