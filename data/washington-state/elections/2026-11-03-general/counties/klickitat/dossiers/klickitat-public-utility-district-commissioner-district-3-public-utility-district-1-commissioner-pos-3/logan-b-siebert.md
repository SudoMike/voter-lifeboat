## Background

- Logan B. Siebert (nonpartisan office), 31, of Goldendale, is a first-time candidate who owns Siebert Custom Slaughter and Meats LLC, opened in March 2020 [S1][S2]. He has lived in Klickitat County for 26 years [S1].
- He completed an apprenticeship with Portland General Electric at the Tucannon Wind Farm, later hauled livestock, and is a reserve officer with the Goldendale Police Department; he served three years on the Goldendale Rodeo Bible Camp board and ten years in 4-H youth development [S1][S2].
- He led the nominating primary (11 district units) with 1,220 votes to Dan Gunkel's 879; Dan Christopher (387) was eliminated [S5].
- The Klickitat County Republican Party "recommended" him to its members, which Gunkel criticized [S4].
- In September 2026 the Sentinel reported he owed $1,407.78 in 2025 and 2026 property taxes; he replied he would pay them "next time I'm in town" [S3].

## Positions

### Rates and transparency
- "I'm running because ratepayers deserve a commissioner who listens"; "Reliable power and fair rates affect every family and business in Klickitat County" [S1].
- Wants the PUD to explain how rates are calculated and why bills include line items such as demand charges [S2].
- Questions how much benefit PUD customers get from privately owned wind, solar, gas and hydro projects in the county, noting long rural lines and low customer density as reasons rates are higher than in Chelan or Douglas [S2].

### Data centers
- Not opposed to data centers but "they're going to pay their fair share for the grid portion of it"; would oppose their drawing municipal or aquifer water, preferring regulated Columbia River withdrawals; does not want costs shifted to existing ratepayers [S2].

### Experience
- "The only way to gain experience as a PUD commissioner is to be elected"; says he is still learning utility regulation and has been attending commission meetings [S2]. "I'm not a career politician" [S1].
