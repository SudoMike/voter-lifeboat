## What this office does

The three-member Board of County Commissioners adopts the county budget and ordinances, sets land-use policy, oversees county departments, public works and (since the transition) the county jail, and appoints county representatives to regional boards [S1][S4]. Commissioners are nominated by district in the primary and elected by the whole county in the general (RCW 36.32.040): the 2022 District 2 race was on all 29 Klickitat precincts [S3]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics

- Incumbent Lori Zoller, in her first term, faces Andy Kallinen, a State Parks ranger and former Army and Coast Guard reservist from Lyle; both prefer the Republican Party [S1].
- Zoller led the August primary with 1,148 votes to Kallinen's 760; Gene Klejeski (667) was eliminated [S2].
- The campaign has turned on the county's takeover of the jail from the Sheriff's Office, which Zoller defends and Kallinen says was badly handled, and on data centers, where Zoller opposed a moratorium in favour of an ordinance [S4].

## Axes that differentiate

- `experience`: Zoller is the incumbent with decades of county project work; Kallinen has no elected county office.
- `safety`: Kallinen is more explicit about adding deputies, dispatchers and a crisis responder; Zoller's record is the jail takeover and compliance.
- `spending`: Zoller runs on lean reorganization and a stable budget; Kallinen on "limited" government while proposing more deputies, dispatchers and a crisis responder.
- `taxes`, `housing`, `climate`, `reform`: little direct evidence from either.
