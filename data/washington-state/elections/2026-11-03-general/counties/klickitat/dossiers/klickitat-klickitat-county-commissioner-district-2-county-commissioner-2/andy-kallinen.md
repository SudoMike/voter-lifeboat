## Background

- Andy Kallinen (Prefers Republican Party) of Lyle is a Washington State Parks ranger with more than 30 years in law enforcement, currently an assistant area manager on a team overseeing about 20 full-time-equivalent employees and a budget he estimates at $2.5 million [S1][S3].
- He served 20 years in the military reserves: 14 years as an Army National Guard officer (platoon leader, staff officer, armor cavalry company commander) and 6 years in the Coast Guard Reserve [S1][S2]. He holds a law and justice degree from Central Washington University (1991) [S1].
- He is in his fourth two-year term on the executive board of Washington Federation of State Employees Local 1466 and was elected to the High Prairie Community Council for 2025-2026 [S1][S2]. He has lived in Klickitat County since 1993 [S3].
- He ran for State Representative, LD 14 Position 1, in 2024, a campaign he describes as one-man and about $3,000 [S3][S4].
- He finished second in the August 4, 2026 District 2 primary with 760 votes, behind incumbent Lori Zoller (1,148) [S5].

## Positions

### Government and spending
- His platform is to make county government "REAL": "Responsible, Effective, Accountable, Limited"; "Government works best when it is closest to the people" [S2].
- Would protect existing county revenue such as landfill contracts, replace lost contracts and grow tourism; pledges to donate his first month's commissioner salary [S2][S3].

### Public safety and the jail
- Calls public safety "the number one responsibility of government"; wants enough deputies to address the state's ranking of 51st of 51 in officers per capita, and adequate staffing and retention at the 911 center and jail [S2]. Would like "two more deputies, at a minimum," possibly multipurpose deputies covering animal control or court security, and more dispatchers to cut overtime and burnout [S3].
- Does not object to running corrections as a separate county department but says "the manner in which it was handled was terrible" and that it caused "turmoil, distrust, and anxiety"; he would have studied root causes and options first [S3].
- On mental health, wants a dedicated crisis responder and to find out why county mental-health positions go unfilled; says jail should be "the last thing" for people in mental-health crisis [S3].
- In his 2024 legislative campaign he said "Crime has increased while liberals have worked to defund the police" and helped draft and testified for HB 2765 (2016), which he calls pro-law enforcement [S2][S4].

### Energy and data centers
- Says energy rates keep rising despite the county's many energy projects; wants the county "fairly compensated when hosting Energy development projects" and best outcomes for ratepayers [S2].
- Wants county land-use rules for large power users such as data centers that account for cumulative load, while noting commissioners do not set PUD rates [S3].

### Transportation
- Would prioritize maintaining county roads and transportation options [S1][S2].

### Social views (not scored for county offices)
- His site says he "believes in the sanctity of life" [S2].
