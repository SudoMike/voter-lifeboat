## Background

- Megan K. Murphy has been a judge of the Court of Appeals, Division III, since January 2025, appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee to replace Judge Rebecca Pennell; she was one of four applicants [S1][S2].
- She began in 2001 as a Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney (about five years), then practised civil litigation, specializing in medical-malpractice defense, and was a partner at Fox Ballard in Seattle [S1][S2].
- Claremont McKenna College (1997); University of Colorado School of Law (2001) [S1].
- She was unopposed for the seat in November 2025 (3,765 Klickitat votes) [S3].
