## What the office does

Court of Appeals judges hear appeals from the superior courts; Division III is based in Spokane and its District 3 seat is on Klickitat County's ballot. The term is six years [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics

Uncontested: Judge Megan K. Murphy, appointed in 2025, is the only candidate.
