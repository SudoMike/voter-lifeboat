## Background

- Rick L Hansen has been Klickitat County East District Court Judge since 2010 and has practised law in Goldendale since 1984 in East District and Superior Courts, with appearances before the Court of Appeals and Supreme Court [S1].
- Graduate of Centerville Grade School and Goldendale High School; BA in business, Eastern Washington University; JD, Gonzaga University [S1].
- He was unopposed in 2018 (3,550 votes on the 18 East District precincts) and 2022 (3,550 votes) [S2][S3]. The East District had 7,550 registered voters in 2025 [S4].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
