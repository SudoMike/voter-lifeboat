## Background

- Greg Gallagher (Prefers Republican Party) has been Klickitat County Treasurer since 2015 [S1]. He is legislative chair of the southeast region of the Washington State Association of County Treasurers and reports nearly 30 years in public and private financial management [S1].
- B.S. in business administration/finance, Central Washington University [S1].
- He was unopposed in 2018 (7,463 votes) [S3], 2022 (7,353 votes) [S2] and the August 2026 primary (5,021 votes) [S4].

## Positions
