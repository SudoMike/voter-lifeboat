## Background

- Dwayne T. Matulovich (Prefers Independent Party) served with the Bingen-White Salmon Police Department (2004-2008), the Goldendale Police Department (2008-2015) and the Klickitat County Sheriff's Office (2015-2023), as patrol officer, firearms instructor, field training officer, search and rescue member and commercial vehicle inspector [S1].
- He left the Sheriff's Office in 2023 and is now a welding foreman for BNSF Railway; he says he left after supporting another candidate in the previous sheriff's race before Bob Songer re-entered it, and because "politics and internal dynamics" in a small department made it hard to see a path forward [S2].
- He is a volunteer firefighter with Rural 7 (since 2024) and was one with Skamania County Fire District 1 (1998-2004); he worked the Burdoin Fire [S1][S2]. He holds a B.S. in exercise science from Central Washington University with a minor in law enforcement [S1].
- He won 4,614 votes (about 57%) in the August 4, 2026 primary, ahead of Tony Warren (2,152) and John Bruce (1,289) [S5].

## Positions

### Public safety and the Sheriff's Office
- "The biggest thing I want to do is start building that trust again"; says residents want "more patrols, more thorough investigations" [S2].
- "Nineteen years of full-time law enforcement experience gives me insight into what needs to be changed" [S1].
- Will improve deputies' training and morale and build relationships with other agencies and county departments while "managing the budget responsibly" [S1].
- Agreed at the July forum on the need for more deputies [S3].

### Role of the sheriff and state law
- "My job is to enforce the laws. It's not to interpret them," leaving constitutional questions to the courts [S3]; said he would rely on the judicial branch to challenge legislation if necessary [S4].
- Will not join the Constitutional Sheriffs and Peace Officers Association: "My focus is going to be on the county" [S3][S4].
- "My allegiance will be to the people of our county, not to a political party" [S1].

### Immigration (not scored for county offices)
- Calls immigration enforcement "a federal job"; would work with federal agencies under agreed policies "as long as ... they follow the rules in the law" [S3].

### Administrative experience
- Acknowledges no law-enforcement administrative experience; cites his work as a railroad first-line supervisor and says he will learn and draw on others' expertise [S2].

## Campaign finance

- PDC reports $15,442.45 raised and $12,359.46 spent (updated 2026-09-17) [S6].
