## What this office does

The sheriff is Klickitat County's elected chief law enforcement officer and runs the Sheriff's Office: patrol, investigations, search and rescue and marine patrol across a large rural county [S1][S3]. The term is four years [S1].

## Race dynamics

- Open seat: Sheriff Bob Songer is retiring [S2].
- Dwayne Matulovich (Prefers Independent Party), a former deputy who left the office in 2023, won an outright majority in the primary (4,614 votes, about 57%); detective sergeant Tony Warren (Prefers Republican Party), endorsed by Songer, had 2,152 and John Bruce 1,289 [S2][S4].
- Forum questions turned on how far each would follow Songer, a vocal member of the Constitutional Sheriffs and Peace Officers Association: neither would join it, but Warren said he might not enforce state laws he judged against residents' interest, while Matulovich said constitutionality is for the courts [S3][S4].
- City records on Warren's discipline and 2019 resignation at the Bingen-White Salmon Police Department became a campaign issue [S4].
- All candidates agreed the county needs more deputies [S3].

## Axes that differentiate

- `experience`: Warren is the serving insider endorsed by the incumbent; Matulovich left the office and runs on rebuilding trust.
- `reform`: both promise change; Matulovich runs against the current administration's approach, Warren for structural change from within.
- `safety`: both enforcement-oriented; Warren's platform is more explicit about drug and property-crime enforcement.
- `spending`: Warren wants three to four more deputies; Matulovich promises responsible budget management. Thin.
