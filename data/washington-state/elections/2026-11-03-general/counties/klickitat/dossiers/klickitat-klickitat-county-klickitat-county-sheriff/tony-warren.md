## Background

- Tony (Anthony) Warren (Prefers Republican Party) is a detective sergeant at the Klickitat County Sheriff's Office under Sheriff Bob Songer, where he also coordinates the field training program and has served on patrol, the warrant team and marine patrol [S1][S2][S4].
- His career began in 1997 as a Skamania County reserve deputy; he joined the Bingen-White Salmon Police Department in 2008 and the Sheriff's Office in 2021 [S1]. His father retired as Skamania County Sheriff [S2]. He owns a small dump truck and excavating business [S1][S2].
- City records show a two-day unpaid suspension at the Bingen-White Salmon Police Department in 2014 after he called in sick following a night of drinking, a 2017 move from detective to patrol pay, and his 2019 resignation a week after notice of an internal investigation; his state separation notice listed no disqualifying misconduct [S5]. He says the resignation followed allegations by his former wife during a divorce and that "nothing related to my departure resulted in a loss of my law enforcement certification"; he denies having been intoxicated on duty and calls excessive-force allegations "absolutely absurd" [S4][S5].
- He finished second in the August 4, 2026 primary with 2,152 votes, behind Dwayne Matulovich (4,614) [S7].

## Positions

### Staffing and operations
- Wants "three or four" more deputies, saying the county has one of the lowest deputy-to-population ratios in the state; wants records, reports and records requests "streamlined" and modernized [S4].
- Would "ensure fiscal responsibility, maximizing the budget to increase essential staffing and training" [S1].
- Would stay active on patrol rather than "manage from behind the desk" [S4]; platform includes internal accountability standards, shift changes for deputy safety and wellness, and "intentional, structural changes" despite the current sheriff's endorsement ("endorsement does not mean duplication"); "True progress requires comprehensive restructuring, not passive management." He calls himself "the only active law enforcement officer in this race" [S3].

### Enforcement and community policing
- Lists drug enforcement, property crime and traffic safety; wants to crack down on drug and abuse-related crime [S3][S4].
- Promises community-oriented policing with deputies in neighborhoods and regular time at each school instead of a full-time school resource officer, and community input on the department's direction [S1][S4].

### Role of the sheriff and state law
- "I agree with [Songer] on a lot of things; I'm just not quite as vocal about it. ... If the state passes a bill that's not good for the citizens of Klickitat County, I have no issue telling them where they can go" [S5]. At the July forum he said laws from Olympia that are not in residents' interest "we may not be enforcing ... within reason" [S6].
- Will not join the Constitutional Sheriffs and Peace Officers Association [S5][S6].
- "My priorities will be clear: protecting the constitutional rights of every resident" [S4].

### Immigration (not scored for county offices)
- Says his position aligns with the state sheriffs' association: keep the peace, take enforcement action if state laws or county ordinances are broken [S6].

## Endorsements
