## Background

- Renea Campbell (Prefers Republican Party) was elected Klickitat County Clerk in 2014, 2018 and 2022 [S1]. She worked 24 years in East District Court, 18 as court administrator, and 20 years as a non-attorney judicial officer [S1].
- She was unopposed in 2018 (7,463 votes) [S3] and won 2022 by 7,237 to 3,524 over Shelby McKern [S2]; unopposed in the August 2026 primary (5,283 votes) [S4].

## Positions

- Lists a new case management system, electronic filing, upgraded document and jury systems, online records through the Washington State Digital Archives, and passport photo services to supplement revenue [S1].
