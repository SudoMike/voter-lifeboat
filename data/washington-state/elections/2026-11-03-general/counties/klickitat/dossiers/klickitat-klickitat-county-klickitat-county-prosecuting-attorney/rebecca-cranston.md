## Background

- Rebecca Cranston (States No Party Preference) was appointed Prosecuting Attorney by the county commissioners at their December 30, 2025 meeting; she was the only applicant [S1][S2]. She had been a deputy prosecuting attorney in the office since 2016, handling civil matters and criminal appeals [S1].
- Before that she was a civil attorney for a nonprofit law firm in Snohomish County, which she founded, and a law clerk for a Snohomish County Superior Court judge; JD cum laude, Seattle University (2014); BA, UC Davis (2010) [S1].
- Her predecessor, David R. Quesnel, was re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S5]. She was unopposed in the August 2026 primary (4,916 votes) [S4].

## Positions

- Says she will "prioritize public safety by collaborating with our criminal justice partners to hold offenders accountable while advocating for victims" [S1].
- As appointed prosecutor she advises the commissioners, including on the hiring of a public records officer in September 2026 [S3].
