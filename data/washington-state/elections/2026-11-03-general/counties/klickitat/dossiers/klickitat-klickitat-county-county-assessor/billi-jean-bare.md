## Background

- Billi Jean Bare (Prefers Republican Party) has been Klickitat County Assessor since 2023 and has worked in the Assessor's office since 2016 [S1].
- She is a Washington State Department of Revenue accredited appraiser and a contributing member of the Clean Energy Property Tax Impacts Task Force with the Washington State Association of Counties [S1].
- Graduate of Goldendale High School; AA in business with an accounting certificate from Blue Mountain Community College; 4-H leader for over 20 years and Klickitat County Fair superintendent [S1].
- She was unopposed in the 2022 general (7,669 votes) [S2] and in the August 2026 primary (5,181 votes) [S3].

## Positions

- Says she has worked toward "fair and accurate assessments, increased transparency" and a better public understanding of how values and taxes are set [S1].
