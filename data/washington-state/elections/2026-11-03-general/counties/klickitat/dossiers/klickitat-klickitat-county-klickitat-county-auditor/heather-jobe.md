## Background

- Heather Jobe (Prefers Republican Party) is the Klickitat County Auditor, after six years as Chief Deputy Auditor; she is secretary of the Washington Association of County Auditors and a Central Klickitat County Parks and Recreation District commissioner [S1].
- Earlier: administrative services director for the City of Toppenish (one year), a business consulting for law firms (10+ years) and a paralegal (20+ years); MBA with a public administration concentration [S1].
- She won the 2022 general 6,036 to 3,575 over JoAnne Enyeart Chambers [S2] and was unopposed in the August 2026 primary (4,874 votes) [S3].

## Positions

- Cites improved customer service and efficiency and "important progress made in enhancing the security, integrity, and transparency of our elections" [S1].
