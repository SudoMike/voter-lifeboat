## What the office does
The county auditor runs elections and voter registration, records deeds and other public documents, issues vehicle and other state licenses and passports, and keeps the county's accounts payable, financial accounting and payroll [S2][S4]. The auditor administers state election law as written and chairs the county canvassing board [S1][S3].

## Race dynamics
Republican Charles Ross, auditor since 2014 and a former 14th District state representative and Naches mayor, seeks a fourth term against Democrat Maria Cuevas, a retired Yakima Valley College sociology instructor making her first run for office [S1][S2]. In the certified August primary Ross received 37.70%, Cuevas 34.37% and Republican Matt Barker 27.93% [S3]. Barker had questioned the office's voter rolls; Ross and his elections staff said the cases he cited showed the system working as designed, and Cuevas said Ross "is doing his job" and that she is running to expand outreach and education (charles-ross.md S5). Cuevas's platform centers on voter turnout, which she says is the lowest in the state, bilingual service, outreach to reduce ballot rejections, plain-language financial reporting and an independent review of the office (maria-cuevas.md S1, S2). Ross runs on continuity and efficient, fair administration of laws he says he does not always agree with [S1][S2]. Both say mail ballots make voting easier [S2]. Cuevas reports $34,372 raised to Ross's $22,583 (charles-ross.md S6; maria-cuevas.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** a three-term incumbent with 20-plus years in elected office vs. a first-time candidate.
2. **Spending and accountability:** Cuevas proposes an independent operational review and public dashboards for payroll and expenditures; Ross cites living within the county's means.
3. **Election administration:** both support vote-by-mail; Cuevas emphasizes outreach, Spanish-language service and signature-cure reporting by precinct.
