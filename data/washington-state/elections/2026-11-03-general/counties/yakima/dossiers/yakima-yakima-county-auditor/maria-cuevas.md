## Background
Maria Cuevas, 69, of Yakima, is a retired educator and sociologist who taught sociology, Chicano and ethnic studies and intercultural communication at Yakima Valley College for nearly two decades, coordinated its Diversity Series and was treasurer of American Federation of Teachers Local 1485 [S1][S2][S3]. She was earlier assistant director of UCLA's Chicano Studies Research Center and worked with the United Farm Workers on a fair-trade agricultural initiative [S2]. She holds a master's in public health and social welfare from UCLA and is ABD in sociology at WSU [S1][S2][S3]. She sits on the boards of the Memorial Foundation and Between the Ridges Alliance for the Common Good, and lists the Yakima Immigrant Response Network and Yakima Indivisible among her community work [S2][S3]. She has held no elected office and runs as a Democrat [S1][S3].

## Positions
- **Turnout and outreach:** says the county's voter turnout is the lowest in the state, calling it "a failure of imagination by an office that has not been built for the full community it serves" [S1][S2]. Would do voter education in churches, schools and workplaces and bilingual ballot guidance meant to prevent rejections [S2][S3]. Her stated election concern is ballots rejected for signature mismatch; she said of the incumbent, "My perspective is that (Ross) is doing his job" [S4].
- **Election reporting:** would publish signature challenges and cures by precinct and apply "the same standard ... to every precinct, English and Spanish, urban and rural" [S2].
- **Vote-by-mail:** calls mail ballots "the most democratic way to ensure that everyone participates" [S3].
- **Finances and accountability:** would rewrite annual financial reports in plain English and Spanish, publish a public dashboard for payroll, accounts payable and major expenditures, commission an independent operational review of the office in year one and publish the results, and keep strong internal controls with an annual public summary [S2].
- **Service:** bilingual staffing in every customer-facing role, online appointments, mobile-friendly digital records and posted counter wait-time targets reported quarterly [S2].
