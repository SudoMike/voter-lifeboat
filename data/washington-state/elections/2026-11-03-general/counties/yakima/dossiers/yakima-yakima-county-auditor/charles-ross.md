## Background
Charles Ross, 54, of Naches, has been Yakima County Auditor since 2014 and seeks a fourth term [S1][S3]. He was a Washington State Representative from the 14th District for eight years (2006-2014) and earlier a Naches town councilman and mayor (1997-2006) [S1][S3]. He is a licensed funeral director (1996-2014) and a U.S. Navy veteran of the Persian Gulf War [S1][S3]. He holds associate degrees from Yakima Valley College and Mt. Hood Community College [S1]. He is a Yakima County Republican precinct committee officer and central committee board member and lists his party preference as Republican [S1]. The county's Auditor page lists him as auditor [S2].

## Positions
- **Administering the law:** "I do not agree with every law the Legislature passes, but I do my best to implement them in a friendly, fair and professional manner" [S1].
- **Elections:** says the office conducts accurate and transparent elections, uses an education and outreach plan to boost turnout and enforces election law fairly; says mail ballots make it easier for people to vote [S3]. Responding to a primary opponent's claims about multiple ballots, said "Sending a ballot out is not a fraudulent activity" and that the cases cited showed the system working as designed [S5].
- **Budget:** says he is part of a team of county officials making medium- and long-term plans "that focus on providing quality services while living within our means and being accountable to taxpayers" [S3].
- **Services:** would continue counter service (car tabs, passports, property transfers), county accounting and payroll, and accurate recording of public documents [S3].
