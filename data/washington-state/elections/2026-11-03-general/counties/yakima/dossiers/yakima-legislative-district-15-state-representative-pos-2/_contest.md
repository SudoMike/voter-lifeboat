## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including K-12 funding, the state tax structure, housing and land-use rules, health funding and criminal-justice statutes. The 15th District covers northern Yakima County, the West Valley, Moxee, Zillah, Selah and parts of Benton County, including Prosser [S2].

## Race dynamics
An open seat: Rep. Jeremie Dufault (R) is running unopposed for the 15th District Senate seat [S2]. Republican Reedy Berg, a Toppenish Middle School history teacher, Yakima City Council member since 2024 and the council's deputy mayor, faces Yakima attorney Liz Hallock, who lists "No Kings" as her party preference and has run for the Legislature, city council and governor before [S1][S2]. Berg led the primary with 48% to Hallock's 30%, with Republican Chase Foster eliminated at 21% [S2]. Berg has raised $15,739 and is endorsed by the Yakima County Republican Party, Dufault and other Republican legislators (reedy-berg.md S2, S11); Hallock reports under the mini option and did not answer the Yakima Herald-Republic's questionnaire (liz-hallock.md S2, S6).

Berg's platform: parental notification on curricula and medical decisions, repeal of the Climate Commitment Act, opposition to the income tax and a sales-tax cut, treatment requirements for housing aid, and full police funding (reedy-berg.md S2, S3). His city record includes voting against sending a property-tax levy to voters and against a Pride Month proclamation on the stated view that there are two genders (reedy-berg.md S6, S7). Hallock's statement centers on economic inequality, opposition to President Trump, treatment of immigrants and reproductive rights; she has campaigned for abortion access since 2022 (liz-hallock.md S1, S4).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** Berg's two-genders statement and Pride vote vs. Hallock's abortion-access advocacy.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** sitting deputy mayor vs. a repeat candidate who has not held government office.
3. **Climate and energy:** Berg would repeal the Climate Commitment Act; Hallock's statement says the environment is being destroyed.
4. **Immigration:** Hallock objects to immigrants being "terrorized"; no Berg position on immigration enforcement.
5. **Taxes, spending, housing, safety, parental rights:** documented for Berg; Hallock has no stated position.
