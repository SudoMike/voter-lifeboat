## Background
Liz Hallock, of Yakima, is a family-law and public-disclosure attorney (Pacific Northwest Family Law) and small-business owner running for the open 15th District, Position 2 seat with "No Kings" as her party preference [S1][S2][S7]. The Yakima Herald-Republic describes No Kings as "a pro-democracy political movement protesting dictatorships and President Donald Trump" [S2]. She holds a B.A. from Princeton and an M.A. in security policy studies from George Washington University's Elliott School [S1]. She owned one of the first legal marijuana stores in the United States (Sweet Relief in Yakima) [S1][S4]. Her listed political experience is as a 2016 Bernie Sanders county-convention delegate and Democratic precinct committee officer [S1].

She has run before without winning: for the 14th District House (2018, and a 2022 bid as a Women's Reproductive Justice Party candidate), Yakima City Council District 5 (2019) and governor (2020, as a Green Party candidate); she has filed as a Democrat, independent and Green Party candidate [S2][S4][S5]. She did not respond to the Yakima Herald-Republic's primary questionnaire requests [S2].

In the August 4 primary she placed second with 5,170 votes (30.10%) to Republican Reedy Berg's 8,271 (48.15%) (unofficial election-night count) [S3].

## Positions
- **Economic inequality and taxes:** "For the .001%, America is in a Gilded Era. For the rest of us, economic inequality is at its peak" [S1]. In 2020 she said small business "is getting destroyed by state and federal policies that favor corporations that exploit tax loopholes" and that bigger business should contribute to housing solutions [S5]. No stated position on Washington's income tax found.
- **Federal power:** says Olympia needs someone "to fight for and protect Yakima residents from the King in the White House" [S1].
- **Immigration:** says "Christian teachings are being hijacked to justify endless wars and to terrorize immigrants" [S1].
- **Climate and environment:** "Our environment and our children's futures are being destroyed" [S1]; listed work with the Citizens' Climate Lobby in 2020 [S5].
- **Guns and protest:** "Lawful gun owners are being shot in cold blood for exercising their right to peacefully protest" [S1]; in 2020 criticized state policies that "threaten the rights of responsible adults, whether it's taking away gun rights or banning flavored vapes" [S5].
- **Healthcare and safety (2020):** listed health care, domestic and gang violence and drug addiction as priorities [S5].
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** "Reproductive rights are being gutted" [S1]. In her 2022 run she said "We need to fight like hell in Eastern Washington to protect abortion rights and safe abortion access" [S4].
- **Parents and schools:** no stated position found as of 2026-10-09.
