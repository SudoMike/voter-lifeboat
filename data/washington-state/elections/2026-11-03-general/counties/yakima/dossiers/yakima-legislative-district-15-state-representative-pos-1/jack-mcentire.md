## Background
Jack McEntire, of Selah, is the Democratic candidate for the 15th District, Position 1 [S1][S4]. He is a chainsaw and stone sculptor (owner of Cascade Carvings and a partner in Sculptures in Stone LLC) and has worked as a fruit picker and laborer [S1]. He holds a B.S. in microbiology and immunology from the University of Washington [S1][S2]. His civic roles include president of the Cascade Chainsaw Sculptors Guild, Democratic precinct committee officer, vice-chair of the Yakima County Democrats, and board seats on the Yakima Art Commission and Yakima Maker Space [S1]. His statement's elected experience lists only party and guild positions, not government office [S1]. He ran for a 15th District House seat against Republican Bruce Chandler in 2018, when Chandler won with about 60% of the vote, and again in 2020 [S2].

Identity note: the House roll calls list a sitting "McEntire", who is Rep. Jim McEntire of the 24th District, not this candidate; no legislative votes are attributed to Jack McEntire here.

## Positions
- **Healthcare:** "We deserve universal health care" [S1]. In 2020 he said "Washington state could provide universal healthcare" and that tying health care to a job "doesn't work so well" [S2].
- **Education and youth:** wants youth programs that teach skills, and says college or vocational education "should be based on ability and need, not money" [S1]; in 2020 called for "universal education", including vocational and college education [S2].
- **Economy:** "They've said: If the rich get richer so will we – this hasn't happened" [S1]; says "Investment in our future is the way to transform our home" [S1].
- **Climate:** in 2020 called climate change "one of the existential threats to society" and said "We need to listen to science, not to rhetoric" [S2].
- **Record vs. renewal:** says "For years we've had no choice for our representatives. We've seen the same faces and ideas" [S1].
- **Taxes, housing, public safety, gender and reproductive policy, parents and schools, immigration:** no stated position found as of 2026-10-09.
