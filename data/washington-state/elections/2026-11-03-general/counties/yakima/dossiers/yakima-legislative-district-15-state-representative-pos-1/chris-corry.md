## Background
Chris Corry, of Yakima, is the incumbent Republican State Representative for the 15th Legislative District, Position 1, and the House Republican Deputy Minority Leader [S1][S2]. He represented the 14th District from 2019 to 2024 and the 15th District since 2025 [S1]. He serves on the Appropriations, Consumer Protection & Business and Rules committees [S2]. His statement lists 15+ years in property and casualty insurance and work as Eastern Washington director of the Washington Policy Center, a B.A. in political science from the University of Washington, and board or advisory roles with Yakima County Crime Stoppers, the Central Washington State Fair Association, the Yakima Salvation Army and Life Choices of Yakima; he is a foster and adoptive parent [S1]. He faces Democrat Jack McEntire [S18].

## Positions
- **Taxes and spending:** his statement promises "limited government that is fiscally responsible" and "reducing tax burdens and regulations on businesses and families" [S1]. He called ESSB 6346 "Washington's first state income tax", said the threshold "will eventually come for all", argued an income tax requires a constitutional amendment and a vote of the people, and said the state should "demonstrate that it can manage the record revenue it already collects" [S4]. He criticized the 2026 supplemental operating budget ("After $80 billion in spending, you would think something would get cheaper") and its reliance on the income tax and pension-fund shifts [S5].
- **Housing:** said the 2026 Legislature did not pass meaningful policies to make housing affordable [S5]. Record: voted for the 2023 middle-housing bill and against the 2025 rent-increase cap (see Record) [S10][S11].
- **Public safety:** said the 2026 session "did not pass legislation that would meaningfully address violent crime or hold repeat offenders accountable" [S5]; voted against the 2021 police-tactics and use-of-force bills [S13][S14].
- **Climate and energy:** voted against the Climate Commitment Act in 2021 [S12]. No campaign statement on climate found.
- **Gender, LGBTQ+ and reproductive policy:** voted against ESHB 1469 (2023), on access to reproductive health care and gender-affirming treatment [S15]. Lists Life Choices of Yakima among his community service [S1].
- **Parents and schools:** his statement lists "parental rights" and "increasing local control for our schools" [S1]. Voted against ESHB 1296 (2025) [S9]. Criticized the Legislature for not acting on the parental-rights initiative and for refusing public hearings on it [S5].
- **Immigration:** voted against E2SSB 5497 (2019, "Keep Washington Working") [S16]. No campaign statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-09. Record: voted against ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S6], and called for any income tax to go to a vote of the people [S4].
- **I-1 (parental rights):** no endorsement statement found; he criticized the Legislature for not acting on "initiatives to ... strengthen parental rights" and for refusing them public hearings [S5]. Record: voted against ESHB 1296, the 2025 amendments to the parental-rights law that I-1 would undo [S9].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no endorsement statement found; he criticized the Legislature for not acting on "initiatives to protect girls' sports" [S5].

## Record
- Voted nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the 9.9% tax on household income over $1 million (2026-03-09, passed 51-46) [S6].
- Voted nay on ESHB 2081, business and occupation tax increases (2025-04-22, passed 50-48) [S7], and on ESSB 5814, excise taxes on select services (2025-04-23, passed 50-47) [S8].
- Voted nay on ESHB 1296 on House passage (2025-03-12) and final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, 59-39) [S9].
- Voted nay on EHB 1217, the residential rent-increase limit, on House passage (2025-03-10) and on the conference report (2025-04-27, passed 54-44); in between he voted yea on the failed motion to concur in the Senate's amendments (2025-04-23, 48-50) [S10].
- Voted yea on E2SHB 1110, middle housing in cities, on House passage (2023-03-06, 75-21) and final passage (2023-04-18, 79-18) [S11].
- Voted nay on E2SSB 5126, the Climate Commitment Act (2021-04-23, passed 54-43) [S12].
- Voted nay on ESHB 1054 (police tactics) and E2SHB 1310 (use of force) on final passage (2021-04-23) [S13][S14].
- Voted nay on ESHB 1469 (2023-02-28, passed 59-38) [S15] and on E2SSB 5497 (2019-04-12, passed 57-38) [S16].
- Secured, with Rep. Jeremie Dufault, more than $7.6 million in 2026 capital-budget projects for the district, including Roza Canal repairs and a Department of Agriculture plant services lab [S5].
- PDC (updated 2026-10-05): $229,050.00 raised and $163,768.46 spent [S17].
