## Background
Keith Effler, 44, is a Yakima commercial photographer and videographer of 15 years who says he and his wife own and are developing residential and commercial property in Yakima [S1][S2][S3]. He chartered a youth arts nonprofit, opened Yakima's first co-working space, was president of the Yakima Valley Society for Cultural Education, preaches at a non-denominational church, tutors eighth graders and is a foster and adoptive parent [S1][S2][S3]. He holds a BA in psychology from Northwest Nazarene University and is taking IAAO courses [S1][S2]. His only elected post is Republican precinct committee officer [S1][S3]. This is his first run for county office [S3].

## Positions
- **Taxes:** "Property tax is burdensome... My solution is to only collect an absolute minimum"; pledges to push boundaries and question everything, "effectively cutting your tax burden as far as the law allows," find loopholes, and make Yakima County "the cheapest tax base in Washington" [S1]. His website says he will "put the taxpayer first above the government" and be "a revolutionary" if possible [S2]. He has also said the assessor cannot lower taxes and that the cause is district budgets and "levies, levies, levies" [S3].
- **Transparency and service:** would build a public "data-depot" (assessyakima.com) showing valuation formulas, parcel tax coefficients, comparable sales and market data, plus webinars, live chat and separate dashboards for homeowners and real-estate professionals, and put all forms online [S2][S3][S4]. Says he will show publicly how values are calculated [S1].
- **Valuation:** wants to stabilize the "swings" in property values of the past four years with consistent market-based methods [S3][S4].
- **Exemptions:** would post the senior exemption form and instructions online so low-income seniors need not travel to the office [S4].
- **Relationship to the state:** "I won't befriend state officials or play games" [S1]; "I'm not going to the government to make government friends" [S2].
