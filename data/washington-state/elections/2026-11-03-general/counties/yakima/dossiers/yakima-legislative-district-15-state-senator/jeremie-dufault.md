## Background
- Republican State Representative for the 15th Legislative District, Position 2; House committees: Consumer Protection & Business (assistant ranking minority member), Housing, and Rules [S2]. The Legislature's site lists his voting records for 2019-2022 and 2025-2026 [S2].
- Former Selah City Council member; works in commercial real estate investments; lieutenant colonel in the U.S. Army Reserve JAG Corps, with service in Kuwait and Afghanistan [S1].
- Education: A.C. Davis High School; B.A., University of Pennsylvania; J.D., Harvard Law School [S1].
- Community service: Yakima County Veterans Board, YV-Tech board, Yakima County Economic Development Board, American Legion, VFW [S1].
- He is the only candidate on the ballot for the 15th District Senate seat [S1].

## Positions
- **Taxes and government:** describes himself as a "common-sense conservative" who "fights for lower taxes and smaller, smarter government" [S1]. On the 2026 tax on income over $1 million he said "these taxes start small and grow" and that lawmakers would return to expand it because of "insatiable demands" to grow government [S5].
- **Parents and schools:** voted against ESHB 1296 (2025), the bill amending the 2024 parental-rights law (see Record) [S4]. No further statement found.

## Positions on the 2026 initiatives
- **I-645 (repeal of the 9.9% tax on income over $1 million):** no statement on the initiative itself found as of 2026-10-09. Record: voted against ESSB 6346, the tax I-645 would repeal [S3], and spoke against it [S5].
- **I-1 (parental rights):** no statement found. Record: voted against ESHB 1296 (2025), the amendments to the parental-rights law that I-1 would undo [S4].
- **I-638 (girls' school sports):** no statement found as of 2026-10-09.

## Record
- Voted nay on House final passage of ESSB 6346, the tax on household income over $1 million (2026-03-09, passed 51-46) [S3][S5].
- Voted nay on ESHB 1296 on House passage (2025-03-12, 56-37) and on final passage as amended by the Senate (2025-04-24, 59-39) [S4].
