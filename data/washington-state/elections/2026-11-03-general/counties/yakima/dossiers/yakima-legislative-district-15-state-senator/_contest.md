## What the office does
A Washington State Senator is one of 49 senators, one per legislative district, elected for a four-year term. Senators write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, and the Senate confirms the governor's appointments. The 15th District covers parts of Yakima and Benton counties [S1][S2].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. The incumbent senator listed for 2026, Nikki Torres (R), is not on the 15th District ballot [S2]; Republican State Rep. Jeremie Dufault of Selah is the only candidate [S1] (jeremie-dufault.md S2).
