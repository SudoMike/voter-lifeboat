## What the office does
A Washington State Representative is one of two House members (plus one Senator) elected from each legislative district to the 98-member State House for a two-year term. Representatives write and vote on state law and the operating, capital and transportation budgets, including K-12 funding, the tax structure, housing rules, labor law and criminal-justice statutes. The 14th District covers east Yakima, the Lower Yakima Valley and most of the Yakama Reservation, west to Lyle and east into part of Pasco [S2].

## Race dynamics
Republican Rep. Deb Manjarrez of Wapato, a CPA and orchardist seeking a second term, faces Democrat Ezequiel Morfin Jr. of Toppenish, an electrician, former Toppenish council member and former United Farm Workers organizer [S1][S2]. Manjarrez led the primary with 51.2% to Morfin's 27.3% (Democrat Tony Sandoval took 21.3%) [S3], and has raised more than three times as much (deb-manjarrez.md S20; ezequiel-morfin.md S8).

Manjarrez runs on limiting spending, tighter grant audits and redirecting Climate Commitment Act revenue; her record includes no votes on the 2026 income tax, the 2025 revenue bills, rent stabilization and immigrant worker protections (deb-manjarrez.md S5-S12, S17). Morfin runs on affordability, "fair taxation," renter protections and limiting state cooperation with federal immigration enforcement (ezequiel-morfin.md S3, S6). At the October interview they split on farmworker collective bargaining (his top priority; she opposes it) and data centers (she supports them with little state regulation; he says no pending more research), and both said the farmworker overtime law should be revisited, for different reasons [S2].

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Taxes and spending:** Manjarrez's no votes on the 2026 income tax and 2025 revenue bills and focus on spending restraint vs. Morfin's call for fairer taxation and more school and childcare funding.
2. **Immigration:** Morfin would limit state cooperation with federal enforcement; Manjarrez voted against immigrant worker protections.
3. **Technology:** Manjarrez supports data centers; Morfin opposes them pending research.
4. **Housing:** Manjarrez voted against rent stabilization; Morfin supports renter protections and down-payment relief.
5. **Climate and energy:** Manjarrez blames climate policy for utility costs and voted against the clean fuels bill; no Morfin climate position.
6. **Parents and schools:** Manjarrez's record and statements on parental notification and record access; no Morfin position.
7. **Record vs. renewal:** one-term incumbent and former county party chair vs. a former city council member.
