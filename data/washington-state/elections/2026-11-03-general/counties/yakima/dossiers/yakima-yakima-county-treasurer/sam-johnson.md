## Background
Sam Johnson, of Yakima, is a financial examiner supervisor in the Division of Credit Unions of the Washington State Department of Financial Institutions, where he says he oversees examinations of complex credit unions, leads teams and works with institution leadership on risk management, budgeting and long-term planning [S2][S3]. He has been president of New Leaders Yakima County, treasurer of the Central Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce and is president of the Yakima Schools Foundation [S2]. He runs with an Independent party preference [S1][S3]. Identity check: the Yakima address, the campaign site and the Herald-Republic report describe the same Yakima candidate [S2][S3][S4]. He did not submit a statement to the general-election voters' guide [S1].

## Positions
- **Internal controls:** would "strengthen internal controls and oversight to prevent fraud, reduce risk, and ensure public funds are managed with discipline and care" [S2].
- **Centralized receipting:** would implement a single countywide payment-receiving system "to ensure consistency, reduce errors, and provide full accountability for every dollar collected" [S2].
- **Investments:** would apply "proven investment strategies" prioritizing security and liquidity while maximizing returns [S2].
- **Transparency:** would expand access to "clear, real-time financial information" on how taxpayer money is collected, managed and distributed [S2].
- **Staff:** would support and invest in the office's existing staff [S2].
- **Framing:** says the office benefits from "fresh ideas, a focus on operational efficiency, and a broader financial perspective" and calls for "disciplined financial leadership that protects taxpayer dollars" [S3].
