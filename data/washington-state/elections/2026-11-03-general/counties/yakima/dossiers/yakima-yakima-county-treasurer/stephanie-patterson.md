## Background
Stephanie Patterson, of Selah, grew up in Roslyn and has worked in the Yakima County Treasurer's Office since 2014, currently as senior manager for investments and banking; she has also been the office's accounting manager, senior accountant and an accountant [S1][S2][S3]. Her statement says she manages the county's investment portfolio, banking functions, general-fund revenues and debt activities, after 10 years of public and private accounting [S1]. She holds a WSU bachelor's in accounting (minor in political science) and the Certified Government Investment Professional credential [S1][S2]. She lists her party preference as Republican [S1][S3].

## Positions
- **Centralized receipting and payments:** would expand centralized receipting across the county and add convenient payment options [S1][S3]; lists "streamlining the handling of government funds" first among her priorities [S2].
- **Internal controls and safeguarding funds:** would ensure proper internal controls over the office's banking and e-commerce functions and protect taxpayer dollars "through transparent accounting and rigorous oversight of county funds" [S2][S3].
- **Investments:** would continue managing the Treasurer's Investment Pool for safety, liquidity and return [S3].
- **Technology:** has helped implement the office's receipting, investment and debt software and will be involved in a new tax software system; promises continual improvement of financial applications [S2][S3].
- **Continuity:** says her office experience makes her "the only candidate capable of seamlessly stepping into the role on day one" [S1].
