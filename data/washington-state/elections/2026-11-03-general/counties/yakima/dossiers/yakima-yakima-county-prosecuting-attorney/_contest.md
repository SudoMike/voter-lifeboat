## What the office does
The county prosecuting attorney charges and tries felonies in Superior Court and misdemeanors in District Court on behalf of the state, decides which cases are filed and what plea offers are made, runs victim and witness services, and is the county government's civil lawyer. The prosecutor also sits on the county canvassing board [S2].

## Race dynamics
Prosecutor Joe Brusic is not seeking a fourth term, so the seat is open [S2]. Both candidates prefer the Republican Party and both are former deputy prosecutors in the office's special assault unit [S1][S2]. Tyler Haueter, a Kennewick assistant city attorney, runs an enforcement-first platform (no plea-downs for gang violence, full prosecution of repeat offenders) and has the county Republican Party's endorsement (tyler-haueter.md S1, S2). Quinn Dalan, a Yakima lawyer since 2008, municipal prosecutor for three Lower Valley towns and a former public defender and legal-aid director, runs on breadth of experience and is endorsed by the Mainstream Republicans, the sheriff's deputies' guild and many retired judges (quinn-dalan.md S1, S3, S4). The county Republican Party ran a months-long online campaign against Dalan questioning her party loyalty and publicizing a 2011 police report involving her now-husband; Haueter says he was not involved [S2]. Dalan has raised about three times as much (quinn-dalan.md S6; tyler-haueter.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Public safety:** Haueter's explicit no-plea-down, maximum-enhancement charging pledges vs. Dalan's general emphasis on accountability and a balanced view from both sides of the courtroom.
2. **Record vs. renewal:** both are first-time candidates with prosecutorial records; Dalan's local career is longer and broader, Haueter stresses current trial work.
