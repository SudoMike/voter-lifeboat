## Background
Tyler Haueter is an assistant city attorney for Kennewick who lives in Yakima County and has not held elected office [S1][S3]. He spent seven years as a U.S. Air Force paralegal, then more than three years as the sole criminal investigator for the Yakima County Department of Assigned Counsel, before earning a J.D. from the University of Idaho College of Law [S1][S2]. He then prosecuted in the Yakima County Prosecutor's Office Special Assault Unit, handling domestic violence, molestation and homicide cases [S2][S3]. His statement lists nearly 19 years in the criminal-justice system, including work as deputy prosecuting attorney, defense attorney and court clerk [S1]. He and his wife have seven children [S1][S2].

## Positions
- **Gangs:** would partner with the Yakima police, sheriff and FBI joint gang task force, charge gang offenses as felonies, pursue every available sentencing enhancement, and allow "no plea-downs for gang violence" [S2][S3].
- **Plea deals and repeat offenders:** would end "soft-on-crime" plea deals and prosecute repeat and violent offenders "to the full extent of the law" [S2][S3].
- **Victims:** would keep the Special Assault Unit and victim/witness services fully resourced; focus on violent crime, domestic violence and crimes against children and seniors [S1][S2].
- **Law enforcement:** would prosecute the cases police bring and treat assaults on officers as priority crimes; "No more cases dropped to make the office's numbers look better" [S2].
- **Property and drug crime:** would direct prosecution resources to property crime, retail and vehicle theft and "drug-fueled disorder" [S2].
- **Approach:** "I will enforce the law firmly, consistently, and without apology" [S1].

## Record
- His campaign lists a 2024 murder conviction (State v. Randy Razey, sentence of more than 23 years) and 2025 child-molestation convictions as cases he prosecuted [S2]. These are campaign claims and were not checked against court records here.
