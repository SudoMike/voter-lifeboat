## Background
- Yakima County District Court judge since March 2022 [S1]; he was appointed to fill the vacancy left by Judge Kevin Roy's retirement, and he won the November 2022 election over Selah attorney Wes Gano, 66.1% to 33.8% in early returns [S2].
- Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney 2000-2022, in the District Court, Juvenile, General Felony, Narcotics, Special Assault (as supervisor) and Gang units, prosecuting domestic violence, sexual assault, firearm, narcotics-trafficking and murder cases [S1].
- Judge pro tem in the Yakima, Union Gap, Wapato and Granger municipal courts 2011-2022; member of the District and Municipal Court Judges' Association (DMCJA) Rules Committee 2024-2026 [S1].
- Education: J.D., Seattle University School of Law (1997); B.A. degrees in business administration (finance) and history, University of Washington (1993) [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
- Says he will "treat all people with respect and fairness" and cites support from judges, law enforcement and community leaders [S1].
