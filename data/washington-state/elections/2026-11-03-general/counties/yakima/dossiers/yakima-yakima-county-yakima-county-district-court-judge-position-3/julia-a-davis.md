## Background
- Yakima County District Court judge since April 2025 [S1]. County commissioners appointed her in March 2025 to the seat of Judge Donald Engel, who resigned; she was one of five applicants and two interviewed [S2]. Because she was appointed, the ballot lists the position as a "4 year short/full term": voters fill both the rest of the current term and the next full four-year term [S4].
- Deputy prosecuting attorney in the Yakima County Prosecuting Attorney's Office 2011-2025, handling domestic violence, sexual assault, crimes against children, DUI, homicide and vehicular homicide in District and Superior Court [S1]; at her appointment she supervised the Special Assault Unit [S2].
- Education: J.D., Gonzaga University School of Law (2010); B.A. in criminal justice, Washington State University (2007) [S1].
- Listed among the court's judges on the county's District Court page [S3].
