## Background
- Yakima County District Court judge since his election in 2010 [S1]; he was re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S3] and is listed among the court's judges [S2].
- Before the bench he ran his own law firm and served as Selah Municipal Court judge, special prosecutor, public defender, Washington assistant attorney general and judge pro tem in several courts in the county [S1].
- Has presented at continuing-education events for attorneys and judges and served on courtroom-technology committees [S1].
- Education: J.D., University of Idaho [S1]. Community service includes Boy Scouts merit-badge counselor, East Valley Little League coach and Yakima County Volunteer Attorney Services [S1].
