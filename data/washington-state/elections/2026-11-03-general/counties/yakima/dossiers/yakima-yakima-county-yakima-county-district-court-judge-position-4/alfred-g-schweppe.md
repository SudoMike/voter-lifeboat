## Background
- Yakima County District Court judge since 2013 and the court's presiding judge, overseeing its administrative operations [S1][S2]. He was re-elected unopposed in 2022 [S3].
- Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney 1995-2001; attorney and partner at Meyer Fluegge and Tenney, P.S., 2002-2012; Washington State Bar member since 1992 [S1].
- Presides over the court's DUI Court, which supervises treatment of repeat DUI offenders [S1].
- Education: B.A. in political science, Seattle University (1983); J.D., Seattle University School of Law (1991). U.S. Army veteran; Yakima County Planning Commission 2010-2012 [S1].

## Judicial approach (from own statements)
- Says judges "should be fair, compassionate, and respectful" and describes DUI Court as promoting public safety "through intensive court monitored treatment of repeat DUI offenders while holding them accountable" [S1].
