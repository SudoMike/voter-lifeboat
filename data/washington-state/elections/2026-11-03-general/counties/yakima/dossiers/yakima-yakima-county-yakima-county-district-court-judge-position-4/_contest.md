## What the office does
Yakima County District Court is a court of limited jurisdiction: it hears traffic and non-traffic infractions, misdemeanor and gross-misdemeanor criminal cases, civil cases up to $100,000, small claims up to $5,000, name changes and anti-harassment and stalking protection orders. It also provides municipal court services for Union Gap, Grandview, Mabton, Tieton and Moxee, with courtrooms in Yakima and Grandview [S1]. Its four judges are elected countywide for four-year terms; every Yakima County ballot carries all four positions [S2].
