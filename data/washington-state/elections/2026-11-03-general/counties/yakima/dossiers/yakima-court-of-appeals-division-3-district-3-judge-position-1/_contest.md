## What the office does
The Court of Appeals hears appeals from superior court decisions. Division III has five judges; its District 3 (Chelan, Douglas, Kittitas, Klickitat and Yakima counties) elects two of them [S2], for six-year terms. All voters in those five counties vote on the seat, so in Yakima County it is a countywide contest [S1].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: Judge Megan K. Murphy, appointed to the court in January 2025, is the only candidate [S1] (megan-k-murphy.md S2).
