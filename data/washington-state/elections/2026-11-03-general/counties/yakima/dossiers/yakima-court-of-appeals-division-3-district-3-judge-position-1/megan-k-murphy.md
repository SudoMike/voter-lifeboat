## Background
- Judge of the Washington Court of Appeals, Division III, since January 2025 [S1]. Gov. Jay Inslee appointed her to replace Judge Rebecca Pennell, who became a U.S. District Court judge; she was one of four applicants [S2].
- Began her career in 2001 as a Yakima County deputy prosecuting attorney (about five years), then moved to civil litigation in private practice [S1][S2]: a Yakima practice focused on medical-malpractice defense, then partner at Fox Ballard in Seattle [S2]. She had appeared before Division III in numerous cases [S2].
- Education: Eisenhower High School (1993); Claremont McKenna College (1997); University of Colorado School of Law (2001) [S1].
- Community service: Yakima Town Hall and Downtown Yakima Rotary [S1]; Yakima Symphony Orchestra board since 2015, including as president and vice president [S2].
