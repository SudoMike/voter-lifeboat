## What the office does
The County Clerk is an elected, four-year office (chapter 36.23 RCW, Article IV, Section 26 of the state constitution): the administrator of the Clerk's Office and executive officer of the Superior Court. The office receives and processes court documents, assists in court proceedings, maintains court files, records and exhibits, and receipts and disburses fees, fines and court-ordered payments [S1][S2].
