## What the office does
The sheriff runs the Yakima County Sheriff's Office, which patrols unincorporated areas and contract cities, runs search and rescue, monitors registered sex offenders and serves civil court papers [S2]. The sheriff sets enforcement priorities and policy within a budget set by the county commissioners [S2].

## Race dynamics
The seat is open: Sheriff Bob Udell is retiring after two terms and is running for county commissioner; he endorsed Lt. Jake Church [S2]. It is the first contested sheriff's race in eight years [S2]. Both candidates prefer the Republican Party [S1]. Church, 26 years in law enforcement and 10 in the sheriff's office, runs on that experience; Roman Kilinkaridis, a West Richland police officer since 2021 and Yakima business owner, runs as an outsider (jake-church.md S4; roman-kilinkaridis.md S3). Both see the budget and deputy staffing as the main issue (jake-church.md S4). They differ on Flock license-plate-reader cameras: Church would keep them with audits, Kilinkaridis would switch the new ones off pending an advisory vote (jake-church.md S4; roman-kilinkaridis.md S2, S3). Church calls the sheriff decertification law (SB 5974) unconstitutional; Kilinkaridis says a sheriff who cannot keep certification probably should not serve (jake-church.md S4; roman-kilinkaridis.md S3). Herald-Republic reporting on West Richland records shows Kilinkaridis was demoted from sergeant in his probationary period, which he attributes to department politics (roman-kilinkaridis.md S4). Church has raised about three times as much (jake-church.md S6; roman-kilinkaridis.md S5).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Record vs. renewal:** sheriff's office lieutenant endorsed by the outgoing sheriff vs. a self-described outsider.
2. **Public safety:** Church emphasizes staffing, analytics-driven enforcement and prosecution follow-through; Kilinkaridis emphasizes youth programs, domestic-violence victims, community engagement and limits on camera surveillance.
3. **Money and power:** Kilinkaridis proposes a public advisory vote on Flock cameras and a public promise ledger.
