## Background
- Incumbent Yakima County Coroner (Prefers Republican Party) [S1]. Chief deputy coroner 2009-2025; self-employed body-removal technician for local funeral homes 2002-2024 [S1].
- Ran the office from September 2024, after Coroner Jim Curtice placed himself on leave; county commissioners unanimously appointed him coroner in May 2025 after Curtice resigned [S2][S3]. The county Republican Party had submitted three names and preferred Dan Williams; commissioners chose Slight [S2][S3].
- Won the November 2025 special election for the rest of Curtice's term, leading Williams about 70% to 28% in election-night returns [S3]. He ran unsuccessfully against Curtice in 2022 [S3].
- Training: medical-legal investigation (2010) and death investigation (2012) courses; continuing education on mass casualties (2023) [S1].

## Positions
- **Office operations:** says he has strengthened relationships with the public and law enforcement, improved accountability in reporting, and set protocols for handling personal effects, illegal drugs and death information [S1].
- **Budget:** describes the office's roughly $600,000 budget as tight for a large county and names aging vehicles and stretchers as the most pressing needs; he chose to refurbish a stretcher (about $3,000) rather than buy a new one (over $60,000) [S2].
- **Autopsies:** with no local forensic pathologist, the office sends cases out of county; he said a new Tri-Cities pathologist has shortened trips that used to go to Thurston County [S2].
- **Public health and safety:** member of county suicide-prevention, drug-epidemic, child-fatality-review and mass-casualty planning groups, and the 2026 FIFA World Cup fatality-management workgroup [S1].
