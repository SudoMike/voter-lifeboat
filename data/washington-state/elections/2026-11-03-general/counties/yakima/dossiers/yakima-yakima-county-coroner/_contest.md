## What the office does
The elected County Coroner investigates sudden, unexpected, violent, suspicious or unnatural deaths [S1], oversees autopsies and may testify in court about a death. In 2024 the office took jurisdiction of 818 of the county's 1,831 deaths [S2].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested: incumbent Coroner Marshall Slight (Prefers Republican Party), appointed in 2025 and elected in the November 2025 special election to finish his predecessor's term, is the only candidate for the full four-year term (marshall-slight.md S1, S3).
