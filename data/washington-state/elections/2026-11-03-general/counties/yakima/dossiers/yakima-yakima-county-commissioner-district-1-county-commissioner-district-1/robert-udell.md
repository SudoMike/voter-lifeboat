## Background
Robert (Bob) Udell is the retiring two-term Yakima County Sheriff; his statement gives his first term as sheriff starting in 2019, and his site says he has been sheriff since 2018 (the year he was elected) [S1][S2]. He has 36 years in the sheriff's office, from patrol deputy and detective to administrative leadership [S2][S3]. He is a fifth-generation Yakima County resident from an apple-growing family, holds a Washington State University degree in horticulture, and formerly owned Udell Apples [S1][S2]. He is a past chief of the Naches Heights Fire Department, chairs the Yakima Valley Local Crime Lab executive committee and Safe Yakima Valley, and serves on the Salvation Army board [S1].

In the August 4 primary he placed first with 12,562 votes (65.35%); Kristoffer Strutner was second with 2,629 (13.68%) [S5].

## Positions
- **Budget:** says the county's hiring and position freezes are a good short-term step but the county must restructure "how we do business" for the long term; taxes are "a last resort," and revenue should come from growth, which he says commissioners can help stimulate [S4]. His statement and site stress fiscal responsibility and using resources efficiently and transparently [S1][S2].
- **Priorities:** public safety, agriculture, water rights and managing the county budget [S3].
- **Partnerships:** emphasizes relationships with cities, towns and agencies, citing as sheriff a modern communications system, a regional SWAT team and a local crime lab [S2].
- **Animal control:** supports the proposed stricter animal ordinances and a county-sponsored shelter built with cities, private donors and public money [S4].
