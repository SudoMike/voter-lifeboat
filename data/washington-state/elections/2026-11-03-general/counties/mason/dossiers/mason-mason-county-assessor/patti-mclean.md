## Background

- Patti McLean (Prefers Independent Party) has been Mason County Assessor since 2019. She started at the front counter of the Assessor's office in 1991 and cites 34 years of experience [S1].
- Community service: former school board director, Nimrod Club member, volunteer for the local grange and blood drives [S1].
- Elections: re-elected in 2022 with 15,990 votes (55.4%) over Republican George Blush (12,804) [S2][S3]. Unopposed in 2026 [S1][S4]. Her 2026 PDC record reports no contribution totals [S5].

## Positions

- Runs as an Independent "because I believe local offices should remain nonpartisan" [S1].
- Says the office must follow state mandates and regulations and report annually to the Department of Revenue, and that its day-to-day duties stay the same under either party [S1].
- Promises "accountability, fairness, and quality customer service," an open-door policy for employees and taxpayers, and tools and information to help taxpayers understand the office [S1].
