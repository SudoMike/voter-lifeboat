## What the office does
Mason County District Court has one elected judge ("the Mason County District Court Judge"), elected county-wide for a four-year term [S1]; the 2022 District Court race appeared on every reporting precinct in the county [S2]. The incumbent describes the judge's job as impartially hearing cases, applying the law to the facts and deciding them fairly [S1].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Judge George Alan Steele, District Court judge since January 2019 and Shelton Municipal Court judge from 2009, is the only candidate; the pamphlet marks him "Unopposed" [S1].
