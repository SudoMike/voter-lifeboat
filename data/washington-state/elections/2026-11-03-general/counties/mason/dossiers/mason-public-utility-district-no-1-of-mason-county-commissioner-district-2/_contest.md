## What the office does
Mason County PUD No. 1 is a publicly owned utility governed by three commissioners, one from each of its districts, who serve six-year terms. The commission sets district policy, approves budgets and expenditures and adopts the strategic plan, and it meets at the district office in Potlatch (S2). The PUD provides electric and water service (S4). Commissioners are nominated by commissioner district, and voters of the entire PUD elect them at the general election (S3).

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Commissioner Ron Gold, who represents District 2 (Skokomish Valley and Potlatch) for the 2021-2026 term and is in his third term, is the only candidate (S1, S2). He submitted no pamphlet statement (S1).
