## What the office does
The treasurer bills and collects property taxes (including foreclosure of delinquent accounts), receives and accounts for the money of the county and its junior taxing districts, deposits and invests those funds, collects real estate excise tax and administers county debt (S2, S3).

## Race dynamics
Brittany A. Pearson (Prefers Democratic Party), appointed treasurer after 25 years on the office's staff, faces retired actuary Randy Lewis (Prefers Republican Party). The ballot fills both the rest of the current term and the next four-year term (S1). Pearson led the certified August 4 primary 54.01% to 45.90% (S4). Pearson runs on knowing the office "from the ground up"; Lewis on bringing actuarial forecasting and asset/liability management to the county's finances (brittany-a-pearson.md, randy-lewis.md).

## Differentiating issue axes
1. **Experience/record:** a 25-year insider now serving by appointment against a first-time candidate from a private-sector actuarial career.
2. Neither candidate states a direction on taxes or county spending; both speak of stewardship and forecasting.
3. Positions on the 2026 statewide initiatives: none found for either candidate.
