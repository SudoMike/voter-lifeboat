## Background

Randy Lewis (Prefers Republican Party; PDC filer name Randal J Lewis) is a Mason County native who retired home after an actuarial career; the treasurer's race would be his first elected office [S1][S2][S4]. He lists chairing rate-setting, asset/liability management and other committees for national and international corporations; a B.S. in mathematics from the University of Washington; and membership in the American Academy of Actuaries, the Society of Actuaries and the International Actuarial Association [S1][S2]. Community service: Shelton Centennial Lions Club, founder and coordinator of the Shelton Math Motivators tutoring program, Shelton School District bond committee, and the Mason County Planning Advisory Commission [S1][S2]. He filed as the only challenger to the appointed treasurer [S5].

In the certified August 4 primary he received 8,921 votes (45.90%) to Brittany Pearson's 10,497 (54.01%) [S3].

## Positions

### County finances
- Says his actuarial "experience in financial forecasting and risk management" would help guide the county, with examples including efforts "to optimize assets vs liabilities" and to address inflation [S1].
- Frames the central question as "what will be the relationship of revenue vs expenditures going forward as the community grows, and increasing demand for County services," and proposes quantifying the county's planning goals "under several potential scenarios" [S1].
- Invites residents to send him "any questions/comments on Mason County financial issues" [S1].
