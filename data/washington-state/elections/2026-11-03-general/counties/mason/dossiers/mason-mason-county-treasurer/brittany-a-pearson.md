## Background

Brittany A. Pearson (Prefers Democratic Party) holds the appointed position of Mason County Treasurer and is listed as treasurer on the county's website [S1][S2][S3]. She reports 25 years in the Mason County Treasurer's Office in several positions, with experience in property tax collection, county finance, banking procedures, investments, customer service, management and daily operations [S1][S2]. She is a Shelton High School graduate with ongoing professional training in county finance and treasury management [S1]. Elisabeth (Lisa) Frazier was elected treasurer unopposed in 2022 [S8] and is described in 2026 as the former treasurer [S4]; the ballot carries both the short (unexpired) and the full four-year term [S1].

In the certified August 4 primary she received 10,497 votes (54.01%) to Randy Lewis's 8,921 (45.90%) [S5].

## Positions

- Priorities: "fiscal responsibility, accuracy, accessibility and excellent customer service"; "safeguarding taxpayer dollars while ensuring the office operates efficiently and professionally" [S1].
