## Background

- Michael Dorcy (Prefers Republican Party) was first elected Mason County Prosecuting Attorney in January 2011 and is in his fourth term [S1]. His campaign site gives his tenure as 2011 to present [S2].
- Before that he was a deputy prosecutor in Mason County (2004-2010) and an associate at Settle & Johnson, PLLC (2000-2003). He holds a JD from Willamette University College of Law and a BA in English literature from Westmont College; he graduated from Shelton High School in 1993 [S1][S2].
- Elections: unopposed in 2022 (20,074 votes; 992 write-ins) [S3] and in 2026 [S1][S5]. PDC summary as queried on 2026-10-09: $2,349.19 raised, $2,594.79 spent [S6].

## Positions

- Campaign theme "Trusted Justice" / "Proven Experience. Trusted Justice." [S1][S2].
- Names indigent defense funding and availability as a primary issue and says he will manage "these legislative shifts" so the office stays efficient and victims receive advocacy without delay [S1]. His site posts the Washington State Bar Association's indigent defense standards and the Supreme Court orders implementing them [S2].
- Promises "a firm and fair approach to public safety" and closer collaboration between law enforcement and his office [S1].
