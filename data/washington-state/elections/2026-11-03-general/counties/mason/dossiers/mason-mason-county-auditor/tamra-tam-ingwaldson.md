## Background

Tamra "Tam" Ingwaldson (Prefers Democratic Party) moved to Mason County in 2007 and has lived at Lake Limerick since 2011 [S4]. She is a first-time candidate for county office; her statement lists no elected experience [S1][S2][S10]. She reports 20 years in corporate telecommunications and more than 20 years in nonprofit management, including a combined 15 years with United Way of Mason County (executive director) and United Way of Kitsap County; she has also worked as a crime victim advocate [S1][S4]. She is now a program support specialist with New Horizon Communities, a group of tiny-home villages in Mason, Thurston and Pierce counties [S1][S4]. She completed a B.S. in business management in 2025 [S1][S4][S7]. Community service: incoming Hood Canal Lions Club president, past president of the Kiwanis Club of North Mason, past board chair of the Lake Limerick Community Club, P.E.O. Chapter B, Saint David of Wales Episcopal Church; she is a Precinct Committee Officer [S1][S4][S7]. The Shelton-Mason County Chamber named her its 2025 Citizen of the Year [S4][S7].

In the certified August 4 primary she placed first with 7,987 votes (40.68%) to incumbent Steve Duenkel's 7,284 (37.10%) [S8].

## Positions

### Elections administration and the office
- Promises elections "that are secure, efficient, and accessible to all," to make the office "more open and responsive," and that "every voter should be able to see democracy in action" [S1].
- Says the current auditor's role as chair of the Mason County Republican Party is "a clear conflict of interest"; promises the office will be administered "fairly and without political influence" and that she will "work for every voter in our county, not just those who vote like me" [S3][S5].
- Says staff effectiveness is "compromised by micromanagement, unreliable systems, unclear procedures, insufficient training, and inadequate staffing," and promises to give staff the tools, technology and support they need [S1][S5].
- Lists the office's duties as public records, "accurate budget information," marriage licenses, licensing and elections [S5]; no specific position found on county budget or reserve questions.
