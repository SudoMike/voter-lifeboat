## What the office does
The Sheriff runs the Mason County Sheriff's Office, the county's law enforcement agency; the incumbent describes sheriffs as "the only elected Law Enforcement Officials" [S1].

## Race dynamics
Uncontested. Sheriff Ryan Spurling (Prefers Republican Party), in office since 2023, is the only candidate; the pamphlet marks him "Unopposed" [S1][S2]. A 2026 state law letting a state board remove sheriffs and adding eligibility requirements was partly blocked by a Thurston County judge in May; Spurling opposed it [S3].
