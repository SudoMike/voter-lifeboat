## Background

- Ryan Spurling (Prefers Republican Party) has been Mason County Sheriff since 2023 [S1].
- He retired after 25 years with the Washington State Patrol and reports more than 22 years of active and reserve military service, including activation for Operation Iraqi Freedom as a battalion acting command sergeant major at Joint Base Lewis-McChord. He holds a bachelor's degree in business administration [S1].
- Community service: two years as board president of the Turning Point domestic violence shelter, 19 years as a church elder, volunteer karate instructor, Kiwanis law enforcement youth camp, North Mason Chamber of Commerce board, Shelton Rotary [S1].
- Elections: unopposed in 2022 (20,608 votes; 955 write-ins) [S2] and in 2026 [S1][S4]. PDC summary as queried on 2026-10-09: $1,692.32 raised, $1,847.32 spent [S5].

## Positions

- Promises a Sheriff's Office that is "transparent and responsible with your tax dollars," and to be accessible to every citizen [S1].
- Says the office must "connect with and protect all our citizens" and meet "the ever-changing challenges society has placed on Law Enforcement" [S1].
- Opposed the 2026 state law allowing a governor-appointed board to remove sheriffs and adding eligibility requirements; after a Thurston County judge enjoined parts of it, he said the judge "really thinks that the legislature overstepped" and, per the Journal, was disappointed that Gov. Bob Ferguson signed what the paper paraphrased him as calling a "clearly unconstitutional bill" [S3].
