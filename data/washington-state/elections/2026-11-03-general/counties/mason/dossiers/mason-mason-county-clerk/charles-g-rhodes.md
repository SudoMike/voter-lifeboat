## Background

Charles G. Rhodes (Prefers Republican Party) has been Mason County Clerk since January 1, 2023, and is a Republican Precinct Committee Officer [S1][S2]. He won the seat in 2022 as the Republican challenger to Clerk Sharon Moore-Fogo, who had worked in the office since 1992 and was appointed clerk in 2017; the final count was 14,727 to 14,223 [S3][S4]. He lists work as agency principal and licensed private investigator at Olympic Process and Investigative Services (2009 to present), senior accountant and owner at All Pro Tax and Accounting, department advocate for Disabled American Veterans (1985-1989), and service as a U.S. Air Force veteran; he is a Certified Fraud Examiner, holds a B.A. in accounting from the University of Arkansas and is president of Charlie's Vision, a nonprofit supporting law enforcement [S1][S2].

In the certified August 4 primary he received 7,858 votes (49.01%) to Tammy Clark's 7,759 (48.39%), a 99-vote margin [S5].
