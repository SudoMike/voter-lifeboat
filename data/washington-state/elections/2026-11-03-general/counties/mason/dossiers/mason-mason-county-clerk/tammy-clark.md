## Background

Tammy Clark (Prefers Republican Party; PDC filer name Michele T. Clark) is challenging the incumbent clerk, a fellow Republican [S2][S6][S7]. She lists no elected experience. She reports more than 20 years of legal management experience: office manager and legal assistant at the Law Office of Stephen Whitehouse in Shelton (2025-2026), Whitehouse & Nichols, LLP, Shelton (2018-2025), and the Law Offices of J. Mitchell Clark (2003-2017), and an associate's degree in business administration from Del Mar College [S1][S2]. Community service: treasurer of the Republican Women's Group of Mason County (2025-2026), America 250 Mason County, Belfair HUB, conciliator at the Dispute Resolution Center of Thurston County (2017-2025; volunteer of the year 2021), and treasurer of the Olympia and Washington State beekeepers' associations (2018-2022) [S1][S3].

In the certified August 4 primary she received 7,759 votes (48.39%) to Rhodes's 7,858 (49.01%) [S5].
