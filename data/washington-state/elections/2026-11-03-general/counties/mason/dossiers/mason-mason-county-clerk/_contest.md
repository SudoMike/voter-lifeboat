## What the office does
The county clerk is clerk of the superior court: it keeps the court's records and files, and collects and disburses fees, fines and other money the court orders paid (S1, S2).

## Race dynamics
Clerk Charles G. Rhodes, elected in 2022 by 504 votes over Clerk Sharon K. Moore-Fogo (S4), faces Tammy Clark; both prefer the Republican Party. The certified August 4 primary was nearly tied: Rhodes 49.01%, Clark 48.39% (S3). Rhodes runs on technology upgrades, modernized procedures and current collections; Clark, a longtime law-office manager, says she will "restore trust," fix staffing and stabilize an office with high turnover, and is endorsed by retired Clerk Pat Swartos (charles-g-rhodes.md, tammy-clark.md).
