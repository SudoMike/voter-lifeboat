## What it does

The Southside School District No. 42 board (Resolution No. 7-25-26) asks voters to replace an expiring levy for educational programs and operations with a four-year excess levy, collected 2027 through 2030 [S1][S2]. The district has about 200 students, according to the statement for [S1][S2].

- **What it pays for.** Educational programs, non-high payments, classified and certificated staffing not funded through the state's prototypical funding model, health and safety staff, afterschool programs, curriculum, professional development, school readiness, operational technology, nutrition and technology costs and special education costs not covered by state or federal funds [S1][S2].
- **Vote needed.** A simple majority [S3].

## Cost

|---|---|---|
| 2027 | $994,006 | $1.97 |
| 2028 | $1,063,587 | $1.97 |
| 2029 | $1,138,038 | $1.97 |
| 2030 | $1,217,701 | $1.97 |

[S1][S2]. Tax exemptions may be available to homeowners 61 or older or disabled who meet income limits [S1][S2].

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

It replaces the four-year levy voters approved in 2022, which is expiring; the maximum rate, $1.97 per $1,000, is "the same amount voters OKed four years ago" [S3]. Because collection is based on assessed values, the district could collect at less than $1.97 [S3].

## Arguments for

Statement for, by Evelyn Huff and James Huff [S1][S2]: Southside School was founded in 1903 with eight children from five families and now serves about 200 students a year; the community "has provided strong support by continuing to approve the yearly levy in the past," and "the students deserve the best we can give them."

## Arguments against

No statement against was submitted [S1]. No committee for or against was found in PDC records [S4].
