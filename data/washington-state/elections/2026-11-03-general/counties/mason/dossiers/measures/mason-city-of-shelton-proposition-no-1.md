## What it does

The Shelton City Council (Resolution 1450-0726) asks city voters to raise the Shelton Transportation Benefit District's sales and use tax "to a new rate of three-tenths of one percent (0.3%) for a term of ten (10) years," for "street and pedestrian maintenance, repair, and construction projects as identified in the City's Transportation programs," or for the time needed to repay transportation debt [S1][S2].

- **Projects named.** City-wide safety improvements, ADA compliance, street resurfacing, multimodal and bike connectivity projects and grant matching funds [S1][S2].
- **Who pays.** Anyone making a taxable purchase in the city, resident or not; state law limits the money to transportation improvements [S1][S2].
- **Council action.** In July 2026 the council unanimously gave preliminary approval to sending the increase to voters, with a final vote scheduled for July 21 [S3].

## Cost

The TBD's sales tax rises from 0.2% to 0.3% (one more cent on a $10 purchase) for ten years [S1][S2]. The ballot materials give no revenue estimate.

## Fiscal mechanics / What it replaces

The TBD was created by a vote of the people (the explanatory statement says in 2016; the Journal says city voters authorized the 0.2% tax in November 2015) and has collected 0.2% since; voters renewed it in 2025 [S1][S2][S3]. The 2025 extension passed on the August 5, 2025 primary ballot [S4]. This measure adds 0.1 percentage point on top of the renewed 0.2% [S1][S3].
