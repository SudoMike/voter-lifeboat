## Background

Randy Neatherlin, of Allyn, is a sitting Mason County Commissioner (S1, S2). He lists 14 years as county commissioner and 6 years as a Port of Allyn commissioner (S1, S2). In November 2024 he won a fourth county commission term representing District 1, running as an independent (Prefers Independent Party), with 18,967 votes (55.0%) over Republican William Harris (S3, S4). The PUD race is nonpartisan (S1).

He lists leadership roles with the county Planning Commission, Board of Health, Transit Authority, Hood Canal Coordinating Council, Habitat for Humanity, the Economic Development Council, the Olympic Regional Clean Air Authority, PRTPO and the North Mason Chamber (S1, S2). He reports an A.A.S. degree, a B.S. in business management, an M.S. in management and leadership, and Lean Six Sigma Black Belt certification. He describes being a "Successful Lifelong Entrepreneur" as his best education (S1, S2). Community service: Rotary, Kiwanis, Masons, Theler Community Center, North Mason School District and North Mason Pee Wees, where he says he coached and mentored more than 1,000 children, and The ARC (S1, S2).

## Positions

### Rates and PUD finances
- "Slow or stop these huge rate increases by budgeting and living within our means." "Aim for zero rate increases for 4 years" (S1, S2).
- Prioritize PUD broadband options, "creating competition, better service and access in rural areas" (S1, S2).
- Local service hubs for quicker customer access and outage response (S1, S2).
- "Investing into infrastructure expansion and upgrades to profit from inevitable future growth" (S1, S2).

### County record he cites
- Says that as commissioner he "rightsized our government personnel, refocusing on Law Enforcement" and took the general fund "from a low of $165K cash on hand to a whopping $29 million," "without raising county taxes for 10 years" (S1, S2).
- In December 2025 he moved to table the proposed 2026 county budget, citing reservations about proposed spending and suggesting cuts (S5). The budget passed unanimously a week later (S6).
