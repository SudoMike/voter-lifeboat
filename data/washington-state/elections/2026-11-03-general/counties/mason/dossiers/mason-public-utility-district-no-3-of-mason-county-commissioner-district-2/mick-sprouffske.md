## Background

Mick Sprouffske (Michael Norman Sprouffske Jr. in PDC records), of Grapeview, is running for the nonpartisan PUD 3 District 2 seat (S1, S2, S3). He reports no elected experience (S1). He has been a licensed professional land surveyor since 2002 and served in the U.S. Navy aboard the USS Henry M. Jackson (S1). He studied at Spokane Falls Community College and South Puget Sound Community College, and holds a UW Lean Six Sigma Green Belt (S1). He says he has more than 35 years of surveying experience in the private and public sectors and is retired from the Washington Department of Natural Resources. There he managed the Public Land Survey Office and administered the DNR Survey Advisory Board, working with all 39 county auditors on compliance with RCW 58.09 and WAC 332-130 (S1).

Community service: volunteer firefighter with Mason County Fire District 5 (1981-1982) and the Friday Harbor Fire Department (1983); assistant coach for Shelton Freestyle Wrestling; vice president of the Washington Council of County Surveyors; state president of the Land Surveyors Association of Washington in 2010; 22 years with the Mason County Forest Festival, now its president; VFW Post 1694 Shelton Honor Guard (S1). He says he has lived in Mason County 41 of his 60 years (S1).

## Positions

### Rates and PUD service
- Says PUDs grew out of the Grange-sponsored Initiative No. 1 of 1929 to bring electricity to rural Washington, and that PUDs today provide electricity, high-speed fiber and water (S1).
- "I truly appreciate the benefit of the public power that Mason PUD 3 provides, the lower rates, reliability and shorter outage times than the typical private utility" (S1).
- Praises the PUD's FIBERHOOD high-speed fiber program as "competitively priced and reliable" (S1).
- "I will advocate for affordable electricity rates as allowed by state mandates" (S1).
- Cites public-policy experience through DNR and the Washington AgForestry Leadership program (S1).
