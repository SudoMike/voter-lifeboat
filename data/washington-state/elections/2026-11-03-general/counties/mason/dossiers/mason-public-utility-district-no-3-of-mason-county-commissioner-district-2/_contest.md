## What the office does
Mason PUD 3 is the public utility serving most of Mason County with electricity and, through its fiber program, broadband (S1, S4). A three-member commission sets the utility's policies, adopts its budget and sets rates and charges. One commissioner is elected every two years to a six-year term (S4). Commissioners are nominated by commissioner district, and the voters of the entire PUD elect them at the general election (S3).

## Race dynamics
Open seat: the District 2 commissioner is Tom Farmer (S4), elected in 2020 with 64.8% (S5), and he is not on the ballot (S1, S2). The race is nonpartisan and had no primary (S1, S6).

Randy Neatherlin is a sitting Mason County Commissioner (District 1), re-elected in 2024 as an independent to a term that runs past this election (S7). His statement cites 14 years on the county commission and 6 years as a Port of Allyn commissioner (S1, S2). His priorities are to "slow or stop these huge rate increases by budgeting and living within our means," with an "aim for zero rate increases for 4 years," plus broadband competition, local service hubs and infrastructure investment (S1, S2).

Mick Sprouffske is a licensed land surveyor retired from the state Department of Natural Resources, a Navy veteran and president of the Mason County Forest Festival (S1). He praises PUD 3's public power and its fiber program, and says he "will advocate for affordable electricity rates as allowed by state mandates" (S1).

Sprouffske reported $5,842 raised; Neatherlin files under the PDC's mini-reporting option (S6).
