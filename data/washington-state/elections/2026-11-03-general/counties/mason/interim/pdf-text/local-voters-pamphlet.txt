Voter Accessibility Voting Assistance The Mason County Auditor’s Office Vote Center offers an Accessible Voting Unit (AVU) that assists voters with visual impairments, cognitive/learning disabilities, or limited mobility to vote independently and privately. The AVU has special features for voters who may have difficulty voting using a standard ballot. It has audio assistance, font control, visual color contrast, touchscreen input, and input through devices (lap paddle and sip-and-puff). The AVU will print the voted ballot, which can then be sealed in a return envelope and deposited in a ballot drop box or the mail. Accessible voting is available starting 18 days before Election Day. To use the Accessible Voting Unit, visit one of our Vote Center locations. (see page 3 for Vote Center locations) Voting Accessibility Advisory Committee (VAAC) The Voting Accessibility Advisory Committee updates the outreach plan by providing input and strategies to the Mason County Elections Department to ensure accessible services for voters with disabilities in our community and to ensure compliance with local, state, and federal requirements. To volunteer to be a committee member or for more information, contact the elections department or visit our website at: www.masoncountywaelections.gov (360) 427-9670 ext. 470 elections@masoncountywa.gov. 04
