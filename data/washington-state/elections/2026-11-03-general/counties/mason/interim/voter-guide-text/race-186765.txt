Statement
It is an honor to serve as Mason County Auditor.  The auditor’s job is one of the most important in county government because it demands responsible oversight of budgets, elections, licensing, permanent record maintenance, and passport service. It is my duty to ensure fiscal accountability, contract compliance, and election integrity.  

If re-elected, I will to continue to work with the county’s commissioners to responsibly steward your tax dollars, provide regular accounting of our county’s financial health, and support innovative voluntary programs to help shield our county’s property owners from title fraud.
