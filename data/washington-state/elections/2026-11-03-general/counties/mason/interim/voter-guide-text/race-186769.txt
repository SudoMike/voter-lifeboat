## Michael Dorcy | party: (Prefers Republican Party) | website: None | email: None
### Statement
Elected Experience

Michael Dorcy was first elected Mason County Prosecuting Attorney in January 2011. Now in his fourth term, he provides firm, fair leadership, managing hundreds of cases from felony trials to providing civil legal counsel for county departments.
Other Professional Experience

Michael served as a Deputy Prosecutor for Mason County (2004–2010) and gained private sector experience as an associate attorney at Settle & Johnson, PLLC (2000–2003).

Education

Michael earned a JD from Willamette University College of Law and a BA in English Literature from Westmont College.

Community Service
Michael serves primarily through his local church, and at community events or speaking to community interest groups.

Statement

With sixteen years of proven experience as your elected Prosecuting Attorney, my commitment to Mason County remains focused on "Trusted Justice." My qualifications are built on a foundation of stability; having navigated the complexities of both criminal prosecution and civil litigation, I offer the deep-rooted expertise necessary to lead our local justice system effectively.
A primary issue facing our community is the evolving landscape of indigent defense funding and availability. My plan involves proactive management of these legislative shifts to ensure that our office remains efficient and that victims of crime receive the advocacy they deserve without administrative delay. I am dedicated to maintaining a firm and fair approach to public safety, ensuring that the rule of law is applied consistently across all cases.
Looking forward, my ideas center on fostering a collaborative environment between law enforcement and the prosecutor’s office to address emerging public safety challenges. By leveraging a record of success and a trusted team, I will continue to prioritize the protection of our residents and the integrity of our courts. I am asking for your support for re-election to ensure Mason County continues to benefit from experienced, reliable leadership in the pursuit of justice for all.
